  description
    This paper introduces Vibe Theory, a speculative philosophical framework centered on experience as ontologically fundamental. It proposes a conceptual model in which physical and mental patterns are understood as arising from the organization of discrete experiential units. The work engages themes from philosophy of mind, panpsychism, and process philosophy, and is intended as a conceptual contribution rather than an empirical proposal.
