\section{The Ten Emanations (Sefirot)}

The \textbf{ten emanations} (sefirot, singular sefirah) are the channels through which Ein Sof becomes
knowable and creates the worlds. They are God as \textbf{structure and process}. They are attributes,
channels, and vessels of the divine light. They are not Ein Sof itself. They flow out of it.

They descend in order. Each one receives from those above it and gives to those below it. The whole
ladder is read two ways. Downward it is creation coming into being. Upward it is the soul returning to
its source.

\subsection{The Ten in Order}

The \textbf{Crown} (Keter) is the first emanation, nearest to Ein Sof. It is the \textbf{superconscious
will}, pure intention before any thought. It is so close to the infinite that it is almost not a
sefirah at all.

\textbf{Wisdom} (Chokmah) is the \textbf{flash of insight}, the seed-point, the first spark of an idea
before it is developed. It is the active father principle.

\textbf{Understanding} (Binah) is the \textbf{developing, structuring intellect} that takes the seed of
Chokmah and grows it into a full form. It is the receptive mother principle, the womb from which the
lower seven are born.

\textbf{Knowledge} (Da'at) is the \textbf{hidden sefirah}. It is the union of Chokmah and Binah brought
into conscious knowing. It is counted only when Keter is treated as hidden above counting. So the tree
always shows ten, but the inventory of named nodes is eleven. Da'at sits on the middle pillar at the
throat.

\textbf{Kindness} (Chesed) is \textbf{expansive love}, boundless giving, the wish to pour out without
limit. It is also called Gedulah, greatness.

\textbf{Strength} (Gevurah) is \textbf{restraint and judgment}, the holy no, limitation and boundary.
It is also called Din, judgment. It is the necessary counterweight to Chesed. Without it, love would
dissolve all form.

\textbf{Beauty} (Tiferet) is the \textbf{balancing center} of the whole tree. It is the reconciliation
of Chesed and Gevurah into compassion. It is the king, the heart, the sun, the most connected sefirah.

\textbf{Victory} (Netzach) is \textbf{persistence and drive}, feeling pushing outward into the world,
the impulse to keep going.

\textbf{Splendor} (Hod) is \textbf{surrender and structure}, the ordering of feeling, intellect
operating within emotion. It is the counterweight to Netzach.

\textbf{Foundation} (Yesod) is the \textbf{channel} that gathers all the upper energies and transmits
them to Malkhut. It is the funnel and the covenant, the bond between the divine structure and the
receiving world.

\textbf{Kingdom} (Malkhut) is \textbf{receptivity}, the vessel that receives all and gives nothing of
its own. It is the indwelling presence of God in the world, also called the \textbf{Shekhinah}, the
bride, the daughter, the Sabbath queen. It is the point where the divine touches creation.

\subsection{The Three Pillars}

The ten sefirot stand in \textbf{three vertical columns}. Two opposites are balanced by a reconciling
axis.

\begin{itemize}
 \item \textbf{Pillar of Mercy} (right, active, giving). Chokmah, Chesed, Netzach.
 \item \textbf{Pillar of Severity} (left, passive, restraining). Binah, Gevurah, Hod.
 \item \textbf{Pillar of Balance} (middle, the axis of consciousness). Keter, Da'at, Tiferet, Yesod,
 Malkhut.
\end{itemize}

The right gives. The left limits. The middle reconciles. Every horizontal triad on the tree repeats
this pattern of two opposites and a center. Chokmah and Binah are balanced by Keter. Chesed and Gevurah
are balanced by Tiferet. Netzach and Hod are balanced by Yesod.

\subsection{The Tree of Life and the 22 Paths}

The standard diagram of the ten sefirot connected by lines is the \textbf{Tree of Life} (Etz Chaim).

\begin{itemize}
 \item The ten sefirot are the \textbf{nodes}.
 \item \textbf{22 paths} connect them, one for each letter of the Hebrew alphabet. The 3 mother
 letters, 7 double letters, and 12 simple letters of the Sefer Yetzirah are distributed across these
 22 paths.
 \item Ten nodes plus 22 paths are the \textbf{32 Paths of Wisdom} by which God engraved creation.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Lightning Flash}

The energy moves on the tree in two directions.

The \textbf{lightning flash}, also called the flaming sword, is the path of \textbf{descent}. It is
creation coming into being. It zigzags down in numerical order. Keter to Chokmah to Binah, across and
down to Chesed, Gevurah, Tiferet, then Netzach, Hod, Yesod, and Malkhut. This is the path that brings
the divine light down into the world.

The \textbf{path of the serpent} is the path of \textbf{ascent}, the return. The serpent winds up the
tree along the 22 connecting paths, crossing the lightning flash. It is the route of the soul climbing
back. It takes each connecting letter-path in turn rather than leaping from node to node. The serpent
traverses all 22 paths where the lightning leapt the ten nodes.
