\section{The Divine Faces and the Repair (Tikkun)}

The shattering left the cosmos broken. The answer is \textbf{Tikkun}, the repair. Tikkun is not merely a
patching of the old order. It is a \textbf{reorganization}. The shattered, isolated sefirot regroup into
\textbf{divine faces} (partzufim), each a full persona that contains all ten and is bound to the others.
This section gives the repair and the faces it produces.

\subsection{Tikkun, the Repair}

\textbf{Repair} (Tikkun) means rectification, mending.

\begin{itemize}
 \item It is the \textbf{restoration} of the broken cosmos. The lights are gathered back. The vessels
 are rebuilt as strong and interconnected. The trapped sparks are lifted out of the husks and
 returned to holiness.
 \item It is \textbf{not instantaneous}. It is the ongoing work of all of history. Every righteous act,
 every commandment, every prayer done with right intention raises sparks and advances the repair.
 \item \textbf{Humanity is the active repairer.} Human beings, placed in the world of moral choice, are
 the agents who complete the mending. This is the \textbf{repair of the world} (tikkun olam).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Partzufim, the Divine Faces}

Where the Tohu sefirot stood alone in a line, the Tikun sefirot \textbf{interrelate} as full personas.
The standard count is five faces. A sixth, Atik Yomin, is sometimes added as the inner aspect of Keter.

\begin{longtable}{l L{3.0cm} L{2.4cm} L{4.2cm}}
\caption{The partzufim, the divine faces of the rectified world.}\\
\toprule
\textbf{Partzuf} & \textbf{Meaning} & \textbf{Sefirah source} & \textbf{Role} \\
\midrule
Atik Yomin & Ancient of Days & Inner of Keter & The innermost, most concealed face. The link to the world above. \\
Arikh Anpin & Long Face, the Patient One & Outer of Keter & The highest revealed face, pure mercy, the macroprosopus. \\
Abba & Father & Chokmah & The masculine seed of wisdom, the higher father. \\
Imma & Mother & Binah & The feminine womb of understanding, the higher mother. \\
Zeir Anpin & Small Face & The six emotions (Chesed to Yesod) & The microprosopus, the emotional son. \\
Nukva & The Female & Malkhut & The bride, the receiving daughter, the Shekhinah. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

The faces pair up. \textbf{Abba} with \textbf{Imma}, father with mother. \textbf{Zeir Anpin} with
\textbf{Nukva}, the emotional son with the bride. The \textbf{union} (zivug) of these pairs is the inner
engine of the ongoing repair. Balanced give-and-take replaces the isolated grabbing of Tohu.

\subsection{Mochin, the Brains}

The \textbf{Small Face} (Zeir Anpin) begins immature. This state is \textbf{small consciousness}
(katnut). It matures by receiving \textbf{brains} (mochin) from Abba and Imma.

\begin{itemize}
 \item The \textbf{brains} (mochin) are the higher intellectual lights from Father and Mother that enter
 Zeir Anpin and give it \textbf{mature or expanded consciousness} (gadlut).
 \item They come as the lights of Chokmah, Binah, and the two halves of Da'at.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Tikkunim of the Beard}

The faces are described anthropically in the Idra Rabba and Idra Zuta of the Zohar, with skull,
forehead, eyes, nose, and beard. The beards carry numbered rectifications, the \textbf{tikkunim}.

\begin{itemize}
 \item The beard of \textbf{Arikh Anpin} has \textbf{13 tikkunim}, the 13 locks. They correspond to the
 13 Attributes of Mercy of Exodus 34. This beard is \textbf{all mercy}, with no judgment mixed in.
 \item The beard of \textbf{Zeir Anpin} has \textbf{9 tikkunim}. Because Zeir Anpin contains judgment,
 its beard has fewer portions. It is \textbf{mercy mixed with judgment}.
 \item The 9 of Zeir Anpin are the channels by which the 13 of Arikh Anpin are received and sweetened
 into the lower world.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Lurianic and Zoharic Models Contrasted}

The reorganization into partzufim belongs to the \textbf{Lurianic} system. The earlier classical
Kabbalah of the \textbf{Zohar} and Cordovero tells a different creation story, with no contraction and
no shattering. The two are contrasted below.

\begin{longtable}{L{3.2cm} L{4.4cm} L{4.4cm}}
\caption{The continuous Zoharic emanation against the Lurianic account of break and repair.}\\
\toprule
\textbf{Feature} & \textbf{Zoharic / Cordoveran} & \textbf{Lurianic} \\
\midrule
First act & Emanation, overflow & Tzimtzum, contraction \\
Is there a void & No & Yes, the chalal \\
Shape of creation & Continuous cascade & Staged process with a break \\
Origin of evil & Imbalanced Gevurah, severity without mercy & Shattering of the vessels, fallen shards \\
Is there a catastrophe & No & Yes, the shattering \\
Human role & Devotion and alignment & Tikkun, actively raising trapped sparks \\
Final structure & The Tree of ten sefirot & The Tree reorganized into paired faces \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

Both keep the double register of the ultimate. Ein Sof is the unknowable infinite, and the ten sefirot
are the knowable structure. The Lurianic system adds the withdrawal, the break, and the repair on top of
that shared foundation. The Zoharic system keeps the emanation unbroken.
