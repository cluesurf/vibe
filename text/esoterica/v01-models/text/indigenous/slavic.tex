\section{Slavic}

The \textbf{Slavic} tradition is reconstructed and partly contested. The pre-Christian Slavs left no texts of their own, and their mythology was recorded only later by Christian chroniclers, foreign observers, and modern folklorists. Some figures are attested thinly or through a single source, and the popular dualist scheme owes much to later romantic reconstruction. The account below gathers the best-attested strands and the widespread folk earth-diver tale.

\subsection{The Creation Model}

At the root some tellings place a single generative source, the principle of kinship and birth, the undivided origin from which all gods, families, and things flow. At the foundation a dual pair is often set, the white god of light, order, and fortune and the black god of darkness, disorder, and misfortune, whose contrast structures the world.

The making itself follows the earth-diver pattern. In the beginning there is only the primordial water and a creating power, accompanied by a dividing counter-power. The counter-power dives to the depths and returns with a handful of primal substance. The creating power scatters this on the waters and it grows into the ordered land. But the counter-power withholds part of the substance by deceit, and this hidden portion swells and is cast out to become the rough, broken places. So the smooth and the jagged world both arise, order and its flaw born of the same act. Over the finished world rises an axis tree joining three realms, roots in the depths, trunk in the living world, crown in the heavens.

\subsection{Key Motifs}

The primal generative source is \textbf{Rod}, the undivided origin whose name means kin and birth, attested thinly. The dual pair is \textbf{Belobog}, the White God of light and order, and \textbf{Chernobog}, the Black God of darkness, the better-attested of the two. The diver who brings up the first mud is a bird or the devil. Over the world rises the \textbf{world tree}, often an oak. Across sky and deep the thunder god \textbf{Perun} and the underworld god \textbf{Veles} contend forever.

The Slavic account fits the shared pattern of the one going out into the many, since a single source and a single dive of substance unfold into the ordered land, the broken terrain, and the three joined realms of the world tree.
