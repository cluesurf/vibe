\section{Finnish}

The \textbf{Finnish} creation account is told in the opening runes of the \textbf{Kalevala}, the national epic compiled by Elias Lonnrot in the nineteenth century from oral Finnic and Karelian poetry. It belongs to the wider northern Eurasian family of cosmic-egg and earth-diver myths.

\subsection{The Creation Model}

In the beginning there is only the medium of air and the boundless primordial waters, the two undifferentiated elements. A feminine spirit of the air descends and floats upon the deep. Stirred by wind and wave, she becomes the carrier of the unborn world, pregnant but unable to bring it forth, suspended for ages on the formless sea.

A bird, the agent of making, seeks a place to nest. The floating spirit raises a single point above the water, the first locus of form, and on it the bird lays its eggs, the concentrated seeds of the cosmos. Heat builds, the point recoils, and the eggs shatter. From the broken shell the world differentiates by parts. One half becomes the lower realm of earth, the other the upper vault of sky. The bright center becomes the sun, the pale becomes the moon, the scattered flecks become the stars, and the dark becomes the clouds. The air-spirit then shapes the raw earth, drawing out coast and depth, and at last brings forth the first being, an aged singer who carries speech, song, and ordering power into the world. Creation is completed by the word.

\subsection{Key Motifs}

The floating spirit is \textbf{Ilmatar}, daughter of the air, also called \textbf{Luonnotar} and the \textbf{Water-Mother}. The world is made from the fragments of the cosmic eggs laid by a waterfowl, in the \textbf{Kalevala} a \textbf{scaup}. The first being is \textbf{Vainamoinen}, the aged singer and shaman whose power works through song, and the sowing spirit \textbf{Sampsa Pellervoinen} clothes the bare ground in greenery.

The Finnish account fits the shared pattern of the one going out into the many, since a single floating spirit and a single seed-form break apart into the many separated realms of the ordered world.
