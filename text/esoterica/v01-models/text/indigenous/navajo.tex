\section{Navajo}

The Navajo, who call themselves the Dine, are an Indigenous people of the southwestern United States. Their cosmology is an emergence story. The first beings rise through a series of underworlds into the present world, which is then made safe and ordered by sacred beings and a pair of heroes.

\subsection{The Creation Model}

In the primordial darkness the first beings lived in the lowest of several underworlds. As they climbed from one world up into the next, they changed and learned the proper ways of living, each world marking a different stage and color. A trickster figure went with them, whose disruptive acts caused trouble but also shaped each world along the way.

When they reached the present world it was barren. One of the sacred beings set the stars in the sky, and others established the order and the rituals of the new world. Then a renewing female power formed the first clans from her own skin and breathed life into them, teaching them to live in harmony with the land. The world was still troubled by monsters. A pair of twin brothers, the sons of the renewing power and the sun, set out with guidance from a wise weaving figure and defeated the monsters one by one, making the world safe. The animating wind was then breathed into all living things, giving them life, thought, and the power to speak.

\subsection{Key Motifs}

The renewing female power is \textbf{Changing Woman} (Asdz\'a\'a N\'adleeh\'e). The first beings are \textbf{First Man} and \textbf{First Woman}. The sacred beings, the Holy People, include \textbf{Talking God}, \textbf{Calling God}, and \textbf{Black God}, who set the stars. The hero twins are \textbf{Monster Slayer} and \textbf{Born For Water}, fathered by \textbf{Sun Bearer}. The weaving guide is \textbf{Spider Woman}. The trickster is \textbf{Coyote}. The animating wind is \textbf{Nilch'i}.

This collection notes that the Navajo account is the one going out into the many through emergence up the layered worlds, with the present world made whole when the renewing power forms the clans and the twins clear away the monsters.
