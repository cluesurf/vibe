\section{Zuni}

The Zuni are an Indigenous Pueblo people of western New Mexico. Their cosmology is an emergence story in which humanity rises through layered wombs of the earth toward the surface, led by twin guides, and then searches for the destined center of the world. The fullest recorded account comes from the late nineteenth-century work of Frank Cushing.

\subsection{The Creation Model}

In the beginning there was only the all-containing maker, who shaped himself into the sun. From the primordial mist came an earth mother and a sky father, and together they conceived all life in the fourfold womb of the world. The first living things were unfinished, webbed and scaled and dwelling in the dark deep beneath the surface.

To bring them up, the sun father produced twin sons, who carried sacred knowledge and weapons. The twins led the people up out of the lowest cave-womb, through one layered world after another, each lighter than the last, until they came out at last into the daylight of the surface. There the people were gradually made whole, losing their scales and webbing and taking their finished form. They then set out to find the middle place, the destined center of the world where they were meant to settle. They divided into groups and searched, and during a river crossing some of the children were transformed into water creatures. After long wandering the people came at last to rest at the center.

\subsection{Key Motifs}

The supreme maker is \textbf{Awonawilona}, who becomes the sun father \textbf{Yatoka}. The earth mother is \textbf{Awitelin Tsita} and the sky father is \textbf{Apoyan Ta'chu}. The twin guides, the Beloved Twins or war gods, are \textbf{Ko'wituma} and \textbf{Wats'usi}, together the \textbf{Ahayuta}. The four layered underworlds are named \textbf{Anosin Tehuli}, \textbf{K'olin Tehuli} (the moss world), \textbf{Awisho Tehuli} (the world of mud), and \textbf{Tepahaian Tehuli} (the wing world). The sought-for center is \textbf{Itiwana}, the middle place.

This collection notes that the Zuni account is the one going out into the many through emergence up the fourfold wombs of the earth, and a returning to a fixed center when the people complete their search for the middle place.
