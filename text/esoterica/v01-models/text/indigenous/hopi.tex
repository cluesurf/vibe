\section{Hopi}

The Hopi are an Indigenous Pueblo people of northeastern Arizona. Their cosmology is a story of successive worlds. Humanity passes upward through a series of earlier worlds, each destroyed when its people fall into disharmony, and emerges at last into the present world, where it learns to live and begins its migrations.

\subsection{The Creation Model}

In the boundless void the ultimate creator first conceived the universe. He sent an executive sky power to give the worlds form, and that power, aided by a wise nurturing female figure, molded the first beings from the earth and brought them to life in the first world.

The people of the first world failed to live in harmony, so it was destroyed and the worthy ones were led up to a second world, and again to a third when its people too fell out of order. Each time the nurturing female figure served as guide, helping the people find the way upward. At last they emerged into the fourth world, the present one. There they found its guardian and first inhabitant, the keeper of death and the earth, who taught them how to live on the land, to respect its cycles, and to plant corn. Spirit beings representing the ancestors and the natural powers then came to visit, bringing rain and teaching through ceremony. The people divided and set out on long migrations across the land, with one of a pair of brothers journeying away under a promise to return.

\subsection{Key Motifs}

The ultimate creator is \textbf{Taiowa} (Tawa), the sun spirit. The executive sky power is \textbf{Sotuknang}. The wise nurturing guide is \textbf{Spider Grandmother} (Kokyangwuti). The guardian of the present world is \textbf{Masauwu} (Maasaw), keeper of death, earth, and fire. The visiting spirit beings are the \textbf{Katsinam}. The pair of guiding brothers are the \textbf{Two Brothers}, one of whom, Pahana, departs eastward under a promise of return. The trickster is \textbf{Coyote}.

This collection notes that the Hopi account is the one going out into the many through a succession of worlds and a final emergence, with the people set on migrations and a promised return that points the order back toward its source.
