\chapter{Eighteen Indigenous and Less-Documented Cosmologies}

This chapter gathers eighteen traditions whose cosmologies are recorded in less detail than the major scriptural systems, or whose written record was assembled later by outside hands. Each tradition is treated briefly, with a single creation model and a few distinctive features, so that the global reach of the same recurring patterns can be seen at a glance.

The sources here are often fragmentary, orally transmitted, or mediated through later recorders. They are treated with care. The accounts below report the broad shape of each story rather than any single authoritative text. Where a tradition preserves a clean numbered scheme, it is set out plainly. Where the record is uncertain, the account stays general.
