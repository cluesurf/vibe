\section{Inuit}

The Inuit are the peoples of the Arctic, spread across Greenland, the Canadian north, and Alaska. Their cosmology is shaped by the sea, the ice, and the long dark. It has no single creation epic that sets out the whole. Instead it holds a ruling life-principle of air and breath, a sea-mother who controls the food of the people, and a class of specialists who travel in spirit between the human world and the world of the powers.

\subsection{The Creation Model}

The ordering principle of the world is the breath and the air. It is the invisible force that fills everything, governs the weather and the winds, and gives each living thing the breath of its life. It is at once the air outside and the breath inside, and it holds the moral order as well, so that breaking the rules of right living disturbs it and the weather turns harsh.

The food of the sea comes from a woman who lives at the bottom of the ocean. In the story she was thrown into the water and her fingers were cut away as she clung to the boat, and from the severed fingers came the seals, the walruses, and the whales. She rules the underworld of the sea and holds the animals. When people break the rules, she grows angry, tangles her hair, and keeps the animals down, and then the people starve until a spirit-specialist travels to her, calms her, and combs her hair so she releases the animals again.

\subsection{Key Motifs}

The ruling principle of breath, air, and weather is \textbf{Sila}. The sea-woman and mother of sea creatures is \textbf{Sedna}, and her sea-floor underworld is \textbf{Adlivun}. The creator and trickster who takes the form of a bird, brings light, and stirs the storms is the raven \textbf{Tulugaq}. The sun and the moon are siblings who fled each other into the sky. The spirit-specialist who mediates with the powers and journeys to Sedna is the \textbf{angakkuq}.

This tradition states the shared pattern as an order that goes out from a single breath that fills the world, with the return enacted again and again in the journey of the spirit-specialist down to the sea-mother to set right the broken order and release the animals back to the people.
