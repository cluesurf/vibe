\subsection{Kabbalah}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.30\linewidth} L{0.62\linewidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
\midrule
\endhead
Ein Sof & The Without End, the unknowable attributeless Infinite, the divine essence beyond all name \\
Or Ein Sof & The infinite light of Ein Sof that filled all reality before creation \\
tzimtzum & The contraction, Ein Sof withdrawing its light to open a void for creation \\
chalal / tehiru & The empty vacated space left by the tzimtzum \\
reshimu & The faint residue or trace of light remaining in the void after the withdrawal \\
kav & The line or ray, the thin measured beam of light that enters the void and builds structure \\
Adam Kadmon & The Primordial Man, the first and highest configuration, the archetypal blueprint of all worlds \\
sefirah (pl. sefirot) & One of the ten divine emanations, attributes and channels of divine light \\
Keter & The Crown, the first sefirah, the superconscious will \\
Malkhut & Kingship, the lowest sefirah, the receiving vessel and indwelling presence \\
Olam ha-Tohu & The World of Chaos, abundant light in weak unbalanced vessels \\
Olam ha-Tikun & The World of Repair, lower lights in strong interconnected vessels \\
shevirat ha-kelim & The shattering or breaking of the vessels, the primordial catastrophe \\
qelippah (pl. qelippot) & Shell or husk, the impure shards of the broken vessels that conceal holiness \\
Sitra Achra & The Other Side, the whole domain of impurity, the dark counter-tree \\
nitzotz (pl. netzotzot) & The holy spark of light trapped inside each husk after the shattering \\
tikkun & Repair or rectification, the gathering of the lights and the raising of the trapped sparks \\
tikkun olam & The repair of the world, humanity's active mending of the cosmic break \\
kavanah & The focused intention that gives a deed or prayer its spark-raising power \\
Atzilut & Emanation, the highest of the four worlds, the sefirot one with Ein Sof \\
Beriah & Creation, the throne world, the seat of mind \\
Yetzirah & Formation, the world of angels and feeling \\
Assiah & Action or Making, the lowest world, including the physical cosmos \\
partzuf (pl. partzufim) & Face or configuration, a sefirot grouping reformed as a full persona after the shattering \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Buddhism}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.30\linewidth} L{0.62\linewidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
\midrule
\endhead
anadi & Beginningless, the lack of any first moment or first cause to the cosmos \\
shunyata & Emptiness, the lack of inherent or independent existence in all things \\
rigpa & Pure awareness, the knowing nature of mind recognized for what it is \\
gzhi & The Ground, the primordially pure and spontaneously present base of mind in Dzogchen \\
buddha-nature & The awakened potential present in every being \\
avidya & Ignorance, the failure of mind to recognize its own empty and luminous nature \\
skandha & Aggregate, one of the five heaps (form, feeling, perception, formations, consciousness) that a person reduces to \\
anatman & No-self, the absence of any permanent self among the aggregates \\
karma & Action, intentional deeds that plant seeds ripening later as experience \\
cetana & Intention, the volitional factor that defines an act as karma \\
alaya & The storehouse consciousness, the mindstream that carries karmic seeds across lives \\
nidana & One of the twelve links in the chain of dependent arising \\
pratityasamutpada & Dependent arising, the lawful chain of causes by which all phenomena come to be \\
samsara & Cyclic existence, the beginningless round of birth, death, and rebirth driven by karma \\
nirvana & Liberation, the blowing out of craving, aversion, and delusion \\
kalpa & An immense cosmic cycle of formation, duration, destruction, and emptiness \\
trailokya & The three realms (desire, form, formless) into which beings are sorted \\
bhavachakra & The wheel of life, the round of the six realms turned by the three poisons \\
Mount Meru & The central mountain of a single world-system in Buddhist cosmology \\
bodhicitta & The awakening mind, the aspiration to attain buddhahood for the sake of all beings \\
karuna & Compassion, the wish that beings be free of suffering \\
bodhi & Awakening, the fruition in which mind recognizes its own nature \\
buddha & One who is fully awake, having cleared the obscurations and realized the nature of mind \\
dharmakaya & The truth body, the empty and omniscient nature of a buddha's mind \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
