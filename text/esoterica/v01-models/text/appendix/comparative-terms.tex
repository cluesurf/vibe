This table sets one plain-English concept on the left and gives the nearest term in each of the six richest
living esoteric traditions. It is a quick way to see how widely a single inner reality is named, and where a
tradition has no close single word for it. An entry of \enquote{-} means there is no clean one-term
equivalent, not that the tradition lacks the idea entirely. Terms are romanized. Fuller per-tradition
mappings, with native script where it helps, follow in the next section.

The pattern is visible at a glance. A few concepts appear in every column, the unknowable source, the divine
names, liberation, the path, the teacher, and grace. Others are nearly unique to one tradition, such as the
contraction in Kabbalah. The blanks are as informative as the entries, since they show where a tradition
folds an idea into another or declines it on principle, as Buddhism declines a personal lord and a soul.
