\begin{abstract}
Every number in the base of this model is computed from its candidate space, not chosen. This
paper walks the chain from nothing to the growing mesh, giving at each step the move, the
reason it is forced, and the experiment that runs it. The numbers $3$, $8$, $24$, $81$,
$10395 \to 1$, and the growth ratio near $18.278$ are recovered by exhaustive enumeration
rather than asserted. The account is split into two registers: a timeless derivation that
fixes the equipment (the tone, the cell, the mesh shape, the laws) and a running dynamics
(the beat) that computes on it. Every claim is backed by a reproducible experiment in the
source repository \cite{cluesurf2026repo}, linked inline.
\end{abstract}

\section{The two registers}

\textbf{Register one, the derivation.} A single logical chain, timeless, not events. It fixes
the equipment: the tone, the cell, the mesh shape, the laws. Sequential, not parallel.

\textbf{Register two, the running.} Each beat, three things happen at once: time advances by
one, the knit updates every cell in parallel, the wake births a shell at the edge. This is the
only parallelism.

\section{Immediate versus computed}

Two things get called ``computation'', keep them apart.

\textbf{Immediate (register one): true by mathematical necessity, no time, no beat.} These do
not happen, they hold. They are theorems about finite structures: given the premises they could
not be otherwise, and they are the same whether or not any universe runs. The seed (nothing
cannot be) is not even a computation, it is a reflection. The tone is $3$, the arrow is the
ordered line, the dimension is $8$, the census is $1{+}8{+}24{+}32{+}16$, the $24$-cell is the
unique self-dual spinful shell, the mesh shape is $\{3,4,3,4\}$, the law is the unique symmetric
knit, and the shell sequence $1, 24, 456, 8376$ is determined. When our experiments enumerate
for these they are checking a timeless fact by finite search, not running the universe.

\textbf{Computed (register two): the beat, the only thing that actually runs.} It takes beats,
it is the dynamics, and it produces nothing without ticking: the knit updating the tones, the
wake birthing each next shell, the beat count rising. The one-line test: would it still be true
with no universe running. Yes means immediate, no means the beat.

\subsection{How each step forces the next}

\textbf{nothing $\to$ one distinction.} Nothing cannot be, because ``there is nothing'' is
already one way things are rather than another, which is itself a distinction. So the denial of
all content produces content, and the least it produces is a single distinction. This is the
seed, a reflection, not a computation.

\textbf{one distinction $\to$ the ternary tone.} A distinction is a cut, and to be a cut it
needs both of its sides and the state where the cut is absent. The two sides are mirror images
(each is only ``not the other'', so there is an operation swapping them, a sign flip $+1$ and
$-1$), and there must be the neutral no-cut state (the vacuum $0$), because a site has to be able
to hold ``no distinction here'' as a value for distinctions to appear and vanish at all. Neither
side may be privileged and the absent state must be representable, so the smallest value set is
$\{-1,0,+1\}$. One distinction and three-toned are the same object.

\textbf{the tone $\to$ the arrow.} Nothing prevents a second distinction after the first, so
distinctions accumulate, and an accumulation has a running size that only ever grows, a one-way
count, which is a before and an after: time. A count needs a number system that stays ordered,
order requires every square to be at least zero, and that holds on the integer line but breaks
at the first imaginary unit ($i^2 = -1$), so the ordered count lives on the integers alone.

\textbf{the tone $\to$ dimension eight.} A structure is not a heap of independent values, the
tones must relate, and to relate two tones is to form a third from them, a product. The base
loses no information, so the product must be undoable (divide back out), which makes it a
division algebra, and division algebras exist only at dimensions $1,2,4,8$ (Hurwitz). The ceiling:
a \emph{zero divisor} is two nonzero values with product exactly zero, $A B = 0$ with neither
zero, which in ordinary numbers never happens; where it does, the product $0$ no longer determines
its factors, so information is lost and the multiplication is irreversible. Through dimension $8$
(the octonions) there are no zero divisors, and at dimension $16$ (the sedenions) they first
appear, so $8$ is the last lossless rung. The floor is a theorem too: the order-three triality
that ties three generations together exists at exactly one rank of the $D$ family, $D_4$, whose
vector dimension is $8$, and nowhere below, so no smaller lossless rung can carry the three
generations. Ceiling and floor land on the top of the reversible ladder, dimension $8$, both
computed.

\textbf{eight $\to$ four slots.} Space is where positions and rotations compose, and composition
in a geometry must associate: stepping by $a$, then $b$, then $c$ must give the same place under
either grouping, or position is not well defined. The octonions do not associate (the luxury shed
at dimension $8$), so the $8$D octonion cannot be space. Take the largest part that does associate:
walking $\mathbb{R}(1)$, $\mathbb{C}(2)$, $\mathbb{H}(4)$, $\mathbb{O}(8)$, associativity holds
through the quaternions and first fails at the octonions, so the quaternions, dimension $4$, are
the largest associative division algebra, the biggest consistent geometric arena. Space is that
core, four axes, and the tone is written across four slots, each $-1$, $0$, or $+1$.

\textbf{four slots $\to$ the 24-cell.} A nearest neighbour is one small move, and a small move
sets each of the four slots to $-1$, $0$, or $+1$, so $3^4 = 81$ words, sorted by how many slots
step: $1{+}8{+}24{+}32{+}16 = 81$. A cell tiles space by matching each face to a neighbour, so it
needs one direction per face, corners equal to faces (self-dual), and of the stepping shells only
the $24$ two-step diagonals give that ($24$ corners, $24$ faces); the $8$ give the $16$-cell
($8$, $16$) and the $16$ give the tesseract ($16$, $8$). Spin then confirms the winner: the $24$
vertices as unit quaternions carry the belt-trick sign, containing $-1$ with $(-1)^2 = +1$, a full
turn flipping the sign, while the tesseract's $16$ half-integer units are not a group and carry no
such element. Self-duality selects, spin confirms, so the cell is the $24$-cell.

\textbf{the 24-cell $\to$ the mesh.} One cell is not space, so copy the cell face to face. The
$24$-cell tiles in exactly two regular ways: three cells around a face closes flat (the honeycomb
$\{3,4,3,3\}$, the dihedral angle being $120$ degrees), four around a face opens into negative
curvature, the honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$. The base takes the hyperbolic one, a premise with a
reason: it is the arena with exponential room and a boundary, which the accumulation (the arrow)
needs, with the flat honeycomb the standing control.

\textbf{the mesh $\to$ the law.} A mesh of tones is inert without an update. The rule must be
local (no action at a distance), reversible (lose nothing), and symmetric (no direction
privileged). The $24$ directions form $12$ opposite lines, a law pairs them into $6$ colliding
pairs, $11!! = 10395$ ways, and the three demands cut all but one, the knit (collide then stream).

\textbf{the law $\to$ the wake.} The arrow was already implied, but the knit is reversible and so
carries no direction. Something must carry the accumulation, so the mesh grows, new cells born at
the open edge and none removed. That monotone growth is the wake, and it is why the clock runs
forward.

\textbf{collide $\to$ stream.} A rule that only collided would let tones interact but never move,
a frozen soup, and a rule that only streamed would let tones fly past but never interact, a free
gas, so the knit is both: collide (local, the symmetric table on opposite lines, conserving) then
stream (every tone steps one cell), synchronously across all cells.

\textbf{beat $\to$ beat.} A beat is a single tick defined as the things that happen in it, so
they are faces of one event: the count rises, the knit rewrites every cell, the wake births a
shell. The next beat runs the same knit on the now-larger mesh with the now-advanced state.

\textbf{knit $\to$ arrow.} The knit is a reversible shuffle, symmetric in time. The wake breaks
the symmetry: the cell count only rises, so to run a beat backward you would have to un-create a
cell, itself a new distinction. The arrow is the un-erasable growth, not the knit.

\textbf{bulk $\to$ cusp.} The growing $4$D interior (the bulk, curved) has a $3$D boundary (the
cusp, flat), because the boundary of a $4$D region is one dimension down. Stable structure and
observers form on the flat cusp, so three of the four dimensions are in view and the radial
fourth is hidden. Perceived space is $3$D and perceived time is the bulk's growth read from the
cusp.

\section{The exact numbers}

\textbf{Register one.} Minimal vacuum-and-mirror alphabet size $3$ (drop the mirror, it falls to
$1$). Census $1{+}8{+}24{+}32{+}16 = 81$. Self-dual: $1$ of $3$ stepping shells. Spin: the
$24$-vertex binary tetrahedral group contains $-1$ with $(-1)^2 = +1$ and $-1 \neq +1$. Pinch:
reversible through dimension $8$, zero divisors at $16$; octonion non-associative triples $28$ of
$35$. Law: $10395 \to 1$ under symmetry.

\textbf{Register two.} Unfolding $1, 24, 456, 8376$ cells per shell from the single $24$-cell by
reflection, bit-for-bit reproducible, per-shell ratio climbing to the warp factor $18.278$, the
flat $4$D lattice ratio near $1.30$ (control). Beat: charge conserved as integer equality over
ten beats, forward ten then backward ten recovers the start exactly, an erasing rule fails both.

\section{The premises it rests on}

The math forces the structure. What it does not force, and the derivation takes as premises: the
vacuum-and-mirror requirement, reversibility (``lose nothing'', the seed itself read as a
condition on change, since merging two distinct states would erase their difference), that the
base carries three generations (which is what makes the triality floor at eight bite), the
associativity of geometric composition, a face per neighbour, the hyperbolic choice among the two
regular $24$-cell honeycombs (flat $\{3,4,3,3\}$ versus hyperbolic $\{3,4,3,4\}$, taken for the
exponential room and the boundary), and the reading of the signed ternary tone as valence (pain,
peace, pleasure). Those are the premises, stated up front, not smuggled in.

\section*{Summary}

Nothing forces one distinction, and one distinction is three-toned (vacuum and mirror). The tone
combines losslessly up to the octonions, whose associative core is $4$D space. The tone across
four slots gives $81$ words whose unique self-dual spinful shell is the $24$-cell, which tiles
curved space into the mesh $\{3,4,3,4\}$. The one symmetric law (the knit) runs on it each beat,
reversibly, while the wake grows the edge, and that growth is time.
