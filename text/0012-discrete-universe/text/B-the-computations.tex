\section{The Computations}

Every claim in this paper is checked by a reproducible computation on an explicit object. This appendix indexes them by the section they support, naming the object computed and the result confirmed. The full suite is a finite, deterministic, runnable battery. Across it the overwhelming majority of results are reproductions and confirmations, a smaller marked set are genuinely novel measured results, and a named handful are negatives or open frontiers. The suite fails the build only on a code crash or a broken internal consistency check, never on a scientific negative.

\subsection*{The first distinction and the arrow}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Minimal alphabet.} Enumerate small value sets, test each for a vacuum and a nontrivial sign flip. Confirms the ternary set is the unique smallest admissible alphabet, that the two-symbol sets both fail.
\item \textbf{Orderability.} Test each rung of the tower for a total order compatible with the ring operations. Confirms only the integers admit one, every higher rung carrying a square root of minus one fails.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The pinch-point}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Composition fails at the sedenions.} Build the doubling product through dimension sixteen, search for zero divisors. Confirms the norm multiplies through dimension eight and fails at sixteen, with the explicit zero divisor of the tower appendix.
\item \textbf{Triality is unique.} Compute the diagram automorphism group of the rank-$n$ rotation algebras. Confirms order six with an order-three element at rank four, and only an order-two flip elsewhere.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Spacetime and the cell}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The 24-cell.} Build the $D_4$ root system, confirm twenty-four roots, self-duality, and rank four.
\item \textbf{The directions are a group.} Confirm the twenty-four directions form the binary tetrahedral group.
\item \textbf{The spinor discriminator.} Confirm the cell's twenty-four directions carry a spinor while the three-dimensional sibling's twelve do not, the selection that forces the substrate.
\item \textbf{Two threes differ.} Compare the symmetry of the ternary value with that of the generations. Confirms the groups differ in order, two against six.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The fermions}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Clifford algebra.} Form the seven left-multiplication matrices from the octonion table. Confirms each squares to minus the identity and they anticommute across all forty-nine products.
\item \textbf{Ladder operators.} Build the three creation operators. Confirms the fermionic anticommutators.
\item \textbf{Spectrum and charges.} Compute the number operator. Confirms spectrum zero through three, multiplicities one, three, three, one, and charges zero, one third, two thirds, one.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Forces and the Higgs}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Gauge dimensions.} Confirms the circle gives the first factor, the unit quaternions the second, and the unit-fixing subgroup of the fourteen-dimensional exceptional group gives the third through fourteen minus six equals eight.
\item \textbf{The weak angle.} Feed in only the two measured couplings, demand one unification scale, output the weak mixing angle. Returns three eighths at unification, running to about 0.231 with supersymmetric-like content and about 0.207 with bare running, with a wrong-charge control predicting a clearly wrong angle.
\item \textbf{Custodial symmetry.} Evaluate the mass ratio parameter for the doublet and the triplet. Confirms one for the doublet, one half for the triplet.
\item \textbf{Anomalies.} Sum the six gauge anomalies plus the global one for one generation. Confirms every sum vanishes.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The law}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Forced rule.} Count the symmetric reversible pairings of the twenty-four directions. Confirms the ten thousand three hundred ninety-five candidates collapse to a single orbit, one rule.
\item \textbf{Reversibility and CPT.} Run the rule backward to the exact start, and apply charge, parity, and time reversal in simulation. Confirms invertibility and combined invariance.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Mass and mixing}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Warp factor.} Stream the shell counts of the substrate outward. Confirms the growth ratio near 18.28 and the throat scale near its square root.
\item \textbf{Hierarchy shape.} Confirms the geometric depth spacing beats a power law, and that the deterministic growth does not secretly fix the depths, the negative that makes the depths genuinely free.
\item \textbf{Cabibbo angle.} Confirms the square root of the down-to-strange ratio reproduces the measured angle to a percent.
\item \textbf{The flavor group.} Quotient the cell's direction group by its center. Confirms the unique alternating group on four letters, and that the order-twenty-four alternatives fail, forcing the trimaximal pattern and the solar angle squared sine of 0.341.
\item \textbf{Quark CP.} Confirms a generic nonzero phase with a Jarlskog measure of the right size, emergent from the CP-symmetric base through the triality phase, with the strong angle held at zero.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The emergent laws}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Lorentz invariance.} Read the isotropy of the dispersion and the self-averaging of anisotropy with scale, with a flat-lattice gamma-ray-burst control.
\item \textbf{Emergent dimension.} Read the cusp as a flat three-dimensional sheet of polynomial growth inside an exponential four-dimensional bulk, with the sibling as a dimension-down control.
\item \textbf{Gravity.} Recover the one-over-r potential by receding the box boundary and extrapolating, derive the two graviton polarizations through the curvature pipeline, recover the wave operator from the causal order as density grows, and compute the one-over-r-squared boundary correlator on the tree, validated against a finite tree.
\item \textbf{The quantum.} Drive a product state through the exchange interaction to a maximally entangled state, reaching the Tsirelson bound, with a product-state control, and measure ballistic quantum-walk spreading against a diffusive classical control.
\item \textbf{Holography.} Confirm the ground state is area-law and a thermal state volume-law, the boundary entanglement logarithmic where a flat control is linear, and the horizon entropy tracking area not volume.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Cosmology and the dark sector}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Three dimensions.} Confirm only three spatial dimensions give stable closed orbits.
\item \textbf{Dark energy.} Confirm the deterministic growth gives constant expansion, with a static control.
\item \textbf{Dark matter and binding.} Confirm the winding number is conserved and the knot persists, the attractive channel has a binding well at finite separation, the same-orientation control has none, and a charge-two configuration weighs about twice a charge-one one.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Computation}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Universality.} Confirm glider-like signals and collisions rich enough to realize logic on the reversible rule.
\item \textbf{Addressing and memory.} Confirm logarithmic navigation and neighbor-stepping on the curved mesh against a flat control, and that a spread-encoded bit survives a bounded erasure where a localized bit is destroyed.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The negatives, recorded as results}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Smooth spacetime does not dominate} under the bare causal-set action, the entropy problem. Manifold-likeness collapses at a phase transition as the coupling rises.
\item \textbf{The mass depths are free.} The integer-shell quantization of the depths returned residuals not below random.
\item \textbf{The bare coupling is free.} The rule has an exact scaling flat direction, so the absolute coupling is a modulus, the ratio and running fixed.
\item \textbf{Three generations is open.} The naive triality reading gives a vector plus two chiralities, the proven negative, leaving the identification with the three families the deepest open conjecture.
\end{itemize}

The discipline throughout is that a result is reported only as strongly as the computation supports it.
