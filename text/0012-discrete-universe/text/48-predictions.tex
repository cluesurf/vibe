\section{Predictions}

A theory earns its keep by being able to be wrong, and this one makes sharp, falsifiable commitments that the real
experiments of this decade will test. Each one below names exactly what would confirm or refute it, and by when.

The neutrino solar angle is the sharpest near-term test. The tetrahedral symmetry of the cell forces the trimaximal
mixing pattern, which fixes the solar mixing angle at a squared sine of 0.341, about two standard deviations above the
currently measured 0.307. JUNO measures this angle to half a percent by around 2028, so if it lands near 0.34 the
prediction is confirmed and if it locks at 0.30 it is a real tension. Maximal leptonic CP violation, the
matter-antimatter asymmetry of the lepton sector locked to maximal atmospheric mixing, is favored by current data with
large errors and will be measured this decade by the long-baseline experiments.

The dark energy equation of state is a constant minus one, the de Sitter value, not an evolving field, and the current
measurement of minus one point oh three favors this, while the recent hint of evolving dark energy is a live tension the
surveys of this decade will settle. Neutrinos are their own antiparticles, Majorana, so neutrinoless double beta decay
must occur, and the searches for it are a direct test. The mass ordering is normal, settled model-independently by JUNO
around 2030.

The structural commitments are equally sharp. There are exactly three generations, no fourth chiral family, already
favored by the invisible width of the Z and the Higgs rate, and a discovered fourth family with its own light neutrino
would break the construction at its root. The gauge group is a product of three algebras rather than a single
grand-unified embedding on one reading, so the dimension-six proton decay of grand unification is not predicted, and a
definite observation of proton decay in the classic channels would favor the other reading. The Higgs is a single doublet
with the mass relation forced to one, so a robust deviation from it, or a second fundamental scalar doublet, would be a
problem. And there is no large physical extra dimension, the warping being internal. Each of these is a place the theory
could be shown wrong this decade.
