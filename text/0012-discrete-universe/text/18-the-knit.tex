\section{The Knit}

One rule runs on the mesh, the same everywhere and every beat, and it has two steps. First, collide: inside each
line, the two tones meeting head-on rearrange by a fixed small permutation. Then, stream: every site hands its tone one
dock along its direction, to the matching site of the neighbor. Collide, then stream. That is the knit, the whole law of
motion, and it carries no adjustable parts.

A tone is a value held at a site, not a thing that travels. To stream is to copy that value one slot onward, to the
matching site of the next dock, while the sites themselves stay fixed. So when the paper says a tone moves, it means
just this, a value passed from slot to slot, beat after beat. What it is for a self to feel itself carried through the
mesh is a higher relational structure built on top of this passing, and this paper does not take it up.

The knit is unique. Demand three things of a law: that it be local, looking only inside a dock, that it be reversible,
losing no information, and that it respect the full symmetry of the cell, the signed coordinate permutations. The
pairings of the twenty-four directions into colliding channels number ten thousand three hundred ninety-five, the double
factorial of eleven, and requiring invariance under the full cell symmetry collapses them to a single one. So the law is
not chosen, it is forced, the same way the substrate is.

Two properties of the knit carry weight. It is reversible: from the result of a beat you can always recover the state
before it, because the collision is a permutation and the streaming is a shift, both invertible, so no information is
ever destroyed. And it is exactly symmetric under the combined reversal of charge, parity, and time, by construction,
with no built-in local arrow. The local law runs the same forward and backward.

One gap remains. The bare single-charge rule, by itself, gives diffusive spreading rather than the
sharp light-cone propagation a massless particle needs, and getting that sharp mode requires a second conserved
quantity, a momentum current, alongside the charge. So the sharp propagating massless mode is a target the richer rule
reaches, not something the barest rule hands over alone, and the maximum signal speed is set by the free streaming, one
cell per beat, which interactions can only respect, never exceed.
