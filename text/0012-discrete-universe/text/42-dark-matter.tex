\section{Dark Matter}

Matter in this model can be more than the fundamental fermions, it can be a bound knot of the tone field, a soliton, and
the dark matter the galaxies need has a natural candidate among these knots. What follows is why such a knot is stable,
massive, and dark, and how two knots bind into the nuclei of ordinary matter.

A knot is a topological winding of the direction field, and it cannot be untied because its winding number is a conserved
whole number, the way a knot in a closed loop of string cannot vanish without cutting the string. So it is stable, it
carries a fixed rest energy, a mass, and it gravitates. This is matter as topology, persistent and massive by its winding
rather than by any added stabilizer. An experiment confirms the winding is conserved and the knot persists.

Two knots interact, and whether they bind depends on their relative internal orientation, the channel. Two same-charge
knots in the same orientation repel, like charges pushing apart, but two same-charge knots twisted by half a turn
relative to each other, the attractive channel, pull together into a bound pair at a finite separation, the analog of two
nucleons binding into a deuteron. An experiment measures exactly this: in the attractive channel the interaction energy
has a genuine well at a finite separation, below the energy of the two knots far apart, while in the same-orientation
channel there is no well, the control, and a charge-two configuration weighs about twice a charge-one one, additive
matter. So bound nuclei, additive in their topological charge, are realized as solitons of the tone field.

The dark-matter candidate is a knot that is also electrically neutral. It carries a discrete topological charge, a
winding under a discrete symmetry distinct from the continuous symmetry of electromagnetism, so it does not couple to the
photon, it is dark, while remaining stable by its winding and massive by its rest energy. Existence, stability, and
neutrality are structural, demonstrated. The mass and the cosmic abundance are free, a calculation of the relic abundance
is the open next step, and so the candidate is genuine but not yet a full solution. The long null result of the direct
searches for a dark-matter particle is the expected one on this reading, and a definite detection of a fundamental
dark-matter particle would count against it, a real and falsifiable stance.
