\section{The Pinch}

Two pressures squeeze the dimension to eight from opposite sides, and they were proved without ever naming the octonions.

The ceiling comes from above. Folding distinctions without losing information means a normed division algebra, the tower stops at the octonions by Hurwitz's theorem (a doubling keeps the norm only while its input is associative, and the octonions are the last associative-input rung), so the dimension is at most eight. This is the requirement of reversibility, the demand that combining differences waste nothing.

The floor comes from below, and it is the model's monism that forces it. There is only one substance, so the directions of space, the vector, and the matter that moves through them, the spinor, cannot be two separate kinds of thing. Being one substance they must have the same size, and for almost every dimension they do not. The exception is eight, where the rotation structure has three eight-dimensional pieces, a vector and two half-spinors, cycled by an order-three symmetry called triality, found nowhere else. As a size match: the vector has n components, the half-spinor two to the power of n over two minus one, and these are equal only at n equals eight, because eight is two cubed. So monism, vector equal to spinor, needs dimension at least eight.

Ceiling and floor meet. Reversibility presses down from above, monism presses up from below, and the dimension is caught at exactly eight, whose composition algebra is the octonions. These are the two load-bearing principles of the whole theory, one demanding that nothing be lost, the other that there be only one kind of stuff, and between them they leave a single algebra standing. The octonions are not chosen, not tuned, not preferred. They are squeezed into being, the maximal reversible algebra and the unique dimension where one substance is both geometry and matter at once, sitting one step from the cliff where folding stops being reversible. The world is built at the largest size where nothing is lost. Everything physical that follows is read off this one algebra.
