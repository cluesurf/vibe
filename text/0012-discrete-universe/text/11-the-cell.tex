\section{The Cell}

Write the tone in four slots, each slot a value in minus one, zero, plus one. There are eighty-one such words. Sort them by length and they fall into distinct shells, and the regular ones are regular four-dimensional shapes. The shell with one nonzero slot has eight points, the sixteen-cell. The shell with two nonzero slots has twenty-four points, the 24-cell. The shell with all four nonzero has sixteen points, the tesseract.

The cell of the mesh is the twenty-four-point shell, the 24-cell, and the reason it is this one and not another is forced. A direction is a way out through a face, to the neighbor on the other side, so the set of directions and the set of faces must be the same set, one direction per face. A shape whose corners match its faces one for one is self-dual, and among the shells of the tone alphabet exactly one is self-dual, the 24-cell, with twenty-four corners and twenty-four faces. The shell below it, the eight-cornered one, has a different shape for its faces, so its directions and faces would never line up. Only the 24-cell closes the loop. Count it to be sure: choose which two of the four slots are nonzero, six ways, and a sign for each, four ways, six times four is twenty-four.

The same twenty-four points arrive four independent ways, and four roads to one figure is how you know it is forced, not chosen. They are the root system of the rotation algebra $D_4$. They are the tone written in four slots, the geometry being the alphabet itself. They are the twenty-four whole-number quaternions of unit length, a finite group closed under multiplication, so composing two directions is exact arithmetic with no rounding. And they are the twenty-four spheres that can touch one in the densest four-dimensional packing, the kissing number.

That cell already carries spin. The twenty-four points are the binary tetrahedral group, the double cover of the rotation group of ordinary space, where a full turn does not return to the start, it acts as minus one, and only a double turn returns. This is the defining mark of a spinor, and here it is exact arithmetic in a finite group. So the tile that builds spacetime carries the handedness of matter before any matter is placed on it. They are the same object seen twice.
