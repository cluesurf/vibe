\section{The Octonions}

The octonions are eight-dimensional: one real unit and seven imaginary units, each squaring to minus one. The rule for how the seven multiply is captured by a small diagram, the Fano plane, seven points and seven lines, and the table is fully anti-symmetric off the diagonal, so swapping two units flips the sign, and it is non-associative, so the grouping of a triple product matters.

The symmetry of the octonions, the transformations that preserve all this multiplication, is the exceptional group $G_2$, of dimension fourteen. Hold one warning firmly. This sevenfold imaginary structure is internal, it is the arena the gauge forces will live in, and it is not physical space. Physical space comes from a different place, the rank of the rotation group, and the seven internal units must never be confused with the four dimensions of spacetime.

One fact makes the octonion the seed of matter. The octonions are non-associative, but the act of multiplying by an octonion is not, because a fixed multiplication is a linear map, and linear maps are matrices, and matrices always associate. Write down, for each of the seven imaginary units, the eight-by-eight matrix that means multiply on the left by this unit. Computed from the table, each squares to minus the identity and any two distinct ones anti-commute. Those are exactly the defining relations of a Clifford algebra on seven generators, seven anti-commuting square roots of minus one.

So the octonion's own internal multiplications form a spin algebra. This is the bridge to matter, because pairing those seven generators into ladder operators builds one full generation of particles. The octonion is not inert. It is already, secretly, spinorial, and the rest of the paper unfolds what is folded inside it.
