\section{Nothing}

Start with truly nothing. No space, no time, no number, no content.

The first move is not an event, because there is no time yet for an event to happen in. It is a logical fact about the blank state, and it is the one premise of the whole model. A complete account of reality is the list of distinctions it contains. The empty list contains no statement at all, not even the statement that there is nothing, because to assert emptiness is already to mark emptiness off against non-emptiness, which is a distinction. Put the same point as being rather than describing. To exist, to be anything rather than not, is already to stand apart from non-being, and that standing-apart is a distinction. Existence and distinction are the same act.

So there are exactly two possibilities. Either there is no content anywhere, ever, in which case there is nothing to explain and no one to ask, or there is content, and the least possible content is one distinction. Given that anything exists at all, one distinction is forced. Nothing is the single state the world cannot be in. There is no spark, no stirring, no random fluctuation, nothing that happens, which matters because the base is deterministic and a spontaneous flicker would be exactly the kind of randomness the model does not allow. The distinction is not an event. It is what existence already is.

That single forced distinction is the only thing put in. Everything in the rest of the paper is its consequence. Why there is something rather than nothing, why the one distinction is there to begin with, is the single question deliberately left outside the system, because no account answers the fact of its own existence without regress. It is accepted as the premise and marked as such.
