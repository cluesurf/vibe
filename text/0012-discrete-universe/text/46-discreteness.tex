\section{Discreteness}

The base is discrete at every scale of its own construction, and the smooth, continuous world is only the coarse view
from a distance. This is a strength, not an approximation, and it forbids one specific thing.

The continuum is emergent, not fundamental. A fine-enough discrete fabric looks continuous from far away, the way sand
looks like a smooth surface from across a beach, and the laws of the continuum, the Dirac and Maxwell equations, Lorentz
invariance, the smooth metric of gravity, all appear by coarse-graining the discrete rule, with the discreteness showing
up only at impossibly high order. The universe is not approaching smoothness over time, it is discrete all the way down,
and smoothness is a viewpoint, not a destination. This reverses the usual order of physics, which takes a continuous
manifold as primitive and then quantizes it. Here the discrete is given and the continuum is what it looks like from
outside.

What this buys is computability, and what computability forbids is the continuum, not infinity. A discrete local base is
computable, simulable region by region to any finite extent, with no infinite precision and no supertasks required,
because each finite region is finite and each step is local. An unbounded universe is perfectly allowed, because being
unbounded does not require any single region to be infinite, only that there is always more, so a beginningless or
endless cosmos is computable as long as it stays discrete. So it is the continuum, the genuine infinity packed into any
finite interval, that the base rules out, while leaving the universe free to be as large or as old as it likes.

This is the fulcrum of the whole program. Real numbers never appear at the base, only as measured outputs, derived
formulas, or comparison data. The base is ternary, integer, finite-symmetry, exact, and the smooth laws ride on top of it
as the large-scale appearance of a fine discrete weave. A computable cosmos means the whole thing is, in principle, a
simulation that runs, region by region, to any finite extent, which is exactly what the reproducible suite behind this
paper is, a finite, exact, runnable model of a discrete universe.
