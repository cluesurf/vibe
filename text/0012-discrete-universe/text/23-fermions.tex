\section{Fermions}

One full generation of quarks and leptons, with the right colors and the right fractional charges, is built from the
octonions by a direct, verified construction, not posited. This is the strongest matter-from-the-seed result in the
paper, and it is exact.

The seven octonion left-multiplications form the Clifford algebra, seven anti-commuting square roots of minus one. Six of
them, paired and complexified, give three ladder operators that obey the canonical anti-commutation relations of
fermions, three fermion modes. The complex structure used to pair them, the i, is the same one that gives the photon's
symmetry, which is why it also selects a chirality, particle from antiparticle.

Build the states on three modes and there are eight, labeled by how many modes are filled: zero, one, two, three, with
multiplicities one, three, three, one. Those multiplicities are a color singlet, a color triplet, a color anti-triplet,
and a color singlet, the color structure of one generation. The electric charge is one third of the number of filled
modes, so the charges are zero, one third (three times), two thirds (three times), and one. That is exactly the
neutrino, the three-colored down-type quarks of charge one third, the three-colored up-type quarks of charge two thirds,
and the electron of unit charge, with the conjugate construction giving the antiparticles, sixteen states, one full
generation.

So the fractional charges are not put in, they are forced, because charge is one third of a count of three color modes.
This is computed and checked on the substrate: the Clifford relations, the ladder relations, the number-operator
spectrum, the multiplicities, and the charges all come out as stated, with the explicit matrices in the open codebase \cite{cluesurf2026repo}. What it fixes is the particle content and its
quantum numbers, the part of the Standard Model that always looked like a list to memorize. What it leaves free is the
masses, and what it leaves open is whether the same structure repeats exactly three times, taken up shortly.
