\section{The Continuum}

The base is discrete, but the world looks continuous, and the bridge between them is emergent symmetry restoration. The
exact symmetry of the cell is a finite group, but under coarse-graining the leading corrections that would betray the
discreteness are high-order, sixth-order anisotropy terms, which by simple power-counting are irrelevant in four
dimensions, flowing to zero in the infrared. So the finite cell symmetry restores to the full continuous rotation
symmetry at large scale, and Lorentz invariance appears as an infrared fixed point.

Run the dynamics and coarse-grain, and the continuum equations come out. The streaming-and-colliding rule, at large
scale, gives the Dirac dispersion for matter and the Maxwell equations for the conserved-charge field, the massless
propagators of the continuum theory. The discreteness does not approach smoothness over time, the mesh is discrete at
every scale of its own construction, and smoothness is only the coarse view from a distance, a fine-enough discrete
fabric looking continuous the way sand looks like a surface from far away.

This is the load-bearing direction of the program. It is the continuum, not infinity, that the discrete base rules out at
the bottom: a discrete computable base forbids a true continuum but permits an unbounded universe, because each finite
region stays finite and each step stays local. So the continuum is a viewpoint, not a foundation, and the foundation
stays discrete, computable, and exact, with the smooth laws emerging on top.
