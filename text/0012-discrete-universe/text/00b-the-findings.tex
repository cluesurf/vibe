\section{The Findings}

Here is everything the model produces, listed first, so you know where the walk is going before it starts. Each finding says what was found, how strong it is, and the experiment in the simulator that demonstrates it. The experiments live in the code as a runnable, reproducible suite. This paper describes each one without citing file paths. Every later section takes one of these findings and shows, piece by piece, exactly how it works.

The labels are exact. \textbf{Derived} means forced from the base with nothing put in. \textbf{Predicted} means a number computed from the structure and compared with experiment, with no fit. \textbf{Reproduced} means known physics run on the substrate as on an adequate stage. \textbf{Free} means a Standard Model parameter the model does not fix. \textbf{Open} means genuinely unsolved.

\subsection*{The foundation}

\begin{finding}[The octonions are forced]
Reversibility caps the dimension at eight, and carrying spinors floors it at eight, so the eight-dimensional algebra, the octonions, is squeezed into being. \emph{Derived.} An experiment builds the doubling tower and exhibits the exact pair of nonzero sedenions whose product is zero, the cliff just past the octonions, and confirms triality is unique to the eight-dimensional rotation group.
\end{finding}

\begin{finding}[Four-dimensional spacetime]
The rank of the octonion's rotation algebra is four. \emph{Derived.} An experiment reads the rank and the root system directly.
\end{finding}

\begin{finding}[The 24-cell and the substrate]
The cell is the unique self-dual shell of the tone alphabet, the 24-cell, and the committed mesh is the four-dimensional hyperbolic honeycomb of 24-cells, selected because it carries spinors where its three-dimensional sibling does not. \emph{Derived, to a finite uniqueness check.} An experiment shows the twenty-four directions form the binary tetrahedral group, and a control shows the sibling's twelve directions carry no spinor.
\end{finding}

\begin{finding}[One base law]
One local reversible rule respects the full cell symmetry, out of ten thousand three hundred ninety-five candidate pairings collapsing to one. \emph{Derived.} An experiment counts the pairings and confirms the single survivor, and confirms the rule is reversible by running it backward to the exact start.
\end{finding}

\subsection*{Mass, mixing, and CP}

Mixing is how the generations blend when the weak force acts, so a particle of one family is seen partly as another. CP violation (charge-parity) is the small imbalance between matter and antimatter. Both are governed here by the geometry.

\begin{finding}[The mass-hierarchy shape]
Masses fall as the warp factor to a depth, turning the ten-thousand-trillion hierarchy into a small depth count. \emph{Derived shape, free depths.} An experiment shows the geometric spacing beats a power law and that the growth does not secretly fix the depths.
\end{finding}

\begin{finding}[The Cabibbo angle]
The square root of the down-to-strange mass ratio, about 0.224, matching to one percent. \emph{Derived.} An experiment computes the ratio and the overlap of the light-generation depths.
\end{finding}

\begin{finding}[The neutrino mixing pattern]
The cell quotients uniquely to the canonical neutrino flavor group, which forces the trimaximal pattern, predicting a solar angle squared sine of 0.341, testable by JUNO around 2028, with maximal leptonic CP tied to maximal atmospheric mixing. \emph{Predicted.} An experiment reads the flavor group out of the cell and computes the mixing column.
\end{finding}

\begin{finding}[Strong-CP and Majorana neutrinos]
The strong force does not violate CP, no axion, natural from the CP-symmetric base. \emph{Derived.} The neutrinos are their own antiparticles, predicting neutrinoless double beta decay and normal ordering. \emph{Predicted.}
\end{finding}
