\section{Charge}

The collision step is chosen to conserve the signed sum of the tones, so the total charge of the mesh is unchanged beat
after beat, exactly the way electric charge is conserved in our world. This conserved quantity is not added, it is a
property of the unique symmetric reversible rule, and it is verified to a part in a billion in the lattice gauge tests.

This conserved charge is the seed of electromagnetism. A local rule that conserves a charge has, in the continuum, a
conserved current, and a conserved current is sourced by a gauge field, the photon. On the substrate this shows up as a
working lattice gauge theory: the charge is conserved exactly, the Gauss law holds, a loop in the field measures the
flux it encloses, and the Aharonov-Bohm phase comes out exact. So the framework of electromagnetism is reproduced on the
mesh, not as an extra assumption but as what a conserved local charge does at scale.

The order on the tone, the lean that puts pain below peace below pleasure, is what makes the charge signed rather than a
bare label. It is the one asymmetry in the value structure, the smallest seed of a preferred sense, and for the physics
it is simply the ordering that turns a three-valued tone into a positive, zero, or negative charge. The strength of the
coupling, the fine-structure constant, is not fixed by the bare law, because it runs with scale, and the geometry fixes
only the ratio of the couplings and how they run, leaving one overall normalization free, which is reported open rather
than fitted.
