\section{Questions}

The full map of what is not yet solved, ranked, with the precise obstruction for each.

The deepest open problem is the generation count. A threefold structure is forced, twice over, by triality and by the
rank of the exceptional Jordan algebra, but its identification with the three observed families is unproven. The naive
triality reading is a proven negative, a vector plus two chiralities rather than three families, and the better route
through the Jordan algebra gives the right count and symmetry but leaves the identification a conjecture and the mass
splitting a free input, because an exact symmetry cannot break deterministically. Closing this gap, deriving that the
three Jordan slots are exactly the three generations and that an asymmetric vacuum splitting them is itself forced, is the
single highest-value open problem.

The second is gravity. The effective inverse-square law, the spin-2 graviton, the Einstein equations as an equation of
state, and the correct light bending are in hand, but a fundamental, reversible, propagating gravity built at the base,
in the full curved four-dimensional bulk, is unsolved, because the effective potential forgets the past and throws motion
away rather than being a true reversible piece of the rule. Related and also open: the sharp interacting massless
light-cone mode needs a second conserved quantity the bare rule lacks, and a stable self that both confines a body and
radiates has been shown only in parts, the persistence problem of whether a pattern written into the churn truly lasts.

The bare causal-set action does not make smooth spacetime dominate, the entropy problem,
needing a restricted action. A handful of statistical estimators, the density-of-states and tempering and path-integral
measures, are ensemble claims that do not fully converge at feasible compute. The absolute masses, the bare coupling,
the strong-CP texture, and the dark-matter relic abundance are free or open, each tied to a specific origin. The exact
closed form of the warp factor is a defined computation not yet done. And the first question, why there is something
rather than nothing, is the one door deliberately left closed, accepted as the single premise. Every one of these is
stated as a gap with a definite shape and obstruction.
