\section{Generations}

This is the single deepest open problem of the program. Matter comes in three copies, three generations, identical in
every charge and differing only in mass. The question is whether the model forces exactly three. The answer is that
it forces a threefold structure but does not yet force three Standard Model generations.

Two genuinely threefold structures appear, and both are forced. The rotation structure of eight-dimensional space has
three eight-dimensional pieces cycled by triality, three peers cycled by a symmetry, the right shape for three families.
And the exceptional Jordan algebra of three-by-three octonionic matrices has rank three, forcing three primitive slots
with an exact symmetric-group symmetry, the number three coming from the rank, not from counting observed families.

But the obvious move fails. The triality triple is not three
generations: the two half-spinors are the two chiralities, the left-handed and right-handed parts, of one generation, and
the vector is the gauge direction, so the triple is a vector plus two chiralities, the content of one family, not three.
This is checked directly and reported as a proven negative.

The better route, through the Jordan algebra, gives the right count and symmetry but leaves two obstacles. Identifying the
three slots with the three observed families, with their distinct masses, is an open conjecture, not a theorem. And the
exact symmetry forces a degenerate vacuum, which a deterministic symmetric dynamics cannot spontaneously break, so the
mass splitting is a free external input carrying no geometric information. So the count three is structurally favored and
empirically supported, the no-fourth-family prediction is real and matches the data, but the identification of the three
with the observed generations remains an open conjecture.
