\section{Holography (P15)}
\label{sec:holography}

AdS/CFT ties bulk geometry to boundary entropy, and black-hole entropy scales as area,
not volume. The microscopic origin is the entanglement across the boundary.
\textbf{Where we landed:} the free-fermion ground-state entanglement entropy follows the
conformal log law in 1D (slope $0.323$, central charge $c=1$) and an area law in 2D (the
entropy grows with the boundary, and an area fit beats a volume fit). Entropy living on
boundaries rather than volumes is the signature behind holography and black-hole
thermodynamics, and it sits naturally with the hyperbolic (AdS-like) substrate of P3.
The 3D Bekenstein-Hawking case (entropy by horizon area) is in
Section~\ref{sec:gravity} (P33). The full holographic correspondence, a bulk-boundary
dictionary and the Ryu-Takayanagi formula, is the long road ahead.
