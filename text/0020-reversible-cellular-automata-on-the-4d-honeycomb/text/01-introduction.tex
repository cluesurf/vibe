\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Moving cellular automata from flat Euclidean grids $\mathbb{Z}^d$ to negatively curved hyperbolic tilings $\mathbb{H}^d$ transforms both their algorithmic addressing and their global dynamics. On a flat lattice, Cartesian integer coordinates give constant-time neighbor queries, polynomial ball growth forces surface-to-volume ratios to vanish, and the Moore--Myhill theorem equates global surjectivity with injectivity on finite configurations~\cite{moore1962,myhill1963}. In hyperbolic space, all three properties break down. Cell counts grow exponentially with graph radius, floating-point coordinates fail within a few hops, boundary shells carry a strictly positive fraction of ball volume, and deciding whether an arbitrary local rule induces a globally injective map becomes undecidable~\cite{margenstern2007hedlund,margenstern2008injectivity,bartholdi2010}.

Over two decades of constructive research, Margenstern established the combinatorial foundations for cellular automata across hyperbolic tilings~\cite{margenstern2007vol1,margenstern2008vol2}. In the hyperbolic plane $\mathbb{H}^2$, geometric sector splitting on the pentagrid $\{5,4\}$ and the heptagrid $\{7,3\}$ yields a two-state Fibonacci substitution whose dominant Pisot root $\varphi^2 = (3+\sqrt{5})/2$ (an algebraic integer above $1$ whose other conjugate lies inside the unit circle) produces a regular spanning-tree numeration with linear-time neighbor arithmetic~\cite{margenstern2006substitution,margenstern2016coords,margenstern2019fib1}.

As spatial dimension and symmetry grow, three sharp boundaries emerge. First, Margenstern's geometric region-splitting numeration for maximal integer representations is neither regular nor context-free in the three-dimensional dodecagrid $\{5,3,4\}$ (where neighbor lookup takes cubic time and quadratic space~\cite{margenstern2003skordev}) and remains non-regular in the four-dimensional 120-cell honeycomb $\{5,3,3,4\}$~\cite{margenstern2004fourd,margenstern2007vol1}, while Gasperin and Margenstern proved non-automaticity for groups whose Cayley graph is the primal pentagrid $\{5,4\}$ or heptagrid $\{7,3\}$~\cite{margenstern2014gasperin}. Second, non-amenability decouples injectivity from surjectivity and makes testing arbitrary hyperbolic rules for global injectivity undecidable~\cite{margenstern2008injectivity}. Third, enforcing rotational or permutation invariance compresses local rule tables so tightly that record-setting hyperbolic universal automata rely on engineered spatial tracks~\cite{margenstern2021dodecagrid,margenstern2021outer,margenstern2023weighted,margenstern2024heptagrid}.

Every regular hyperbolic tiling $\{p,\dots,4\}$ with last Schl\"afli entry $4$ has facet dihedral angle $2\pi/4 = \pi/2$, so its facet reflections generate a right-angled Coxeter group (RACG) whose dual cell graph is biautomatic with strictly regular Cartier--Foata commuting-clique normal forms~\cite{cartier1969,brink1993,davis2008racg}. Switching from geometric region-splitting numeration to algebraic Cartier--Foata normal forms restores finite-state regularity in every dimension. Within four-dimensional hyperbolic space $\mathbb{H}^4$, exactly two regular right-angled honeycombs exist: the compact 120-cell honeycomb $\{5,3,3,4\}$ and the paracompact 24-cell honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$~\cite{coxeter1956,coxeter1973}, where \emph{paracompact} means every vertex lies on the ideal boundary at infinity with a flat Euclidean vertex figure.

Table~\ref{tab:tiling-overview} shows why $\{3,4,3,4\}$ offers an unusually tractable four-dimensional computational substrate compared to compact $\{5,3,3,4\}$. Replacing the 120-cell by the self-dual 24-cell shrinks the right-angled Coxeter automaton from $120$ generators, $2{,}641$ cliques, and clique number $4$ over $\mathbb{Z}[\varphi]$ down to $24$ generators, $217$ cliques, and clique number $3$ over the exact quadratic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}]$, collapsing under the $1{,}152$-element Weyl symmetry group $W(F_4)$ of the 24-cell to just four symmetry orbits. One $4 \times 4$ upward transition matrix governs spherical ball shells, parabolic cusp shells descending from a flat cubic \emph{horosphere} (a limit sphere tangent to an ideal vertex at infinity), and linear-time bitmask neighbor lookup. Lifting that automaton to Depth~2 ($19$ states) and Depth~5 ($34{,}523$ states) collapses the adjacency spectral radius bracket by more than $10.2\times$ in Lean~4 (certifying $\lambda_{\mathrm{gap}} > 13/25$) and by over $187\times$ in exact big-integer arithmetic (width below $0.0047$).

Because $\{3,4,3,4\}$ has an asymptotic boundary-to-volume fraction of $94.53\%$, verifying global injectivity of an arbitrary local rule table after the fact is algorithmically intractable. A partitioned cellular automaton~\cite{margolus1984,morita2017book} resolves this structurally by factoring every discrete step into a local cell bijection $L$ followed by a bijective one-hop facet stream. Both factors invert locally, guaranteeing simultaneous global injectivity and surjectivity for all time despite non-amenability. Using the involutive Coxeter facet-exchange stream $S_{\mathrm{exch}}$ on $W(\Gamma_{24})$ and a Lean~4-certified sharp four-dimensional chirality threshold (two ordered orthogonal anchors orient planar gates canonically under the $576$-element proper rotation group $W(F_4)^+$ with cyclic $C_4$ stabilizer), we construct to our knowledge the first strictly spatially homogeneous, $W(F_4)^+$-isotropic, globally bijective universal cellular automata in four-dimensional hyperbolic space with zero special origin cell. A $5$-symbol automaton $U_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)}$ ($5^{24}$ composite states per cell) achieves weak universality via Morita rotary circuits on flat horospheres, while a $15$-symbol automaton $U_{\mathrm{strong}}^{(15)}$ achieves finite-seed strong universality by launching unbounded constant-particle geodesic counter rays directly from finite horosphere circuits into empty four-dimensional bulk space~\cite{bennett1973,minsky1961,morita2001}.

To probe the opposite limit where explicit tracks and specialized species are removed, we also examine a canonical minimal unitary ternary quantum cellular automaton $U$ invariant under $W(F_4)^+$ over the cyclotomic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ (generated by the primitive ninth root of unity $\zeta_9 = e^{2\pi i/9}$ with denominator $6 = 2 \cdot 3$ required by ternary coins and $12$-line normalization). Seven Lean~4-certified algebraic obstructions explain precisely why uninterrupted trackless scattering of this automaton cannot compute ballistically, echoing and quantifying the track requirement observed across planar and three-dimensional hyperbolic automata~\cite{margenstern2021outer,margenstern2023weighted}.

Table~\ref{tab:notation} fixes standard terminology across all sections, following Coxeter, Margenstern, and Morita: each 24-cell tile of $\{3,4,3,4\}$ is called a \emph{cell}, each of its $24$ octahedral faces carries one partitioned \emph{port}, each antipodal facet pair forms a \emph{line}, each discrete update is a \emph{step}, and each flat cubic vertex figure at infinity is a \emph{horosphere}. Core algebraic, geometric, combinatorial, spectral, chiral-bijectivity, and obstruction theorems are formally verified in Lean~4 using only standard logic axioms (zero unproven gaps, zero custom axioms, zero unchecked evaluation), while multi-step circuit trajectories, Depth-5 spectral collapse, and negative controls are verified by deterministic exact-arithmetic companion scripts~\cite{pollard2026repo}. Where infinite-graph density, Poincar\'e's polyhedron faithfulness bridge, or analytic trigonometric lifting sit outside finite Lean kernels, each scope boundary is stated explicitly beside the corresponding theorem. To understand why $\{3,4,3,4\}$ provides this four-orbit algebraic structure in the first place, we begin with its exact four-dimensional classification and quadratic-ring geometry.
