\section{Universality}
\label{sec:universality}

Reversible cellular automata on non-amenable hyperbolic tilings avoid post-hoc injectivity undecidability~\cite{margenstern2008injectivity} by factoring every synchronous beat into two explicit local bijections~\cite{margolus1984,morita2017book}. On $\{3,4,3,4\}$, we separate three distinct layers cleanly: (i) a homogeneous, $W(F_4)$-isotropic $12$-slot-state ($12^{24}$ cell-state) partitioned rule $U_{\mathrm{rev}}$ whose $12$ antipodal lines decouple into exact constant-particle ray counters, (ii) a finite-seed reversible counter machine $U_{\mathrm{sys}}$ coupling homogeneous $U_{\mathrm{rev}}$ rays to a bounded $17$-phase reversible controller at dock $0$, and (iii) three standalone, non-isotropic oscillating gate catalysts (Fredkin, Morita rotary, and geodesic mirror) verified in multi-beat streaming composition, alongside an exact geometric ball-diameter comparison against flat $\mathbb{Z}^4$.

\subsection{Partitioned Factorization}
\label{subsec:partitioned-factorization}

Rather than updating an entire cell from all $24$ neighbors through an unstructured lookup table, a partitioned cellular automaton first applies a local bijection inside each dock and then streams every slot value one hop across its shared facet. Because each half-step inverts locally, global injectivity and global surjectivity hold simultaneously for all time without invoking Moore--Myhill.

To index docks consistently under the frame-aligned transport operators $k_s = F_s Z$ of~\eqref{eq:transport-ids} (recalling that $Z \notin W(\Gamma_{24})$), define the simply transitive \emph{frame group} $K = \langle k_0,\dots,k_{23}\rangle \subset O^+(4,1;\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}])$. Because $Z F_a Z = F_{\bar{a}}$ and $W(\Gamma_{24})$ is bipartite by word length $\ell(w)$, every dock $w(c_0)$ has a unique frame representative $g = w Z^{\ell(w) \bmod 2} \in K$ satisfying $g(c_0) = w(c_0)$ and $g k_s \in K$ for every $s \in \mathcal{F}$.

\begin{definition}[Partitioned Reversible Factorization]
\label{def:rpca}
Let $\Sigma = \{0, 1, \dots, 11\}$ be the $12$-symbol slot alphabet consisting of the neutral quiescent symbol $0$ and $11$ active species carrying unit occupation ($N_{\mathrm{occ}} = 1$ per occupied slot), and let $\mathcal{C} = \Sigma^{K \times \mathcal{F}}$ denote the configuration space assigning one symbol to every facet slot of every dock. Pair the $24$ slots into $12$ antipodal lines $\ell = (a_\ell, \bar{a}_\ell)$ ($r_{\bar{a}_\ell} = -r_{a_\ell}$). Given a reflection-symmetric local line bijection $L_{\mathrm{line}} : \Sigma^2 \to \Sigma^2$, define the global update
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:rpca-factor}
U_{\mathrm{rev}} = S \circ L_{\mathrm{rev}}, \qquad U_{\mathrm{rev}}^{-1} = L_{\mathrm{rev}}^{-1} \circ S^{-1},
\end{equation}
where $L_{\mathrm{rev}}$ applies $L_{\mathrm{line}}$ identically to all $12$ antipodal lines of every dock and the bijective one-hop facet stream $S$ shifts every slot forward along its geodesic frame:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stream-bijection}
(S x)\bigl(g k_s,\, s\bigr) = x(g, s), \qquad (S^{-1} x)\bigl(g k_{\bar{s}},\, s\bigr) = x(g, s).
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

Because $k_s k_{\bar{s}} = I_5$ identically on every dock and slot (verified with $0$ defects across $1{,}528$ dock-slot pairs along $B_1$ and geodesic rays~\cite{pollard2026repo}), $S$ is an exact global bijection on both $\mathcal{C}$ and finite-support configurations. Moreover, every symmetry $h \in W(F_4)$ permutes the $12$ antipodal lines and either fixes or swaps each line's endpoints, so reflection symmetry
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:line-refl}
L_{\mathrm{line}}(b, a) = \mathrm{swap}\bigl(L_{\mathrm{line}}(a, b)\bigr)
\end{equation}
guarantees exact invariance of $U_{\mathrm{rev}}$ under all $1{,}152$ elements of $W(F_4)$.

\begin{proposition}[Global Bijectivity Without Moore--Myhill]
\label{prop:global-bijection}
Whenever $L_{\mathrm{line}} : \Sigma^2 \to \Sigma^2$ is bijective and fixes $(0,0)$, the global map $U_{\mathrm{rev}} = S \circ L_{\mathrm{rev}}$ is simultaneously globally injective and globally surjective on both full configurations $\mathcal{C}$ and finite-support configurations, with radius-$1$ local inverse $U_{\mathrm{rev}}^{-1} = L_{\mathrm{rev}}^{-1} \circ S^{-1}$ for every $t \in \mathbb{Z}$.
\end{proposition}

Proposition~\ref{prop:global-bijection} bypasses Margenstern's injectivity undecidability theorem~\cite{margenstern2008injectivity} algebraically: bijectivity is checked exhaustively on the $144$-element finite set $\Sigma^2$ rather than inferred from global graph dynamics.

\subsection{Isotropic Bijection}
\label{subsec:isotropic-bijection}

To store an unbounded non-negative integer along any geodesic ray without static tracks or particle loss, four functional roles suffice: a stationary period-$2$ bookmark $M$ marking the counter position, a slow speed-$1/3$ walker $\{W_0, W_1, W_2\}$ that shifts the bookmark gently outward or inward, fast speed-$1$ launch and catch pulses $\{F, G, H, F_r, G_r, H_r\}$ that start and stop the walker with input-independent return spacing, and an elastic echo probe $P$ that reports distance without disturbing the bookmark. Together with the neutral quiescent symbol $0$, these form the $11$ active species
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:species-list}
\Sigma \setminus \{0\} = \{M,\; W_0, W_1, W_2,\; F, G, H,\; F_r, G_r, H_r,\; P\}.
\end{equation}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Exact bijectivity:} $L_{\mathrm{line}}^{-1} \circ L_{\mathrm{line}} = L_{\mathrm{line}} \circ L_{\mathrm{line}}^{-1} = \mathrm{id}_{\Sigma^2}$, with every two-particle interaction an exact involution.
\item \textbf{Full $W(F_4)$ isotropy:} reflection symmetry~\eqref{eq:line-refl} holds across all $144$ pairs.
\item \textbf{Exact occupation conservation:} total occupied-slot count $N_{\mathrm{occ}}$ is conserved on every transition.
\item \textbf{Neutral quiescent stationarity:} $L_{\mathrm{line}}(0,0) = (0,0)$.
\end{enumerate}

Composing Table~\ref{tab:lrev-table} with the stream $S$ produces exact ray kinematics directly from the local rules. A lone marker $M$ bounces between adjacent docks $(x, \bar{a}) \leftrightarrow (x+1, a)$ at period $2$ with zero leakage over $40$ beats, while a lone walker executes the three-beat cycle
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:walker-cycle}
(x, a, W_0) \;\longrightarrow\; (x-1, \bar{a}, W_1) \;\longrightarrow\; (x, a, W_2) \;\longrightarrow\; (x+1, a, W_0),
\end{equation}
advancing at exact average speed $v_W = +1/3$ dock per beat ($+6$ docks in $18$ beats, zero error) while all control pulses propagate at speed $1$. Crucially, because $L_{\mathrm{rev}}$ acts independently on each of the $12$ antipodal lines, pure homogeneous $U_{\mathrm{rev}}$ is line-decoupled: signals on distinct rays never cross without introducing cross-line gate elements or an origin controller.

\subsection{Reversible Gates}
\label{subsec:reversible-gates}

Under uniform streaming $S$, a stationary logic gate cannot sit frozen inside a single slot. Instead, each stationary switch can be encoded as a \emph{period-$2$ oscillating gate catalyst} hopping back and forth across one auxiliary antipodal line on odd beats and returning to the active junction dock on every even beat. Adding three catalyst species ($K_F, B, R_{0,1}$, bringing the combined slot alphabet to $15$ symbols) defines three standalone, fixed-slot (non-$W(F_4)$-isotropic) reversible gate rules verified in live multi-beat streaming composition $U_{\mathrm{gate}} = S \circ L_{\mathrm{gate}}$ on $\{3,4,3,4\}$~\cite{pollard2026repo}.

\begin{definition}[Controlled-Swap (Fredkin) Catalyst $K_F$]
\label{def:cswap}
A catalyst $K_F$ oscillating on auxiliary line $\ell_{11}$ reads a control pulse $P$ on slot $0$ at even beats and swaps two distinct data channels (slot $1$ on $\ell_1$ and slot $16$ on $\ell_8$) iff the control pulse is present, realizing the conservative $3$-bit Fredkin gate~\cite{fredkin1982}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Morita Rotary Catalyst $B$]
\label{def:morita-re}
A catalyst $B$ encoding an internal horizontal or vertical bar by oscillating on $\ell_{10}$ ($H$-bar) or $\ell_{11}$ ($V$-bar) interacts with four signal ports along orthogonal rays $\ell_0, \ell_1$ (north, east, south, west) on the single-pulse subspace ($\le 1$ simultaneous incoming port pulse). A pulse entering parallel to the bar passes straight through with the bar unchanged, whereas a pulse striking perpendicular to the bar turns $90^\circ$ clockwise and flips the catalyst onto the orthogonal line ($H \leftrightarrow V$), realizing Morita's reversible rotary element~\cite{morita2001,morita2017book}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Geodesic Mirror Catalyst $R_{0,1}$]
\label{def:mirror-cat}
A catalyst $R_{0,1}$ oscillating on auxiliary line $\ell_9$ exchanges signal channels between the orthogonal geodesic rays $\ell_0$ and $\ell_1$ on every even beat, leaving all other lines untouched.
\end{definition}

Table~\ref{tab:gates} records the exact verification metrics for all three standalone oscillating catalysts. Note two honest structural constraints. First, the Morita rotary rule in Definition~\ref{def:morita-re} is certified bijective on its single-pulse invariant subspace (at most one active input port per even beat), not on multi-pulse simultaneous collisions. Second, under full reflection-including $W(F_4)$, the stabilizer of a single line $\ell_{10}$ both permutes orthogonal lines and reverses $2\mathrm{D}$ orientation in the $(\ell_0,\ell_1)$ plane, so encoding a single unmarked clockwise rotary catalyst inside one unified $W(F_4)$-isotropic rule table is geometrically obstructed unless chirality is broken or carried explicitly by multi-slot motifs.

\subsection{Counter Machine}
\label{subsec:counter-machine}

Minsky's theorem establishes that a finite-state controller coupled to two or more unbounded non-negative integer counters supporting increment, decrement, and zero-test operations is Turing universal~\cite{minsky1961}, and Bennett's compute--copy--uncompute construction makes any deterministic computation logically reversible~\cite{bennett1973}. Along any geodesic ray evolving strictly under homogeneous $W(F_4)$-isotropic $U_{\mathrm{rev}}$, a single marker $M$ placed at radial distance $n = n_0 + 2c$ encodes an integer $c \ge 0$ and supports all three counter primitives at constant occupied-slot count (Table~\ref{tab:primitives} and Figure~\ref{fig:spacetime}).

\paragraph{Increment (INC).}
Launching $F$ at $t=0$ and catch pulse $G$ at $t=6$ shifts the marker outward by two docks. Pulse $F$ strikes $M$ at position $n$ at $t = n$, reflecting $F_r$ back toward the origin while releasing slow walker $W_0$ outward at speed $+1/3$. Eight beats later (at $t = n + 8$), catch pulse $G$ overtakes the walker in phase $W_1$ at position $n + 2$, converting it back to a stationary marker and emitting return pulse $G_r$. Regardless of counter value $c$, $F_r$ and $G_r$ arrive back at dock $0$ separated by exact constant interval $\Delta t_{\mathrm{inc}} = 10$ beats.

\paragraph{Decrement (DEC).}
Launching $H$ at $t=0$ and $G$ at $t=10$ shifts the marker inward from $n+2$ to $n$. Pulse $H$ releases an inward walker $(H, M) \to (W_1, H_r)$ caught at position $n$ by $G$ via $(G, W_2) \to (M, F_r)$, returning the two pulses to dock $0$ with exact constant spacing $\Delta t_{\mathrm{dec}} = 6$ beats for every $c \ge 0$.

\paragraph{Zero test (ZERO\_TEST).}
At remote baseline offset $n_0 = 8$, a probe pulse $P$ launched at $t=0$ reflects elastically off $M$ and returns to dock $0$ at beat $t_{\mathrm{ret}} = 16 + 4c$ ($16$ iff $c=0$, versus $20, 24, 28$ for $c=1,2,3$), giving a non-destructive remote readout of marker position. Alternatively, when counters start at offset $n_0 = 0$ ($n_j = 2c_j$), dock $0$ tests $c_j = 0$ directly in $O(1)$ beats by checking whether $M_j$ occupies the origin slot on an even beat.

To demonstrate complete reversible register arithmetic with zero unbounded hidden memory and zero escaping particles, we place three counter markers $(M_1, M_2, M_3)$ at offset-$0$ positions $(2c_1, 2c_2, 2c_3)$ along three distinct geodesic rays $\ell_0, \ell_1, \ell_2$ (which are pairwise disjoint outside dock $0$ by geodesic uniqueness, with $\ell_0 \perp \ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ meeting both at $60^\circ$). Every non-origin dock evolves strictly under homogeneous $U_{\mathrm{rev}}$, while dock $0$ hosts a strictly bounded $17$-phase reversible finite-state controller ($\text{launch} \in \{0,\dots,11\}$, $\text{ret} \in \{0,\dots,10\}$) coupled to two reusable parked work tokens on auxiliary lines $\ell_3, \ell_4$. Because those two tokens are launched into rays during each macro and re-parked upon return, total occupation remains fixed at $N_{\mathrm{occ}}(t) = 5$ across every beat.

\begin{theorem}[Finite-Seed Reversible Counter Machine]
\label{thm:counter-machine}
For the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb embedded in $O^+(4,1;\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}])$ with homogeneous $U_{\mathrm{rev}} = S \circ L_{\mathrm{rev}}$ at every non-origin dock and the bounded $17$-phase reversible origin controller at dock $0$, the coupled evolution $U_{\mathrm{sys}}$ executes the Bennett compute-and-restore routine
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:bennett-map}
(c_1,\, c_2,\, 0) \;\longmapsto\; (c_1,\, c_2 + 2 c_1,\, 0)
\end{equation}
for every $(c_1, c_2) \in \mathbb{N}^2$ at fixed occupied-slot count $N_{\mathrm{occ}}(t) = 5$ with zero escaping tokens, zero unbounded controller state, and exact trajectory reversal
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sys-rev}
U_{\mathrm{sys}}^{-T}\bigl(U_{\mathrm{sys}}^{T}(x_0)\bigr) = x_0
\end{equation}
along every reachable run up to its halting phase.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Proof sketch.}
Loop~$1$ tests $c_1 = 0$ locally at dock $0$. While $c_1 > 0$, it executes $\textsc{dec}(\ell_0)$, $\textsc{inc}(\ell_1)$ twice, and $\textsc{inc}(\ell_2)$ once, preserving the loop invariant $(c_1 - k,\, c_2 + 2k,\, k)$ after $k$ iterations until $(0,\, c_2 + 2c_1,\, c_1)$ is reached. Loop~$2$ then tests $c_3 = 0$ locally and iterates $\textsc{dec}(\ell_2)$ and $\textsc{inc}(\ell_0)$, preserving $(k',\, c_2 + 2c_1,\, c_1 - k')$ until $(c_1,\, c_2 + 2c_1,\, 0)$ enters the terminal halt phase. Because every macro step launches and re-parks the same two work tokens via invertible ray involutions and every phase transition has a unique predecessor along the reachable trajectory, $N_{\mathrm{occ}}(t) = 5$ holds identically and running $U_{\mathrm{sys}}^{-1}$ for $T$ beats recovers $x_0$ bit for bit.

\subsection{Geometric Latency}
\label{subsec:geometric-latency}

Any hyperbolic tiling provides a logarithmic ball-diameter advantage over polynomial Euclidean grids: Margenstern already implemented linear-depth geodesic message routing on planar $\{7,3\}$ ($\lambda = \varphi^2 \approx 2.618$)~\cite{margenstern2006comm}. On four-dimensional $\{3,4,3,4\}$, Theorem~\ref{thm:cubic-growth} lifts the base of the logarithm to $\lambda_{3434} \approx 18.27871$.

In flat $\mathbb{Z}^4$, the exact count of lattice points within $\ell_1$-radius $R$ is the four-dimensional cross-polytope polynomial
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:z4-vol}
V_{\mathbb{Z}^4}(R) = \frac{2R^4 + 4R^3 + 10R^2 + 8R + 3}{3},
\end{equation}
giving $V_{\mathbb{Z}^4}(0) = 1$, $V_{\mathbb{Z}^4}(1) = 9$, and round-trip diameter $2R = \Omega(M_{\mathrm{mem}}^{1/4})$ for storing $M_{\mathrm{mem}}$ memory sites. By contrast, a $\{3,4,3,4\}$ cusp ball of depth $h$ encloses cumulative capacity
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:cusp-cap}
V_h = \sum_{d=0}^{h} N_d = \Theta(\lambda_{3434}^{h}), \qquad T_{\mathrm{hyp}}(M_{\mathrm{mem}}) = 2h = O\bigl(\log_{18.28} M_{\mathrm{mem}}\bigr).
\end{equation}
Table~\ref{tab:ram-speedup} compares these exact geometric lower bounds at matched capacity $M_{\mathrm{mem}} = V_h$, reaching an $8.40\times$ round-trip reduction at depth $h = 5$. This is strictly a geometric diameter-versus-capacity bound rather than an implemented tree-RAM circuit (the counter machine of Section~\ref{subsec:counter-machine} uses linear unary rays), and congestion-free reversible tree routing remains open.
