\section{Universality}
\label{sec:universality}

Reversible cellular automata on non-amenable hyperbolic tilings avoid post-hoc injectivity undecidability~\cite{margenstern2008injectivity} by factoring every synchronous step into two explicit local bijections~\cite{margolus1984,morita2017book}. We establish the first strictly spatially homogeneous, rotation-invariant ($576$-element chiral $W(F_4)^+$-isotropic), globally bijective universal cellular automata in four-dimensional hyperbolic space with zero special origin cell: a $5$-symbol weakly universal Morita rotary automaton $U_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)}$ operating on flat $\{4,3,4\}$ horospheres and a $15$-symbol finite-seed strongly universal automaton $U_{\mathrm{strong}}^{(15)}$ coupling finite horosphere circuits to empty four-dimensional bulk geodesic counter rays, grounded in a Lean~4-certified sharp four-dimensional chirality threshold.

\subsection{Partitioned Factorization}
\label{subsec:partitioned-factorization}

Rather than updating an entire cell from all $24$ neighbors through an unstructured lookup table, a partitioned cellular automaton first applies a local bijection $L : \Sigma^{24} \to \Sigma^{24}$ identically inside every cell and then streams every port value one hop across its shared facet. Because each half-step inverts locally, global injectivity and global surjectivity hold simultaneously for all time without invoking Moore--Myhill.

Two canonical bijective one-hop facet streams act on $\{3,4,3,4\}$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Involutive Coxeter facet-exchange stream $S_{\mathrm{exch}}$ on $W(\Gamma_{24})$:} For every word $w \in W(\Gamma_{24})$ and facet $s \in \mathcal{F}$, exchange port $s$ across the shared facet with neighbor $w F_s$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:exch-stream}
(S_{\mathrm{exch}}\,x)(w F_s,\, s) = x(w, s), \qquad S_{\mathrm{exch}}^2 = I.
\end{equation}
Because $F_s^2 = I_5$ identically, $S_{\mathrm{exch}}$ is an exact global involution. On any flat horosphere slice $(X,Y) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ generated by the four commuting cusp reflections $\{E^+, E^-, N^+, N^-\} \subset L_{\mathrm{cusp}}$, $S_{\mathrm{exch}}$ preserves the four horosphere ports uniformly at every cell without odd-row mirror parity flips, and any stationary catalyst symbol placed on a single port bounces across its shared facet at exact period $2$ with zero separate bounce step.
\item \textbf{Frame-aligned geodesic stream $S$ on $K$:} Defining the simply transitive frame group $K = \langle k_0,\dots,k_{23}\rangle \subset O^+(4,1;\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}])$ from $k_s = F_s Z$ ($g = w Z^{\ell(w)\bmod 2}$), the frame stream
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stream-bijection}
(S x)(g k_s,\, s) = x(g, s), \qquad (S^{-1} x)(g k_{\bar{s}},\, s) = x(g, s)
\end{equation}
is used for the line-decoupled geodesic counter $U_{\mathrm{rev}}$, the bounded-origin Bennett verification benchmark $U_{\mathrm{sys}}$, and the unitary ternary automaton $U$ of Section~\ref{sec:obstructions}.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{proposition}[Global Bijectivity Without Moore--Myhill]
\label{prop:global-bijection}
Let $\Sigma$ be any finite port alphabet containing a quiescent symbol $0$, and let $L : \Sigma^{24} \to \Sigma^{24}$ be any bijection fixing the all-$0$ cell. Then the partitioned cellular automaton $U = S_{\mathrm{exch}} \circ L$ on $\Sigma^{W(\Gamma_{24}) \times \mathcal{F}}$ is simultaneously globally injective and globally surjective on both full and finite-support configurations, with exact radius-$1$ local inverse $U^{-1} = L^{-1} \circ S_{\mathrm{exch}}$ for all $t \in \mathbb{Z}$ (proved polymorphically in Lean~4~\cite{pollard2026repo}). The exact same statement holds for $S \circ L$ on $\Sigma^{K \times \mathcal{F}}$ with inverse $L^{-1} \circ S^{-1}$.
\end{proposition}

Proposition~\ref{prop:global-bijection} bypasses Margenstern's injectivity undecidability theorem~\cite{margenstern2008injectivity} algebraically: global bijectivity reduces completely to certifying a finite local bijection on $\Sigma^{24}$.

\subsection{Sharp 4D Chirality Threshold}
\label{subsec:chirality-threshold}

Compiling planar reversible logic such as Morita's clockwise rotary element~\cite{morita2001} into a single spatially homogeneous, rotation-invariant $4\mathrm{D}$ local rule faces an immediate geometric obstruction: how many oriented anchor ports inside a 24-cell are strictly necessary and sufficient to define a canonical $90^\circ$ rotation on an orthogonal $2\mathrm{D}$ plane? In Lean~4, we resolve this threshold sharply for both chiral proper rotations $W(F_4)^+$ ($|W(F_4)^+| = 576$, $\det = +1$) and the full reflection-including Weyl group $W(F_4)$ ($|W(F_4)| = 1{,}152$).

For any ordered pair $(a, b) \in \mathcal{F}^2$ of orthogonal $D_4$ roots ($r_a \cdot r_b = 0$), partition the remaining facets via the inner products $(r_a \cdot r_x,\, r_b \cdot r_x)$:
\begin{align}
Q(a,b) &= \{\, q \in \mathcal{F} : r_a \cdot r_q = 0 \text{ and } r_b \cdot r_q = 0 \,\}, \label{eq:q-set}\\
P(a,b) &= \{\, p \in \mathcal{F} : r_a \cdot r_p = +1 \text{ and } r_b \cdot r_p = +1 \,\}. \label{eq:p-set}
\end{align}
For the canonical cusp pair $(a,b) = (U^+, U^-) \subset L_{\mathrm{cusp}}$, $Q(a,b) = \{E^+, N^+, E^-, N^-\}$ is the exact $4$-port horosphere wire cross, while $P(a,b)$ is an orthogonal $4$-port bulk-diagonal frame (a union of two antipodal lines) disjoint from both $\{a,b\}$ and $Q(a,b)$. Define canonical order-$4$ rotations $J^Q_{a,b} : Q(a,b) \to Q(a,b)$ and $J^P_{a,b} : P(a,b) \to P(a,b)$ uniquely by the positive $4 \times 4$ integer root determinant:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:det-rot}
\det[r_a,\, r_b,\, r_q,\, r_{J^Q_{a,b}(q)}] = +2, \qquad
\det[r_a,\, r_b,\, r_p,\, r_{J^P_{a,b}(p)}] = +4,
\end{equation}
together with the unique northern coupling port $q_N(p) \in Q(a,b)$ satisfying $r_p \cdot r_{q_N(p)} = r_p \cdot r_{J^Q(q_N(p))} = +1$ and perpendicular predicate $\mathrm{perp}(p, q) \iff q \in \{q_N(p),\, (J^Q)^2(q_N(p))\}$.

\begin{theorem}[Sharp 4D Chirality Threshold]
\label{thm:chirality-threshold}
Every claim below is machine-checked unconditionally in Lean~4 over exact $D_4$ integer root vectors and $W(F_4)$~\cite{pollard2026repo}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{$k = 1$ obstructed under $W(F_4)^+$:} For every facet $a \in \mathcal{F}$ and every orthogonal pair $(p,q)$ perpendicular to $a$, there exists an explicit proper rotation $g \in W(F_4)^+$ ($\det g = +1$) fixing $a$ while swapping $p \leftrightarrow q$, negating $\det[r_a, r_b, r_p, r_q] \neq 0$ and preventing any single-anchor rule from orienting a $2\mathrm{D}$ plane.
\item \textbf{$k = 2$ sharp and sufficient under $W(F_4)^+$:} The $144$ ordered orthogonal pairs $(a,b)$ form a single transitive $W(F_4)^+$-orbit with cyclic order-$4$ pointwise stabilizer $C_4$, exact cardinalities $|Q(a,b)| = |P(a,b)| = 4$, and pairwise disjointness of $\{a,b\}$, $Q(a,b)$, and $P(a,b)$. The maps $J^Q_{a,b}$ and $J^P_{a,b}$ in~\eqref{eq:det-rot} are unique order-$4$ cycles whose square $(J^Q_{a,b})^2$ is swap-invariant under $(a,b) \leftrightarrow (b,a)$, preserve $\mathrm{perp}(J^P(p), J^Q(q)) = \mathrm{perp}(p, q)$, and commute unconditionally with all $576$ proper rotations $g \in W(F_4)^+$.
\item \textbf{$k^* = 4$ required under full $W(F_4)$:} Every facet triple $(a,b,c) \in \mathcal{F}^3$ is fixed pointwise by an improper reflection $g \in W(F_4) \setminus W(F_4)^+$ ($\det g = -1$), and all $576$ improper reflections reverse~\eqref{eq:det-rot}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

Theorem~\ref{thm:chirality-threshold} gives the exact minimal symmetry budget of four-dimensional hyperbolic space: one ordered pair of orthogonal anchor symbols $(A, B)$ suffices to orient every local planar gate identically under all $576$ proper rotations of the 24-cell, with no global coordinate frame and no privileged origin cell.

\subsection{Homogeneous Chiral Universality}
\label{subsec:homog-chiral-univ}

Equipped with Theorem~\ref{thm:chirality-threshold}, we construct a strictly spatially homogeneous, $W(F_4)^+$-isotropic, globally bijective $5$-symbol cellular automaton on $\{3,4,3,4\}$ that natively embeds arbitrary planar Morita reversible circuits~\cite{morita2001,morita2017book} on any flat $\{4,3,4\}$ horosphere slice.

\begin{definition}[$5$-Symbol Automaton $U_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)}$]
\label{def:u-univ5}
Let $\Sigma_5 = \{0, P, A, B, R\}$ be the $5$-symbol port alphabet consisting of quiescent $0$, signal pulse $P$, two orthogonal chiral anchors $A, B$, and rotary control token $R$. Define the local cell bijection $L_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)} : \Sigma_5^{24} \to \Sigma_5^{24}$ purely from the cell's own $24$ port values as follows. Whenever a cell contains exactly one $A$ on port $a$, exactly one $B$ on an orthogonal port $b$ ($r_a \cdot r_b = 0$), zero catalysts in $Q(a,b)$, and zero $P$ tokens in $P(a,b)$, let $m = |\{p \in P(a,b) : x_p = R\}|$ count the $R$ tokens on $P(a,b)$ and fix all anchors and outer ports outside $Q(a,b) \cup P(a,b)$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{$m = 0$ (Horosphere wire and simultaneous crossover):} Apply $(J^Q_{a,b})^2$ on $Q(a,b)$ (routing opposite horosphere ports straight through, allowing two orthogonal pulses to cross simultaneously without interference).
\item \textbf{$m = 4$ ($90^\circ$ corner turner):} Apply $J^Q_{a,b}$ on $Q(a,b)$ (swapping $A \leftrightarrow B$ reverses the turn to $(J^Q_{a,b})^{-1}$).
\item \textbf{$m = 1$ (Morita Reversible Rotary Element):} Let $p_{\mathrm{bar}} \in P(a,b)$ carry the unique $R$ token. If exactly one port $q \in Q(a,b)$ carries $P$, route $P$ straight to $(J^Q)^2(q)$ leaving $p_{\mathrm{bar}}$ fixed when $\neg\mathrm{perp}(p_{\mathrm{bar}}, q)$ (parallel hit), and route $P$ to $J^Q(q)$ while advancing $R$ to $J^P(p_{\mathrm{bar}})$ when $\mathrm{perp}(p_{\mathrm{bar}}, q)$ (perpendicular hit). All $0$-pulse and $\ge 2$-pulse states on $Q(a,b)$ remain fixed.
\item \textbf{Fallback (All other cells, including bulk bounce):} Fix every antipodal line containing any catalyst $\{A,B,R\}$ pointwise, and swap antipodal ports on every catalyst-free line.
\end{enumerate}
Set $U_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)} = S_{\mathrm{exch}} \circ L_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)}$ on $\Sigma_5^{W(\Gamma_{24}) \times \mathcal{F}}$.
\end{definition}

Because $P(a,b)$ is an exact union of two antipodal lines, the fallback branch never transports non-catalyst tokens into or out of $P(a,b)$ and keeps catalyst sets strictly invariant, guaranteeing clean partition disjointness across all $5^{24}$ configurations. Placing anchor and control tokens at $t = 0$ directly on even-parity horosphere cells $(X,Y)$ ($X+Y$ even) and on their immediate $1$-step bounce neighbors $w F_s$ for odd-parity cells ($X+Y$ odd) synchronizes all catalysts to arrive simultaneously with signals on every active step. Because the $600$ catalyst bounce neighbors of a $10 \times 10$ horosphere patch are pairwise distinct and disjoint from the horosphere, neighboring tiles never interfere.

\begin{theorem}[Homogeneous Weak Universality in 4D]
\label{thm:homog-weak-univ}
The $5$-symbol automaton $U_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)} = S_{\mathrm{exch}} \circ L_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)}$ on $\{3,4,3,4\}$ satisfies:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Global bijectivity, isotropy, and conservation (Lean~4):} Across the entire configuration space $\Sigma_5^{24}$ ($5^{24}$ states per cell), $L_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)}$ is an exact bijection with explicit local inverse $(L_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)})^{-1}$, commutes unconditionally with all $576$ proper rotations $g \in W(F_4)^+$, conserves every species count $(N_P, N_A, N_B, N_R)$ individually, and fixes the quiescent cell. By Proposition~\ref{prop:global-bijection}, $U_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)}$ is globally bijective on $\Sigma_5^{W(\Gamma_{24}) \times \mathcal{F}}$.
\item \textbf{Flat horosphere embedding and weak universality:} In Lean~4, the four horosphere reflections in $Q(U^+, U^-)$ generate non-trivial commuting cusp-fixing translations $T_E = F_{E^+} F_{E^-}$ and $T_N = F_{N^+} F_{N^-}$ ($T_E T_N = T_N T_E$, $2B(T_E c_0, c_0) = 2B(T_N c_0, c_0) = -18$, $T_E \neq T_N$). Embedding Morita's reversible rotary element, straight wires, simultaneous crossovers, and $90^\circ$ turners homogeneously on this flat $\mathbb{Z}^2$ horosphere makes $U_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)}$ weakly Turing universal~\cite{morita2001,morita2017book}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{definition}[$15$-Symbol Automaton $U_{\mathrm{strong}}^{(15)}$]
\label{def:u-strong15}
Let $\Sigma_{15} = \Sigma_{\mathrm{ray}} \cup \{A, B, R\}$ be the $15$-symbol port alphabet (identifying horosphere pulse $P$ with ray probe $P$). Extend $L_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)}$ to $L_{\mathrm{strong}}^{(15)} : \Sigma_{15}^{24} \to \Sigma_{15}^{24}$ by applying $L_{\mathrm{line}}^{\mathrm{exch}}$ on every catalyst-free antipodal line in the fallback branch and activating the $m = 3$ sector on $P(a,b)$: when exactly three ports of $P(a,b)$ carry $R$, let $p_{\mathrm{empty}} \in P(a,b)$ be the unique non-$R$ bulk launch port and let $(q_0, q_1, q_2, q_3)$ be the cyclic $J^Q$-ordering of $Q(a,b)$ starting from $q_0 = q_N(p_{\mathrm{empty}})$. Whenever the $5$-port tuple $(q_0, q_1, q_2, q_3, p_{\mathrm{empty}})$ carries exactly one token from the $11$-state launcher alphabet, permute those $11$ single-particle states via three disjoint $3$-cycles and one transposition:
\begin{align}
(q_0{:}P \to p_{\mathrm{empty}}{:}F \to p_{\mathrm{empty}}{:}F_r \to q_0{:}P), &\qquad
(q_1{:}P \to p_{\mathrm{empty}}{:}G \to p_{\mathrm{empty}}{:}G_r \to q_1{:}P), \label{eq:launcher-cycles}\\
(q_2{:}P \to p_{\mathrm{empty}}{:}H \to p_{\mathrm{empty}}{:}H_r \to q_2{:}P), &\qquad
(q_3{:}P \leftrightarrow p_{\mathrm{empty}}{:}P), \label{eq:launcher-cycles-2}
\end{align}
fixing all other states on $(q_0, q_1, q_2, q_3, p_{\mathrm{empty}})$ identically. Set $U_{\mathrm{strong}}^{(15)} = S_{\mathrm{exch}} \circ L_{\mathrm{strong}}^{(15)}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Homogeneous Strong Universality in 4D]

The $15$-symbol rule $U_{\mathrm{strong}}^{(15)}$ of Definition~\ref{def:u-strong15} on $\{3,4,3,4\}$ satisfies:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Global bijectivity, isotropy, and conservation (Lean~4):} Across all $15^{24}$ local configurations, $L_{\mathrm{strong}}^{(15)}$ is an exact bijection with explicit local inverse, commutes unconditionally with all $576$ proper rotations $g \in W(F_4)^+$, conserves total occupied-port count $N_{\mathrm{occ}}$, and fixes the quiescent cell. By Proposition~\ref{prop:global-bijection}, $U_{\mathrm{strong}}^{(15)}$ is globally bijective on $\Sigma_{15}^{W(\Gamma_{24}) \times \mathcal{F}}$ with zero special origin cell.
\item \textbf{Finite-seed strong Turing universality:} Every bounded reversible Minsky/Bennett counter-machine controller~\cite{minsky1961,bennett1973} compiles into a finite horosphere network of $0$-$R$/$1$-$R$/$4$-$R$ Morita RE tiles~\cite{morita2001,morita2017book} whose $3$-$R$ launchers drive unbounded single-marker counter rays in empty $4\mathrm{D}$ bulk space (each ray strictly disjoint from the horosphere and all catalyst bounce cells).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Geometric Latency}
\label{subsec:geometric-latency}

Any hyperbolic tiling provides a logarithmic ball-diameter advantage over polynomial Euclidean grids: Margenstern already implemented linear-depth geodesic message routing on planar $\{7,3\}$ ($\lambda = \varphi^2 \approx 2.618$)~\cite{margenstern2006comm}. On four-dimensional $\{3,4,3,4\}$, Theorem~\ref{thm:cubic-growth} lifts the base of the logarithm to $\lambda_{3434} \approx 18.27871$.

In flat $\mathbb{Z}^4$, the exact count of lattice points within $\ell_1$-radius $R$ is the four-dimensional cross-polytope polynomial
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:z4-vol}
V_{\mathbb{Z}^4}(R) = \frac{2R^4 + 4R^3 + 10R^2 + 8R + 3}{3},
\end{equation}
giving $V_{\mathbb{Z}^4}(0) = 1$, $V_{\mathbb{Z}^4}(1) = 9$, and round-trip diameter $2R = \Omega(M_{\mathrm{mem}}^{1/4})$ for storing $M_{\mathrm{mem}}$ memory sites. By contrast, a $\{3,4,3,4\}$ cusp ball of depth $h$ encloses cumulative capacity
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:cusp-cap}
V_h = \sum_{d=0}^{h} N_d = \Theta(\lambda_{3434}^{h}), \qquad T_{\mathrm{hyp}}(M_{\mathrm{mem}}) = 2h = O\bigl(\log_{18.28} M_{\mathrm{mem}}\bigr).
\end{equation}
Table~\ref{tab:ram-speedup} compares these exact geometric lower bounds at matched capacity $M_{\mathrm{mem}} = V_h$, reaching an $8.40\times$ round-trip reduction at depth $h = 5$. This is strictly a geometric diameter-versus-capacity bound rather than an implemented tree-RAM circuit (the bulk counters of Section~\ref{subsec:counter-machine} use linear unary rays), and congestion-free reversible tree routing remains open.
