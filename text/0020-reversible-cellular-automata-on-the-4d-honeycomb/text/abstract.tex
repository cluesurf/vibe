\begin{abstract}
Cellular automata on hyperbolic tilings face three structural barriers in higher dimensions: geometric region-splitting numerations lose formal-language regularity in three and four dimensions, non-amenable boundary growth breaks the Euclidean Moore--Myhill equivalence and renders global injectivity undecidable for arbitrary rules, and high local symmetry constrains trackless computation. We resolve these barriers constructively on the unique paracompact four-dimensional regular honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$ of 24-cells meeting four per triangular ridge, whose ideal vertices carry flat cubic horospheres $\{4,3,4\}$. Realized over the exact integer-ring Lorentz isometry group on $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}]^5$, its $24$ orthogonal facet reflections generate a right-angled Coxeter group whose commuting-clique (Cartier--Foata) normal forms stay strictly regular across four cell-symmetry orbits. This yields linear-time bitmask neighbor reduction, an exact cubic shell recurrence with growth rate above eighteen, exact exponential wall-crossing counts, a Lean~4-certified Depth-2 spectral bracket trapping the adjacency spectral radius strictly inside $(11.4125, 11.4982)$ (certifying a normalized spectral gap above $13/25$), and an exact big-integer Depth-5 bracket narrowing the interval width below $0.0047$. Under the involutive Coxeter facet-exchange stream, we prove a sharp four-dimensional chirality threshold in Lean~4 ($k=1$ anchor obstructed even under proper rotations $W(F_4)^+$, ordered orthogonal $k=2$ anchors sharp with cyclic $C_4$ stabilizer under $W(F_4)^+$, and $k^*=4$ required under full $W(F_4)$) and construct two strictly spatially homogeneous, $576$-rotation $W(F_4)^+$-isotropic, globally bijective universal cellular automata with no special origin cell: a $5$-symbol weakly universal Morita rotary automaton and a $15$-symbol finite-seed strongly universal automaton coupling horosphere circuits to empty four-dimensional bulk geodesic counter rays, both proved bijective, equivariant, and conservative across all $5^{24}$ and $15^{24}$ cell states in Lean~4 and verified in live four-dimensional simulations. Finally, a certified case study of a minimal unitary ternary automaton over an exact ninth-root cyclotomic ring isolates seven algebraic obstructions to trackless ballistic computation.
\end{abstract}
