\begin{abstract}
Cellular automata on hyperbolic tilings face three structural barriers in higher dimensions: geometric region-splitting numerations lose formal-language regularity in three and four dimensions, non-amenable boundary growth breaks the Euclidean Moore--Myhill equivalence and makes global injectivity undecidable for arbitrary rules, and high local symmetry constrains trackless computation. We address these barriers constructively on the unique paracompact four-dimensional regular honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$ of 24-cells meeting four per triangular ridge, whose ideal vertices carry flat cubic horospheres $\{4,3,4\}$. Realized over the exact integer-ring Lorentz isometry group on $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}]^5$, its twenty-four orthogonal facet reflections generate a right-angled Coxeter group whose commuting-clique (Cartier--Foata) normal forms stay strictly regular across four cell-symmetry orbits. This yields linear-time bitmask neighbor reduction, an exact cubic shell recurrence with growth rate above eighteen, a Lean~4-certified Depth-2 spectral gap exceeding thirteen twenty-fifths, and an exact big-integer Depth-5 bracket narrowing the adjacency spectral radius below half a percent of a unit. Under the involutive facet-exchange stream, we prove a sharp four-dimensional chirality threshold in Lean~4 showing that two ordered orthogonal anchors suffice to orient planar gates across all $576$ proper cell rotations, and construct two strictly spatially homogeneous, rotation-invariant, globally bijective universal automata with no special origin cell: a five-symbol weakly universal Morita rotary automaton and a fifteen-symbol finite-seed strongly universal automaton coupling horosphere circuits to empty four-dimensional bulk counter rays. Finally, a certified case study of a minimal unitary ternary automaton isolates seven algebraic obstructions to trackless ballistic computation, leaving full reflection-invariant compilation, minimal bijective state counts, and isotropic surjectivity decidability open.
\end{abstract}
