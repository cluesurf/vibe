\section{Obstructions}
\label{sec:obstructions}

Removing localized chiral anchor motifs, bulk markers, and tracks raises a sharp structural question paralleling Margenstern's minimal-state boundaries on planar and three-dimensional hyperbolic grids~\cite{margenstern2018halting,margenstern2021outer,margenstern2023weighted,margenstern2024heptagrid}. When a reversible automaton on $\{3,4,3,4\}$ carries only three signed port tones $\{-1,0,+1\}$ and obeys the full $576$-element chiral symmetry group $W(F_4)^+$ (which acts faithfully on the $12$ lines as a $288$-element group), how far can uninterrupted trackless dynamics compute? We analyze one canonical unitary ternary quantum cellular automaton (QCA)~\cite{schumacher2004,arrighi2019} as a Lean~4-certified case study, isolating seven exact algebraic obstructions that separate trackless autonomous scattering from engineered universal circuits.

\subsection{Minimal Unitary Automaton}
\label{subsec:minimal-qca}

A quantum cellular automaton lifts a partitioned classical bijection to a local unitary matrix acting on finite-support Fock superpositions of port configurations while preserving exact $\ell^2$ norm and inner products. Restricting amplitudes to the cyclotomic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ (with $\zeta_9 = e^{2\pi i/9}$, $\omega = \zeta_9^3$, $\zeta_6 = e^{i\pi/3}$, and $\zeta_{18} = e^{i\pi/9}$) keeps every transition exact without floating-point approximation: the cube roots $\omega$ generate the unitary ternary line coin over $1/2$, while $\zeta_{18}$ and denominator $6 = 2 \cdot 3$ provide the smallest cyclotomic ring supporting a $W(F_4)$-equivariant $12 \times 12$ cross-line scattering matrix.

\begin{definition}[Minimal Chiral Unitary Automaton $U$]
\label{def:bare-u}
Assign each facet port a ternary tone $\tau \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$ (neutral quiescent $0$ plus two opposite active tones, optionally tagged by a finite conserved internal role label $r$). On each discrete step, evolve finite-support Fock states by composing seven site-local unitary layers with the exact bijective facet stream $S$ of~\eqref{eq:stream-bijection}:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:bare-u}
U = S \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}} \circ H_c \circ M_{\mathrm{meet}} \circ M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18}) \circ K_{\mathrm{flip}} \circ H_c \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}.
\end{equation}
Table~\ref{tab:u-layers} specifies each local factor. Because the three central layers $K_{\mathrm{flip}}$, $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$, and $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ mutually commute, the seven-layer site-local unitary $L_{\mathrm{bare}}$ is palindromic up to central commutation.
\end{definition}

Every factor in Table~\ref{tab:u-layers} is machine-checked unitary in Lean~4 over exact cyclotomic arithmetic, certifying exact global unitarity on finite-support Fock states for all $t \in \mathbb{N}$~\cite{pollard2026repo}:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:global-unitarity}
(U^{\dagger})^t U^t = U^t (U^{\dagger})^t = I.
\end{equation}
Just as Proposition~\ref{prop:global-bijection} secured classical bijectivity constructively, algebraic factorization guarantees global unitarity without entering the undecidable generic-CA regime~\cite{margenstern2008injectivity}.

\subsection{Seven Obstructions}
\label{subsec:seven-obstructions}

While factorization guarantees exact reversibility, combining $W(F_4)^+$ symmetry with~\eqref{eq:bare-u} imposes seven concrete obstructions on uninterrupted trackless evolutions $U^t$ (Table~\ref{tab:obstructions}). Two verification boundaries apply cleanly. Obstructions~1, 2, and 5 are proved directly in Lean~4 on the full infinite-honeycomb Fock unitary and $O^+(4,1;\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}])$ transport algebra~\cite{pollard2026repo}. For Obstructions~3, 4, 6, and 7, Lean~4 proves the exact cleared-denominator algebraic and matrix theorems on the reduced $6\times 6$ and $2\times 2$ operators, while extracting those reduced blocks from the full eight-layer evolution $U = S \circ L_{\mathrm{bare}}$ (verified in exact 4D big-integer arithmetic) and lifting rational inequalities to real trigonometric derivatives sit outside the finite Lean kernel. Because these proofs govern the specific operator $U$ in Definition~\ref{def:bare-u}, they form a certified case study rather than a blanket impossibility theorem over all $W(F_4)^+$-symmetric automata.

\paragraph{1. Line-parity superselection.}
Because every cross-line transition in $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ transfers matched pairs of excitations simultaneously off a line, the mod-$2$ parity $\pi_{\ell,\tau,r} \in \mathbb{Z}_2$ of excitations carrying tone $\tau$ and role $r$ on every line $\ell \in \{0,\dots,11\}$ is strictly invariant for all $t \in \mathbb{N}$. Lean~4 proves conservation of all $48$ parities in the $2$-role Fock kernel, and exact 4D big-integer runs over $3{,}515$ branches verify all $216$ parities for $9$ roles with zero discrepancies. An odd-parity excitation on line $\ell_0$ can therefore never evacuate onto another line.

\paragraph{2. Geodesic uniqueness.}
From~\eqref{eq:transport-ids}, any two distinct non-antipodal geodesics $\gamma_1, \gamma_2$ passing through $c_0$ satisfy
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:geodesic-uniq}
k_{a_1}^{m_1} c_0 = k_{a_2}^{m_2} c_0 \;\Longrightarrow\; m_1 = m_2 = 0
\end{equation}
for all integers $m_1, m_2$, and left-translation in the frame group $K$ extends $|\gamma_i \cap \gamma_j| \le 1$ to arbitrary pairs of geodesics. Because $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ acts as the identity (zero chiral phase angle) whenever fewer than four lines participate, any family of $m$ unguided geodesic rails produces at most
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:junction-cap}
J \le \left\lfloor \frac{m(m-1)}{12} \right\rfloor
\end{equation}
four-line chiral junctions, ruling out dense ballistic loop crossings.

\paragraph{3. Dispersion floor.}
Along any collinear geodesic, a $k$-body bound string ($k \ge 1$) has exact group velocity
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:group-vel}
v_k(p) = \frac{\sin p}{\sqrt{3 k^2 + \sin^2 p}}
\end{equation}
as a function of crystal momentum $p \in [-\pi,\pi]$. Its first and second derivatives obey the strict global floor
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:disp-floor}
\bigl(v'_k(p)\bigr)^2 + \bigl(v''_k(p)\bigr)^2 \ge \frac{9 k^4}{(3 k^2 + 1)^3} > 0, \qquad
\text{values } \Bigl(\frac{9}{64},\, \frac{144}{2{,}197},\, \frac{729}{21{,}952}\Bigr) \text{ for } k = 1, 2, 3.
\end{equation}
Consequently, the Rayleigh doubling time $T_R$ of a Gaussian wavepacket of initial width $\sigma_0$ scales quadratically ($T_R \propto \sigma_0^2$) at generic momenta and still scales cubically ($T_R \propto \sigma_0^3$) at inflection-point Airy momenta where $v'_k(-\pi/2) = 0$ (verified numerically in companion scripts~\cite{pollard2026repo}), preventing free pulses from preserving fixed width across arbitrary circuit distances.

\paragraph{4. Yang--Baxter collapse.}
On the $3! = 6$-dimensional three-body distinct-role permutation sector of a single ballistic line, the rational Yang $R$-matrix $\tilde{R}_{pq}(w) = w I - i P_{pq}$ (where $P_{pq}$ is the two-particle swap operator and $w$ is the spectral rapidity parameter) obeys the exact Yang--Baxter equation (three-body scattering factorizing identically into pairwise collisions) over $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ for all rapidities, verified symbolically in Lean~4. Integrability collapses $n$-packet interaction freedom to $3n - 1 < 4^n - 1$ parameters for all $n \ge 1$. Moreover, on the four-body exchange gate $G_{\mathrm{ex}}$ acting on the six-dimensional tone-conserving subspace, exact clearance of any non-computational leakage state $\psi_{\mathrm{leak}}$ (with factor $4$ clearing denominators into $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$) forces the pairwise rapidity difference $w_{AD} = u_A - u_D$ to vanish identically:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:gex-leak}
\langle \psi_{\mathrm{leak}} |\, 4 G_{\mathrm{ex}} \,| \psi_{\mathrm{leak}} \rangle = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; w_{AD} = u_A - u_D = 0.
\end{equation}
Equal rapidities prevent two ballistic packets from ever colliding in a single pass.

\paragraph{5. Unitary halting obstruction.}
Applying the Linden--Popescu obstruction~\cite{linden1998} directly to $U$ in Lean~4 shows that any unitary fixed point $U \psi_{\mathrm{halt}} = \psi_{\mathrm{halt}}$ cannot be reached from any distinct state $\phi \neq \psi_{\mathrm{halt}}$ in finite time $t$, because unitarity preserves inner products identically:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:halting-nogo}
\langle \psi_{\mathrm{halt}},\, U^t \phi \rangle = \langle \psi_{\mathrm{halt}},\, \phi \rangle.
\end{equation}
Any reversible universal system must therefore signal completion through a state-flag or oscillating boundary transition rather than an absorbing fixed point.

\paragraph{6. Diffraction ceiling and role pigeonhole.}
For any single full-line pair entering $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$, the probability of scattering onto any specific other line obeys
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:mpair-cap}
p_{\mathrm{hop}} = \frac{\sin^2(\pi/18)}{36}, \qquad \frac{837}{10^6} < p_{\mathrm{hop}} < \frac{838}{10^6}, \qquad p_{\mathrm{off}} = 11\,p_{\mathrm{hop}} \approx 0.9214\%.
\end{equation}
Of the $11$ other $D_4$ root lines ($3$ orthogonal and $8$ meeting at $60^\circ$), $10/11$ of every off-line event leaks isotropically onto unintended lines, and coherent $m$-line focusing remains below $1\%$ for all $1 \le m \le 11$. In parallel, encoding $n$ three-role logical qubits inside $9$ conserved role labels hits a constructive pigeonhole collision for every $n \ge 4$.

\paragraph{7. Mod-$2$ triality quotient.}
Reducing the exact $O^+(4,1;\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}])$ transport matrices modulo $2$ maps the $12$ antipodal line transports onto the Klein four-group $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$, partitioning the $12$ lines into three symmetry-equivalent $4$-line $D_4$ triality coordinate frames. Using this quotient, periodic $K_{\mathrm{flip}}$-wall crystals (alternating opposite-tone wall occupations that trigger $K_{\mathrm{flip}}$ reflection) trap excitations strictly inside $U$ without modifying the local rule: a single-cell crystal yields a period-$12$ standing compacton (a strictly compactly supported qubit obeying $U^{12} = +I_2$ with zero exact-arithmetic discrepancies), and an $8$-cell cavity confines waves with zero leakage over $200$ steps~\cite{pollard2026repo}. Likewise, a $3\mathrm{D}$ $\mathbb{H}^3$ periodic Bragg mirror (a layered reflecting crystal blocking wave propagation in all directions across an exact $5/8$ spectral band gap) suppresses transmission below $1.2 \times 10^{-17}$ across $16$ layers. Yet those mirrors are themselves periodic spatial backgrounds, confirming that localization inside $U$ still requires pre-structured spatial media.

\subsection{Symmetry Comparison}
\label{subsec:symmetry-comparison}

Table~\ref{tab:symmetry-frontier} places our four-dimensional $\{3,4,3,4\}$ architectures directly beside Margenstern's planar and three-dimensional hyperbolic cellular automata. Two structural distinctions stand out when every parameter is listed side by side. First, an outer-totalistic (OT) rule on the $12$-faced dodecagrid~\cite{margenstern2021outer} depends only on neighbor-state multiplicities and is therefore invariant under the full symmetric group $S_{12}$ of order $12! = 479{,}001{,}600$, far larger than both $A_5$ ($60$) and $W(F_4)^+$ ($576$). Second, a partitioned cellular automaton storing $|\Sigma|$ states on each of $24$ facets has $|\Sigma|^{24}$ composite states per cell ($5^{24} \approx 5.96 \times 10^{16}$ for $U_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)}$ and $15^{24} \approx 1.68 \times 10^{28}$ for $U_{\mathrm{strong}}^{(15)}$), which is not directly comparable to a $5$- or $6$-state non-partitioned CA storing one symbol per cell. Across the entire table, our $5$-symbol and $15$-symbol rules are to our knowledge the first strictly homogeneous, rotation-invariant, globally bijective universal automata in four-dimensional hyperbolic space (whereas prior uniform four-dimensional hyperbolic universality proofs by Margenstern are dissipative~\cite{margenstern2008vol2}). Note also that $U_{\mathrm{univ}}^{(5)}$ embeds Morita's planar circuits on a flat $\mathbb{Z}^2$ horosphere inside $\{3,4,3,4\}$, whereas $U_{\mathrm{strong}}^{(15)}$ genuinely couples a finite horosphere controller into unbounded $4\mathrm{D}$ bulk geodesics. Every confirmed universal construction still relies on localized spatial structures (tracks, markers, or anchor motifs), and trackless symmetric universality remains open.
