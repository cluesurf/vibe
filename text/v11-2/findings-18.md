# Findings 18, Predictions, Falsifiability, and the Scoreboard

What the theory predicts that could come out wrong, what would falsify it, and what it does not yet predict. A theory
that includes consciousness is still science if it makes real, checkable predictions, and it does. Companion to
`findings.md`. Cite `\cite{pollard2026vibetest}`.

## What would falsify it

The theory is exposed in specific ways. A measured LORENTZ VIOLATION at the lattice scale would kill it, and this is
already a live test, the linear violation parameter sits near zero and PASSES the gamma-ray-burst bound while a naive
flat lattice is EXCLUDED, so the substrate had to be hyperbolic to survive a real observation. If `sin^2 theta_W` did
NOT run to the measured value, the SO(10) embedding would be wrong. If the generation count were not three, the
triality reading would fail. If space-dimension selection gave something other than `d = 3`, the orbit argument would
fail. Why it matters, the theory can be wrong in named, checkable ways, it is not unfalsifiable, and one of its tests
(Lorentz violation) it already had to pass against data.

## The one unfalsifiable part, named honestly

The single posit is the feeling premise, that a vibe is genuinely felt. This is not testable from outside (no
experiment distinguishes a felt substrate from an identical unfelt one), and the paper marks it wherever it bears
weight. Everything ABOVE the premise, the structure, the dynamics, the numbers, is testable. Why it matters, the
theory isolates its one act of faith to a single line and builds a falsifiable tower on top of it, rather than
hiding untestable assumptions throughout.

## The open numbers, what it does not yet predict

Honesty about the gaps. The COUPLING CONSTANT (the fine-structure number near 1/137) is not derived, the rule fixes
the form of the dependence but not the value. The GENERATION MASSES are open, the count is forced but the three
families come out degenerate (Boyle's conjecture). DARK MATTER has a candidate geometric mechanism (nonlocal gravity
through the bulk) but no specific prediction. The exact match of `sin^2 theta_W` and the `{3,4,3,4}` growth recurrence
are open. CURVED-BULK gravity and the MEASUREMENT problem are open. Why it matters, the predictions that are made are
sharpened by an explicit list of the ones that are not, so a reader knows exactly where the theory commits and where
it is silent.
