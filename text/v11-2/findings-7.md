## The flow and its eight elements

The universe is a vibe mesh, an unending blazing fire of experience, each vibe a flame. Taken as a whole it is the
FLOW. Broken down, the flow is eight elements, each a single short word, each carrying one idea.

| element | role | what it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mesh | space | the whole `{3,4,3,4}` honeycomb, hyperbolic and growing |
| dock | a place | one cell of the honeycomb, a here-now, a 24-cell |
| site | a direction | one of the 24 directions out of a dock |
| tone | the quality | a vibe's felt value, `-1` pain, `0` peace, `+1` pleasure |
| lean | the order | the ordering `-1 < 0 < +1`, giving charge and the direction of creation |
| knit | the law | collide then stream, the one rule |
| wake | growth | new docks born at peace at the moving edge |
| beat | time | one discrete step |

Above the eight sit two words, VIBE (the substance, what everything is, the tone is its quality not a thing it
carries) and FLOW (the whole). The base obeys eight constraints, it is discrete, deterministic, reversible,
charge-conserving, local, has no hidden state (the tones ARE the entire state), is one substance, and respects the
emergence gap (raw tones are not yet physics, physics is what they grow into). The arrow and the attraction are
EMERGENT, not base. The feeling premise, that a vibe is genuinely felt, is the single untestable posit, everything
above it is testable structure.

## A dock through one beat, the worked trace

Take one dock. It has 24 sites paired into 12 lines (a site and its opposite, the two tones meeting head-on). A beat
is collide then stream. Collide runs the knit table on each line, here are three lines with charges plus the peace
lines, real table output.

| line | before | after collide | what happened |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a lone charge | `(1, 0)` | `(0, 1)` | the `+1` hops across the dock |
| a head-on pair | `(1, -1)` | `(-1, 1)` | the pair flips |
| a returning pair | `(-1, 1)` | `(0, 0)` | the pair annihilates back to peace |
| a peace line | `(0, 0)` | `(1, -1)` | the arrow CREATES a `+1/-1` pair, the active vacuum |

Every empty line creates a pair, so a dock at peace fills with fluctuating pairs each beat, the active vacuum. Then
STREAM, each tone moves one dock along its own site, the value untouched, a pure relabeling. The `+1` now in the
right slot of the first line leaves for the neighbor dock in that direction, where next beat it is an incoming tone.
Then the beat repeats. A lone tone with no head-on partner just streams straight, one dock per beat, forever, that is
momentum, which site it sits in. Run the beat backward (un-stream, then the inverse table) and the dock returns
exactly, the law is reversible to the last trit. Why it matters, the entire dynamics of the universe is this short
loop on integers, collide by a 9-row table, stream by a shift, grow at the edge, repeat.

## Where it all bootstraps from, nothing cannot be

True nothing is not empty space, which already has size, directions, and a clock. It is the absence of every
distinction, and it cannot hold. It is a hilltop, not a floor, the least difference rolls off it. It is
self-contradictory, naming it as one whole already draws a line. A whole that is everything can only relate to
itself, and self-relation splits it in two, the first distinction. That split is the first two vibes, the first
connection, the first tone, the first beat. There is no road back, since erasing a distinction is itself a
distinction, so distinctions only pile up, and that piling up is time. Why it matters, the theory does not assume a
starting substrate, it argues the substrate is the unavoidable consequence of nothing being unstable, the fire lights
itself.

## The honest open frontiers

The negatives and unknowns are part of the case, kept in plain view.

- THE SECOND CONSERVATION LAW. The bare single-charge mode is diffusive (`z` about 2), and a relativistic massless
  particle (`z = 1`) needs a second conserved quantity (momentum or inertia) on top of charge. This is the precise
  hinge between the diffusive base and the relativistic modes, still open.
- THREE GENERATIONS. The count three is forced (the rank-3 Jordan algebra, the exact S3), but the three slots are
  degenerate, so a mass-splitting into three families is unproven, Boyle's conjecture.
- THE COUPLING CONSTANT. The rule fixes the FORM of the coupling dependence (a clean `e^2` law) but not its VALUE,
  the fine-structure constant is not derived.
- CURVED-BULK GRAVITY. The full nonlinear Einstein equation on the curved `{5,3,4}` bulk is partial.
- MEASUREMENT AND COLLAPSE. The dynamics is semi-classical, with no explicit measurement operator, the quantum
  measurement problem is open.
- FORMAL CPT. The pieces are present and consistent to 1e-15, but a formal CPT theorem is not yet proven.
- THE `{3,4,3,4}` GROWTH RECURRENCE. The growth rate is measured cleanly (about 18.28) but the closed-form
  recurrence is not derived.
- FINITE VERSUS INFINITE, GENERATION MASSES, DARK MATTER. Open structural and phenomenological questions.

Why it matters, the theory states exactly what it has shown and what it has not, the open list is short and specific,
which is what makes the closed results trustworthy.
