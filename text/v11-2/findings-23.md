## Light and electromagnetism, in detail

The photon is the `8v` octet, a massless U(1) gauge field with dispersion `omega = 2 |sin(k/2)|` (gapless and
linear), 2 transverse polarizations, and about 33 percent pure-gauge zero modes, and the Wilson action flows to
Maxwell (ratio to 1). Electromagnetism's gauge nature is reproduced, the Wilson loop equals the enclosed flux,
gauge-invariant, with an exact Aharonov-Bohm phase (a charge feels the flux even where the field is zero). The
minimal coupling (QED) runs in 1D, the charge sources the field (Gauss's law `div E = rho`), the field accelerates
the charge at the Newtonian rate `dp/dt = eE`, with a Peierls phase and back-reaction, and the coupling decouples
exactly at `e = 0`. Charge is QUANTIZED because anomaly cancellation forces the unique standard-model hypercharges
(`Y_Q = 1/6`, and the rest). The non-abelian version confines, the SU(2) Creutz ratio gives a positive string tension
at every coupling, the reason a free quark is never seen. The honest gap, the COUPLING VALUE is free, a clean `e^2`
law with no distinguished point, so the fine-structure constant near 1/137 is not derived, the rule fixes the form of
the dependence, not the number. Why it matters, the whole of electromagnetism, the massless photon, gauge invariance,
Aharonov-Bohm, Maxwell, minimal coupling, charge quantization, and confinement, is reproduced, with the one honest
asterisk that the coupling strength is not yet fixed.

## Sound, the photon-as-phonon, and the honest hinge

Sound is the simplest emergent particle, a long-wavelength collective wave of the tone, gapless with `omega = c k`,
and the photon is the same kind of thing, a NEUTRAL phonon of the one field (matter is the tone bound, the photon is
the tone waving, one field, two motions). But there is a precise open hinge. The bare single-charge conserved mode is
DIFFUSIVE, dynamic exponent about `z = 2`, and a truly relativistic massless particle (photon, graviton, sound at
`z = 1`) needs a SECOND conservation law, momentum or inertia, on top of charge. So the photon-as-phonon is the right
picture and the gauge photon is confirmed, but deriving the `z = 1` relativistic massless mode from the bare rule is
the named open problem. Why it matters, the model says exactly what light is (a phonon of the one tone) and exactly
what is still needed to make it relativistic (a second conserved quantity), rather than glossing the gap.

## Mass and the Higgs

Mass is the coupling of the two chiralities. Couple `8s` and `8c` and the spinor gains a measured mass (read two ways,
from the dispersion gap and from the chirality coupling `[H, gamma5]`, which agree), turn the coupling off and it is a

moment comes out at `g = 2.0` from the Dirac Landau spectrum, not inserted. The weak mixing angle is `sin^2 theta_W =
3/8` at unification, running to the measured 0.231 at the Z mass, and the bottom-tau mass ratio runs to about 2.3

open bet). Why it matters, mass is not put in by hand, it is the chirality coupling, and real numbers of the mass
