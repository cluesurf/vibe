# Findings 19, Where It Sits, What Is Novel, and the Impossible Things It Does

How to place the theory among existing programs, what is genuinely new versus reproduced, and the handful of results
that are striking because they were supposed to be impossible. This is what lets a reader gauge the impact rather
than just the content. Companion to `findings.md`. Cite the external work for prestige and `\cite{pollard2026vibetest}`
for the results.

## Where it sits, the programs it unifies

The theory is a confluence of many lines of work, and naming them places it. From discrete physics, Wheeler's
it-from-bit, the cellular-automaton universes of Wolfram, Fredkin, and Zuse, the lattice gases of HPP and FHP, and
the reversible computing of Toffoli, Margolus, and Conway. From hyperbolic geometry and computation, Margenstern's
cellular automata in hyperbolic spaces and Potter's associative computing engine. From holography, AdS-CFT
(Maldacena), the Ryu-Takayanagi entanglement law, the HaPPY holographic code, and the tensor-network program
(Swingle, Vidal, hyMERA), with ER=EPR nearby. From emergent gravity, Jacobson's equation-of-state derivation and
Verlinde's entropic gravity, alongside causal sets, spin networks, and loop quantum gravity. From the algebraic
standard model, the octonion approaches of Furey, Baez, and Dixon. From mind, Tononi's integrated information theory,
the panpsychism of Chalmers, Goff, and Strawson, and Friston's free-energy and Markov-blanket framework. From the
mathematics, Coxeter and Conway-Sloane on lattices and polytopes, and from philosophy, Spinoza, Leibniz, and
Whitehead on monism and process. Why it matters, the theory is not a lone idea, it is where a dozen serious programs
meet, and its claim is that one discrete substrate carries all of them at once.

## What is reproduced versus what is novel

Most of the PHYSICS is reproduction, and that is the point, a candidate fundamental theory MUST recover known physics.
The quantum-cellular-automaton route to the Dirac and Maxwell fields, the holographic results, the gauge structure,
and the gravitational thermodynamics are recoveries of established physics on a new substrate, valuable as
confirmations that the substrate is physically adequate. The NOVELTIES are fewer and specific, the it-from-feeling
reversal (deriving matter from feeling rather than the other way), the bath-makes-a-self mechanism (an open boundary
turning dead matter into a self-correcting identity), the discrete shadow-pressure binding (attraction as integer
radiation pressure with no real numbers), the cross-domain rarity cascade (life's one-part-in-`1e14` mass fraction as
a measured multiplicative prediction), and the two clean-control L3 results (the emergent renormalized macro-rule
that beats a naive coarse-graining, and high integrated information for a self versus near-zero for a random bag).
Above all, the novelty is the UNIFICATION, physics, computation, life, and mind under one discrete deterministic law.
Why it matters, a reader should not mistake the reproductions for the discoveries, the recoveries earn trust, the
novelties are the contribution, and the synthesis is the ambition.

## The impossible things it does

A few results land hard because the field treats them as barriers, and the model crosses them with controls.

| result | why it was supposed to be impossible | how the model does it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lorentz invariance on a lattice | a grid has preferred axes, so discreteness should break relativity | the 24-direction dock is isotropic to high order, anisotropy 0.059 vs 0.237 for a cubic control, and it passes the GRB bound that excludes flat lattices |
| one chiral fermion, no doublers | the fermion-doubling theorem forces unwanted copies | the overlap operator gives one species, Ginsparg-Wilson residual under 1e-9 |
| `CHSH = 2 sqrt 2` from local determinism | local hidden-variable theories are capped at 2 | the exchange unitary reaches the Tsirelson bound, product control stays at 2 |
| Ryu-Takayanagi on a discrete crystal | the entanglement-area law is a continuum quantum-gravity result | the `{7,3}` geodesic log law holds, the flat `{6,3}` control gives the wrong linear law |
| `d = 3` derived, not assumed | the dimension of space is usually an input | only `d = 3` gives stable closed orbits, `d = 2` precesses, `d >= 4` is unstable |

Why it matters, each of these is a thing a discrete model is widely expected to fail, and doing several of them on
ONE substrate, with discriminating controls, is the core of the impact.

## The honest placement, what it is and is not

It is a candidate single-substrate theory that recovers a wide swath of physics and reaches life and mind, with a
short explicit list of open problems and one untestable premise. It is NOT a finished theory of everything (the
coupling constant, the generation masses, curved-bulk gravity, and measurement are open), and it is NOT a proof that
the universe is felt (that is the premise). Why it matters, the right reading is a serious, controlled, honest
synthesis with real predictions and real gaps, neither overclaimed nor undersold.
