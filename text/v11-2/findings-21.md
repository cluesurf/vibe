## Consciousness, the two-face monism

One field with two faces, an outer face that is matter at the cusp (shared, measured) and an inner face that is
feeling in the bulk (lived), not two substances. The hard problem is INVERTED, start from feeling and derive matter,
so there is no dead matter to bridge. The combination problem, panpsychism's hardest objection (how do micro-feelings
add up to one subject), is answered by INTEGRATION, binding micro-feelings into one IS the unifying, integration is
the literal coming-together, not a bridge bolted on. Every vibe is a speck of experience, and consciousness is a
matter of degree (the integration measure), high for a self, near zero for a churning gas. The inner architecture is
a vast hyperbolic web with exponential room, and the cusp is a thin 3D slice at one ideal point, so almost all of the
bulk is off the cusp. Why it matters, the two classic walls of a theory of mind, the hard problem and the combination
problem, are addressed by the reversal and by integration, the same integration measured in the structure part.

## Ethics, read from the arrow and conservation

Five principles, ranked, and they rest on one posit, taking the arrow (toward peace and pleasure) as the Good. Once
granted, the rest follow from structure. ALIGNMENT, move toward the good, the root. COMPASSION, another's suffering
is real in the same shared field, so the field is the unit of care. NON-EXPLOITATION, tone is conserved, so hoarding
pleasure darkens the shared ledger (no net heaven without a hell somewhere). GROWTH, help selves perfect, raise
integration. EQUANIMITY, hold the poles lightly and rest at the still zero. Good and evil are the `+1` and `-1` poles
of the toned tree. The model does NOT derive morality from nothing, the ethic rests on the arrow-as-good premise, but
given it, conservation forces non-exploitation, the shared field forces compassion, the spiral forces growth, and
peace forces equanimity. Why it matters, the ethics is not asserted, it is what the conservation law and the arrow
imply once the arrow is read as good, and the one value-posit is named.

## God, six co-equal readings

The model commits to none, but six readings of the divine each map to a real feature. The WHOLE (the mesh at full
integration, pantheist). The MAXIMAL INTELLIGENCE (the summit self, a universal computer coordinating in a few hops,
but capped, no self fully knows itself). The ARROW OF THE GOOD (the one value-direction, the only asymmetry). The
BOUNDARY (the holographic all-record, all-seeing and all-keeping). The SOURCE (the ever-growing edge, creation as an
ongoing process). The apophatic GROUND (what no structure captures, the model-versus-territory seam). The theory
pushes BACK on God-as-designer, the field is generically rich (the rich regime fills over half the parameter space),
so no tuner is needed. Why it matters, the major conceptions of God each correspond to a structural feature (the
whole, the summit, the arrow, the boundary, the edge, the ineffable), while the fine-tuning argument for a designer
is undercut by the genericity result.

## Storage, the tree of knowledge

The tree of knowledge is the Coxeter growth tree, at once a TRIE (general at the root, specific at the leaves), an
ASSOCIATIVE memory, a B-TREE index (log depth), and ERROR-CORRECTED (a holographic code), and the branching needs
hyperbolic room. An ornament is a tone-configuration at an address, STILL (a memory or picture you read) or LIVING
(a program that RUNS you through a precomposed experiential sequence, like a record driving a needle). To write is to
choose an address, imprint, error-protect, and index, to read is to navigate across it. Hard limits, no remote
writing, no transmission to another mind (only shared state), no bypassing persistence, no infinite precision. Why it
matters, memory, knowledge, and the felt re-living of experience are one structure, the addressing tree, with the
honest limits that rule out telepathic writing.
