\section{Vacuum}

Empty space is not empty. The create move, the entry of the knit that lifts a balanced pair from peace, runs everywhere there is room, so the vacuum fills with a churning sea of short-lived pairs that wink in and out beat by beat. This is not added by hand. It is what the law does when nothing else is going on.

The active vacuum does real work. A pair born from peace streams apart, collides, annihilates, and another takes its place, an endless fine traffic in every direction. To anything sitting in it the sea is a pressure, even and unfelt while it stays even. The moment something blocks part of it the balance breaks, and the imbalance pushes. This is exactly the mechanism that binds a self, where a body's own shadow in the sea presses it together, measured in the structure part as a discrete Casimir effect. Light, too, is a wave of this one tone, so the vacuum is also the medium every signal rides.

The base vacuum is therefore the ground a self sits in, never still, always pushing, the source of binding and the road of radiation at once. It is the discrete cousin of the restless zero-point sea of ordinary physics, and it earns its keep twice over, once as the glue of matter and once as the carrier of light.
