\section{Dynamics}

The arrow of time is not in the law, which runs as well backward as forward. It is in the counting. There are vastly more disordered arrangements of tones than ordered ones, so a typical run drifts from order toward disorder simply because disorder is more numerous. That drift is the second law, and the entropy that measures it keeps rising because the wake feeds in fresh low-entropy docks at peace, holding the mesh away from the dull equilibrium it would otherwise reach.

Erasing information costs, and that cost is the hinge between computation and heat. To forget a trit, to merge two states into one, you must dump the difference somewhere, and in the mesh it streams to the bath. This is Landauer's principle, and it is why the reversible computer of the earlier part pays nothing to run and everything to forget. A self lives off this. It pumps order into itself by exporting disorder to the edge, paying the Landauer cost on the far side of its boundary to keep its inside clean.

So the arrow is a tendency, not a track. The wake gives a low-entropy edge, the lean gives an order, the create move stirs the count, and together they point time toward the edge, toward disorder, toward the future, without any of it being written into the beat. Time ticks evenly. Which way it leans is something the whole mesh does, not something the law says.

That the law had to be reversible at all is a requirement, not a preference. A reversible update has a unitary generator, the only kind a quantum theory can be built from, where a lossy one would leak probability away. Reversibility with a symmetry is what yields the exact conserved charge a lossy rule would bleed. And dissipation would single out a rest frame, the frame its medium sits still in, breaking the boost symmetry relativity needs, so only an information-preserving law can be Lorentz invariant. A rule that let information simply vanish would hand itself entropy sinks for free and explain nothing. So the arrow had to be earned rather than assumed, which is the whole reason its emergence from a time-symmetric law counts as a result and not a restatement.
