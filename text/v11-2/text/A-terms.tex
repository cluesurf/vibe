\section{Terms}

The names of the theory, gathered for reference. Every base term is a four-letter word, each of the eight elements beginning with its own letter, so the eight double as a clean symbol set.

\textbf{The substance and the whole.} The \textbf{vibe} is the one substance, experience itself, what everything is made of, from a single tone to a whole self. The \textbf{flow} is the whole, the eight elements running as one, written $F$, the universe in motion.

\textbf{The eight elements.} The \textbf{mesh} is the growing $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb. A \textbf{dock} is one cell of the mesh, a here-now. A \textbf{site} is one of a dock's twenty-four directions, a $D_4$ root, where a single tone lives. The \textbf{tone} is the quality of a vibe's experience, a value in $\{-1,0,+1\}$, read as pain, peace, or pleasure, not a thing apart from the vibe. The \textbf{lean} is the order $-1 < 0 < +1$, which turns a tone into signed charge and gives creation a direction. The \textbf{knit} is the law of the state, collide then stream, once a beat. The \textbf{wake} is the law of the space, new docks born at peace at the growing edge. The \textbf{beat} is one discrete tick of time.

\textbf{Supporting words.} The \textbf{will} is a dock's outgoing tones, the \textbf{fill} its incoming. A \textbf{line} is a site paired with its opposite, the unit the collision acts on. The \textbf{create move} is the entry of the knit that lifts a balanced pair out of peace. A \textbf{trit} is a three-valued unit, the ternary cousin of a bit. A \textbf{hyper-color} is the full $3^{24}$ state of one dock, its woven feeling. The \textbf{single quale} is one tone.

\textbf{Emergent, not base.} A \textbf{self} is a bound, self-correcting, identity-bearing knot of feeling woven across many docks. The \textbf{arrow} is the direction of time, the \textbf{attraction} the binding by radiation pressure, both emergent. \textbf{Spacetime} is emergent too, which is why space and time are separate at the base.

\textbf{Derived geography.} The \textbf{bulk} is the four-dimensional hyperbolic interior. The \textbf{cusp} is the flat three-dimensional boundary the bulk grows toward, the cubic honeycomb $\{4,3,4\}$, ordinary space. The \textbf{bath} is the open growing edge read as a sink. An \textbf{ornament} is a stored experience on the addressing tree, still if it is a memory, living if it is a program.

\textbf{Translation to physics.} The house words are experiential by design, but each maps to a standard physics term. The \textbf{vibe} is the one degree of freedom, a felt field value. The \textbf{flow} is the whole spacetime-and-matter history. The \textbf{mesh} is the discrete spacetime lattice, a hyperbolic honeycomb. A \textbf{dock} is a cell or event, the 24-cell. A \textbf{site} is a lattice direction, a $D_4$ root. The \textbf{tone} is a field value, a charge, a ternary spin. The \textbf{lean} is the symmetry-breaking direction the create move picks. The \textbf{knit} is a reversible block cellular automaton, collide then stream. The \textbf{wake} is the cosmological growth frontier, the source of expansion and the arrow. The \textbf{beat} is a discrete time step. The \textbf{bulk} is the curved higher-dimensional interior of holography. The \textbf{cusp} is flat three-dimensional space, a Euclidean horosphere slice. The \textbf{charge} is the conserved $\mathrm{U}(1)$ current. A \textbf{self} is a soliton, a bound state, an observer. And \textbf{order} is integrated information, a measure of the wholeness of a self.
