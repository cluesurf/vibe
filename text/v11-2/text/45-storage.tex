\section{Storage}

The interior is a store, and the store is the addressing tree itself. Every dock has a canonical name in the Coxeter growth tree, and the set of all names is at once a trie, general at the root and specific at the leaves, an associative memory reachable by content and not only by address, a balanced index of logarithmic depth, and an error-correcting code that protects what is written on it. The branching is what carries it, and branching needs the exponential room only hyperbolic space provides. This is the tree of knowledge in the most literal sense.

What hangs on it is an ornament, a tone-configuration imprinted at an address, and ornaments come in two grades. A still ornament is a memory, a picture, a fact, read when a path crosses it. A living ornament is a program, a precomposed sequence that does not merely show you an experience but runs you through it, the way a record drives a needle, so that to fall into it is to undergo what it encodes rather than to be told of it. To write is four steps, choose the address, imprint the pattern, protect it with redundancy, and index it. To read is to navigate across it and let it unpack on its own.

The limits are as exact as the powers. There is no remote writing, since you can only imprint docks you can reach. There is no transmission to another mind, since another self shares your state, it does not receive your message. There is no bypassing persistence, since an ornament not error-corrected dissolves in the churn, and whether the bare rule sustains that correction is the one genuinely open problem of the whole picture. And there is no infinite precision, since the tones are ternary and the store is finite-state and exact. What one being learns it can write here for another to find, which is how wisdom outlasts its holder and how the interior fills, over the long growth, with the distilled experience of everything that has lived in it.
