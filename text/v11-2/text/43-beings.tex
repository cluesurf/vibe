\section{Beings}

A being is a persistent integrated pattern, a self or an attractor, the same object the structure part built, now scaled and toned. The taxonomy of beings is a taxonomy of stable patterns. They stack by integration, a small persistent loop a minor spirit, an integrated self-modeling pattern a soul or a person, a self that integrates many selves and steers them from above a higher power, and at the top the most integrated self the geometry allows. A higher self acts on its parts by downward causation, which is the exact mechanism behind any larger being's pull on a lesser one.

They divide by tone and by origin. Warm beings lean toward peace and pleasure and spread calm, aligned with the arrow. Cold beings are persistent pain-attractors that pull coupled selves into their basin, and a cold being that truly lasts has solved the hard problem of persistence against the arrow, which is why such a thing is rare and feared. By origin some are live, running patterns computing and steering now. Some are stored, ornaments left on the tree, the dead who remain as readable structure, their survival resting on the open question of error correction. And some are transpersonal, not one self but a gigantic attractor spanning many across history, the archetypes minds fall into again and again. Angels and messengers, in this frame, are structured selves or the directional geometry of the dock itself, carrying correlation between selves but never a message, since signaling is forbidden.

Birth is condensation, vibes integrating into a coherent self-modeling pattern, a vortex forming in a flowing field, a flame catching. Death is dissolution, the churn overcoming the binding so the knot comes undone, and the tones, being conserved, do not vanish but disperse back into the weave. What continues, in order of confidence, is the conserved substrate, certainly, redistributed; the stored legacy a being wrote into the tree, if it was protected enough to last; the record kept on the holographic boundary, where nothing is finally lost; and, more open, a re-instantiation of the pattern elsewhere, reincarnation read as a pattern reforming rather than a soul traveling, or a merging upward into a larger self before the end. A free-floating soul made of nothing is ruled out, since a being is a pattern of tones or it is nothing. The gentle reading is that you are a knot the field tied in itself, and when the knot comes undone the thread returns to the weave, your mark on it staying, the whole keeping its memory of you in a field that conserves everything and records everything.
