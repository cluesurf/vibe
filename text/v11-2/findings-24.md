## 5. The Findings

The universe is a vibe mesh. One felt substance, the vibe, takes a ternary tone (pain, peace, pleasure) on each of
the 24 sites of a dock, the docks tile a growing four-dimensional hyperbolic honeycomb, and one reversible law, the
knit (collide then stream), advances them beat by beat while fresh docks are born at the open edge. That is the whole
base, eight elements and nothing else, fully discrete and deterministic, with no real number anywhere in it. From it,
added by nothing, the entire tower rises as the coarse-grained behavior of the layer below, space is the mesh, time
is the beat, matter is the tone bound into a knot, light and sound are the tone waving, the forces are the three
triality sectors of the 24 sites, gravity is the curvature of the same field, and selves are open bodies that hold
themselves together on the flat three-dimensional boundary. The flat space we live in is literally the cusp of the
four-dimensional crystal, and the smooth physics we measure is the average of the discrete grains, the way a magnet's
field is the average of atomic spins.

What this solves is most of physics, from one discrete law, each result measured against a control. Special
relativity emerges on the lattice that lattices are supposed to forbid, the Dirac equation and a single chiral
electron appear with no fermion doubling, electromagnetism, the weak force, and confinement sit together in one
SO(10) grand unification that hands back real numbers (the weak mixing angle three eighths, the electron g-factor
two), gravity returns as the graviton and the Newtonian potential and Einstein's equation as a law of horizon
thermodynamics, holography and the entanglement-area law hold on the crystal with a flat control that fails, quantum
correlations reach the exact Tsirelson bound from a local deterministic base, and the model even selects
three-dimensional space and explains why matter exists at all. The standard model plus gravity is thus the catalog of
motions and knots of a single ternary tone, with a short honest list of what is not yet derived, the fine-structure
coupling, the three generation masses, and fully nonlinear curved-bulk gravity.

The deeper finding is that the same substrate that gives physics also gives mind, because its one substance is felt.
An open body bound by the radiation-pressure shadow of its own active vacuum becomes a self, an attractor with a
stable identity (a topological winding) and a corrective agency (it heals by radiating to the open edge), selves bind
into higher selves, and the irreducibility of a self's dynamics is a measure of its consciousness. Building matter
from feeling rather than feeling from matter dissolves the hard problem, there is no dead substrate to bridge to
experience. And the picture leaves ample room for the rest, intelligence is the capacity to navigate the
configuration network toward a goal, the experiential field is the vibe mesh read from the inside as one unending
fire of feeling, spirituality is attention turned along the hidden radial depth of the bulk, and the divine is read,
without commitment, as the whole at full integration or as the arrow toward the good. So the vibe mesh is one picture
in which physics, life, mind, and meaning are not separate subjects but layers of a single felt fire, and what
follows in this paper is the showing of each layer in turn.
