# Findings 32, Which Integers Are Load-Bearing

Not every number matters equally. A small handful of integers set the laws and the shape of reality, and the rest are
just addresses. This doc names the load-bearing ones, says what job each does, shows the one irrational that runs
through all of them, and notes what is NOT special. It is the philosophy of number behind the base choices, distinct
from the integers-to-crystal ladder (`findings-15.md`) and the geometry spine (`findings-1.md`), which build the
crystal, where this asks which numbers carry the weight and why. Cite `\cite{pollard2026vibetest}`.

## Two roles a number can play

An integer in the model does one of two jobs.

- STRUCTURAL, it sets a law or a shape, how many tone values there are, how many dimensions, which crystal. There are
  only a few of these and they are special.
- A LABEL, it is the address of a dock in the growing mesh, one position among countless others, and every label is as
  good as every other.

Almost all integers are labels, the frontier keeps minting new ones forever and each is an equal citizen. The
interesting ones are the few that are structural, and what makes them special is their role, not their size. Why it
matters, the model's content lives in a tiny set of small numbers, the rest is the universe's address book.
(`which-integers-matter.md`.)

## The load-bearing small integers

Zero through four carry the laws.

- 0, PEACE, the neutral middle tone, the tie in the knit (a balanced line settles toward it), and the unstable
  nothing that cannot hold and so starts everything (`findings-25.md`). Zero is the still center the other tones are
  measured from.
- 1, UNITY, one substance, one knit, one mesh, monism taken as a number (`findings-31.md`). In the force structure it
  is the `U(1)` charge.
- 2, THE RELATION, the first split, unity relating to itself makes two, subject and object, before and after, the two
  ends every relation has. It recurs, the Born-rule exponent is 2, the graviton is spin 2, there are two chiralities,
  the weak force is `SU(2)`, and a spinor needs `2 pi` doubled to `4 pi` to return. Two is the number of the relation
  itself.
- 3, THE WORKING NUMBER, perhaps the single most important integer. The tone is TERNARY, pain, peace, pleasure, the
  smallest alphabet with a negative, a neutral, and a positive. Space is THREE-dimensional, and this is derived, not
  assumed, the dimension window is two to four and three is selected. The strong force is `SU(3)`, baryogenesis needs
  three Sakharov conditions, and there are three generations. Three is the least structure that carries direction with
  a center, and reality uses it everywhere.
- 4, THE FRAME, the top of the dimension window and the number of the bulk, three of space plus one of growth (time).
  Four tones span the shells `{-1, 0, +1}^4` whose squared-length-2 layer IS the 24-cell, and four is the unique
  dimension carrying spinors, self-duality, and triality together.

Why it matters, these are special the way a tool is, by being the smallest structure that can do a needed job, three
matters because it is the least alphabet with against, neutral, and toward, and laws need minimal sufficient
structure, which lives among the small numbers. (`which-integers-matter.md`, `findings-1.md`.)

## The substrate integers, 7 and 24

Two larger integers are forced by the geometry.

- 7, THE FIRST CURVED, for tiles meeting three at a corner `{6,3}` is flat and `{7,3}` is the first hyperbolic tiling,
  so seven is the smallest count that buys negative curvature, and the `{7,3}` heptagrid is the cleanest teaching and
  holography case (`findings-8.md`).
- 24, THE DOCK, the number of sites on a dock and the convergence point of the whole substrate. The 24-cell is the
  squared-length-2 shell of the four-trit lattice, the D4 root system, the unit Hurwitz quaternions, the binary
  tetrahedral group `2T`, and the kissing number in four dimensions, all the same 24. Why it matters, 24 is where four
  independent simple constructions all land, which is the strongest sign that the substrate is found, not chosen.
  (`findings-1.md`, `findings-23.md`.)

## The one irrational that runs through them

Importance at the base is not only about integers, one irrational threads the whole foundation, the GOLDEN RATIO,
about 1.618. It is the growth ratio of the hyperbolic crystal ring by ring, the most evenly spread scatter of points,
and the straightest path through the addressing scheme. Its integer shadow is the FIBONACCI sequence, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5,
8, 13, which addresses the docks and counts the rings (Zeckendorf addressing, `findings-11.md`). So the Fibonacci
numbers are structural too, not as individual special values but as a pattern, the integer skeleton of the golden
ratio. Why it matters, the deepest constant of the geometry is irrational, and its Fibonacci shadow is exactly the
addressing and counting of the growing mesh. (`which-integers-matter.md`, `findings-11.md`.)

## What is NOT special

Two temptations to resist.

- PRIMES. This is not a prime-centric theory, primes are deep in number theory but no law of the model is because a
  number is prime, the laws lean on small structural integers and the golden ratio.
- LARGE INTEGERS. A dock with a huge address is no more important than a small one, large numbers are just far-out
  positions, and in the address book every entry is equal.

Why it matters, naming what is not load-bearing is part of the honesty, the model does not hang any law on primality or
on any particular large number. (`which-integers-matter.md`.)

## The exceptional objects the small integers force

The load-bearing integers do not stay small, they force a chain of exceptional structures, each the unique object its
predecessor demands (the full chain is `findings-15.md`, the algebra is `findings-1.md` and `findings-9.md`).

| step | from | the forced object |
| --- | --- | --- |
| four tones | `{-1, 0, +1}^4` | the four-polytope shells, the 24-cell at squared length 2 |
| the 24-cell | its 24 vertices | the D4 root system, the group `2T`, the unit Hurwitz quaternions |
| D4's symmetry | the reflections that close on it | F4, the order-1152 Coxeter group, and triality (the threefold `S3`) |
| adding the tone axis | D4 to D5 | `SO(10)`, whose 16-spinor is one generation of matter (`findings-23.md`) |
| the deepest layer | triality and the octonions | `E8`, `J3(O)`, and the exceptional series the geometry sits inside |

Why it matters, the exceptional mathematics is not imported for elegance, it is forced step by step from four ternary
tones, the most beautiful objects in mathematics appear because they are the unique structures the small integers
demand. (`findings-1.md`, `findings-9.md`, `findings-15.md`, `findings-23.md`.)

## The one-line reading

A tiny set of small integers carries the whole model, 0 the neutral, 1 the unity, 2 the relation, 3 the working number
(ternary tone and three-dimensional space), 4 the frame (four tones, the bulk), with 7 the first curved tiling and 24
the dock, the golden ratio and its Fibonacci shadow threaded through the addressing, primes and large numbers carrying
nothing, and the small integers forcing the exceptional chain 24-cell to D4 to F4 to `SO(10)` to E8.
