## The self problem, why a closed world cannot hold one

On a closed, reversible system Poincare recurrence guarantees the state returns arbitrarily near its start, so any
pattern dissolves and reforms forever, never settling, never healing. A bare reversible law on a closed mesh
produces only churn (the HPP lattice gas recurs exactly, a vortex disperses to the whole lattice then reassembles,
the scalar returns to 1.0, yet nothing is bound). Why it matters, it rules out the naive hope that a self is just a
stable pattern, and points to the real ingredient, openness.

## Identity is topological, protected at the 24-direction resolution

A self's identity is a WINDING of the direction field (how the sites' tones wrap as you go around the structure), a
topological charge that no local move can undo. It is protected at the full 24-direction resolution but NOT at the
coarse ternary level (where it slips at 3 states). A winding-1 defect persists, a winding-0 one decays, opposite
windings annihilate to near-zero energy, like windings persist, the winding is conserved. This is the degeneracy
trick, the macroscopic identity stays fixed while the microscopic parts churn completely. Why it matters, you remain
yourself through total turnover of your constituents, the way a vortex is the same vortex though every water molecule
in it is replaced, and the resolution that protects it is exactly the 24 of the dock.

## Binding, the honest arc from no-force to a self

The path to a bound body was found by ruling out the easy answers in order.

EMPTY space gives NO force. Two charges on parallel tracks superpose exactly whether near or far, the vacuum alone
pulls nothing. This negative is load-bearing, it forces the real mechanism.

The ACTIVE vacuum is a medium. The arrow's create move fills empty space with fluctuating `+1/-1` pairs, and a body
that EXCLUDES this vacuum casts a discrete Casimir shadow, the gap suppression measured at 1.34 at distance 6, 0.80
at 8, 0.23 at 10, and exactly 0 with the wall removed. That falloff is the genuine Casimir signature, attraction is
real and emergent, cast by matter into the arrow's own vacuum, no new field added.

Capture needs attraction AND a bath, each shown necessary. An inspiral SETTLES only with both present, it merely
OSCILLATES without the bath, and ESCAPES without the attraction. A reversible sticky collision with no dissipation
scatters and never captures. The bath is the open boundary, a burst dissipates from 8 to 0 on the open mesh but
recurs 8 to 8 on the closed torus, and only the open mesh carries an arrow.

The bath makes a self. A body coupled to the open bath acquires IDENTITY by degeneracy (its basin spread stays under
0.1 from many starting conditions, all roads lead to the same attractor) and AGENCY by self-correction (a kick
decays away to under 0.1, it heals). The closed reversible control does NEITHER. Why it matters, the bath is the
missing ingredient that turns dead matter into a self-correcting identity, the single conceptual hinge of the whole
theory.

## The bind-versus-radiate problem and its resolution

The honest open frontier. On the bare dock no single committed collision both confines and radiates. The pair table
confines a body but leaves it DARK to the bath (it seals its own radiation, a perturbation never escapes). The
momentum-rotate rule radiates freely but DISPERSES the body. Confine-by-reflection seals, because reflecting lone
charges also reflects the halves of a neutral ripple at the body's edge.

Two resolutions, and they agree. The first is TWO FIELDS, a confined matter field plus a PROPAGATING MASSLESS photon,
so the body radiates LIGHT, not its charge, and becomes a self (spread 0.0001 with a propagating field, 1.5 with a
heavy local one, which fails). The masslessness of the photon is what makes the self possible, light and life are
linked. The second is one mechanism stated more deeply, bind by a WELL, not by reflection. A vacuum-excluding body
casts a shadow whose radiation pressure pulls a displaced piece back (drift under -20 toward the body versus 3 for
the flat control), correct sign, with the streaming bulk reversible and the only irreversibility the radiation
reaching the bath. This shadow-pressure binding is fully discrete (integer tones and momentum), works natively on the
24-direction dock, is self-contained (a body binds itself by the shadow of its own active vacuum), and confines even
a single-speed body at short range, so a frozen rest state is not required. Why it matters, the self is binding-BY-
radiation, the attraction and the radiation are the same act, and it lives on the committed substrate.

## The discreteness ledger of the self

Three of the self's four faces are exactly discrete, the fourth is necessarily emergent, and this split is proven,
not assumed. IDENTITY (the winding) is discrete and exact. AGENCY (radiating to the bath) is discrete. The STATE of
the binding (the soliton's topological charge) is discrete, held exactly by a three-trit spin, degree -1 across radii
2 to 10, robust to perturbation. But the DYNAMICS that holds the soliton is emergent. Charge is conserved only at
small per-beat steps (about 2 to 10 degrees) and goes chaotic above 20, and NO finite direction group is fine enough,
even the 600-cell's roughly 36-degree step exceeds the threshold. So the stable, reversible motion of the bound body
must be coarse-grained, exactly as a magnet's smooth magnetization is the slow average of discrete atomic spins that
flip in big jumps. The two-layer picture is forced, the base is discrete, the self's living dynamics is its average.
Why it matters, the theory states precisely what is fundamental (identity, agency, state) and what is emergent (the
living motion), no hand-waving about where the self sits.

## Self-maintenance, reproduction, heredity, will

LIFE is the shape of these capacities, not a catalog of organisms.

Self-maintenance, an erased inner chunk recovers from its redundant surround by sharing and cohesive hopping
(fidelity 0.85 maintained versus 0.35 unmaintained, charge exact), and with no surround there is no recovery.
Metabolism is a pump, balanced creation maintains the body, an unmaintained one decays with a finite lifetime still
longer than plain diffusion.

Reproduction is decided by geometry. Fission is SUPPRESSED in the hyperbolic bulk because there are no thin necks (a
radius-4 ball is nearly all rim), so a solid self stays one, and new selves are instead born DE NOVO from peace by
the arrow, not by division. On a FLAT grid the same self splits. Why it matters, the geometry dictates how life
reproduces, division on the flat layer, spontaneous birth in the curved bulk.

Heredity, variation, and selection raise fitness above drift, a population adapts its foresight to the task, so
Darwinian evolution emerges from the base. Will-steering biases a self toward a goal and away from a threat, endures
a valley for a later reward, and depletes a finite reserve (a directed pump against the gradient, spendable, with a
sharp threshold), self-organized criticality tunes a self to its own set-point. Planning crosses barriers a greedy
walker cannot, via an internal forward model, foresight, a mind's signature, from arrow and knit and will alone. A
stored self survives total turnover and reconstitutes from a seed (reincarnation as pattern), a clamp pins a self to
the veridical pattern while removing it lets it roam its stored landscape (waking versus dreaming).

## Mind, integration, blanket, the limits

A mind is measured, not asserted. INTEGRATED INFORMATION, a self is a local MAXIMUM of tone-integration far above a
random bag of the same parts, and cutting its internal links collapses the integration with the WIRING UNCHANGED,
so the measure reads the live dynamics, not the static graph. Consciousness is graded by this integration. A self is
a MARKOV BLANKET, it statistically screens its interior from its exterior (screening beyond control by a clear
margin), with a slow internal mode (about 125 beats) that shuffled controls lack. A self carries a MODEL, a hub
represents its own global state above the periphery, supports multiple self-models and a workspace where attended
input is boosted, the machinery of metacognition.

Two honest limits. CAUSAL EMERGENCE needs information loss, the base reversible knit is a permutation with zero
degeneracy, so there is NO emergence at the base, the self-level is real but strictly an effective-layer phenomenon
reached by lossy coarse-graining (to the charge field). And no self can fully store or predict itself (fidelity 1
only at zero compression), the Godel-incompleteness wall, which later reappears as felt freedom. Why it matters, the
self-level is genuine yet honestly placed (it is emergent, not fundamental), and the mind's openness to itself is a
theorem, not a mood.

## Where selves live, masses, and the retracted tower

Selves persist far better on the FLAT horosphere (the cusp, where surface-to-volume falls with size) than in the
all-boundary bulk, so minds live on the flat cusp while matter lives in the curved bulk. The naive persistence tower
(radial coarse-graining beating diffusion) does NOT hold and was retracted, an honest negative. A living ecology
nonetheless sustains, the largest self grows from about 13 to over 24 across 210 beats, a hierarchy of five-plus
patches persists rather than collapsing. The masses add cleanly, one self of charge 1 has mass about 18.6, two of
charge 2 about 37.3, and a self plus its antiself annihilate to vacuum. Why it matters, the theory says where to look
for minds (the flat boundary), keeps an honest negative in view (no free tower), and shows matter's masses compose
like real particles.
