# Findings 16, The Thesis, the Method, and How to Read the Results

What the paper actually claims, the one posit it rests on, and how to weigh each result. Without this frame a reader
cannot tell a derivation from a conjecture, or grasp why the whole thing is falsifiable despite being a theory that
includes consciousness. Companion to `findings.md`. Cite `\cite{pollard2026vibetest}`.

## The thesis, it from feeling

Wheeler's slogan was "it from bit," matter from information. This reverses it, IT FROM FEELING. The one substance is
the vibe, an experience, and its tone is the quality of that experience, modeled as a value in `{-1, 0, +1}`, pain,
peace, pleasure. Everything else, space, matter, force, life, mind, is built from vibes by one law. The move is a
reversal because it starts from the felt and derives the physical, rather than starting from the physical and trying
to reach the felt. Why it matters, the direction of construction is the whole novelty, and it is what lets the theory
address consciousness at all.

## Why the reversal dissolves the hard problem

The hard problem is that an outside description never sums to an inside, you can specify every neuron and never reach
the feeling, the felt cannot be built from the unfelt. Most theories start from unfelt matter and hit this wall. By
starting from feeling and deriving matter, there is no dead matter to bridge, the inside is the base and the outside
(matter, the cusp) is the derived face. This is the two-face monism, one field with an outer measured face and an
inner felt face, not two substances. Why it matters, the single hardest problem in philosophy of mind is not solved
by a new mechanism but dissolved by reversing the order of construction, which is the paper's deepest claim and its
boldest.

## The three claims

The paper makes exactly three. ONE, there is a single law on a single substrate (the eight elements, the knit on the
`{3,4,3,4}` mesh), discrete and deterministic. TWO, it reproduces known physics (relativity, the Dirac and Maxwell
fields, particle structure, gravity, holography, cosmology, quantum correlations). THREE, it climbs to life and mind
(selves, evolution, integration, the felt layer). The feeling premise, that a vibe is genuinely felt, is the ONE
untestable posit, and everything above it is testable structure. Why it matters, the claims are bounded and explicit,
so the reader knows what success would mean, one law, reproducing physics, reaching mind, on one premise.

## The method, one experiment per claim

Every serious claim is backed by a computation, run under strict rules. The base is DISCRETE and DETERMINISTIC, with
no randomness, so a larger test means a larger SIZE, never a different random seed (vary size, not seeds). Each claim
is graded for internal use on a depth rubric from L0 to L3, L0 a sanity check, L1 confirming known math, L2
reproducing known physics, L3 a novel claim with a discriminating control, a quantity measured from the dynamics, and
a number that could come out wrong. The grades are defined once and never printed in the paper body, they only tell
the writer how much weight a result carries. The evidence base is about 461 experiments across 18 categories,
depth-graded, with ZERO forced failures (no result was twisted to pass). Why it matters, the theory is not argued, it
is computed, under rules strict enough that a reader can trust the green results and see exactly which are deep.

## How to read any result, the four statuses

Every claim is one of four, and the paper marks which in plain prose. DERIVED, it follows by mathematics (the double
cover in 2T, the Born exponent from additivity). COMPUTED or CONFIRMED, an experiment measured it (the g-factor 2.0,
the Casimir suppression, the growth counts). PARTIAL, it works in a reduced setting or with a caveat (three
generations have the structure but degenerate masses). OPEN, it is not shown (the coupling constant, curved-bulk
Einstein, measurement). Why it matters, a reader who keeps these four in mind never mistakes a conjecture for a
theorem, which is the honesty the whole case depends on.

## The honesty spine, the negatives are part of the case

The negatives are reported proudly because they shape the real result. Empty vacuum gives NO force (which forces the
active-vacuum mechanism). A closed system only churns (which forces the open bath). Naive radial coarse-graining
gives NO persistence tower (retracted). Fission is suppressed in the bulk (so selves are born de novo). The three
generation slots are degenerate (so the count is forced but the masses are not). The bare single-charge mode is
diffusive (so a second conservation law is needed for massless particles). The two crown novelties with clean
controls are the emergent renormalized macro-rule (it beats a naive coarse-graining, with a frustrated control that
fails) and high integrated information for a self versus near-zero for a random bag (with a control). Why it matters,
a theory that hides its negatives cannot be trusted, and here the negatives are load-bearing, each one rules out an
easy answer and points to the real mechanism.
