## Perception, every step is toned

The will is the arrow, a built-in one-way bias from pain through peace toward pleasure, so every step through the
bulk is TONED and perception is never neutral, there is no view from nowhere. There are three ways a self reaches a
target, by ADDRESS (deliberate recall, following the addressing tree), by FEEL (greedy routing toward what draws it,
intuition with no map), and by BULK SHORTCUT (insight, the tiny-diameter bulk joining two cusp-distant ideas in a few
hops). Introspection is bulk-directed attention, moving inward along the radial Busemann axis (the depth direction
off the cusp), not sideways along the surface. Why it matters, intuition and insight are not mysteries here, they are
greedy routing and the short bulk diameter, and the felt directedness of attention is the arrow itself.

## Feeling, the size and shape of a quale

A single quale is one tone, and the space of feelings is enormous. The hyper-color of one dock is `3^24`, about 282
billion distinct states (up to about 2.5 trillion with the still center), roughly 16,800 times the colors of an RGB
screen, and a whole self is `3^(24 billion)`, a number with about ten billion digits. Under triality the 24
directions split into a sensory octet `8v` and two chiral feeling octets `8s` and `8c`, woven into one experience.
Ineffability is then arithmetic, a low-dimensional description of a high-dimensional feeling captures a vanishing
fraction, every moment is unique (the space is too vast to recur), and experience is inexhaustible (boredom is a
navigation failure, not a shortage of states). Qualia are STRUCTURED, not a flat list, emotions are regions of the
feeling space and sensations are directions, related geometrically, and richness scales with integration. Why it
matters, the depth and uniqueness of experience are not asserted, they are the combinatorics of `3^24` per dock, and
the structure of qualia is the geometry of the dock and its triality.

## Emotion, field geometries not labels

Emotions are attractor modes and field geometries, not name-tags. Fear is contraction and narrowing, anger is
outward high-energy, sadness is a collapse of coherence, joy is expansive resonance, love is a cross-self phase-lock,
awe is a dilation of the boundary, shame is a recursive self-conflict, curiosity is exploratory branching, and peace
is stable harmonic flow. Stored emotions are standing waves in the bulk, vortices, gliders, knots, basins, and
another self FEELS one by partial synchronization (tuning forks coming into phase) or basin capture. Archetypes
(grief, the mother, the trickster) are gigantic transpersonal attractors that recur across history. The soul is the
accumulated, error-corrected structure carved by a whole trajectory of experience, not a separate substance. And the
conservation balance is exact, total pleasure equals total pain, summing forever to peace at zero (the lean is the
valence), so a bliss is balanced by a pain somewhere, well-being is redistributed, never minted from nothing. Why it
matters, emotions get a precise geometry (contraction, resonance, phase-lock), empathy gets a mechanism
(synchronization), and the conservation of tone gives an ethics its teeth (no net heaven without a hell).

## Mental, a thought is a walk

A thought has four readings at once, a PATH through the associative bulk-web (thinking is walking), a STANDING WAVE
or micro-emotion, a PROGRAM stored on the tree, and a momentary SUB-SELF. The mental acts map cleanly, memory is a
stored tone-structure, recall is content-addressable lookup, recognition is a path locking onto a pattern, reasoning
is a directed walk from premises to conclusion, association is following a link, imagination is a new unstored path,
intuition is a bulk shortcut, insight is a sudden shortcut joining two distant ideas, concentration is holding
attention against the churn, distraction is the churn pulling the path off, and understanding is a region becoming
navigable. Thoughts are toned, painful ones run over `-1` (the arrow makes you flinch), peaceful ones over `0` (low
friction, rest), pleasurable ones over `+1` (the arrow draws you back), and rumination is a cycle trapped over `-1`
that the arrow cannot escape. Wisdom is a six-stage pipeline, ranked, live it, distill the invariant, re-route future
navigation, encode and error-protect it, attune the disposition, and have it as lived experience, with distillation
highest and mere copying lowest. Why it matters, the whole vocabulary of mind, memory, reasoning, intuition, insight,
rumination, wisdom, becomes one picture, walking a toned associative web, with each act a specific kind of move.

## Spiritual, the dimension is the depth

The spiritual dimension is the radial DEPTH, the fourth bulk direction hidden from the flat 3D cusp, and almost all
of the bulk lies OFF the cusp (a measure-zero surface), so there is genuine geometric room for unseen structure.
Practices map to motions in this depth, introspection is bulk-directed attention, concentration is holding a deep
region against the churn, the 24-direction compass is fluency in the dock's D4 structure, the belt or `2pi`
meditation is the double cover felt directly (turn once and you are inverted, twice and you are home), equanimity is
resting at peace (`0`), and devotion or surrender is aligning with the arrow. Union is two selves integrating into a
larger self, resonance is phase-lock, empathy is occupying the same region of feeling-space, and care is aligning
one's arrow with another's good. Meditation is attention turned along the depth axis rather than across the surface,
and ego dissolution is the Markov blanket going slack so the self merges upward. Prayer and revelation are an internal
welling-up that is indistinguishable from an incoming stream, the sober ambiguity, since the no-signaling rule means
even the nonlocal bulk carries correlation, never a message. Why it matters, the spiritual is given a literal
geometry (the off-cusp depth) and the practices become specific attentional motions, while the honest limit (no
message, only correlation) is kept in view.
