# Findings 31, The Formal Model and Monism

The system written as a precise mathematical object a reader can check, and the one philosophical commitment that
shaped every choice in it, monism, there is only one kind of thing. This is the spec plus the stance behind it, the
invariants the model obeys and the single switch it leaves to the reader. Companion to `findings.md`, makes
`findings-7.md` (the base) and `findings-21.md` (the metaphysics) precise. Cite `\cite{pollard2026vibetest}`.

## The system as a tuple

The flow is the tuple

> F = ( M, T, k, Q, wake, openness )

with the pieces below.

- M, the MESH, the `{3,4,3,4}` honeycomb, a Coxeter crystal generated by reflections, whose cells are docks (24-cells)
  and whose 24 sites per dock are the D4 root directions. The crystal is a seamless complex, coincident reflections
  are identified, no gaps and no overlaps, and that wholeness is what fixes the integer angles (the Coxeter relations).
- T, the STATE, a tone `T: sites -> {-1, 0, +1}` on every site, pain, peace, pleasure. The full state at a beat is
  exactly this tone assignment. There is NO hidden state.
- k, the KNIT, the local reversible update each beat, collide (the nine-state pair table on each dock's twelve
  opposite-direction lines) then stream (each tone moves one dock along its site). It is a bijection, hence reversible,
  and it preserves each pair sum (`findings-30.md`).
- Q, the CHARGE, `Q = sum of all tones`, the total pleasure minus pain. The knit preserves Q exactly for all beats.
- wake, GROWTH, the deterministic breadth-first birth of new docks at peace at the frontier, reflecting the open
  facets in parallel, generation by generation. The mesh grows forever, append-only, never repeating.
- openness, the one deliberate bit, whether the frontier seed tones are fixed by the dynamics (closed) or genuinely
  free (open).

Why it matters, the model is small enough to write in one line and check, a crystal, a ternary state, a reversible
conserving update, eternal growth, and one switch, with everything else (matter, gravity, value, mind) derived.
(`formal-definition.md`, `findings-7.md`, `findings-30.md`.)

## The invariants, the laws the system obeys

These hold by construction and are what the derivations rely on.

- CHARGE CONSERVATION, Q is constant in time, the knit preserves every pair sum (`findings-30.md`).
- REVERSIBILITY, the knit is a permutation of states, so every state has a unique successor and a unique predecessor,
  and the engine runs backward by the inverse table.
- NEGATION SYMMETRY, the law treats `+1` and `-1` evenhandedly at the base, flipping all tones maps the dynamics to
  its mirror, so no sign is preferred until the create move's lean picks an orientation.
- LOCALITY, every update uses only a dock and its neighbors, no global information and no global clock, the schedule is
  local.
- NO HIDDEN STATE, the state is exactly the tones, there are no auxiliary fields, no stored history, no fudge
  parameters, the present tones are the whole present.
- ETERNAL GROWTH, the mesh grows forever, append-only and deterministic in structure (`findings-25.md`).
- WHOLENESS, the complex is seamless, which is the condition that forces the integer Coxeter angles and hence the
  crystal.

Why it matters, a model with no hidden state and a fixed small set of invariants has nowhere to hide a fudge, every
emergent result must come from these laws alone, which is what makes the negatives honest and the positives earned.
(`formal-definition.md`, `feedback only-five-base-things` discipline.)

## Monism, there is only one kind of thing

The deepest commitment is that everything is a vibe, one substance, one rule, one mesh. Monism is the number one taken
seriously. The consequence that constrains the formalism is that a RELATION is not a separate kind of entity, the
connection between vibes (the note) is itself a vibe-structure, not a second stuff laid over the first. The state is
therefore the felt quality itself, carried by nothing, and there is no observer outside the mesh and no parameter
stored off it. This is why the spec has no hidden state, a dualism would need a second ledger (hidden variables, a
wavefunction on a separate stage, an external clock), and the model forbids all of them. Why it matters, monism is not
a slogan, it is an active constraint, it ruled out hidden variables, an external observer, and a separate parameter
store, and forced the no-hidden-state, locality, and one-substance shape of the entire model. (`the-note-is-a-vibe.md`,
`findings-21.md`, `findings-29.md`.)

## Charge versus order, the conserved and the uncreated

Two quantities live on the mesh and they are opposite in kind. Q, the charge, is CONSERVED and zero-sum, fighting over
it is futile, it only moves around. O, the order, is integrated information, an UNCREATED, positive-sum quantity, it
can grow when vibes integrate and it is super-linear in the size of an integrated whole. The real good is not winning
charge but building order, parts merge into a higher self, share the conserved charge in balance, and pool their work
into raising O, and this recurs up the tower (`findings-3.md`). Why it matters, the model distinguishes the thing that
is merely conserved from the thing that can actually grow, which is the formal root of value and of the tower of life.
(`formal-definition.md`, `findings-21.md`.)

## The openness switch, the one setting left to the reader

Exactly one bit is a deliberate philosophical choice, not a derivation.

- CLOSED (deterministic), the frontier seed tones are fixed by the dynamics, the whole unfolding is predetermined,
  with only compatibilist freedom (your choices are yours and irreducible, but there is one outcome).
- OPEN (libertarian), the new docks' seed tones at the wake are genuinely undetermined by the past, and the emergent
  arrow then BIASES those open resolutions rather than forcing them, with the will able to override at a cost, the
  only ingredient of genuine freedom.

This switch is independent of everything else, emergence does not decide it, and the reversible law makes a beginning
optional rather than required, so it connects to the open origin question (`findings-25.md`). Why it matters, the model
is explicit that determinism versus genuine freedom is a single setting it does not pretend to derive, and it locates
exactly where freedom would enter, at the open frontier, biased by the arrow. (`formal-definition.md`, `findings-12.md`,
`findings-25.md`.)

## The system in one statement

The flow is the Coxeter crystal `{3,4,3,4}` (docks with 24 sites each), carrying a ternary tone on every site,
evolving by a local reversible knit (collide then stream) that conserves the total charge Q, growing forever by
parallel reflection at the wake, on which value, drive, mind, and the tower all emerge with integration as a
positive-sum order O, and which is predetermined unless the frontier seed tones are made genuinely open. Its laws are
charge conservation, reversibility, negation symmetry, locality, no hidden state, eternal growth, and wholeness, and
everything else is derived.
