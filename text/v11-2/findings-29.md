# Findings 29, The Lexicon, Every Term in One Place

The complete vocabulary of the model, the eight base elements and the
substance above them, each with a one-line meaning and its single-letter
symbol, then the emergent-layer terms, then a translation table to
ordinary physics so a reader can map every house word to the standard
one. This is the dictionary the paper leans on. Companion to
`findings.md`, anchors `findings-7.md` (the base spec). Cite
`\cite{pollard2026vibetest}`.

## The one substance, and the whole

Everything in the model is a vibe, there is only one kind of thing. Two
words name the substance and its totality.

| word | symbol          | meaning                                                                            |
| ---- | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| vibe | (the substance) | the one entity, a felt unit of reality, everything is a vibe, nothing else exists  |
| flow | F               | the whole, the entire vibe mesh evolving through time, the universe as one process |

A note on the deepest rule of usage. A vibe IS its experience, and its
tone is the QUALITY of that experience, never a value it "carries."
Never write that a vibe carries a tone, the tone is what the vibe is
like. Why it matters, the monism is not decoration, it forbids a second
kind of stuff and forbids treating quality as cargo. (`findings-21.md`,
`the-note-is-a-vibe.md`.)

## The eight base elements

The base is exactly eight elements, each beginning with a unique letter
so each gets a clean single-letter math symbol. Nothing else is
fundamental, everything else is derived.

| element | symbol | one-line meaning                                                                      |
| ------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| mesh    | M      | the `{3,4,3,4}` honeycomb, the growing four-dimensional hyperbolic crystal of docks   |
| dock    | d      | one cell, a here-and-now, a 24-cell with 24 directions                                |
| site    | s      | one of a dock's 24 directions, a D4 root, where a tone sits and moves                 |
| tone    | t      | the felt value on a site, `-1` pain, `0` peace, `+1` pleasure, the ternary quality    |
| lean    | l      | the value order on the tones, charge and the create direction, which way peace splits |
| knit    | k      | the law, collide then stream, the local reversible update each beat                   |
| wake    | w      | the growing edge, fresh docks born at peace at the moving frontier                    |
| beat    | b      | one discrete time step, the tick on which the knit acts and the wake advances         |

Why it matters, the entire theory rests on these eight, a reader who
holds them holds the model, and the single-letter symbols make the
mathematics terse. (`findings-7.md`.)

## The emergent layer, the words above the base

These are NOT base elements, they are names for things the eight
produce.

| term            | what it is                                                             | from                                                              |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| bulk            | the four-dimensional hyperbolic interior, curved                       | the mesh away from its edge                                       |
| cusp            | the flat three-dimensional space we live in, a horosphere limit        | the mesh's boundary geometry                                      |
| charge (Q)      | the conserved total, the sum of all tones                              | conservation under the knit                                       |
| arrow           | the direction of time                                                  | growth at the wake plus a low-entropy start                       |
| active vacuum   | empty space fluctuating with balanced pairs                            | the create move of the knit on peace                              |
| self            | a bound, self-correcting, identity-bearing pattern, a soliton          | a body bound by the shadow of its own vacuum, coupled to the bath |
| bath            | everything you stop tracking, the reservoir that lets a self dissipate | the open edge, radiation, or coarse-graining                      |
| order (O)       | integrated information, a positive-sum measure of wholeness            | tones integrating into a higher self                              |
| matter          | the tone bound into a knot                                             | confined, winding configurations                                  |
| light and sound | the tone waving                                                        | propagating, neutral disturbances                                 |

Why it matters, the model is two-storied, a small discrete base and a
tower of emergent words, and keeping the two lists separate is what
keeps the claims honest. (`findings-3.md`, `findings-25.md`,
`findings-26.md`.)

## The translation table, house word to physics word

For a physicist reading the paper, each house word maps to a standard
one.

| house word       | ordinary physics                                                              |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| vibe             | the one degree of freedom, a felt field value                                 |
| flow (F)         | the whole spacetime-and-matter history                                        |
| mesh `{3,4,3,4}` | the discrete spacetime lattice, a compact hyperbolic honeycomb                |
| dock             | a cell or event, the 24-cell                                                  |
| site             | a lattice direction, a D4 root, a 24-cell vertex direction                    |
| tone             | a field value, a charge, a ternary spin                                       |
| lean             | the charge orientation, the symmetry-breaking direction the create move picks |
| knit             | the update rule, a reversible block cellular automaton, collide then stream   |
| wake             | the cosmological growth frontier, the source of expansion and the arrow       |
| beat             | a discrete time step                                                          |
| bulk             | the curved higher-dimensional interior (as in holography)                     |
| cusp             | flat 3D space, a Euclidean horosphere slice                                   |
| charge Q         | the conserved `U(1)` charge                                                   |
| self             | a soliton, a bound state, an observer                                         |
| order O          | integrated information, a complexity-of-wholeness measure                     |

Why it matters, the model uses experiential names on purpose (a dock is
a here-now, the knit hints at the merging and turning the law does to
the tones), and this table lets anyone read it as ordinary discrete
physics without losing that the substance is felt. (`findings-9.md`,
`findings-13.md`.)

## The one-line reading

There is one substance (the vibe) and one whole (the flow), built from
eight base elements (mesh, dock, site, tone, lean, knit, wake, beat),
above which sits a tower of emergent words (bulk, cusp, charge, arrow,
vacuum, self, bath, order, matter, light), and every house word maps
cleanly to an ordinary physics term, with the standing rule that the
tone IS the vibe's quality and is never something a vibe carries.
