\section{Prayer}

Prayer, on this reading, is the setting of an intention into the world, and the model is unusually precise about what that can and cannot be. Two things are forbidden outright. There is no sorcery, no reaching in from outside the law to bend an outcome, because the law is causally closed, with nothing injected from beyond it. And there is no signaling, no sending of a wish across the world without a channel, because the dynamics has a light cone. Whatever prayer is, it is not magic and it is not transmission.

What it is, is real all the same. A self writes. To set an intention is to imprint a pattern into the bulk, an ornament hung at an address, and the deterministic flow then carries that pattern forward, computing its consequences beat by beat. The will becomes a program. To pray for good, to seed a peaceful and well-aimed intention, is to write structure into the shared substrate and let the world run it, and since the world is one field of which the praying self is a living part, that writing is a genuine change to reality in the bulk, not a request handed to something outside it. The universe a self prays to, the largest pattern it calls God, is the same field it writes into and is woven from.

Two further channels are open, both within the law. A self can align its own arrow with the good, the turning toward peace and pleasure the spiritual practices cultivate, which is prayer as devotion, reshaping the one who prays. And selves that lie near one another through the bulk, bound by a shared history or a matched rhythm, can fall into correlation along the interior's short hidden paths, their inner states moving together though no message passes. None of this is mailing a wish to a sky. It is the writing of intention into a world that is listening because the writer is part of it.
