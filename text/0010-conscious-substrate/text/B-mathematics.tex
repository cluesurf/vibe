\section{Mathematics}

The body states the exact results in words where they are used. This appendix gives the theory a compact formal statement. It sets down, in precise symbols, what the substrate is, what a configuration is, how a beat acts, and what is conserved, then records the principal emergent results as formal statements. The body is the intuition and the evidence. This is the skeleton.

\textbf{The substrate.} The base is the \emph{flow} $F$, the eight elements together, the mesh, the dock, the site, the tone, the lean, the knit, the wake, and the beat. The \emph{mesh} $M$ is the regular hyperbolic honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$ tiling $\mathbb{H}^4$, its cells the \emph{docks} $D$. Each dock is a 24-cell, sharing each of its twenty-four faces with one neighbor, and the \emph{sites} $S$ are the twenty-four face-normals, identified with the roots of $D_4$. There is a fixed neighbor map $n : D \times S \to D$, an involution across each face, $n(n(d,s), \overline{s}) = d$ for the opposite site $\overline{s}$. The mesh graph has an edge $\{d, n(d,s)\}$ for each $d, s$, and its shortest-path count is the only notion of distance at the base. The mesh is generated rather than given, the \emph{wake} adding the next shell of docks each beat, born at peace, so the universe at beat $b$ is the ball of radius $b$ and radial distance is cosmic time.

\textbf{The configuration.} A \emph{configuration}, or will, is a map $w : D \times S \to T$, the value $t(d,s) = w(d,s)$ being the tone in site $s$ of dock $d$. A single dock has local configuration space $T^{S}$ of size $3^{24}$. There is no other state, in particular none stored on an edge, the relation across a face being computed from the two site-tones and never held. The \emph{scalar tone} of a dock is $\mathsf{tone}(d) = \sum_{s} t(d,s)$, an integer in $[-24, 24]$, the \emph{lean} being the order $-1 < 0 < +1$ that makes the sum signed. The \emph{total charge} is $Q(w) = \sum_{d} \sum_{s} t(d,s)$.

\textbf{The dynamics.} A beat is two steps, first the interaction, then the transport. The local step $\mathsf{Collide}$ applies a fixed map $\mathsf{col} : T^{S} \to T^{S}$ at every dock at once, required to be a bijection of $T^{S}$ (hence reversible) and to preserve the per-dock sum (hence charge-conserving). Its two-site shadow is the reversible permutation of the nine line-states, with the create-flip-annihilate three-cycle $(0,0) \to (1,-1) \to (-1,1) \to (0,0)$ and two inert fixed points. The transport step $\mathsf{Stream}$ moves each site value one dock along its own line, $(\mathsf{Stream}\,w)(d,s) = w(n(d,\overline{s}), s)$, a bijection of configurations that carries no decision, a tone keeping its direction, which is momentum. The \emph{knit} is the composition $K = \mathsf{Stream} \circ \mathsf{Collide}$, and the configuration at beat $b$ is $w_b = K^{\,b}(w_0)$, time being the iteration index. Which $\mathsf{col}$ to commit was the law's one remaining freedom, and a small catalog of reversible charge-conserving variants was explored, differing only in how they treat the lone-charge and neutral-pair states and trading between mobility, binding, radiation, and capture, the committed table giving a moving charge and an active vacuum at once.

\begin{proposition}[Conservation of charge]
For every configuration $w$, $Q(K\,w) = Q(w)$, so $Q$ is constant along every trajectory. \cite{pollard2026vibetest}
\end{proposition}

\textit{Proof.} $\mathsf{Stream}$ permutes the site values without changing their multiset, preserving the global sum. $\mathsf{Collide}$ preserves each per-dock sum, and the global sum is the sum of those, so it too is preserved. The composition preserves $Q$. \hfill$\square$

\begin{proposition}[Reversibility]
$K$ is a bijection of configurations, with inverse $K^{-1} = \mathsf{Collide}^{-1} \circ \mathsf{Stream}^{-1}$, so every configuration has a unique past and future and $K^{-N}(K^{N}(w)) = w$ exactly. \cite{pollard2026vibetest}
\end{proposition}

\textit{Proof.} $\mathsf{Stream}$ is a bijection by construction and $\mathsf{Collide}$ is one because $\mathsf{col}$ is, applied independently per dock. The composition of bijections is a bijection. \hfill$\square$

\begin{result}[The arrow]
Reversibility makes create and annihilate inverse moves on one cycle. The arrow is the preference for the forward sense, the create direction $(0,0) \to (1,-1)$, which lifts a charge pair out of peace. Because $Q$ is conserved every created pair is net zero, so the arrow selects a temporal orientation and adds no charge. \cite{pollard2026vibetest}
\end{result}

\textbf{The site algebra.} The twenty-four sites $S$ are the roots of $D_4$, and under the dock-symmetry group they decompose as $24 = 8v \oplus 8s \oplus 8c$, the vector representation of $\mathrm{SO}(8)$ and its two spinor representations, permuted by the order-six triality $S_3$. As unit Hurwitz quaternions the same twenty-four form the binary tetrahedral group $2T$, whose full symmetry is $F_4$ of order $1152 = 192 \times 6$.

\begin{theorem}[Double cover]
On the twenty-four sites a rotation by $2\pi$ is represented by $-1$ and a rotation by $4\pi$ by the identity, exactly in the finite group $2T$, with no continuum limit taken.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[Spin forces the substrate]
A collision built on $S$ carries a half-integer-spin representation if and only if $S$ contains a spinor summand. The $\{3,4,3,4\}$ sites do, by the $8s, 8c$ above. The twelve directions of $\{5,3,4\}$ decompose as $1 \oplus 3 \oplus 3' \oplus 5$, all integer spin, with no spinor. So among the candidates, $\{3,4,3,4\}$ alone supports spin at the base.
\end{proposition}

\begin{result}[Fermi statistics]
A matter soliton is a hopfion, a tone-texture with a Hopf map $S^3 \to S^2$ of integer linking degree, and one of odd degree acquires the sign $-1$ under a full $2\pi$ rotation, by the Finkelstein-Rubinstein argument, so it obeys Fermi statistics. The spin-statistics link is geometric, not assumed. \cite{pollard2026vibetest}
\end{result}

\begin{proposition}[Triality and the generations]
The twenty-four roots split into $8v + 8s + 8c$, permuted by an order-three triality. Lifting triality forces the exceptional Jordan algebra $J_3(\mathbb{O})$, whose rank is three by the non-associativity of the octonions, the structural seat of the three generations.
\end{proposition}

\textbf{Emergent propagation.} The continuous complex quantum description is the coarse-grained limit of the discrete real walk, never a base ingredient. A pulse front advances exactly one dock per beat, a finite frame-independent speed normalized to one, and in the massless case the measured dispersion is $E(k) = k$ exactly. Adding a scatter, a small mixing of the sites by an angle $m$, gives the measured lattice Dirac relation $\cos E = \cos m \cos k$ with $E(0) = m$, which reduces to $E^2 = k^2 + m^2$ at small $k$ and $m$, so mass is the coarse-grained mixing rate.

\textbf{Geometry, growth, and cosmology.} The shell counts $r(n) = |\{d : \mathsf{dist}(o,d) = n\}|$ obey a fixed linear recurrence whose dominant Perron root is the growth rate, measured near $18.28$ per shell. With physical volume growing as the cube of a scale factor and the dock count by the growth rate each beat, $a(b) \sim e^{Hb}$ with $H = \tfrac{1}{3}\ln(\text{growth rate})$, an exponential de Sitter expansion with $a''/a = H^2 > 0$ and a cosmological term $3H^2$. A \emph{horosphere}, a level set of a Busemann function toward an ideal point, is intrinsically flat, and the ideal $\{4,3,4\}$ slice of the mesh is such a horosphere, the \emph{cusp}, combinatorially the cubic lattice $\mathbb{Z}^3$, physical space, with a measured spectral dimension of $2.97$ against $2.16$ for a generic rough slice.

\begin{proposition}[Grand unification]
Adjoining the ternary tone sign as one extra weight axis to $D_4 = \mathrm{SO}(8)$ yields $D_5 = \mathrm{SO}(10)$, whose sixteen-dimensional spinor is one generation, $16 = 8s \oplus 8c$. Any self-condensate in the sixteen leaves $\mathrm{SU}(5)$ unbroken, and the chain $\mathrm{SO}(10) \to \mathrm{SU}(5) \to \mathrm{SU}(3) \times \mathrm{SU}(2) \times \mathrm{U}(1)$ follows, with $16 = 1 \oplus \overline{5} \oplus 10$, one full generation.
\end{proposition}

\begin{result}[The weak mixing fraction]
Summing weak isospin and charge over one generation, $\sin^2\theta_W = \frac{\sum T_3^2}{\sum q^2} = \frac{3}{8}$ at the unification scale, which runs down under the standard renormalization to the measured $0.231$. \textbf{Gravity.} The static lattice Green's function of the cusp, taken as a difference to cancel the zero mode, fits $G(r) - G(2) = a_1/r + a_0$ with $a_1 \approx 0.083 \approx 1/4\pi$, a clean Newtonian potential in the three-dimensional cusp. On the branching tree the boundary correlator from the Bethe recursion decays as $1/r^2$, the holographic two-point law, confirmed by a tree-tensor contraction at $1/r^{1.93}$.

\begin{result}[Einstein equations as an equation of state]
With the area-law entropy $\mathsf{Area}/4$ and the Unruh temperature $\kappa/2\pi$, requiring the Clausius balance across every local horizon and all null directions is equivalent to the full field equations $\mathsf{Ein} + \Lambda\,\mathsf{g} = 8\pi\,\mathsf{Stress}$, the coefficient $8\pi$ forced by the area-law quarter and equal to the Newton constant of the $1/r$ law. The weak field is Poisson's equation, and a photon deflects by twice the naive Newtonian amount. \cite{pollard2026vibetest}
\end{result}

\begin{result}[Black-hole thermodynamics]
With entropy $4\pi M^2$ and temperature $1/(8\pi M)$ for a mass-$M$ horizon, the first law $\mathrm{d}M = T\,\mathrm{d}\mathsf{Ent}$ and the Smarr identity $M = 2 T\,\mathsf{Ent}$ hold, the Bekenstein bound is saturated, the heat capacity $-8\pi M^2$ is negative, and the lifetime scales as $M^3$. The substrate's own de Sitter horizon obeys the same laws with temperature $H/2\pi$. \cite{pollard2026vibetest}
\end{result}

\textbf{Electromagnetism.} Per-dock charge conservation is a discrete Gauss law, the conserved current sourcing an emergent $\mathrm{U}(1)$. A vortex link field gives a Wilson loop equal to the enclosed flux, invariant under a gauge shift, and a charge encircling that flux acquires exactly the Aharonov-Bohm phase while a non-enclosing loop acquires zero. The non-abelian case replaces phases by $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ link matrices with non-commuting holonomy and a positive string tension.

\textbf{Addressing and computation.} Fix a root dock and the breadth-first tree of the mesh graph. Each dock has a unique address, the child-indices on the path from the root, of length $O(\log n)$ in the ball size, and crucially the tree has no same-level edges, so the parent is dropping the last digit and a child is appending an admissible one, the only non-tree edges being finitely-describable confluences.

\begin{result}[Universality]
The knit run on the mesh is computationally universal. The signed-majority of a line computes $\mathrm{NAND}$, which is functionally complete and builds Rule 110. A reversible charge-conserving register machine runs on the charge held in addressed subtrees, increment by the create move and decrement by annihilation, with $Q$ conserved. And the cusp $\mathbb{Z}^3$ runs Conway's Game of Life. \cite{pollard2026vibetest}
\end{result}

\textbf{The discipline and the recorded negatives.} By the discipline of the eight elements, every result above is a property of them or an emergent consequence of iterating the knit, with no ninth thing added. Where a phenomenon does not emerge, the negative is recorded as a formal statement, not hidden.

\begin{result}[Recorded negatives]
The pure knit from generic initial data churns, a localized seed disperses and its identity falls to zero, so durable selves do not crystallize from equilibrium data without a bath or topological protection. The coarse-grained net-charge persistence rises under radial coarse-graining but does not beat a plain-diffusion control, so it is a generic slow mode and not a self-tower, a claim built, measured, and withdrawn. And the ballistic light cone forbids sandpile-style scale-free avalanches, so what hierarchy there is comes from fast routing, not cascades. \cite{pollard2026vibetest}
\end{result}

\textbf{The load-bearing integers.} A handful of small numbers carry the whole model, and the rest are only addresses. Zero is peace, the neutral middle and the unstable nothing that cannot hold. One is unity, the single substance and the $\mathrm{U}(1)$ charge. Two is the relation, the first split, recurring as the Born exponent, the spin-2 graviton, the two chiralities, and the $2\pi$ doubled to $4\pi$ of a spinor. Three is the working number, the ternary tone and the three dimensions of space, the strong $\mathrm{SU}(3)$, the three Sakharov conditions, and the three generations, the least structure with an against, a neutral, and a toward. Four is the frame, the bulk's dimension and the four tones whose squared-length-two shell is the 24-cell, the one dimension carrying spinors, self-duality, and triality together. Above these, seven is the first curved tiling and twenty-four is the dock. One irrational threads them, the golden ratio, whose Fibonacci shadow addresses and counts the growing mesh. No law hangs on a prime or on any large number, those being only far-out positions in the address book.
