\section{The Claim}

The claim is simple to state. Feeling is fundamental, and matter is what feeling settles into at scale. Where Wheeler said \emph{it from bit}, the world built up from yes-or-no answers, this says \emph{it from feeling}. The base layer is not information but experience, and information is just one of the things experience does when you read it from outside.

The paper makes three claims in all. First, that a single law on a single substrate can be written down, made of feeling and nothing else. Second, that running it reproduces a long list of known physics, a list specific enough that it could have come out wrong. Third, that the same law, read at larger scales, gives a graded and testable picture of life, mind, and the shape of experience itself.

One thing is not claimed. The paper does not prove that the substrate feels. That single step is a posit, and it is marked plainly wherever it bears weight. Everything above it can be built, stepped, and measured. Below it lies the choice to call the structure experience rather than only to describe it. What the paper argues is that the choice is fruitful, not that it is forced.
