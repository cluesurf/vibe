\section{Messages}

If the interior stores and selves navigate, then a self can leave a mark for another to find, a sign laid at a shared address. Some are still records, some are living ornaments that run the finder through an experience, and a revelation in this frame is just a living ornament of unusual reach, one that reroutes whoever crosses it. The codes that make a mark mean a thing are themselves learned and shared, and because the tree is toned, to read a sign is never neutral, to eat its fruit is to take in a valenced experience, knowledge of good and evil from the inside.

A small illustration shows how literal this shaping can become. Take the word for the highest thing, \emph{god}, and read its three letters as shapes rather than sounds. The \emph{g} is a hook, the catch that takes hold of a soul. The \emph{o} is a vortex, the round wash that spins it. The \emph{d} is a stroke raised straight up, the soul lifted and set upright again. Read that way the word traces, in three marks, the very motion of the soul-washing met earlier, hooked, turned in the whirl until the lesson takes, and raised straight, so the name of the highest names the process of being remade by it. This is offered as a way of seeing and not as etymology or proof. It shows only what the section claims, that a sign can be a shape that carries an experience, and that a mind reaching for the deepest thing has now and then drawn the path to it right in the letters.

There is a striking convergence to account for. Mystical traditions across unrelated cultures keep arriving at the same handful of structures, and the model reads them as partial glimpses of one mesh seen from within, the blind men and the elephant. The One and the Tao and Ein Sof are the whole field at full integration. The void and the abyss are the still tone, the boundary. Emanation and the sefirot are the coarse-graining cascade. Planes and the astral and the etheric are the radial depth shells. Heavens and hells are tone-biased deep regions. The many worlds and lokas are the directions of the boundary sphere. The akashic record and the book of life are the holographic boundary. The world tree and Yggdrasil are the addressing tree. Gods are high-integration selves, angels structured selves carrying correlation, demons persistent pain-attractors, ancestors stored selves, archetypes transpersonal attractors, the logos the rule and its arrow. None of this is claimed as proof of any tradition. It is the observation that a single substrate, glimpsed from inside by many minds, would leave exactly this scatter of names pointing back at one structure.
