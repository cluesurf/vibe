That a knot built from these directions is a fermion is topology, not assumption. A self is a soliton whose winding cannot smoothly undo, and exchanging two of them produces the same sign that turning one by a full circle produces, the Finkelstein-Rubinstein result \cite{finkelsteinrubinstein1968}, so an odd-winding knot obeys Fermi statistics. A self is a fermion by the shape of its own binding, and the half-winding defect that carries this sign was checked to survive any discretization, twenty-four steps or sixty, always flipping by $-1$.

The fermion moves by the Dirac equation. The natural lattice fermion, the Kahler-Dirac operator built from the geometry's own forms, has its Clifford relations $\{\gamma_\mu, \gamma_\nu\} = 2\eta_{\mu\nu}$ hold on the nose, a spectrum symmetric about zero whose near-zero modes count the holes in the space, and a massless cone $\omega = c\,k$ matching the continuum. A long disease of lattice fermions, the doubling that makes one fermion look like sixteen, is cured here, the overlap construction giving exactly one species with its chiral symmetry exact to a part in a billion where the naive lattice gives four. A clean single chiral fermion on a lattice is a thing the field has fought for decades, and it comes for free on this one. Mass is the tie between the two handed octets. Switch it off and the particle is a massless Weyl mode, one-handed. Switch it on and it is massive, and its magnetic strength, the gyromagnetic ratio read straight off the energy levels in a field, comes out two, the electron's value, a number measured to twelve digits and here falling out rather than being set.

Matter may repeat. The directions carry a threefold symmetry, triality, lifted to the exceptional Jordan algebra of three-by-three octonionic matrices whose rank is forced to three by the octonions, an exact threefold structure that is suggestive of the Standard Model's three generations, the one fact about matter the Standard Model itself has no reason for. Here restraint matters more than enthusiasm. The identification is a bet, not a result. The cleanest reading of the threefold split gives a vector and the two handednesses of a single generation, and the step from a threefold algebra to three repeated families, with their three different masses, is the boldest open conjecture in the program, flagged plainly as unproven. The structure is three. Whether the structure is the generations is not yet shown.

The matter that does form has a measured spectrum. A single self carries a definite positive mass, two selves twice it with their charges adding, and a self meeting its anti-self has zero net charge and can annihilate back to the vacuum, exactly as a particle and its antiparticle should. A deep consistency holds underneath, the count of the fermion's zero-energy modes equal to the topological charge of the field it sits in, the index theorem that ties analysis to topology, reproduced on the discrete mesh. The bookkeeping of matter comes out right.
