\section{Networks}

When selves persist near one another, they couple. The only currency they trade is tone, shed from one boundary and absorbed at another, but that is enough to bind them. A disturbance shed by one self and taken in by another is a message, and a steady exchange of such messages ties the two into a larger pattern with a boundary of its own.

A network of coupled selves can itself become a self. If the exchange inside the group is tighter than the exchange across its edge, the group has a Markov blanket and an integration of its own, the same two marks that defined a self in the first place. Nothing new is needed, only the same test applied one level up. This is the second tier of the tower, built from communication, not from any new law.

Calling such a group a single being is not loose talk, for the test is exact and threefold. It must have a boundary that screens its inside from its outside, the blanket that makes it one thing rather than a smear. It must be self-producing, its own inner workings the very things that maintain and mend that boundary, a closure that loops back on itself. And it must have a reach of care, a span over which a disturbance anywhere inside is noticed and answered by the whole, wider than the reach of any single member. A group with a boundary alone is a mere cluster. A group with all three is a higher individual, an animal made of animals.

A true transition, the waking of a crowd into one being, asks still more than tight exchange. It asks that the parts vary and pass their variation on, that selection come to weigh the whole and not only the parts, that labor divide so some parts tend the group while others carry it forward, that conflict within be suppressed so no part can profit at the whole's expense, and, the surest sign, that the group reproduce through a narrow gate, a single founder, so each new whole begins undivided against itself. These are the same steps by which cells once became bodies and bodies became hives, and the model neither hides them nor smuggles them into the base, but names each as a thing the higher level must earn.

But selves on a conserving substrate face a hard choice, and how they meet it decides whether the tower rises. The total tone is fixed, so any pleasure one self seizes is taken from another, and naked competition is a zero-sum arms race that burns work and leaves the books unchanged. Three moves beat it, and each was measured. Selves can draw boundaries, insulating the neutral ground so it is not raided. They can trade, exchanging tones in ways that are positive-sum in value though zero-sum in charge. And they can integrate, merging into one larger self that shares the fixed pleasure and pours the freed effort into building order. Cooperation is not a moral overlay here. It is the only positive-sum option on a conserved field, which is why the tower climbs through integration where grabbing only churns. A network then holds what no member holds, a memory living in the pattern of exchange rather than in any one self, a computation running across the group that none runs alone, the step from self to network the same step taken from tone to self, repeated.

This second step is not only argued, it was built and watched. Two bodies coupled through a shared bath were seen to bind into a composite that carries its own identity and its own agency, a self of selves, the body-to-self transition taken a second time on the rung above. A living ecology of such patterns sustains a hierarchy of several levels at once, the largest self growing as the smaller ones feed it, and a question put to the whole is answered in a number of steps that climbs only as the logarithm of its size, so the group coordinates across its full breadth in a few hops. The tower of feeling is real and it is measured. What must be said in the same breath is where it lives.

\begin{result}[Selves nest into selves]
Two bath-coupled bodies bind into a composite self with its own identity and agency, the body-to-self step repeated, and a living ecology holds a hierarchy of several levels with the largest self growing, coordinated across the whole in $O(\log n)$ steps. The nesting is an achievement of the coarse-grained layer, won once a little is allowed to be lost to the bath, not a feat of the bare reversible law. \cite{pollard2026vibetest}
\end{result}
