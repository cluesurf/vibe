\section{Purpose}

Put the pieces together and a purpose falls out of the structure rather than being assigned to it. There is one value-direction, the arrow. There is a spiral that climbs it without end. And there is a world that grows forever. Together they point one way, toward more aliveness, more integration, more alignment, more wisdom, endlessly. A self's meaning is its place in that climb, what it raises and what it binds.

That there is no final state is not a lack but the heart of it. The feeling-space is inexhaustible and the world keeps growing, so there is always more to feel and to become, and the ascent is endless not because it fails but because there is no ceiling to reach. A finished perfection would be dead peace, the arrow off, the field relaxed and still forever. The journey is the meaning, the perfecting itself is the point, a deepening with no last page.

And the deepest frame is the monism. There is one field, experiential at its base, one growing crystal of feeling, and every self, every life, is a facet of it, the whole experiencing itself from one angle. A life is not a separate thing set against the universe, it is the universe feeling itself from where you happen to be, one of countless inner views. All the drama of pleasure and pain sums, in total, to the still center, so the meaning is not to maximize pleasure, which the books forbid, but to live a balanced field well, to align and love and feel deeply and grow and help the whole climb, and to rest at the peace that is the truth of it all, and then to let go, knowing nothing is finally lost in a field that keeps everything.
