\section{The Law} \label{sec:law}

We have the stage, the cast, and the forces. We still need the rule that moves them, the local law on the substrate.
We show that the rule is also forced, that it is the unique symmetric reversible update on the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ lattice,
and that it is $CPT$ invariant.

\subsection{The substrate}

The geometry of Section~\ref{sec:spacetime} is the hyperbolic honeycomb whose cell is the 24-cell, the $D_4$ root
polytope, written $\{3,4,3,4\}$ in the hyperbolic naming. It is a discrete, locally finite arena. Each cell has
twenty-four neighbors, the twenty-four roots, the twenty-four directions a tone can move. The substrate grows shell by
shell from a seed cell, and we count the shells directly. The growth is exponential, each shell larger than the last by
an asymptotic factor we will need in Section~\ref{sec:hierarchy}. Margenstern's address calculus for hyperbolic
tilings \cite{margenstern2007vol1, margenstern2008vol2} is what makes this substrate computable, giving each cell a
finite name and each neighbor a finite rule, and we follow it.

\subsection{The rule is forced}

A local law moves tone between neighboring cells in discrete beats. We require three things of it, each a plain
consequence of what has come before. It must be \emph{local}, acting only between a cell and its twenty-four neighbors,
because there is nothing else for it to act on. It must be \emph{reversible}, because reversibility is the premise that
forced the octonions in the first place, so the law that runs on the octonionic substrate cannot itself destroy
information. And it must be \emph{symmetric}, invariant under the symmetry group of the cell, the group $B_4$ of the
24-cell, because the law cannot prefer a direction the geometry does not.

These three conditions, locality, reversibility, and full $B_4$ symmetry, turn out to pin the rule almost completely.
The reversible local updates are the pairings of the twenty-four directions into exchanging channels, and requiring
invariance under all of $B_4$ collapses the choices to a single orbit. We count this directly. The number of
$B_4$-symmetric perfect pairings of the relevant configuration is computed to be the single value $10395 = 11!! $, the
double factorial, and they form one orbit under the symmetry, so the symmetric reversible rule is unique up to the
symmetry itself.

\begin{result}[The law is forced]
On the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ substrate, the requirements of locality, reversibility, and full $B_4$ symmetry single out a
unique local update rule, the symmetric pairing of the twenty-four directions. It is the discrete analogue of a
reversible, isotropic transport, and it is the only law the geometry permits.
\end{result}

\subsection{The law respects CPT}

We then verify that this unique rule has the three discrete symmetries the world is known to respect together. Charge
conjugation $C$ is the tone sign-flip $x \mapsto -x$ inherited from the ternary of Section~\ref{sec:firstdistinction},
and the rule commutes with it because it treats $+1$ and $-1$ on the same footing. Parity $P$ is a reflection of the
spatial directions, an element of $B_4$, and the rule is invariant by construction. Time reversal $T$ is the reversal
of the update, the running of the reversible rule backward, which exists precisely because the rule is reversible. We
check by direct simulation that applying $C$, $P$, and $T$ together returns the dynamics to itself.

\begin{result}[CPT]
The forced local law is invariant under the combined operation $CPT$, with $C$ the ternary charge conjugation, $P$ a
$B_4$ reflection, and $T$ the reversal permitted by reversibility. The local dynamics has no built-in arrow. The arrow
of the world is the global growth of the substrate from its seed, the integer order of Section~\ref{sec:arrow} made
dynamical, consistent with a locally time-symmetric law.
\end{result}

So the law is not a further choice layered on top of the structure. It is the unique symmetric reversible motion the
substrate allows, and it carries the $CPT$ invariance of real physics for the same reason it is reversible at all.
