\subsection{What is and is not established}

\textbf{Demonstrated and validated:} the geometry and its dominance, the law, the
Lorentz-safe substrate, the field content with mass, the gauge and graviton operators
from the action, the electroweak masses, the Newtonian limit, the conserving Einstein
equation, singularity resolution, the area law, the quantum pillars, the arrow,
expansion, inflation, and that the one model runs end-to-end.

\textbf{Meets observation:} the everpresent $\Lambda$ matches the dark-energy magnitude
to order of magnitude, and the absence of linear Lorentz violation is confirmed.

\textbf{Open or beyond reach:} the Born-rule derivation, the full nonlinear Einstein
equation, the chiral gauge theory and the rest of the Standard Model, Hawking
radiation, the large-$N$ free-energy crossing, a fully emergent spatial geometry from a
growth rule, and the framework's foundational claim about experience.
