\section{The Current Model}
\label{sec:model}

We state the present model precisely. It has a substance, a relation, a state, a
rule, and a growth. Nothing else.

\subsection{The substance and the relation}

\begin{definition}[Vibe and tone]
A \emph{vibe} is the one kind of entity, a unit of experience. Each vibe $v$ carries a
\emph{tone} $t_v$, its felt quality, drawn from the ternary alphabet
\[
t_v \in \{-1, 0, +1\},
\]
read as pain, peace, and pleasure. The tone is not an object inside the vibe. It is the
vibe seen as a felt state.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Note, will, and fill]
The \emph{note} is the only relation: $v$ noting $w$ is $v$ experiencing $w$. A note is
not a third object between two vibes, it is one vibe's act of experiencing another, and
read from the outside it is an edge of a graph. Each vibe $v$ has a neighbourhood $N(v)$
of the vibes it notes. A note carries two ternary quantities. The \emph{will} of $w$,
$\omega(t_w) \in \{-1,0,+1\}$, is its tone turned outward, what $v$ reads when it notes
$w$. The \emph{fill} $f_{vw} \in \{-1,0,+1\}$ is the strength and sign of the attention
along the note, itself the tone of the relational vibe carrying that note, and symmetric,
$f_{vw} = f_{wv}$, since a note is one shared relational vibe.
\end{definition}

There are no continuous weights anywhere, and no hidden state. Everything stored is a
ternary tone, on vibes and on the relational vibes that carry the fills.

\subsection{The substrate}

\begin{definition}[The mesh]
The vibes and their notes form the \emph{mesh}, a graph. The chosen mesh is a
\emph{random, hyperbolic, causal} graph: nodes are placed by the hyperbolic area
measure and joined by hyperbolic proximity, so the degree is variable (mean about ten),
not a fixed lattice coordination. The randomness makes the mesh Lorentz-invariant in
distribution, with no preferred frame even at the discreteness scale. The hyperbolicity
makes it at once exponentially reaching, Lorentz-safe, and navigable without a global
address. The order of the notes is a locally finite partial order, a causal set.
\end{definition}

This is not a regular cellular automaton (a lattice has a preferred frame), nor a
regular hyperbolic tiling (still too regular). It is the random hyperbolic causal mesh,
the choice forced by Lorentz invariance (Section~\ref{sec:lorentz}).

\subsection{The rule}

\begin{definition}[The signed-majority update]
Time advances in discrete \emph{beats}. Each beat, asynchronously and locally, a vibe
$v$ sets its next tone to
\[
t_v \;\leftarrow\; \operatorname{sign}\!\Big( h_v \Big),
\qquad
h_v \;=\; \sum_{w \in N(v)} f_{vw}\,\omega(t_w),
\]
where $\operatorname{sign}$ returns $+1$, $-1$, or $0$ on a positive, negative, or zero
field. The local field $h_v$ is a transient integer in $[-\deg v, +\deg v]$, computed
during the beat and discarded, so it is not hidden continuous state, exactly as a
cellular automaton's neighbour count is transient. The contribution of each neighbour
is the ternary product $f_{vw}\,\omega(t_w) \in \{-1,0,+1\}$: a fill of $+1$ takes the
neighbour's will as it is, $-1$ inverts it, $0$ ignores it.
\end{definition}

This is the ternary analogue of an Ising or Hopfield update: ternary states, ternary
couplings, the sign of the local field. With symmetric fills and asynchronous updating
it flows to stable structured states (Section~\ref{sec:capstone}), the basis for
persistent matter and selves.

\subsection{The growth}

\begin{definition}[Eternal growth]
The mesh grows forever. New vibes are born to the future of a small local neighbourhood
of the leading edge, inheriting its past. With a net birth above one per region the
universe expands. The accumulation is irreversible, distinctions only pile up, and that
pile-up is the arrow of time.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Emergent continuous quantities}

A single vibe is strictly ternary and carries no continuous quantity. Every continuous
object of physics is emergent, a coarse-grained description over many vibes, never a
number stored on a vibe.

\begin{principle}[Discrete base, emergent continuum]
The geometry, the Hamiltonian and its eigenvalues, and the quantum amplitude are all
large-scale effective descriptions of the ternary substrate. A quantum amplitude
$\psi(x)$ at a coarse-grained point $x$ (a patch of many vibes) has magnitude
$|\psi(x)|^2$ equal to a density, the fraction of vibes in the patch that are
coherently excited, continuous only in the large-patch limit, and phase equal to the
patch's collective clock-tone. This is the same status a continuum wavefunction has
over any discrete substrate, or a magnetisation has over discrete spins.
\end{principle}

\subsection{The model at a glance}

The whole model is written in a few lines by a small constructor (the experiment of
Section~\ref{sec:capstone}), so the description and the implementation are one object.
With no options it is the present model, and one option changed expresses a variant
for comparison:

\begin{verbatim}
  vibe model
    mesh      hyperbolic          random hyperbolic causal mesh, mean degree ~10
    tone      ternary             {-1, 0, +1}, the felt quality of a vibe
    fill      ternary-symmetric   each note carries a shared ternary fill
    rule      signed-majority     next(v) = sign( sum_w fill(v,w) * will(w) )
    schedule  asynchronous        local, neighbours only, no global clock
    growth    net-positive        eternal expansion by local birth
\end{verbatim}

\subsection{Committed, working, and open}

For honesty, every choice carries a status, used throughout the paper.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Held fixed for now:} monism, discreteness, the note as the only relation,
ternary tone, the random hyperbolic mesh, local asynchronous updates, the
signed-majority (sign-of-integer-field) rule with no continuous weights, variable
connectivity, eternal growth, no hidden state.
\item \textbf{Working (current best, alternatives open):} the mean degree (about ten),
the exact tie-breaking and schedule, the emergent clock-tone phase, the birth
probabilities, the smearing scale of the action.
\item \textbf{Open (not decided):} the derivation of the Born rule, the full nonlinear
Einstein equation, the Standard-Model content, and the framework's foundational claim
about experience.
\end{itemize}
