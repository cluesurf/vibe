\subsection{Open problems}

Six sharply bounded open problems lie beyond the theorems certified in this paper, each testing whether this discrete
substrate scales to continuum physics.

\textbf{Continuum 4D Yang--Mills limit ($a \to 0$):} Extending the strong-coupling 4D $D_4$ Wilson mass gaps
($\beta_* = 0.285141$ for $SU(2)$ and $0.563019$ for $SU(3)$) toward $\beta \to \infty$ requires multi-scale
renormalization group control \cite{balaban1989}, since strong-coupling contraction certifies exponential decay only at
lattice-scale correlation lengths rather than diverging correlation length at fixed physical mass. Establishing whether
$D_4$ isotropy tightens multi-scale block-spin bounds and whether native subgroups ($2T$, $\Sigma(648)$) admit any
non-freezing crossover decides whether the 24-cell's gauge geometry survives the continuum limit.

\textbf{Dynamical electroweak breaking and 4D chiral gauging:} Deriving chiral matter representations, dynamical Higgs
condensation ($m_W / m_Z = \cos\theta_W$), Yukawa masses, dynamical 3+1D $SU(3)_C$ confinement, and bulk-covariant
four-dimensional chiral gauging beyond static $2T < SU(2)_L$ background walls remains open. Promoting cusp-wall fluxes
to dynamical four-dimensional gauge links confronts mirror-fermion doubling on the deep bulk wall directly. Constructing
a non-perturbative mirror-gapping mechanism that preserves local gauge invariance and spontaneous symmetry breaking is
the sharp test of whether these building blocks unify into one chiral gauge automaton.

\textbf{Nonlinear 4D quantum gravity, dark-sector coupling, and permanent charge asymmetry:} Unifying scalar
backreaction with nonlinear Einstein dynamics ($G_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}$), deriving $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$ from bare
$U$, restoring relativistic two-body bound-state WEP, overcoming the exact $3.1 \times 10^{-15}$ sterile dark-sector
null, and achieving permanent husk trapping with $\Delta B \neq 0$ and dynamical $\mathcal{CP}$ breaking remain open.
Weak-field lapse infall and linearized gravitons probe separate metric limits, whereas relativistic gravity couples
stress-energy nonlinearly to curvature and equates inertial and passive mass for bound composites. Deriving bulk
localization, sterile gravitational visibility, and permanent husk trapping from unitary evolution tests whether gravity
and dark-sector phenomenology arise natively on $\{3,4,3,4\}$.

\textbf{Light mass generation, autonomous universality, and mesh-level compilation:} Generating an infrared mass scale
$m_{\mathrm{eff}} \ll 1$ from bare $m = \pi/3$ alongside 3+1D fermionic statistics remains open. Past externally
scheduled 3-qubit spin-independent universal exchange-gate synthesis on $\mathbb{C}^{126}$, four computational
frontiers remain inside bare $U$: overcoming the $v(u) = \sqrt{3}u/(3u^2+1)$ $w = 0$ zero-relative-velocity SWAP
obstruction, exceeding the $\binom{9}{2} = 36$ single-pass ballistic crossing cap, scaling past nine internal roles
($n \le 3$ DFS qubits) without cross-line $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ leakage, and bypassing the four unbounded-loop
obstructions \cite{pollard2026railway} (for example via high-density $k \ge 5$ waveguides or gauge links) or compiling
recurrent agents strictly into bare $U$. Crossing this infrared and confinement barrier separates fixed-depth ballistic
scattering from long-lived composite matter and unbounded computation on the infinite mesh.

\textbf{Precision Standard Model constants and the vacuum-energy gap:} Reproducing Koide's $10^{-5}$ lepton-mass
precision, deriving $\alpha(0) = 1/137.036$, fixing $\Omega_c / \Omega_b$ dynamically, and closing the 61-decade Hubble
and $122$-decade cosmological constant gap \cite{weinberg1989} remain open. Because exponential trees accommodate
multi-decade ratios post hoc whenever integer depths are assigned externally, a predictive continuum theory must derive
bulk depths, dimensionless couplings, and infrared vacuum-energy cancellation dynamically without free tuning.

\textbf{Operational Born bridge:} Within pure linear unitarity on the fixed honeycomb, the operational bridge linking
squared weights $|\psi_k|^2$ to single-outcome empirical frequencies remains an explicit interpretive postulate. Even
with monotonic hyperbolic decoherence, redundant classical records, and a unique quadratic branch measure ($p = 2$),
linear superposition alone does not select one realized outcome per run. Clarifying whether single-outcome statistics
require a dynamical collapse extension, a typicality postulate, or an observer-level axiom marks the boundary between
unitary honeycomb dynamics and laboratory measurement.
