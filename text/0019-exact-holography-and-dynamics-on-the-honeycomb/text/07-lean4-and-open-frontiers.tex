\subsection{Open problems and honest boundaries}

Six sharply bounded open problems lie beyond the theorems certified in this paper.

\textbf{Continuum 4D Yang--Mills limit ($a \to 0$):} Section~\ref{sec:gauge}'s unconditional 4D $D_4$ mass gaps
($\beta_* = 0.285141$ for $SU(2)$ and $0.563019$ for $SU(3)$) hold strictly inside the strong-coupling lattice regime.
Crossing toward $\beta \to \infty$ requires multi-scale renormalization group control \cite{balaban1989}, and the
finite subgroups $2T$ and $\Sigma(648)$ face weak-coupling freezing transitions whose continuum scaling window remains
open.

\textbf{Dynamical electroweak breaking and 4D chiral gauging:} Section~\ref{sec:gauge} establishes massless chiral
$2T < SU(2)_L$ kinematics, left--right parity breaking, and faithful $\mathbb{Z}_{18}$ hypercharges, while dynamical
Higgs condensation ($m_W / m_Z = \cos\theta_W$), Yukawa mass generation, dynamical 3+1D $SU(3)_C$ confinement, and
bulk-covariant four-dimensional domain-wall gauging with mirror symmetric mass generation remain open.

\textbf{Nonlinear 4D quantum gravity and origin of $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$:} Section~\ref{sec:gravity} establishes causal
weak-field backreaction, gravitating negative binding energy, and ghost-free 3D $D_4$-isotropic spin-2 waves, while
deriving full nonlinear Einstein backreaction ($G_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}$), permanent husk matter trapping, and
the microscopic dynamical origin of $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$ directly from bare $U$ remains open.

\textbf{Light mass generation, autonomous bare-$U$ routing, and field compilation:} Section~\ref{sec:composites} shows
that exact $E = mc^2$ and sub-$1\%$ $1/\gamma$ clocks emerge once $m_{\mathrm{eff}} \ll 1$, whereas bare $U$ sits at
$m = \pi/3$. Deriving an infrared mechanism for $m_{\mathrm{eff}} \ll 1$, emergent 3+1D fermionic statistics, an
autonomous unclocked universal Turing machine inside bare $U$ \cite{pollard2026railway}, and compiling abstract
homeostatic or recurrent agents down to bare-$U$ field dynamics remain open.

\textbf{Precision Standard Model constants and the vacuum-energy gap:} As Section~\ref{sec:composites}'s $2{,}801$-base
null audit shows, $\{3,4,3,4\}$'s Steinberg warp compresses five decades of fermion mass ratios only at order unity once
depths are fixed externally, without reproducing Koide's $10^{-5}$ precision, deriving $\alpha(0) = 1/137.036$, fixing
$\Omega_c / \Omega_b$ dynamically, or closing the 61-decade Hubble ($122$-decade cosmological constant) gap
\cite{weinberg1989}.

\textbf{Operational Born bridge:} Section~\ref{sec:decoherence} locks any consistent branch measure uniquely to $p = 2$
via exact cyclotomic envariance and three-dimensional Gleason non-contextuality, while showing that hyperbolic outward
export enforces strict Markovianity where flat baths fail. Within pure linear unitarity on the fixed honeycomb, the
operational bridge linking squared weights $|\psi_k|^2$ to single-outcome empirical frequencies remains an explicit
interpretive postulate.
