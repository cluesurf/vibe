\subsection{Open frontiers and honest boundaries}

Six clearly bounded open problems remain beyond the certified theorems of this manuscript (note that active exploratory
drafts on dynamical spin-2 backreaction, bulk-covariant chiral gauging, and additional Lean~4 modules remain under
adversarial revision and are intentionally excluded from every claim in this paper):
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Continuum 4D Yang--Mills ($a \to 0$):} Section~\ref{sec:gauge}'s unconditional mass gaps
($\beta_* = 0.285141$ for $SU(2)$, $0.563019$ for $SU(3)$) operate in the strong-coupling lattice regime. Crossing the
intermediate crossover toward $\beta \to \infty$ requires multi-scale Balaban-style block-spin renormalization
\cite{balaban1989}, and Track~W16's finite subgroups $2T$ and $\Sigma(648)$ face weak-coupling freezing transitions
whose continuum scaling window remains open.
\item \textbf{Dynamical Higgs/EWSB and bulk-covariant 4D chiral gauging:} Track~W16 establishes massless chiral
$2T < SU(2)_L$ kinematics, $\mathcal{P}_{LR}$ breaking, and faithful $\mathbb{Z}_{18}$ hypercharges, while dynamical
scalar Higgs condensation ($m_W/m_Z = \cos\theta_W$), Yukawa mass generation, full dynamical $SU(3)_C$ QCD confinement
in 3+1D, and bulk-covariant 4D domain-wall gauging with mirror symmetric mass generation remain open.
\item \textbf{Dynamical nonlinear 4D gravity and origin of $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$:} Deriving self-consistent Einstein
backreaction ($G_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}$, $\gamma_{\mathrm{PPN}} = \beta_{\mathrm{PPN}} = 1$) and the dynamical
symmetry-breaking origin of the cusp-anchored warp profile $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$ from microscopic quantum states remains open.
\item \textbf{Microscopic light Dirac mass generation and 3+1D spin-statistics:} Section~\ref{sec:composites} shows that
exact $E = mc^2$ and sub-$1\%$ $1/\gamma$ moving clocks emerge cleanly inside bare $U$'s $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$
architecture once $m_{\mathrm{eff}} \ll 1$, whereas verbatim bare $U$ sits at cutoff mass $m = \pi/3$. Deriving a
microscopic infrared mechanism yielding $m_{\mathrm{eff}} \ll 1$, a single-potential radial $1s \leftrightarrow 2s$
relativistic 2-body clock, and emergent 3+1D fermionic exchange statistics from bare ternary tones remains open.
\item \textbf{Precision Standard Model constants and the cosmological-constant gap:} As Track~W18's $2{,}801$-base null
audit demonstrates, $\{3,4,3,4\}$'s Steinberg warp compresses five decades of fermion hierarchies only at $O(1)$ once
depth assignments are fixed, without deriving Koide's $10^{-5}$ precision, $\alpha(0) = 1/137.036$, or resolving the
61-decade bare Hubble ($122$-decade $\Lambda$) gap \cite{weinberg1989}.
\item \textbf{Operational Born bridge and branch measurement:} Track~W17 locks any consistent branch measure uniquely to
$p = 2$ via envariance and 3D Gleason non-contextuality, while Track~W14B quantifies lumpability, QFI horizons, and
Markovian outward export. Across pure unitary evolution on the fixed honeycomb, the operational Born postulate linking
$|\psi_k|^2$ to definite single-outcome observations remains explicitly an interpretive bridge rather than a theorem of
linear unitarity alone.
\end{enumerate}
