\subsection{Open problems}

Six sharply bounded open problems lie beyond the theorems certified in this paper, each testing whether this discrete
substrate scales to continuum physics.

\textbf{Continuum 4D Yang--Mills limit ($a \to 0$):} Section~\ref{sec:gauge}'s strong-coupling 4D $D_4$ mass gaps
($\beta_* = 0.285141$ for $SU(2)$ and $0.563019$ for $SU(3)$) hold strictly inside the strong-coupling regime. Crossing
toward $\beta \to \infty$ requires multi-scale renormalization group control \cite{balaban1989}, while $2T$ and
$\Sigma(648)$ face weak-coupling freezing transitions. Strong-coupling contraction certifies exponential decay only at
lattice-scale correlation lengths, whereas continuum Yang--Mills theory requires diverging correlation length at fixed
physical mass. Testing whether $D_4$'s rank-4 isotropy tightens multi-scale block-spin bounds and whether native
subgroups admit any non-freezing crossover decides if the 24-cell's gauge geometry survives the continuum limit.

\textbf{Dynamical electroweak breaking and 4D chiral gauging:} Section~\ref{sec:gauge} certifies static background
$2T < SU(2)_L$ domain-wall kinematics and algebraic anomaly checks on input one-generation chiral matter, while deriving
chiral representations, dynamical Higgs condensation ($m_W / m_Z = \cos\theta_W$), Yukawa masses, dynamical 3+1D
$SU(3)_C$ confinement, and bulk-covariant four-dimensional chiral gauging with mirror symmetric mass generation remain
open. Promoting static cusp-wall fluxes to dynamical four-dimensional gauge links confronts mirror-fermion doubling on
the deep bulk wall directly. A non-perturbative mirror-gapping mechanism preserving local gauge invariance and
spontaneous symmetry breaking is the decisive test of whether these building blocks unify into one chiral gauge
automaton.

\textbf{Nonlinear 4D quantum gravity, dark-sector coupling, and permanent charge asymmetry:} Section~\ref{sec:gravity}
treats causal scalar backreaction with assumed $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$, vacuum linearized $D_4$-isotropic spin-2 waves,
kinematic LLY curvature, and transient bare-$U$ charge pumping separately. Unifying scalar backreaction with nonlinear
Einstein dynamics ($G_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}$), deriving $w(D)$ from bare $U$, restoring relativistic two-body
bound-state WEP, overcoming bare $U$'s exact $3.1 \times 10^{-15}$ sterile dark-sector null, and achieving permanent
husk trapping with $\Delta B \neq 0$ and dynamical $\mathcal{CP}$ breaking remain open. Weak-field lapse infall and
linearized gravitons probe separate metric limits, whereas relativistic gravity couples stress-energy nonlinearly to
curvature and equates inertial and passive mass for bound composites. Deriving bulk localization, sterile gravitational
visibility, and permanent husk trapping from unitary evolution tests whether gravity and dark-sector phenomenology arise
natively on $\{3,4,3,4\}$.

\textbf{Light mass generation, strong unbounded-loop computability, and mesh-level compilation:}
Section~\ref{sec:composites} shows that $E = mc^2$ and sub-$1\%$ $1/\gamma$ clocks emerge once $m_{\mathrm{eff}} \ll 1$,
