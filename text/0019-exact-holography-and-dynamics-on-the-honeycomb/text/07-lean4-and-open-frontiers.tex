\subsection{Open problems and honest boundaries}

Six sharply bounded open problems lie beyond the certified theorems established in this paper. Keeping these boundaries
explicit separates proved mathematical structure from speculative physical extrapolation:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Continuum 4D Yang--Mills limit ($a \to 0$):} Section~\ref{sec:gauge}'s unconditional 4D $D_4$ mass gaps
($\beta_* = 0.285141$ for $SU(2)$ and $0.563019$ for $SU(3)$) operate strictly inside the strong-coupling lattice
regime. Crossing the intermediate crossover toward $\beta \to \infty$ requires multi-scale renormalization group control
\cite{balaban1989}, and the finite gauge subgroups $2T$ and $\Sigma(648)$ face weak-coupling freezing transitions whose
continuum scaling window remains open.
\item \textbf{Dynamical electroweak breaking and 4D chiral gauging:} Section~\ref{sec:gauge} establishes massless chiral
$2T < SU(2)_L$ kinematics, left--right parity breaking, and faithful $\mathbb{Z}_{18}$ hypercharges, while dynamical
Higgs condensation ($m_W / m_Z = \cos\theta_W$), Yukawa mass generation, dynamical 3+1D $SU(3)_C$ confinement, and
bulk-covariant four-dimensional domain-wall gauging with mirror symmetric mass generation remain open.
\item \textbf{Nonlinear 4D gravity and origin of $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$:} Deriving self-consistent Einstein backreaction
($G_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}$ with $\gamma_{\mathrm{PPN}} = \beta_{\mathrm{PPN}} = 1$) and the dynamical origin of
the cusp-anchored warp profile $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$ from microscopic quantum states remains open.
\item \textbf{Microscopic light mass generation and 3+1D spin-statistics:} Section~\ref{sec:composites} shows that exact
$E = mc^2$ and sub-$1\%$ $1/\gamma$ moving clocks emerge cleanly inside bare $U$'s contact architecture once
$m_{\mathrm{eff}} \ll 1$, whereas the verbatim bare beat sits at cutoff mass $m = \pi/3$. Deriving an infrared mechanism
that generates $m_{\mathrm{eff}} \ll 1$, constructing a single-potential radial $1s \leftrightarrow 2s$ relativistic
composite clock, and deriving emergent 3+1D fermionic exchange statistics from bare ternary tones remain open.
\item \textbf{Precision Standard Model constants and the vacuum-energy gap:} As Section~\ref{sec:composites}'s
$2{,}801$-base null audit demonstrates, $\{3,4,3,4\}$'s Steinberg warp compresses five decades of fermion mass ratios
only at order unity once depth assignments are fixed externally, without reproducing Koide's $10^{-5}$ precision,
deriving $\alpha(0) = 1/137.036$, or closing the 61-decade Hubble ($122$-decade cosmological constant) gap
\cite{weinberg1989}.
\item \textbf{Operational Born bridge:} Section~\ref{sec:decoherence} locks any consistent branch measure uniquely to
$p = 2$ via exact cyclotomic envariance and three-dimensional Gleason non-contextuality, while showing that hyperbolic
outward export enforces strict Markovianity where flat baths fail. Within pure linear unitarity on the fixed honeycomb,
the operational bridge connecting squared branch weights $|\psi_k|^2$ to definite single-outcome empirical frequencies
remains an explicit interpretive postulate.
\end{enumerate}
