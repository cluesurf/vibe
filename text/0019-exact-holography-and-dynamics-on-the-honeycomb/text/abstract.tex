\begin{abstract}
We analyze a reversible ternary quantum cellular automaton $U$ on the fixed 4D hyperbolic honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$,
separating native theorems from added structure. Each 24-cell updates 24 signed ternary slots by one palindromic beat
over the cyclotomic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$, observed on the flat cubic horosphere $\{4,3,4\}$ (the husk).
Exact bounds certify spectral gap above one half and size-independent bulk export. Classification isolates a unique
covariant holographic tensor forming a static all-radius isometry separating motion from transverse refinement
kinematically, though bare $U$ exits in one beat. Bare dynamics yields a compacton no-go theorem, an integrable
collinear bound tower (mixed-tone repulsion, mass-deformed clocks), weak autonomous BQP universality beside exact
unbounded-loop obstructions, and parity superselection requiring links for spin-1 bosons. Added gauge links support
single-speed Maxwell--Dirac waves and static $2T$ chiral walls, beside separate strong-coupling $SU(2)/SU(3)$ Wilson
gaps on flat $D_4$. Separate gravitational extensions yield scalar infall, binding weight, and redshifts versus
linearized $D_4$-isotropic spin-2 waves. Chiral phases pump transient husk charge from parity-asymmetric states, while
sterile modes decouple in bare $U$ and gain cored halos via auxiliary kernels. Open-ray models yield Quantum Darwinism
and idealized Markovianity, envariance and Gleason's theorem select $p{=}2$, and controls test boundary-filtered
autonomy. Core proofs compile in Lean~4, leaving continuum limits, nonlinear gravity, Turing universality, and the Born
bridge open.
\end{abstract}
