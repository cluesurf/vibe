\begin{abstract}
We analyze a reversible ternary quantum cellular automaton $U$ on the fixed 4D hyperbolic honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$,
separating native theorems from added structure. Each 24-cell updates 24 signed ternary slots by one palindromic beat
over the cyclotomic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$, observed on the flat cubic horosphere $\{4,3,4\}$ (the husk).
Exact bounds certify spectral gap above one half and size-independent bulk export. Classification isolates a unique
covariant holographic tensor forming a static all-radius isometry separating motion from transverse refinement
kinematically, though bare $U$ exits in one beat. Bare dynamics gives a compacton no-go theorem, an integrable collinear
bound tower (mixed-tone repulsion, mass-deformed clocks), and parity superselection requiring links for spin-1 bosons.
Added gauge links support single-speed Maxwell--Dirac waves and static $2T$ chiral walls, beside separate
strong-coupling $SU(2)/SU(3)$ Wilson gaps on flat $D_4$. Two separate gravitational extensions give scalar infall,
binding weight, and redshifts, versus linearized $D_4$-isotropic spin-2 waves. Chiral phases pump transient husk charge
from parity-asymmetric states, while sterile modes decouple in bare $U$, gaining cored halos via auxiliary
$\mathbb{H}^4$ or holographic kernels. Reduced open-ray models yield Quantum Darwinism and idealized Markovianity,
cyclotomic envariance and Gleason's theorem select $p{=}2$, and abstract controls illustrate boundary-filtered autonomy.
Core proofs compile in zero-dependency Lean~4, leaving continuum limits, nonlinear gravity, bare-$U$ universality, and
the Born bridge open.
\end{abstract}
