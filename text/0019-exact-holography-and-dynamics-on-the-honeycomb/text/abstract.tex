\begin{abstract}
We investigate the spectral geometry, discrete holography, many-body dynamics, gauge structure, and quantum foundations
of a deterministic, reversible ternary quantum cellular automaton defined on the fixed, infinite four-dimensional
hyperbolic 24-cell honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$. Each 24-cell carries 24 signed ternary states evolving under a single
palindromic unitary beat over the cyclotomic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$, with three-dimensional observables read
on the flat cubic horosphere $\{4,3,4\}$ surrounding an ideal cusp. Exact integer bounds tighten the $l^2$ spectral
radius of $\{3,4,3,4\}$ to $[11.439047, 11.443713]$, proving a normalized spectral gap above $1/2$. On the same
geometry, exhaustive ternary classification isolates a unique $W(F_4)$-covariant holographic tensor $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$
that glues into an all-radius hyperbolic code, admits a dynamical bridge to the microscopic beat, and realizes an
inductive continuum limit along bulk depth without Zeno supertasks. For bare many-body dynamics, we rule out
finite-support compactons unconditionally, establish an exact collinear composite tower with closed-form
$1/\sqrt{3k^2+1}$ dispersion and relativistic light-mass convergence, and prove an all-body parity superselection theorem
showing that massless spin-1 bosons require dynamical link registers. Adding native finite-subgroup links yields a
single-speed gauge--matter automaton with exact Gauss laws, while block-alternating Dobrushin bounds push unconditional
4D $D_4$ lattice Yang--Mills mass gaps past $3\times$ Bakry--\'Emery. Multi-body partial traces establish Quantum
Darwinism, $p = 2$ Born--Gleason uniqueness, and Markovian outward export, backed by 341 machine-checked Lean~4
theorems. All claims maintain strict tier separation, leaving continuum gauge limits, dynamical gravity, and the
operational Born bridge explicitly open.
\end{abstract}
