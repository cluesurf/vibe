\begin{abstract}
We investigate the spectral geometry, discrete holography, many-body dynamics, gauge structure, emergent gravity,
matter--antimatter asymmetry, quantum foundations, and multiscale organization of a deterministic, reversible ternary
quantum cellular automaton on the fixed four-dimensional hyperbolic 24-cell honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$. Each 24-cell
carries 24 signed ternary states updated by a single palindromic unitary beat over the cyclotomic ring
$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$, with three-dimensional observables read on the flat cubic horosphere $\{4,3,4\}$ surrounding
an ideal cusp. Exact integer bounds certify a normalized spectral gap exceeding one half, turning the fixed hyperbolic
bulk into an unfillable entropy sink. Exhaustive ternary classification isolates a unique symmetry-covariant
holographic tensor that glues across all radii into an exact bulk-to-boundary isometry and resolves Zeno's supertask
dilemma by separating finite-hop longitudinal motion from transverse bulk-depth refinement. For bare many-body
dynamics, we prove an unconditional compacton no-go theorem, derive an exact collinear composite tower exhibiting
quantum chaos, entangling collisions, and relativistic light-mass clocks, and establish an all-body parity
superselection theorem proving that massless spin-1 bosons require dynamical link registers. Adding native
finite-subgroup links yields a single-speed gauge--matter automaton alongside unconditional 4D $D_4$ lattice
Yang--Mills mass gaps, while coupling to a causal metric field produces inverse-square infall, gravitating negative
binding energy, non-tautological clock redshifts, and ghost-free $D_4$-isotropic spin-2 waves. On the curved cusp, bare
chiral phases pump transient net husk charge while deep-bulk neutral modes remain sterile yet gravitationally visible
through cored halos and boundary entropy. Finally, hyperbolic outward export yields Quantum Darwinism, Born--Gleason
uniqueness, strict Markovianity, and selective boundary-filtered autonomy, backed by a zero-dependency Lean~4
formalization, with continuum gauge limits, nonlinear quantum gravity, and the operational Born bridge left explicitly
open.
\end{abstract}
