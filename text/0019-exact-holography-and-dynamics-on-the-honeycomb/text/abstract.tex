\begin{abstract}
We study a deterministic, reversible ternary quantum cellular automaton on the fixed four-dimensional hyperbolic
24-cell honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$, mapping what physics follows from its native geometry and where extra structure is
forced. Each 24-cell holds 24 signed ternary slots updated by one palindromic unitary beat over
$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$, with three-dimensional observables read on the flat cubic horosphere $\{4,3,4\}$ around an
ideal cusp. Exact integer bounds certify a normalized spectral gap exceeding one half, turning the bulk into an
unfillable entropy sink. Exhaustive classification isolates a unique symmetry-covariant holographic tensor gluing
across all radii into an exact isometry, resolving Zeno's supertask dilemma by separating finite-hop motion from
transverse bulk-depth refinement. In bare many-body dynamics, we prove a compacton no-go theorem, derive a collinear
composite tower showing quantum chaos and light-mass relativistic clocks, and prove an all-body parity superselection
theorem forcing dynamical links for massless spin-1 bosons. Native finite-subgroup links give a single-speed
gauge--matter automaton and unconditional 4D $D_4$ lattice Yang--Mills mass gaps, while a local metric field yields
causal inverse-square infall, gravitating binding weight, non-tautological clock redshifts, and ghost-free
$D_4$-isotropic spin-2 waves. Bare chiral phases pump transient net husk charge while sterile bulk modes form cored
dark halos, and hyperbolic export enforces Quantum Darwinism, Born--Gleason uniqueness, Markovianity, and
boundary-filtered autonomy, backed by a zero-dependency Lean~4 suite, leaving continuum gauge limits, nonlinear gravity,
and the operational Born bridge open.
\end{abstract}
