\begin{abstract}
We study the spectral geometry, discrete holography, many-body dynamics, gauge structure, quantum foundations, emergent
gravity, matter--antimatter asymmetry, and multiscale organization of a deterministic, reversible ternary quantum
cellular automaton defined on the fixed, infinite four-dimensional hyperbolic 24-cell honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$. Each
24-cell carries 24 signed ternary states updated by one palindromic unitary beat over the cyclotomic ring
$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$, with three-dimensional observables read on the flat cubic horosphere $\{4,3,4\}$ around an
ideal cusp. Exact integer bounds prove a normalized spectral gap exceeding one half, turning the fixed hyperbolic bulk
into an unfillable entropy sink. Exhaustive ternary classification isolates a unique symmetry-covariant holographic
tensor that glues across all radii into an exact bulk-to-boundary isometry and resolves Zeno's supertask dilemma by
separating finite-hop longitudinal motion from transverse depth refinement. For bare many-body dynamics, we rule out
finite-support compactons unconditionally, derive an exact collinear composite tower with quantum chaos, entangling
collisions, and relativistic light-mass clocks, and prove an all-body parity superselection theorem showing that
massless spin-1 bosons require dynamical link registers. Coupling native finite-subgroup links yields a single-speed
gauge--matter automaton alongside unconditional 4D $D_4$ lattice Yang--Mills mass gaps, while coupling to a causal
metric field produces inverse-square infall, gravitating negative binding energy, non-tautological clock redshifts, and
ghost-free $D_4$-isotropic spin-2 waves. On the curved cusp, bare chiral phases pump transient net husk charge while
leaving deep-bulk neutral modes sterile yet gravitationally visible through cored halos and boundary entropy. Finally,
hyperbolic outward export establishes Quantum Darwinism, Born--Gleason uniqueness, strict Markovianity, and selective
boundary-filtered autonomy, backed by a zero-dependency Lean~4 formalization, with continuum gauge limits, nonlinear
quantum gravity, and the operational Born bridge left explicitly open.
\end{abstract}
