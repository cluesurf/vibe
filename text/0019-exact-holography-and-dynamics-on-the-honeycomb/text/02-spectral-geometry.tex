\section{Spectral Geometry}
\label{sec:spectral}

Negative curvature governs diffusive return, two-body scattering thresholds, and local entropy export on the infinite
24-regular $\{3,4,3,4\}$ dock graph through its $l^2$ adjacency spectral radius $\rho = \|A\|_{l^2(V) \to l^2(V)}$.
On any flat Euclidean lattice $\mathbb{Z}^d$, the adjacency spectrum reaches the vertex degree ($\rho/\deg = 1$), so the
normalized spectral gap vanishes and diffusive bulk excitations recycle indefinitely. On $\{3,4,3,4\}$, a simple global
Busemann Schur weight bounds $\rho$ from above only at $104/7 \approx 14.857143$ against a spherical-shell lower bound
of $11.013439$. Working strictly on the bare dock graph, we collapse that uncertainty by nearly three orders of
magnitude to $\rho = 11.4414 \pm 0.0024$, certify a normalized spectral gap $\lambda_{\mathrm{gap}} = 1 - \rho/24 > 1/2$,
and prove a size-independent geometric ceiling on local entropy export into the fixed bulk.

\subsection{Hyperbolic decay}

To appreciate the physical weight of the spectral radius $\rho$, contrast a four-dimensional flat Euclidean lattice
$\mathbb{Z}^4$ (degree $8$) or flat $D_4$ lattice (degree $24$) against the curved hyperbolic honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$
(also degree $24$). Every flat lattice is amenable: as a ball $B_r$ of radius $r$ grows, its boundary-to-volume ratio
$|\partial B_r|/|B_r|$ decays as $4/r \to 0$. By Kesten's amenability criterion, this vanishing surface ratio forces
$\rho/\deg = 1$, zero normalized spectral gap ($\lambda_{\mathrm{gap}} = 0$), and zero Cheeger isoperimetric constant
($h = 0$). Physically, diffusive probability on a flat grid spreads only algebraically ($t^{-2}$ in four dimensions), so
disordered energy and entanglement entropy emitted by a finite region remain trapped nearby and recycle back unless an
external infinite heat bath or non-unitary absorbing boundary is appended by hand.

Negative curvature reverses every entry of that ledger without sacrificing strict reversibility or fixed spatial volume
(Table~\ref{tab:flat-vs-hyperbolic}). Because concentric shells of $\{3,4,3,4\}$ expand exponentially at rate
$\lambda \approx 18.28$, certifying $\rho < 12$ ($\lambda_{\mathrm{gap}} > 1/2$) carries two concrete physical
consequences. First, under unbiased diffusive bulk transport, the normalized $l^2$ operator $A/24$ contracts amplitude
norms by strictly more than one half on every single beat ($\|A/24\| \le 0.476822 < 1/2$), suppressing lingering
diffusive clouds exponentially faster than $2^{-t}$. Second, shrinking the bracket width for $\rho$ from $3.84$ down to
$0.004666$ ($0.04\%$ relative uncertainty) fixes $\rho$ sharply enough to govern multi-body dynamics later in the paper:
in Section~\ref{sec:composites}, the exact upper bound on $\rho$ places the two-body isotropic pair contact eigenvalue

into resonances rather than forming bound states.

\subsection{Spectral upper bounds}

Because the dock graph $W(\Gamma_{24})$ is bipartite by word length $|u|$, every undirected edge $\{x, y\}$ has a unique
upward orientation $x \to y = x s_b$ with $|y| = |x| + 1$, where the generator $b$ lies outside the right-descent clique
$D(x)$ (the set of generators shortening $x$, which pairwise commute in $\Gamma_{24}$) and inside $D(y)$. By Nagnibeda's
reciprocal Schur criterion \cite{nagnibeda1997}, any positive edge-weight assignment obeying $w(x \to y) w(y \to x) = 1$
bounds the spectral radius from above:
\begin{equation}
\rho(\{3,4,3,4\}) \le \sup_{u \in V} \sum_{v \sim u} w(u \to v).
\label{eq:schur-criterion}
\end{equation}
Intuitively, a naive global Busemann weight stalls at $104/7 \approx 14.86$ because it assigns identical weights to every
upward hop, ignoring the sharp local contrast between single-descent tips ($|D(u)| = 1$, with $23$ outward facets) and
three-descent ridges ($|D(u)| = 3$, with $21$ outward facets). To let the Schur weights adapt to local geometry without
truncating the infinite graph, we condition each edge weight on a finite local horizon that both endpoints can compute
identically. Exact edge reciprocity holds automatically on all infinitely many docks whenever the edge label $e$ is
deterministically computable from both endpoints' Depth-$L$ suffix trees $\mathrm{St}_L$ (the finite local word-ending
neighborhood around each vertex). The maximal shared label at Depth~$L$ is the \emph{upward-end flag orbit}
$e = [\mathrm{St}_L(y), b]_{W(F_4)}$. The lower vertex $x$ constructs $\mathrm{St}_L(y)$ from its own suffix tree and
the step label $b$, while the upper vertex $y$ already carries $\mathrm{St}_L(y)$ and its descent $b$.

This upward-end flag structure reduces the infinite-graph inequality \eqref{eq:schur-criterion} to a finite nonlinear
eigenvalue problem. Each flag $e$ has multiplicity $d_e$ and belongs to a unique owner state $v = [\mathrm{St}_L(y)]$,
so its reciprocal weight appears in only one downward sum $b_v$. Setting $y_v = 1/b_v$, introducing positive simplex
weights $\theta_e$ summing to unity over each owner set $B_v$, and letting $P(\theta)$ denote the resulting
$\theta$-weighted upward transition matrix on flag-owner states converts every row sum into the fixed-point system
\begin{equation}
b_v = \sum_{e \in B_v} \frac{d_e}{f_e},
\qquad
f_e = \frac{d_e}{\theta_e}\, y_{\mathrm{owner}(e)},
\qquad
S_v(b, \theta) = \bigl(P(\theta)\, y\bigr)_v + \frac{1}{y_v}.
\label{eq:howard-row}
\end{equation}
Alternating monotone row equalization, exact AM--GM scalar rescaling, left-Perron dual updates, and Howard policy
updates on $\theta$ solves the Depth-5 automaton to a primal-dual gap below $10^{-8}$. Across Depths~$2$ through~$4$,
the upper optima shrink monotonically while the dual-weighted 4-cycle curl
\begin{equation}
\kappa_{\mathrm{curl}} = \left|\ln \frac{f_a\, f_{ab}}{f_b\, f_{ba}}\right|
\label{eq:four-cycle-curl}
\end{equation}
decays from $0.1370$ (Depth~2) to $0.0181$ (Depth~4). Because the exact $\rho$-harmonic eigenfunction on a graph yields
a conservative edge-ratio potential with zero circulation around every 4-cycle, this shrinking curl provides a direct
geometric diagnostic that the finite-depth flag weights are converging onto the true infinite-graph ground state.
Quantizing the Depth-5 weights to exact rationals at denominator $Q = 10^6$ and evaluating every row ceiling in
arbitrary-precision integer arithmetic proves
\begin{equation}
\rho(\{3,4,3,4\}) \le 11.443713,
\qquad
\frac{\rho}{24} \le 0.476822 < \frac{1}{2},
\qquad
\lambda_{\mathrm{gap}} \ge 0.523178.
\label{eq:rho-upper}
\end{equation}
As an independent structural check, the Depth-5 automaton reproduces exact spherical shell counts through radius $r = 6$
with zero reciprocity defects across the full radius-3 ball.

\subsection{Markov witnesses}

Tightening the matching lower bound requires overcoming a geometric blind spot of radial functions. Standard
spherical-shell Rayleigh--Ritz subspaces stall near $11.33$ because word-length spheres freeze the stationary
clique-size distribution near $(0.77, 0.22, 0.01)$, heavily overweighting single-descent tips ($|D| = 1$). By contrast,
the optimal Depth-5 dual measure places $59.7\%$ of its weight on multi-descent junctions ($|D| \ge 2$) where two or
three orthogonal facets meet simultaneously. Any trial vector that depends only on radial distance from one origin dock
cannot concentrate amplitude on those multi-descent junctions and therefore leaves a permanent spectral gap between the
variational lower bound and the true $\rho$.

To decouple the bulk trial measure from word length while keeping every edge of the infinite graph exact, we construct
positive Markov product witnesses on the unique left-normal Cartier--Foata factorization $w = C_1 C_2 \cdots C_k$
\cite{cartier1969}, the canonical greedy grouping of a Coxeter word into maximal commuting blocks $C_t$. Each block is
one of the $216$ non-empty cliques of the commuting-facet graph $\Gamma_{24}$ (which has $24$ vertices, $96$ edges, $96$
triangles, and zero 4-cliques), and every generator in $C_t$ lies in the forbidden-commutation set $F(C_{t-1})$
(failing to commute with at least one generator in $C_{t-1}$). Evaluating an exact Rayleigh quotient on an infinite graph
normally fails when appending one step triggers an unbounded cascade of block rearrangements. On $\Gamma_{24}$, that
cascade never occurs.

\begin{theorem}[Exact Upward Insertion and $Q_3$ Zero-Cascade]
\label{thm:q3-cascade}
For any reduced Cartier--Foata normal form $w = C_1 \cdots C_k$ ($k \ge 0$) in $W(\Gamma_{24})$ and upward edge
$w \to w s_b$ ($|w s_b| = |w| + 1$), let $m \in \{0, \dots, k\}$ be the maximal integer such that
$C_{k-m+1}, \dots, C_k \subseteq \mathrm{Adj}(b)$ (taking $F(C_0) := V(\Gamma_{24})$). Then $w s_b = C_1 \cdots C_k
\{b\}$ when $m = 0$ (including $k = 0$), and for every $1 \le m \le k$,
\begin{equation}
w s_b = C_1 \cdots C_{k-m}\,(C_{k-m+1} \cup \{b\})\, C_{k-m+2} \cdots C_k
\label{eq:cf-insertion}
\end{equation}
is already in exact Cartier--Foata normal form with zero cascade on the trailing blocks $C_{k-m+2}, \dots, C_k$ (and no
leading prefix when $m = k$). Furthermore, the commuting link $\mathrm{Adj}(b)$ in $\Gamma_{24}$ is isomorphic to the
triangle-free 3-cube $Q_3$ ($8$ vertices, $12$ edges, $20$ non-empty cliques, and $212$ valid adjacent clique pairs).
\end{theorem}

Geometrically, Theorem~\ref{thm:q3-cascade} means that appending a facet step $s_b$ slides $b$ leftward through at most
one chain of commuting blocks and merges it into a single earlier clique without disturbing any trailing block. That
stability allows exact evaluation of the infinite-graph Rayleigh quotient for any $W(F_4)$-invariant 2-step
Cartier--Foata Markov witness of the form
\begin{equation}
\psi(C_1, \dots, C_k)
= W_k \left(\prod_{t=3}^{k} \omega(C_{t-2}, C_{t-1}, C_t)\right) v(C_{k-2}, C_{k-1}, C_k).
\label{eq:cf-markov-witness}
\end{equation}
Because the commuting link $\mathrm{Adj}(b) \cong Q_3$ is triangle-free and contractive, its slide-transfer matrix has
spectral radius $\rho(T_{Q_3}) = 0.210548 < 1$. Deep slides therefore decay geometrically, and summing over all slide
depths $m = 0, 1, \dots, \infty$ closes analytically via a $57 \times 57$ pair-orbit Perron eigenvector and a
$212$-state $Q_3$ Neumann resolvent $(I - T_{Q_3}^T)^{-1}$.

Optimizing the $4{,}440$ triple-orbit bulk and boundary weights reaches the asymptotic lower bound $\rho \ge 11.439240$.
Quantizing those weights to positive integers on finite Cartier--Foata heights $k \in [10, 420]$ and summing slide
depths in exact integer arithmetic (omitting only strictly nonnegative tail terms) certifies the concrete
finite-support bound $\rho \ge 11.439047$ with zero square roots and zero asymptotic limits.

\subsection{Spectral bracket}

Numerical eigenvalue solvers on finite balls approach $\rho$ strictly from below and cannot certify an upper bound on an
infinite graph. Because reciprocal Schur inequalities and Cartier--Foata witnesses reduce to finite rational tables, the
same machinery translates directly into machine-checked Lean~4 proofs valid over all infinitely many docks
simultaneously. A Depth-1 rational witness first establishes $\rho \le 335/28 < 12$ and
$\lambda_{\mathrm{gap}} \ge 337/672 > 1/2$. Formalizing the 19-state Depth-2 automaton, 23-flag reciprocity at
$Q = 10{,}000$, Theorem~\ref{thm:q3-cascade}'s $Q_3$ zero-cascade algebra, and a 57-orbit 1-step Cartier--Foata witness
collapses the kernel-verified bracket by $10.2\times$:
\begin{equation}
\frac{913}{80} = 11.4125 < \rho(\{3,4,3,4\}) \le \frac{387{,}141{,}227}{33{,}670{,}000} \le 11.498106 < 11.5,
\qquad
\lambda_{\mathrm{gap}} > \frac{13}{25} > \frac{1}{2}.
\label{eq:lean-spectral-bracket}
\end{equation}

Alongside $\rho$, the Lean~4 formalization proves the exact Steinberg cubic \eqref{eq:steinberg-cubic} unconditionally
for both spherical shells and cusp beams, certifying $18.2787 < \lambda < 18.2788$ and $l_D = 4 \cdot 5^{D-1}$ for all
$D \ge 1$. This exponential bulk geometry resolves a central thermodynamic tension of reversible discrete physics: how a
fixed, strictly unitary, volume-preserving system can shed local entropy indefinitely without creating new space or
violating microscopic reversibility. Consider any finite dock region $B$ with $n_c(B)$ crossing links to its exterior
and per-slot alphabet size $d$ ($d = 3$ for tone alone, and $d = 19 = 1 + 2 \cdot 9$ for the full empty-or-occupied
slot register with 9 internal roles). Because on-site layers leave the von Neumann entanglement entropy
$S(B) = -\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_B \ln \rho_B)$ of $B$ strictly invariant while the stream $S$ swaps outgoing boundary
slots $O$ for incoming exterior slots $I$, Araki--Lieb subadditivity \cite{araki1970} yields the Landauer-exact entropy
export capacity ceiling \cite{landauer1961}:
\begin{equation}
S(B) - S(B_{\mathrm{after}}) = S(O \mid B_r) - S(I \mid B_r) \le n_c(B)\bigl(\ln d - s_{\mathrm{in}}\bigr)
\equiv n_c(B)\, \Delta,
\label{eq:landauer-export}
\end{equation}
where $B_r$ is the interior remainder, $s_{\mathrm{in}} := S(I \mid B_r)/n_c(B)$ is the conditional entropy per
incoming slot, and $\Delta := \ln d - s_{\mathrm{in}}$. When incoming slots outside a finite excitation's light cone
are unentangled and quiescent ($s_{\mathrm{in}} = 0$), each boundary slot carries full capacity $\Delta = \ln d$.

The dynamical difference between one-dimensional confinement and hyperbolic bulk access appears immediately in the
per-dock rate $n_c(B)\Delta / |B|$. Under purely line-confined dynamics without cross-line mixing, excitations escape
only through the two endpoints of each interior antipodal track, capping the per-dock export capacity at
$2\Delta / \ell_{\max}$ for maximum interior chord length $\ell_{\max}$ (reproducing the vanishing $O(R^{-1})$
bottleneck of a flat lattice). Once cross-line mixing couples interior tracks to all 24 facets on $\{3,4,3,4\}$,
however, the certified Cheeger constant $12.33 \le h(\{3,4,3,4\}) \le 21.52$ (the infimal boundary-to-volume ratio
$\inf_{|B|<\infty} n_c(B)/|B|$) guarantees a strictly positive, size-independent lower bound on the per-dock geometric
export capacity ceiling across every finite region $B$:
\begin{equation}
\frac{n_c(B)\, \Delta}{|B|} \ge h(\{3,4,3,4\})\, \ln 19 \ge 12.33 \ln 19 = 36.30\text{ nats/dock/beat}.
\label{eq:cheeger-floor}
\end{equation}
Whether $B$ contains one dock or $10^{20}$ docks, its boundary always supplies more than twelve outward links per
interior dock to fresh, unentangled exterior slots. In thermodynamic terms, the fixed hyperbolic bulk functions as an
unfillable cold entropy sink: global unitarity and fixed spatial volume remain exact, yet local entropy can disperse
irreversibly into exponentially growing deep-bulk shells without ever hitting a finite-bath revival ceiling. Equation
\eqref{eq:cheeger-floor} establishes the geometric capacity rather than a universal saturation rate, because actual
export depends on cross-line coupling and vanishes for strictly line-confined states.

On the bare $\{3,4,3,4\}$ dock graph, exact arithmetic therefore pins the spectral radius to $0.04\%$ ($824\times$
concrete and $859\times$ asymptotic collapse), certifies a normalized spectral gap above one half alongside Steinberg
exponential growth, and establishes a size-independent $36.30\text{ nats/dock/beat}$ export ceiling that turns a fixed,
reversible mesh into an unbounded cold sink without expanding space. Two limitations remain directly at this geometric
level: the exact irrational value of $\rho(\{3,4,3,4\})$ is bracketed rather than closed in elementary form, and
Eq.~\eqref{eq:cheeger-floor} certifies geometric export capacity rather than proving dynamical thermalization for
arbitrary interacting states. Because the same exponential boundary dominance places $94.53\%$ of any large hyperbolic
ball on its outer shell, Section~\ref{sec:holography} next tests whether $W(F_4)$ symmetry admits a high-distance
ternary holographic tensor that encodes bulk quantum information isometrically onto that boundary and organizes
transverse continuum refinement.
