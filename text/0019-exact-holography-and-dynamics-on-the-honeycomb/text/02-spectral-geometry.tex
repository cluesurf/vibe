\section{Spectral Geometry: $859\times$ Collapse, Steinberg Growth, and Fixed-Mesh Export}
\label{sec:spectral}

The $l^2$ spectral radius $\rho = \|A\|_{l^2(V) \to l^2(V)}$ of the 24-regular Cayley graph of $\{3,4,3,4\}$ governs
dispersion, operator decay, and hyperbolic gap excision across all subsequent sections. Paper~0018 proved
$\rho \le 104/7 \approx 14.857143$ ($\rho/24 \le 13/21$) by a global Busemann Schur test (\code{Vibe/SchurGap.lean}),
leaving a bracket width of $3.843704$ above the lower bound $11.013439$. By combining Depth-5 Cartier--Foata upward-end
flag optimization with exact 2-step Cartier--Foata Markov witnesses (Wave-3 Track~W7, Tier~A), we collapse this bracket
by \textbf{$824\times$} in concrete exact \code{BigInt} arithmetic and \textbf{$859\times$} asymptotically, pinning
$\rho = 11.4414 \pm 0.0024$.

\subsection{Depth-5 upward-end flag automaton and Howard policy upper bounds}

Because $W(\Gamma_{24})$ is bipartite by word length $|u|$, every undirected edge $\{x, y\}$ carries a unique upward
orientation $x \to y = x s_b$ ($|y| = |x| + 1$, $b \notin D(x)$, $b \in D(y)$, where $D(u)$ is the right-descent clique
of $u$). Under Nagnibeda's reciprocal Schur criterion \cite{nagnibeda1997}, any positive edge-weight assignment satisfying
$w(x \to y) w(y \to x) = 1$ yields the unconditional upper bound $\rho \le \sup_{u \in V} \sum_{v \sim u} w(u \to v)$.
Reciprocity holds identically on the infinite graph if the edge label $e$ is computable deterministically from both
endpoints' Depth-$L$ suffix trees $\mathrm{St}_L$. The maximal shared label is the \emph{upward-end flag orbit}
$e = [\mathrm{St}_L(y), b]_{W(F_4)}$: the lower endpoint $x$ computes $\mathrm{St}_L(y) = \mathrm{step}_L(\mathrm{St}_L(x), b)$
from $b$, while the upper endpoint $y$ already holds $\mathrm{St}_L(y)$ and its descent $b \in D(y)$.

Crucially, every upward-end flag $e = [\mathrm{St}_L(y), b]$ owns a unique state $v = [\mathrm{St}_L(y)]$. Writing
$b_v = \sum_{e \in B_v} d_e / f_e$, $y_v = 1/b_v$, and simplex splits $\theta_e > 0$ ($\sum_{e \in B_v} \theta_e = 1$)
transforms the row sum into $S_v(b, \theta) = (P(\theta) y)_v + 1/y_v$. Alternating monotone row equalization, exact
AM--GM scalar rescaling, left-Perron dual updates (certifying the convex dual lower bound
$D(w) = 2 \sum_e \sqrt{w_{\mathrm{owner}(e)} d_e \sum_v w_v a_{v, e}}$), and Howard policy updates on $\theta$ solves
the Depth-5 automaton ($34{,}523$ states: $1$ root, $26{,}061$ $|D|=1$, $7{,}707$ $|D|=2$, $754$ $|D|=3$, and
$43{,}312$ flags, built in $O(1)$ per step via a $2{,}197 \times 1{,}152$ coset table) to primal-dual gap
$9.61 \times 10^{-9}$ in $1.49\text{ s}$. Converting the optimal weights to exact rationals with denominator $Q = 10^6$
and evaluating every row ceiling in exact \code{BigInt} arithmetic proves
\begin{equation}
\rho(\{3,4,3,4\}) \le 11.443713,
\qquad
\frac{\rho}{24} \le 0.476822 < \frac{1}{2},
\qquad
\lambda_{\mathrm{gap}} = 1 - \frac{\rho}{24} \ge 0.523178.
\label{eq:rho-upper}
\end{equation}
Across Depths $2, 3, 4, 5$, the upper optima drop geometrically ($11.4975 \to 11.4541 \to 11.44565 \to 11.44369$,
shrinking by ratios $0.101, 0.195, 0.231$), while the dual-weighted 4-cycle curl
$|\ln((f_a f_{ab})/(f_b f_{ba}))|$ decays monotonically ($0.1370 \to 0.0462 \to 0.0181$), showing convergence toward the
curl-free $\rho$-harmonic potential ratio. The Depth-5 automaton reproduces all exact ball shell sizes through $r = 6$
($1, 24, 456, 8{,}376, 153{,}192, 2{,}800{,}344, 51{,}187{,}080$) with zero integrality or reciprocity defects across all
$8{,}857$ docks and $10{,}968$ upward edges of $B_3$.

\subsection{Exact Cartier--Foata Markov witnesses and the $Q_3$ zero-cascade theorem}

Spherical-shell Rayleigh--Ritz spaces $V_L$ stall near $11.33\text{--}11.34$ because word-length shells pin the
stationary clique-size distribution at $[0.7685, 0.2181, 0.0134]$, whereas the Depth-5 dual weights concentrate $59.7\%$
of their mass on $|D| \ge 2$ states ($[0.4033, 0.4347, 0.1619]$). To free the bulk clique measure without dropping a
single edge of the infinite Cayley graph, we pass to positive Markov product witnesses on the unique left-normal
Cartier--Foata factorization $w = C_1 C_2 \cdots C_k$ \cite{cartier1969}, where each $C_t$ is one of the $216$ non-empty
cliques of $\Gamma_{24}$ ($f$-vector $(1, 24, 96, 96, 0)$) and $C_t \subseteq F(C_{t-1}) = V \setminus (C_{t-1} \cup
\mathrm{CommonAdj}(C_{t-1}))$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact Upward Insertion and $Q_3$ Zero-Cascade Resolvent]
\label{thm:q3-cascade}
For any generator $b \in \{0, \dots, 23\}$ and any upward edge $w \to w s_b$ ($|w s_b| = |w| + 1$) with Cartier--Foata
normal form $w = C_1 \cdots C_k$, let $m \ge 0$ be the unique integer such that $C_{k-m+1}, \dots, C_k \subseteq
\mathrm{Adj}(b)$ and $b \in F(C_{k-m})$. Then $w s_b = C_1 \cdots C_k \{b\}$ for $m = 0$, while for $m \ge 1$,
\begin{equation}
w s_b = C_1 \cdots C_{k-m}\,(C_{k-m+1} \cup \{b\})\, C_{k-m+2} \cdots C_k
\end{equation}
is already in exact Cartier--Foata normal form with zero cascade on $C_{k-m+2}, \dots, C_k$. Moreover, $\mathrm{Adj}(b)$
in $\Gamma_{24}$ is isomorphic to the triangle-free 3-cube $Q_3$ ($8$ vertices, $12$ edges, $20$ non-empty cliques, $212$
valid adjacent clique pairs).
\end{theorem}

For any $W(F_4)$-invariant 2-step Cartier--Foata Markov witness
$\psi(C_1, \dots, C_k) = W_k \bigl(\prod_{t=3}^k \omega_{t-2,t-1,t}\bigr)\allowbreak v_{k-2,k-1,k}$ with
$\omega_{a,b,c} = \omega(C_a, C_b, C_c)$ ($57$ pair-orbit states, $4{,}440$ triple-orbit weights),
Theorem~\ref{thm:q3-cascade} evaluates the exact all-slide-depth Rayleigh quotient ($m = 0, 1, \dots, \infty$) via a
$57 \times 57$ Perron eigenvector and a $212$-state $Q_3$ Neumann resolvent $(I - T_{Q_3}^T)^{-1}$ ($\rho(T_{Q_3}) =
0.210548 < 1$, analytical reverse-mode gradient verified to $5.11 \times 10^{-10}$). Optimizing all $4{,}440$ bulk and
boundary weights reaches the asymptotic lower bound $\rho \ge 11.439240$, while quantizing the weights to positive
integers on finite Cartier--Foata heights $k \in [10, 420]$ and summing slide depths $m \le \min(24, k-2)$ in exact
\code{BigInt} arithmetic (dropping only nonnegative terms) certifies the concrete finite-support bound
$\rho \ge 11.439047$ with zero square roots and zero floating point.

Alongside $\rho$, \code{Vibe/CuspGrowth.lean} (17 theorems) unconditionally proves the exact Steinberg cubic recurrence
\eqref{eq:steinberg-cubic} for both spherical shells $|\partial B_r|$ and cusp shells $N_D$, certifies
$18.2787 < \lambda < 18.2788$ and $l_D = 4 \cdot 5^{D-1}$ ($\forall D \ge 1$), and discharges all conditional cusp
resistance and subluminality theorems of \code{Vibe/Geometry.lean}. Pairing the certified Cheeger constant
$12.33 \le h(\{3,4,3,4\}) \le 21.52$ (\code{note/export-bound.md}, proved via the 4-design Busemann minorant
$L(w) \ge 21.737$ with hyperbolic step $s = \ln(3 + 2\sqrt{2}) = 1.7627$) with Araki--Lieb subadditivity
\cite{araki1970} yields the Landauer-exact fixed-mesh entropy export bound \cite{landauer1961}: for any finite region
$B$ with $n_c(B)$ crossing links and per-slot alphabet size $d$ ($d = 3$ tone-only, $d = 37$ full slot),
\begin{equation}
S(B) - S(B_{\mathrm{after}}) = S(O \mid B_r) - S(I \mid B_r) \le n_c(B)\,(\ln d - s_{\mathrm{in}}),
\label{eq:landauer-export}
\end{equation}
where $O$ and $I$ are the outgoing and incoming crossing slots. On the calm $|V_1\rangle$ background, every incoming
slot outside a finite region's light cone has zero entropy ($s_{\mathrm{in}} = 0$, certified in \code{E-RLT-0122}),
fixing $\Delta = \ln d$. Under line-only dynamics without cross-line mixing, export is capped by $2\Delta/\lambda_{\max}$
(the same $1/\lambda$ scaling as a flat grid). With cross-line mixing on $\{3,4,3,4\}$, however, the Cheeger floor gives
an area-to-volume export capacity of at least $h\Delta \ge 12.33 \ln 37 = 44.5226\text{ nats/dock/beat}$ independent of
region size, establishing why the fixed hyperbolic bulk functions as an irreversible cold entropy sink without creating
or destroying docks.
