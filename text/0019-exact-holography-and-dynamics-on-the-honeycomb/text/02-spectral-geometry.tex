\section{Spectral Geometry and Fixed-Mesh Export}
\label{sec:spectral}

How strongly does negative curvature suppress diffusive return on the infinite 24-regular $\{3,4,3,4\}$ dock graph, and
how tightly can we pin down its exact $l^2$ spectral radius $\rho = \|A\|_{l^2(V) \to l^2(V)}$? Answering this question
determines both the decay rate of random walks and the hyperbolic gap excision mechanism that governs two-body bound
states in Section~\ref{sec:composites}. A simple global Busemann Schur weight bounds $\rho$ from above only at
$104/7 \approx 14.857143$ against a spherical-shell lower bound of $11.013439$. Here we collapse that uncertainty by
nearly three orders of magnitude (Tier~A), pinning $\rho = 11.4414 \pm 0.0024$ and certifying a normalized spectral gap
$\lambda_{\mathrm{gap}} = 1 - \rho/24 > 1/2$.

\subsection{Upward-end flag optimization and upper bounds}

Because the dock graph $W(\Gamma_{24})$ is bipartite by word length $|u|$, every undirected edge $\{x, y\}$ has a unique
upward orientation $x \to y = x s_b$ with $|y| = |x| + 1$, where the generator $b$ lies outside the right-descent clique
$D(x)$ and inside $D(y)$. By Nagnibeda's reciprocal Schur criterion \cite{nagnibeda1997}, any positive edge-weight
assignment obeying $w(x \to y) w(y \to x) = 1$ bounds the spectral radius from above:
\begin{equation}
\rho(\{3,4,3,4\}) \le \sup_{u \in V} \sum_{v \sim u} w(u \to v).
\label{eq:schur-criterion}
\end{equation}
On an infinite graph, exact edge reciprocity holds automatically whenever the edge label $e$ is deterministically
computable from both endpoints' Depth-$L$ suffix trees $\mathrm{St}_L$. The maximal shared label at Depth~$L$ is the
\emph{upward-end flag orbit} $e = [\mathrm{St}_L(y), b]_{W(F_4)}$. The lower vertex $x$ constructs $\mathrm{St}_L(y)$
from its own suffix tree and the step label $b$, while the upper vertex $y$ already carries $\mathrm{St}_L(y)$ and its
descent $b$.

This upward-end flag structure carries an immediate algorithmic simplification. Each flag $e$ belongs to a unique owner
state $v = [\mathrm{St}_L(y)]$, so its reciprocal weight appears in only one downward sum $b_v = \sum_{e \in B_v} d_e / f_e$.
Introducing $y_v = 1/b_v$ and positive simplex weights $\theta_e$ ($\sum_{e \in B_v} \theta_e = 1$) converts the row
sums into a clean fixed-point system:
\begin{equation}
f_e = \frac{d_e}{\theta_e}\, y_{\mathrm{owner}(e)},
\qquad
S_v(b, \theta) = \bigl(P(\theta)\, y\bigr)_v + \frac{1}{y_v}.
\label{eq:howard-row}
\end{equation}
Alternating monotone row equalization, exact AM--GM scalar rescaling, left-Perron dual updates, and Howard policy
updates on $\theta$ solves the Depth-5 automaton to a primal-dual gap below $10^{-8}$. Across Depths~$2$ through~$5$,
the upper optima shrink geometrically while the dual-weighted 4-cycle curl
$|\ln((f_a f_{ab})/(f_b f_{ba}))|$ decays steadily from $0.1370$ to $0.0181$, confirming convergence toward the
curl-free $\rho$-harmonic potential ratio. Quantizing the Depth-5 weights to exact rationals at denominator $Q = 10^6$
and evaluating every row ceiling in arbitrary-precision integer arithmetic proves
\begin{equation}
\rho(\{3,4,3,4\}) \le 11.443713,
\qquad
\frac{\rho}{24} \le 0.476822 < \frac{1}{2},
\qquad
\lambda_{\mathrm{gap}} \ge 0.523178.
\label{eq:rho-upper}
\end{equation}
As an independent structural check, the Depth-5 automaton reproduces exact spherical shell counts through radius $r = 6$
with zero reciprocity defects across the full radius-3 ball.

\subsection{Cartier--Foata Markov witnesses and zero cascade}

Why do standard spherical-shell Rayleigh--Ritz subspaces stall near $11.33$? The reason is structural: word-length
spheres freeze the stationary clique-size distribution near $(0.77, 0.22, 0.01)$, heavily overweighting single-descent
vertices, whereas the optimal Depth-5 dual measure places $59.7\%$ of its weight on multi-descent states
$(|D| \ge 2)$. To decouple the bulk path measure from word length while keeping every edge of the infinite graph exact,
we construct positive Markov product witnesses on the unique left-normal Cartier--Foata factorization
$w = C_1 C_2 \cdots C_k$ \cite{cartier1969}, where each block $C_t$ is one of the $216$ non-empty cliques of the
commuting-facet graph $\Gamma_{24}$ ($24$ vertices, $96$ edges, $96$ triangles, zero 4-cliques) and each successive
block lies in the forbidden-commutation set $F(C_{t-1})$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact Upward Insertion and $Q_3$ Zero-Cascade Resolvent]
\label{thm:q3-cascade}
Let $w = C_1 \cdots C_k$ be a reduced Cartier--Foata normal form in $W(\Gamma_{24})$, and let $w \to w s_b$ be any
upward edge ($|w s_b| = |w| + 1$). Let $m \ge 0$ be the unique integer such that $C_{k-m+1}, \dots, C_k \subseteq
\mathrm{Adj}(b)$ and $b \in F(C_{k-m})$. Then $w s_b = C_1 \cdots C_k \{b\}$ when $m = 0$, and for every $m \ge 1$,
\begin{equation}
w s_b = C_1 \cdots C_{k-m}\,(C_{k-m+1} \cup \{b\})\, C_{k-m+2} \cdots C_k
\label{eq:cf-insertion}
\end{equation}
is already in exact Cartier--Foata normal form with zero cascade on the trailing blocks $C_{k-m+2}, \dots, C_k$.
Furthermore, the commuting link $\mathrm{Adj}(b)$ in $\Gamma_{24}$ is isomorphic to the triangle-free 3-cube $Q_3$
($8$ vertices, $12$ edges, $20$ non-empty cliques, and $212$ valid adjacent clique pairs).
\end{theorem}

Theorem~\ref{thm:q3-cascade} allows exact evaluation of the infinite-graph Rayleigh quotient for any
$W(F_4)$-invariant 2-step Cartier--Foata Markov witness of the form
\begin{equation}
\psi(C_1, \dots, C_k)
= W_k \left(\prod_{t=3}^{k} \omega(C_{t-2}, C_{t-1}, C_t)\right) v(C_{k-2}, C_{k-1}, C_k).
\label{eq:cf-markov-witness}
\end{equation}
Because the $Q_3$ slide-transfer matrix has spectral radius $\rho(T_{Q_3}) = 0.210548 < 1$, summing over all slide
depths $m = 0, 1, \dots, \infty$ closes analytically via a $57 \times 57$ pair-orbit Perron eigenvector and a
$212$-state $Q_3$ Neumann resolvent $(I - T_{Q_3}^T)^{-1}$. Optimizing the $4{,}440$ triple-orbit bulk and boundary
weights reaches the asymptotic lower bound $\rho \ge 11.439240$. Quantizing those weights to positive integers on
finite Cartier--Foata heights $k \in [10, 420]$ and summing slide depths in exact integer arithmetic (omitting only
strictly nonnegative tail terms) certifies the concrete finite-support bound $\rho \ge 11.439047$ with zero square roots
and zero asymptotic limits.

\subsection{Formal spectral bracket and Landauer export}

The same reciprocal Schur and Cartier--Foata machinery translates directly into machine-checked Lean~4 proofs. A
Depth-1 rational witness first establishes $\rho \le 335/28 < 12$ and $\lambda_{\mathrm{gap}} \ge 337/672 > 1/2$.
Formalizing the 19-state Depth-2 automaton, 23-flag reciprocity at $Q = 10{,}000$, Theorem~\ref{thm:q3-cascade}'s
$Q_3$ zero-cascade algebra, and a 57-orbit 1-step Cartier--Foata witness collapses the kernel-verified bracket by
$10.2\times$:
\begin{equation}
\frac{913}{80} = 11.4125 < \rho(\{3,4,3,4\}) \le \frac{387{,}141{,}227}{33{,}670{,}000} \le 11.498106 < 11.5,
\qquad
\lambda_{\mathrm{gap}} > \frac{13}{25} > \frac{1}{2}.
\label{eq:lean-spectral-bracket}
\end{equation}

Alongside $\rho$, the Lean~4 formalization proves the exact Steinberg cubic \eqref{eq:steinberg-cubic} unconditionally
for both spherical shells and cusp beams, certifying $18.2787 < \lambda < 18.2788$ and $l_D = 4 \cdot 5^{D-1}$ for all
$D \ge 1$. This exponential bulk geometry also controls local thermodynamics on the fixed mesh. Consider any finite
dock region $B$ with $n_c(B)$ crossing links to its exterior and per-slot alphabet size $d$ ($d = 3$ for tone alone,
$d = 37$ for the full slot register). Because on-site layers leave the entropy of $B$ unchanged while the stream $S$
swaps outgoing slots $O$ for incoming slots $I$, Araki--Lieb subadditivity \cite{araki1970} yields the Landauer-exact
entropy export bound \cite{landauer1961}:
\begin{equation}
S(B) - S(B_{\mathrm{after}}) = S(O \mid B_r) - S(I \mid B_r) \le n_c(B)\bigl(\ln d - s_{\mathrm{in}}\bigr),
\label{eq:landauer-export}
\end{equation}
where $B_r$ is the interior remainder and $s_{\mathrm{in}}$ is the entropy per incoming slot. On the neutral quiescent
mesh state $|V_1\rangle$, every incoming slot outside a finite excitation's light cone is strictly calm ($s_{\mathrm{in}} = 0$).
Under purely line-confined dynamics without cross-line mixing, errors escape only through segment endpoints, capping
export per dock at $2\Delta / \lambda_{\max}$ (the same $1/\lambda$ scaling as a flat lattice). With cross-line mixing
on $\{3,4,3,4\}$, however, the certified Cheeger constant $12.33 \le h(\{3,4,3,4\}) \le 21.52$ guarantees a
size-independent area-to-volume export floor $h\Delta \ge 12.33 \ln 37 = 44.52\text{ nats/dock/beat}$. Thus the fixed
hyperbolic bulk acts as an unfillable cold entropy sink without creating or destroying space.

Because every finite region sits inside an exponentially wider hyperbolic boundary, the natural next question is whether
bulk quantum information can be encoded holographically onto that boundary, how the microscopic beat interacts with such
a code, and how a discrete mesh resolves continuum subdivision without a supertask.
