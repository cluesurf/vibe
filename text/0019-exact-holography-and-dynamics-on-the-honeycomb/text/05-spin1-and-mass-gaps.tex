\section{Spin-1 Obstruction, Gauge Dynamics, and Mass Gaps}
\label{sec:gauge}

Can massless transverse spin-1 gauge bosons emerge purely as multi-body bound states of the bare automaton $U$, with no
link variables added? Answering this question sharply separates what the bare rule generates on its own from where
dynamical gauge registers become structurally necessary. Here we prove an all-body parity superselection obstruction in
bare $U$ (Tier~A), construct a unified single-speed discrete gauge--matter automaton built purely from native 24-cell
and qutrit subgroups (Tier~B), and push unconditional zero-truncation 4D $D_4$ lattice Yang--Mills mass gaps past
$3\times$ Bakry--\'Emery (Tier~C).

\subsection{All-body spin-1 obstruction in the bare rule}

In the bare automaton, perturbative contact-RPA analysis requires an unphysical coupling $g_c = 8.7353$ to close the
vector gap, and exact two-body $1^{--}$ dipoles suffer complete $K$-layer cancellation that leaves a fixed $2\pi/3$ gap
and zero transverse velocity. A natural question remains: could higher-body multi-axis scattering ($k \ge 3$) or a
finite-density neutral pair condensate unlock a massless transverse photon? Exact symmetry analysis rules out that
possibility across all excitation numbers $k \ge 1$ and all densities $\rho \ge 0$ (Tier~A):

\begin{theorem}[All-Body Bare-$U$ Spin-1 Algebraic Obstruction]
\label{thm:spin1-obstruction}
Let $N_l^{(\tau)}$ be the total count of tone $\tau \in \{-1, +1\}$ on antipodal $D_4$ line $l \in \{0, \dots, 11\}$,
and let $P_l^{(\tau)} = (-1)^{N_l^{(\tau)}}$. Under the bare automaton $U$ over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ with no
external link registers:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Exact $\mathbb{Z}_2^{24}$ per-line tone-parity superselection:} Because $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$
transfers identical pairs in steps $\Delta N_l^{(\tau)} = \pm 2$ while all other layers preserve every $N_l^{(\tau)}$
individually, $[U, P_l^{(\tau)}] = 0$ identically. Against any parity-definite background $|\Omega\rangle$ at any
density $\rho \ge 0$ (including $|V_1\rangle$ and every pair-coherent condensate), a single-dipole creation operator
$O_l^\dagger(x)$ on line $l$ decouples strictly from every distinct line $m \neq l$ for all time:
\begin{equation}
\langle\Omega | O_m(y)\, U^t\, O_l^\dagger(x) | \Omega\rangle = 0
\qquad (\forall\, m \neq l,\; \forall\, t \in \mathbb{Z}),
\label{eq:z24-superselection}
\end{equation}
forbidding single-dipole cross-line transfer and elementary $(+,-)$ four-cycle plaquette ring-exchange.
\item \textbf{Exact flat-transverse-band theorem for $\min(N_+, N_-) = 1$:} Whenever a sector holds a single negative
(or positive) excitation, $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ never moves that lone tone, so its transverse coordinates commute strictly
with bare $U$ ($[U, X_{-,\perp}] = 0$, verified across all $10{,}710$ real-space states on $\mathbb{Z}^2 / 3\mathbb{Z}^2$
with $288$ active $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ firings). Conjugating by $e^{i q_\perp \cdot X_{-,\perp}}$ enforces $v_T = 0$
identically for all $k \le 3$, making $k = 4$ ($Q = 0$) the algebraic minimum for nonzero transverse dispersion.
\item \textbf{Exhaustive $k = 4$ mutual exclusion and absence of local $U(1)$:} Every one of the $220$ triples of $D_4$
lines is fixed pointwise by an improper reflection $g \in W(F_4)^-$, forcing $\chi_{\mathrm{odd}} = 0$ on all
$43{,}296$ quads of at most three lines so $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ and $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ never coexist below $k = 5$.
Every local layer preserves charge $Q(g)$, whereas any spatially varying $U(1)$ gauge rotation fails to commute with the
stream $S$ ($\|[G_2(\pi/3), S_2]\|_2 = 1$).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

Exact sparse multi-axis diagonalizations ($\text{unitarity} \le 3.3 \times 10^{-16}$) confirm that the obstruction
persists at $k = 4$ and finite density $\rho \in [1/6, 1]$. On $\mathbb{Z}^2 / 3\mathbb{Z}^2$ at $k = 4$
($\dim = 9{,}282$), $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ opens tiny cross-axis leakage ($1.345 \times 10^{-3}$) yet leaves the dipole
peak gapped at $-2\pi/3$ ($\min|\omega| = 0.3665$, $v_T = 0.0083$). Across finite-density neutral pair condensates,
both exact inter-sector $P_{\mathrm{bg}} = 0 \to P = \pm q$ transverse Kubo response (cross-checked against full
$4{,}096$-dimensional real-space evolution to $2.78 \times 10^{-15}$) and stationary multi-axis Floquet Bogoliubov
diagonalization on three-dock two-line $D_4$ ($\dim = 210, \rho = 2/3$) yield zero gapless weight ($W(\Delta E = 0) = 0$)
with dominant transitions at $-2.47$ and $+2.96\text{ rad}$. Excluding an unmotivated thermodynamic spontaneous breaking
of discrete $\mathbb{Z}_2^{24}$ into a pair-only spin liquid, \textbf{dynamical gauge link registers are structurally
required}.

\subsection{Unified single-speed gauge--matter automaton}

Once Theorem~\ref{thm:spin1-obstruction} forces dynamical gauge links, a clean geometric question arises: can those
links be built entirely from finite groups native to the 24-cell and ternary qutrit, while unifying photon and fermion
speeds? Uncoupled bare pair transport ($c_{\mathrm{eff}} = 0.0213$) and warped cusp-beam propagation ($0.9869$) differ
in speed by a factor of $46.4\times$.

Matching a palindromic split-step three-dimensional $\{4,3,4\}$ cusp Weyl/Dirac automaton at the bare coin angle
$\theta = \pi/3$ ($\cos(\pi/3) = 1/2$) directly to a discrete-time Maxwell link beat ($\tau_E = \tau_B = 1/2$, sub-beat
speed $c_0 = 1/2 < 1/\sqrt{3}$, $\max\mathrm{CFL} = \sqrt{3}/2 = 0.8660 < 1$) resolves this mismatch completely
(Tier~B). Photons and fermions lock to one shared light speed to $9.40 \times 10^{-8}$ at $|q| = 10^{-3}$, with exactly
two degenerate transverse massless polarizations and zero propagating longitudinal mode.

The same 24-cell geometry supplies exact non-Abelian gauge building blocks without external imports. The 24 unit Hurwitz
vertices of the 24-cell form the binary tetrahedral group $2T = \mathrm{SL}(2,3) < SU(2)_L$, which integrates all $126$
monomials of degree $\le 5$ identically to Haar measure on $SU(2)$ (an exact spherical 5-design and unitary 2-design,
with first deviation $1/64$ at degree~6). Its 24-cell electric Laplacian has exact spectrum
$\{0\,(\times 1), 1/2\,(\times 4), 1\,(\times 9), 5/4\,(\times 8), 3/2\,(\times 2)\}$, reproducing $j = 0 < 1/2 < 1$ and
the $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ commutator algebra. Together with the qutrit Clifford subgroup
$\Delta(27) \trianglelefteq \Sigma(648) < SU(3)_C$ (an exact unitary 2-design in $SU(3)$ with frame potentials $(1, 2, 7)$)
and the ring's native $\mathbb{Z}_{18}$ phase group (which, unlike $\mathbb{Z}_6$, preserves all six Standard Model
hypercharges and all four $B - L$ charges faithfully), the unified discrete gauge architecture passes every structural
and dynamical audit summarized in Table~\ref{tab:unified-gauge}.

\subsection{Unconditional 4D \texorpdfstring{$D_4$}{D4} lattice Yang--Mills mass gaps}

Alongside discrete gauge automata, the 24-cell's flat four-dimensional partner lattice $D_4$ provides an ideal arena for
constructive lattice Yang--Mills theory (Tier~C) \cite{wilson1974, celmaster1982}. Every site has 24 nearest neighbors,
every link borders eight equilateral triangles, $O(a^2)$ rotational artifacts vanish identically via
Equation~\eqref{eq:d4-isotropy}, and reflection positivity across the $x_0 = 0$ site hyperplane holds exactly
\cite{osterwalder1978, seiler1982}. Standard 24-cell Bakry--\'Emery and Combes--Thomas curvature bounds
\cite{bakry1985, combes1973} certify an unconditional infinite-volume Osterwalder--Schrader mass gap up to
$\beta_{\mathrm{BE}}^{24c} = N^2 / (16\sqrt{7})$ ($0.094491$ for $SU(2)$, $0.212605$ for $SU(3)$).

How far can zero-truncation constructive methods push this unconditional window without any Migdal--Kadanoff
approximation? Four exact analytical theorems break clean through the single-link Dobrushin ceiling \cite{dobrushin1970,
dobrushin1985, holley1987, chen1997, kotecky1986}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{No-free-lunch ceiling for single-link sweeps:} Any block cover updated solely by iterated single-link
conditionals yields influence matrix $C_{\mathrm{pure}} = (I - A_{\mathrm{in}})^{-1} A_{\mathrm{out}}$, whose critical
point $\rho(C_{\mathrm{pure}}) = 1$ coincides identically with the single-link ceiling $\rho(A) = 1$. Breaking that
ceiling structurally requires genuine multi-link marginalization $\alpha_{13} < c$.
\item \textbf{Exact 1+2 block-alternating complete triangle cover:} Freezing one spatial axial direction preserves
$x_0 = 0$ reflection positivity and drops the link Perron root from $16$ to $4 + 4\sqrt{7} = 14.5830$ (proved in Lean~4),
while a four-direction alternating-plane forest lowers it further to $\Lambda_{88} = 14.5684$. Because every active
triangle has three unfrozen links, updating one link conditionally and the remaining two-link wedge by exact
marginalization produces the symmetrized internal coupling and $3 \times 3$ block factors
\begin{equation}
\gamma_{\mathrm{shared}} = \frac{1}{2}\bigl(\alpha_{13} + c(1 + \alpha_{13})\bigr) < c,
\qquad
g_{\mathrm{self}} = \frac{c}{1 - 2c\gamma_{\mathrm{shared}}},
\qquad
g_{\mathrm{other}} = \frac{c\gamma_{\mathrm{shared}}}{1 - 2c\gamma_{\mathrm{shared}}}.
\label{eq:block-factors}
\end{equation}
\item \textbf{Sharp linear-split potential for $SU(2)$:} Subtracting the midpoint linear staple term
$\beta c_1 (U_1^\dagger U_3^\dagger) \cdot W_2$ from the integrated Bessel potential $V_2$ collapses the
Holley--Stroock residual oscillation from $0.56$ to $\mathrm{osc}(\widetilde{V}_2) \le 0.007953$ (at $\beta = 0.2773$),
cutting $\alpha_{13}$ from $0.04578$ to $0.03224$.
\item \textbf{Exact $SU(3)$ $\ln Z$ convexity and $K = 2$ Peter--Weyl Schur complement:} Expanding $V_2(U_1, U_3)$ around
the background staple $M_2$ keeps the linear staple inside $\operatorname{conv}(SU(3))$ and bounds the convex remainder
uniformly by $0 \le \widetilde{V}_2 \le \frac{\beta^2}{6} v_{\max}^*(\beta)$. A deterministic branch-and-bound sweep
over $20{,}384$ boxes at $\beta_0 = 0.5635$ (analytic Rouch\'e tail $B = 9.22 \times 10^{-20}$, minimum Cholesky pivot
$1.11 \times 10^{-3}$, $48 \times 48$ Weyl quadrature check $1.36 \times 10^{-15}$) certifies
$\alpha_{13}^{SU(3)}(\beta_0) \le 0.04055$.
\end{enumerate}

Evaluating time-tilted block Dobrushin matrices $G(m)$ ($\rho(G(m)) < 1$) yields unconditional infinite-volume mass gaps
strictly past $3\times$ Bakry--\'Emery for $SU(2)$ ($\beta_* = 0.285141$, $30.55\times$ Koteck\'y--Preiss) and past
$60\times$ Koteck\'y--Preiss for $SU(3)$ ($\beta_* = 0.563019$, $2.65\times$ Bakry--\'Emery), summarized in
Figure~\ref{fig:mass-gap-bars} and Table~\ref{tab:d4-mass-gaps}. These remain strong-coupling lattice theorems and do
not address the continuum Clay Yang--Mills limit $\beta \to \infty$ \cite{jaffe2000ym, balaban1989}.

zero external noise, how do irreversible local decoherence, classical records, Born probabilities, and Markovian
macro-histories emerge?
