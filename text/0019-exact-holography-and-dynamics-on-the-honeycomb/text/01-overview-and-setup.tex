\section{Overview}
\label{sec:overview}

This paper tests how much continuum-like mathematical physics arises from a single fixed, reversible discrete automaton
in four hyperbolic dimensions and where additional gauge, metric, or continuum structure becomes structurally mandatory.
Discrete microscopic models routinely fail in one of two opposite ways: either they remain too simple to support bound
matter, field propagation, and classical records, or they smuggle in continuum behavior through unstated approximations.
We evaluate one explicit automaton across spectral geometry, discrete holography, many-body scattering, lattice gauge
theory, causal weak-field gravitational extensions, linearized spin-2 waves, husk--bulk charge asymmetry, quantum
foundations, and multiscale organization \cite{schumacher2004, arrighi2019, farrelly2020}, pairing every positive
theorem with its exact no-go boundary and backing core proofs with deterministic verification and a zero-dependency
Lean~4 formalization \cite{demoura2021lean4, cluesurf2026repo}.

We define each geometric object, operator, and convention at first use, and separate four mathematical scopes explicitly
throughout: exact theorems of the bare automaton $U$ and its native $\{3,4,3,4\}$ geometry with nothing added,
parameter-deformed kinematics and reduced open-bath collision models, dynamical extensions adding discrete subgroup
gauge links, Clifford circuits, or a local metric field on the same geometry, and external constructive strong-coupling
lattice Yang--Mills bounds, static $\mathbb{H}^4$ continuum kernels, and abstract evolutionary controls.

\subsection{Related frameworks}

Existing discrete programs in mathematical physics typically solve one structural constraint at the price of another.
Flat Euclidean quantum cellular automata (QCAs) preserve exact unitarity, finite propagation speed, and translation
symmetry \cite{schumacher2004, arrighi2019, farrelly2020}, yet every amenable flat grid has zero normalized spectral gap
and vanishing Cheeger ratio, so emitted entropy and local excitations recycle indefinitely unless an external bath is
appended. Causal-set approaches achieve statistical Lorentz invariance through random Poisson sprinklings of partial
orders, but sacrifice regular local symmetry, deterministic algebraic amplitudes, and a local unitary evolution
operator. Static two-dimensional hyperbolic tensor networks \cite{pastawski2015happy} realize exact holographic
error-correcting codes and discrete Ryu--Takayanagi geometry \cite{ryu2006rt}, yet they carry no intrinsic unitary
dynamics and terminate on a one-dimensional boundary rather than a flat three-dimensional space.

Lattice gauge and discrete gravity models on four-dimensional hypercubic grids $\mathbb{Z}^4$ provide mature
strong-coupling and Monte Carlo toolkits \cite{wilson1974, osterwalder1978, kotecky1986}, yet $\mathbb{Z}^4$ breaks
fourth-order rotational isotropy at leading $O(a^2)$ order, introduces cubic anisotropy in linearized spin-2 waves, and
relies on four-link plaquettes whose strong-coupling cluster bounds tighten earlier than three-link triangles on the
body-centered hypercubic lattice $D_4$ \cite{celmaster1982}. Meanwhile, finite-bath unitary collision models preserve
microscopic reversibility without approximation, yet finite Hilbert-space dimension forces Page entropy turnover and
Poincar\'e revivals \cite{page1993} instead of genuine one-way radiative dilution.

\subsection{The honeycomb mesh}

The spatial substrate is the fixed paracompact hyperbolic honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$ in $\mathbb{H}^4$, tiled by self-dual
24-cells meeting four per triangular ridge with flat cubic cusps $\{4,3,4\}$ at infinity \cite{coxeter1956,
coxeter1973}. We refer to this fixed tessellation as the \emph{mesh} and to each individual 24-cell as a \emph{dock}
(reserving \emph{cell} for geometric polytopes and cellular automata, and avoiding \emph{tile}). Each dock is bounded by
24 octahedral facets called \emph{slots}, indexed by the 24 roots of the four-dimensional root system $D_4$
\cite{conway1999}:
\begin{equation}
\Phi(D_4) = \bigl\{\,\text{all permutations of } (\pm 1, \pm 1, 0, 0) \in \mathbb{R}^4\,\bigr\},
\qquad
|\Phi(D_4)| = 24.
\label{eq:d4-roots}
\end{equation}
Among all regular convex polytopes in dimension four or higher, the 24-cell is the unique self-dual figure without a
three-dimensional or higher-dimensional analogue. Unlike a four-dimensional hypercubic grid, its $D_4$ root vectors
cancel all leading $O(a^2)$ rotational artifacts identically through exact fourth-order $SO(4)$ isotropy:
\begin{equation}
\sum_{\mathbf{r} \in \Phi(D_4)} r_a r_b r_c r_d
= 4\bigl(\delta_{ab}\delta_{cd} + \delta_{ac}\delta_{bd} + \delta_{ad}\delta_{bc}\bigr).
\label{eq:d4-isotropy}
\end{equation}
This single tensor identity governs two later physical results directly: on the flat $D_4$ lattice it restores isotropic
linearized spin-2 gravitational wave speeds across coordinate and diagonal axes (Section~\ref{sec:gravity}), and its
minimal 3-link triangular plaquettes widen constructive Yang--Mills mass-gap windows compared with 4-link hypercubic
plaquettes (Section~\ref{sec:gauge}) \cite{celmaster1982}. Pairing each slot $\mathbf{r}$ with its antipodal opposite
$-\mathbf{r}$ forms 12 straight \emph{lines} passing through the dock. Grouping these lines into three complementary
orthogonal coordinate-plane pairs yields three disjoint 8-slot \emph{banks} (inscribed 16-cells). The full symmetry
group of one dock is the Weyl group $W(F_4)$ of order $1{,}152$, with proper rotation subgroup $W(F_4)^+$ of order $576$
and outer triality quotient $W(F_4) / W(D_4) \cong S_3$. That outer $S_3$ triality permutes the three 8-slot banks and
proves decisive in Section~\ref{sec:holography}, where untwisted $W(F_4)$ symmetry alone hits an algebraic distance
ceiling that only triality breaks.

$\{4,3,4\}$ around each ideal point at infinity (a \emph{cusp}). Compact regular honeycombs lack flat Euclidean

its three-bank triality. Fixing one ideal cusp foliates $\mathbb{H}^4$ into concentric \emph{horospheres} (level
surfaces orthogonal to geodesics approaching the cusp, tiled by flat unit cubes) indexed by integer depth

This four-dimensional bulk to three-dimensional boundary geometry organizes every phenomenon examined in
Sections~\ref{sec:spectral} through~\ref{sec:lean-and-open}. The outermost slice $D = 0$ is the \emph{husk}, where
three-dimensional observables are read. An observer restricted to $D = 0$ sees a locally flat cubic space with 6
lateral neighbors per dock. Behind every husk dock, however, lies an exponentially widening four-dimensional \emph{beam}
of bulk docks whose floor counts grow roughly eighteenfold per depth step. Each husk dock has zero upward neighbors, 6
lateral neighbors inside $D = 0$, and 18 downward neighbors in $D = 1$, and all 6 lateral slots pair antipodally across
the dock center directly with downward bulk slots in $D = 1$. That one-sided antipodal tilt means straight motion along
husk axes naturally couples the flat 3D slice to the deep 4D interior: it supplies an unbounded bulk entropy reservoir
(Section~\ref{sec:spectral}), anchors radial holographic encoding and transverse continuum subdivision
(Section~\ref{sec:holography}), governs gravitational localization versus bulk leakage (Section~\ref{sec:gravity}), and
channels parity-asymmetric chiral phases into transient husk--bulk charge separation (Section~\ref{sec:gravity}).

\subsection{Unitary dynamics}

Each local ingredient of the microscopic rule is constrained by minimality, exact discrete symmetry, and algebraic
closure with zero continuous knobs. Every slot holds a signed ternary \emph{tone} $\tau \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$. A binary
alphabet $\{0, 1\}$ admits no nontrivial charge-conjugation involution leaving a neutral state fixed, so signed ternary
tones form the smallest alphabet admitting both a neutral center $0$ and an exact involution $\mathcal{C} : \tau
\mapsto -\tau$. Every occupied slot ($\tau = \pm 1$) also carries a discrete internal \emph{role} label
$j \in \{0, \dots, 8\} \cong \mathbb{Z}_3^2$, the minimal ternary double-index space distinguishing identical-role from
distinct-role collisions (needed for mixed-tone level repulsion and role-labeled bound states in
Section~\ref{sec:composites}) while keeping every local dimension a power of $3$. The all-zero configuration is the
\emph{neutral quiescent mesh state} $|V_1\rangle$, an exact stationary fixed point with zero spontaneous activity. In
the bare automaton, a \emph{link} denotes purely conceptual adjacency across a shared facet, with no independent gauge
register attached.

Quantum amplitudes live strictly inside the exact cyclotomic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$, where $\zeta_9$ satisfies
the sixth-degree cyclotomic polynomial $\zeta_9^6 + \zeta_9^3 + 1 = 0$ (geometrically $\zeta_9 = e^{2\pi i/9}$). Rather
than introducing tunable real angles or coupling constants, each element of this ring is forced by exact unitarity and
symmetry on the 24-cell. Three-valued ternary phase turns require the primitive cube root $\omega = \zeta_9^3$
($\omega^2 + \omega + 1 = 0$), equal-weight deterministic splitting across antipodal slots requires denominator $2$,
exact determinant matching between singly and doubly occupied lines requires the sixth root $\zeta_6 = 1 + \omega$, and
$W(F_4)$-isotropic pair scattering across all 12 lines requires denominator $12 = 4 \cdot 3$ together with the eighteenth
root $\zeta_{18} = -\zeta_9^5$. Because every entry of the evolution operator belongs to $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$, no
transcendental constants, free parameters, or floating-point rounding enter the discrete update, and exact operator
identities translate directly into algebraic Lean~4 proofs.

One discrete time step, called a \emph{beat}, applies a single palindromic eight-layer unitary operator:
\begin{equation}
|\psi(t + 1)\rangle = U |\psi(t)\rangle,
\qquad
U = S \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}} \circ D \circ M_{\mathrm{meet}} \circ M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18}) \circ K \circ D \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}.
\label{eq:bare-beat}
\end{equation}
Here every local \emph{coin} is a deterministic unitary splitting amplitude across directions with zero randomness:
\begin{equation}
D = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 2 + \omega & -\omega \\ -\omega & 2 + \omega \end{pmatrix},
\quad
C = D^2 = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 1 + \omega & 1 - \omega \\ 1 - \omega & 1 + \omega \end{pmatrix},
\quad
M_{\mathrm{meet}}^{\mathrm{diff}} = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 1 + \omega & \omega - 1 \\ \omega - 1 & 1 + \omega \end{pmatrix}.
\label{eq:local-coins}
\end{equation}
Each layer in Eq.~\eqref{eq:bare-beat} plays one distinct physical role on a dock. The half-coin $D$ drives free
antipodal dispersion on singly occupied lines and multiplies doubly occupied (full) lines by $\det D = \zeta_6$, keeping
single- and two-body phases algebraically coherent. At two-excitation contact on a line, $K$ swaps opposite-sign full
lines with a fermion-like $-1$ sign, while $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ imparts phase $\omega$ to identical-role same-sign full
lines and mixes distinct-role same-sign pairs via $M_{\mathrm{meet}}^{\mathrm{diff}}$. Without cross-line coupling,
excitations would remain permanently trapped on one-dimensional antipodal tracks. The 12-line pair mixer therefore acts
on any sole identical-tone, identical-role antipodal pair as the unique $W(F_4)$-invariant isotropic rank-1 unitary
perturbation across all 12 lines:
\begin{equation}
M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18}) = I_{12} + \frac{\zeta_{18} - 1}{12} J_{12},
\label{eq:mpair}
\end{equation}
where $J_{12}$ is the $12 \times 12$ all-ones matrix.

The outer phase layer $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ applies the chiral four-body phase $\zeta_6^{\sigma(g)\chi_{\mathrm{odd}}(g)}$
built from the canonical $W(F_4) \times \mathbb{Z}_2^{\mathcal{C}}$ orientation sign $\chi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ (which is
$\mathcal{C}$-odd, flipping sign when all tones negate, $\mathcal{P}$-odd under spatial reflection, and
$\mathcal{CP}$-even). Symmetry forces $\chi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ to vanish identically at occupation $k \le 3$, so chiral
asymmetry switches on strictly at threshold $k^* = 4$ (an intrinsic four-body interaction with no lower-body bias). The
final layer $S$ streams every occupied slot $(g, a)$ across facet $a$ to $(g k_a, a)$ with strict unit light-cone speed
($c = 1$). Because the three central interaction layers $K$, $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$, and $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ commute
pairwise, sandwiching them symmetrically inside $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}} \circ D$ and $D \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ before
streaming enforces exact time-reversal $\mathcal{T}$, combined $\mathcal{CP}$, $\mathcal{CPT}$, and order-576
$W(F_4)^+$ covariance for all $t \in \mathbb{Z}$. Throughout the rest of this paper, this bare rule $U$ is held
completely fixed: whenever bare $U$ encounters an algebraic or geometric obstruction, we prove the obstruction as a
theorem and isolate the minimal added structure required to overcome it.
