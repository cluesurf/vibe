\subsection{Quantum Darwinism and Born--Gleason uniqueness}

Does hyperbolic decoherence merely scramble phase information into global entanglement, or does it broadcast redundant
classical copies into disjoint bulk observers? Decomposing the mutual information between the system $S$ and an
environment fragment $F$ into classical Holevo information and quantum discord \cite{zurek2009, ollivier2001} gives a
sharp quantitative diagnostic:
\begin{equation}
I(S : F) = \chi_Z(S : F) + D_Z(S : F).
\label{eq:darwinism-decomp}
\end{equation}
On the open hyperbolic trajectory, $\chi_Z$ forms a flat classical plateau across fragments $f \in [2, 9]$ (redundancy
$R_{0.05} = 6$, $R_{0.02} = 4$, discord $D_Z \le 0.0113$) before jumping to $2 H(S) = 2\text{ bits}$ at $f = 12$. On the
finite closed honeycomb tree at $t = 24$, the three disjoint $D_4$ frames achieve classical redundancy $R_{0.10} = 3$
($F_0: I = 1.0054 = \chi_Z(0.9951) + D_Z(0.0103)$, cleanly explaining the slight $I > H(S)$ overshoot as residual
discord), and the ternary qutrit reaches a $\log_2 3$ plateau with $R_{0.05} = 3$. By contrast, a deterministic
scrambled control destroys the plateau altogether: at the Page midpoint $f = 6$ \cite{page1993}, $I = 1.0029$ is
dominated by quantum discord ($\chi_Z = 0.3678$, $D_Z = 0.6352$, zero classical redundancy).

To test probability emergence inside the exact cyclotomic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$, observe that while
$1/\sqrt{3} \notin \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_9)$, the phase-rotated factor $-i/\sqrt{3} = (-1 - 2\omega)/3$ lies strictly inside
$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/3]$. Applying exact fine-graining $(-i Z_3 F_3)$ and bipartite copy unitaries certifies Zurek

swap transpositions across rational pairs $(1,1)$ through $(3,5)$ ($\le 4.4 \times 10^{-16}$).

Stress-testing rival power laws $P_p(k) = |c_k|^p / \sum_j |c_j|^p$ across $p \in \{1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4\}$ under
