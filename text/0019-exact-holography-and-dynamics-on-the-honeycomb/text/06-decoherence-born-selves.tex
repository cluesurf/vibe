\section{Decoherence}
\label{sec:decoherence}

Closed unitary dynamics on a finite or flat Euclidean lattice faces two persistent obstructions to classical emergence
and memoryless open evolution. Because finite periodic boxes reflect emitted excitations back onto their source (forcing
Poincar\'e revivals), while infinite flat $\mathbb{Z}^d$ baths have $\lambda_{\mathrm{gap}} = 0$ and slow algebraic
$t^{-d/2}$ return, local coherence revives or decays only algebraically and past environment records flow back into the
subsystem, breaking Markovianity. Standard open-system models bypass these obstructions by tracing out or externally
resetting ancillas, whereas collapse theories add non-unitary axioms that break linearity directly.

On $\{3,4,3,4\}$, exponential branching and the exact cyclotomic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ allow these
mechanisms to be tested inside a globally unitary, reversible automaton with zero randomness, zero collapse axioms, and
zero artificial bath resets across rate-matched multi-body ablations, Quantum Darwinism with Holevo--discord separation,
exact cyclotomic Born--Gleason uniqueness, and a falsifiable macro-level testbed culminating in a 27-dimensional
zero-reset swap test.

\subsection{Born uniqueness}

Local decoherence alone cannot explain why independent observers agree on one objective classical outcome: a generic
scrambling unitary also drives a subsystem to maximal entropy, yet hides all information inside global multi-body
entanglement until an observer controls half the environment (the Page midpoint \cite{page1993}). Under Quantum
Darwinism \cite{zurek2009}, decomposing the quantum mutual information between system $S$ and an environment fragment
$F$ into classical Holevo capacity $\chi_Z$ (accessible locally on $F$) and quantum discord $D_Z$ (the non-classical
remainder) \cite{ollivier2001} separates redundant classical broadcasting from scrambling:
\begin{equation}
I(S : F) = \chi_Z(S : F) + D_Z(S : F).
\label{eq:darwinism-decomp}
\end{equation}

Quantifying classical broadcasting by redundancy $R_\delta$ (counting disjoint environment fragments each capturing at
least $(1 - \delta) H(S)$), the open hyperbolic trajectory forms a flat classical $\chi_Z$ plateau across fragments
$f \in [2, 9]$ with redundancy $R_{0.05} = 6$ ($D_Z \le 0.011$) before jumping to $2 H(S)$ at $f = 12$
(Table~\ref{tab:decoherence-darwinism}). Multiple independent observers intercepting separate outward rays therefore
read the same $Z$ pointer record without disturbing one another or the system. Both the finite closed honeycomb tree at
$t = 24$ (across its three disjoint $D_4$ frames) and the ternary qutrit likewise form classical plateaus, whereas a
deterministic scrambled control hides information until the Page midpoint $f = 6$, leaving mutual information dominated
by quantum discord with zero classical redundancy.

\subsection{Markovian horizons}

Three falsifiable macro-level tests separate dynamically closed effective organizations from ill-posed coarse averages
on $\{3,4,3,4\}$, probing algebraic closure, holographic sensitivity horizons, and zero-reset memorylessness.

microscopic evolution never leaks out of the projected subspace, measured by the closure error

the collinear $k = 2$ doublon ($4.50 \times 10^{-16}$), the $k = 3$ trion ($3.96 \times 10^{-16}$), and the $W(F_4)$

Conversely, unaliased three-dimensional cusp beam sums ($8{,}857$ docks across $63$ beams) diverge in a single beat

closed macro-dynamics because opposite microscopic directions separate immediately onto distinct bulk branches, showing
that only symmetry-adapted or bound-state sectors evolve autonomously.

\textbf{27D zero-reset Markovian swap test:} Standard Markovian master equations achieve memoryless evolution by

channel ordinarily requires coupling $A$ at every beat to a freshly initialized external ancilla and discarding the
previous one. On $\{3,4,3,4\}$, exponential downward shell growth (expanding from $1$ to $18$ to $330$ docks with
shell-1 branching $b_1 = 330/18$) supplies those fresh modes intrinsically: excitations scattered from the central dock
into shell $D + 1$ disperse across exponentially many outgoing docks whose amplitude overlap with trajectories returning
to $A$ decays exponentially with the radial spectral gap $\lambda_{\mathrm{gap,tree}} = 0.5565$. By contrast, a finite
trapped bath saturates its ancilla space immediately ($\|\Delta\rho_A\|_1 = 1.1056$) and an infinite 1D geodesic bath
with unit cross-section retains a non-vanishing reflecting-coin return channel ($\|\Delta\rho_A\|_1 = 0.4922$). Only
exponential branching decouples previously recorded bath states from $A$'s incoming channels fast enough to turn global
linear unitarity into one-way pointer records without resets or collapse axioms, while embedding multi-body Quantum
Darwinism beyond collision reductions remains open.
