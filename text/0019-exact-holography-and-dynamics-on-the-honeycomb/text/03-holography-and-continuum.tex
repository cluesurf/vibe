\section{Holographic Coding and Continuum Subdivision}
\label{sec:holography}

Does the 24-cell's own Weyl symmetry group $W(F_4)$ support a high-distance ternary holographic tensor that tiles the
entire $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb into an exact bulk-to-boundary isometry \cite{pastawski2015happy}? Understanding how
hyperbolic geometry protects bulk quantum information on its boundary matters both for discrete holography and for
explaining how continuous fields can arise from a rigid lattice. Here we classify covariant ternary dock tensors
exhaustively, construct the all-radius holographic network and its dynamical bridge to the bare automaton, and show how
transverse bulk-depth subdivision resolves Zeno's supertask dilemma.

\subsection{Unique covariant ternary holographic tensor}

A ternary quantum stabilizer code $[[n, k, d]]_3$ encodes $k$ logical qutrits ($\mathbb{C}^3$) into $n$ physical qutrits
as the joint $+1$ eigenspace of commuting Weyl--Heisenberg operators built from shift $X|a\rangle = |a+1 \bmod 3\rangle$
and clock $Z|a\rangle = \omega^a|a\rangle$, with minimum Pauli error weight $d$ needed to corrupt logical information.
Commutation corresponds directly to symplectic orthogonality in $\mathrm{GF}(3)^{2n}$, turning code classification on
one 24-slot dock into modular representation theory on a 48-dimensional symplectic space splitting under $W(F_4)$ into
$U_3 \oplus U_9 \oplus V_-$. By channel--state (Choi) duality, a pure state on $n$ qutrits is \emph{$m$-uniform} (every
$m$-qutrit marginal maximally mixed) precisely when its tensor defines an exact isometry from any $m$ input legs to the
remaining $n - m$ legs. Exhaustive classification on the bare 24-cell establishes three sharp levels of coding power.

\textbf{Classical and CSS obstruction:} Every projective monomial lift of $W(F_4)$ over $\mathrm{GF}(3)$ is
gauge-equivalent to pure global signs $\pm P_g$, and only four of the 32 $\mathrm{GF}(3)[W(F_4)]$-submodules are
self-orthogonal. Consequently, \textbf{zero self-dual $[24, 12]_3$ codes} preserve $W(F_4)$, capping
Calderbank--Shor--Steane (CSS) codes (built from separate $X$- and $Z$-type classical codes) at $[[24, 14, 3]]_3$. Even
the extremal $d = 9$ self-dual codes $S_{24}$ \cite{pless1968} and $QR_{24}$ have automorphism orders indivisible by
$9 = |\mathrm{Syl}_3(W(F_4))|$, intersecting $W(F_4)$ across all $24!$ relabelings at most in the order-12 Coxeter
subgroup $C_{12}$.

\textbf{Untwisted symplectic ceiling:} Exhaustive Goursat enumeration of all $3{,}380$ untwisted $W(F_4)$-invariant
isotropic subspaces proves exact ceilings of $[[24, 0, 4]]_3$ for pure states (forced by the irreducible 9-dimensional
module $U_9$) and $[[24, 1, 6]]_3$ for degenerate bulk-encoding codes.

\textbf{Unique $S_3$-triality tensor $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$:} Exhaustive Wedderburn Hom-socle classification across all
projective local-Clifford classes $W(F_4) \to \mathrm{PGL}_2(3)$ and all site-dependent induced actions proves that
coupling outer triality $W(F_4) / W(D_4) \cong S_3 = \langle K, P \rangle < \mathrm{GL}_2(3)$ splits
$U_9 \otimes \mathbb{F}_3^2 = W_9^{(4)} \oplus W_9^{(6)}$. This overcomes the weight-4 barrier, isolating exactly two
pure 5-uniform states $[[24, 0, 6]]_3$ and a \textbf{unique full-$W(F_4)$-covariant 5-uniform bulk-encoding tensor
$[[24, 1, 8]]_3$} ($d_{\mathrm{pure}} = 6$, logical distance $d = 8$). The bulk qutrit is encoded directly on the
three-bank triality mode $\mathbf{1}_{F_0} - \mathbf{1}_{F_1}$, with $W(F_4)$ covariance, 5-uniformity across all
$42{,}504$ five-leg subsets, distances $d_{\mathrm{pure}} = 6$ and $d = 8$, and 100\% bulk erasure
recovery across all $536{,}155$ boundary erasures of size $|E| \le 7$ machine-checked in Lean~4.

\subsection{All-radius holographic code and cusp isometry}

Because every non-origin dock $g$ of $\{3,4,3,4\}$ has an inward facet set forming a non-empty clique of $\Gamma_{24}$,
its inward degree obeys $1 \le |\mathrm{In}_R(g)| \le 3$, leaving a two-facet safety margin strictly inside the
$m \le 5$ universal isometry ceiling of $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$. Homogeneous gluing fails symmetry because none of the 24
valid $\det(M) = 2$ qutrit Bell matrices in $\mathrm{GL}_2(3)$ commute with the triality phase gate $P$. Bipartiteness
of $\{3,4,3,4\}$ resolves this uniquely: placing the canonical tensor $T_+$ on even docks ($\det g = +1$), its conjugate
$T_- = K^{\otimes 25} T_+$ on odd docks ($\det g = -1$), and $|\Phi^+\rangle$ ($M = K$) on every internal edge yields an
exact $[3,4,3,4]$-covariant all-radius bulk-to-boundary isometry
$V_R : (\mathbb{C}^3)^{\otimes |B_R|} \to (\mathbb{C}^3)^{\otimes |\partial B_R|}$ ($V_R^\dagger V_R = I$ for all
$R \ge 0$).

\textbf{Algebraic encoding rate:} Alternating bipartite boundary sizes $n_R = 24 |S_R| - n_{R-1}$ yield an exact
asymptotic bulk-to-boundary rate $r_\infty = \lim_{R\to\infty} |B_R|/|\partial B_R|$ whose rescaled value
$u = 24 r_\infty$ solves a depressed cubic:
\begin{equation}
r_\infty = \frac{\lambda + 1}{24(\lambda - 1)} = 0.046490,
\qquad
u^3 - 12 u + 12 = 0.
\label{eq:happy-cubic}
\end{equation}

\textbf{Radial distance amplification:} Direct bit-sliced $\mathrm{GF}(3)$ contraction of the radius-1 ball produces
$B_1 = [[552, 25, 8]]_3$ with full $W(F_4)$ covariance (global distance $d = 8$ set by peripheral shell-1 bulk qutrits,
whereas non-alternating $T_+/T_+$ gluing breaks triality). Because every single-leg Pauli pushes through $T_-$ with
minimum weight $5$, deep-bulk distance amplifies fivefold in one shell, giving the central origin bulk qutrit exact
logical distance $d^{(0)}(B_1) = 40$ (erasure threshold $|E| = 39$ vs.\ $40$). On $B_2$, induced-edge bounds certify
$d^{(0)}(B_2) \ge 98$, paired with explicit zero-leakage pushed origin logicals of weight $204$ ($Z_L$) and $205$
($X_L$). Greedy wedge reconstruction recovers 100\% of bulk qutrits on $B_2$ and $B_3$ under uniform boundary erasure up
to $p_{\mathrm{greedy}}^\infty = (5\lambda - 19)/(24\lambda) = 16.50\%$, and recovers the origin under structured
erasure of $44.97\%$ of $\partial B_2$.

\textbf{Ryu--Takayanagi, FLM, and cusp isometry:} On pure $[[552, 0]]_3$, the discrete Ryu--Takayanagi area law
$S(A) = |\gamma_A|\ln 3$ \cite{ryu2006rt} (equating boundary entanglement entropy to the minimum bulk cut area
$|\gamma_A|$) holds identically across all $55{,}455$ multi-cone subsets with $k \le 5$, contiguous $k = 6$, and
bulk-snapping partial cones, with sharp $0 \to 16\ln 3$ mutual-information transitions and computable stabilizer
deficits at larger cuts ($k = 8, 10, 12$). On $[[552, 25, 8]]_3$, the Faulkner--Lewkowycz--Maldacena (FLM) bulk-entropy
correction $S(A) = |\gamma_A|\ln 3 + S_{\mathrm{bulk}}$ \cite{faulkner2013flm} holds in both exact regimes ($k \le 5$
and $k \ge 17$), while a central mixed black hole yields zero shift for $k \le 7$, a classical horizon across
$k = 8\text{--}16$, and a universal $+1\ln 3$ entropy jump for $k \ge 17$. Along the horosphere cusp foliation, every
dock at depth $D \ge 1$ satisfies $|\mathrm{up}(g)| + |\mathrm{lat}(g)| \le 3 < 5$, yielding exact lateral-Bell isometry
for all $D \ge 1$, while at $D = 0$ the unique pure-$Z$ weight-6 stabilizer $g_L = Z^{\otimes 6}|_L$ induces a single
volume-independent global $\mathbb{Z}_3$ Gauss law verified on closed even cusp tori.

\subsection{Dynamical bridge to the bare automaton}

Because the HaPPY construction above is static, a central question follows: does the microscopic beat $U$ preserve the
code subspace $P_{\mathrm{code}}$ of $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$, or how do the two dynamics connect? Changing variables on each
antipodal line to $u_l = x_{a_l} + x_{b_l}$ and $v_l = x_{a_l} - x_{b_l} \pmod 3$ factorizes $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$ into
symmetric and antisymmetric 12-qutrit blocks, enabling exact evaluation of code survival across the full
$3^{24}$-dimensional dock Hilbert space (Table~\ref{tab:holo-bridge}):
\begin{equation}
p_{\mathrm{code}}(L) = \frac{1}{3}\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(P_{\mathrm{code}} L P_{\mathrm{code}} L^\dagger\bigr).
\label{eq:pcode}
\end{equation}

Two exact results characterize the bare automaton. First, because stabilizer $Z^{\otimes 24} = +1$ forbids
$Q \equiv \pm 2 \pmod 3$, the 12-line pair mixer $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ acts as the \textbf{exact identity} on
the $+1$ code subspace ($p_{\mathrm{code}} = 1$). Every other interacting bare layer ($D, K, M_{\mathrm{meet}},
L_4 = D M_{\mathrm{meet}} K D$, and $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$) is strictly non-Clifford (lying outside the
stabilizer-preserving Clifford group, with nonzero stabilizer R\'enyi magic $M_2$) and leaks $94.70\%$ to $99.999\%$

\subsection{Transverse subdivision and continuum bridges}

Since Zeno of Elea, discrete models of space have faced an apparent dilemma: either space terminates at a rigid pixel
floor that breaks continuous wave equations, or infinite subdivision is placed along the line of travel, forcing a
moving particle to cross infinitely many sub-steps in finite time (a Zeno supertask). The hyperbolic bulk of
$\{3,4,3,4\}$ resolves this dilemma geometrically by separating motion and subdivision into orthogonal directions
(Figure~\ref{fig:zeno-subdivision}). Infinite discrete space exists all at once as the algebraic Coxeter orbit
$M = \operatorname{colim}_r B_r$ rather than by sequential manufacture. Each step deeper in bulk depth $D$ keeps every
24-cell at fixed unit size in $\mathbb{H}^4$ while shrinking its projected boundary cone diameter on the ideal 3D cusp
$\mathbb{R}^3$ by $a_1^{-D} = \lambda^{-D/3} \to 0$.

Longitudinal motion from $A$ to $B$ never traverses infinitely many infinitesimal points: every beat of $U$ is a finite
one-hop facet swap $S$ ($c = 1$), so any finite-energy packet with cutoff $\Lambda$ occupies a finite depth scale
$D_* \sim \log_{a_1}(\Lambda)$ and travels from $A$ to $B$ in finitely many discrete beats, while long-range
correlations arch through shallower coarse shells in $O(\log d(A, B))$ hops. Infinite subdivision lives strictly
\emph{transversely} along bulk depth $D$, available timelessly whenever higher probe energy $E \sim a_1^D$ resolves
deeper shells. Because a finite discrete patch has finite Hilbert-space dimension while continuum quantum field theory
carries a Type~$\mathrm{III}_1$ von Neumann algebra (whose sharp spatial boundaries carry infinite entanglement), no
naive bijective isomorphism exists on a finite patch. Instead, three rigorous homomorphisms connect the discrete mesh to
continuous physics.

\textbf{Exact inductive-limit isometry (Bridge~A):} The all-depth HaPPY embeddings $V_D : \mathcal{H}_D \to
\mathcal{H}_{D+1}$ ($V_D^\dagger V_D = I$) define canonical isometries $\Phi_D : \mathcal{H}_D \to \mathcal{H}_\infty =
\overline{\operatorname{colim}_D \mathcal{H}_D}$ obeying $\Phi_{D+1} \circ V_D = \Phi_D$ and faithful observable
embeddings $\alpha_D(O) = V_D O V_D^\dagger$ with zero approximation error (intertwining bare $U$ itself into a
continuum $H_{\mathrm{CFT}}$ remains open).

\textbf{Paley--Wiener band-limited dynamical homomorphism (Bridge~B):} Sinc--Fourier interpolation maps
$l^2(a\mathbb{Z}^3)$ unitarily onto the band-limited space $\mathrm{PW}_{\pi/a}(\mathbb{R}^3) \subset L^2(\mathbb{R}^3)$
(functions whose Fourier support lies inside $[-\pi/a, \pi/a]^3$) and intertwines discrete unitary beats with continuum
Dirac, Maxwell, and Thirring propagators to $O(t(\Lambda a)^2)$, verified quantitatively in Sections~\ref{sec:composites}
and~\ref{sec:gauge}.

\textbf{Hyperfinite transfer (Bridge~C):} Passing to an ultraproduct over $a_D = a_1^{-D} \to 0$ lifts every all-scale
discrete Lean~4 invariant automatically to infinitesimal finite differences via transfer.
