\section{Holography}
\label{sec:holography}

Section~\ref{sec:spectral} showed that negative curvature on $\{3,4,3,4\}$ produces an eighteenfold radial volume growth
and a normalized spectral gap exceeding one half, turning the fixed hyperbolic bulk into an unbounded entropy sink. Two
structural questions follow directly from that geometry. The first is whether the 24-cell's own Weyl symmetry group
$W(F_4)$ organizes quantum information holographically from the four-dimensional bulk onto the three-dimensional boundary
without arbitrary tensor choices. The second is whether a fixed discrete mesh can support arbitrarily fine spatial
resolution without breaking its local rule or forcing moving excitations through infinitely many sub-steps.

Standard discrete approaches stall at both hurdles. Existing two-dimensional hyperbolic tensor-network models such as the
HaPPY pentagon code \cite{pastawski2015happy} live on flat-free hyperbolic planes ($\{5,4\}$), permit multiple
inequivalent perfect-tensor choices under local symmetry, and lack a flat three-dimensional boundary. Conversely,
conventional lattice-continuum programs obtain continuous resolution by shrinking the lattice spacing $a \to 0$ directly
along the direction of travel, which either modifies the microscopic update rule at every scale or recreates Zeno's
supertask paradox. Here we classify $W(F_4)$-covariant ternary dock tensors exhaustively, assemble the all-radius and
flat-cusp holographic network, test its exact dynamical boundary against bare $U$, and separate longitudinal finite-hop
motion from transverse bulk-depth refinement.

\subsection{Covariant code tensor}

Asking for full $W(F_4)$ symmetry on a single 24-slot ternary dock leaves zero ad-hoc freedom in choosing a holographic
tensor: the 24-cell's symmetry eliminates classical, CSS, and untwisted stabilizer constructions before isolating one
unique $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$ bulk-encoding state. On one 24-slot dock, commuting ternary Weyl--Heisenberg operators built
from shift $X|a\rangle = |a+1 \bmod 3\rangle$ and clock $Z|a\rangle = \omega^a|a\rangle$ correspond to isotropic
subspaces of $\mathrm{GF}(3)^{48}$. Under $W(F_4)$, that phase space decomposes into two symplectic copies of
$U_3 \oplus U_9 \oplus V_-$ of dimensions $3$, $9$, and $12$. By channel-state (Choi) duality, an $n$-qutrit pure state
is \emph{$m$-uniform} (all $m$-leg marginals maximally mixed) precisely when its tensor defines an exact isometry from
any $m$ legs to the remaining $n - m$ legs. Exhaustive classification on the bare 24-cell separates coding power into
three sharp algebraic levels.

\textbf{Classical and CSS obstruction:} The simplest holographic ansatz builds separate $X$-type and $Z$-type stabilizer
groups from a classical self-dual ternary code. That route fails completely under 24-cell symmetry. Every projective
monomial lift of $W(F_4)$ over $\mathrm{GF}(3)$ is gauge-equivalent to pure global signs $\pm P_g$, and only four of the
$32$ $\mathrm{GF}(3)[W(F_4)]$-submodules are self-orthogonal. Consequently, \textbf{zero self-dual $[24, 12]_3$ codes}
preserve $W(F_4)$, capping Calderbank--Shor--Steane (CSS) constructions at $[[24, 14, 3]]_3$. Even the extremal $d = 9$
self-dual Pless symmetry code $S_{24}$ \cite{pless1968} and ternary quadratic-residue code $QR_{24}$ are incompatible
with the 24-cell: their automorphism orders are indivisible by $9 = |\mathrm{Syl}_3(W(F_4))|$, so across all $24!$ slot
relabelings they intersect $W(F_4)$ at most in the order-12 Coxeter subgroup $C_{12}$.

\textbf{Untwisted symplectic ceiling:} Allowing mixed $X$/$Z$ stabilizers without twisting the local Clifford frame
raises the distance only partially. Exhaustive Goursat-lemma enumeration of all $3{,}380$ untwisted $W(F_4)$-invariant
isotropic subspaces proves exact ceilings of $[[24, 0, 4]]_3$ for pure states and $[[24, 1, 6]]_3$ for degenerate
bulk-encoding codes. The obstruction is the irreducible nine-dimensional module $U_9$, which forces weight-4 stabilizers
whenever slot permutations act identically on all three 8-slot banks without mixing local ternary quadratures.

\textbf{Unique $S_3$-triality tensor $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$:} The 24-cell possesses an outer symmetry absent from hypercubic
grids, namely the triality quotient $W(F_4) / W(D_4) \cong S_3$ permuting its three disjoint 8-slot banks. Coupling that
outer $S_3$ action to local ternary Clifford rotations $\langle K_{\mathrm{c}}, P_{\mathrm{tri}} \rangle <
\mathrm{GL}_2(3)$ splits the obstructing module directly:
\begin{equation}
U_9 \otimes \mathbb{F}_3^2 = W_9^{(4)} \oplus W_9^{(6)}.
\label{eq:triality-split}
\end{equation}
Exhaustive Wedderburn Hom-socle (irreducible-submodule intertwiner) classification across all projective local-Clifford
classes $W(F_4) \to \mathrm{PGL}_2(3)$ and all site-dependent induced actions proves that this triality twist overcomes
the weight-4 barrier uniquely. It isolates two pure 5-uniform states $[[24, 0, 6]]_3$ and a \textbf{unique
full-$W(F_4)$-covariant 5-uniform bulk-encoding tensor $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$} ($d_{\mathrm{pure}} = 6$, logical distance
$d = 8$).

Structurally, this uniqueness removes the main arbitrariness of discrete tensor-network holography. In two-dimensional
pentagon codes \cite{pastawski2015happy}, local dihedral symmetry permits distinct families of six-leg perfect tensors
with $m \le 2$ uniformity, whereas the four-dimensional 24-cell's own Weyl group eliminates every candidate except one
specific encoding in which the bulk logical qutrit lives on the bank difference $\mathbf{1}_{F_0} - \mathbf{1}_{F_1}$
between two of the three 8-slot banks. Every five-leg subset ($42{,}504$ in total) is maximally mixed, and Lean~4
machine-checks $W(F_4)$ covariance, distances $d_{\mathrm{pure}} = 6$ and $d = 8$, and $100\%$ bulk erasure recovery
across all $536{,}155$ boundary erasures of size $|E| \le 7$.

\subsection{All-radius isometry}

Gluing this unique dock tensor across the entire $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb produces an exact all-radius bulk-to-boundary
isometry whose bulk erasure protection amplifies fivefold in a single hyperbolic shell while preserving flat-cusp
foliation isometry. The geometric reason global gluing succeeds is a strict inward-degree margin. Every non-origin dock

its inward degree obeys $1 \le |\mathrm{In}_R(g)| \le 3$. Because $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$ is 5-uniform, every dock retains a
two-facet safety margin strictly inside its $m \le 5$ universal isometry ceiling, allowing every radial shell to map
inward legs plus its own bulk qutrit isometrically onto its outward legs.

Symmetry also determines the edge contraction uniquely. Homogeneous $T_+/T_+$ gluing breaks triality because none of the
24 valid $\det(M) = 2$ qutrit Bell matrices in $\mathrm{GL}_2(3)$ commute with the triality phase gate $P_{\mathrm{tri}}$.
Because $\{3,4,3,4\}$ is bipartite by Coxeter word parity, placing the canonical tensor $T_+$ on even docks
($\det g = +1$), its conjugate $T_- = K_{\mathrm{c}}^{\otimes 25} T_+$ on odd docks ($\det g = -1$), and $|\Phi^+\rangle$

\begin{equation}

\;\;(V_R^\dagger V_R = I),
\quad

\label{eq:happy-cubic}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
p_{\mathrm{code}}(L) = \frac{1}{3}\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(P_{\mathrm{code}} L P_{\mathrm{code}} L^\dagger\bigr).
\label{eq:pcode}
\end{equation}

What this resolves is purely kinematic, and the scope boundary must remain equally sharp. Because any finite discrete

\begin{equation}
\Phi_D : \mathcal{H}_D \to \mathcal{H}_\infty = \overline{\operatorname{colim}_D \mathcal{H}_D},
\qquad
\Phi_{D+1} \circ V_D = \Phi_D,
\qquad
\alpha_D(O) = V_D O V_D^\dagger,
\label{eq:colimit-bridge}
\end{equation}
providing faithful all-scale observable embeddings with zero approximation error. Because Section~\ref{sec:holo-bridge}
