\section{Holographic Coding and Continuum Subdivision}
\label{sec:holography}

The 24-cell's Weyl symmetry group $W(F_4)$ tightly constrains whether a high-distance ternary holographic tensor can
tile the entire $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb into an exact bulk-to-boundary isometry \cite{pastawski2015happy}. Resolving
this classification determines both how hyperbolic geometry protects bulk quantum information on its boundary and how
kinematic continuum subdivision operates on a fixed discrete mesh. Here we classify covariant ternary dock tensors
exhaustively, construct the all-radius holographic network and its dynamical bridge to the bare automaton, and establish
the kinematic separation of finite-hop motion from transverse bulk-depth refinement.

\subsection{Unique covariant ternary holographic tensor}

On one 24-slot dock, commuting ternary Weyl--Heisenberg operators built from shift $X|a\rangle = |a+1 \bmod 3\rangle$
and clock $Z|a\rangle = \omega^a|a\rangle$ correspond to isotropic subspaces of $\mathrm{GF}(3)^{48}$, splitting under
$W(F_4)$ into two symplectic copies of $U_3 \oplus U_9 \oplus V_-$ ($\dim = 3, 9, 12$). By channel--state (Choi) duality,
an $n$-qutrit pure state is \emph{$m$-uniform} (all $m$-leg marginals maximally mixed) precisely when its tensor defines
an exact isometry from any $m$ legs to the remaining $n - m$ legs. Exhaustive classification on the bare 24-cell
establishes three sharp levels of coding power.

\textbf{Classical and CSS obstruction:} Every projective monomial lift of $W(F_4)$ over $\mathrm{GF}(3)$ is
gauge-equivalent to pure global signs $\pm P_g$, and only four of the 32 $\mathrm{GF}(3)[W(F_4)]$-submodules are
self-orthogonal. Consequently, \textbf{zero self-dual $[24, 12]_3$ codes} preserve $W(F_4)$, capping
Calderbank--Shor--Steane (CSS) codes (built from separate $X$- and $Z$-type classical codes) at $[[24, 14, 3]]_3$. Even
the extremal $d = 9$ self-dual Pless symmetry code $S_{24}$ \cite{pless1968} and ternary quadratic-residue code
$QR_{24}$ have automorphism orders indivisible by $9 = |\mathrm{Syl}_3(W(F_4))|$, intersecting $W(F_4)$ across all
$24!$ relabelings at most in the order-12 Coxeter subgroup $C_{12}$.

\textbf{Untwisted symplectic ceiling:} Exhaustive Goursat-lemma enumeration of all $3{,}380$ untwisted
$W(F_4)$-invariant isotropic subspaces proves exact ceilings of $[[24, 0, 4]]_3$ for pure states (forced by the
irreducible 9-dimensional module $U_9$) and $[[24, 1, 6]]_3$ for degenerate bulk-encoding codes.

\textbf{Unique $S_3$-triality tensor $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$:} Exhaustive Wedderburn Hom-socle (irreducible-submodule
intertwiner) classification across all projective local-Clifford classes $W(F_4) \to \mathrm{PGL}_2(3)$ and all
site-dependent induced actions proves that coupling outer triality
$W(F_4) / W(D_4) \cong S_3 = \langle K_{\mathrm{c}}, P_{\mathrm{tri}} \rangle < \mathrm{GL}_2(3)$ splits
$U_9 \otimes \mathbb{F}_3^2 = W_9^{(4)} \oplus W_9^{(6)}$. This overcomes the weight-4 barrier, isolating two pure
5-uniform states $[[24, 0, 6]]_3$ and a \textbf{unique full-$W(F_4)$-covariant 5-uniform bulk-encoding tensor
$[[24, 1, 8]]_3$} ($d_{\mathrm{pure}} = 6$, logical distance $d = 8$). The bulk qutrit is encoded on the difference
$\mathbf{1}_{F_0} - \mathbf{1}_{F_1}$ between two of the three 8-slot banks, with $W(F_4)$ covariance, 5-uniformity
across all $42{,}504$ five-leg subsets, distances $d_{\mathrm{pure}} = 6$ and $d = 8$, and 100\% bulk erasure recovery
across all $536{,}155$ boundary erasures of size $|E| \le 7$ machine-checked in Lean~4.

\subsection{All-radius holographic code and cusp isometry}

Because every non-origin dock $g$ of $\{3,4,3,4\}$ has an inward facet set forming a non-empty clique of $\Gamma_{24}$,
its inward degree obeys $1 \le |\mathrm{In}_R(g)| \le 3$, leaving a two-facet safety margin strictly inside the
$m \le 5$ universal isometry ceiling of $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$. Homogeneous gluing fails symmetry because none of the 24
valid $\det(M) = 2$ qutrit Bell matrices in $\mathrm{GL}_2(3)$ commute with the triality phase gate $P_{\mathrm{tri}}$.
Bipartiteness of $\{3,4,3,4\}$ resolves this uniquely: placing the canonical tensor $T_+$ on even docks ($\det g = +1$),
its conjugate $T_- = K_{\mathrm{c}}^{\otimes 25} T_+$ on odd docks ($\det g = -1$), and $|\Phi^+\rangle$
($M = K_{\mathrm{c}}$) on every internal edge yields an exact $\{3,4,3,4\}$-covariant all-radius bulk-to-boundary
isometry ($R \ge 0$) on the $n_R$ dangling boundary legs of ball $B_R$ ($n_R = 24 |S_R| - n_{R-1}$, distinct from vertex
shell size $|\partial B_R|$):
\begin{equation}
V_R : (\mathbb{C}^3)^{\otimes |B_R|} \to (\mathbb{C}^3)^{\otimes n_R}
\;\;(V_R^\dagger V_R = I),
\quad
r_\infty = \lim_{R\to\infty}\frac{|B_R|}{n_R} = \frac{\lambda + 1}{24(\lambda - 1)} = 0.046490,
\label{eq:happy-cubic}
\end{equation}
whose rescaled encoding rate $u = 24 r_\infty$ solves the depressed cubic $u^3 - 12 u + 12 = 0$.

\textbf{Radial distance amplification:} Direct bit-sliced $\mathrm{GF}(3)$ contraction of the radius-1 ball produces

\textbf{Ryu--Takayanagi, FLM, and cusp isometry:} Partitioning the boundary of $B_1$ into the 24 shell-1 boundary cones
($k \in \{1, \dots, 24\}$), the discrete Ryu--Takayanagi (RT) area law $S(A) = |\gamma_A|\ln 3$ \cite{ryu2006rt}

Choi state), while mixing the central bulk qutrit (a HaPPY black-hole analog) produces a sharp $+1\ln 3$ relative entropy
jump at $k \ge 17$. Along the horosphere cusp foliation, every dock at depth $D \ge 1$ satisfies
$|\mathrm{up}(g)| + |\mathrm{lat}(g)| \le 3 < 5$, giving exact lateral-Bell isometry for all $D \ge 1$, whereas at
$D = 0$ the unique pure-$Z$ weight-6 stabilizer $g_L = Z^{\otimes 6}|_L$ induces a volume-independent global
$\mathbb{Z}_3$ charge sum rule verified on closed even cusp tori.

\subsection{Dynamical bridge to the bare automaton}

Because the HaPPY network above is static, we evaluate directly whether the microscopic beat $U$ preserves the code
subspace $P_{\mathrm{code}}$ of $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$ and classify what auxiliary unitary dynamics preserve it. Changing
variables on each antipodal line to $u_l = x_{a_l} + x_{b_l}$ and $v_l = x_{a_l} - x_{b_l} \pmod 3$ factorizes
$[[24, 1, 8]]_3$ into symmetric and antisymmetric 12-qutrit blocks, enabling exact evaluation of code survival across
the full $3^{24}$-dimensional dock Hilbert space (Table~\ref{tab:holo-bridge}, bottom):
\begin{equation}
p_{\mathrm{code}}(L) = \frac{1}{3}\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(P_{\mathrm{code}} L P_{\mathrm{code}} L^\dagger\bigr).
\label{eq:pcode}
\end{equation}

Two exact results characterize the bare automaton. First, because stabilizer $Z^{\otimes 24} = +1$ forbids
$Q \equiv \pm 2 \pmod 3$, the 12-line pair mixer $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ acts as the \textbf{exact identity} on
the $+1$ code subspace ($p_{\mathrm{code}} = 1$). Every other line-local bare layer ($D, C = D^2, K, M_{\mathrm{meet}},
L_4 = D M_{\mathrm{meet}} K D$) is strictly non-Clifford (lying outside the stabilizer-preserving Clifford group, with
nonzero stabilizer R\'enyi magic $M_2$) and leaks $94.70\%$ to $99.999\%$ out of $P_{\mathrm{code}}$ in one beat. The
chiral four-body phase $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ likewise exits $P_{\mathrm{code}}$ ($p_{\mathrm{code}} < 1$, breaking

stabilizers with zero logical coupling, giving a \textbf{72-line (144-qutrit) localized quantum shield} (threshold
$r = 4$ lines per cone), whereas global bare $U$ still leaves $P_{\mathrm{code}}$ in a single beat.

\subsection{Transverse subdivision and continuum bridges}

floor that breaks continuous wave equations, or infinite subdivision is placed along the line of travel, forcing a
moving particle to cross infinitely many sub-steps in finite time (a Zeno supertask). The hyperbolic bulk of
$\{3,4,3,4\}$ separates motion and subdivision into orthogonal directions kinematically
(Figure~\ref{fig:zeno-subdivision}). Infinite discrete space exists all at once as the algebraic Coxeter orbit
$M = \operatorname{colim}_r B_r$ rather than by sequential manufacture. Each step deeper in bulk depth $D$ keeps every
24-cell at fixed unit size in $\mathbb{H}^4$ while shrinking its projected boundary cone diameter on the ideal 3D cusp
$\mathbb{R}^3$ by $a_1^{-D} = \lambda^{-D/3} \to 0$.

Longitudinal motion from $A$ to $B$ never traverses infinitely many infinitesimal points: every beat of $U$ is a finite
one-hop facet swap $S$ ($c = 1$), so any finite-energy packet with cutoff $\Lambda$ occupies a finite depth scale
$D_* \sim \log_{a_1}(\Lambda)$ and travels from $A$ to $B$ in finitely many discrete beats, while long-range
correlations arch through shallower coarse shells in $O(\log d(A, B))$ hops. Infinite subdivision lives strictly
\emph{transversely} along bulk depth $D$, available timelessly whenever higher probe energy $E \sim a_1^D$ resolves
deeper shells. Because a finite discrete patch has finite Hilbert-space dimension while continuum quantum field theory
carries a Type~$\mathrm{III}_1$ von Neumann algebra (whose sharp spatial boundaries carry infinite entanglement), no
naive bijective isomorphism exists on a finite patch. Instead, three sharply scoped homomorphisms connect the discrete
mesh to continuous physics.

\textbf{Exact inductive-limit isometry (Bridge~A):} The static all-depth HaPPY embeddings
$V_D : \mathcal{H}_D \to \mathcal{H}_{D+1}$ ($V_D^\dagger V_D = I$) define canonical isometries
$\Phi_D : \mathcal{H}_D \to \mathcal{H}_\infty = \overline{\operatorname{colim}_D \mathcal{H}_D}$ obeying
$\Phi_{D+1} \circ V_D = \Phi_D$ and faithful observable embeddings $\alpha_D(O) = V_D O V_D^\dagger$ with zero
approximation error. As Section~3.3 proved, non-Clifford bare $U$ breaks this static code subspace in one beat, so
intertwining bare $U$ into a continuum $H_{\mathrm{CFT}}$ remains open.

\textbf{Paley--Wiener band-limited dynamical homomorphism (Bridge~B):} On flat cubic grids $a\mathbb{Z}^3$,
sinc--Fourier interpolation maps $l^2(a\mathbb{Z}^3)$ unitarily onto the band-limited space
$\mathrm{PW}_{\pi/a}(\mathbb{R}^3) \subset L^2(\mathbb{R}^3)$ (Fourier support inside $[-\pi/a, \pi/a]^3$) and
intertwines discrete unitary beats with continuum Dirac, Maxwell, and Thirring propagators to $O(t(\Lambda a)^2)$,
tested in Sections~\ref{sec:composites} and~\ref{sec:gauge} on mass-deformed Thirring and added Maxwell--Dirac rules.

\textbf{Hyperfinite transfer (Bridge~C):} Passing to a first-order ultraproduct over $a_D = a_1^{-D} \to 0$ lifts
all-scale discrete algebraic identities automatically to infinitesimal finite differences via transfer, without
constructing a standard renormalized continuum QFT measure.
