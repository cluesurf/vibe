\section{Holographic Coding and Continuum Subdivision}
\label{sec:holography}

Does the 24-cell's own Weyl symmetry group $W(F_4)$ support a high-distance ternary holographic tensor that tiles the
entire $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb into an exact bulk-to-boundary isometry \cite{pastawski2015happy}? And if so, how does
the bare eight-layer automaton $U$ interact with that code, and what does the resulting shell tower imply about
continuum fields and infinite subdivision? Here we resolve the ternary covariant tensor classification completely
(Tier~A), construct the all-radius holographic network and its dynamical bridge (Tier~A/B), and show how transverse
bulk-depth subdivision resolves Zeno's supertask paradox.

\subsection{Unique covariant ternary holographic tensor}

Each dock holds 24 qutrit legs indexed by the $D_4$ roots, spanning a 48-dimensional symplectic space over
$\mathrm{GF}(3)$ that splits under $W(F_4)$ into three parity and representation blocks $U_3 \oplus U_9 \oplus V_-$.
Exhaustive modular and symplectic classification establishes three sharp tiers of coding power (Tier~A):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Classical and CSS obstruction:} Every projective monomial lift of $W(F_4)$ over $\mathrm{GF}(3)$ is
gauge-equivalent to pure global signs $\pm P_g$, and only four of the 32 $\mathrm{GF}(3)[W(F_4)]$-submodules are
self-orthogonal. Consequently, \textbf{zero self-dual $[24, 12]_3$ codes} preserve $W(F_4)$, capping CSS codes at
$[[24, 14, 3]]_3$. Even the extremal $d = 9$ self-dual codes $S_{24}$ \cite{pless1968} and $QR_{24}$ have automorphism
orders indivisible by $9 = |\mathrm{Syl}_3(W(F_4))|$, intersecting $W(F_4)$ across all $24!$ relabelings at most in the
order-12 Coxeter subgroup $C_{12}$.
\item \textbf{Untwisted symplectic ceiling:} Exhaustive Goursat enumeration of all $3{,}380$ untwisted $W(F_4)$-invariant
isotropic subspaces proves exact ceilings of $[[24, 0, 4]]_3$ for pure states (forced by the irreducible 9-dimensional
module $U_9$) and $[[24, 1, 6]]_3$ for degenerate bulk-encoding codes.
\item \textbf{Unique $S_3$-triality tensor $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$:} Exhaustive Wedderburn Hom-socle classification across all
projective local-Clifford classes $W(F_4) \to \mathrm{PGL}_2(3)$ and all site-dependent induced actions proves that
coupling outer triality $W(F_4) / W(D_4) \cong S_3 = \langle K, P \rangle < \mathrm{GL}_2(3)$ splits
$U_9 \otimes \mathbb{F}_3^2 = W_9^{(4)} \oplus W_9^{(6)}$. This shatters the weight-4 barrier, isolating exactly two
pure 5-uniform states $[[24, 0, 6]]_3$ and a \textbf{unique full-$W(F_4)$-covariant 5-uniform bulk-encoding tensor
$[[24, 1, 8]]_3$} ($d_{\mathrm{pure}} = 6$, logical distance $d = 8$).
\end{itemize}
The bulk qutrit is encoded directly on the three-bank triality mode $\mathbf{1}_{F_0} - \mathbf{1}_{F_1}$. In the
accompanying Lean~4 formalization, exact $W(F_4)$ covariance, 5-uniformity across all $\binom{24}{5} = 42{,}504$
five-leg subsets, exact distances $d_{\mathrm{pure}} = 6$ and $d = 8$, and 100\% bulk erasure recovery
($I(\mathrm{bulk} : E^c) = 2\ln 3$) across all $536{,}155$ boundary erasures of size $|E| \le 7$ are machine-checked.

\subsection{All-radius holographic code and cusp isometry}

Because every non-origin dock $g$ of $\{3,4,3,4\}$ has an inward facet set forming a non-empty clique of $\Gamma_{24}$,
its inward degree obeys $1 \le |\mathrm{In}_R(g)| \le 3$, leaving a two-facet safety margin strictly inside the
$m \le 5$ universal isometry ceiling of $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$. However, homogeneous gluing fails symmetry: none of the 24
valid $\det(M) = 2$ qutrit Bell matrices in $\mathrm{GL}_2(3)$ commute with the triality phase gate $P$. Bipartiteness
of $\{3,4,3,4\}$ solves this uniquely. Placing the canonical tensor $T_+$ on even docks ($\det g = +1$), its conjugate
$T_- = K^{\otimes 25} T_+$ on odd docks ($\det g = -1$), and $|\Phi^+\rangle$ ($M = K$, or equivalently $|\Psi^-\rangle$)
on every internal edge yields an exact, $[3,4,3,4]$-covariant all-radius bulk-to-boundary isometry
$V_R : (\mathbb{C}^3)^{\otimes |B_R|} \to (\mathbb{C}^3)^{\otimes |\partial B_R|}$ ($V_R^\dagger V_R = I$, zero overlap
defect for all $R \ge 0$).

Four geometric and information-theoretic properties follow directly from this contraction (Tier~A):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Algebraic encoding rate:} Alternating bipartite boundary sizes $n_R = 24 |S_R| - n_{R-1}$ yield an exact
asymptotic bulk-to-boundary rate $r_\infty = \lim_{R\to\infty} |B_R|/|\partial B_R| = (\lambda + 1)/(24(\lambda - 1)) =
0.0464895582$, where $u = 24 r_\infty$ solves the depressed cubic
\begin{equation}
u^3 - 12 u + 12 = 0.
\label{eq:happy-cubic}
\end{equation}
\item \textbf{Radial distance amplification:} Direct bit-sliced $\mathrm{GF}(3)$ contraction of the radius-1 ball
produces $B_1 = [[552, 25, 40]]_3$ with full $W(F_4)$ covariance (whereas naive non-alternating $T_+/T_+$ gluing breaks
triality). Because every single-leg Pauli pushes through $T_-$ with minimum weight $5$, deep-bulk distance amplifies
fivefold in one shell ($d^{(0)}(B_1) = 40$, sharp erasure threshold at $|E| = 39$ vs.\ $40$). On $B_2$, exact
$\Gamma_{24}$ induced-edge bounds certify $d^{(0)}(B_2) \ge 98$, paired with explicit zero-leakage pushed origin
logicals of weight $204$ ($Z_L$) and $205$ ($X_L$). Greedy wedge reconstruction recovers 100\% of bulk qutrits on $B_2$
and $B_3$ under uniform boundary erasure up to $p_{\mathrm{greedy}}^\infty = (5\lambda - 19)/(24\lambda) = 16.50\%$,
and recovers the origin under structured erasure of $44.97\%$ of $\partial B_2$.
\item \textbf{Ryu--Takayanagi and FLM structure:} On pure $[[552, 0]]_3$, the discrete Ryu--Takayanagi area law
$S(A) = |\gamma_A|\ln 3$ \cite{ryu2006rt} holds exactly across all $55{,}455$ multi-cone subsets with $k \le 5$,
contiguous $k = 6$, and bulk-snapping partial cones, with sharp $0 \to 16\ln 3$ mutual-information phase transitions and
exact computable stabilizer deficits at larger cuts ($k = 8, 10, 12$). On $[[552, 25]]_3$, the
Faulkner--Lewkowycz--Maldacena formula \cite{faulkner2013flm} holds identically in both exact regimes ($k \le 5$ and
$k \ge 17$), while a central mixed black hole yields zero shift for $k \le 7$, a classical horizon across $k = 8\text{--}16$,
and a universal $+1\ln 3$ entropy jump for $k \ge 17$.
\item \textbf{All-depth cusp-to-bulk isometry:} Along the horosphere cusp foliation, every dock at depth $D \ge 1$
satisfies $|\mathrm{up}(g)| + |\mathrm{lat}(g)| \le 3 < 5$ (forming a clique of $\Gamma_{24}$), yielding exact
lateral-Bell isometry for all $D \ge 1$. At the husk $D = 0$, a unique pure-$Z$ weight-6 stabilizer $g_L = Z^{\otimes 6}|_L$
induces a single volume-independent global $\mathbb{Z}_3$ Gauss law with exact bulk isometry verified on closed even
cusp tori ($2 \times 2 \times 2$ and $4 \times 2 \times 2$).
\end{itemize}

code subspace $P_{\mathrm{code}}$ of $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$, or how do the two dynamics connect? Changing variables on each
antipodal line to $u_l = x_{a_l} + x_{b_l}$ and $v_l = x_{a_l} - x_{b_l} \pmod 3$ factorizes $[[24, 1, 8]]_3$ into
symmetric and antisymmetric 12-qutrit blocks, enabling exact evaluation of code survival
$p_{\mathrm{code}}(L) = \frac{1}{3}\operatorname{Tr}(P_{\mathrm{code}} L P_{\mathrm{code}} L^\dagger)$ across the full

Two sharp surprises emerge (Tier~A). First, because stabilizer $Z^{\otimes 24} = +1$ forbids $Q \equiv \pm 2 \pmod 3$,

($p_{\mathrm{code}} = 1$). Every other interacting bare layer ($D, K, M_{\mathrm{meet}}, L_4 = D M_{\mathrm{meet}} K D$,

\subsection{Transverse subdivision and continuum bridges}

Infinite discrete space $\{3,4,3,4\}$ exists all at once as the algebraic Coxeter orbit $M = \operatorname{colim}_r B_r$
rather than by sequential manufacture. Remarkably, the hyperbolic bulk supplies the exact dual construction for an
infinitely subdividable continuum field without ever splitting a 24-cell (Figure~\ref{fig:zeno-subdivision}). Each step
deeper in bulk depth $D$ keeps every 24-cell at fixed unit size in $\mathbb{H}^4$ while shrinking its projected boundary
cone diameter on the ideal 3D cusp $\mathbb{R}^3$ by $a_1^{-D} = \lambda^{-D/3} \to 0$.

This geometry eliminates the physical ultraviolet tension behind Zeno's supertask paradox. Longitudinal motion from $A$
to $B$ never traverses infinitely many infinitesimal points: every beat of $U$ is a strictly finite one-hop facet swap
$S$ ($c = 1$), and any finite-energy packet with cutoff $\Lambda$ occupies a finite depth scale
$D_* \sim \log_{a_1}(\Lambda)$ and travels from $A$ to $B$ in finitely many discrete beats (with long-range correlations
arching through shallower coarse shells in $O(\log d(A, B))$ hops). Infinite subdivision lives strictly
\emph{transversely} along bulk depth $D$, available timelessly whenever higher probe energy $E \sim a_1^D$ resolves
deeper shells.

Because a finite discrete patch has finite Hilbert-space dimension while continuum quantum field theory carries a
Type~$\mathrm{III}_1$ algebra, no naive bijective isomorphism exists on a finite patch. Instead, three rigorous
homomorphisms bridge the discrete mesh to continuous physics:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Exact inductive-limit isometry (Bridge~A):} The all-depth HaPPY embeddings $V_D : \mathcal{H}_D \to
\mathcal{H}_{D+1}$ ($V_D^\dagger V_D = I$) define canonical isometries $\Phi_D : \mathcal{H}_D \to \mathcal{H}_\infty =
\overline{\operatorname{colim}_D \mathcal{H}_D}$ obeying $\Phi_{D+1} \circ V_D = \Phi_D$ and faithful observable
embeddings $\alpha_D(O) = V_D O V_D^\dagger$ with zero approximation error (while intertwining bare $U$ itself into a
continuum $H_{\mathrm{CFT}}$ remains open).
\item \textbf{Paley--Wiener band-limited dynamical homomorphism (Bridge~B):} Sinc--Fourier interpolation maps
$l^2(a\mathbb{Z}^3)$ unitarily onto $\mathrm{PW}_{\pi/a}(\mathbb{R}^3) \subset L^2(\mathbb{R}^3)$ and intertwines
discrete unitary beats with continuum Dirac, Maxwell, and Thirring propagators to $O(t(\Lambda a)^2)$, verified
quantitatively in Sections~\ref{sec:composites} and~\ref{sec:gauge}.
\item \textbf{Hyperfinite transfer (Bridge~C):} Passing to an ultraproduct over $a_D = a_1^{-D} \to 0$ lifts every
all-scale discrete Lean~4 invariant automatically to infinitesimal finite differences via transfer.
\end{enumerate}

Having established how static holographic structure and transverse bulk refinement bridge the fixed mesh to continuum
fields, we now turn the microscopic automaton on and ask what happens when multiple excitations actually collide inside
bare $U$: do they localize, scatter chaotically, or form relativistic composite clocks?
