\section{Bound States, Level Repulsion, and Moving Clocks}
\label{sec:composites}

Multi-excitation collisions under the bare beat $U$ over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ test whether local unitary gates can
assemble stable bound matter, mediate entangling scattering, and support relativistic kinematics such as $E = mc^2$ and
$1/\gamma$ time dilation. We establish four sharp dynamical regimes: an unconditional no-go theorem forbidding
compactons (strictly finite-support periodic eigenstates), a spectral excision proof dissolving isotropic pair bound
states into Feshbach resonances, an exact integrable collinear composite tower with closed-form dispersion, and a clear
separation between cutoff-heavy bare clocks and the relativistic light-mass deformation limit.

\subsection{Ballistic expansion and hyperbolic gap excision}

A strictly localized excitation on a curved hyperbolic graph always reaches an outer frontier dock whose outward facets
outnumber its inward facets by at least seven to one. Whenever the local half-coin splits amplitude across an antipodal
line, a strictly positive fraction leaks outward into untouched shells, making finite-support recurrence impossible.
This geometric mechanism yields an all-body no-go theorem for the bare automaton (with the exact Lorentz
height-convexity and Pell no-periodicity core machine-checked in Lean~4, and the multi-body Fock instantiation verified
deterministically):

\begin{theorem}[Unconditional Compacton No-Go on $\{3,4,3,4\}$]
\label{thm:compacton-nogo}
Under the bare automaton $U$, zero nontrivial finite-support periodic eigenstates $U^T |\psi\rangle = \lambda
|\psi\rangle$ ($|\psi\rangle \neq |V_1\rangle$) exist for any excitation count $k \ge 1$ and any integer period
$T \ge 1$.
\end{theorem}

The proof combines two exact algebraic identities. First, for every dock $g$ and antipodal root pair $(a, -a)$, the
even-integer Lorentz height $H_2(g) = -2 B(g c_0, c_0) \in 2\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}$ obeys universal midpoint convexity:
\begin{equation}
H_2(g k_a) + H_2(g k_{-a}) = 6\, H_2(g).
\label{eq:height-convexity}
\end{equation}
Consequently, no frontier dock $g_*$ attaining the instantaneous maximum height $M(t)$ can possess an antipodal inward
pair, bounding its inward degree by $|\mathrm{In}_H(g_*)| \le 3$ and leaving at least $21$ outward facets. Second, any
$m$-particle state arriving on an antipodal-free subset of facets exits strictly outward with exact Gram-diagonalized
probability $P_{\mathrm{out}} = 1 - (3/4)^m > 0$ ($0.25$ through $0.6836$ for $m = 1\text{--}4$, including active chiral
quads). After at most one turnaround beat, the wavefront expands ballistically according to the Pell recurrence
\begin{equation}
M(t + 1) \ge 6\, M(t) - M(t - 1),
\label{eq:pell-frontier}
\end{equation}
ruling out finite-support breathers unconditionally.

Relaxing finite support to square-integrable $l^2$ wavefunctions exposes a sharp distinction between isotropic pair
mixing $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ and collinear contact scattering $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ inside bare $U$. On the
identical-tone two-body sector, $U$ factorizes as $U_{\mathrm{dec}} \circ M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$, where
$U_{\mathrm{dec}}$ is the decoupled line-local beat without $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$. Denoting by $K(z)$ the free two-body
resolvent of $U_{\mathrm{dec}}$ projected onto identical-pair states reduces bound-state existence on $\{3,4,3,4\}$ to a
scalar Birman--Schwinger eigenvalue equation on $l^2(V(\{3,4,3,4\}))$:
\begin{equation}
\bigl(I - (\zeta_{18} - 1)\, K(z)\bigr) f = 0.
\label{eq:birman-schwinger}
\end{equation}
Because negative curvature narrows the free propagation band through Section~\ref{sec:spectral}'s spectral bound
($\rho/24 \le 0.4772$ already at Depth~4), Parseval estimates across the momentum gaps and outer wings rigorously
\textbf{excise $83.12\%$ of the spectral circle $S^1$}, absorbing the flat-4D-$D_4$ bound pocket
(Table~\ref{tab:scattering-gates}). Exact radius-3 Dyson--Volterra evolution ($8{,}857$ docks) and the infinite Bethe
cavity tree $\mathbb{Z}^{*12}$ covering the remaining arcs show that hyperbolic curvature dissolves the flat pole into a
finite-lifetime \textbf{Feshbach resonance} (a metastable quasi-bound state embedded in the scattering continuum,
lifetime $\tau = 12.73$ beats) paired with a destructive-interference Fano antiresonance zero. Likewise, exact
infinite-volume $16 \times 16$ resolvents across all four chiral four-line classes ($k^* = 4$) certify zero
$\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ bound states, though resonant interference yields strong transient chiral trapping and mirror
asymmetry (Table~\ref{tab:scattering-gates}).

\subsection{Collinear composite tower and Bethe strings}

Exact $l^2$ bound states survive inside bare $U$ along any single straight antipodal geodesic line of $\{3,4,3,4\}$.
When same-sign excitations carry distinct internal roles along one line, both $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ and
$\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ act as the identity, eliminating cross-line leakage altogether.

In this collinear sector, bare $U$ becomes exact Yang--Baxter integrable \cite{yang1967, baxter1972} (multi-body
scattering factorizes into pairwise two-body collisions obeying the braid relation, with $6 \times 6$ braid defect
$5.70 \times 10^{-16}$ and three-body Bethe quantization error $1.78 \times 10^{-15}$). Its rational XXX rapidity and
universal Bethe $k$-string pole law (a $k$-particle bound complex whose quasimomenta align at $u = -i h$,
$h \in \mathbb{Z}/2$) take compact closed forms:
\begin{equation}
u(p, \alpha) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\,\frac{\sin E_\alpha(p)}{\sin p},
\qquad
z(h) = e^{2 i p(h)} = \frac{1 - 3h + 3h^2}{1 + 3h + 3h^2}.
\label{eq:bethe-pole-law}
\end{equation}
Every symmetric $P = 0$ composite bound state on $\{3,4,3,4\}$ is an exact Bethe $k$-string of
Equation~\eqref{eq:bethe-pole-law}, realizable verbatim in four dimensions up to $k \le 9$ via the nine-point internal
role alphabet $\{0, \dots, 8\}$ (and forbidden for $k \ge 4$ in any three-role truncation because pigeonhole collisions
activate $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$). Beyond the Lean-proved $k = 2$ doublon in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/13]$ and $k = 3$ trion in
$\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/7]$, Equation~\eqref{eq:bethe-pole-law} resolves the four-body tetron bound state in the continuum
(BIC) via one exact eigenstate in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/49]$ parametrized by ordered even-sublattice separations
$d_1, d_2, d_3 \ge 0$ (hard-core exclusion $d_1 + d_2 \ge 1$, $d_2 + d_3 \ge 1$, contact spins $(s_j, s_{j+1}) = (0, 1)$
when $d_j = 0$) and orientations $s = (s_1, s_2, s_3, s_4) \in \{0, 1\}^4$ (zero defects across $1{,}220$ exact integer
stencils across all five contact regimes):
\begin{gather}
\psi_4(d_1, d_2, d_3 \mid s) = \left(\frac{13}{49}\right)^{d_1+d_3-2}\left(\frac{1}{49}\right)^{d_2-1} \bar{q}^2\,
q^{\mathbf{1}_{s_1=0} + \mathbf{1}_{s_4=1}}\, p^{\mathbf{1}_{s_2=0} + \mathbf{1}_{s_3=1}}, \nonumber\\
p = \frac{-1 - 4\omega}{13},
\qquad
q = \frac{-29 - 12\omega}{49},
\label{eq:tetron-exact}
\end{gather}
matching finite-ring diagonalization ($M = 10$) to $3.89 \times 10^{-9}$ overlap defect. Because straight hyperbolic
geodesics intersect the curved $D = 0$ horosphere on only two adjacent docks, these exact bare-$U$ composites are
strictly one-dimensional geodesic complexes rather than three-dimensional husk-confined particles. Their boosted
dispersion across the open Brillouin zone $P \in (-\pi, \pi)$ obeys
\begin{equation}
E_k(P) = \left(\frac{2\pi k}{3} - \pi\right)
+ \arcsin\!\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3k^2+1}}\right)
+ \arcsin\!\left(\frac{\cos P}{\sqrt{3k^2+1}}\right) \pmod{2\pi},
\label{eq:universal-dispersion}
\end{equation}
proved analytically for $k = 1$, verified to $9.6 \times 10^{-14}$ for $k = 2$, confirmed on finite rings for $k = 3, 4$,
and conjectured for $k \le 9$. Table~\ref{tab:composite-tower} records the resulting $k = 1\text{--}5$ tower. Neither the
doublon (protected by a quartic zone-edge gap $(\pi - P)^4 / (64\sqrt{3})$) nor the trion (separated from all eight
multi-particle continua by at least $5.17 \times 10^{-5}$) dissociates anywhere on $(-\pi, \pi)$.

\subsection{Level repulsion, scattering gates, and neutral-pair no-go}

Although pure distinct-role collinear motion is integrable, the full bare automaton $U$ is \emph{not} globally
integrable: mixing opposite tones ($K + M_{\mathrm{meet}}$) or mixing identical and distinct roles
($M_{\mathrm{pair}} + M_{\mathrm{meet}}$) breaks Yang--Baxter factorization immediately
(Table~\ref{tab:scattering-gates}). In the collinear three-role sector $(+,+,+,-)$ at $L = 5, Q = +2$, consecutive
Floquet eigenphase spacing ratios exhibit level repulsion consistent with the Circular Orthogonal Ensemble (COE,
$\langle r \rangle = 0.5164$ across $336$ gaps, finite-size scaling to larger $L$ remains future work). On the full
four-dimensional honeycomb, exact conservation of per-line parity $N_a \bmod 2$ forces $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ and
$\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ to be mutually exclusive at $k = 4$, first coexisting at $k = 5 = 2 + 1 + 1 + 1$ (confirmed in
exact integer arithmetic across $3{,}072$ branches).

Within the collinear sector, isolated bare-$U$ composite collisions realize native entangling gates
(Table~\ref{tab:scattering-gates}). Colliding a tuned momentum-$p_*$ pulse with a stationary doublon on $\mathrm{Sym}^3$
imparts a near-exact controlled-$\pi$ phase with $0.23\%$ breakup, while for separable distinct-role inputs
$|(0,1)_{\mathrm{Sym}}\rangle \otimes |(2)\rangle$, exact Yang fusion implements a lossless partial role-SWAP with a
$+\pi/2$ elastic phase ($P_{\mathrm{el}} = 1/3$, $P_{\mathrm{swap}} = 2/3$).

Extending collinear gates into general composable four-dimensional circuits meets four quantified obstructions inside
bare $U$ alone.

\textbf{Opposite-tone breakup:} Colliding opposite-sign excitations through the swap layer $K$ splits doublons with
$64.21\%$ breakup probability rather than imparting an elastic phase.

\textbf{Zero cross-line contact coupling:} Distinct-role $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ composites propagate independently along
intersecting $D_4$ lines with exact zero cross-line coupling ($\mathrm{diff} = 0$).

\textbf{Weak diffusive pair blockade:} Three-body $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ blockade imparts only $-0.00912\pi$ per beat
while diffusing identical-role pairs isotropically across all 12 lines.

\textbf{Balanced neutral-pair no-go:} Encoding logical trits in neutral pairs $\{(-1,+1), (0,0), (+1,-1)\}$ fails under
activity conservation, ballistic $S_K = -1$ dispersal ($P_{\mathrm{same}}(40) = 0.0113$), and the exact
$(-I, \mathcal{C})$ Annihilation Theorem forcing $\chi_{\mathrm{odd}} \equiv 0$ on every $m$-rail neutral-pair junction
(all-time decoupling across all $264$ two-rail and $1{,}760$ three-rail junctions). We return to the resulting
substrate-versus-bare-$U$ computability boundary in Section~\ref{sec:lean-and-open}.

\subsection{Light-mass relativity, clocks, and warp null tests}

Equation~\eqref{eq:universal-dispersion} exposes a fundamental physical distinction between the discrete-cutoff regime
and the continuum infrared regime. At the bare automaton's heavy coin mass $m = \pi/3$, every $k$-body composite has
strictly additive inertial mass $m_{k,\mathrm{inert}} = k\sqrt{3}$ despite $76.84\%$ quasienergy binding at $k = 2$,
giving species-dependent maximum speeds $v_{k,\max} = 1/\sqrt{3k^2+1}$ and a large Lorentz remainder ratio
$R_2 = m_{2,\mathrm{inert}} / \Omega_2(0) = 7.1406 \neq 1$.

Yet the contact interaction $4 n_+ n_-$ inside $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ is mathematically a discrete-time Floquet version of
the $(1+1)$-dimensional massive Thirring model (a relativistic Dirac fermion with local four-fermion contact coupling)
\cite{thirring1958}. Deforming bare $U$ by holding $\phi_{\mathrm{meet}} = 2\pi/3$ fixed while lowering the coin mass
$m \to 0$ restores Einstein relativity: by $m = 0.025$, quasienergy binding and inertial-mass defect converge together
toward the exact $50\%$ continuum limit, driving all relativistic ratios toward unity (Table~\ref{tab:clocks-warp}).

This mass-remainder insight explains why discrete composite clocks can exhibit wrong-sign acceleration when boosted. For
any two-level composite with rest masses $M_g, M_e$ ($\Delta M = M_e - M_g$), rest inertias
$m_{g,\mathrm{inert}}, m_{e,\mathrm{inert}}$ ($\bar{m}_{\mathrm{inert}} = (m_{g,\mathrm{inert}} +
m_{e,\mathrm{inert}})/2$), mean group speed $v_{\mathrm{avg}}$, and inertia remainders $R_j = m_{j,\mathrm{inert}}/M_j$,
the low-velocity clock dilation coefficient obeys an exact algebraic limit:
\begin{equation}
D_{\mathrm{clock}}
\equiv \lim_{P \to 0}\frac{\omega(P) - \omega(0)}{-\frac{1}{2} v_{\mathrm{avg}}^2 \omega(0)}
= \frac{m_{g,\mathrm{inert}} m_{e,\mathrm{inert}}}{\bar{m}_{\mathrm{inert}}^2}\, R_e
\left(1 + \frac{M_g}{\Delta M}\frac{R_e - R_g}{R_e}\right).
\label{eq:anti-dilation-law}
\end{equation}
Whenever $R_g > R_e$ and $(M_g/\Delta M)|(R_e - R_g)/R_e| > 1$, the factor flips negative and the moving clock speeds up
instead of slowing down, confirmed on a parameter-deformed $m = 0.72$ contact-knot automaton
(Table~\ref{tab:clocks-warp}). Matching $R_g \approx R_e$ in a separate parameter-deformed, decoupled flavor-switched
two-body Thirring clock ($m = 0.09$) restores real-space $1/\gamma(v/c)$ time dilation across $v \in [0, 0.8c]$ to below
$0.86\%$, while a one-body two-flavor Dirac clock satisfies $\partial E / \partial m|_P = \sqrt{1 - (v / v_{\max})^2}$
for $m \in (0, \pi/2)$ and $|P| \le \pi/2$ ($0.067\%$ live accuracy at $m = \pi/3$, with $v_{\max} = 1/2 < c$).

Finally, we subject the honeycomb's Steinberg warp factor $\lambda$ ($a_1 = \lambda^{1/3}$) to a strict external
anti-numerology audit against Standard Model fermion mass ratios and Cabibbo--Kobayashi--Maskawa (CKM) quark mixing
angles (Table~\ref{tab:clocks-warp}). Fixing integer bulk depths by hand yields order-unity prefactors in $[0.69, 1.76]$
across five decades, yet sweeping $2{,}801$ rival bases $b \in [2, 30]$ under linear and Jeffreys log-uniform priors
proves that statistical significance vanishes once exponents vary freely. An exhaustive check against Koide's empirical
charged-lepton relation \cite{koide1983} likewise confirms that no discrete depth triple approaches
$Q_{\mathrm{PDG}} = 0.6666605$, so the Steinberg warp compresses hierarchies only at order unity once depth assignments
are supplied externally.
