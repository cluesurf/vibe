\section{Bound States, Scattering Chaos, and Moving Clocks}
\label{sec:composites}

When multiple excitations collide under the bare beat $U$ over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$, can they lock into localized
four-dimensional breathers, or does hyperbolic curvature disperse them? And if bound states do form, can they scatter
chaotically, mediate entangling gates, and obey relativistic kinematics such as $E = mc^2$ and $1/\gamma$ time dilation?
This is the core dynamical test of any discrete automaton: whether local unitary gates can assemble stable composite
matter and relativistic clocks rather than featureless dispersion. We answer these questions across four regimes: an
unconditional no-go theorem forbidding compactons, a spectral excision proof dissolving isotropic pair bound states into
Feshbach resonances, an exact integrable collinear composite tower with closed-form dispersion, and a sharp separation
between lattice-heavy bare clocks and the relativistic light-mass limit.

\subsection{Ballistic expansion and hyperbolic gap excision}

Intuitively, a strictly localized excitation on a curved hyperbolic graph always sits on an outer shell whose outward
facets outnumber its inward facets by at least seven to one. Every time the local half-coin splits amplitude across an
antipodal line, a strictly positive fraction leaks outward into untouched shells, making finite-support recurrence
impossible. This geometric intuition yields a sharp all-body no-go theorem for the bare automaton (machine-checked in
Lean~4):

\begin{theorem}[Unconditional Compacton No-Go on $\{3,4,3,4\}$]
\label{thm:compacton-nogo}
Under the bare automaton $U$, zero nontrivial finite-support periodic eigenstates $U^T |\psi\rangle = \lambda
|\psi\rangle$ ($|\psi\rangle \neq |V_1\rangle$) exist for any excitation count $k \ge 1$ and any integer period
$T \ge 1$.
\end{theorem}

The proof combines two exact algebraic identities. First, for every dock $g$ and antipodal root pair $(a, -a)$, the
even-integer Lorentz height $H_2(g) = -2 B(g c_0, c_0) \in 2\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}$ obeys universal midpoint convexity:
\begin{equation}
H_2(g k_a) + H_2(g k_{-a}) = 6\, H_2(g).
\label{eq:height-convexity}
\end{equation}
Consequently, no frontier dock $g_*$ attaining the instantaneous maximum height $M(t)$ can possess an antipodal inward
pair, bounding its inward degree by $|\mathrm{In}_H(g_*)| \le 3$ and leaving at least $21$ outward facets. Second, any
$m$-particle state arriving on an antipodal-free subset of facets exits strictly outward with exact Gram-diagonalized
probability $P_{\mathrm{out}} = 1 - (3/4)^m > 0$ ($0.25$ through $0.6836$ for $m = 1\text{--}4$, including active chiral
quads). After at most one turnaround beat, the wavefront expands ballistically according to the Pell recurrence
\begin{equation}
M(t + 1) \ge 6\, M(t) - M(t - 1),
\label{eq:pell-frontier}
\end{equation}
ruling out finite-support breathers unconditionally.

Relaxing finite support to square-integrable $l^2$ wavefunctions exposes a sharp contrast between isotropic pair
mixing $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ and collinear contact scattering $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ inside bare $U$. On the
identical-tone two-body sector, $U$ factorizes as $U_{\mathrm{dec}} \circ M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$, reducing
bound-state existence on the infinite four-dimensional honeycomb to a scalar Birman--Schwinger equation on
$l^2(V(\{3,4,3,4\}))$:
\begin{equation}
\bigl(I - (\zeta_{18} - 1)\, K(z)\bigr) f = 0.
\label{eq:birman-schwinger}
\end{equation}
Because negative curvature narrows the free-propagation band through Section~\ref{sec:spectral}'s bound
$\rho/24 \le 0.477170$ (opening a hyperbolic core $P_{\mathrm{hyp}} = 0.3417\pi > q_c = 0.2441\pi$), combining Parseval
bounds in the momentum gaps ($\sup\hat{t} = 0.5763 \ll \cot(\pi/18) = 5.6713$) and outer wings ($\le 1.0025$) rigorously
\textbf{excises $83.12\%$ of the spectral circle $S^1$}. This excised zone absorbs the entire flat-4D-$D_4$ bound pocket
($E_{\mathrm{flat}}(0) = 1.770647\text{ rad}$). Exact radius-3 Dyson--Volterra evolution ($8{,}857$ docks) and the
infinite Bethe cavity tree $\mathbb{Z}^{*12}$ (covering the remaining $16.88\%$ arcs, sum rule $1.0032$) show that
hyperbolic curvature dissolves the flat bound pole into a finite-lifetime \textbf{Feshbach resonance} at
$E_{\mathrm{res}} = 1.6559\text{ rad}$ ($\mathrm{FWHM} = 0.0785\text{ rad}$, lifetime $\tau = 12.73$ beats) paired with
a Fano antiresonance zero at $E_{\mathrm{meet}}(0) = 1.609267\text{ rad}$. Likewise, exact infinite-volume $16 \times 16$
resolvents across all four chiral four-line classes ($k^* = 4$) certify zero $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ bound states
($\min|\det| = 5.79 \times 10^{-4} > 0$), though resonant interference produces up to a $207.6\times$ chiral trapping
boost at $t = 16$ and a $4.58\times$ mirror asymmetry.

\subsection{Collinear composite tower and Bethe strings}

If isotropic pair mixing disperses on the curved bulk, how can any exact $l^2$ bound state survive inside bare $U$? The
answer lies along any single straight antipodal geodesic line of $\{3,4,3,4\}$. When same-sign excitations carry
distinct internal roles along one line, both $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ and $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ act identically as the
identity, eliminating cross-line leakage altogether.

In this collinear sector, bare $U$ becomes exact Yang--Baxter integrable \cite{yang1967, baxter1972} ($6 \times 6$ braid
defect $5.70 \times 10^{-16}$, three-body Bethe quantization error $1.78 \times 10^{-15}$). Its rational XXX rapidity
and universal Bethe $k$-string pole law ($u = -i h$, $h \in \frac{1}{2}\mathbb{Z}$) take compact closed forms:
\begin{equation}
u(p, \alpha) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\,\frac{\sin E_\alpha(p)}{\sin p},
\qquad
z(h) = e^{2 i p(h)} = \frac{1 - 3h + 3h^2}{1 + 3h + 3h^2}.
\label{eq:bethe-pole-law}
\end{equation}
Every symmetric $P = 0$ composite bound state on $\{3,4,3,4\}$ is an exact Bethe $k$-string of
Equation~\eqref{eq:bethe-pole-law}, realizable verbatim in four dimensions up to $k \le 9$ via the automaton's
nine-point internal role alphabet $\{0, \dots, 8\}$ (and forbidden for $k \ge 4$ in any three-role truncation because
pigeonhole collisions turn on $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$). Beyond the Lean-proved $k = 2$ doublon in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/13]$
and $k = 3$ trion in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/7]$, Equation~\eqref{eq:bethe-pole-law} resolves the four-body tetron bound
state in the continuum (BIC) via one exact closed-form eigenstate in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/49]$ across all five contact
regimes (zero defects across $1{,}220$ exact integer stencils):
\begin{gather}
\psi_4(d_1, d_2, d_3 \mid s) = \left(\frac{13}{49}\right)^{d_1+d_3-2}\left(\frac{1}{49}\right)^{d_2-1} \bar{q}^2\,
q^{\mathbf{1}_{s_1=0} + \mathbf{1}_{s_4=1}}\, p^{\mathbf{1}_{s_2=0} + \mathbf{1}_{s_3=1}}, \nonumber\\
p = \frac{-1 - 4\omega}{13},
\qquad
q = \frac{-29 - 12\omega}{49},
\label{eq:tetron-exact}
\end{gather}
matching finite-ring diagonalization ($M = 10$) to $3.89 \times 10^{-9}$ overlap defect. Furthermore, every $k$-body
symmetric composite obeys a single universal closed-form boosted dispersion across the entire open Brillouin zone
$P \in (-\pi, \pi)$:
\begin{equation}
E_k(P) = \left(\frac{2\pi k}{3} - \pi\right)
+ \arcsin\!\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3k^2+1}}\right)
+ \arcsin\!\left(\frac{\cos P}{\sqrt{3k^2+1}}\right) \pmod{2\pi}.
\label{eq:universal-dispersion}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Chaos, scattering gates, and neutral-pair no-go}

Although pure distinct-role collinear motion is integrable, the full bare automaton $U$ is \emph{not} globally
integrable. Mixing opposite tones ($K + M_{\mathrm{meet}}$, Yang--Baxter defect $1.1950$) or mixing identical and
distinct roles ($M_{\mathrm{pair}} + M_{\mathrm{meet}}$, defect $1.3994$) breaks factorization immediately. In the
collinear three-role sector $(+,+,+,-)$ at $L = 5, Q = +2$, exact Floquet level-spacing ratios reach circular orthogonal
ensemble (COE) quantum chaos: $\langle r \rangle = 0.5164$ across $336$ non-degenerate gaps, matching the COE benchmark
$0.5307$ rather than integrable Poisson statistics $0.3863$. On the full four-dimensional honeycomb, exact conservation
of per-line parity $N_a \bmod 2$ forces $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ and $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ to be mutually exclusive at
$k = 4$, first coexisting at $k = 5 = 2 + 1 + 1 + 1$ (confirmed in exact integer arithmetic across $3{,}072$ branches).

Within the collinear sector, bare-$U$ composite collisions realize native entangling gates. Colliding a momentum-$p_*$
pulse ($p_* = -\arctan(6\sqrt{3}/5)$) with a stationary doublon on $\mathrm{Sym}^3$ imparts a controlled-$\pi$ phase
($\Delta\phi_{\mathrm{CZ}} = 0.99943\pi$, transmission $98.84\%$, breakup $0.23\%$, Wigner fidelity $0.9445$). For
separable distinct-role inputs $|(0,1)_{\mathrm{Sym}}\rangle \otimes |(2)\rangle$, exact Yang fusion implements a
lossless partial role-SWAP with a $+\pi/2$ elastic phase ($P_{\mathrm{el}} = 1/3$, $P_{\mathrm{swap}} = 2/3$, zero
breakup).

Extending collinear gates into general composable four-dimensional circuits hits four quantified obstructions in bare
$U$ alone. \textbf{Opposite-tone breakup:} colliding opposite-sign excitations through $K$ splits doublons with
$64.21\%$ breakup probability. \textbf{Zero cross-line contact coupling:} distinct-role $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ composites
never couple across intersecting $D_4$ lines ($\mathrm{diff} = 0$). \textbf{Weak diffusive pair blockade:} three-body
$M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ blockade imparts only $-0.00912\pi$ per beat while diffusing identical pairs isotropically across
all 12 lines. \textbf{Balanced neutral-pair no-go:} encoding logical trits in neutral pairs
$\{(-1,+1), (0,0), (+1,-1)\}$ fails under $D$-conservation, ballistic $S_K = -1$ dispersal
($P_{\mathrm{same}}(40) = 0.0113$), and the exact $(-I, \mathcal{C})$ Annihilation Theorem forcing
$\chi_{\mathrm{odd}} \equiv 0$ on every $m$-rail neutral-pair junction (all-time decoupling across all $264$ two-rail
and $1{,}760$ three-rail junctions).

\subsection{Light-mass relativity, clocks, and warp null tests}

Equation~\eqref{eq:universal-dispersion} exposes a fundamental physical distinction between the cutoff lattice regime
and the continuum infrared regime. At the bare automaton's heavy coin mass $m = \pi/3$, every $k$-body composite has
strictly additive inertial mass $m_{k,\mathrm{inert}} = k\sqrt{3}$ despite $76.84\%$ quasienergy binding at $k = 2$,
giving species-dependent maximum speeds $v_{k,\max} = 1/\sqrt{3k^2+1}$ and a large Lorentz remainder ratio
$R_2 = m_{2,\mathrm{inert}} / \Omega_2(0) = 7.1406 \neq 1$.

Yet the contact interaction $4 n_+ n_-$ inside $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ is mathematically a discrete Floquet massive Thirring
model \cite{thirring1958}. Holding the bare phase $\phi_{\mathrm{meet}} = 2\pi/3$ fixed while lowering only the coin
mass $m \to 0$ (continuum light-mass scaling) restores exact Einstein relativity cleanly: at $m = 0.025$, quasienergy binding
reaches $51.08\%$ ($50\%$ limit), inertial mass drops in lockstep by $50.08\%$ (defect-to-binding ratio $0.9804 \to 1$),
$R_2 = 1.0206 \to 1$, $c_{\mathrm{eff}} = 0.9898 \to 1$, and quartic dispersion ratio $\rho_2 = 0.9952 \to 1$.

This mass-remainder insight also explains why naive lattice composite clocks can exhibit wrong-sign acceleration when
boosted. For any two-level composite whose ground and excited rest inertia remainders $R_j = m_{j,\mathrm{inert}} / M_j$
differ, the low-velocity clock dilation coefficient obeys an exact algebraic law:
\begin{equation}
D_{\mathrm{clock}}
\equiv \frac{\omega(v) - \omega(0)}{-\frac{1}{2} v_{\mathrm{avg}}^2 \omega(0)}
= \frac{m_{g,\mathrm{inert}} m_{e,\mathrm{inert}}}{\bar{m}_{\mathrm{inert}}^2}\, R_e
\left(1 + \frac{M_g}{\Delta M}\frac{R_e - R_g}{R_e}\right).
\label{eq:anti-dilation-law}
\end{equation}
Whenever $R_g > R_e$ and $(M_g/\Delta M)|(R_e - R_g)/R_e| > 1$, the factor flips negative and the moving clock speeds up
instead of slowing down, confirmed quantitatively on an $m = 0.72$ contact-knot automaton (Table~\ref{tab:clocks-warp}).
Matching $R_g \approx R_e$ restores clean real-space $1/\gamma(v/c)$ time dilation across $v \in [0, 0.8c]$ to below
$0.86\%$ in a decoupled flavor-switched two-body Thirring clock ($m = 0.09$), while an exact all-mass one-body
two-flavor Dirac theorem $\partial E / \partial m|_P = \sqrt{1 - (v / v_{\max})^2}$ achieves $0.067\%$ live accuracy
even at bare $U$'s heavy coin $m = \pi/3$.

Finally, we subject the honeycomb's Steinberg warp factor $\lambda = 18.2787077744$ ($a_1 = \lambda^{1/3}$,
$\lambda^{-1/2} = 0.2339$) to a strict external anti-numerology audit against Standard Model fermion mass ratios and CKM
mixing angles (Table~\ref{tab:clocks-warp}). Assigning integer bulk depths yields order-unity prefactors in
$[0.69, 1.76]$ across five decades (leptons $\lambda^{-(0,1,3)}$, up quarks $\lambda^{-(0,2,4)}$, down quarks
$a_1^{-(0,4,7)}$, $|V_{us}| \approx \lambda^{-1/2}$ within $+4.0\%$). However, sweeping $2{,}801$ rival bases
$b \in [2, 30]$ under both linear and Jeffreys log-uniform priors proves that statistical significance vanishes once
exponents are allowed to vary freely ($p_{\mathrm{log}} = 0.6994$ and $0.6226$), and an exhaustive Koide control
\cite{koide1983} confirms that no discrete depth triple approaches $Q_{\mathrm{PDG}} = 0.6666605$ (best triple $(0,1,3)$
gives $Q = 0.67870$, $1{,}955\times$ worse, inside a $20.5\%$ base plateau). The Steinberg warp therefore compresses
hierarchies only at order unity once depth assignments are supplied externally.
