\section{Particle Dynamics}
\label{sec:composites}

Section~\ref{sec:holography} showed that the bare beat $U$ over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ leaves static stabilizer
protection immediately because its line-local layers inject non-Clifford magic. That shifts the focus directly to
many-body dynamics, testing what bound matter, entangling computation, and relativistic kinematics this single fixed
unitary rule generates on $\{3,4,3,4\}$.

Reversible discrete cellular automata almost always fail matter in one of two opposite ways. Linear or weakly coupled
ballistic rules disperse every multi-body packet into featureless spreading waves with no persistent composites, while
classical glider and railway automata localize structures only by freezing deterministic tracks that lack quantum
superposition, entangling gates, and Lorentz kinematics \cite{arrighi2019, farrelly2020}. Bare $U$ cuts sharply across
both failure modes, establishing five distinct dynamical regimes: an unconditional no-go theorem forbidding
finite-support breathers, hyperbolic spectral excision turning isotropic pair poles into Feshbach resonances, an exact
integrable collinear bound-state tower, non-integrable chaos alongside exact weak autonomous BQP universality and four
unbounded-loop barriers, and a clean separation between cutoff-heavy bare clocks and the relativistic light-mass limit.

\subsection{Ballistic transport}

Negative curvature prevents any finite-support lump of bare-$U$ amplitude from freezing in place and dissolves flat-space
isotropic pair bound states into finite-lifetime resonances. On a flat lattice, carefully balanced interference can
sometimes trap strictly finite-support periodic structures (\emph{compactons}). On $\{3,4,3,4\}$, hyperbolic branching
eliminates that mechanism unconditionally: every finite excitation reaches an outer frontier dock whose outward facets
outnumber its inward facets by at least seven to one. Whenever the local half-coin splits amplitude across an antipodal
line, a strictly positive probability escapes into untouched shells on every beat, ruling out finite-support recurrence
across all particle numbers. The exact Lorentz height-convexity and Pell no-periodicity core is machine-checked in
Lean~4, and the multi-body Fock instantiation is verified deterministically:

\begin{theorem}[Unconditional Compacton No-Go on $\{3,4,3,4\}$]
\label{thm:compacton-nogo}
Under the bare automaton $U$, zero nontrivial finite-support periodic eigenstates $U^T |\psi\rangle = \lambda
|\psi\rangle$ ($|\psi\rangle \neq |V_1\rangle$) exist for any excitation count $k \ge 1$ and any integer period
$T \ge 1$.
\end{theorem}

The proof rests on two exact algebraic identities. First, for every dock $g$ and antipodal root pair $(a, -a)$, the
even-integer Lorentz height $H_2(g) = -2 B(g c_0, c_0) \in 2\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}$ obeys universal midpoint convexity:
\begin{equation}
H_2(g k_a) + H_2(g k_{-a}) = 6\, H_2(g).
\label{eq:height-convexity}
\end{equation}
No frontier dock $g_*$ attaining the instantaneous maximum height $M(t)$ can therefore possess an antipodal inward pair,
bounding its inward degree by $|\mathrm{In}_H(g_*)| \le 3$ and leaving at least $21$ outward facets. Second, any
$m$-particle state arriving on an antipodal-free subset of facets exits strictly outward with exact Gram-diagonalized
probability $P_{\mathrm{out}} = 1 - (3/4)^m > 0$ ($0.25$ through $0.6836$ for $m = 1\text{--}4$, including active chiral
quads). After at most one turnaround beat, the wavefront expands ballistically according to the Pell recurrence
\begin{equation}
M(t + 1) \ge 6\, M(t) - M(t - 1),
\label{eq:pell-frontier}
\end{equation}
forbidding finite-support breathers unconditionally. Physically, any stable composite of bare $U$ must therefore be an
extended square-integrable ($l^2$) state carrying infinite exponential tails rather than a hard-edged classical blob.

Relaxing finite support to $l^2$ wavefunctions reveals a second consequence of curvature, sharply separating isotropic
pair mixing $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ from collinear contact scattering $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$. On the identical-tone
two-body sector, bare $U$ factorizes as $U_{\mathrm{dec}} \circ M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$, where $U_{\mathrm{dec}}$
is the decoupled line-local beat without $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$. Denoting by $K(z)$ the free two-body resolvent of
$U_{\mathrm{dec}}$ projected onto identical-pair states reduces isotropic bound-state existence on $\{3,4,3,4\}$ to a
scalar Birman--Schwinger eigenvalue equation on $l^2(V(\{3,4,3,4\}))$:
\begin{equation}
\bigl(I - (\zeta_{18} - 1)\, K(z)\bigr) f = 0.
\label{eq:birman-schwinger}
\end{equation}
On the flat four-dimensional $D_4$ lattice, diffusive walks return frequently enough that this contact attraction binds a
true isotropic two-body state at $E_{\mathrm{flat}}(0) = 1.7706\text{ rad}$. On curved $\{3,4,3,4\}$,
Section~\ref{sec:spectral}'s spectral gap ($\rho/24 \le 0.4772$ already at Depth~4) compresses the free propagation band
so tightly that Parseval estimates across the momentum gaps and outer wings rigorously \textbf{excise $83.12\%$ of the
spectral circle $S^1$}, absorbing the flat bound pocket completely (Table~\ref{tab:scattering-gates}).

Exact radius-3 Dyson--Volterra evolution ($8{,}857$ docks) and the infinite Bethe cavity tree $\mathbb{Z}^{*12}$ covering
the unexcised arcs determine the physical fate of that dissolved bound state. Rather than vanishing without trace,
hyperbolic curvature converts the flat pole into a finite-lifetime \textbf{Feshbach resonance} (a metastable quasi-bound
state embedded in the scattering continuum with lifetime $\tau = 12.73$ beats) paired with a destructive-interference Fano
antiresonance zero. Exact infinite-volume $16 \times 16$ resolvents across all four chiral four-line classes ($k^* = 4$)
likewise certify zero $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ bound states, though resonant interference still generates strong transient
chiral trapping and mirror asymmetry (Table~\ref{tab:scattering-gates}).

\subsection{Bound states}

Exact $l^2$ bound states survive intact inside bare $U$ along any single straight antipodal geodesic line of
$\{3,4,3,4\}$, forming a closed-form tower of Bethe $k$-strings with exact cyclotomic wavefunctions. The protection
mechanism is purely algebraic: when same-sign excitations traveling along one straight line carry pairwise-distinct
internal roles from the nine-point alphabet $\{0, \dots, 8\}$, both $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ and $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ act
as the exact identity. Cross-line diffraction shuts off altogether, leaving excitations free to bind purely through
collinear contact scattering $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$.

In this collinear sector, bare $U$ becomes exact Yang--Baxter integrable \cite{yang1967, baxter1972}: multi-body
scattering factorizes into pairwise collisions obeying the braid relation (with $6 \times 6$ braid defect
$5.70 \times 10^{-16}$ and three-body Bethe quantization error $1.78 \times 10^{-15}$). Its rational XXX rapidity and
universal Bethe $k$-string pole law (a $k$-particle bound complex whose quasimomenta align vertically at $u = -i h$,
$h \in \mathbb{Z}/2$) take compact closed forms:
\begin{equation}
u(p, \alpha) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\,\frac{\sin E_\alpha(p)}{\sin p},
\qquad
z(h) = e^{2 i p(h)} = \frac{1 - 3h + 3h^2}{1 + 3h + 3h^2}.
\label{eq:bethe-pole-law}
\end{equation}
Every symmetric $P = 0$ composite bound state on $\{3,4,3,4\}$ is an exact Bethe $k$-string of
Equation~\eqref{eq:bethe-pole-law}, realizable verbatim in four dimensions up to $k \le 9$ via the nine distinct internal
roles (and forbidden for $k \ge 4$ in any three-role truncation because pigeonhole collisions reactivate
$M_{\mathrm{pair}}$).

Each species in this tower lives over an explicit cyclotomic ring without floating-point approximation: the Lean-proved
$k = 2$ doublon lies in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/13]$, the Lean-proved $k = 3$ trion lies in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/7]$, and
Equation~\eqref{eq:bethe-pole-law} resolves the four-body tetron bound state in the continuum (BIC) via one exact
eigenstate in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/49]$. Parametrized by ordered even-sublattice separations $d_1, d_2, d_3 \ge 0$
(hard-core exclusion $d_1 + d_2 \ge 1$, $d_2 + d_3 \ge 1$, contact spins $(s_j, s_{j+1}) = (0, 1)$ when $d_j = 0$) and
orientations $s = (s_1, s_2, s_3, s_4) \in \{0, 1\}^4$, the tetron satisfies zero defects across $1{,}220$ exact integer
stencils covering all five contact regimes:
\begin{gather}

three-dimensional husk-confined particles.

Boosting any collinear $k$-string across the open Brillouin zone $P \in (-\pi, \pi)$ produces a unified closed-form
dispersion relation:
\begin{equation}

+ \arcsin\!\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3k^2+1}}\right)

across all momenta.

\subsection{Scattering gates}

Integrability is strictly confined to the same-sign distinct-role geodesic sector. Mixing opposite tones
($K + M_{\mathrm{meet}}$) or mixing identical and distinct roles ($M_{\mathrm{pair}} + M_{\mathrm{meet}}$) breaks

with the Circular Orthogonal Ensemble (COE, $\langle r \rangle = 0.5164$ across $336$ gaps, against Poisson $0.3863$ and
asymptotic COE $0.5307$, with finite-size scaling to larger $L$ left to future work). In four dimensions, exact
conservation of per-line parity $N_a \bmod 2$ keeps $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ and $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ mutually exclusive at
$k = 4$, forcing their first nonlinear four-dimensional coexistence at $k = 5 = 2 + 1 + 1 + 1$ (certified in exact
integer arithmetic across $3{,}072$ branches).

Inside the clean collinear sector, uninterrupted bare-$U$ collisions execute native entangling logic and multi-stage
ballistic memory transfer with zero external time-dependent controls (Tables~\ref{tab:scattering-gates}
and~\ref{tab:universality-obstructions}). Colliding a tuned momentum-$p_*$ pulse with a stationary doublon on
$\mathrm{Sym}^3$ imparts an approximate controlled-$\pi$ phase with only $0.23\%$ breakup. Allowing that pulse to

$2\pi$ with doubled Wigner delay and only $0.31\%$ cumulative breakup, while a spatially separated five-body two-station
collision reproduces the additive phase truth table to $0.034\pi$.

Removing $\mathrm{Sym}^k$ pre-symmetrization entirely in favor of pairwise-distinct roles $\{0, 1, 2\}$ guarantees
$M_{\mathrm{pair}} = I$ verbatim in four dimensions. For separable inputs $|\{0,1\}_{\mathrm{Sym}}\rangle \otimes |2\rangle$,
exact Yang fusion implements a lossless partial role-SWAP with a $+\pi/2$ elastic phase ($P_{\mathrm{el}} = 1/3$,

\subsection{Quantum universality}

These scattering primitives settle a deeper computational question: bare $U$ is neither a toy integrable wave equation nor
a classical railway Turing machine. Strictly under its own scattering dynamics, bare $U$ achieves exact, leakage-free,
spin-independent universal quantum gate synthesis (\emph{weak autonomous BQP universality}) inside verbatim four
dimensions, while four exact algebraic theorems obstruct finite-seed unbounded-loop computation.

\textbf{Exact 4D leakage elimination and dense single-qubit control:} In any two-role exchange encoding, identical roles
meeting at a dock trigger $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ and leak amplitude across all $11$ partner lines. Embedding two
or three three-particle decoherence-free-subspace (DFS) qubits in six ($\{0, \dots, 5\}$) or all nine ($\{0, \dots, 8\}$)
pairwise-distinct internal roles closes that loophole completely: exact arbitrary-precision four-dimensional evolution
confirms $M_{\mathrm{pair}} = I$ identically, with zero off-line branches and exact reversibility
(Table~\ref{tab:universality-obstructions}). Within a single three-spin DFS qubit, the bare on-dock exchange operators
$\langle M_{01}, M_{12}\rangle$ satisfy $|\operatorname{Tr}_{SU(2)}(M_{01} M_{12})| = 5/4$ (Niven-irrational rotation
angle, topologically dense in $SU(2)$) and $M_{ij}^2 = -M_{ij}^\dagger$, so every inverse word requires only positive
forward beats. Continuous-rapidity Yang scattering $E_{pq}(\alpha) = R_{pq}(\cot(\pi\alpha/2))$ synthesizes exact $T$
and $T^\dagger$ in $1$ collision and Hadamard $H$ in $3$ collisions, while in the separate single-excitation slot-qubit
encoding a three-full-line chiral catalyst $L_7$ yields $|\operatorname{Tr}(L_7 C)|^2 = 25/16$, giving an independent
dense-$SU(2)$ proof.

\textbf{Spin-independent 5-spin CZ/CNOT and full 126-dimensional Toffoli:} On two DFS qubits, the five adjacent exchange
generators span the full 80-dimensional Lie algebra $\mathfrak{su}(9)$ on the $S_{\mathrm{tot}} = 1$ sector, and a
60-collision closed-form singlet-DFS CZ prepares a Bell state saturating Concurrence $C = 1$ and the Tsirelson
Bell--CHSH bound $2\sqrt{2}$ \cite{clauser1969}. However, direct projection reveals that this singlet-scoped CZ leaks
$10.6\%$ on the triplet pair-spin sector $S_{AB} = 1$, which every register of $n \ge 3$ qubits necessarily populates.
True multi-qubit universality therefore requires a spin-independent two-qubit gate acting identically on both $S_{AB} = 0$
and $S_{AB} = 1$ with matching relative global phase.

We construct that gate analytically on active spins $(1, 2, 3, 4, 5)$ (leaving the control qubit's gauge spin $0$
untouched). On four spins $(2, 3, 4, 5)$, the five-collision sequence using only SWAP ($w = 0$) and bare on-dock
$M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ ($\alpha = \pm 2/3$, $w = \pm 1/\sqrt{3}$),
\begin{equation}

\label{eq:leakage-clear}
\end{equation}
cancels the $S_{2345} = 1$ Racah amplitude ratio $-1/8$ identically and clears the $S_{345} = 3/2$ leakage state
strictly into the logical qubit subspace. Conjugating $P_{45}$ by $W_0$ and a two-pulse alignment rotation builds a
15-collision controlled swap $U_4$, and the $S_3$ braid $U_4 P_{12} U_4 P_{12} U_4$ plus local framing yields an exact
53-collision CZ and 54-collision CNOT matching identically across both $S_{AB} = 0$ and $S_{AB} = 1$
($\|U(0) - U(1)\|_F \le 7.43 \times 10^{-16}$, zero leakage to machine precision,
Table~\ref{tab:universality-obstructions}).

Executing the standard six-CNOT three-qubit Toffoli circuit directly on the full nine-spin $\binom{9}{4} = 126$-dimensional
Hilbert space ($S_{ABC} = 1/2$, all 16 logical-plus-gauge states across both $S_{AB} = 0, 1$, all nine roles distinct so
$M_{\mathrm{pair}} = I$ in 4D) with explicit adjacent $R(w = 0)$ routing SWAPs requires $373 = 13 + 324 + 36$ strictly
adjacent Yang scattering collisions and satisfies
\begin{equation}
\|U_{16} - U_{\mathrm{Toff}} \otimes I_2\|_F = 8.82 \times 10^{-15},
\qquad
\|U_8(0) - U_8(1)\|_F = 2.81 \times 10^{-15},
\label{eq:toffoli-cert}
\end{equation}
with machine-zero leakage and cross-gauge mixing across both sectors (Table~\ref{tab:universality-obstructions}). In the
same three-spin DFS encoding, replacing all single-qubit gates and all framing Hadamards inside every CNOT with discrete
fixed-angle bare $\langle M_{01}, M_{12}\rangle$ words yields a $\mathbb{C}^{126}$ Toffoli at process fidelity $0.997907$
with machine-zero leakage.

\textbf{Four exact obstructions to finite-seed unbounded-loop computation:} On the underlying $\{3,4,3,4\}$ graph, a
dedicated classical railway cellular automaton achieves strong Turing universality starting from a finite self-extending
seed \cite{pollard2026railway}. By contrast, four exact algebraic barriers prove why verbatim bare quantum $U$ cannot
implement that finite-seed static-track railway architecture directly (Table~\ref{tab:universality-obstructions}, bottom).

\textbf{No finite-support static tracks or mirrors:} By Theorem~\ref{thm:compacton-nogo}, every finite-support core
expands along the Pell--Chebyshev frontier sequence and loses at least $1 - (3/4)^m \ge 1/4$ of its outer norm on every
beat. No static walls, mirrors, or localized tracks can persist inside bare $U$, and colliding opposite-sign excitations
through $K$ breaks doublons with $64.21\%$ probability rather than reflecting them elastically.

\textbf{Single-excitation parity lock:} Exact $\mathbb{Z}_2^{24}$ per-line tone-parity conservation (proved across all
particle counts in Section~\ref{sec:gauge}) locks every odd-parity single excitation permanently onto its initial
geodesic line with zero off-line branches, while distinct-role $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ lines cross intersecting $D_4$ axes
with exact zero coupling.

\textbf{Balanced neutral-pair annihilation:} Encoding logical trits in neutral antipodal pairs
$\{(-1,+1), (0,0), (+1,-1)\}$ fails under activity conservation, ballistic $S_K = -1$ dispersal, and the exact
$(-I, \mathcal{C})$ Annihilation Theorem forcing $\chi_{\mathrm{odd}} \equiv 0$ on every $m$-rail junction, which
decouples all $264$ two-rail and $1{,}760$ three-rail junctions for all time.

\textbf{Isotropic diffraction ceiling:} Even for even-parity pairs permitted to change lines, $W(F_4)$-isotropic

\subsection{Moving clocks}

Equation~\eqref{eq:universal-dispersion} separates lattice-cutoff kinematics sharply from the relativistic continuum
limit. At the bare automaton's heavy coin mass $m = \pi/3$, every $k$-body composite lies deep in the cutoff regime:
rest inertia remains strictly additive ($m_{k,\mathrm{inert}} = k\sqrt{3}$) despite $76.84\%$ quasienergy binding at
$k = 2$, yielding species-dependent top speeds $v_{k,\max} = 1/\sqrt{3k^2+1}$ and a large Lorentz remainder ratio
$R_2 = m_{2,\mathrm{inert}} / \Omega_2(0) = 7.1406 \neq 1$. Bare composites therefore move slower than light and do not
obey Einstein's mass-energy equivalence at the bare cutoff scale.

The underlying contact interaction $4 n_+ n_-$ inside $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$, however, is mathematically a discrete-time
Floquet version of the $(1+1)$-dimensional massive Thirring model (a relativistic Dirac fermion with local four-fermion
contact coupling) \cite{thirring1958}. Deforming bare $U$ by holding $\phi_{\mathrm{meet}} = 2\pi/3$ fixed while lowering
the coin mass $m \to 0$ restores continuum relativity quantitatively: by $m = 0.025$, quasienergy binding and
inertial-mass defect converge together toward the exact $50\%$ continuum limit ($E = mc^2$), driving $R_2$,
$c_{\mathrm{eff}}$, and $\rho_2$ toward unity (Table~\ref{tab:clocks-warp}).

This inertia-remainder mechanism also explains a subtle discrete-clock phenomenon: boosted composite clocks on a lattice
can actually speed up instead of slowing down whenever ground and excited states sit at mismatched cutoff remainders. For
any two-level composite with rest masses $M_g, M_e$ ($\Delta M = M_e - M_g$), rest inertias
$m_{g,\mathrm{inert}}, m_{e,\mathrm{inert}}$ ($\bar{m}_{\mathrm{inert}} = (m_{g,\mathrm{inert}} +
m_{e,\mathrm{inert}})/2$), mean group speed $v_{\mathrm{avg}}$, and inertia remainders $R_j = m_{j,\mathrm{inert}}/M_j$,
the low-velocity clock dilation coefficient obeys an exact algebraic limit:
\begin{equation}
D_{\mathrm{clock}}
\equiv \lim_{P \to 0}\frac{\omega(P) - \omega(0)}{-\frac{1}{2} v_{\mathrm{avg}}^2 \omega(0)}
= \frac{m_{g,\mathrm{inert}} m_{e,\mathrm{inert}}}{\bar{m}_{\mathrm{inert}}^2}\, R_e
\left(1 + \frac{M_g}{\Delta M}\frac{R_e - R_g}{R_e}\right).
\label{eq:anti-dilation-law}
\end{equation}
Whenever $R_g > R_e$ and $(M_g/\Delta M)|(R_e - R_g)/R_e| > 1$, the dilation factor flips negative and the moving clock
accelerates, confirmed live on a parameter-deformed $m = 0.72$ contact-knot automaton (Table~\ref{tab:clocks-warp}).
