\label{sec:composites}

\subsection{Unconditional compacton no-go and hyperbolic spectral-gap excision}

\begin{theorem}[Unconditional Compacton No-Go on $\{3,4,3,4\}$, \code{BallisticDoublon.lean}]
\label{thm:compacton-nogo}

|\psi\rangle$ ($|\psi\rangle \neq |V_1\rangle$) exist for any excitation count $k \ge 1$ and any integer period $T \ge 1$.
\end{theorem}

The proof rests on two machine-checked identities (Tier~A). First, for every dock $g$ and antipodal root pair $(a, -a)$,
the exact even-integer Lorentz height $H_2(g) = -2 B(g c_0, c_0) \in 2\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}$ obeys the universal convexity
identity $H_2(g k_a) + H_2(g k_{-a}) = 6 H_2(g)$ (zero defects across $106{,}284$ dock-line pairs on $B_3$, proved
symbolically for all words in \code{BallisticDoublon.lean}). Consequently, every frontier dock $g_*$ attaining maximum
height $M(t)$ has zero antipodal inward pairs ($|\mathrm{In}_H(g_*)| \le 3$, leaving $\ge 21$ outward facets). Second,
arriving frontier excitations occupy an antipodal-free subset where $m$-particle on-site states exit strictly outward
with exact Gram-diagonalized probability $P_{\mathrm{out}} = 1 - (3/4)^m > 0$ ($0.25, 0.4375, 0.578125, 0.683594$ for
$m = 1\text{--}4$, including $12/16$ chiral $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$-active quads). After at most a finite turnaround,
the wavefront expands ballistically via the Pell recurrence $M(t+1) \ge 6 M(t) - M(t-1)$, forbidding finite-support
recurrence unconditionally.

Relaxing finite support to $l^2$ wavefunctions exposes a sharp divergence between $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ and
$M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ (Wave-3 Track~W9, Tier~A). On the collinear 2-body identical-tone sector,
$U|_{\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{col}}} = U_{\mathrm{dec}} \circ M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ reduces the infinite-volume
4D problem to a scalar Birman--Schwinger equation $(I - (\zeta_{18} - 1) K(z)) f = 0$ on $l^2(V(\{3,4,3,4\}))$.
Combining Parseval bounds in the all-$P$ gaps $(0, \pi/3) \cup (-\pi, -2\pi/3)$ ($\sup \hat{t} = 0.5763 \ll
\cot(\pi/18) = 5.6713$) and outer wings ($\sup \hat{t} = 1.0025 \ll 5.6713$) with our Challenge~1 bound

classes ($k^* = 4$), exact infinite-volume $16 \times 16$ Birman--Schwinger resolvents give

\subsection{Quantum chaos, collinear scattering gates, and neutral-pair no-go (Track~W12)}

Bare $U$ is \emph{not} globally integrable. Mixing opposite tones ($K + M_{\mathrm{meet}}$, $6 \times 6$ YBE defect

$011000001000$, $96$ $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ diffs at $t = 1$ and $16$ $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ diffs at $t = 2$ across

For native bare-$U$ computation (Tier~A), colliding a pulse of momentum $p_* = -\arctan(6\sqrt{3}/5)$ with a $P = 0$

\subsection{Light-mass Thirring $E = mc^2$, moving clocks, and warp null audit (Track~W18)}
