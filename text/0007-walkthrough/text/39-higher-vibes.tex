\section{Higher Vibes: Minds Made of Minds}

We have built the physical world from the crystal. Now we turn to the inner world, the mind. The bridge is one idea: recursion. The same structure that gives a single vibe repeats at higher and higher levels, so that minds are made of minds, all the way up. This chapter introduces the higher vibe and, crucially, what it is not.

\subsection{A crowd that acts as one}

Take a tightly knit cluster of vibes, all closely connected, settling into a shared, stable pattern, moving together, holding together. Step back, blur your eyes, and that whole cluster can be read as a single unit, a higher vibe. It has its own overall tone (the majority lean of its members), it relates to other clusters as a unit, and it behaves, at its own scale, like one vibe writ large.

This is how minds and beings enter the theory. A cell is a higher vibe made of molecules. You are a higher vibe made of cells. A single base vibe is a quiver of feeling. A higher vibe is an organized crowd of them that has become a coherent something. Mind is not a different kind of stuff added on top. It is vibes, gathered.

\subsection{The crucial constraint: no new layer}

Here is the point that keeps the theory honest, and it is subtle. A higher vibe does not have its own separate, stored tone sitting somewhere. There is no extra memory bank where the cluster's ``mood'' is written down. The higher vibe's tone is just the aggregate of its members' tones, the overall lean of the crowd, read off on demand. It is a summary, not a new thing.

Why insist on this? Because the theory has exactly one kind of stuff, the base vibe, and one place things are stored, the base tones. If higher vibes had their own stored states, we would be sneaking in a second layer of reality, and the strict one-substance claim would break. So the rule is firm: it is ternary feeling all the way down, and the higher levels are views of that one base, generalized into stable patterns, never stored separately. A mind is what a vast crowd of base vibes looks like when you read it coarsely. It is real, but it is not extra.

\subsection{The same law at every level}

Remarkably, the higher level does not just look like the lower level. It obeys the same kind of rule. When you coarsen the crystal along its genuine coherent clusters, the higher vibes update one another by the same signed-majority logic that governs base vibes. The law is self-similar: the same shape of rule at every scale.

This is not automatic, and there is a precise condition for it. It holds when you coarsen along the system's real coherent regions, not arbitrary blocks, and when you keep track of the true connection strengths. Carve the crystal into meaningless chunks and the neat higher-level law does not appear. Carve it along the actual integrated wholes and it does. The simulation confirms this sharply: along real wholes the higher rule holds, along arbitrary blocks it does not. So there is a right way to see the higher level, and along that way the universe looks the same at every scale, vibes of vibes, governed by one rule repeated.

\subsection{Where this is going}

This recursion is the engine of the whole inner world. The next chapters cash it out: what exactly makes a crowd cohere into a genuine self (integration), how selves stack into a tower from cell to body, how the variety of experience lives in the patterns, and how freedom, life, and dreams all sit on this recursive structure. But the core idea is here: minds are higher vibes, higher vibes are organized crowds of base vibes read as units, nothing is stored at the higher level, and the same law runs at every scale.

\textbf{Takeaway:} a higher vibe is a coherent crowd of base vibes read as a single unit, which is how minds and beings enter the theory. It has no separately stored tone, only the aggregate of its members, so it is a summary not a new layer, keeping the one-substance claim intact. Along the real coherent wholes, the same signed-majority law holds at every scale.
