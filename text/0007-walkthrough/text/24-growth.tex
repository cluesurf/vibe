\section{Growth: The Mesh That Builds Itself}

The crystal is not a finished object that the rule runs on. It is growing, all the time, adding new vibes at its frontier, forever. Growth is as fundamental as the rule. This chapter is about how the crystal builds itself, where new vibes go, and why this is not the paradox it first seems.

\subsection{New vibes appear at the edge}

At the leading edge of the crystal, new cells are continually added. Each new vibe comes into being attached, by notes, to some of the existing frontier vibes. It does not appear in the middle. It appears at the rim, the present, joining on to what is already there. The deep interior is never touched. All the building happens at the edge.

This is the same one-way growth that gave us the arrow of time. The crystal only ever gets bigger. Cells are added, never removed. Notes accumulate, never undone. The past is the frozen interior. The present is the rim where new cells are being tied on. The future is the room, always available in hyperbolic space, for the next cells to come.

\subsection{Where does a new vibe go?}

Be exact here. The question ``where does a new vibe go?'' assumes there is a pre-existing space with empty slots waiting to be filled. There is not. Remember, space is not a container. It is the pattern of connections. A vibe has no coordinates of its own. Its only ``where'' is what it is connected to.

So a new vibe does not go anywhere. It comes into being already attached, by notes, to some existing vibes, and those attachments are its entire location. It is a new knot tied onto the web, and its position simply is which threads it is tied to. Asking where it went before it was tied on is like asking where a new friendship is located before the two people met. There is no ``where'' apart from the connection itself.

\subsection{Self-generation is not magic}

``Vibes producing more vibes'' can sound like getting something from nothing. It is not. It is ordinary recursion, the most familiar thing in mathematics. The rule ``add one'' applied to the number one generates all the counting numbers, endlessly, from a tiny rule and a seed. Nobody thinks that is magic or a free lunch. The crystal grows the same way: a fixed rule, applied to a seed, unfolds an unlimited structure, with no new information injected and nothing left to chance. The whole infinite crystal is implied by the seed and the rule, the way all the integers are implied by one and ``add one.''

\subsection{Deterministic and append-only}

Two features of the growth matter for later.

It is deterministic. There is no randomness in how the crystal grows. Given the rule and the seed, the crystal grows in exactly one way. Run it again and you get the identical crystal. This is why the whole model is reproducible: nothing is rolled at any step.

And it is append-only. Once a cell is added and its notes are set, they never change. The interior is frozen. Growth only ever adds. It never edits or deletes. This is what makes the past permanent and the structure a faithful record of its own history, and it is why the model can keep growing forever without ever rebuilding what came before.

\subsection{The picture to hold}

So the final image of the substrate is not a static crystal but a living one. Picture the Poincare disk again, with new rings of cells blossoming at the ever-receding rim, beat after beat, the inside fixed and the edge alive, the whole thing self-similar so that every new patch looks like the old ones, growing a branching tree from a single seed, forever. That growing crystal, run by the signed-majority rule, is the complete machine. Everything in the rest of the walkthrough is a pattern this machine makes.

\subsection{What governs the growing}

What decides how many new cells a frontier vibe spawns, and where they attach? Not a separate, arbitrary growth law. The same fixed structure that defines the crystal, the kaleidoscope rule from a few integers, also dictates exactly how each cell branches into the next, how many children it has and how they connect. Growth is that tiling structure unfolding step by step, deterministic and fixed, with no choices made along the way. So there are really two faces of one rule: how tones update on the web, and how the web itself extends, and both are set in advance with nothing left to chance.

\textbf{Takeaway:} the crystal grows forever by adding new vibes at its frontier, each born already tied by notes to the existing edge, so a new vibe does not go anywhere, its connections are its location. The growth is ordinary recursion from a seed, fully deterministic and append-only, with the interior frozen as the past and the rim alive as the present.
