\section{The Rule: Signed Majority}

Everything so far has been setup: the crystal, the vibes, the tones, the notes, the fills. Now comes the law that makes it move, the single rule by which a vibe updates itself. It is as plain as everything else, and it is the engine of the entire universe in this theory.

\subsection{The rule in words}

At each moment, a vibe updates its tone by taking a kind of weighted vote among its neighbors. Here is the whole procedure:

1. Look at each neighbor you experience. 2. Take that neighbor's tone (its outward will, plus one, zero, or minus one). 3. Multiply it by the fill of the note connecting you (also plus one, zero, or minus one): this says whether that neighbor reinforces you, opposes you, or is ignored. 4. Add up all those results into a single running total. 5. Set your new tone to the sign of that total. A positive total becomes pleasure. A negative total becomes pain. A tie, exactly zero, becomes peace.

That is the entire law of physics in this theory. A vibe asks its neighbors how they lean, weighs each by how that neighbor counts for it, adds it up, and takes the sign. Then everyone does it again.

\subsection{A worked feel}

Suppose a vibe has four neighbors. Two of them are at pleasure, with reinforcing fills, so they each contribute plus one. One neighbor is at pain with a reinforcing fill, contributing minus one. One neighbor is at pleasure but with an opposing fill, which flips it, contributing minus one. Add them: plus one, plus one, minus one, minus one, total zero. A tie. So this vibe settles to peace next moment.

Change one neighbor and the total tips, and the vibe goes to pleasure or pain instead. That is all there is to it. It is a tug of war, and the vibe's next state is just which side won, or a draw.

\subsection{What the rule does not have}

The power of the rule is as much in what it leaves out as in what it includes.

There are no fractional weights. Everything is plus one, zero, or minus one. The running total is a plain whole number that exists only for the instant of the vote and is then forgotten. No smooth real numbers live anywhere in the machinery. This is the discreteness the theory insists on: the world is built from counting, not from continuous dials.

There is no hidden state. A vibe is its tone and the notes it stands in, and nothing else lurking behind the scenes. It does not secretly remember, or store extra variables. Everything about it is on the surface. Memory and history, when they appear, will be patterns spread across many vibes, never hidden inside one.

There is no global clock dictating one universal ``now.'' Vibes update locally, in their own neighborhoods, without a master timekeeper, for a reason we will see in the chapter on time: a universal now would secretly pick a preferred frame, which the theory forbids.

\subsection{Why this rule, and not another}

The rule was not hand-picked to produce a desired outcome. It is close to the simplest local rule that respects the theory's commitments: use only neighbors, use only the three-valued tones and fills, use a momentary whole-number tally, and turn that tally back into a tone in the plainest way, by its sign. It is the three-valued cousin of rules physicists already use for magnets and for memory networks. Given the ingredients, it almost writes itself.

And recall from Part II that such a rule, applied across a vast structure, is universal. This humble sign-of-the-vote law, repeated over the crystal an unimaginable number of times, can in principle produce any pattern at all. The simplicity of the law is not a weakness. It is the whole point. The complexity of the world will come from the patterns, not from the rule.

\subsection{One at a time, not all at once}

Do all the vibes update together in a single global tick, or each on its own? Each on its own, locally, without a global lockstep. A single universe-wide update would need a master clock marking one shared instant for everything, and that shared instant would be a preferred frame, exactly what relativity forbids. So updates happen here and there, in their own local order, with no cosmic now synchronizing them. This is not a detail. It is the same reason there is no universal clock, and it is what keeps the rule consistent with relativity even though it is built from simple local steps.

\textbf{Takeaway:} the one law is signed majority. A vibe weighs each neighbor's tone by the fill of the connecting note, adds them into a whole-number tally, and takes the sign as its next tone. No fractions, no hidden state, no global clock. This simple, universal rule, run over the crystal, is the engine of everything.
