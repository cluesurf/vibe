\section{The Tone}

A distinction has three parts, not two. There is the marked side, the unmarked side, and the boundary between them from which both sides are cut. The boundary is real, because without it nothing separates the two sides.

So the smallest carrier of a distinction is a three-valued quantity. Write its values minus one, zero, and plus one, with zero the boundary or vacuum and the outer two the mirrored sides. This three-valued carrier is the \textbf{tone}, the one property the substance has, and in the fullest reading its three values are felt as pain, peace, and pleasure, though the physics needs only that there are three, ordered and mirrored.

A short test forces exactly three. Call an alphabet admissible if it has both a vacuum, a resting not-yet-marked value, and a sign flip that moves at least one element, so the two sides are mirror images. The set of zero and one has a vacuum but no mirror for the one, and fails. The set of minus one and plus one has a mirror but no vacuum, and fails. The set of minus one, zero, and plus one has both, and is the smallest that does. Any larger admissible set works but is not minimal. So the ternary tone is the unique minimal carrier of a distinction. This is derived.

One distinction does two jobs at once, and keeping them apart matters for everything that follows. The splitting itself, the edge, the holding of one place apart from another, is the seed of structure, the grid that will become the mesh. The value, which side you are on, is the tone that sits at one spot in that grid. Grid from the edge, tone from the value. The where and the what are different, born together from the one distinction, and the model never lets them blur.

Keep one more fact in hand. Under the sign flip the vacuum is fixed and the outer pair swaps, so the symmetry of the tone is the two-element group, and the three values partition as one plus two, a lone vacuum and a mirrored pair. Much later a different three appears, the three families of matter, with the full symmetric group on three peers. These are different threes, at different layers, and the model verifies that no symmetry maps one onto the other.
