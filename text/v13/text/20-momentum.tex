\section{Momentum}

An uncollided tone is handed one dock onward each beat along its site, forever, its value never spreading out. This
straight, undispersed passing is exactly momentum. A tone's momentum is simply which of the twenty-four sites it
occupies, and the total momentum of a dock is the integer vector sum over its sites of the tone times the direction. So momentum is not added to
the model, it is what ballistic streaming is, and it is an exact integer quantity like everything else at the base.

The free streaming sets the maximum speed, the light cone. A free tone advances exactly one dock per beat, so the causal
front advances at the beat count, which is the hard speed limit, and a diffusing thing crawls only as the square root of
the beat count, far slower. The full interacting rule respects this bound, a perturbation never spreads faster than one
cell per beat, the locality of the rule made into a light cone. So nothing outruns one cell per tick, because that is all
the rule does in a tick, and a free signal goes exactly that fast.

The bare single-charge rule alone gives diffusive modes, not
the sharp ballistic mode a massless relativistic particle needs, and a perturbation of the busy vacuum is reabsorbed
rather than propagating. The sharp propagating massless mode is the free-streaming tone, and a fully interacting
propagating light-cone mode needs the second conserved quantity, the momentum current alongside the charge, which is a
named open frontier. The light cone itself, the maximum speed, is sharp and forced. The fully interacting massless
propagator is the part still open.
