\section{Free}

The model forces the architecture of physics and leaves the absolute values free, exactly the freedom the
Standard Model already has. This section states which side of the line each thing falls on, and why the free side is a feature rather than a
failure.

What is forced. The ternary tone and the octonions. Four-dimensional spacetime and the 24-cell and the committed
mesh. Three spatial dimensions. The unique local law. One generation's particle content, with the color
multiplicities one, three, three, one and the exact fractional charges. The gauge group and the Higgs as a single
doublet. The complete anomaly cancellation. The geometric shape of the mass hierarchy, the Cabibbo angle to a percent,
and the trimaximal neutrino mixing pattern. Emergent Lorentz invariance, gravity at the effective level, the Born rule,
the Tsirelson bound, holography, and the continuum limit. This is the architecture of physics, the part that always
looked arbitrary, and it is forced from one premise.

What is free. The absolute masses, equivalently the Yukawa couplings, equivalently the depths each particle sits at. The
absolute gauge coupling, the fine-structure constant, which runs, so the geometry fixes its ratio and its running but not
its one bare normalization. The absolute mixing angles and CP phases beyond the patterns. The dark-matter mass and
abundance. These are exactly the roughly nineteen free parameters of the Standard Model, and the framework does not
remove them, it identifies each with a specific geometric origin, a depth, a phase, a modulus, and shows why it is free.

Two famous problems shrink rather than survive. The hierarchy problem, the ten-thousand-trillion ratio between the
Planck and electroweak scales, becomes a small depth count of about thirteen warp shells, so the worst fine-tuning in
physics is reduced to a small finite integer. And the deepest free parameter, the overall scale, is partly the wrong
question: every physical observable is a dimensionless ratio, and an absolute scale in any unit is a choice of ruler, not
a measured fact, so there is no physical free dimensionful parameter at all, only a convention, and the genuine free
parameters are all dimensionless. A direct test of whether the deterministic growth secretly fixes the depths came back
negative, so the freedom is genuine, the same the Standard Model has, confirmed not to be a hidden gap. The model forces
which particles, which forces, which charges, how many families, and the shape of the hierarchy, and gives the
geometric home of every free number.
