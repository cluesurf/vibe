\begin{abstract}
Reality is modeled as a single growing mesh of one substance carrying a ternary value in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$. The base
is a small fixed set of elements. The committed substrate, argued for on selection grounds rather
than assumed, is the four-dimensional hyperbolic honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$, whose cells are 24-cells, whose twenty-four
local directions form the $D_4$ root system, and whose flat ideal boundary is the cubic honeycomb of ordinary
three-dimensional space. One local law collides and streams the values once each beat, reversibly and conserving a
$U(1)$ charge, while the mesh grows at its open edge. From this, checked by direct computation on the exact mesh,
follow spinors and the exact double cover, one full generation of quarks and leptons with the correct fractional
charges and color, the Standard Model gauge group with $\sin^2\theta_W = 3/8$ at unification, the Higgs as a single
doublet with custodial $\rho = 1$, complete anomaly cancellation, the Dirac dispersion and a finite light cone,
emergent Lorentz invariance, a Newtonian potential carrying the Einstein equations at the effective level with light
bending of the correct amount, the Born rule and the Tsirelson bound, holography and black-hole thermodynamics, a
deterministic cosmology with $w = -1$ dark energy, and dark-matter candidates. The same mesh is computationally
universal with a solved address for every cell in logarithmic cost. The absolute masses and couplings are free,
exactly the Standard Model's own free parameters, each given a geometric origin, and the identification of the forced
threefold structure with three generations carrying distinct masses is the single deepest open conjecture. Every
experiment behind these claims was carried out as open research, with the full reproducible source code available
\cite{cluesurf2026repo}. This is exploratory, feasibility-stage work, offered as a direction worth pursuing.
\end{abstract}
