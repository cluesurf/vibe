\section{Mixing}

The generations mix, and the geometry fixes the pattern of the mixing even where it leaves the absolute values free.
The quark sector gives an exact ratio and a structural account of the rest. The lepton sector gives the single sharpest
prediction in the program.

Quark mixing first. The Cabibbo angle, the dominant mixing of the first two families, comes out as the square root of
the down-to-strange mass ratio, about 0.224, matching the measured 0.224 to a percent, because the light generations
carry a left-right symmetry that ties the mixing to the masses. The subdominant angles are then understood, not just
fit. The mixing is set by the left-handed depths while a mass is a left depth times a right depth, so the two are
independent data, and the third-generation angle is large because the heavy top breaks the left-right symmetry that
protects the light generations. The smallest angle's shortfall is the imprint of the CP phase. The values themselves
stay free, the Standard Model's own freedom, but their hierarchy and origin are geometric.

Quark CP violation is present and generic rather than added. The base is exactly CP symmetric, so CP violation is
emergent, and the complex structure it needs is supplied by the cube-root-of-unity phase that triality carries. Three
generations are the precondition for a physical phase, and three are forced, so a nonzero phase is generic, with a
Jarlskog measure of order a few parts in a hundred thousand, the right size. The exact phase is free. The strong force,
by contrast, does not violate CP, the famous puzzle, and here that is natural rather than tuned. The CP-symmetric
reversible base sets the strong angle to zero, and the same real-determinant triality structure that delivers the quark
phase keeps the strong angle zero while doing so, the shape of a Nelson-Barr mechanism rather than a new particle.

The lepton sector is the crown. The cell's twenty-four directions are the binary tetrahedral group, and quotienting it
by its center gives the alternating group on four letters, uniquely: the other order-twenty-four candidates fail, the
symmetric group is not this group, the binary octahedral group is too large, and the order-twenty-seven group does not
divide twenty-four. That alternating group is exactly the canonical neutrino flavor symmetry, and with no extra
generator it forces the trimaximal mixing pattern. The pattern predicts the solar mixing angle as a squared sine of
0.341, about two standard deviations above the measured 0.307, which JUNO measures to half a percent by around 2028, the
sharpest near-term test of the whole framework. The same pattern ties maximal leptonic CP violation to maximal
atmospheric mixing, a phase near minus ninety degrees that the long-baseline experiments test this decade, and predicts
the smallest neutrino angle near the Cabibbo angle over root two, consistent with the measured value. A single combined
shift of the solar angle and the phase is the sharp consistency test: the framework cannot lower the solar angle and
keep maximal CP independently.

The neutrinos are their own antiparticles, Majorana, from the seesaw, which makes neutrinoless double beta decay occur
and is searched for now. The mass ordering is normal, favored structurally because the larger organizing group pairs
the neutrino's Dirac mass with the up-type quarks, so the heaviest neutrino sits with the top-like slot, while the
right-handed scale that fixes it remains free, and JUNO settles the ordering model-independently around 2030.
