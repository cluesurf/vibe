\section{Dark Energy}

The universe expands, and the expansion accelerates, and on this mesh that acceleration is not a mysterious extra
substance but the growth of the mesh itself. New docks are born at the open edge each beat, at the resting value of
peace, pushing the boundary outward, and that steady birth is the expansion, costing no added energy, simply what a
mesh growing at its edge looks like from within. Because the discreteness caps how much tone can pile up, there is no
point of infinite density, no Big-Bang singularity, only the growing edge, so the deterministic genesis is a beginning
without a blowup.

The sharp, testable claim is the kind of dark energy this gives. The expansion is driven by the steady growth, a
constant rate, so the equation of state is exactly minus one, the de Sitter value of a true cosmological constant, NOT an
evolving field whose pressure drifts with time. The evolving, everpresent kind that some discrete approaches predict
comes from the random fluctuations of a sprinkling, and the deterministic substrate has no such fluctuations, so it is
absent here. An experiment shows the deterministic growth gives a constant expansion with a static control at zero net
birth, and the distinction is real: the measured equation of state is currently minus one to a few percent, favoring
this, and the recent hint of an evolving dark energy is a live tension that the next decade's surveys will settle. If the
evolving trend strengthens it disfavors the prediction, and if it relaxes to minus one it confirms it.

The value of the cosmological constant, how large the dark energy is, is the famous fine-tuning problem, and it is the
free dimensionful scale, a choice of ruler rather than a number the model fixes, the same kind of freedom as the absolute
masses. So the model predicts the EQUATION OF STATE, a constant minus one, sharply and falsifiably, while leaving the
VALUE free, and says clearly which is which.
