\section{The Higgs}

The Higgs is the connection along the quaternionic internal direction. Because the quaternions are two complex
dimensions, this connection is a single weak doublet, which is exactly the Standard Model Higgs. So the Higgs is not
added, it is the gauge field of one of the tower's rungs read as a scalar, the background alignment the vacuum settles
into.

The quaternionic structure has a consequence that is measured. It carries an automatic custodial symmetry, which forces
the tree-level relation between the weak boson masses and the weak angle to take the value one. This is a real prediction
with a number in it, and a robust tree-level deviation from it, or the discovery of a second fundamental scalar doublet
that is not a composite, would be a problem for the construction.

Read at the level of feeling, the Higgs is the tone the whole vacuum has chosen, and a particle's mass is the resistance
it meets pushing against that chosen alignment. The deeper a particle sits in the warped throats of the mesh, the more
the warp factor suppresses its scale, which is where the mass hierarchy comes from. The Higgs gives the mechanism, the
geometry gives the shape of the spread, and the absolute masses are the free inputs, the same numbers the Standard Model
leaves free, taken up next.
