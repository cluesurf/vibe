\section{Gravity}

Gravity is the one part of physics the bare law does not hand over simply. What the substrate gives, and what it does
not, can be stated exactly.

What it gives. On the flat physical-space layer, the cusp, a mass digs a shallow potential whose falloff is set by the
dimension, and in three flat dimensions that is one over the distance, the Newtonian law, with a logarithm in two
dimensions, the dimensional fingerprint. This is recovered once the finite-box boundary is receded so it stops
steepening the falloff: the exponent converges to minus one. The curvature of a small wobble of the geometry, built
through the standard pipeline of the connection and the Ricci and Einstein tensors with nothing typed in, gives exactly
two physical massless modes, the two polarizations of the graviton, with pure-gauge wobbles annihilated to machine
precision. And the discrete causal order of the substrate, averaged, reproduces the wave operator, its magnitude
converging to the continuum value as the density grows. Pushed to the level of fields, the geometry obeys the Einstein
equations the way an equation of state does, with the energy crossing a local horizon treated as heat and the books
balanced, so the field equation falls out rather than being laid on by hand. The one sharp observable that tells true
gravity from Newton's, the bending of starlight by exactly twice the naive amount, comes out right because the metric
curves time and space together.

What it does not give, yet. The little potential is effective, not fundamental: its relaxation forgets the past and its
downhill steps throw motion away, so it is an instantaneous Newtonian stand-in rather than a true reversible piece of
the base. A stable, reversible, propagating gravity built at the base, in the full curved four-dimensional bulk, is a
genuine gap, the deepest single open problem in the program.

So gravity is forced at the effective level, the inverse-square law, the spin-2 graviton, the Einstein equations as an
equation of state, the correct light-bending, all reproduced or derived, while the fundamental reversible version
remains open. Intuitively: pour influence out of a mass in three dimensions and it thins as $1/r$, ripples in the shape
of space have exactly two polarizations, and the discrete before-and-after order smoothed out is the wave equation, but
weaving these into one reversible law at the base is the part still unsolved.
