\section{The Base: Five Elements, One State}

Everything in the theory rests on five base elements and a single state variable. Nothing else is ever added. Every higher phenomenon is a composition of these, demonstrated by experiment. If a construction requires a sixth ingredient, that is a finding, not a license. So far none has been required.

\subsection{The one substance and the one state}

\begin{axiom}[Vibe]
The one substance is the vibe, a unit of felt state, and its only state is the ternary tone in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$. The relation between vibes is perception, not stored state.
\end{axiom}

The only thing that exists is the vibe, a unit of experience, a felt state. This is a monist position. Reality is one experiential substance, and the physical world is a structure within feeling rather than a separate material.

\begin{definition}[Experience]
The subjective flow of states, the felt going-through of reality that is the one substance of the theory.
\end{definition}

A vibe's only state is its tone, a ternary value, $-1$ (pain), $0$ (peace), $+1$ (pleasure). There are three states per vibe and nothing more. There is no hidden state. Anything a vibe appears to know is read from its own tone and from the tones of its neighbors.

\subsection{The five base elements}

The foundation consists of exactly five base elements, defined one at a time below.

\begin{definition}[The mesh, $\{5,3,4\}$]
Vibes sit on the hyperbolic dodecagrid, a tiling of hyperbolic three-space by dodecahedra, with twelve neighbors per cell and four cells around each edge. The tiling is exact, regular, negatively curved, and grows exponentially outward. This is the space.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The ternary tone]
The state of each vibe, a value $-1$, $0$, or $+1$, the felt value of the vibe. Peace ($0$) is the neutral value. Pain ($-1$) and pleasure ($+1$) are the charged extremes.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The conserving perception rule]
One local update on each edge, that is, on each pair of neighbors, driven by what each vibe perceives of the other. It conserves the total charge. The full rule appears in the next section.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Reflection, the eternal growth]
The crystal keeps growing. New vibes are born at the frontier forever. This is the universe's expansion and its source of the new. New cells are born at peace ($0$).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The arrow]
A single value-direction, from pain through peace to pleasure. Peace is the optimum, but pleasure is the pull, so the arrow biases the dynamics toward closing the gap between the current tone and the desired one. This is the only asymmetry, and it is the seed of value, life, and intention.
\end{definition}

\subsection{The note is perception, not a thing}

A correction is built into the base. The note, the relation between vibes, is not a separate stored object. The note is what a vibe sees of its surrounding vibes. It is perception. There are no stored fills or edge-states, only the tones, and the dynamics is driven by each vibe perceiving its neighbors' tones. This keeps the state minimal, just the tones, and the ontology clean, with no hidden variables.

\begin{definition}[Perception]
The converting of a neighbor's state into what a vibe registers of it. The note is perception.
\end{definition}

Earlier work treated the note as a separate face-vibe. That was an error and was corrected. The note is perception.

\subsection{Why exactly these five}

Each element earns its place by supplying a distinct capacity.

\begin{itemize}
  \item The mesh gives space and locality. Its hyperbolic curvature gives a small diameter and a tree-like hierarchy, which later power fast integration and intelligence.
  \item The tone gives a minimal felt state. It is ternary so that it can register peace or one of two opposite charges, a conserved charge of $U(1)$ type.
  \item The rule gives dynamics and conservation, which is to say the physics.
  \item Reflection gives time, growth, and novelty, which is to say the cosmology.
  \item The arrow gives value and direction. Without it the universe relaxes to dead peace. With it the universe lives.
\end{itemize}

Four of these, the mesh, the tone, the rule, and reflection, are value-neutral mechanism. The fifth, the arrow, is the single value posit, and it is what turns mechanism into life and mind. Decision, freedom, and intention all trace back to the arrow together with the four.

\subsection{The non-negotiables}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{One state only, the tone.} There are no hidden variables. Memory, selves, and models are all patterns of tones, never new state.
  \item \textbf{Five elements, nothing inserted.} Every later capability, a self, the will, planning, a quantum field, must be shown to emerge from these five. The discipline of the project is to never add a sixth.
  \item \textbf{The note is perception.} The only relation is what a vibe sees. It is computed, not stored.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Summary}

Reality is vibes, felt states, each carrying a ternary tone on the $\{5,3,4\}$ hyperbolic crystal. Five base elements, the mesh, the tone, the conserving perception rule, reflection, and the arrow, together with one state, the tone, and one relation, the note, which is perception rather than a stored object, are the entire foundation. Everything else in the theory is an emergent composition of these five. The central discipline is to never add a sixth.
