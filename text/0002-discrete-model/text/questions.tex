\section{Open Questions and Build Order}
\label{sec:questions}

Several questions remain genuinely open, and the model's value lies partly in making them precise.

The two fate forks of Section~\ref{sec:emergence} are the deepest. Whether the whole can genuinely forget a distinction, and whether a changeless state can be fully aware, together fix the ending of the cosmology and whether its beginning needed explaining. Both turn on premises the framework derives rather than assumes, so both are open to revision.

The matter construction of Section~\ref{sec:matter} is the hardest technical problem. Getting exactly the right component count without spurious doublers on a hyperbolic mesh, deriving full exchange statistics, and fixing precisely when a note crystallizes into a relational vibe are all unproven. So is the deepest consistency question, whether the inner spinor and the emergent metric co-emerge consistently rather than in a circle.

The engine of beats, made fully rigorous, requires showing that the vibe population grows without bound under the chosen rule, or, if not, estimating the cycle length. The exact rule remains to be found by searching the critical band for the rule that maximizes coherence or integrated information. And the central program, recovering relativity and quantum mechanics as true coarse-grained limits, is a long undertaking that would move the framework from philosophy toward physics.

A concrete build order organizes the work. First, simulate the minimal model, ternary tones over structural notes with asynchronous updates, and verify harmony descent, the formation of coherent selves, the instability of uniform unity, the deliberation gap, and emergent note-distance. Second, search the critical band for the rule. Third, build the spinor construction on a small tile-neighborhood and check its spectrum. Fourth, derive one physical law as a true limit, beginning with gravity as a combinatorial curvature flow. Fifth, test the discriminating predictions of Section~\ref{sec:physics}. Each step is finite and simulable, which is the practical payoff of insisting that the base be discrete.
