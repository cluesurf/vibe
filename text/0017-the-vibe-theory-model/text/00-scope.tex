\section*{Scope}
\label{sec:scope}

This page is the paper in brief. Each line names what it rests on, and the sections after give the numbers. Every
run is on a flat periodic box, a stand-in for the honeycomb whose results carry over only for one dock over one beat,
and the runs made on balls of the true honeycomb are mostly negative (Section~\ref{sec:base}). Held means L2 at best:
no result below is L3 by the program's own rubric.

\subsection*{Filled in since \emph{The Missing Amplitude}}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The missing amplitude is supplied.} The rule now carries exact amplitudes in a ring of the form
$\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/6]$, fixed by how the links lift. The meeting (the fear beat) is the one non-Clifford gate, and a
moving vibe is quantum while a static one is classical (Section~\ref{sec:light}).
\item \textbf{A rule with a reason for every piece}: the $W(F_4)$ knit, the collision, the doublet lock, the meeting,
the pass, the no-veto store and the coin, each chosen over a stated alternative (Section~\ref{sec:rule}). It is a
working rule, not a final one: its matter moves only along lines, which fails isotropy, and it moves nothing on
the true honeycomb's surface.
\item \textbf{A working vacuum}, balanced, reversible and covariant on 51 of 51 cases (Section~\ref{sec:rule}).
\item \textbf{Light}, as compact $U(1)$ from column sums, with two polarizations and Coulomb's $1/(24\pi r)$
(Section~\ref{sec:light}).
\item \textbf{Entangled knots.} The rule makes knots whose best CHSH exceeds 2, but no record the model makes violates
CHSH. Contextuality, Tsirelson's bound, no-cloning, teleportation and GHZ are known theorems of any qutrit circuit
(L1), the rule's share being that it makes the knot (Section~\ref{sec:light}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Built, at L2 on the flat box}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Gravity from an added depth register.} One trit per link, summed into a depth, gives $1/r$, light bending
by 2 and $\alpha$ held constant. The step field is added to the rule, the factor 2 follows by construction, and
gravity's speed is a chosen constant. The same fall for every body failed its gate (Section~\ref{sec:gravity}).
\item \textbf{Horizons}: a radius of $2GM$ and Hawking's redshift scaling. The horizon's area law is bookkeeping, a flat
1.44 to 1.47 of $A/4G$, and the rule's own state reads a volume law (Section~\ref{sec:gravity}).
\item \textbf{Spin 2 from unlabeled docks}: sliding the docks forces linearized Einstein--Hilbert and the ADM structure.
A wave speed of exactly $c$ and $\lambda = 1$ needs a slide whose time part varies by place, which holds only if the
present has no preferred slice, untested on the adopted rule (Section~\ref{sec:gravity}).
\item \textbf{Randall--Sundrum's short-range number} in the flat bulk, with its 4/3 factor derived
(Section~\ref{sec:gravity}).
\item \textbf{A composite at relativistic speed} on a line (Section~\ref{sec:changelog}).
\item \textbf{Motion in 3d, one-body, on a candidate rule.} Line-only motion is ruled out by observation. A stated rule
change gives every excitation one limiting speed $c^* = c/2$ and isotropic motion, with the vacuum inert and gravity's
sign kept. Its thresholds were set after a probe, and binding on it is open (Section~\ref{sec:motion}).
\item \textbf{The binding problem, narrowed by theorems}: the band sum, the Kesten floor, theorems A and B, the first
order walls of $\mathbb{Z}_3$ and of the bulk, and a $2I$ string that loosens continuously on the husk
(Section~\ref{sec:binding}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Still open}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Binding that moves.} Whether the beat keeps the ordered $2I$ vacuum long enough, and then a light bound
member moving in 3d with inertia equal to energy. This is the program's sharpest open question.
\item \textbf{Measurement and the Born rule.} No pointer basis or redundant record is made by the rule, and the Born
rule is posited, not derived (Section~\ref{sec:base}).
\item \textbf{The second law on the working rule.} What is held is a coarse rise on an earlier knit, from a start 2
percent short of its ceiling, on a classical fragment (Section~\ref{sec:light}).
\item \textbf{A Planck temperature} for horizons, and the bulk's warp, radion and brane bending.
\item \textbf{One light speed} for the husk light on the candidate rule.
\item \textbf{The electron across lines and $g$}, \textbf{the weak force and CP violation}, \textbf{the value of
$\alpha$}, \textbf{a bounded self}, and \textbf{one rule for every row} (Section~\ref{sec:open}).
\end{itemize}

The candidate rule of Section~\ref{sec:motion} is not yet the working rule: it is adopted only once binding works
on it.
