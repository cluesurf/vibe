\item \textbf{Binding.} Whether the beat keeps the ordered $2I$ vacuum at small angle, then a light member bound by its
string moving in 3d with $R \to 1$ (Section~\ref{sec:binding}).

\item \textbf{The bulk.} The warp, the radion's stabilization and brane bending are still taken from the continuum.
\item \textbf{One light speed.} On the current coin a locked vibe tops out at $1/2$ a beat and every stable husk light

\item \textbf{The electron in 3d, and g.} Three loves under the pass bind on one husk line (binding 1.079, spin-half
share 0.982, \code{E-SPN-0093}), not yet across lines, and $g$ needs the coupling to light.
\item \textbf{The weak force.} The locked rule keeps P, C, T, CP and CPT under all 48 husk parities, and bulk chirality
never reaches the husk, a theorem (\code{E-FRC-0248}, \code{E-FRC-0249}). It needs a spin apart from the slot and an
orientation in depth. CP violation is open with it.
\item \textbf{The value of $\alpha$.} $\alpha = (s/24)\sqrt{3\kappa/2}$ per vibe, with $s$ the drift's share of
$\kappa$. No depth gives $1/137$, and nothing is read off (\code{E-FRC-0242}).
\item \textbf{A bounded self.} Shared history gives one component of all 24,576 vibes or closed components of exactly
8 on one line, nothing between (\code{E-SLF-0177}, \code{E-SLF-0178}).
\item \textbf{One rule for every row.} Many held results ran on an earlier rule and have not been rerun.
\end{itemize}
