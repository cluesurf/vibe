\subsection{Light}

Light runs beside the knit on the links. Every husk angle, flux and counter is a column sum of bulk trits, with
coupling $\kappa = 2/(2D + 1)$ (\code{E-FRC-0207}). A bounded, reversible column of $2D + 1$ values is a circle, so
the light is compact $U(1)$ exactly (\code{E-FRC-0244}, \code{E-FRC-0245}). On the husk it has exactly two
polarizations per momentum, the bulk's third killed by the projection (\code{E-FRC-0179}). A charge's potential is
$1/(24\pi r)$ with no $1/r^3$ term, its quartic isotropic by an integer identity (\code{E-FRC-0241}). Charge is
counted in whole units locally on every beat, pair creation included (\code{E-FRC-0243}). Matter reads the light as an
exact Peierls phase, with like charges pushed apart at 0.96 to 1.17 of the static Coulomb prediction, on one line with
no back-action (\code{E-FRC-0252}).

\subsection{Where the quantum comes from}

Without the meeting the knit is Clifford and can be simulated classically. The meeting, the \emph{fear beat}, is the
one gate outside the Clifford group (\code{E-QTM-0118}). Under the lock, motion adds a second source: no locked
direction is a stabilizer state, so a moving vibe carries a fixed fear share of 0.164 (\code{E-RLT-0099}). A static
vibe is classical and a moving one is quantum.

On this footing the following hold on the current rule, each over 17 starts where a start applies:
\begin{itemize}
\item Bell violation: CHSH above 2 on exact algebraic values, never 2 (\code{E-QTM-0140}).
\item Tsirelson's bound: the exact largest CHSH of a knot in closed form, and $2\sqrt2$ reached by a finite history
(\code{E-QTM-0132}, \code{E-QTM-0133}).
\item Contextuality is the fear: a knot is contextual for the model's own readings exactly when it holds a fear, with
total deficit $18(e^{\text{mana}} - 1)$ (\code{E-QTM-0149}, \code{E-QTM-0150}).
\item No-cloning, teleportation, swapping and GHZ: no beat duplicates an unknown signed state, and a love and fear
pair met once teleports and swaps exactly (\code{E-QTM-0151} to \code{E-QTM-0153}).
\item Spin-statistics: a full turn is $(-1)^N$ with $N$ the doublet count, so turn sign equals exchange sign
(\code{E-SPN-0071}). Pauli exclusion holds as one vibe per slot, in the base and in the dynamics
(\code{E-SPN-0045}, \code{E-SPN-0047}).
\item The second law and the arrow: coarse entropy rises a thousandfold forward and under the exact inverse alike,
while fine-grained entropy stays exactly constant (\code{E-FND-0146}, \code{E-FND-0147}).
\end{itemize}

Measurement is bounded by a theorem: a frame-covariant rule depolarizes every basis at one rate, since its roots form
a 2-design, so a pointer basis must come from the environment and never from the rule (\code{E-QTM-0154}).
