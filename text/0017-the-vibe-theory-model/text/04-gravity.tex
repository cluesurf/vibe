\label{sec:gravity}

A gravity has to fall as $1/r$ (a massless field), only attract (even spin), act the same on everything (a
charge-blind source), arrive at $c$ (GW170817 put it within 1.7 s over 130 million light years), bend light by 2

$r^{-6.5}$ (\code{E-FRC-0253}), and a second light sourced by content, which repels like sources as spin 1 must
(\code{E-GRV-0074}).
