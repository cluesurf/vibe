\subsection{Corrections}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Results on earlier rules} stand as results about those rules. CP violation with CPT exact was measured on
the turning weave, and the locked rule keeps CP exactly, so it is open again. The Cabibbo angle within four percent was
never tested against a pre-registered null. No magnetic monopoles was an identity of an earlier light, and compact
$U(1)$ admits heavy ones. The vacuum clock of period three belonged to the charge rule, and the working vacuum's cycle
is 12 beats.
\item \textbf{Gravity was once given as solved} from an area-law potential and an area-law invariant, both on a
hand-written walk and graded stand-in. On the working vacuum the entanglement is a volume law at equilibrium
(\code{E-GRV-0083}), and gravity read as the clock rate alone bends light by 1, not 2 (\code{E-GRV-0088}).
\item \textbf{Within the open-pieces branch.} A reading of 31\% frozen flat weight in the mass-string composite was
the moving pair's own component, and the true figure is 0.16\% per member (\code{E-SPN-0148}). A looser string's $R$
of 1.751 was a different bound level (\code{E-SPN-0111}). ``Finer layering shortens the range'' was wrong: finer
layering gives a gapless tower. A membrane horizon ``fix'' still left a frozen interior hidden, and was restated.
\item \textbf{Instrument defects} caught before gate runs are disclosed in each experiment's header, and no gate was
moved after a run.
\end{itemize}
