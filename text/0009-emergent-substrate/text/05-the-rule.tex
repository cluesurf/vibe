\section{The Rule, Exactly}

This section gives the single complete specification of the rule, what exists, how it updates, and exactly how
many parts it has. This is the modern form on the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ crystal, a directional lattice gas that
\emph{streams} then \emph{collides}. It supersedes an earlier single-tone pairwise version on $\{5,3,4\}$ with
$12$ neighbours and a nine-state edge shuffle. Everything below is broken into clean integer counts so the whole
machine can be held in mind at once. Nothing is left out. We then zoom all the way in to a single cell and watch
one beat act on it, which puts the geometry and the rule together in one concrete picture.

\subsection{In essence}

On the fixed $\{3,4,3,4\}$ crystal, every cell keeps one ternary value per direction, $24$ of them, and each beat
is two steps. First \emph{stream}, in which every directional value moves to the neighbour it points at. Then
\emph{collide}, a local, charge-conserving, reversible rewrite of a cell's $24$ values.

\subsection{What exists, the four base elements}

The \emph{mesh} is the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb, the four-dimensional hyperbolic crystal. Its cells are
$24$-cells $\{3,4,3\}$, so each cell has $24$ faces, hence $24$ neighbour-directions, the coin. The mesh is
generated by reflection, the Coxeter group $[3,4,3,4]$, and it grows forever, one shell per beat. Reflection is
how it is built and growth is it enlarging, so neither is a separate element. Its ideal $\{4,3,4\}$ cubic element
is the flat three-dimensional physical cusp.

The \emph{tone} is the value carried in each slot, exactly one of $-1$, $0$, or $+1$. Never a fraction, never a
continuous amplitude. The base is discrete. The \emph{beat} is discrete time. Reality advances in synchronous
ticks, each beat applying the rule once. The forward direction of the beats is the arrow, which lives in the
rule. The \emph{rule} is the directional lattice-gas update below.

The one enrichment over the oldest model is that the tone is now directional. A cell holds one ternary slot per
direction, $24$ slots, rather than a single scalar.

\begin{definition}[The directional tone]
Each cell $A$ holds slots $[s_1, \ldots, s_{24}]$, one per face, each in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$. Slot $s_k$ is the
charge currently moving through face $k$ of $A$. The cell's scalar tone, its net charge, is the sum
$\sum_{k} s_k$.
\end{definition}

Nothing is lost in this enrichment, since the scalar tone is recovered as the sum of the slots. The slots only add
the direction a charge is heading, and that is what gives the rule genuine momentum and lets it carry spin.

\subsection{The beat, stream then collide}

\paragraph{Stream, which is momentum.} Every slot moves to the neighbour it points at. Slot $k$ of cell $A$
leaves $A$ through face $k$, arrives at neighbour $B_k$, and lands in $B_k$'s slot for the same line of travel,
so it keeps going the same way. Nothing decides or randomises direction. A charge heading north simply keeps
going north. Streaming is a global permutation of all slots in the crystal, each slot having exactly one
destination slot and the map being a bijection, so it is conflict-free and applied to every cell at once. No
edge-colouring is needed, which was the old pairwise model's bookkeeping. Because streaming preserves direction, a
charge travels ballistically, one cell per beat.

\paragraph{Collide, which is interaction, the arrow, and mass.} After streaming, each cell holds a fresh set of
$24$ incoming slots. Apply a fixed local collision that reads the $24$ incoming slots and rewrites them as $24$
outgoing slots, by a map that conserves the total charge and is reversible. The collision plays the role of the
coin operator in a quantum walk, but here it is a discrete, charge-conserving, reversible local map with no
fractions. There are four move-types.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Pass-through.} A lone charge with nothing to interact with keeps its slot and streams straight on.
\item \emph{Annihilate.} An incoming $+1$ and $-1$ that meet cancel to $0$ and $0$, charge-neutral.
\item \emph{Create.} A cell of all-peace can emit a $+1$ and a $-1$ into two opposite outgoing slots,
charge-neutral, and the pair flies apart with opposite momenta. This is pair production, and it is the arrow. The
rule prefers this forward direction over its reverse, which is what makes the beats flow toward more structure
rather than back to nothing.
\item \emph{Scatter.} The collision can turn an incoming direction partly into other outgoing directions. The
amount of this mixing is the mass. No scatter is a massless charge going dead straight at light speed. More
scatter is a heavier, slower, zig-zagging charge.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The scalar pairwise shadow}

The move-types are easiest to see in the simplest possible setting, a single scalar tone per cell and a single
pair of neighbours, the two-direction shadow of the full $24$-direction rule. A pair holds two tones, so there
are $3 \times 3 = 9$ pair-states, and the rule is one reversible permutation of them, written out completely.

\caption{The nine-state scalar pairwise shadow, one reversible permutation of all two-tone pair states.}
\end{table}

Two facts fall straight out of the table. The total charge is conserved, since no move changes the sum $a + b$.
And the rule is reversible, since the table is a permutation of the nine states with a well-defined inverse.
Reading off the move-types, there are two hops, one per charge sign, one create-flip-annihilate three-cycle,
$(0,0) \to (+1,-1) \to (-1,+1) \to (0,0)$, and two inert fixed points. Creation and annihilation are the same
operator at the two ends of one cycle, forced to be inverses by reversibility, and that cycle is the arrow. This
table is the scalar pairwise shadow of the full rule, the same move-types in two directions. In the full rule the
hop becomes the stream, the charge living in a direction slot and moving along it, while create, annihilate,
inert, and scatter are the per-cell collide on the $24$ slots. The shadow is the cleanest way to see the moves,
and the directional version above is the real thing.

\subsection{The three properties that fall out}

\begin{result}[Conservation, reversibility, spin]
\emph{Charge is conserved.} Every move-type preserves the sum of the slots, so the total tone over the universe is
constant, the discrete Gauss law that sources the emergent $U(1)$. \emph{The rule is reversible.} Stream is a
permutation and collide is a bijection, so the whole beat has an exact inverse, verified forward-then-back to zero
error at scale \cite{pollard2026vibetest}. \emph{Spin lives in the collision's symmetry on the $24$ directions.}
The $24$ roots of $D_4$ decompose as $8v + 8s + 8c$, so a collision built on them can carry genuine spinors, a
state that flips sign under a full $2\pi$ rotation.
\end{result}

The spinor property is the concrete reason the substrate must be $\{3,4,3,4\}$. The $12$ directions of
$\{5,3,4\}$ decompose as $1 + 3 + 3' + 5$, all integer spin and no spinor, so a $\{5,3,4\}$ collision cannot
carry spin. This is taken up in detail when we turn to fermions.

\subsection{The framing}

This is not free. It enlarges the state, from one ternary value per cell to $24$ ternary slots per cell. There are
two fair readings. Either it is still tone, just directional, with the scalar tone recovered as the sum of the
slots, an enriched tone rather than a new element, and the four elements stand. Or the coin has become a base
feature, and the base has grown. Either way it is a deliberate change from the one-tone-per-cell model, made to
get momentum and spin, both of which the scalar read-all-faces rule could not be shown to carry. The pay-off is
that it propagates by construction, it stays fully discrete, conserving, and reversible, and on $\{3,4,3,4\}$ it
carries spin.

What is not in the rule is everything continuous. Continuous amplitudes, the unitary quantum walk, the complex
Dirac field, and mass as a mixing angle are the effective description of this discrete rule after coarse-graining,
the way a lattice gas coarse-grains to fluid equations, never the base. Selves, particles as bodies, gravity, and
nesting are emergent on the cusp and above. The base stays the four elements and the stream-collide rule, nothing
more.

\subsection{Anatomy of a single vibe}

The clearest way to grasp what a vibe looks like in the whole model is to zoom all the way in to one cell and
watch one beat act on it. A cell is a $24$-cell, a four-dimensional solid with $24$ octahedral faces. Each face is
shared with exactly one neighbour cell, so the cell has $24$ neighbours, call them $B_1$ through $B_{24}$, one
across each face. The $24$ face-directions are the $D_4$ root system, the coin. That is the whole local geometry,
a cell and its $24$ face-neighbours, and distance is step-counting on this graph. Step-counting is what space will
come to mean.

What the cell stores is its $24$ ternary slots $s_1$ through $s_{24}$, one per face, each in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$. Slot
$s_k$ is the charge currently heading out through face $k$. That is the cell's entire state, $24$ ternary values
and nothing else, with no hidden state. The cell's scalar tone, the familiar pain, peace, or pleasure value, is
just the sum $s_1 + \cdots + s_{24}$. A cell's state space is therefore $3^{24}$, but in practice almost all of it
is near-empty, a single moving charge being one slot at $+1$ and the other $23$ at $0$.

As for how the cell relates to its neighbours, face $k$ of cell $A$ is the same shared octahedron as one face of
neighbour $B_k$. The two cells meet exactly there. $A$'s outgoing slot $s_k$ and $B_k$'s slot for the same line of
travel are the two ends of that shared face. There are no edge weights and no stored links. The relation is just
the shared face and the matching slots. The note, the perception across the face, is computed from this and never
stored.

\subsection{One beat, walked through}

A beat is two steps. In the stream step every slot moves to the neighbour it points at. Slot $s_k$ of $A$ leaves
through face $k$, arrives at neighbour $B_k$, and lands in $B_k$'s slot for the same line of travel, so it keeps
going the same way. At the same instant $A$ receives, into each of its own slots, whatever its neighbours streamed
toward it. After streaming, $A$ holds a fresh set of $24$ incoming slots. Nothing decided direction. A charge
heading north simply kept going north. This is momentum, the charge travelling one cell per beat, ballistically.

In the collide step $A$ reads its $24$ incoming slots and rewrites them as $24$ outgoing slots, by the fixed local
map that conserves the sum and is reversible. The move-types are the four already named, pass-through, annihilate,
create, and scatter, with the nine-state table above as their scalar shadow.

A worked example makes it concrete. Start with cell $A$ holding $s_3 = +1$ and all other $23$ slots at $0$, a
single $+1$ charge heading through face $3$, with all neighbours empty. On the first beat, $s_3$ leaves $A$
through face $3$, arrives at neighbour $B_3$, and lands in $B_3$'s straight-ahead slot. Now $A$ is all zero and
$B_3$ holds one incoming $+1$. At $B_3$ the collision finds one incoming charge with nothing to meet, so it is
pass-through, and the charge keeps its direction. Beat after beat the $+1$ streams in a straight line, one cell
per beat, never spreading. That is a massless particle, with $E = k$. Turn on a mixing scatter collision and the
charge starts turning into other slots, its track zig-zags, and its effective speed drops. That is mass, with
$\cos E = \cos m \cos k$. Start instead from an all-peace region with the arrow's create move on, and a $+1$ and a
$-1$ are emitted into opposite slots and fly apart, which is pair production. Throughout, the total over all cells'
slots never changes. Charge is conserved.

This fixes the intuition. Nothing moves through space. Space is the cell-graph, and the only motion possible is a
tone-pattern redrawn one cell over each beat, a glider. Momentum is which slot a charge lives in. Mass is how
much the collision mixes slots. The arrow is the create move. Conservation is simply that the collision never
changes the sum. One cell, $24$ ternary slots, two steps a beat. That is the whole machine, and everything that
follows is what this machine does at scale. The entire universe is a ternary tone on the $24$ directions of every
cell of a growing $\{3,4,3,4\}$ crystal, updated each beat by one streaming move and one reversible local
collision. In the carrying images this single machine is the fire's one law of burning and the song's one rule of
harmony. The mesh is the instrument, the tone its notes, the beat its pulse, and the stream and collide are how
each note sounds and resolves into the next.
