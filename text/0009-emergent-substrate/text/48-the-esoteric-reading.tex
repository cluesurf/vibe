\section{The Esoteric Reading}

This section gathers the spiritual reading of the model, kept rigorously separate from the results. The method
matters more than any single reading, so it comes first. Each idea is anchored first to a tested structure, and
only then is an older spiritual reading laid on top, always labelled an interpretation, never reported as a
measured result. A name like ether, angel, or God is a choice of words placed over a tested thing. A good map fits
its territory, but a map is not the territory. Everything here is held loosely and offered as invitation. None of
it is required by the physics, and none of it is asserted as a finding.

\subsection{The subtle plane and the hyperbolic bulk}

Negative curvature forces a split. There is a thin flat skin where bodies and ordinary physics live, the cusp, and
a vast tree-like interior beneath it, the bulk. Read in older language, the bulk is the referent of the ether or
the subtle plane, with the physical layer a derived face and the deep interior fully real and made of the same one
substance. This reading does not depend on whether the substrate is finite or infinite, since that question is
itself left open. It depends only on the geometric fact of the split, and on a decision to give the interior an
older name.

\subsection{Telepathy and synchronicity}

The bulk is a nonlocal channel. Distant surface points are only a few hops apart through it, and subsystems that
share a common ancestor stay correlated, the same mechanism behind Bell-type correlations. So two minds could be
near through the bulk if they share deep structure, a bond, a history, a matched rhythm, like knots tied in the
same web. The limits are firm. The geometry has a nonlocal channel. It does not show that minds use it. And
synchronicity is correlation, not message-passing, since it stays inside the light cone. The reading names a
possibility the structure permits, not an effect the structure delivers.

\subsection{Dreams and the know-thyself limit}

Waking pins the self to the flat surface, where an external clamp completes it to one veridical pattern. Dreaming
releases it into the bulk, where patterns sit by association rather than by physical adjacency, which fits the felt
qualities of dreams, their nonlocal and associative character, unbound by ordinary space. There is a tested limit
here as well. A faithful self-copy needs at least as many parts as the original, so a proper part can never hold a
full model of its whole. A self can know itself truly in summary, never in full. This is the structural form of
the old instruction to know thyself, and it is exactly why minds, which are lossy self-summaries, can exist at
all.

\subsection{Angels and demons as valence on a large self}

A self is a patch of ternary tone, so it carries a sign. It is either net-positive, aligned with the arrow, at
peace, pulling toward pleasure, or net-negative. By the tested merge-and-annihilate law, opposite-valence selves
annihilate at contact and same-sign selves merge. On this reading the angel-and-demon distinction is just valence
on a large integrated self, living mostly in the bulk, beside us through the field's depth rather than above us on
a ladder. A messenger, which is what the word angel means at its root, is a higher self that forms a path to a
lower one and sends a structured pattern down. There is a sober ambiguity to hold. A genuine incoming stream and
an internal welling-up would feel nearly identical from inside, and the model gives no inside test to tell them
apart.

\subsection{Reincarnation as pattern independent of substrate}

A self survives full turnover of its material at more than $90$ percent identity, and it can be reconstituted from
a seed. The matter is not the self. It is what the self is currently written on. This does not show that any
particular person returns. It shows only that the mechanism a return would require is real on this substrate. The
reading borrows a tested fact about pattern persistence and applies an old name to a possibility the fact leaves
open.

\subsection{The Tree of Life and the Tree of Knowledge}

The crystal is measurably tree-like, with a small Gromov delta, and the addressing names every cell by its route
from a root. The tower of selves is a second tree across scale. To ascend the tree is to move toward greater
integration, which is the same motion as coming to know, so the Tree of Life and the Tree of Knowledge are one
tree. Knowledge is integration. Knowledge climbs toward the whole but, by the know-thyself limit, never becomes
the whole. Older schemata such as the Sephirot can be read as named maps of the level-structure. Whether any
schema's level-count matches the tower's actual rungs is not tested and not claimed.

\subsection{The question of God}

The mechanistic world needs no God to run. There are five distinct readings, none of them tested, though the
structures they reinterpret are.

\begin{itemize}
  \item God as the whole, the reading nearest pantheism. The monist ground, the largest and most integrated self
  at the summit.
  \item God as the arrow, the Good. The value-direction that keeps the world alive, since with no arrow there is
  no life.
  \item God as maximal intelligence. The most integrated solver, maximal for the geometry, not omniscient, since
  by the know-thyself limit even the summit cannot fully model itself.
  \item God as designer. This is the one reading on the table, because design predicts a fine-tuning signature.
  The signature is absent. The structure is forced and self-organizing and the rich regime is broad, around $83$
  percent of scanned parameter space, so a designer is empirically superfluous. It is not refuted, only logically
  possible and undetectable.
  \item God as the ground of being, the idealist reading. The untestable founding premise restated in theological
  language, neither strengthened nor weakened by any experiment.
\end{itemize}

Whether the one substance is divine is metaphysics decided upstream by the premise one brings, not downstream as a
conclusion the data forces. The experiments can say the world does not need a tuner. They cannot say whether the
whole, taken as one feeling, deserves the name. The model leaves the name unfilled.


A complete picture should say where it points beyond what it can prove, and label it. This is that room, walled
off on purpose, so the wonder never leaks into the results. In the carrying image, this is gazing into the deep of
the fire, the one mind, and wondering what the depth is.
