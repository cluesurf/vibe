\section{Glossary}

Plain-English meanings for the technical terms, written so a newcomer can follow and an expert is not talked down
to. In the paper, each term is also glossed briefly on first use. This appendix is the reference for those
glosses.

\begin{description}
  \item[vibe:] The one kind of thing in the theory, a unit of experience that notices its neighbours. Everything
  else is a pattern of vibes.
  \item[monism:] The view that there is only one kind of fundamental thing, here the vibe, experience itself.
  \item[note:] The relation between vibes, what a vibe perceives of its neighbours. It is the only relation in the
  model, and it is computed (perception), never stored. There is no hidden edge-state, the no-hidden-state rule. A
  note is what a vibe sees, not a thing sitting on an edge.
  \item[link:] A connection between vibes, more broadly a vibe-structure, a stable relational pattern. In the
  monism a link is itself made of vibes and notes, not a separate kind of thing. The link is the note seen as a
  standing connection.
  \item[fill:] The weight or strength with which a neighbour's tone contributes to a cell's update, the coupling in
  the signed-majority perception, the collision. The fills are part of the one rule, not new state. A proposed
  adaptive-fills sixth ingredient was tested and rejected, in keeping with the nothing-added discipline.
  \item[will:] A self's directed pump, the arrow held by a self and aimed at a goal, a finite, spendable bias the
  self adds against the local gradient. It is not a base ingredient, it is an emergent steering the rule respects,
  the texture of real willpower, since it can be depleted.
  \item[tessellation / honeycomb:] A way of tiling space edge-to-edge with repeating cells, like a crystal lattice
  or 3D wallpaper, here in curved space.
  \item[hyperbolic space:] Space with constant negative curvature, saddle-shaped at every point, so there is more
  room the further out you go and volumes grow exponentially with radius.
  \item[Schl\"afli symbol:] A short code, for example $\{3,4,3,4\}$, that names a regular tiling by which shapes
  meet and how many, the way a chemical formula names a molecule.
  \item[Coxeter group:] The group of mirror reflections that generates a regular tiling.
  \item[coin:] Our term for the set of neighbour-directions a cell can pass signals along, its local compass.
  \item[cusp / horosphere:] A flat sheet inside the curved space. A horosphere is a surface centred on a point at
  infinity, and it is intrinsically flat (Euclidean). The cusp is the horosphere where physical space lives.
  \item[spinor:] The mathematical object that describes a particle with half-integer spin. Its strange signature is
  that it must be turned $720$ degrees, two full rotations, not $360$, to return to itself.
  \item[fermion:] A particle of matter such as an electron or quark. Two identical fermions cannot share a state,
  Pauli exclusion, which is why matter takes up space. Fermions have half-integer spin, so they are described by
  spinors.
  \item[gauge field / gauge symmetry:] A gauge field is the kind of field that carries a force, the electromagnetic
  field being one. Gauge names a redundancy, many equivalent ways to write the same physical field, and that
  redundancy is the force's symmetry.
  \item[Standard Model:] The established, experimentally confirmed theory of all known particles and forces except
  gravity.
  \item[grand unified theory (GUT), $\mathfrak{so}(10)$:] A single larger symmetry that contains the Standard
  Model's forces as pieces, unifying them at high energy. $\mathfrak{so}(10)$ is one such unifying symmetry.
  \item[Dirac equation:] The equation for how a relativistic spin-$\tfrac{1}{2}$ particle, like an electron, moves,
  marrying quantum mechanics to special relativity.
  \item[Lorentz invariance:] The rule that physics looks the same to all observers moving at constant velocity, the
  symmetry of special relativity, with a finite top speed, light.
  \item[soliton / hopfion:] A soliton is a stable, self-holding lump in a field, a knot that cannot be untied. A
  hopfion is a particular 3D soliton shaped like linked rings.
  \item[Skyrme term:] An extra piece of the dynamics that keeps a soliton from shrinking to nothing, fixing its
  size.
  \item[holography:] The principle that everything in a region of gravitating space can be encoded on its
  lower-dimensional boundary, like a 3D scene stored on a 2D hologram.
  \item[renormalization group (RG), running:] How the effective strength of a force changes with the scale you
  probe. Running is that change with scale, and the beta function is its rate.
  \item[root system / crystallographic:] A root system is the symmetric pattern of vectors behind a continuous
  symmetry, a Lie group. Crystallographic means the pattern fits a repeating lattice, allowing only 2, 3, 4, or
  6-fold symmetry, never 5 or 7-fold.
  \item[triality:] A rare extra 3-fold symmetry of $\mathfrak{so}(8)$ that swaps its vector and two spinor pieces.
  Here it gives the three generations of matter.
  \item[de Sitter:] A universe that expands exponentially forever, driven by a cosmological constant, dark energy.
  \item[spectral dimension:] Dimension as measured by how a random walk spreads or returns. It equals the ordinary
  dimension for clean spaces and reveals roughness when it does not.
  \item[compact / paracompact:] A compact tiling has all its pieces bounded. A paracompact one has an ideal element
  reaching to infinity, a cusp. $\{3,4,3,4\}$ is paracompact, and its cusp is physical space.
  \item[the carrying metaphors:] The recurring images, the universe as a structure of consciousness, a mind,
  pictured as a blazing fire that grows like a tree from a seed, singing a song, flowing as a river on a deep
  ocean, woven as a fabric, with beings as its sparks, braids, and whirlpools.
\end{description}
