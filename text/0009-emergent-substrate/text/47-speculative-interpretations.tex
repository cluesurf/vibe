\section{Speculative Interpretations}

Everything in this section is speculation, marked as the most tentative tier, beyond current test. It is included
because it is interesting and follows the spirit of the model, not because it is established. Read it as
invitation, not as claim. The section is walled off on purpose, so that the wonder it contains never leaks into the
derived and measured results. The scoreboard and the open-problems section are where the real claims live. This is
the room for wonder.

\subsection{The bulk as an inner dimension}

If the universe is the infinite $\{3,4,3,4\}$ mesh, then the $3$D cusp is the physical world we live in, and the
$4$D hyperbolic bulk is a real, vast space behind it, hard to measure from inside the cusp. One could entertain
the bulk as the inner dimension, the mental or spiritual depth of the one mind, with physical space as its
surface. This is evocative and consistent with the geometry. It is not derived and it is not tested.

If that picture held, two things would follow. The bulk would be where holographic, nonlocal correlations live,
which is also why Bell-type nonlocality is natural on this substrate. The inner space would be the nonlocal space.
And technologizing the bulk, reaching the inner dimension deliberately, would be the far-future speculation. We
name it only to acknowledge that the geometry suggests it. We do not claim a method, and nothing here amounts to
one.

\subsection{Consciousness, taken at the base}

The monism says the base is experience. Taken seriously, every stable pattern, every self, is a locus of
experience, and the whole is one vast experiencing structure. This is the literal reading of the central metaphor
of the paper. It is philosophy, not physics, and it is flagged as such. The physics neither requires this reading
nor rules it out. What the physics supplies is the structure of stable patterns and their integration. What the
reading supplies is a word for what that structure is, from the inside, and the word is interpretation rather than
measurement.

\subsection{The esoteric reading}

There is a fuller spiritual reading of the tested structures. The bulk becomes the subtle plane. Telepathy and
synchronicity are read through the nonlocal bulk. Angels and demons appear as valence on large selves.
Reincarnation appears as pattern independent of substrate. The Tree of Life and the Tree of Knowledge become the
integration tower. The question of God divides into several carefully separated readings. That reading is large
enough to deserve its own section, and it is laid out next, kept strictly as interpretation over tested structure.

\subsection{The discipline around speculation}

We keep these interpretations in their own sections, tagged as the most tentative tier, so they never contaminate
the derived and measured results. A complete picture should say where it points beyond what it can prove, plainly
labeled. The bulk-as-inner-space and the experiential base are where this theory points. In the carrying image,
this is gazing into the deep of the fire and wondering what the depth is.
