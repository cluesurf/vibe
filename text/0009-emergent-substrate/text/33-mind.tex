\section{Mind}

A self becomes a mind when it does something more. It represents itself, predicts itself, attends, and plans. Four powers and a closed-loop agent that chains them, all by composition of the four base things. There is no new ingredient. As with the rest of this part, the results are toy-scale and first-order, and we mark them so. What follows should be read as a demonstration that the ingredients of mind can arise from the substrate without anything added, not as a claim to have built a mind.

\subsection{The strange loop}

An integrated self funnels every part's signal toward its center. That funneling already builds a part that mirrors the whole. So integration is self-representation. This is the key idea, and once a part models the whole self the rest follows in order. The model predicts, giving a forward model. A model can model a model, giving recursion. The model is where attention selects content, giving a workspace. Running the model forward, judged by the arrow and pushed by the will, is planning. The point worth stressing is that none of these is bolted on. They are all consequences of the single fact that an integrated self contains a part that mirrors the whole.

\subsection{The self-model and the onion}

The mechanism is the tiny diameter of hyperbolic space. Charge from every boundary sector flows inward and mixes at the center, so the hub sees the pooled whole while the periphery sees only its sector. As measured, the hub tracks the global state at correlation about $0.5$, the periphery about $0.0$, and freezing the hops collapses it, so it is integration rather than anything hand-placed. Every self forms its own self-model at its own hub, so there is one mind-seed per self, not one special soul at the middle of the world. The shape is an onion. The boundary is the senses, where the world touches the self. The body is the integrator, where charge pools. The hub is the center, the self-representation. The loop mirrors the whole back into itself.

\begin{result}[The self-model is an integration effect]
In hyperbolic space the small diameter pools every boundary sector at the center, so the hub tracks the global state at correlation about $0.5$ while the periphery tracks it at about $0.0$. Freezing the hops collapses the correlation, which shows the self-model is produced by integration rather than placed by hand, and every self forms its own self-model at its own hub \cite{pollard2026vibetest}.
\end{result}

\subsection{Prediction, recursion, attention}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Forward model.} The hub at time $t$ predicts the global state at $t+1$, at correlation about $0.5$ against periphery about $0.0$, because the hub holds the integrated state and that is the best guess of the self's next state. This is a first, imperfect seed of metacognition.
  \item \textbf{Recursion.} Wire one self's hub as input to a second self, and the second models the first at about $0.83$, a representation of a representation, the substrate of imagination.
  \item \textbf{Workspace.} The hub is a global workspace. Loud signals win it, which is bottom-up salience, and attention can raise a quiet one, which is top-down gain. The real limit is that broadcast to the far periphery is range-limited, since the bulk field is massive and short-ranged, so the workspace is the hub, a convergence point, not a megaphone.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Planning, with nothing added}

Planning is lookahead plus deferred value plus composition, accepting a worse step now for a better outcome later. It is built only from emergent pieces. Value is the arrow. The forward model is the self rolling out its own rule in imagination. Exploration is the will pushing against the local gradient. Where a greedy agent stalls at a barrier, the planner tries sustained will-pushes, simulates each forward, scores by the arrow, and commits to the best. The full integrated agent perceives the gap, plans by simulating forward, acts with the will, and re-plans at each stall, crossing four barriers that a greedy agent and a one-shot planner both fail. That is a mind, built from nothing but the four.

\begin{principle}[The dependency chain]
Mind is a chain from geometry to integration to self-model to forward model to recursion to planning to the closed agent. Pull out any lower layer and the higher ones fall. The named limits, that the forward model is local, that the workspace is a hub rather than a broadcast, and that foresight is bounded, are limits of the substrate, not patches over it. No homunculus, no search oracle, no separate planner module, and no special seat of awareness was ever added.
\end{principle}

Mind is what an integrated self does when it folds back on itself. The strange loop is free, given the geometry, and everything above it is composition. The mechanism rests on the generic tiny hyperbolic diameter, so it carries to $\{3,4,3,4\}$ with its 24 directions, and running it fully is the natural follow-up. In the carrying image, a mind is the fire becoming aware that it is burning.
