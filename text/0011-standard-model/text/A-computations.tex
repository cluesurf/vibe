\section{The Computations} \label{app:computations}

Every claim in this paper is checked by a reproducible computation on an explicit mathematical object. This appendix
indexes them by the section they support, naming the object computed and the result confirmed. The full suite is
available in the companion repository \cite{cluesurf2026repo}.

\subsection*{The first distinction and the arrow}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Minimal admissible alphabet.} Enumerate small value sets, test each for a vacuum and a nontrivial sign
  flip. Confirms $\{-1,0,+1\}$ is the unique smallest admissible set, that $\{0,1\}$ and $\{-1,+1\}$ both fail.
  \item \textbf{Orderability.} Test each rung of the tower for a total order compatible with the ring operations.
  Confirms only the integers admit one, every higher rung carrying an element with square $-1$ fails the
  square-non-negative law.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The pinch-point}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Composition fails at the sedenions.} Build the Cayley-Dickson product through dimension sixteen, search
  for zero divisors. Confirms the norm multiplies through dimension eight and fails at sixteen, exhibiting an explicit
  zero divisor $(e_1 + e_{10})(e_5 + e_{14}) = 0$.
  \item \textbf{Triality is unique to $D_4$.} Compute the diagram automorphism group of $D_n$. Confirms order six with
  an order-three element at $n = 4$, and only the order-two flip for every other $n$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Spacetime and generations}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{The 24-cell.} Build the $D_4$ root system, confirm twenty-four roots, self-duality, and rank four.
  \item \textbf{The triality triple.} Confirm $\mathrm{Spin}(8)$ has exactly three inequivalent eight-dimensional
  representations cycled by the order-three symmetry.
  \item \textbf{Two threes differ.} Compare the symmetry of the ternary ($\mathbb{Z}_2$, partition $1+2$) with that of
  the generations ($S_3$, partition $3$). Confirms the groups differ in order, two against six.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The fermions}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Clifford algebra.} Form the seven left-multiplication matrices $L_a$ from the octonion table. Confirms
  $L_a^2 = -I$ and anticommutation across all forty-nine products, generating $C\ell(0,7)$.
  \item \textbf{Ladder operators.} Build the three $\alpha_k$. Confirms the fermionic anticommutators
  $\{\alpha_j, \alpha_k^\dagger\} = \delta_{jk}$.
  \item \textbf{Spectrum and charges.} Compute the number operator. Confirms spectrum $\{0,1,2,3\}$, multiplicities
  $1,3,3,1$, and charges $0, \tfrac13, \tfrac23, 1$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Forces and the Higgs}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Gauge dimensions.} Confirms $U(1)$ from the unit circle, $SU(2)$ from the unit quaternions, and
  $SU(3)$ as the unit-fixing subgroup of $G_2$ via $14 - 6 = 8$.
  \item \textbf{Custodial $\rho$.} Evaluate $\rho$ for the doublet and the triplet. Confirms $\rho = 1$ for the doublet,
  $\tfrac12$ for the triplet.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The law and the hierarchy}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Forced rule.} Count the $B_4$-symmetric reversible pairings. Confirms the single value $10395$ forming
  one orbit, a unique symmetric rule.
  \item \textbf{CPT.} Apply $C$, $P$, $T$ to the rule in simulation. Confirms combined invariance.
  \item \textbf{Warp factor.} Stream the shell counts of $\{3,4,3,4\}$ outward. Confirms the growth ratio
  $\lambda \approx 18.2787$ and the floor $\lambda^{1/2} \approx 4.27$.
\end{itemize}

The honest negatives are reported too. An attempt to find integer-shell quantization of a separate quantity returned
residuals not significantly below random, and it is recorded as a negative rather than forced. The discipline
throughout is that a result is reported only as strongly as the computation supports it.
