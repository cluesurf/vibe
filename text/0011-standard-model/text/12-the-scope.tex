\section{The Scope of the Construction} \label{sec:scope}

It is fair to ask how far this reaches. Is it only an abstract argument about groups and dimensions, or does the same
substrate, run as actual physics, give back the particles and the things made of them. This section collects the whole
coverage in one place, honestly sorted into three tiers.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Derived.} Forced by the construction with no input from measurement. The architecture of the Standard
  Model.
  \item \textbf{Predicted.} A number computed from the structure and then compared with experiment, with no fit, no
  dial turned to match.
  \item \textbf{Reproduced.} The emergent dynamics on the substrate, run as ordinary quantum mechanics, returns a known
  result. These are consistency checks that the physics built on top is the real physics, not fresh derivations of the
  Standard Model.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What it all says}

Read the two tables together and the scope is clear. From a single premise, the entire fundamental roster of one
generation, four matter particles with their exact colors and fractional charges, the three forces that act on them,
and the one scalar that gives them weight, is \emph{derived}, not chosen. A handful of the numbers that the Standard
Model leaves as free inputs, the weak mixing angle, the $W$ to $Z$ mass ratio, the proton lifetime, the neutrino scale,
come out as \emph{predictions} with no fit. And the whole tower of ordinary matter built on top, atoms and their
spectra, molecules and their bonds, nuclei and their binding, and the spin statistics that hold it all together, is
\emph{reproduced} by the same substrate run as physics.

What remains free is exactly what is free in the Standard Model already, the absolute masses and couplings, which here
become a list of integer depths on one geometric ladder rather than a list of unrelated small numbers. Nothing else is
put in by hand. One distinction, folded without loss, gives the particles, the forces, the Higgs, and the world they
assemble into.
