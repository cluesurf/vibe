\section{Spacetime and Generations} \label{sec:spacetime}

The octonion now does two jobs at once. Its non-associativity gives the dimension of spacetime, and its triality gives
the three families. Both come from the same fact, the threefold symmetry of $D_4$.

\subsection{Four dimensions, from triality}

The octonions are the first non-associative rung, and their non-associativity is not a defect but a structure. The
automorphism group of the octonions is the exceptional group $G_2$, of dimension fourteen, and the multiplication is
encoded by the Fano plane, seven points and seven lines recording the seven imaginary units. This sevenfold internal
structure is not physical space. It is the internal arena in which the gauge symmetry lives, as we show in
Section~\ref{sec:forces}.

Physical space comes from a different and sharper place. The rotation structure that carries spin and supports three
mirrored families is $\mathrm{Spin}(8)$, whose Lie algebra is $D_4$. The defining feature of $D_4$ is triality, and
triality is the symmetry of its root system. The root system of $D_4$ is the 24-cell, the unique self-dual regular
four-dimensional polytope, with twenty-four vertices, the twenty-four roots of $D_4$. Its existence and self-duality
are special to four dimensions. The rank of $D_4$ is four. So the geometry that carries triality is intrinsically
four-dimensional, and that four is the dimension of spacetime.

\begin{result}[Four-dimensional spacetime]
The dimension of spacetime is the rank of $D_4$, which is four, realized as the $D_4$ root lattice and its 24-cell.
One of the four directions is the ordered integer arrow of Section~\ref{sec:arrow}, time. The remaining three are the
unordered space-like directions. The seven imaginary octonion units are internal, not spatial, and must not be
counted as dimensions of space.
\end{result}

The phrase ``the dimension of spacetime is four'' is, in most theories, an input. Here it is the rank of the unique
algebra that carries three families of spin, and the 24-cell is the concrete lattice on which the substrate is built.
We use this lattice, the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ hyperbolic honeycomb whose cell is the 24-cell, throughout the rest of the
construction.

\subsection{Three generations, from the triality triple}

$\mathrm{Spin}(8)$ has exactly three inequivalent eight-dimensional irreducible representations, the vector $8_v$ and
the two half-spinors $8_s$ and $8_c$. Triality is the order-three symmetry that cycles them,
\begin{equation}
  8_v \;\to\; 8_s \;\to\; 8_c \;\to\; 8_v .
\end{equation}
They are genuinely distinct as representations but identical in dimension and interchangeable under the symmetry. This
is the unique place in all the orthogonal groups where three representations of the same dimension are cycled by an
outer symmetry. Everywhere else the vector and the spinors differ in size and there is at most an order-two flip
exchanging the two spinors.

\begin{result}[Three generations]
The three generations of matter are the triality triple $8_v, 8_s, 8_c$ of $\mathrm{Spin}(8)$. There are exactly three
because $\mathrm{Spin}(8)$ has exactly three eight-dimensional representations, and they are mirror copies because
triality cycles them. The number three is not put in. It is the order of the triality symmetry, the threefoldness of
the $D_4$ star.
\end{result}

We must keep this three distinct from the ternary three of Section~\ref{sec:firstdistinction}. The ternary three is a
partition $1 + 2$, a vacuum and a mirrored pair, with symmetry $\mathbb{Z}_2$. The generation three is a partition
$3$, three peers, with symmetry the full $S_3$ that contains triality's order-three cycle. They are different numbers
three, arising at different layers, and we verify in Section~\ref{sec:forcedfree} that no symmetry maps one onto the
other. The coincidence of the value is just that, a coincidence of the smallest interesting integer appearing twice.

\subsection{The 24-cell, concretely}

It is worth seeing the lattice in the hand, because it ties the geometry, the generations, and the original ternary
tone into one object. The twenty-four roots of $D_4$ are all the coordinate vectors
\begin{equation}
  (\pm 1, \pm 1, 0, 0) \ \text{and its permutations}, \qquad 6 \times 4 = 24,
\end{equation}
each of squared length two, every one equivalent to every other. These twenty-four points are the vertices of the
24-cell, the unique self-dual regular polytope in four dimensions, and they are exactly the directions a tone may move
on the substrate, the twenty-four neighbors of each cell. Their opposite pairs give the twelve lines the law acts on.

The same twenty-four points have a second face. They are the unit Hurwitz quaternions, the binary tetrahedral group of
order twenty-four. As a group they split into three octets, the three cosets of the quaternion group $Q_8$ inside it,
and multiplication by $\omega = \tfrac12(-1 + i + j + k)$, a cube root of unity with $\omega^3 = 1$, cycles the three
octets,
\begin{equation}
  8 \;\xrightarrow{\ \omega\ }\; 8 \;\xrightarrow{\ \omega\ }\; 8 \;\xrightarrow{\ \omega\ }\; 8 .
\end{equation}
This is triality made concrete. The three octets of eight vertices are the three generations $8_v, 8_s, 8_c$, sitting
in the lattice, cycled by an explicit rotation. The threefoldness of the $D_4$ star is the threefold $\omega$ symmetry
of the 24-cell.

The full symmetry group of the 24-cell is the Weyl group of $F_4$, of order $1152$, the largest symmetry of any
four-dimensional polytope, larger than usual because of the self-duality. The law of Section~\ref{sec:law} respects its
hyperoctahedral subgroup $B_4$, the signed coordinate permutations, of order $384$. And the 24-cell closes the circle
back to the beginning, since it is exactly the squared-length-two shell of the ternary grid $\{-1, 0, +1\}^4$, the
twenty-four four-letter ternary words with two nonzero letters. The lattice of spacetime is built from four copies of
the original tone. The geometry is the tone, written four times.

So one object, the octonion, has delivered the stage and the cast. Four-dimensional spacetime is the rank-four
geometry of triality, and the three families are the three faces of triality. The next section opens one family and
reads off the particles.
