\section{Forced and Free} \label{sec:forcedfree}

Honesty about a derivation means saying exactly where it stops. This section draws the line between what the one
premise forces and what it leaves open, and it settles two technical points that the construction depends on.

\subsection{What is forced}

From the single premise, that one reversible distinction is made, the following are forced, each as the unique solution
to a short requirement, each checked by computation.

\begin{itemize}
  \item The ternary value $\{-1, 0, +1\}$, as the smallest carrier of a distinction with vacuum and conjugation.
  \item The integer arrow, as the unique orderable structure, giving time its one-way direction.
  \item The octonions, as the pinch-point of reversibility (ceiling eight) and three-family triality (floor eight).
  \item Four-dimensional spacetime, as the rank of $D_4$, with its 24-cell lattice.
  \item Three generations, as the triality triple $8_v, 8_s, 8_c$.
  \item One generation's particle content, with color multiplicities $1, 3, 3, 1$ and charges $0, \tfrac13, \tfrac23, 1$.
  \item The gauge group $SU(3) \times SU(2) \times U(1)$, as the symmetries of the algebra tower.
  \item The Higgs as a single $SU(2)$ doublet, with custodial $\rho = 1$.
  \item The local law, as the unique $B_4$-symmetric reversible update, and its $CPT$ invariance.
  \item The geometric shape of the mass hierarchy, with warp ratio $\lambda \approx 18.28$ fixed by the substrate.
\end{itemize}

This is the architecture of the Standard Model, the part that has always looked arbitrary. It is forced.

\subsection{What is free}

The following are not derived here.

\begin{itemize}
  \item The absolute values of the masses and couplings, the roughly nineteen numbers of the Standard Model. The
  hierarchy fixes their geometric \emph{pattern} and one scale, but not the individual depths or the overall units.
  \item The single cosmological premise itself, that a distinction is made at all, which is the one door left closed.
\end{itemize}

These are exactly the free inputs of the Standard Model as it already stands. So the account adds no new arbitrariness.
It removes most of the old arbitrariness and leaves the rest where it was. This is not a theory of everything. It is a
demonstration that the \emph{structure} of the Standard Model is not a list of accidents but a forced consequence of
reversibility and three-family matter.

\subsection{Two threes are not the same three}

The argument uses the integer three twice, the ternary tone and the three generations, and a careful reader will worry
that one is smuggled from the other. They are different objects, and we verify it. The ternary three is a set with a
distinguished element, the vacuum, so its symmetry group fixing that element is $\mathbb{Z}_2$, and its parts partition
as $1 + 2$. The generation three is a set of three peers cycled by triality, so its symmetry is the full $S_3$, of order
six, and its parts partition as $3$. There is no symmetry carrying the partition $1 + 2$ to the partition $3$, because
any such map would have to send the fixed vacuum to one of three interchangeable peers, destroying the distinction the
vacuum carries. We confirm computationally that the symmetry groups differ, $\mathbb{Z}_2$ of order two against $S_3$
of order six. The two threes coincide only in value, the smallest interesting integer appearing at two different
layers. Neither is derived from the other.

\subsection{The octonions are forced, not assumed}

The other worry is that the octonions are slipped in by hand and the rest is post hoc. They are not. The octonions are
the meeting point of two independent inequalities, one from above and one from below, each provable without mentioning
the octonions at all. Reversibility, an information condition, gives $\dim \le 8$ by Hurwitz. Three mirrored families, a
matter condition, gives $\dim \ge 8$ by the uniqueness of triality. Neither inequality knows about the other, and only
the value eight satisfies both. The octonions are what dimension eight is. So they are a conclusion, not a premise, and
the rest of the paper is reading off a forced object, not decorating an assumed one.
