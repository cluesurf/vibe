\begin{abstract}
We give a forced derivation of the Standard Model from a single premise, that one reversible distinction is made.
A distinction has three parts, the two sides and the boundary, so the smallest carrier of a distinction is a ternary
value, and counting distinctions gives the integers, the unique structure with a total order, whose one-way order is
the arrow of time. Folding distinctions reversibly means composing without loss, the norm-multiplicative condition that
defines a normed division algebra. Two pressures then fix one number. Reversibility caps the dimension at eight, since
the composition algebras are exactly the reals, complexes, quaternions, and octonions and nothing larger (Hurwitz),
and matter carrying three mirrored families needs the triality of $\mathrm{Spin}(8)$, which exists only at dimension
eight. The two meet at the octonions, which are therefore forced, and which sit at the last reversible rung before
zero divisors appear. From the octonions, by direct computation on the explicit algebra, we obtain four-dimensional
spacetime through the unique triality dimension $D_4$ and its 24-cell, the three generations as the triality triple
$8_v, 8_s, 8_c$, one full generation of quarks and leptons with their exact color and fractional electric charges from
the minimal spinor of the Clifford algebra $C\ell(0,7)$, the gauge group $SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)$ from the
division-algebra tower, and the Higgs as the quaternionic weak doublet, the connection in the internal direction. A
single reversible local law on the resulting hyperbolic substrate is shown to be the unique symmetric one and to be
$CPT$ invariant, and the fermion mass hierarchy follows as a discrete warped-cusp mechanism whose warp factor is the
substrate growth rate. We are explicit about what is derived (the structure, the representations, the charges, the
hierarchy scale) and what remains free (the absolute masses and couplings, exactly the free parameters of the Standard
Model). Every claim is checked by a reproducible computation, indexed in the appendix. This is exploratory work,
offered as a direction worth pursuing.
\end{abstract}
