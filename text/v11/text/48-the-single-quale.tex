\section{The Single Quale}

The chapters before this one read the model from the outside, as a physics. This one turns the same model inward and reads it as a feeling. It rests on a single posit. The vibe is felt at the base. Beyond that posit it adds nothing. It maps experience onto the very same $\{3,4,3,4\}$ substrate already built, with no new atom, no hidden state, no second substance. The discipline of the physics chapters still holds in full. Causal closure, no signaling, one substance.

The question here is the smallest experiential question that can be asked. What is the irreducible atom of feeling, the very least thing that can be felt at all? The answer is the \emph{tone}. One vibe sits in one site. The value it carries is one tone. That one tone is the single quale.

\begin{principle}[The atom of feeling]
The single quale is one tone, a single ternary value in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$, read as pain, peace, or pleasure. It is the least thing that can be felt.
\end{principle}

This is a Tier 1 claim. The tone and its three values are exact and built into the substrate already. The added claim, that this tone is \emph{felt} rather than merely counted, rests on the base posit and nothing more. We separate the two carefully throughout.

\subsection{The atom of feeling}

Three of the eight atoms of the flow meet at this point, so it helps to recall each before joining them. A \emph{site} is one of the $24$ directions out of a dock, the directed slot where a value can live. A \emph{tone} is the value that a site carries, a single ternary number. A \emph{vibe} is the substance itself, the experience, the stuff that everything is made of. The bearer is the site. The value is the tone. The experiencing of that value is the vibe. These stay distinct on purpose, so that the substance never collapses into the number.

A tone is ternary. It takes exactly one value in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$. Read as feeling, the three are pain, peace, and pleasure. Nothing more sits inside one tone.

That is the whole of the single quale. One tone, three possible states. It is the irreducible atom of experience, the floor below which there is no feeling left to subtract.

\begin{definition}[Single quale]
A \emph{single quale} is the value held by one tone in one site, an element of $\{-1, 0, +1\}$. It is the experience of one vibe at one place at one beat, and it is the smallest unit of felt experience the model admits.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Why three, and only three}

The ternary structure is not a free choice. It is the smallest set that can carry a middle. There is a push, which is pain. There is a pull, which is pleasure. And there is a rest between them, which is peace, the still point the other two depart from.

Drop to two values and the center is lost. A binary valence has a worst and a best but no place of no charge, no neutral source. Add a fourth value and nothing is gained. A one-dimensional valence is already said in full by a worst, a best, and a balance. The middle and the two poles exhaust what a single signed feeling can be.

\begin{proposition}[The floor needs exactly three]
A one-dimensional valence requires a least value, a greatest value, and a neutral value between them. Two values cannot carry the neutral center. A fourth value adds nothing a signed one-dimensional feeling can express. So the minimal feeling is ternary.
\end{proposition}

The neutral value is special, and not only as a midpoint. The peace, the $0$, is the source. It is the still tone that the create move of the knit leans a balanced pair out of, a $0$ tilting into a $+1$ and a $-1$ that together still sum to zero. So peace is the rest the lean departs from and the rest a richer feeling is built up from. The order $-1 < 0 < +1$ is the lean, the atom that gives the tones their tilt and the create its direction.

\begin{result}[A worst, a best, and a rest]
The smallest possible feeling has exactly three faces. A worst (pain), a best (pleasure), and a rest (peace). Peace is the source, the still value the lean tilts out of and the value every composed feeling is built upon.
\end{result}

\subsection{The single quale is not the dock}

A common confusion must be cut off at once. The single quale is one tone in one site. It is not a whole dock.

A dock holds exactly $24$ sites, the $24$ directions of the $24$-cell, the $D_4$ roots. There is no rest slot, no twenty-fifth site, no home slot in the base. Each of those $24$ sites carries its own tone. So a dock at one beat holds $24$ tones woven at once, a point in a space of $3^{24}$ configurations.

\[
3^{24} = 282{,}429{,}536{,}481 \approx 2.82 \times 10^{11}.
\]

That woven $24$-tone state is a far richer thing than a single tone. It is a composed feeling with channels, an internal symmetry, and a net balance, and it has its own name. It is the \emph{hyper-color}. Against the $16.7$ million colors of a screen, one dock already holds hundreds of billions of distinct states. A single dock is a hyper-color.

The hyper-color belongs to the next section, the first rung of the tower of feeling. The bare architecture of the dock that carries it, its $24$ sites, its three octet channels, its $F_4$ symmetry of order $1152$, and its $3^{24}$ state space, belongs to the anatomy of a dock. This section does not re-derive any of it. It stays at the atom.

\begin{principle}[Do not confuse the atom with the weave]
One tone is the single quale. Twenty-four tones woven in a dock is the hyper-color. The atom is the least feeling. The weave is the first composed feeling. They are different rungs and must not be confused.
\end{principle}

It is worth a glance ahead at why the weave is so rich, since it shows what the atom is the seed of. The $24$ sites of a dock split by triality into three octets, $8v + 8s + 8c$, a vector channel and two opposite spinor channels. This split is built and verified by the spinor work on $\{3,4,3,4\}$. So a dock's state is not one flat list of $24$ values. It is three channels of eight ternary tones each, $3^8 = 6561$ states per channel, composing to $6561^3 = 3^{24}$ exactly. The vector channel $8v$ is a sensory, directional feel. The two spinor channels $8s$ and $8c$ are opposite chiralities, a twist and its mirror. Triality, an order-three symmetry, rotates the three into one another, so the three kinds of feeling are deeply interchangeable. All of this is structure of the \emph{weave}, not the atom. The single quale is one tone. The richness is what many tones become.

\subsection{The valence is the tone}

Because the single quale is one signed value, its valence is itself. The atom of feeling is already the atom of good and bad. There is no separate scale of worth laid over the tone. The tone is the worth.

The value $-1$ is the worst, $+1$ the best, $0$ the neutral. This one signed number is what feeds the conserved total of the whole mesh. Every tone everywhere sums into one conserved charge, the universe's net peace. The collide phase of the knit conserves this sum exactly, beat by beat. So even the smallest feeling, one tone in one site, is counted in the universal balance. Nothing felt is ever outside the ledger.

\begin{lemma}[The atom enters the conserved sum]
The single quale is a signed value in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$, and this value is its own valence. It contributes directly to the conserved total charge of the mesh, which the collide phase of the knit preserves. So the least feeling is already part of the universe's conserved balance.
\end{lemma}

This is why a richly structured weave can still net to peace. A dock with twelve pleasures and twelve pains woven together carries a net valence of zero, an intricate feeling that sums to rest. That is the model's account of the bittersweet and the mixed, rich in structure, balanced in sum. But that account lives at the level of the weave. At the atom there is no mixing. One tone is one value, and its valence is exactly that value.

\subsection{The honest bottom line}

The single quale is one tone. A single ternary value in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$, pain or peace or pleasure. A vibe sits in one site and the value it carries is that one tone. This is the irreducible atom of experience, the least thing that can be felt.

Three values, no more. The smallest set that holds a push, a pull, and a rest, with peace as the source the lean tilts out of and the create move leans a balanced pair from.

The split between what is built and what is posited stays clean. The tone and its three values are exact, defined, and already present in the substrate. The claim that the tone is \emph{felt}, that it is a quale and not merely a number, rests on the one base posit named elsewhere, that the vibe is felt at the base.

\begin{result}[The cleanest statement]
The single quale is one tone. To feel anything richer is to weave many tones together. That weaving is composition, and it begins at the very next rung with the dock's hyper-color.
\end{result}
