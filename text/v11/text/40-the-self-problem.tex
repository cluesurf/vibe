\section{The Self Problem}

A self is a knot in the vibe that stays itself. It persists across countless beats. It sheds a disturbance into the bath. It heals when struck. The base is the eight atoms, the mesh, the dock, the site, the tone, the lean, the knit, the wake, and the beat, and nothing more. So the first question about a self is honest and sharp.

\begin{principle}[The self question]
Does a self emerge from the pure knit, the collide-then-stream law on the eight atoms, or does it not?
\end{principle}

The answer has two halves, and stating both keeps the account honest. \textbf{Identity and agency emerge.} A bound, self-repairing \textbf{body does not come from the eight atoms alone}. This section poses that problem cleanly. It states the obstruction. It reports the honest null. It gives the discrete-self findings that pin down exactly where each piece of the self lives. It then names the one missing ingredient, a short-range attraction.

That ingredient is the active vacuum itself. \textbf{The body binds itself}, by the shadow of its own absence in the active vacuum, with no new field and the bulk law reversible. The base stays exactly the eight atoms. The attraction is emergent. Committing nothing into the base makes the self-from-the-base claim stronger.

\begin{result}[The binding is emergent, not base]
The self closes at the eight atoms. The binding is read off the lean's own active vacuum as a collective shadow pressure. No new field is added, and the bulk law stays reversible. The full mechanism is the next section, How a Self Holds Itself Together.
\end{result}

\subsection{The obstruction}

A self wants two contradictory things from one local knit.

\begin{itemize}
\item A \textbf{wall} to bind it, so the knot stays localized.
\item A \textbf{window} to radiate, so a disturbance can leave for the bath.
\end{itemize}

No single reversible local knit on the tone is both. This is not for lack of effort. It is a real obstruction, traced through every candidate knit one by one. Each candidate either holds the body and seals the radiation channel, or opens the channel and lets the body erode.

\begin{principle}[Bind and radiate look locally contradictory]
A single local knit that turns a charge back, and so binds, seals the radiation channel. A single local knit that lets a charge go, and so radiates, erodes the body. No one local knit on the tone does both.
\end{principle}

The lean's create move, which is the active vacuum, does bind a free body. That is a real positive. Its net charge stays small and localized. But seen as a local pinning, pinning is exactly what seals the disturbance. The momentum-conserving knit radiates but does not bind. So a single local knit gives binding without a radiating body, or radiation without a body to hold.

The resolution turns the obstruction inside out. The body does not bind by a local wall at all. It binds by the \textbf{shadow it casts in the active vacuum}, a collective pressure that pulls its pieces home while staying transparent to the waves it sheds. That is bind-by-well, made reversible. The full account is the next section. The rest of this section is the problem it answers.
