\section{Determinism and Freedom}

The knit is fully deterministic. There are no dice anywhere in the base. Each beat the tones in every dock collide by a fixed table, then stream one dock along their sites, and the same input yields the same output every time. At first reading this looks like a cage. Every event is fixed in advance, every choice already settled, every self a puppet walking a pre-written script. This section argues that the reading is mistaken, and that the mistake is a confusion of two different ideas that happen to share a word.

\begin{principle}[Determined is not predestined]
Being determined is not the same as being predestined. Freedom was never the opposite of being determined. Freedom is the opposite of being coerced and the opposite of being random.
\end{principle}

Coercion and randomness are the two ways a choice can fail to be yours. A coerced choice is forced from outside, against the grain of who you are. A random choice is forced from nowhere, with no author at all. Between those two failures sits a real third thing. A choice that is fully caused, authored from within, and unknowable until it is lived. The rest of this section locates that third thing inside the eight atoms. It adds nothing to the base. It only reads the model from the inside.

\subsection{Determined yet not predestined}

Two facts pry the words apart. Determinism is a statement about how one beat follows from the last. Predestination is a stronger claim, that the whole future already exists somewhere, finished, waiting to be read off. The knit gives the first. It does not give the second, and two features of the mesh are why.

\subsubsection{No finished total state}

The mesh grows without end. Fresh peace docks are born at the wake every beat, at the open and ever-expanding frontier. So no single moment ever holds all the information of all of time. The total state is never complete, because there is always more mesh tomorrow than there is today.

\begin{proposition}[No total state to read off]
The mesh is unbounded and grows at the wake every beat. Therefore at no beat does any configuration contain the data of all later beats. A future that exists nowhere in advance cannot be looked up, cannot be read off, and cannot be the script a puppet walks.
\end{proposition}

This is the structural reason predestination fails. Predestination requires a finished book of all events. The mesh writes that book one line at a time at its growing edge, and the later lines are simply not yet written. There is no place where they sit in advance.

\subsubsection{No shortcut to the future}

The second fact is computational irreducibility. For a universal computing system there is in general no closed form for the far future that runs faster than the system itself. You cannot skip ahead. The only machine that can compute the next ten thousand beats is the mesh, running ten thousand beats.

\begin{theorem}[Computational irreducibility]
For a system capable of universal computation there is in general no algorithm that predicts its state at beat $n$ in fewer than order $n$ steps. The system is its own fastest simulator. The far future is therefore caused but not forecastable, determined as it happens rather than known before it happens.
\end{theorem}

Put the two facts together. The future is caused, since each beat follows from the last by the knit. It is not pre-written, since there is no finished total state. And it is not forecastable, since there is no shortcut faster than living it. The future is determined as it happens, not sitting there in advance.

\begin{principle}[Determined by the whole, opaque to the part]
Apparent randomness is the local view. Every event is fixed by the whole mesh it sits in, but no local part holds enough of the mesh to predict it. An event is determined by the whole and opaque to the part.
\end{principle}

\subsection{Determined yet self-authored}

A self is an emergent thing, a bound and self-correcting knot woven across many docks over many beats. The knit is reversible and has no attractors, so a self does not sit in a stable well that holds it in place for free. It persists only by doing work, continually re-knitting its own pattern against the churn of the bulk. This is the same reason memory costs work. To keep a pattern is to fight erasure, and fighting erasure spends effort.

A choice is that self resolving an urge. An urge is a pull that arrives through perception from a deeper, slower part of the mesh, and a choice is the self gathering that pull into one definite next act. Four things are true of the resolving at once, and together they are the definition of freedom.

\subsubsection{The compatibilist core}

The four facts reconcile two things long thought to be at war, full causation and real authorship. The key is to see that authorship was never the absence of cause. It is a particular kind of cause, a cause that loops back through the self.

\begin{principle}[Free means caused by you]
Free does not mean uncaused. Free means caused by you.
\end{principle}

The puppet's strings are real. The self is fully caused by its own past tones and the urges that reach it. But the strings are attached to the self's own integrated structure, not to a hand outside it. When the self models itself and steers itself, the causal loop closes. The self is caused by the self. That closed loop in the dynamics is what the phrase ``I decided'' names. It is not a gap in the causation. It is a particular shape the causation takes.

\begin{definition}[Self-authored choice]
A choice is \emph{self-authored} when the cause of the next act runs through the self's own integrated model of itself. The self is both the thing acted on and the thing doing the acting, so the causal arrow closes into a loop. Authorship is this loop, not an exception to cause.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Self-opacity}

The self cannot read its own next move off in advance, and neither can anything else. This is the irreducibility of Table~\ref{tab:four-facts-freedom}, now seen from the inside. There is no shortcut to the resolution faster than living it, so the choice is surprising even to its author.

This is not a gap in the self's knowledge, a fact it could in principle look up if only it were cleverer or had more data. It is structural. The only machine that computes the self's resolution is the self, running forward. Any faster machine would be a smaller machine computing a larger one, which is impossible. So the resolution arrives only when it arrives.

\begin{result}[Opaque even to its author]
Because the self is its own fastest simulator, no process, including the self itself and including the whole universe, can know the self's resolution without letting the self resolve. A choice is therefore one definite path, fully authored, and unknowable until it happens. Surprising even to the one who makes it.
\end{result}

\subsection{The will, finite and spendable}

The will is a directed pump. It is a sustained push that biases the self's resolution against the local gradient, a way of leaning toward a chosen target even when the easy slope leans the other way. The will is not an exception to the knit. It is an input the knit respects, a bias the self holds while it re-knits itself each beat.

The will was measured. A self with an active pump was put through three situations, and its behavior was read off the dynamics rather than assumed.

The third row is the telling one. The capacity to push against the gradient runs out, and when it does the self snaps back to the greedy choice at a sharp threshold. So the will is not a free and limitless veto. It is a finite, spendable, directed capacity, which is the texture of real willpower as it is actually felt and spent.

\begin{result}[The will is a finite pump]
A directed bias against the local gradient, measured in the dynamics, lets a self steer toward chosen targets and delay gratification until the bias is depleted, after which it relapses sharply. The will is therefore a finite and spendable capacity inside the knit, not an exception to it.
\end{result}

\subsection{Novelty at the wake}

The mesh is never finished. Fresh peace docks are born at the wake every beat, whether or not there was ever a first one. The frontier is always open, always growing, always adding what was not there before.

So the future is not stored anywhere waiting to be retrieved. It is computed as it happens, at the wake. The novelty is real, not an illusion of ignorance, because the information that would fix the far future does not yet exist in any moment. There is nothing to be ignorant of, because the thing to know has not been written.

\begin{principle}[Written at the edge]
A future that exists nowhere in advance cannot be the script a puppet walks. It is written, beat by beat, at the wake.
\end{principle}

\subsection{Why it matters}

A deterministic base does not abolish freedom. It locates it. The mistake was to set freedom against determinism, when its true opposites were coercion and randomness all along.

\begin{result}[Freedom located]
Freedom is the integrated self computing its own resolution, unknowable in advance even to the universe, and yours. One path, but authored, surprising even to its author.
\end{result}

Freedom is not a new base thing. It is not a ninth atom smuggled in beside the eight. It is what the eight atoms, the mesh, the dock, the site, the tone, the lean, the knit, the wake, and the beat, do when a self grows integrated enough to fold back on itself and pump against its own gradient. The familiar image of a landscape of valleys, where a choice rolls toward the deepest well, is a way to picture the self weighing its options. It is not a claim about the reversible microdynamics, which has no valleys and no attractors. The weighing is an emergent shape, the microdynamics is the substrate it runs on.
