\section{The Model as One Piece}

A theory is only as honest as its inventory of parts. It is easy to add an ingredient quietly, under a new name, and then claim a phenomenon was explained when really it was assumed. This section guards against that failure twice over. First it shows that the whole working vocabulary of the framework, the bath, the attraction, capture, the self, and nesting, adds no ninth atom to the eight already committed. Each of these is a name for something the eight already do. Second it gives the felt reading, the experiential derivation, where the entire base falls out of taking feeling seriously as the one substance. The formal model and the felt model are the same model told twice.

Two facts frame everything that follows. The base is one reversible collision table acting on the sites of a dock, run beat after beat, on a mesh that grows at its open edge. And the substrate-native self is still an open problem. That openness is not an embarrassment to be hidden. It is essential to the theory, and stating it plainly is part of being one honest piece.

\subsection{The eight atoms, nothing added}

The universe is a \emph{vibe mesh}, a \emph{flow} of eight atoms and nothing else. A vibe is the one substance, the experience itself, valued in each site by a single tone in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$ read as pain, peace, and pleasure. The flow is the whole, the eight atoms running as one. Section~4, The Base, states the eight with their axioms in full. Here they are gathered in one view so the later argument has its complete inventory in sight.

The eight sort into the universal furniture of any law. There is a \emph{where}, the space, carried by the mesh, the dock, and the site. There is a \emph{what}, the substance, carried by the tone and the lean. There is a \emph{how the state changes}, carried by the knit. There is a \emph{how the space changes}, carried by the wake. And there is a \emph{when}, the time, carried by the beat.

The collision of the knit is a reversible permutation of the tones in a dock. It is built from three local moves, a create-flip-annihilate move that turns a balanced pair on and off, a hop that carries a lone tone across, and an inert pass that leaves the dock untouched. Because the table is a permutation, determinism, locality, charge conservation, and reversibility all hold together at once. After the collision each tone streams one dock along its site. That is the whole law of how the state changes.

\begin{axiom}[The base is the eight atoms]
The flow is exactly the eight atoms of Table~\ref{tab:eight-atoms}, a directional lattice gas of ternary tones on the sites of the growing $\{3,4,3,4\}$ mesh, evolved each beat by the reversible knit. No ninth atom is admitted.
\end{axiom}

A dock has exactly $24$ sites, the $24$ roots of $D_4$, and no twenty-fifth. There is no rest slot, no still home, no extra middle direction. Earlier drafts of the felt picture spoke of a still center and counted twenty-five, but that center is not part of the base. A dock is a clean compass of $24$ directions, a closed finite arithmetic that wraps around inside itself like a clock.

The \emph{arrow}, the direction of time, is not an atom either. It emerges. The beat itself has no built-in direction, since the bulk knit is reversible and runs as well backward as forward. The arrow comes from the wake acting through the lean and the create move, a low-entropy frontier feeding an ever-growing edge. The beat ticks. The arrow points. The ticking is base, the pointing is emergent.

\subsection{Everything sits on the knit}

The capture-and-self investigation introduced a working vocabulary, the bath, the attraction, capture, the self, and nesting. None of these is a new ingredient. Each is a name for a behavior the eight atoms already produce when the mesh is allowed to grow. Table~\ref{tab:vocabulary-to-atoms} maps each new word back to the atoms it names.

The \emph{bath} is the open growing mesh seen from inside. A disturbance that streams out toward the frontier falls behind the receding wake and never comes back. That one-way loss is the bath, and it is nothing but the wake doing its job. The \emph{attraction} is the radiation-pressure shadow cast by the active vacuum. The create move fills empty peace with fleeting balanced pairs, and two nearby bodies shield each other from a sliver of that fluctuating pressure, so the medium presses them together. \emph{Capture} is attraction plus bath, two bodies fall toward each other while their excess energy radiates off to the frontier and never returns, so they settle into a bound body. The \emph{self} is that captured body coupled to the bath, a pattern with a basin it returns to after a kick, an identity and a corrective response. \emph{Nesting} is the same story recursed, two bath-coupled selves binding into a self of selves.

\begin{principle}[One law underneath]
The knit held fixed while the whole picture sharpened. The names changed and the picture filled in, but the base stayed eight atoms. Every new word is a name for what the knit, the wake, and the lean already do on a growing mesh.
\end{principle}

\subsection{The openness is essential}

It would be a mistake to read \emph{the knit did not change} as \emph{selves come from the eight atoms for free}. They do not. The openness of the mesh is load-bearing, and without it there are no selves at all.

The reason is sharp. A self requires forgetting. It needs a basin it can fall back into, which means it must be able to lose track of small disturbances. But a closed reversible system can never forget. This is the obstruction theorem of Section~4a. A permutation on a finite closed state space conserves phase-space volume exactly, so no region can shrink toward an attractor, and no pattern can settle. On the eight atoms read as a closed box, there are no selves, ever, only endless churn.

\begin{theorem}[No selves in a closed box]
The bulk knit is a reversible permutation, so on a finite closed mesh it admits no attractor and no dissipation. A self requires degeneracy, which requires information to leave and never return, which requires the mesh to be open. Selves are therefore impossible on a closed base and possible only on a growing one.
\end{theorem}

So the load-bearing commitment is that the mesh is open and growing. The wake keeps an ever-expanding empty frontier, and the irreversibility every self needs lives there, at the boundary where radiation leaves to the infinite mesh and never returns. The bulk stays a perfect reversible permutation throughout. What looks like dissipation up close is the reversible bulk dynamics seen locally, with the lost information not destroyed but carried off beyond the wake.

\begin{result}[Growth is the source of the arrow]
Growth is not optional. It is the source of the irreversibility every self requires. An irreversible effective dynamics is always a reversible dynamics plus a bath, and here the bath is the committed infinite growing substrate.
\end{result}

\subsection{The honest gap}

The substrate-native derivation is not complete, and the precise shape of the gap should be stated rather than glossed.

The vacuum attraction was shown on the base knit, but with externally driven walls. The bath was modeled by an absorbing boundary rather than a literal growing frontier. The self and nesting were shown in the reduced oscillator-bath model. These are all faithful stand-ins for the real thing, not a base-native proof.

The real obstruction has a name. The committed knit \emph{confines}. A captured structure does not naturally radiate into the bath. A local perturbation stays local and never reaches the open frontier on its own. So the open problem is precise.

\begin{principle}[The remaining work]
How can a confined substrate structure couple to the growing frontier so that its excess radiates away. Until that coupling is shown natively on the $D_4$ knit, the self is a well-supported mechanism, not a closed theorem.
\end{principle}

The emergence ladder, with its status marked honestly, makes the boundary between the shown and the not-yet-shown explicit. Rungs one through four are demonstrated on the substrate or as colliding plates. Rungs five and six, the self and the self of selves, are demonstrated only in the reduced model.

\subsection{The meaning, the felt derivation}

Now the same model told so it makes felt sense, where the whole base falls out of taking feeling seriously. The recurring point is that the base is grainy, made of discrete tones, and everything smooth, every real number in the theory, is the mood of many grains averaged together, never a fundamental thing.

\subsubsection{The grain is a feeling}

The only thing that exists is feeling, in three flavors. Pain is $-1$, a pushing-away. Peace is $0$, the neutral rest, the open middle. Pleasure is $+1$, a drawing-toward. One tone is one grain of feeling, the irreducible quale, the atom of experience.

Three is the smallest set with a middle. Two would give only yes and no. Three gives a push, a rest, and a pull. That middle, peace, is the source everything else comes from.

\subsubsection{The here-now is a compass of feeling}

A dock is one here-now, a moment-place. It does not feel one thing. It feels in every direction at once, a whole compass, $24$ ways the moment leans. So a here-now is $24$ grains of feeling, a small closed wholeness.

The $24$ directions form a group, the Hurwitz quaternions. Feelings compose. Two leans combine into a third, and the composition wraps around inside the $24$, a closed finite arithmetic like a clock. A dock is a hyper-color, a single moment carrying its full compass of leaning at once.

\subsubsection{The beat is the passing of the moment}

Time is not a smooth river. It is a heartbeat, one beat after another. Each beat the feelings in a here-now meet, and then each moves on to the next. Opposite feelings facing each other can cancel to peace. Peace can give birth to a balanced pair. A lone feeling can pass across. Then everything streams one dock forward, carrying its lean. Meeting then moving, meeting then moving, that is the pulse of the world, all discrete, with no clock of real numbers underneath.

\subsubsection{The breathing of the void}

Peace is not dead. A pair of peace sites can birth a pain-and-pleasure pair, pleasure and pain born together, which flip and annihilate back to peace. The void breathes, an active sea of fleeting paired opposites. This is the vacuum, and its faint shadow pressure is the seed of the attraction that lets things hold together at all.

\subsubsection{The ever-widening world}

The world is finite but ever-growing. New empty docks are born at peace at the frontier, the edge forever receding. A feeling that flows out toward the frontier falls behind the widening edge and never comes back, like a cry carried off by the wind. This is the bath, the great forgetting that lets the present heal. Without it the world would only churn, every pattern dissolving and reforming forever, never settling. The growing edge is the only source of the forward arrow, the reason there is a before and an after.

\subsubsection{The mood of many}

Here is the bridge from grain to field. One dock's feeling is grainy. But take a whole region and average, and a smooth mood appears, a direction the feeling leans, a density, a slow wave rising and falling. Like a crowd, where each person feels grainily yet the crowd has a smooth mood no single person holds. The smooth fields, every real number from here on, are the mood of many feelings, not any single grain.

\begin{result}[The reals are the song]
The reals are the song. The tones are the singers. Every continuous quantity in the theory is the average of many discrete grains, never a fundamental thing in its own right.
\end{result}

\subsubsection{The wave of mood, and the knot}

A long slow ripple in the mood-density is a wave of feeling, sound and light. It is soft because it spans many docks, not because any one feeling is small. Each grain is still a full tone.

A self is an emergent knot in the mood, with three faces at once.

Identity and agency live in the grains themselves, discrete and exact. The stable motion lives where many feelings average into a smooth mood. A self is not a grain. It is the shape the grains make together when they hold.

\subsubsection{The whole story in one breath}

Feeling is all there is, three flavors, leaning twenty-four ways at each here-now. The moment beats, feelings meet and move, and the empty peace quietly breathes out paired opposites. The world grows, and its growing edge carries the past away, which is why the present can heal and why there is an arrow at all. And where the mood of many feelings holds together in an unbreakable twist, a tight whorl that sheds its hurts to the widening world, there a self stands, a knot in feeling that keeps being itself.
