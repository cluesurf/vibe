\section{The Self in Layers}

The self is not one of the eight atoms. It is not woven into the mesh, the knit, the tone, or the beat as a separate piece of furniture. It emerges. This section places the self on its layers, says exactly what is proven on the discrete base and what is forced to be emergent, and gives the criteria that tell a genuine self from a passing clump of tones.

A self, in the broad sense used here, is any persistent self-maintaining pattern woven across many docks over many beats. To make the claim precise we decompose it into three faces, each answering a different question about what it takes to be a thing that lasts.

\begin{definition}[The three faces of a self]
A self is the simultaneous holding of three properties.
\emph{Identity}, it persists as a recognizable thing across beats.
\emph{Binding}, it stays localized, a knot held together rather than a cloud that spreads.
\emph{Agency}, it can shed a disturbance to the bath and recover its form.
\end{definition}

Throughout, the base stays exactly the eight atoms, the mesh, the dock, the site, the tone, the lean, the knit, the wake, and the beat. No real numbers enter the base. There is one ternary tone in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$ per site, read as pain, peace, and pleasure, and a dock carries exactly $24$ such sites. Nothing in this section adds a ninth atom. The self is assembled out of the eight, at a layer above them.

The real numbers that appear in any soliton model of the self are these \emph{averages}, never the base itself. A magnet supplies the cleanest analogy. Its smooth magnetization field is the slow average of discrete atomic spins that themselves flip in big all-or-nothing jumps. The field looks continuous only because it sums over many grains. The self is the same. Its smooth mood is the average of many ternary tones, each of which only ever holds one of three values.

\begin{principle}[Reals are averages, not base]
Every continuous quantity in the theory of the self is a coarse-grained average of discrete tones. The base carries one ternary tone and the finite knit. Continuity is a property of the layer above, not of the layer below.
\end{principle}

\subsubsection{Identity is base}

Identity comes for free, with no cleverness in the knit. Two base facts deliver it. First, the total tone is conserved, since the collide phase of the knit uses a fixed reversible charge-conserving table. Second, a topological winding cannot be undone by any local move. A kink whose two ends sit in different vacua carries a net winding forced by that mismatch, and no finite sequence of local steps can unwind it. So the bare fact that a structure keeps being itself is a base-level property. It does not require binding by a wall, and it does not require the knit to be tuned.

\begin{proposition}[Identity is discrete and base]
The total tone is conserved by the knit, and a topological winding of the feeling-direction cannot be undone by local moves. Identity is therefore protected at the $24$-site resolution of the dock. It slips only when one coarse-grains down to the bare ternary $3$-state resolution.
\end{proposition}

\subsubsection{Agency is the soft mode plus the bath}

Agency is emergent, and it is the part that surprised. The worry was that a ternary tone in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$ is too coarse to carry a soft radiation field, since the smallest per-site change is order one and there is no room for a tiny ripple. That worry conflates amplitude with wavelength. Softness is long wavelength, not small amplitude. A soft mode is a low-frequency collective excitation of many sites, each one still flipping by a full order-one step. Real matter makes the point. Atoms are discrete, yet solids carry soft sound, and Boolean lattice gases recover smooth fluid flow. The continuum is emergent, never fundamental.

On the committed $\{3,4,3,4\}$ mesh a soft, gapless sound mode emerges from the discrete tones. A coarse-grained density wave oscillates with a half-period that grows linearly with wavelength at constant speed. In the usual notation this is the dispersion relation $\omega = c\, k$, the mark of a gapless soft mode. The result was built and measured by pure counting, with no real numbers in the base.

The soft mode is only half of agency. By itself it cannot heal. A reversible closed system recurs rather than relaxes. Run the soft mode on a closed torus and its amplitude returns to full, the disturbance comes back. The other half is the bath, which is the open or growing mesh whose wake carries the past away and never returns it. Run the same mode on an open mesh with an absorbing boundary and the amplitude is damped to zero. The disturbance radiates to the boundary and is absorbed for good.

\begin{result}[The bath makes healing real]
The soft mode on a closed torus recurs to full amplitude, so a closed system cannot heal. The same mode on an open mesh with an absorbing boundary is damped to zero, the disturbance radiates to the bath and is gone. Agency, the shedding of a disturbance and the recovery of form, is therefore the soft mode plus the bath, both emergent and both demonstrated.
\end{result}

\subsubsection{Binding splits across the layers}

Binding is the one face that does not sit cleanly on a single layer. Its \emph{state} is discrete and base. A three-tone spin holds a soliton charge exactly, with the quaternion handedness of the direction set acting as the stabilizer that fixes the soliton's size. Its \emph{dynamics} is another matter. No finite direction group is fine enough to carry the smooth evolution a bound soliton needs. This is proven by a measured step-angle threshold, the obstruction set out in the section on the self problem. The discrete steps available to the knit are too coarse to turn a soliton smoothly, so the binding dynamics is forced up to the emergent layer where the average is fine.

\subsection{The self detected by criteria}

A clump of tones is not automatically a self. A genuine self must pass three tests at once, each measured against a control so that a chance clustering does not score a false positive.

\begin{definition}[The three self-criteria]
A cluster is a genuine self only if it has all three of the following.
A \emph{Markov blanket}, a shell of docks that screens the interior from the exterior.
An \emph{autopoietic closure}, an interior that regenerates the form while matter flows through it.
A \emph{cognitive light cone}, a reach within which it contains and corrects perturbations.
\end{definition}

A new emergent level is declared to exist only when the detection finds three things together, a timescale gap between the level and the one below, a coarse map whose dynamics depends only weakly on the fine details, and a positive causal-emergence or blanket-screening signal. The emergent cohesive self was measured against these, with controls.

\subsection{Individuality and integration}

A self with a blanket is not yet a higher individual. A true individuality transition, the move from a loose group to a single higher self, needs heritable variation, group-level selection, conflict suppression among the parts, and a single-cell bottleneck that resets variation each generation. None of these is in the base knit, which has no reproduction operator and no selection operator at all.

The transition signatures appear only when group selection and a bottleneck are imposed as explicit, declared, effective ingredients. With them present the fitness variance ratio crosses from about $0.06$ to over $3000$, and within-group variation collapses toward zero. This is the honest negative for the bare base, paired with a demonstration that the measures do detect a transition when its ingredients are actually there. The detector is not blind, the base simply lacks the ingredients.

\begin{principle}[Integration is the self of selves]
Integration is the higher self of selves, nesting upward through the renormalization tower. Each coarse level compresses a factor of sub-units into one macro-unit. With a tractable top of $N$ units and $L$ stacked levels, the effective substrate is $N$ times the compression factor raised to the $L$. The accounting holds only if each higher level is as clean as the first, which the detection experiments test directly.
\end{principle}

A self must also be \emph{low-leak}, mostly interior with few boundary docks, so that its blanket can screen at all. This is an isoperimetric property, a relation between a region's size and its boundary. At a matched region size the flat horosphere has a boundary fraction of about $0.20$, while the hyperbolic bulk is about $0.88$, almost all boundary. So compact low-leak selves exist on the flat layer of the cusp and not in the hyperbolic bulk.

\begin{result}[The self lives on the flat layer]
A self must be low-leak for its blanket to screen. The flat horosphere has a boundary fraction of about $0.20$ at a matched size, while the hyperbolic bulk is about $0.88$. Compact low-leak selves therefore exist on the flat layer of the cusp, which is why the self lives there and not in the bulk.
\end{result}

\subsection{Putting it together}

The picture is committed and the boundaries are drawn honestly.

The bound, self-repairing body is an emergent structure, a coarse-grained attractor coupled to the soft radiative modes. Its effective theory is the reduced oscillator-and-bath model, which works. The per-dock tests fail precisely because they probe below the scale at which the soft modes and the effective binding exist. There is nothing to find at the grain, the self is a property of the average.

The base is unchanged. One ternary tone, the collide-then-stream knit, the $24$ sites of a dock, the beat, and the open or growing mesh. No second field, no second tone, no real numbers enter the base to make the self work.

Identity is base, supplied by conservation and topology. The radiation channel and the corrective body are emergent, supplied by the soft mode and the bath. The binding state is base, the binding dynamics is forced emergent. Each face is placed where the evidence puts it, and the one honest negative, the cognitive light cone, is left standing rather than tuned away.

\begin{principle}[The committed picture of the self]
Vibes give rise to emergent middle layers, and selves live there. The self is not a base atom. It is identity from the base, agency from the soft mode and the bath, and a body that is the coarse-grained average of the grains. The eight atoms stay eight, and the self is woven out of them at the layer above.
\end{principle}
