\begin{abstract}

This paper proposes one discrete, local, deterministic substrate for reality and reads it both as physics and as experience. Reality is a growing crystal of feeling. We call the whole thing a \emph{vibe mesh}, a flow of eight atoms. Everything that follows is built from those eight and nothing else.

Space is a discrete hyperbolic honeycomb, the regular tessellation $\{3,4,3,4\}$. It is finite at any beat and forever growing. We call the whole weave the \emph{mesh}. Each here-now in it is a \emph{dock}, what the simulation code calls a cell. Out of every dock run exactly $24$ \emph{sites}, the directed slots along which substance moves. These $24$ directions are the $D_4$ root system, the $24$-cell. There is no rest slot and no twenty-fifth site. The flat cubic cusp of this honeycomb is ordinary three-dimensional space. Time is the growth itself, counted in \emph{beats}.

On each site sits one \emph{tone}, a ternary value in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$, read as pain, peace, pleasure. This is the only substance, the single quale of the theory. The tone is the value, the site is its bearer, and the experience the value names is the vibe. A whole dock of $24$ tones is one hyper-color. The tones obey a fixed order, $-1 < 0 < +1$, which we call the \emph{lean}. The lean is what gives charge, the signed sum of tones, and it sets the direction of the create move. Matter is the tone bound into a knot. Light is the tone waving. There is no second stuff.

\begin{definition}[The eight atoms]
The base of the theory is exactly eight atoms, each a four-letter word starting with a distinct letter, so each is a clean single-symbol variable. They are the \emph{mesh} ($m$, the whole honeycomb), the \emph{dock} ($d$, a here-now), the \emph{site} ($s$, one of the $24$ directions), the \emph{tone} ($t$, the ternary value), the \emph{lean} ($l$, the value order), the \emph{knit} ($k$, the state law), the \emph{wake} ($w$, the growing edge), and the \emph{beat} ($b$, the discrete step). The eight running as one are the \emph{flow}, written $F$.
\end{definition}

The law of how the state changes is the \emph{knit}, a directional lattice gas of two phases, collide then stream. In the collide phase the $24$ tones in a dock interact by one fixed table. In the stream phase each tone moves one dock along its site. The knit is reversible and charge-conserving, and the bulk runs backward exactly as cleanly as it runs forward.

\begin{principle}[Where the arrow comes from]
The arrow of time is not a base atom. It enters only at the open, growing edge of the mesh, the \emph{wake}. New docks wake into being at peace at the frontier, and outbound disturbance falls behind the wake and never returns. This low-tone frontier, acting through the lean, is the one source of asymmetry. The arrow and the attraction are both emergent, never put in by hand.
\end{principle}
