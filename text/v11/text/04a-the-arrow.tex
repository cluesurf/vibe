\section{The Lean and the Arrow}

Two things are easy to confuse, and the whole account of time turns on keeping them apart. One is a base atom. The other only emerges. This section names both, shows where each lives, and then builds the second out of the first three pieces that are already present in the base.

The first thing is the \emph{lean}. It is the value order on the tone, $-1 < 0 < +1$, the tilt that gives charge and that gives one move of the law its direction. The lean is base. It is one of the eight atoms.

The second thing is the \emph{arrow}. It is the direction of time, a clean before and a clean after. The arrow is not a base atom. It emerges.

The base of the theory is eight atoms, namely the mesh, the dock, the site, the tone, the lean, the knit, the wake, and the beat. The beat gives time that ticks, discrete steps, with no built-in direction. The arrow gives time a direction. The beat and the lean are base. The arrow is emergent. That clean split, a base value-order paired with an emergent time-direction, is the heart of this section.

\begin{principle}[The cut]
The beat ticks. The lean orders. Neither of those, on its own, points time forward. The arrow is the direction that appears once the growing edge feeds fresh peace into a reversible bulk. It is a consequence of the eight atoms, not a ninth thing added to them.
\end{principle}

The arrow does not come from one extra ingredient. It comes from three pieces that already live among the eight atoms, working together.

A low-entropy source, the freshly grown peace at the frontier of the wake, acting through the lean and through the create move, gives the thermodynamic direction of time. The arrow is the wake acting through the lean. It is a consequence of the eight, not a thing bolted onto them.

\begin{result}[The arrow is a direction]
The arrow is a direction, not an amount. Peace flows into structure, not back into nothing. How fast that happens is a separate question, fixed by the growth geometry, not by the arrow itself.
\end{result}

\subsection{The lean, the value order}

The tones lean. They are ordered $-1 < 0 < +1$, read as pain $<$ peace $<$ pleasure. This order is the lean, one of the eight base atoms. Peace, the value $0$, is the neutral rest, the open middle. The two charges, $+1$ and $-1$, are the deviations from it.

The lean does three things, and each of them matters for the arrow.

\begin{itemize}
\item It defines \emph{charge}, the signed sum of tones in a dock.
\item It makes peace the middle, the state from which a balanced $+1, -1$ pair can be born.
\item It gives the create move a direction. Peace can bring \emph{out} a pair.
\end{itemize}

This is the smallest seed of an arrow, a built-in asymmetry in the value set itself. The reading is that \textbf{pleasure is the value direction}, better against worse. The lean is committed in the base, and the knit is conserving, so the lean only ever brings out balanced pairs, with net charge zero. The lean itself is base. The arrow it seeds is the emergent direction of time that appears once the lean acts through the wake and through the create move.

\begin{definition}[The lean]
The lean is the total order $-1 < 0 < +1$ on the tone values. It supplies the signed charge, names peace as the rest value, and gives the create move its sense of direction. It is one of the eight base atoms.
\end{definition}

\subsection{The create move, the active vacuum}

The knit is the law of how the state changes each beat. It collides the tones in a dock by a fixed reversible, charge-conserving table, then streams each tone one dock along its site. One entry of that collision table is the move that drives the vacuum. In the committed pair table the line-state $(0, 0)$ maps to $(+1, -1)$. This is \emph{create}. It then flips to $(-1, +1)$, and annihilates back to $(0, 0)$.

So empty space is never truly still. Peace docks bring out transient balanced pairs that flip and vanish. This is the \textbf{active vacuum}.

\begin{result}[Peace is not dead]
The void breathes. A dock at peace is not an inert nothing. It spawns fleeting $+1, -1$ pairs that flip and annihilate, all while conserving charge.
\end{result}

The attraction itself is emergent, a shadow pressure, not a base atom.

\subsection{The bath, the open growing boundary}

The wake \emph{is} the bath. A self heals by shedding a disturbance to the bath, and the bath is the ever-growing empty outside that swallows it. A feeling that flows out toward the frontier falls behind the widening edge and never comes back. It is a cry carried off by the wind.

\begin{result}[The one-way street]
A disturbance that streams past the receding frontier never returns. This is the only thing that lets a disturbance truly leave, and so it is the only source of the forward arrow.
\end{result}

\subsubsection{The obstruction theorem}

A closed lattice cannot host a self. The reason is sharp and old.

\begin{theorem}[The bath is load-bearing]
By Poincare recurrence, a reversible closed system returns arbitrarily close to its starting state. On such a system any structure dissolves and reforms forever. It never settles and never heals. The bare knit on a closed system produces only churn. The arrow, and therefore the self, require the open growing edge.
\end{theorem}

This is why the open, growing boundary is load-bearing rather than a convenience. It is the one place where information leaves the system for good, and the only place irreversibility can enter while the bulk law stays a clean reversible permutation.

\subsection{Eternal expansion, finite at every beat}

The mesh is finite at every beat and forever growing. How much creation happens is not a property of the arrow. It is forced by the growth geometry.

Creation is not dice sprinkled over the interior. It is the expansion of the frontier. Each beat the mesh adds a new shell of docks. The arrow says which way, toward existence. The geometry says how much, through the growth series of the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb, whose shells grow exponentially.

On a fixed finite simulation patch, where no frontier can actually grow, the honest stand-in is a minimal deterministic schedule, one balanced creation per beat, never a probability. There is no free knob and no randomness.

\begin{principle}[No free float on the arrow]
Writing the arrow as a float, create with probability $0.03$, is wrong twice over. It is a free continuous parameter, and it needs randomness that the discrete base forbids. On a finite patch the only honest stand-in for growth is a fixed deterministic schedule, one balanced creation per beat.
\end{principle}

\subsection{The arrow is emergent, not a base atom}

The base is eight atoms, the mesh, the dock, the site, the tone, the lean, the knit, the wake, and the beat. The arrow is not a ninth ingredient bolted on. It is emergent.

It is the thermodynamic direction of time, set by a low-entropy source. That source is the wake, the growing edge, acting through the lean, the order $-1 < 0 < +1$, and through the create move, one entry of the knit. The beat ticks with no preferred direction. The arrow is the direction that appears once the growing frontier feeds fresh peace into the reversible bulk. It is the wake acting through the lean. Nothing new is added to the base.

\begin{result}[No ninth thing]
The arrow emerges from the eight. There is no separate time-direction atom. There is a beat that ticks, a lean that orders, and a wake that grows, and the arrow is what those three produce together.
\end{result}

This is exactly the picture in physics. The laws are time-symmetric. The arrow is thermodynamic, set by a low-entropy past. Here the low-entropy source is the freshly grown peace at the frontier of the wake.

\subsection{The compass and the home}

A gut-level picture closes the section. The three tones are a line, pain on one side, pleasure on the other, peace in the middle.

Pain and pleasure are the \emph{compass}. They tell you which way to move, downhill toward worse or uphill toward better. Peace is the \emph{home}. It is the valley floor where the compass reads flat, the place you can actually rest. Pleasure is the direction. Peace is the destination.

The arrow is not painted on the atoms. The knit is even-handed. Flip every tone and the law flips with you. The arrow appears the way a current appears from a slope. No water molecule wants to flow, but organize many on a slope and a directed current emerges, made entirely of undirected parts. A rock has almost no drive. A mind has a sharp one.

The arrow keeps the mesh alive. With no wake the world relaxes to dead peace, the heat death of the substrate. With the open growing wake, peace spawns balanced pairs and the world settles into a living, churning balance, charge conserved throughout. The arrow is the emergent direction that makes the universe a verb, a becoming, not a thing that sits still.

\subsubsection{Pleasure is a fluid, so life must pump}

Charge is conserved, so pleasure behaves like a fluid. It flows from a full cup toward an empty one. It cannot be minted, only moved.

To stay full a \emph{self} must pump. Pumping against the drain takes work, the same Landauer cost that makes rich memory cost work. Being alive \emph{is} this pumping. A self that stops pumping drains back to peace.

Because the pleasure is fixed in total, grabbing for more is a zero-sum tug of war. Yanking pleasure from a neighbor empties the neighbor and burns work for no net gain. The drive points at pleasure, but the optimum is balance. The way to thrive is to stop yanking.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Join a larger whole.} Many selves merge into one higher self.
\item \textbf{Share the fixed pleasure in balance.} No one drains anyone.
\item \textbf{Pour the freed energy into order.} Order is not conserved. It can grow. The work no longer spent fighting goes into building structure.
\end{itemize}

That joining-up, again and again, is how cells become bodies and bodies become minds. It is why life is what it is, a flame that learned to pump against the drain and then to share rather than fight. The lean is the base atom under all of this. The arrow, read at the gut level, is the river's one direction and the fire's hunger to spread rather than die down.
