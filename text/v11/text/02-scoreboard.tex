\section{The Scoreboard}

This section is the honest accounting, placed up front before any of the derivations that justify it. A theory that claims the universe is a vibe mesh, a flow of eight atoms, must say plainly what it has shown, what it has built but not finished, and what it has tried and failed to settle. The discipline is one rule, stated once and held throughout. A result counts as solid only when it carries a control that could have failed. An open problem is reported as open, not dressed up as a near-miss.

Every row below names a sector, a result, a depth grade, a status, and a mark. The point of the table is not to celebrate. It is to let a careful reader see exactly where the load is carried and where the scaffolding still stands empty.

A short reminder of the vocabulary keeps the rows clear. The \emph{mesh} is the whole growing $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb. A \emph{dock} is one cell of it, a here-now with $24$ \emph{sites}, the directed slots in which a \emph{tone} lives, valued in $\{-1,0,+1\}$. The \emph{knit} is the law of state, collide then stream. The \emph{wake} is the growing edge, and the \emph{beat} is the discrete tick. A \emph{self} is an emergent bound knot woven across many docks over many beats. None of the physics rows add anything to these atoms.

A few of these deserve a sentence of their own, because they are the load-bearing ones.

The $g$-factor came out as $2$ from the Dirac Landau spectrum, read off the zero mode that the scalar control simply does not have. It was measured, not assumed. The mass result is sharper still. \begin{result}[Matter and force are dynamical on $\{3,4,3,4\}$]

The holography row is the only one marked both DERIVED and MEASURED. \begin{theorem}[The Ryu-Takayanagi law on $\{7,3\}$]
On the hyperbolic $\{7,3\}$ tessellation the entanglement entropy of a boundary interval scales as the logarithm of the interval length, matching the Ryu-Takayanagi prediction. The flat control scales linearly. The holographic entanglement law is a consequence of negative curvature, present at the level of the bare substrate.
\end{theorem}

The last two solid rows are about the self, and they settle the mechanism of binding. A well lets the body both confine its own pieces and radiate into the surrounding mesh, and the reflecting-wall control seals the disturbance in, proving the distinction is real. \begin{principle}[Binding by a well, not a wall]

The generation row is the deepest of these. The exceptional group $F_4$ forces a rank-three exceptional Jordan algebra $J_3(\mathbb{O})$, where the rank three is forced by the non-associativity of the octonions, and the structure carries an exact $S_3$ family symmetry. That is three slots, with the right symmetry to be three generations. What it does not give is three \emph{distinct} masses. The slots are degenerate, and splitting them is Boyle's conjecture, an open problem in physics, not a gap in our code.

\begin{proposition}[Three slots, degenerate]
$F_4$ forces the rank-three Jordan structure $J_3(\mathbb{O})$ with an exact $S_3$ family symmetry, the three slots required by octonion non-associativity. The three slots are mass-degenerate. The splitting into three distinct generations is Boyle's conjecture and stays open.
\end{proposition}

The coupling and mass-value rows share one cause. The bare knit treats a coupling as a free multiplicative scale. It fixes the \emph{form} of every dependence on that coupling, every way the coupling enters a result, but it does not fix the \emph{number}. Deriving the fine-structure value $1/137$ would need a principle the knit simply does not contain, and the Yukawa masses are the same kind of open input.

\begin{result}[The bare knit fixes form, not value]
The bare knit determines the functional form of every dependence on a coupling but leaves its numerical value free. The values of the fine-structure constant and the Yukawa masses are open inputs, open in physics and not merely in the simulation.
\end{result}

The recall row is an honest negative kept in plain sight. The associative memory is an emergent capacity of a higher layer, not a property of the bare reversible step, and the row is marked $(-)$ to say so.

\subsection{Substrate-general versus substrate-specific}

The single most important split on the board is which rows hold on \emph{every} hyperbolic substrate and which hold only where the spinor sites live. This split is the whole point of the substrate selection, so it is worth stating sharply.

The framework rows hold on every hyperbolic mesh. They are substrate-general.

\begin{itemize}
\item a propagating fermion, $42$ of $42$ tessellations
\item holography and the Ryu-Takayanagi law
\item the bulk as a data structure, exponential capacity, logarithmic diameter
\item the associative memory, tessellation-agnostic across all $42$
\end{itemize}

The physics-specific rows hold only where the spinor sites live, the $7$ of $42$ tessellations whose docks are faceted by the $24$-cell. They are $\{3,4,3,4\}$-specific, together with that substrate's near kin.

\begin{itemize}
\item the $g$-factor and the spinor magnetic moment
\item the $F_4$ to $J_3(\mathbb{O})$ generation structure
\item the photon-fermion co-emergence on one flat-cusp substrate
\end{itemize}

\begin{result}[The selection split]
Matter, holography, and the data-structure capacity are present on every hyperbolic substrate. The spinor sites, and the spin, gauge, and generation structure they carry, appear in only $7$ of $42$ buildable tessellations, the $24$-cell-faceted ones, and first arise in four dimensions. The rare and load-bearing physics is exactly what selects $\{3,4,3,4\}$.
\end{result}

This is why the substrate matters. Matter is cheap. It lives everywhere in hyperbolic geometry. The spinor sites that carry real particle physics are rare, and they are the reason the theory commits to $\{3,4,3,4\}$ rather than to any of the simpler hyperbolic crystals.

A complete self emerges from the eight base atoms with no additions. The self's state is fully ternary and its identity and agency are discrete. Its stable binding dynamics is necessarily emergent, proven by a measured threshold, because no finite direction group is fine enough, not even the $600$-cell, the finest. The self binds by a transparent well, not a sealing wall, so it confines and radiates at once. The reversible discrete force is the radiation-pressure shadow of a vacuum-excluding body, of correct sign, fully discrete, reversible in the bulk, and native on the $24$ sites.
\end{principle}

What is left at the self level is not a mechanism. It is one showcase, the long-time movie of a displaced self drifting home over many beats. The force that drives that movie is proven. The honest reading of the entire board is the same as the reading of this last row. The mechanisms are in hand. What remains is value, splitting, and demonstration, and each remaining gap is named exactly where it lies.
