\section{Open Problems}

A model that hides its gaps cannot be trusted with its wins. This section is the honest list of what the theory has not yet done. None of the items below are buried in a footnote or dressed up as something they are not. Each is a concrete next step, named exactly, and each is a problem at the physics level rather than a flaw in the base.

The base itself is committed and closed. The universe is a vibe mesh, a flow of eight atoms. The mesh is the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb. A dock is one cell with exactly $24$ sites. A tone in $\{-1,0,+1\}$ sits in each site. The lean orders those values. The knit is the one local law, collide then stream, charge conserving and reversible. The wake is the growing edge. The beat is the discrete step. Nothing in the list that follows asks for a ninth atom. Every open problem is a downstream physical number or behavior that the eight atoms are meant to produce but that has not yet been read off in full.

\begin{principle}[The honesty discipline]
A result is reported as solid only when it has a control that could have failed. An open problem is reported as open rather than papered over. The strongest results in this theory came from knowing when not to claim, and the open list below is the other half of that same discipline.
\end{principle}

\subsection{The exact collision, and the numbers it gates}

The knit has two phases. Collide, where the tones in a single dock interact through a fixed reversible charge-conserving table. Stream, where each tone then moves one dock along its site. The collide phase is the knit's last free part. It is pinned by four demands at once, that it conserve charge, that it be reversible, that it be local, and that it respect the lattice symmetry of the dock.

What is not yet done is running that \emph{actual discrete collision} end to end to read absolute physical numbers off it. Many of the downstream results in this paper were obtained with emergent-layer tools, the continuous Dirac operator and spectral methods, rather than by stepping the discrete collision through a full dynamical simulation. This is not a gap in principle. One concrete discrete collision has been pinned and verified to conserve both charge and momentum, and it reproduces the Dirac dispersion end to end. So the method exists and has been exercised once.

\begin{result}[The blocking method is in hand]
Radial coarse-graining is the renormalization running, verified end to end. The one-dimensional decimation gives $K' = \tfrac{1}{2}\ln\cosh 2K$, which equals direct block-spin summation exactly with zero error. The flow runs to the Gaussian fixed point and the beta function is computed. The running is a checked method, not a claim.
\end{result}

The make-or-break frontier is to feed the actual four-dimensional collision, not the one-dimensional toy, through that same blocking, and to read the Standard Model beta coefficients straight off it. Deriving those coefficients needs the full dynamical simulation, not the group theory alone. The blocking machine is ready. The remaining step is to run it on the real collision.

\subsection{The coupling value}

The bare knit treats the coupling as a free multiplicative scale. It fixes the form of every dependence on the coupling but not its value. The radiated field follows a clean square law with no special or critical point, vanishing only at zero coupling. So the coupling enters purely as an overall scale, and the knit alone cannot say which scale.

\begin{principle}[The knit fixes mechanisms, not values]
Deriving $1/137$ needs a principle the knit does not contain. A fixed point, anomaly matching, or the full charged spectrum with its running. None of these is present in the bare knit. The model fixes the form of the coupling dependence and leaves the value open.
\end{principle}

The mass values have the same status. The mass \emph{mechanism} is measured. The Dirac mass is the coupling between the two chiralities, confirmed two agreeing ways, and it vanishes into a massless Weyl fermion when the two chiralities decouple. But the Yukawa \emph{values} are the same kind of open input as the coupling. The knit fixes the mechanism. It does not fix the number. This is partial by design, not hidden.

\subsection{Three generations and the splitting}

The exceptional structure forces a rank-three Jordan algebra, with three forced by octonion non-associativity. There is an exact $S_3$ family symmetry across the three slots. The count of three is therefore established on solid algebraic ground.

But the three slots are \emph{degenerate}. They carry the same mass.

\begin{definition}[Boyle's conjecture]
The splitting of the three exactly degenerate generation slots into three distinct masses is Boyle's conjecture. The count of three is settled by the algebra. The mechanism that lifts the degeneracy and separates the three masses is open.
\end{definition}

The family symmetry that forces three is itself a win, and it is reported as solid. The splitting that turns three equal slots into the observed mass hierarchy is the named open piece sitting on top of it.

\subsection{Curved-bulk gravity on the alternate substrate}

Clean three-dimensional Newtonian gravity is a $\{3,4,3,4\}$ result. It lives on the flat cusp of that substrate. This much is shown.

It does \emph{not} transfer cleanly to the alternate substrate $\{5,3,4\}$. That substrate's bulk is hyperbolic, so the potential is screened, and its cusp is flat two-dimensional, so the potential there is logarithmic rather than the inverse-distance Newtonian form. Two attempts to extract a clean gravity signal on $\{5,3,4\}$, one using the real-space Green's function and one using the spectral gap, did not discriminate cleanly at reachable sizes. The hyperbolic bulk is boundary-dominated, so the bulk signal is swamped by the boundary at every size we can reach.

\begin{result}[An honest non-measurement]
Curved-bulk gravity on $\{5,3,4\}$ did not measure cleanly. It is reported as open rather than as a failure. The measure-first discipline caught it before it became a false claim. The clean Newtonian result remains the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ flat-cusp result, exactly where it was demonstrated.
\end{result}

\subsection{Dynamical settling onto the cusp}

The cusp \emph{is} flat three-dimensional space, and matter on it is stable. Both are shown. What is not shown is that matter \emph{settles} onto the cusp from a generic start. This is the matter-condensation problem.

It was tested, and the result is an honest negative.

\begin{result}[Matter churns, it does not settle]
Running the actual mod-three wave knit on the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ bulk from a generic start, the activity stays uniform. The band ratio is about one. There is no concentration toward the cusp. Matter churns, it does not settle.
\end{result}

This is exactly what a single reversible conserving knit must do from an equilibrium start. No dissipation, no concentration. So settling needs special low-entropy initial conditions, the same low-entropy wake that supplies the arrow of time. The arrow is emergent, not base, and it rides on the wake as a low-entropy source acting through the lean. The base adds nothing to force settling on its own, so this stays an honest open gap. The candidate mechanism is identified. The dynamical demonstration is not yet in hand.

\subsection{Higher-order binding between selves}

A self is an emergent object, a bound self-correcting identity-bearing knot woven across many docks over many beats. A single self holds itself together by a transparent well and the radiation-pressure shadow of the active vacuum. That self-binding mechanism is in hand and is not a frontier.

What is not yet measured is clean binding \emph{between} units, the cross-gap attraction that would build super-selves and the nesting tower at the dynamical level. The same shadow pressure is the natural candidate. A body's shadow should pull other bodies too, and that pull is the attraction we call gravity, which is itself emergent rather than base. But on the curved bulk that signal did not yet measure cleanly, and matter does not yet condense from a generic start, so between-unit binding is the place to watch. It is the dynamical companion of the coarse-grained nesting tower, not yet demonstrated as a clean force between two separated bodies.

\subsection{The continuum limit}

The base is discrete and deterministic. The tone is ternary, the knit is a finite permutation, and there are no real numbers anywhere in the substrate. Continuity is only emergent. Lorentz invariance, the Lie groups, smooth gravity, and the de Sitter expansion law are all coarse-grained readings of the discrete substrate.

Showing the full continuum limit means showing that the discrete symmetry restores to the continuous one in the infrared across the whole tower of emergent layers. The discrete-to-continuous restoration is verified for the leading cases. It is established in pieces. The complete chain, the single proof that the whole tower restores at once, is a frontier.

\begin{principle}[Discreteness is base, continuity is emergent]
There are no real numbers in the base. Every continuous structure is a coarse-grained reading of the discrete substrate. The restoration of continuous symmetry from the finite knit is verified for the leading cases and open as one unbroken chain.
\end{principle}

\subsection{The finite-versus-infinite question}

Whether the universe has a true first beat or is eternal and beginningless is open, and it is deliberately left to the reader. Both stories are self-consistent under the knit. The knit is reversible in the bulk, so it neither demands a first beat nor forbids one. This is not a gap to be closed by more computation. It is a genuine fork that the base does not settle, and the theory says so rather than picking a side it cannot justify.
