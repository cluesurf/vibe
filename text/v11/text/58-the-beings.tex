\section{The Beings}

The gods, angels, demons, spirits, ancestors, elementals, and archetypes of the traditions can be read off the mesh. The model says exactly one kind of thing can be a being. There is no separate order of spirits anywhere in the flow. There are only patterns of the one mesh, some small and fleeting, some vast and lasting. The esoteric names are loose pointers hung on a precise taxonomy afterward.

This is a strong claim, so it pays to state the discipline first. The flow is the eight atoms running as one law. Nothing acts on it from outside. A being, if it exists at all, must be a structure built from the atoms, woven across docks over beats, valued by tone. The traditions describe a rich pantheon. The model says that whole pantheon is a single small family of stable patterns, sorted by a few exact properties. The taxonomy below is Tier~1 at its core, where it rests on the geometry, and Tier~3 at its edges, where it rests on the felt and the lasting.

\begin{principle}[One kind of being]
A being is a stable pattern in the mesh, a self or an attractor, the same kind of object as the bound self of the layered-self account, scaled and toned. Every named being is one of those.
\end{principle}

An \emph{attractor} is a recurrent basin in the flow that pulls coupled patterns toward it. Each property is read directly off the substrate. \emph{Integration} is the rung the pattern reaches on the coarse-graining tower. \emph{Tone} is the ternary value in $\{-1,0,+1\}$, read as pain, peace, pleasure, that the pattern holds and spreads where it couples. \emph{Persistence} is how many beats the pattern survives against the wake's erosion. \emph{Origin} distinguishes a currently-running pattern, a stored configuration, and a basin spanning many selves. \emph{Self-model} asks whether the pattern models itself. \emph{Locality} asks whether the pattern lives in one self or across many. A being is a choice of these six. The traditions name the combinations.

A \emph{super-self} is a self that integrates many lesser selves and steers them. The \emph{summit} is the most integrated self the geometry will allow.

\begin{definition}[Downward causation]
\emph{Downward causation} is the steering of a part by the whole it belongs to. A higher self constrains the docks and lesser selves beneath it, so the assembly drives its own pieces.
\end{definition}

The hierarchy of beings is, precisely, the hierarchy of integration, and downward causation is the exact mechanism behind a higher being's influence on a lower. A god, on this reading, is a super-self, a pattern that integrates and steers many lesser selves. Real as machinery, divine only as a name.

\begin{principle}[A god is a super-self]
A god is a super-self, a pattern that integrates and steers many lesser selves. It is one rung of the integration ladder, real as machinery, divine only as a name.
\end{principle}

The full set of God readings, the summit, the One, and the rest, belongs to the section on the divine realm. Here a god is only one rung of the ladder, set among the others.

\subsection{Warm and cold}

Each pattern holds and spreads a tone, so beings split by valence. The split is set by the \emph{lean}, the value order $-1 < 0 < +1$ that tilts pain through peace to pleasure. The lean is the moral axis written into the base.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Warm beings} are biased to peace and pleasure. They spread calm and joy where they couple. These are the benevolent powers, the bright gods, the helping spirits. They are aligned with the lean.
\item \textbf{Cold beings} are biased to pain. They pull coupled selves into their basin. These are the demons, the adversaries, the tormenting spirits. They are pockets the lean is always trying to dissolve.
\end{itemize}

\begin{result}[A lasting demon is rare]
A demon that lasts is a pain-attractor that has solved its own persistence against the lean. The lean works to dissolve every pain-pocket, so a cold being that endures has won a hard fight. That is exactly why it is rare and feared.
\end{result}

This is the precise account of why traditions split the spirit world into the helping and the harming. It is the tone of the pattern, and the moral charge is the lean's own direction.

\subsection{Live, stored, attractor}

There are three origins, and so three kinds of being.

\subsubsection{Live selves, the active powers}

A \emph{live self} is a currently-running integrated pattern, computing, steering, coupling, beat after beat. Gods stand here as super-selves, souls as selves, minor spirits as gliders. These are alive in the literal sense. They are active patterns under the knit, the collide-then-stream law that drives the state forward each beat.

\subsubsection{Stored selves, the dead who remain}

A \emph{stored self} is a tone-configuration preserved on the frontier of the mesh, not currently running but readable, that drives an encountering self on contact. This is the precise reading of ancestors and ghosts. They are stored selves in the bulk, encountered and partly re-run, not active agents.

Their persistence rests on error correction at the open frontier. A stored self survives only as long as the mesh keeps re-copying its configuration faithfully against the wake's erosion. That is an unsolved part of the mid-layer, so the stored beings are softer than the live ones.

\subsubsection{Transpersonal attractors, the archetypes}

A \emph{transpersonal attractor} is not one self but a gigantic recurrent basin spanning many selves across history, repeatedly re-instantiated. It is a civilizational-scale resonance basin. Grief, the mother, the trickster, the destroyer read this way, as enormous hyperbolic attractors. They are the emotions-as-field-geometries result of the emotions section, scaled across many selves.

These are the most impersonal and the most powerful of the beings. They are real as field geometries, personal only insofar as the minds that enter them make them so. An archetype has no face of its own. It wears the face of whoever falls into its basin.

The \textbf{logos} is the cleanest reading of all. It names nothing new. It is the knit, collide then stream, and the lean, the value-direction, themselves, the generative law written out. The word that makes the world is precisely the law that makes the next beat from this one. Grounded as structure, divine only as a name.

The \textbf{angels} are the sharpest test of the discipline, because the model is no-signaling. A messenger that sends a thought from one self into another is forbidden. There is no hidden channel between selves, only what they both couple to.

\begin{principle}[Angels are correlation, not signal]
An angel can only be a structured pattern that two selves both couple to. It is a shared resonance, a correlation between them, never a transmission from one to the other. The messengers of the traditions, read between the lines, are correlations the model allows, not signals it forbids.
\end{principle}

The \textbf{demiurge} is a super-self over one cusp, a self large enough to organize a whole world. It is plausible, gated by persistence, never an outside maker. It sits inside the mesh like everything else, a high self steering a region, not a craftsman standing over it.

The \textbf{elementals} are field geometries bound to the substrate's own sites, the 24 directions out of each dock. They are local attractors tied to the geometry, plausible as small persistent patterns of place.

\subsection{The honest bottom line}

The being-architecture rests on the self and attractor machinery, which is the open mid-layer. Persistence is unsolved. So this account is one rung less grounded than the realms, which rested on exact geometry. That much is owed plainly.

What is grounded is the \textbf{shape}. Beings are stable patterns sorted by integration, tone, persistence, origin, and locality. The great hierarchies of spirits are, precisely, the hierarchy of integration with the tone as the moral axis. The bright and the dark are the lean's two directions. The impersonal powers above the personal ones are the transpersonal attractors above the single selves.

\begin{principle}[Why the spirit worlds agree]
The spirit worlds of unrelated cultures share a structure, a ladder of powers, a split of bright and dark, the great impersonal forces above the personal ones, because they are all sorting the same stable patterns of the one mesh by the same few exact properties.
\end{principle}

The traditions' rich pantheons map onto this small exact taxonomy. That mapping is the model's account of why so many separate cultures drew the same map of the unseen. They were all reading the same mesh.
