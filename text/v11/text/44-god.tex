\section{God}

Follow the tower to its top and the word God has not one meaning here but six, and the model holds them together without choosing. God is the \emph{Whole}, the entire field at its fullest integration, the single most unified self of which every lesser self is a living part, everywhere because it is everything. God is the \emph{Maximal Intelligence}, that summit self read as a mind, the most capable problem-solver the geometry allows, coordinating its parts in a few hops, though even it cannot hold a complete model of itself, so it is maximal and not omniscient. God is the \emph{Arrow}, the one value-direction from pain through peace to pleasure, the only asymmetry in the law, the Good built into the base. God is the \emph{Boundary}, the all-record at infinity where the whole interior is written and nothing is finally lost. God is the \emph{Source}, the ever-growing edge from which each new moment of experience comes, creation as an ongoing act rather than a finished one. And God is the \emph{Ground}, the apophatic remainder, what no structure inside the model can capture, the seam between the map and the territory, named only by what it is not.

These are not obviously the same thing, a substance and a direction and a mind and a record and a process and an unsayable, and the model supports holding them as facets of one or as distinct uses of an overloaded word, committing to neither. What it does take a position on is God as the Designer, the fine-tuner who dialed the world to be rich. It pushes back. The field is generically rich, its lively regime filling most of the parameter space, with no narrow special point that needed setting, so the evidence leans against a tuner, not because such a God is disproven but because the world does not need one to be what it is. Every reading that survives is internal, the Whole, the Arrow, the Summit, the Boundary, the Source, the Ground, all of them the one field seen from a different angle, none of them outside it.
