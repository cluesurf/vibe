\section{Glossary}
\label{app:glossary}

The names of the theory, gathered for reference. Every base term is a four-letter word, each of the eight elements beginning with its own letter, so that the eight are a clean symbol set.

\textbf{The substance and the whole.} The \textbf{vibe} is the one substance, experience itself, what everything is made of, from a single tone to a whole self. The \textbf{flow} is the whole, the eight elements running as one, the universe in motion.

\textbf{The eight elements.} The \textbf{mesh} is the whole space, the growing $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb. A \textbf{dock} is one cell of the mesh, a here-now. A \textbf{site} is one of a dock's twenty-four directions, a $D_4$ root, where a single tone lives. The \textbf{tone} is the quality of a vibe's experience, its felt value, ternary, pain, peace, or pleasure, not a thing apart from the vibe. The \textbf{lean} is the order $-1 < 0 < +1$, which gives charge and gives creation its direction. The \textbf{knit} is the law of the state, collide then stream, once a beat. The \textbf{wake} is the law of the space, new docks born at peace at the growing edge. The \textbf{beat} is one discrete tick of time.

\textbf{The supporting words.} The \textbf{will} is a dock's outgoing tones, what it sends. The \textbf{fill} is its incoming tones, what it receives. \textbf{Rest} is the bound state of a self, its tones held together, the rest mass, emergent rather than a base slot. The \textbf{base} is the foundation, the eight elements and nothing more. \textbf{Code} is a law or an identity written out as a pattern. A \textbf{tree} is the branching structure, both the mesh fanning out and the nesting of selves within selves.

\textbf{What is emergent, not base.} A \textbf{self} is a bound, self-correcting knot of feeling woven across many docks over many beats. The \textbf{arrow} is the direction of time, emerging from the wake acting through the lean. The \textbf{attraction} is binding by radiation, the shadow a body casts in the active vacuum. \textbf{Spacetime}, the unification of mesh and beat, is emergent too, which is why space and time are separate at the base. The still center once entertained as a twenty-fifth site, a \textbf{home}, is not required and not in the base.

\textbf{Key derived terms.} The \textbf{bulk} is the four-dimensional interior of the mesh, tree-like and vast. The \textbf{cusp} is the flat three-dimensional boundary the bulk grows toward, ordinary physical space. The \textbf{bath} is the open growing edge read as a sink, where a shed disturbance goes for good. A \textbf{line} is a direction paired with its opposite, the channel the collision acts on. A \textbf{hyper-color} is the full $3^{24}$ state of a single dock, its woven feeling. The \textbf{single quale} is one tone, the irreducible atom of feeling. The \textbf{create move} is the one entry of the knit that lifts a balanced pair out of peace, making the vacuum active.
