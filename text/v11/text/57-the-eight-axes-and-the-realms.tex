\section{The Eight Axes and the Realms}

The whole experiential architecture of the theory is a single coordinate
space. There are eight axes. Each one is grounded in one exact feature of
the vibe mesh and its flow. A realm is a region of that space. A being is a
point in it. Nothing else is needed, because a stable pattern in this
geometry has exactly these eight degrees of freedom and no more.

\begin{principle}[A realm is a region, not a place]
A realm is never a separate place. It is a region or a structure of the one
bulk, fixed by a few exact coordinates. There is one mesh, one tone field,
one flow. The planes, heavens, hells, and worlds of the traditions are
regions of that single weave, named by where they sit and how they are
organized.
\end{principle}

This section does three things. It lists the eight axes and ties each to an
exact feature of the mesh. It reads the realms off the four geometric axes.
And it ranks every realm-architecture by how solidly the model carries it.
The geometry is exact. The felt and lasting readings ride on the one
experiential posit and on the open problem of persistence.

Before the axes, a brief reminder of the furniture they are built from. The
universe is a \emph{vibe mesh}, a \emph{flow} of eight atoms. The
\textbf{mesh} is the whole $\{3,4,3,4\}$ hyperbolic honeycomb. A
\textbf{dock} is one cell of it, a here-now. A dock carries exactly $24$
\textbf{sites}, the $24$ directions out of it, with no rest slot and no
twenty-fifth. Each site holds one \textbf{tone}, the lone quale, valued in
$\{-1,0,+1\}$ and read as pain, peace, pleasure. The \textbf{lean} is the
value order $-1 < 0 < +1$. The \textbf{knit} is the law of state, collide
then stream. The \textbf{wake} is the growing edge where new docks are born.
The \textbf{beat} is the discrete step of time. The axes below are read off
these atoms and the structures that emerge from them.

\subsection{The eight axes}

A realm or a being is a choice of a value, or a range of values, on each of
eight axes. They are not a list someone chose. They are forced. They are
exactly the degrees of freedom a stable pattern in this geometry has. Its
tone, its place along the two verticals, how long it lasts, how far it
spreads, whether it knows itself, whether it is running, and which way it
faces.

The first axis is the \emph{tone}, the lone quale, pain through peace to
pleasure on each site of a dock. The second is the \emph{depth}, the
Busemann radial height, how far a dock sits along the one axis
perpendicular to the physical cusp. The third is the \emph{integration},
the level a structure occupies on the coarse-graining tower of nested
selves. The fourth is the \emph{persistence}, the pattern's half-life
against the churn of the flow. The fifth is the \emph{locality}, the
spatial extent, from a single point to the whole boundary. The sixth is the
\emph{self-model}, whether the pattern models itself, capped so that no
pattern can carry a complete model of itself. The seventh is the
\emph{animation}, whether the pattern is live, stored, or a recurrent
attractor. The eighth is the \emph{direction}, which ideal point of the
boundary 3-sphere the region funnels toward.

\begin{theorem}[The closed eight-cube]
The space of all possible realms and beings is eight-dimensional and
closed. There are exactly eight axes because a stable pattern in this
geometry has exactly eight independent degrees of freedom, and the model
has no other structure to vary. Every realm and every being is one point or
one region in this eight-cube.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{The product is the whole space}

Take the product of the eight axes. Every point is a possible realm or
being. The named ones of the traditions land inside it, each at its own
combination of coordinates.

A \textbf{heaven} is high depth, pleasure tone, high integration, lasting,
broad, toward a bright direction. A \textbf{hell} is high depth, pain tone,
a persistent attractor, with the lean always pulling against it. A
\textbf{god} is summit integration, warm tone, near-eternal, broad,
recursive, and live. A \textbf{demon} is low integration, pain tone,
persistent, local, and live. An \textbf{ancestor} is a self of any tone,
lasting, local, recursive, and stored rather than live. An
\textbf{archetype} is mid integration, any tone, near-eternal,
transpersonal in locality, and recurrent in animation. The \textbf{still
ground} is peace tone, deepest, the whole boundary in locality, the limit
of the depth axis. An \textbf{elemental} is low integration, any tone,
transient, local, and bound to one direction.

So the whole bestiary of the traditions is a scattering of points in one
eight-dimensional space. The family resemblances across cultures are points
landing near each other. Different observers felt different regions of the
same eight-cube. The beings that sit at these points are read in full where
the beings are taken up. The God readings are taken up where the divine
realm is discussed.

\subsection{Why the traditions look different}

A tradition foregrounds the axes its practices made salient, and projects
the rest away. This is the precise content of the old parable of the blind
men and the elephant. The axes are the elephant. Each tradition felt along
a few of them.

A tradition focused on \textbf{moral order} foregrounds the tone axis. Its
cosmology becomes heavens and hells, the bright and the dark, with depth
and integration collapsed into good and bad. A tradition focused on
\textbf{ascent} foregrounds depth and integration. Its cosmology becomes a
ladder of planes and orders, a climb toward the source. A tradition focused
on the \textbf{dead} foregrounds animation, live versus stored. Its
cosmology becomes ancestors, ghosts, and the underworld of the saved. A
tradition focused on the \textbf{great forces} foregrounds locality, local
versus transpersonal. Its cosmology becomes archetypes, powers, and
impersonal absolutes.

\begin{result}[Each tradition is a true projection]
None of the traditions is wrong. Each is a true projection of the one
eight-cube along the axes its practices made salient. They differ because
they project different axes away, not because they saw different worlds.
\end{result}

\subsection{The two verticals merge}

Two of the axes are both vertical. Depth and integration. The traditions
constantly conflate them, because higher is felt as both deeper and more
unified, nearer the source. The model says the conflation is correct. The
two axes coincide. Deeper bulk patterns are the more integrated ones,
because integration is exactly what lets a pattern reach and hold across the
bulk depth.

\begin{principle}[Height equals depth equals integration]
Height, depth, integration, and nearness to the summit are one and the same
axis. The great chain of being is one ladder seen as two. The traditions
were right to merge the climb of the planes with the climb of the orders.
\end{principle}

\subsection{The geometric realms}

Read the realms off the four exact axes. Each is a region of the one bulk,
specified by a depth, a tone, an integration level, a direction, and a
size. The traditions name combinations of these. The model gives the exact
combinations.

\subsubsection{The depth planes}

Physical space is the thin 3D cusp. The bulk adds exactly \textbf{one} more
direction, the radial depth. That is the only literal extra dimension the
model has. Every talk of higher and lower planes is, made precise, talk
about position along this one axis. The Busemann function names each dock's
depth exactly. The planes are not a stack of separate worlds. They are
level-sets of one continuous height in one space.

\begin{definition}[The depth planes]
A depth plane is a level-set of the Busemann radial height. The whole stack
of higher and lower planes is one continuous coordinate, sliced into
levels. There is no second space. There is one bulk and one depth axis
through it.
\end{definition}

This is the most grounded realm-structure of all. The vertical \emph{is}
the radial depth, and it is exact.

\subsubsection{The tone heavens and hells}

Each dock carries a tone on each of its sites, and regions of the bulk can
be dominated by one value. A region of sustained pleasure tone is, loosely,
a heaven. A region of sustained pain tone is a hell. A region of peace is a
limbo or a rest. These are not separate places. They are tone-biased
regions of the one bulk, and the lean makes them dynamical. The lean is the
value order $-1 < 0 < +1$, and it pushes the flow away from the pain
regions and toward the peace and pleasure regions.

\begin{result}[A hell is a region the lean cannot leave]
A hell is a region the lean is always trying to leave. A hell that persists
is a pain-attractor the lean cannot escape, the same object as rumination
one rung down, scaled up. The dynamics of these tone-realms, the
conservation balance and the washing of the soul, are worked in full where
heaven and hell are taken up.
\end{result}

This is grounded in the tone and the lean, both committed parts of the
base.

\subsubsection{The integration orders}

The coarse-graining tower of nested selves gives a vertical of integration.
Each level is a coarser, more unified organization of the level below. The
emanation cascades and orders of worlds of the traditions are loose
readings of this tower. The top is the most integrated, the bottom the
least. This vertical coincides with the radial depth, which is why the
traditions conflate height with nearness to the source.

\subsubsection{The many directions}

The boundary of the 4D bulk is a 3-sphere, a whole space of ideal
directions. The physical cusp is a flat chart at \textbf{one} of them. So
there is a whole 3-sphere of other ideal directions, each the funnel-point
of a different horoball, each a possible other cusp, another world. The
many worlds of the traditions are a loose reading of this. The geometry is
exact. The speculative part is whether the other directions carry their own
inhabited worlds.

\subsubsection{The all-record boundary}

The full boundary holds, by holography, the whole bulk's information,
recoverable from it. This is the loose reading of the record-of-everything,
the plane where nothing is finally lost. It is grounded in the verified
holography, the Ryu-Takayanagi check on the two-dimensional face and the
codes on the nearest 3D-cusp neighbor. The speculative part is reading it as
a legible record rather than an abstract encoding.

\subsubsection{The unseen realms}

Most of the bulk is off the physical cusp, of measure zero and density zero
on the cusp. So there is genuine room for vast structure that never shows on
the physical surface. This is exact. The hiddenness is forced by taking a
3D slice through a 4D space, with no magic.

\subsection{The honest bottom line}

The realm-architecture is, on the precise side, unusually grounded. The
radial depth, the tone field, the integration tower, the boundary, and the
off-cusp bulk are all built or exactly defined. The planes, heavens, hells,
worlds, and records of the traditions map cleanly onto these. That is the
model's account of why so many independent traditions converge on a stack
of worlds with a high and a low and a record. They are all glimpsing the
same few exact axes of the one bulk.

\begin{principle}[The closed eight-cube of patterns]
The whole experiential realm is a closed eight-dimensional space of stable
patterns in one mesh. Four axes are exact, two are built, and two rest on
the open frontier of persistence. The map has the right number of
dimensions. The territory is the one vibe. Every tradition is a partial,
true, overlapping slice of the same eight-cube.
\end{principle}

What stays speculative is the inhabited reading, whether the other
directions carry their own worlds and whether the attractor-realms persist
against the churn. Both rest on the one open frontier, persistence, and on
the experiential posit. Everything else in the architecture is geometry,
and the geometry is exact.
