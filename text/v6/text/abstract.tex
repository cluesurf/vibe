\begin{abstract}
This paper is a conceptual map of Vibe Theory. It tells the whole story in plain terms: what the theory says reality
is, and how the physical, the mental, and the spiritual come out of one substance. That
substance is the \emph{vibe}, a unit of experience carrying a ternary \emph{tone}, read as pain,
peace, or pleasure, and related to others only by the \emph{note}, one vibe experiencing
another. The vibes form a growing hyperbolic crystal updated by a single local rule, and
everything else is a pattern in it. We trace the foundation down to the whole numbers, whose
symmetries lead to a hyperbolic crystal whose dimension is narrowed to three. We show how space,
time, matter, the forces, gravity, the quantum, and the cosmos emerge as patterns, and how mind
enters as the same structure recurring, selves made of selves, in a tower from a single quantum
of feeling up to the whole. We are explicit throughout about what is solid, what is partial, and
what is a hypothesis worth taking seriously.
\end{abstract}
