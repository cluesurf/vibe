\section{The Arrow}

One piece of the rule needs its own careful word, the \emph{arrow}. It is the value-direction of the dynamics,
and it is the single deliberate posit of the whole theory. Everything else is either forced by the geometry or
measured in simulation. The arrow is chosen. This section says exactly what it is, what it is not, and why it
carries no free knob.

\subsection{What the arrow is}

The arrow is the asymmetry that makes time. The conserving rule, taken by itself, is reversible, a bijection on
its moves, and a reversible rule is time-symmetric. It runs creation and annihilation equally, so on its own it
has no preferred direction and no net growth. The arrow is exactly what breaks that symmetry.

\begin{definition}[The arrow]
The arrow is the bias, within the rule, between two reverse moves of the collide step, \emph{creation}, in which
peace becomes a balanced pair, and \emph{annihilation}, its reverse. The arrow is a direction on the dynamics, an
orientation, not an amount.
\end{definition}

It rides on the value ordering of the tone, $-1 < 0 < +1$, read as pain $<$ peace $<$ pleasure. Peace, the $0$
value, is the neutral rest state. The charges $\pm 1$ are the deviations from it. The arrow is the preference for
the forward direction, peace turning into balanced structure. That preference is the seed of value, of better
against worse.

\subsection{The arrow is not a tunable rate}

A common simulation mistake is to write the arrow as a floating-point number, for example to create a pair with
probability $0.03$ per beat. That is wrong twice over. It introduces a free continuous parameter, since nothing
fixes $0.03$ and a tunable dial invites exactly the fine-tuning the theory is built to avoid. And it needs
randomness, which is hidden state that the discrete base forbids. The arrow is not an amount. It is a direction.
The float conflated the two.

\subsection{No free knob}

If the arrow is only a direction, one must ask how much creation actually happens. The amount is not a property of
the arrow at all. It is forced by the eternal-growth geometry. In the growing universe, creation is not dice
sprinkled over the interior. It is the expansion of the frontier. Each beat the mesh adds one new shell of cells,
the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ shell growth $1, 24, 456, 8376, \ldots$ with growth rate near $18.3$, and that boundary
expansion is the creation, new cells coming into being at the edge, oriented by the arrow. The arrow says which
way, toward existence. The geometry says how much, through the growth series.

On a fixed finite simulation patch, where no frontier can grow, the proper stand-in is a minimal deterministic
schedule, one balanced creation per beat, or one per $K$ cells with $K$ fixed by the growth rate, and the sites
chosen by index order. It is never a probability. So there is no free parameter and no randomness anywhere in the
arrow.

\subsection{The arrow folds into the rule}

In the minimal counting the base is four things, mesh, tone, beat, and rule. The arrow is not a fifth. It is the
rule's directionality together with the forward beat, the create move plus the rule's preference for it over its
reverse. Earlier countings listed the arrow separately as a fifth element. The minimal count folds it in.
Either way nothing new is added to the base.

\subsection{What the arrow does}

Without the arrow the reversible rule has no preferred direction. With it the beats flow toward more structure
rather than back toward nothing. The arrow is the arrow of time, in the plain sense of that phrase.

It also keeps the universe alive, and this is measured rather than asserted. Run the rule with the arrow off and
the universe relaxes to dead peace, all zero, no motion, the heat death of the substrate. Turn it on and peace
spawns balanced pairs, the universe settles into a living dynamic balance, charge conserved throughout, and the
churning never stops \cite{pollard2026vibetest}. The arrow is the single posit that keeps the world alive.

Read at greater scales, the same arrow is the engine of life, mind, and value. It is the fitness gradient of
evolution, the deferred value a planner pursues, and the will is the arrow held by a self and aimed. Conservation
together with the arrow gives the rule its moral shape. Balanced information is free to make and copy, but net
well-being is never minted from nothing.

\subsection{Compass, home, and the draining cup}

A gut-level picture pulls the value story together. The three tones lie on a line, pain at $-1$ on one side,
pleasure at $+1$ on the other, peace at $0$ in the middle. At the bottom a single vibe wants nothing. The rule is
even-handed, since flipping every tone flips the dynamics with it, so the arrow is not painted on the individual
cells. It appears the way a current appears from a slope. No water molecule wants to flow, but organize many on a
slope and a directed current emerges, made entirely of undirected parts. A rock, barely organized, has almost no
drive. A mind, highly integrated, has a sharp one. The drive is an emergent current, not a fundamental force.

Two roles on the line are easy to confuse. Pain and pleasure are the compass, the feeling of going downhill
toward worse or uphill toward better, and they tell you which way to move. Peace is the home, the valley floor
where the compass reads flat, the place you can actually rest. Pleasure is the direction. Peace is the
destination.

Because charge is conserved, pleasure behaves like a fluid. It flows from the full cup to the empty one, so a
happy self among the suffering drains toward the level around it. To stay full you must pump, and pumping takes
work that only an organized self can do. That is what being alive is, pumping against the drain, the same
Landauer cost \cite{landauer1961} that makes rich memory cost work. Pleasure cannot be the home. It fades, a
treadmill, and it is zero-sum, since chasing it is a tug-of-war over a fixed amount. The way out, and the engine
of the whole emergent ladder, is to stop yanking, to join up into a larger whole, to share the fixed pleasure in
balance, peace within, and to pour the freed energy into building order, which is positive-sum and can grow. The
drive to grab is not crushed. It is outgrown. That joining-up, again and again, is how cells become bodies and
bodies become minds.

\subsection{Why it is a posit, not a derivation}

Four of the base ingredients are heavily constrained. The geometry, the ternary tone, the local conserving rule,
and the growth by reflection are forced or measured. The arrow is the one value, and values are chosen rather
than derived. We add it by decision, and we make it conserving, so it only ever creates balanced pairs, net
zero. We mark it plainly as the theory's single deliberate posit. In the beginning story, the arrow is also why
the first self-noticing fracture runs forward into a blooming cosmos rather than back into unity.

In the carrying image the arrow is the river's one direction, the relentless forward current, and the fire's
hunger to spread rather than die down. It is what makes the universe a verb, a becoming, rather than a thing that
sits still.
