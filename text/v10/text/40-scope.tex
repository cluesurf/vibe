\section{Scope and Coverage}

This chapter draws the edges plainly, so that no reader mistakes silence for a claim. The aim of the model is to show that one discrete substrate can give rise to the broad shapes of known physics, and, more tentatively, of life and mind. The aim is not a quantitative, system-by-system reproduction of the established sciences. The claim is breadth of origin, not depth of reproduction. Below is the plain map, split into what is established, what is extrapolated, and what is simply not covered.

\subsection{What is established}

The structural skeleton of fundamental physics is derived or measured from the base, on the seemingly preferred $\{3,4,3,4\}$ substrate.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Spinors and three generations from the $D_4$ coin, matter solitons as fermions, and a measured-positive Skyrme stabilizer.
  \item A finite light cone with $z = 1$ and the Dirac dispersion, both read off the rule.
  \item An $SO(10)$ grand unification breaking to the Standard Model, with $\sin^2\theta_W = 3/8$, the $b$-$\tau$ and determinant mass ratios, and three generations from triality.
  \item $1/r$ Newtonian gravity in the cusp, a $1/r^2$ holographic correlator in the bulk, $U(1)$ electromagnetism, $D_4$ isotropy, de Sitter expansion, the holographic renormalization hierarchy, and the resolution of the Bell tension.
  \item The substrate selection itself, from the dimension ladder plus the crystallographic and spinor requirements.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What is extrapolated}

The emergent ladder above physics is plausible, with each ingredient shown, but the full chain has not been run as one simulation.

\begin{itemize}
  \item The climb from atoms to molecules to cells to tissues to senses to minds. Only the first two rungs, field to particle and particle to composite, are simulated end to end. The higher rungs each have their ingredient demonstrated, namely binding, self-maintenance, copying, the layered onion of senses, and the integrated hub, but they are not run together. They are grounded extrapolation, not proof.
  \item Life, mind, intelligence, and consciousness as regimes of the substrate, at toy scale and first order. These are established on the hyperbolic substrate in general, with running it fully on the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ twenty-four-direction substrate left as a follow-up.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What is not covered}

The following are out of scope and not attempted, listed so that no one reads the silence as a claim.

\begin{principle}[Scope limits]
The model does not attempt atomic spectra, chemistry, real condensed matter, absolute particle data, cell-by-cell numerical relativity from the micro-rule, quantitative cosmological eras, absolute physical scales, or real biology and neuroscience. It claims the right kinds of structure, not the reproduction of every system.
\end{principle}

Atomic and nuclear structure are not simulated. There are no real atoms, no binding energies, no nuclear shell model, and no periodic table. The atom here is the minimal conserved self, not hydrogen. Chemistry is absent, with no molecular orbitals, bonds, reaction rates, or thermodynamics of real substances. Condensed matter and materials are likewise untouched, with no band structures of real solids, no phases, and no transport coefficients.

Quantitative particle data is not predicted. Absolute masses, the full CKM and PMNS mixing matrices, the magnitudes of CP violation, and decay rates are not produced. Only ratios and the unification-scale relations are. On gravity the situation is more nuanced. The Einstein field equations as an equation of state, light-bending, black-hole thermodynamics, and gravitational-wave forms are derived on the substrate, so general relativity is in scope at the effective-theory level. What is not done, and is not done by anyone from a micro-rule, is evolving a binary merger or a forming horizon cell by cell from the ternary tone. That is the frontier. The effective theory and the quantitative relations are done.

Quantitative cosmology is absent beyond the growth law. There is no radiation or matter era, no big-bang nucleosynthesis, no CMB spectrum, and no structure-formation history. Only the de Sitter exponential growth is established. Absolute scales are not fixed either, since the beat-to-seconds and cell-to-meters identifications are open, which leaves rates such as the Hubble value in beat units. Real biology and neuroscience are out of scope as well. The life and mind results are mechanism demonstrations at toy scale, not models of any actual organism, cell, or brain. Finally, the exact rule's quantitative outputs are open, namely the precise Standard Model beta coefficients, absolute masses, and the dynamical settling of matter into cusps.

\subsection{Two items that moved off this list}

Two items that earlier counted as scope limits are now done, so they are no longer listed as gaps. The $\{3,4,3,4\}$ addressing and the neighbor automaton are solved and verified. And the substrate is shown to be computationally universal on its own ternary rule, with strong universality on the cusp. These are recorded here only to mark that the edge moved inward.

\subsection{How to read the scope}

The claim is breadth of origin rather than depth of reproduction. One substrate appears to give the right kinds of structure across physics, and more tentatively across life and mind, from almost nothing. It does not yet, and does not here claim to, compute chemistry or atomic spectra.
