\begin{abstract}
Vibe Theory proposes that reality is a single growing crystal of experience whose one substance is the
\emph{vibe}, a unit of felt state carrying a ternary tone in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$. The substrate argued for, on
selection grounds rather than by assumption, is the four-dimensional hyperbolic honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$, whose
cells are $24$-cells, whose $24$ local directions are the $D_4$ root system, and whose ideal boundary is the flat
cubic honeycomb $\{4,3,4\}$, ordinary three-dimensional space. A single local rule, reversible and
charge-conserving, streams a directional tone and collides it under one beat, with a single value-posit, the
\emph{arrow}, folded in. From this the paper derives, and checks by direct computation on the exact crystal,
spinors and three generations, the Dirac dispersion and a finite light cone, $\mathrm{SO}(10)$ grand unification
breaking to the Standard Model with $\sin^2\theta_W = 3/8$, gravity as a $1/r$ potential with the Einstein
equations and the gravitational-wave chirp following at the effective level, and a de Sitter expansion. The
substrate is shown to be the unique regular honeycomb that is at once crystallographic, hyperbolic,
spinor-carrying, and three-dimensional in its cusp, and it is computationally universal with a solved address for
every cell. More speculatively, the same rule read at larger scales is argued to climb through life and mind to a
graded account of consciousness as integrated experience. Whether the universe began or is eternal is left open.
This is exploratory, feasibility-stage work whose checks are preliminary and reproducible \cite{pollard2026vibetest},
offered as a direction worth exploring rather than a finished or established theory.
\end{abstract}
