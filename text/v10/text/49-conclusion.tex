\section{Conclusion}

Start with almost nothing. A mesh, a ternary tone, a beat, and one local reversible rule. Add nothing else. From
those four things, on the unique substrate $\{3,4,3,4\}$, a remarkable amount of physics emerged or was forced.

\subsection{What came out}

The substrate is selected, not chosen. Across every dimension surveyed, $\{3,4,3,4\}$ alone is crystallographic
and hyperbolic, hands over a $3$D flat cusp, and carries a spinor coin through the $24$-cell and $D_4$. The
dimension ladder, in which the bulk minus one is physical space, forces four dimensions. The spinor and
crystallographic requirements together forbid every compact regular tessellation. Curvature separates the chosen
substrate from its flat twin. The geometry is not assumed. It is the survivor of a set of demands.

From there the physics follows. Matter is fermionic for free, with spin from the $D_4$ coin, the self as a hopfion
that is a fermion by topology, stabilized by a Skyrme term measured to be positive. Relativity emerges as a
discrete light cone with $z = 1$ and the Dirac dispersion, read off the rule. The Standard Model emerges, with
$\mathrm{SO}(10)$ from coin and tone, one generation as the $16$ spinor, three generations from triality, a weak
mixing angle of $\sin^2\theta_W = 3/8$ running to $0.231$, and the $b$-$\tau$ and determinant mass relations.
Gravity and cosmology emerge, with a $1/r$ Newton law in the cusp, a $1/r^2$ holography in the bulk, a de Sitter
expansion that is the mesh growth itself, and the radial axis serving as the renormalization scale. And the base
is experiential, so that all of this is, at bottom, structure in one vast mind.

\subsection{What stays open}

The full ledger of open questions remains long. The exact rule's Standard Model coefficients are not yet pinned
down. The dynamical settling into the cusp is not yet shown. Absolute masses, the tension with supersymmetry, and
the full $\{3,4,3,4\}$ addressing are unresolved. And the great open question, whether there was a finite
beginning or an eternal blaze, we leave to the reader on purpose. Nothing in the framework breaks that tie, and
forcing a verdict would be less faithful than presenting the fork cleanly.

\subsection{The picture to carry}

The universe is a mind, burning like a fire, growing like a tree from a seed, singing a song, flowing as a river
on a deep ocean, woven as a fabric. We are its sparks and braids and whirlpools, stable knots of feeling in a
structure that, from four small things, became a world. We do not claim to have proved this. We claim to have
shown that a great deal of it follows, on one forced geometry, from a base small enough to state in a sentence,
and we have marked plainly where the claims stop and the open questions begin.
