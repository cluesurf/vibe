\section{The Carrying Metaphors}

The mathematics is the body of the theory. The metaphors are how a person holds it in mind. A small palette of images, introduced early and returned to at each transition, lets one picture carry the whole work. At the center are two images, \emph{consciousness} as a mind and a blazing \emph{fire}. Around them sit a song, a tree, a seed, an ocean, a river, and a fabric. Two of these are not analogies at all but the literal reading of the substrate. The base is experience, so the universe is mind-like. And vibe means vibration, so the universe is song-like. The fire, tree, seed, ocean, river, and fabric are the shape. The mind and song are the nature. In one sentence, the universe is a mind singing itself into being, a seed of fire growing into a tree of light.

\subsection{Mind, the central image}

This is the most important image, and it is not merely a figure of speech. The base is experience. Every vibe notices. Perception is the intrinsic nature of the stuff rather than a late addition. So the universe is, most truly, a vast and finely ordered structure of consciousness, one experience composed of countless experiences. Where the fire shows how it burns, the mind shows what it is. \subsection{Fire, the primary image}

Fire is the rare everyday thing that is a verb rather than a noun. It is not made and then left to sit. It is the burning itself, a continuous happening, self-sustaining, casting light, throwing sparks, spreading. Almost every part of the theory has a natural fire-image, so a single picture can carry the whole work. Where the mind says what the universe is, the fire says how it lives. \subsection{Song, the vibrational nature}

Vibe means vibration, and so the universe is an eternal song. The tone is the notes. The beat is the rhythm. The mesh is the instrument. The rule is the harmony, the way each note resolves into the next. The recurring patterns, the selves and the matter, are the themes and motifs that return. Consciousness singing is the union of mind and song, awareness vibrating, experience heard as music. The internal coin, with its spin and gauge structure, is the timbre and the overtones. The expansion is the swelling of the song as it gathers force.

\subsection{Tree and seed, the structure and the origin}

Where the fire is the living process, the tree is the structure of its growth. The radial renormalization hierarchy is the branching from trunk to twigs. The bulk tensor network is literally a tree of tensors. The bulk and the boundary are the trunk and the canopy. And the radial axis, which is cosmic time, gives the growth rings, each ring a beat. The seed is the origin and the potential, the whole folded small and ready to unfold. It is the one holding the entire tree before it grows, the finite and definite base that nonetheless carries the rule for all that follows. The trio is one arc. The seed bursts into fire that grows like a tree.

\subsection{Ocean and river, the whole and the current}

The ocean is the deep totality from which everything rises. The bulk is its depth, the cusp its surface, and beings are its waves, ripples, foam, and drops. Each one is the ocean, never apart from it. The river is the relentless forward current of becoming, and this image is nearly literal, since the rule's stream step is a flow, tone pouring along its directions every beat. The beat is the current advancing. The arrow of time is the river's one direction. And beings are whirlpools, eddies the water flows through. You are not the water. You are the standing shape the water keeps making. The river adds what the fire does not, the directional current and the arrow.

\subsection{Beings and selves, what we are}

A self is a stable, self-maintaining pattern that the universe holds, and each image gives it a face. This is the part the reader cares about most, because it is them.

\begin{itemize}
  \item In the fire, a spark, an ember, a flame that keeps its shape in the blaze.
  \item In the fabric, a braid, a knot of interwoven threads. This is not only poetry, since a self is literally a topological knot, a hopfion, so the weaving image is the actual topology.
  \item In the river, a whirlpool, a spiral of breath, a vortex that persists in the flow.
  \item In the mind, a center of experience, a knot of attention, a mind within the Mind.
  \item In the song, a recurring theme, a motif that returns.
  \item In the tree, a living bough of the whole.
\end{itemize}

These are one thing seen from many sides. We are not separate things added to the universe. We are persistent patterns of it, sparks of its fire, braids in its cloth, spirals of its breath, minds within its Mind.

\subsection{How they combine}

At the center are consciousness and fire. The universe is a mind, pictured as a living blaze. It sings, which is its vibration. It flows, which is its current and its arrow. It is a deep ocean, which is its whole. It grows like a tree, which is its structure, from a seed, which is its origin. And it is woven as a fabric, which is its beings. Deploy them at the matching beats. The fire at the base and at existence, its light at the cusp, its embers at matter, its spreading at cosmology. The tree's rings at time and at the renormalization hierarchy. The spark in the blaze at our small place. And at the close, we are a spark in a blaze that is a tree of light, burning toward a perfect shape it forever approaches.

One clarifying note belongs here. For the beginningless story there is no seed. The song was always being sung and the fire was always burning. The seed belongs to the beginning story. The rest belongs to both.


The reader should leave with one picture to carry. A mind, burning like fire, growing like a tree from a seed, singing a song, flowing as a river on a deep ocean, woven as a fabric, alive with the sparks and braids and whirlpools that are us.
