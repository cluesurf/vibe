\section{Open Problems}

This is the full list of what is not yet done. None of it is hidden, and each item is a concrete next step rather than a vague worry. The list also moves. Several items that earlier sat here are now closed, and where that has happened the text says so. What remains is mainly the exact collision and the absolute numbers it gates, the candidate fifth ingredient, the dynamical settling of matter, a residual tension over supersymmetry, and the deliberately open question of whether the cosmos has a first beginning.

\subsection{The exact collision}

The base posits one local reversible rule, stream and collide while conserving, and the collision is the rule's last unfixed freedom. Many downstream results were demonstrated with emergent-layer tools, the continuous Dirac operator and spectral methods such as the kernel polynomial method and Lanczos, rather than by running the actual discrete collision end to end. This is the central verification gate.

\begin{principle}[The collision is the make-or-break frontier]
One concrete discrete collision has been pinned and verified to conserve charge and momentum and to reproduce the Dirac dispersion end to end, so the gate is not wholly open. But deriving the precise Standard Model beta coefficients, and the rest of the tower, from that collision needs the full dynamical simulation, not the group theory alone.
\end{principle}

The mechanism behind the running is now concrete and checked. Radial coarse-graining is the renormalization group, and the one-dimensional decimation $K' = \tfrac{1}{2}\ln\cosh 2K$ equals direct block-spin summation exactly, with error zero, flowing to the Gaussian fixed point \cite{pollard2026vibetest}. So the statement that radial coarse-graining is the renormalization group is no longer a claim but a verified method. The remaining step is to feed the actual four-dimensional collision, not the one-dimensional toy, through the same blocking to read off the Standard Model beta coefficients. That is the make-or-break simulation.

\subsection{No absolute quantitative predictions yet}

The blunt headline is that beyond the group-theory ratios, namely $\sin^2\theta_W = 3/8$, the $b$-$\tau$ relation, and the determinant relation, the program has computed essentially no absolute falsifiable number. There is no mass spectrum, no gauge-coupling values, no CKM or PMNS mixing angles, no Newton constant, and no absolute cosmological constant. All of these wait on the exact collision above. This is stated plainly rather than letting the ratio predictions imply more than they deliver.

\subsection{The candidate fifth ingredient}

The base binds within a unit but not cleanly between units. That single gap sits behind several open holes at once, including cross-gap gravitational attraction, super-selves, and the nesting tower between selves. Higher-order binding is named as the one plausible candidate for a possible fifth ingredient. It is not yet proven either to emerge from the existing four ingredients or to be irreducible to them. It is a place to watch, not a license to add. The model holds firmly to its commitment of exactly five base things and refuses to slip in a sixth under cover of this gap.

\subsection{Dynamical settling into the cusp}

The cusp is shown to be flat three-dimensional space, and matter is shown to be stable there. What is not yet shown is that matter settles onto the cusp layer dynamically from generic initial conditions. This is the matter-condensation problem.

\begin{principle}[Settling is a recorded negative]
Running the actual mod-3 wave rule on the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ bulk from random initial conditions, the activity stays uniform, with band ratio near one and no concentration toward the cusp. Matter churns rather than settling. This is exactly what a single reversible conserving rule must do from equilibrium initial conditions, since there is no dissipation to drive concentration \cite{pollard2026vibetest}.
\end{principle}

So dynamical settling needs either special low-entropy initial conditions or the candidate fifth ingredient. That means this problem and the fifth-ingredient problem share a root. The model refuses to add a fifth thing to force the result, so this stays a genuine open gap rather than a patched one.

\subsection{Absolute masses}

The mass ratios, the $b$-$\tau$ relation and the determinant relation, are derived. The absolute mass scale is not. The Yukawa magnitudes and the overall Higgs vacuum expectation value are not fixed by the substrate yet. Only the ratios and the running are.

\subsection{The supersymmetry tension}

The clean unification needs MSSM content. If nature has no low-energy supersymmetry, the bare Standard Model running lands near $\sin^2\theta_W \approx 0.21$, short of the observed $0.231$. This is a real tension. The substrate prefers supersymmetry, and the absence of supersymmetry would be a problem to confront rather than to paper over.

\subsection{Full addressing, now solved}

This was an open problem and is now closed. The $\{3,4,3,4\}$ substrate has unique, self-describing, $O(\log n)$ addresses and a finite neighbor automaton. It has no same-shell cousins, so the hard two-dimensional piece is absent, and greedy routing delivers complete success at stretch $1.004$ on the enumerated builds. The core adjacency invariants survive to two million cells \cite{pollard2026vibetest}. The item is listed here only to record that the frontier moved.

\subsection{The finite-versus-infinite question}

Whether the universe has a true first beginning or is eternal and beginningless is open, and deliberately left to the reader. Both are self-consistent under the rule. This is not a defect to be resolved but a fork the model is built to survive either way.


A model that hides its gaps cannot be trusted with its wins. These items are the frontier, and the list itself moves. The addressing is now solved, the renormalization mechanism is now a checked method, and the cusp-settling negative is now measured. What remains is mainly the exact collision and the absolute numbers it gates, plus the candidate fifth ingredient and the open finite-versus-infinite question.
