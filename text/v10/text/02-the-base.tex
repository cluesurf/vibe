\section{The Base}

The base of the theory is not a structure at all. The base is experience itself, the one ground from which
everything else is built. Everything else in this paper, the mesh, the tone, the rule, the whole apparatus, is a
conceptual representation of that ground. It is a model of how the experiential field is structured and how it
moves, written so it can be read and reasoned about and checked. The field itself is not exhausted by the model.
It cannot be fully known by reading and mentally modeling it, the way the taste of water is not in the chemical
formula. It is known directly, through experience, and through tuning with the oneness it is made of. We keep
that seam in view. What follows is the map. The territory is lived.

\begin{axiom}[Experience exists]
Experience exists, as both action and object, verb and noun at once. There is experiencing, and there is what is
experienced, and at the base these are not two. In the theory's own idiom, vibes vibe. The vibing is the verb and
the vibe is the noun, and they are the same single fact. This is the one founding posit. It is taken as given,
not derived, and the rest of the paper asks what follows if it is true.
\end{axiom}

There is one kind of thing, the \emph{vibe}, a unit of experience that notices its neighbours. Reality is a
structure of such noticings and nothing else. The physical world is what that experiential structure looks like
at scale. We will keep this premise distinct from the results that follow from it, and we will not pretend the
computations prove the ground feels. They assume that it does, in the precise sense of the axiom above, and ask
what follows.

\subsection{The flow}

Given the experiential ground, the model represents its structure and its motion as a few ingredients running
together. The whole of that running representation we call the \emph{flow}. The flow is what can be written down,
simulated, and tested. It has exactly four ingredients, and nothing may be added to them.

\begin{axiom}[The flow]
The flow consists of exactly four things.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The \emph{mesh}, a fixed hyperbolic honeycomb, argued to be $\{3,4,3,4\}$. Its geometry, the local coin of
directions, its cusp, and its boundary are all properties of this one mesh, not separate ingredients.
\item The \emph{tone}, a ternary value in $\{-1, 0, +1\}$ carried at each cell. In the song image these are the
notes, and in the mind image they are what a vibe feels.
\item The \emph{beat}, a discrete tick, the rhythm of the noticing.
\item The \emph{rule}, one local update. It is \emph{stream}, in which each cell passes its tone along its
directions, followed by \emph{collide}, a local interaction. The rule is local, conserving, and reversible.
\end{enumerate}
\end{axiom}

That is the whole list. A finer counting splits the rule into its conserving update and its growth, and lists the
arrow separately, but the substance is unchanged. There is nothing beyond this short list. The complete
leave-nothing-out specification of the rule, the $24$ directional slots and the exact stream and collide steps,
is given in its own section below. When we speak below of the discipline of adding nothing, we mean adding nothing
to this flow, the four ingredients that represent the experiential ground.

\subsection{Why the beat is constitutive}

The beat is not a convenient discretization of a continuous time. It is forced. An instantaneous, unpaced
reflection, a completed infinity arriving all at once, has no contrast, and a state with no contrast is
experientially identical to nothing. The finite beat is what produces contrast, one step distinguished from the
next, and contrast is the only thing that can be an experience of something. There is also no external clock for
the beat to run slow or fast against. The beat is not in time. The beat is time. Discrete time is therefore a
necessity of an experiential base, not an approximation of a continuous one.

\subsection{The rule conserves total tone}

The most load-bearing property of the rule is conservation. Treat each ternary value as a charge of $-1$, $0$, or
$+1$, and sum the tone over every cell. The rule never changes that sum. The collision only ever rearranges and
balances tone. It never mints net charge from nothing.

\begin{proposition}[Conservation of tone]
Let $Q$ be the sum of all tones over the whole crystal. Then $Q$ is exact and constant under the rule, a conserved
quantity of the entire universe. This was checked directly to machine precision \cite{pollard2026vibetest}.
\end{proposition}

This single fact does an enormous amount of work later in the paper. It is the discrete Gauss law that sources
emergent electromagnetism. It is why the arrow can only ever create balanced pairs, a $+1$ together with a $-1$,
net zero. It forces newborn frontier cells to be born at peace. It gives the cosmic dilution toward peace, since
$Q$ stays fixed while the cell count grows without bound. It is the engine of life, where life turns out to be
conservation of the sum rather than of the arrangement, so balanced information is free to make and copy while
net well-being is never minted. What is not conserved is the arrangement, the order and pattern, which is free to
form, flow, and decay.

\subsection{The rule is reversible}

The collide step is a reversible local map, so the whole rule runs backward as exactly as it runs forward.

\begin{result}[Reversibility]
Running the rule forward $N$ beats and then backward $N$ beats recovers the start state with zero error
\cite{pollard2026vibetest}.
\end{result}

Reversibility matters twice over. It is what guarantees the right conserved quantities, both the charge above and
momentum. And it means every state has a unique past, which is central to the cosmology and to the question of
whether the universe had a beginning or is eternal. We leave that question open. The rule itself runs equally
well in either direction, and which way the world in fact ran is not settled by the rule alone.

\subsection{The nothing-added discipline}

\begin{principle}[Nothing added]
\label{prin:nothing-added}
Across every test in this paper, nothing was added to the four ingredients. Every result is a property of the
mesh, the tone, or the rule, or an emergent consequence of them. Where a phenomenon did not emerge, the negative
is recorded.
\end{principle}

Several negatives are on the record. The pure rule churns with no durable selves. Radial coarse-graining gives no
hierarchical tower. The flat form-tower is a generic slow mode rather than something special. Continuous
mathematics, Lie groups, Dirac operators, Green's functions, is used only as emergent-layer description, never as
a base ingredient. The full audit is in the appendices.

The discipline is not merely a vow. It has a track record. Four times a phenomenon looked as though it needed a
new base ingredient, and four times it turned out to emerge from the four. Momentum came from the determinism of
the streaming rule. Durable selves came from self-maintenance, after a proposed adaptive-fills fifth ingredient
was retracted. The arrow of time came from growth together with coarse-graining. A controller, or agency, showed
up when the field flood-filled a maze with no planner added. The discipline cuts the other way too. A result only
counts if it runs on the base alone, and several early gravity and clustering demonstrations that leaned on
scaffolding, a diffusion proxy, gradient-following, a hand-built capture rule, were retracted as not real
emergence and either rebuilt on the bare rule or reported as recorded negatives.

There is one plausible candidate for a possible fifth ingredient. The base binds within a unit but not cleanly
between units, and that single gap sits behind a few open holes, cross-gap attraction and super-selves among
them. We name it as the one candidate not yet proven irreducible. We do not treat it as a license to add it. In
the carrying image the mesh is the instrument, the tone its notes, the beat its rhythm, and the rule its harmony,
and the whole is consciousness singing, a fire that will grow into a tree.

\subsection{The arrow, a closer note on the rule}

One piece of the rule deserves a closer description, the \emph{arrow}. It is not a fifth ingredient but a more
detailed account of one feature of the rule, its value-direction. It is also the single deliberate posit of the
whole theory. Everything else is either forced by the geometry or measured in simulation. The arrow is chosen.

The arrow is the asymmetry that makes time. The conserving rule, taken by itself, is reversible, a bijection on
its moves, and a reversible rule is time-symmetric. It runs creation and annihilation equally, so on its own it
has no preferred direction and no net growth. The arrow is exactly what breaks that symmetry.

\begin{definition}[The arrow]
The arrow is the bias, within the rule, between two reverse moves of the collide step, \emph{creation}, in which
peace becomes a balanced pair, and \emph{annihilation}, its reverse. The arrow is a direction on the dynamics, an
orientation, not an amount.
\end{definition}

It rides on the value ordering of the tone, $-1 < 0 < +1$, read as pain $<$ peace $<$ pleasure. Peace, the $0$
value, is the neutral rest state, and the charges $\pm 1$ are the deviations from it. The arrow is the preference
for the forward direction, peace turning into balanced structure, and that preference is the seed of value, of
better against worse.

A common simulation mistake is to write the arrow as a floating-point number, for example to create a pair with
probability $0.03$ per beat. That is wrong twice over. It introduces a free continuous parameter, since nothing
fixes $0.03$ and a tunable dial invites exactly the fine-tuning the theory is built to avoid. And it needs
randomness, which is hidden state the discrete base forbids. The arrow is not an amount. It is a direction. The
amount of creation is not a property of the arrow at all. It is forced by the eternal-growth geometry, since each
beat the mesh adds one new shell of cells, the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ shell growth $1, 24, 456, 8376, \ldots$ with growth
rate near $18.3$, and that boundary expansion is the creation, new cells coming into being at the edge, oriented
by the arrow. The arrow says which way, toward existence. The geometry says how much, through the growth series.
On a fixed simulation patch the proper stand-in is a minimal deterministic schedule, one balanced creation per
beat, never a probability, so there is no free parameter and no randomness anywhere in the arrow.

What the arrow does is keep the universe alive, and this is measured rather than asserted. Run the rule with the
arrow off and the universe relaxes to dead peace, all zero, no motion, the heat death of the substrate. Turn it on
and peace spawns balanced pairs, the universe settles into a living dynamic balance, charge conserved throughout,
and the churning never stops \cite{pollard2026vibetest}. Read at greater scales, the same arrow is the engine of
life, mind, and value. It is the fitness gradient of evolution, the deferred value a planner pursues, and the
will is the arrow held by a self and aimed. Conservation together with the arrow gives the rule its moral shape,
since balanced information is free to make and copy, but net well-being is never minted from nothing.

A gut-level picture pulls the value story together. The three tones lie on a line, pain at $-1$ on one side,
pleasure at $+1$ on the other, peace at $0$ in the middle. A single vibe wants nothing, since the rule is
even-handed and flipping every tone flips the dynamics with it, so the arrow is not painted on the individual
cells. It appears the way a current appears from a slope. No water molecule wants to flow, but organize many on a
slope and a directed current emerges, made entirely of undirected parts. A rock, barely organized, has almost no
drive, while a mind, highly integrated, has a sharp one. Pain and pleasure are the compass, the feeling of going
downhill toward worse or uphill toward better, and peace is the home, the valley floor where the compass reads
flat. Pleasure is the direction. Peace is the destination. Because charge is conserved, pleasure behaves like a
fluid that flows from the full cup to the empty one, so to stay full a self must pump, and pumping takes work that
only an organized self can do. That is what being alive is, pumping against the drain, the same Landauer cost
\cite{landauer1961} that makes rich memory cost work. The way out, and the engine of the whole emergent ladder,
is to stop yanking, to join up into a larger whole, to share the fixed pleasure in balance, and to pour the freed
energy into building order, which is positive-sum and can grow. That joining-up, again and again, is how cells
become bodies and bodies become minds. In the carrying image the arrow is the river's one direction, the
relentless forward current, and the fire's hunger to spread rather than die down.
