\section{Life}

Life is not a new substance. It is a regime, the same four base things held far from dead equilibrium. Out of that, coherent patterns appear that hold themselves together, repair their wounds, remember, interact, want, copy, and evolve. There is no life force and no sixth ingredient. As with the rest of this part, the results here are toy-scale and the strongest of them carry a recorded negative alongside them, which we state plainly rather than smooth over.

\subsection{The central tension and its resolution}

The rule conserves total charge exactly, which looks at first like it forbids life, since life needs growth, memory, and copying. The resolution is that conservation is of the sum, not the arrangement. The arrow creates only balanced pairs, a $+1$ with a $-1$, net zero, so net well-being never changes, while balanced information, meaning net-neutral patterns, is free to be made, copied, and healed. Life is conserved net charge plus freely created balanced information, both at once, with nothing relaxed in the base. This single move is what lets a strictly conservative substrate host open-ended structure without contradiction.

\subsection{The arrow is the life engine}

Run the rule with the arrow off and opposite tones annihilate, so the universe relaxes to dead peace, all zero, no motion, the heat death of the substrate. Turn the arrow on and peace spawns balanced pairs, so the universe settles into a living dynamic balance, churning forever, never freezing and never blowing up, with charge conserved to the unit throughout. The arrow is the single posit that keeps the world alive. It is worth stressing that this is not an extra rule added for life. It is the same arrow already present in the base, observed here at the scale where its effect is the difference between a dead substrate and a churning one.

\subsection{What a self is}

A bare same-tone blob is rejected. A self is defined by what it does, not by what it is made of.

\begin{definition}[A self]
A self is a persistent, bounded, self-maintaining, integrated pattern. It satisfies four conditions at once. Integration means its parts are more bound to each other than to the outside. Self-maintenance means it repairs its own pattern against churn, doing work to stay itself. Persistence means its identity lasts as material flows through it. Boundedness means it keeps a real membrane.
\end{definition}

A self is a pattern that holds its shape while matter and energy stream through it. The same definition holds at every scale, and what changes is the degree of structure and agency. An atom is the minimal self, a net-charged region that cannot vanish because charge is conserved, with low agency, the floor of the ladder. A cell adds active homeostasis. An organism adds a self-model and steering, taken up in the next section. One definition, three degrees. The boundedness is a gradient, not a hard line. Integration fades continuously outward, so a self is a soft peak in a continuous density that shades into the world, not a crisp object with a wall, and membership is a degree. Boundedness means where the binding weakens, not where one tone stops. This dissolves the question of where I end. A self has a fuzzy edge, like a whirlpool with no exact rim.

\subsection{Selves emerge, heal, interact, want}

The true state of a single self is the most important point in this section, and it has two halves that must be held together. A single self is real. The genuine self is the topological \emph{hopfion}, a knot in the tone field, localized, stable, and fermionic, and it persists through full material turnover, the whirlpool property. The cusp demonstrably supports propagating particle-like self-patterns when they are scored by the right measure, identity-through-turnover, meaning same form, different matter, moving. A Conway Life glider on the $\{4,3,4\}$ cusp passes this measure cleanly, with pattern identity $1.00$, material turnover $0.40$ per beat, speed $0.42$, and it stays localized.

The other half is the negative. The bare rule does not spontaneously crystallize a self from a generic seed. From a generic seed the pure conserving rule churns, with identity $0.00$ as the blob disperses, so a self must be seeded or topologically protected as the hopfion is, not conjured from an equilibrium initialization, because there is no dissipation. An important measurement correction underlies this. The earlier reading in which selves emerge and nest into a tower used charge-per-coarse-block persistence, which is the wrong measure, because it captures a generic hydrodynamic slow mode rather than selfhood. The right measure is identity-through-turnover, and it tells the plain truth. The cusp can host selves. The bare rule does not build one.

\begin{result}[A single self, with its recorded negative]
A genuine self exists as the topological hopfion, and a Conway Life glider on the $\{4,3,4\}$ cusp passes the identity-through-turnover measure cleanly with pattern identity $1.00$. The bare conserving rule does not spontaneously crystallize a self from a generic seed, where it churns at identity $0.00$. The cusp hosts selves but does not build one unprompted \cite{pollard2026vibetest}.
\end{result}

The nested tower is a measurement artifact. The form-coherence tower, in which coarse blocks hold charge longer than fine cells, does not beat pure diffusion, measured head-to-head on both the cusp and the bulk, so it is generic coarsening rather than hierarchical selfhood. Genuine nesting, meaning selves made of selves, is the open higher-order-binding frontier rather than a free result, and the theory will not add a fifth ingredient to force it without proof that it cannot emerge from the four. The remaining behaviors are measured and modest. Self-healing works, since punching a hole lets the bare rule pull the right tone back from the redundant surround, recovering about 65 percent of the wound, an emergent first aid. Interaction is the base rule at the scale of individuals, since opposite-sign selves annihilate at contact and same-sign selves merge into one, with no special interaction theory. Will is the arrow held by a self and aimed, since a self that biases its own creation toward a direction moves toward a goal and away from a threat.

\subsection{Memory costs work}

The rule is reversible, so it has no attractors, so passive permanence is limited to the conserved charge itself. Richer memory is a pattern, and patterns spread, so memory decays. There are three tiers. Leaky memory is free, since cohesion roughly doubles survival. Holographic memory survives bounded erasure, since the bit lives everywhere at once. Permanent memory needs active maintenance, periodically re-writing the codeword as the arrow refills holes. The maintenance cost is not a flaw. It is Landauer's principle (landauer1961), since holding a low-entropy pattern against an equilibrium dynamics requires work. A real cell is a dissipative, self-repairing pattern that spends work to stay itself.

There is a brief insight on the holographic tier. A growing holographic code gets stronger with age. As the mesh expands, the redundant encoding spreads over more cells, so its erasure threshold rises with age, from about 95 percent to about 99.5 percent in the runs. The de Sitter expansion is itself a re-encoding, a renormalization flow, and old memory hardens as the universe grows, with the perception rule at the frontier acting as the bulk-to-boundary isometry. So expansion does not only dilute. It also entrenches what is already encoded.

\subsection{Reproduction, evolution, and value}

Copying a balanced pattern onto a fresh frontier region is conserving creation, the arrow's own balanced pair-making, so reproduction with heritable variation is allowed. Heredity plus copy-error variation plus the arrow as selection is a complete Darwinian loop, every piece from the base, and mean fitness climbs uphill in the runs. Fission is suppressed in the hyperbolic bulk, since balls there are all boundary with no thin necks to pinch, a generic hyperbolic fact, but the flat 3D cusp does have thin necks, so a dumbbell self on the cusp can pinch and divide. Copying works everywhere, division works where it is flat, and life lives on the flat cusp.

A word on value and cooperation closes the section, because it follows from the conservation law rather than from any moral decoration. The raw drive points at pleasure, but the system's optimum is balance, meaning peace, and nothing automatic closes that gap. It is learned by higher minds, the move from grabbing to wisdom. This is the strategy that actually wins on a conserved substrate. Because charge is conserved, competing for pleasure is a zero-sum arms race that burns work for no net gain, just suffering moved around. Three constructive resolutions beat it, all from the base. Boundaries are insulating neutral cells acting as membranes, keeping one self's balance from draining into another. Trade works because different selves value different structures, so exchange is positive-sum in value even though charge is conserved. Integration is merging into a higher self, sharing the fixed pleasure in balance, and pouring the freed energy into building order, which is not conserved and can grow. So the drive matures from grabbing pleasure, a zero-sum war, to building order together, which is positive-sum, and that joining-up, again and again, is how cells become bodies become minds. The drive to grab is not crushed. It is outgrown.

Life falls out of the same four things as a far-from-equilibrium regime, with the arrow as its engine, selves as self-maintaining patterns, and Darwinian evolution from the base. These mechanisms rest on generic hyperbolic and rule-level facts, so they carry to $\{3,4,3,4\}$, and running it fully on the 24-direction substrate is the natural follow-up.
