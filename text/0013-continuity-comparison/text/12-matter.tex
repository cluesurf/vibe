\section{Matter}

This is the richest correlation and the sharpest open problem. Many independent
programs reach the same exceptional algebra from the top. The base is the only
one that builds it from the bottom, a discrete ternary automaton. The shared
nucleus sits on one chain, from $D_4$ through the octonions toward $E_8$. The
24-cell is the $D_4$ root system, its 24 vertices the unit integral octonions
\cite{coxeter1973, conway2003, hurwitz1898}. $F_4$, the order-1152 cell symmetry,
is the automorphism group of the Albert algebra $J_3(\mathbb{O})$ of dimension
27, the cell's internal state \cite{gunaydin1973}. The Standard Model gauge group
descends from the octonions \cite{furey2018, baez2002octonions}, and the weak
mixing angle $\sin^2\theta_W = 3/8$ at unification is the standard grand-unified
value \cite{georgi1974}. The nucleus is firm. The three generations are open on
every side.

\begin{finding}
One full generation is built, not posited (\texttt{E-FND-0020}), with the 24-cell coin a
firm base result (\texttt{E-GMT-0026}). The cell's 24 directions are the octonion seed,
their seven imaginary units acting by left multiplication as an exact
seven-generator Clifford algebra. Pairing six generators into three ladder
modes gives eight states with color multiplicities $1,3,3,1$ and electric
charges $0$, $1/3$ thrice, $2/3$ thrice, and $1$, the neutrino, three down
quarks, three up quarks, and the electron, with a mirror copy supplying the
antiparticles for sixteen states in all. Given the octonion seed the structure
is rigid: the Clifford algebra, the three modes, the $1,3,3,1$ pattern, and the
fractional charges are all \textbf{Forced}. This is Tier F for one generation.
Three chiral generations are Tier O, \textbf{Open}.
\end{finding}

The word ``Forced'' is exact for one generation and must not be stretched
further. The 24 directions are the root system of $D_4$, the same 24 unit
integral octonions, so the nucleus is not added by hand, the cell's own directions
are the octonions \cite{hurwitz1898}. Their left-multiplication matrices square
to minus one and anticommute, the defining pattern of a Clifford algebra. This is
exact rather than approximate, because what compose are the multiplication
matrices, not the non-associative octonions. Climb one level and the cell's
finite symmetry $F_4$ is the automorphism group of $J_3(\mathbb{O})$, a 27-state
internal algebra whose long roots are the 24 directions \cite{gunaydin1973,
baez2002octonions}. Pairing the generators into three modes and setting the
electric charge to a third of the filled count delivers one generation's colors
and charges with no free parameter.

The qualification falls on $3/8$. The weak mixing angle at unification is a
counting fact about the charges above, and the number that appears is the
standard grand-unified value of Georgi and Glashow and of $SO(10)$
\cite{georgi1974}, which the base reproduces from its discrete charge content and
which then runs to about $0.231$ at the $Z$ mass. The counting is firm once the
one-generation charge content is in hand, but $3/8$ is a known group-theory
value, reproduced and not freshly derived, and the low-energy match uses standard
renormalization-group running, which is external physics input. That running is
Tier B. It is fed in, and it must never be read as a pure substrate prediction.
No Standard Model coupling or mass is derived here. They remain the free
parameters the Standard Model already has, each now carrying a geometric origin
rather than a value.

The trouble begins at the threefold. $D_4$ has a unique order-three symmetry,
triality, cycling three eight-dimensional pictures written $8v + 8s + 8c$, and it
is tempting to read those three as three generations. That reading fails. The
$8s$ and $8c$ are the two handednesses of one generation and $8v$ is the vector
that carries the gauge force, so the natural triple is a vector plus two
chiralities, not three matter families (\texttt{E-SPN-0041}). A cleaner threefold lives in
the Albert algebra, whose frame has exactly three primitive slots, three because
the Jordan rule holds for octonionic Hermitian matrices up to size three and
breaks at size four where non-associativity sets in. The counting is forced with
size four as the control that must fail, and it does. But identifying those three
algebraic slots with the three observed generations is Boyle's conjecture, and it
is unproven \cite{boyle2020}.

The mass hierarchy is a recorded negative (\texttt{E-SPN-0039}). If the three slots were
the three generations their masses should split widely, and the base supplies no
mechanism. The three slots carry an exact threefold family symmetry, a symmetric
vacuum keeps all three masses equal, and the two asymmetric vacua that do split
them give two different ratios with no preferred choice, so the splitting carries
no geometric information. The mass hierarchy is a free Yukawa input, not a
substrate output, because deterministic dynamics cannot break an exact symmetry
on its own. For this base Boyle's conjecture resolves negative on the hierarchy
(\texttt{E-SPN-0016}). The continuum embeddings that try to fit three generations into
$E_8$ hit the Distler-Garibaldi no-go, which forces mirror fermions nature does
not contain and which has stood for over fifteen years \cite{distler2010}. The
only conceivable escape is that the no-go is a continuum theorem while the base is
discrete, and that has not been shown.

The status is stated plainly. Two real, forced threefolds exist on the substrate,
triality of order three and the Albert algebra as three times nine, and their
identification with the three observed generations is Open on both sides. The
naive dimensional match fails. This is a single wall, the same wall Furey,
Lisi, Dixon, and Dray-Manogue attack from the continuum side
\cite{furey2018, baez2002octonions}. Triality having the right count is necessary,
not sufficient, and the base does not imply a derivation it has not made. One
generation is compelling and forced. Three chiral generations are not
derived.
