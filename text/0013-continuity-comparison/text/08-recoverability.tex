\section{Recoverability}

One number ties together results the base reaches separately: entropy, the area
law, the boundary screen, and measurement. The recoverability of an observer is
$R$, the accessible structure over the total structure. It is a single currency
for three things at once. Thermodynamics is lost recoverability. Decoherence is
recoverability flowing into the environment. A horizon is recoverability bleeding
across a boundary. The functional is imported from the conserved-current program
\cite{herbert2025chronoflux} and realized directly on the substrate.

\begin{finding}
\texttt{E-GRV-0040} lays a localized deterministic signal on the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ mesh and
reads the same $R$ with three observers. The global observer recovers everything,
so $R = 1$ at every beat. The window observer watches $R$ fall toward zero as
structure streams across a fixed boundary, the horizon regime. The coarse
observer holds $R$ capped under one half as the fine signed current cancels
inside blocks, the decoherence regime. A lossy control destroys structure, so
even the global observer reads only $0.958$. Tier F, \textbf{Derived}. One
functional read by three observers gives the three faces of recoverability.
\end{finding}

Start with what accessible-over-total means. Lay down a structured signal. The
total is everything in that signal, the whole of what is really there. The
accessible part is how much of it a given observer can reconstruct, given what it
is allowed to see and how finely it can look. Recoverability is the accessible
part divided by the total, running from zero to one. The key move is that the
total never changes. Only what an observer can reach changes, so the same signal
yields a different number for a different observer, and that single number is the
whole story.

The global observer sees the entire mesh at full resolution and watches every
beat. Under the reversible rule it recovers everything, so its recoverability is
exactly one, forever. The rule is a reversible permutation that never destroys
structure, only moves it from here to there, so nothing the signal carried is
ever lost to a watcher who can see all of it. This is conservation read as a
number. The window observer can only look through a bounded patch. The signal
does not stay put, and beat by beat structure streams out of the window into the
region beyond. The observer cannot follow it across the boundary, so its
recoverability bleeds toward zero. Nothing was destroyed. The global observer of
the first reading still reads one. The structure is simply beyond reach, on the
far side of a boundary the windowed observer cannot cross. That is what a horizon
is.

The coarse observer can see the whole mesh, but only through a blur. It reads
averages over blocks and never the fine signed detail inside a block. The signal
carries pluses and minuses sitting side by side inside those blocks, and when the
observer averages they cancel, so the fine structure vanishes from its view and
its recoverability is capped under a half. The structure is fully present. The
global observer still reads one. The coarse observer just cannot resolve what
cancels inside its blocks. That is decoherence, a signal present and unreadable at
the same time, lost to averaging rather than to destruction.

The lossy control is the discriminator that keeps this clean. Three restricted
observers all report loss, and the danger is that loss from restriction looks
identical to loss from destruction, in which case the whole picture would prove
nothing. So we run a rule that genuinely destroys structure as it goes. Now even
the unrestricted global observer cannot read one. Its recoverability drops to
$0.958$. Under the reversible base only restricted observers lose and the global
observer always recovers everything. Under a lossy base the structure itself is
gone, so even the all-seeing observer comes up short. A limited observer's loss is
lost access. A lossy base's loss is lost structure. The single number tells them
apart.

These are not three physics problems forced under one label. They are the same
ratio read by observers with three different limits. Conservation is the global
reading, a horizon is the windowed reading, and decoherence is the coarse
reading. The base did not need a new mechanism for thermodynamics. A reversible
conserving rule plus a bounded observer already carries it, and $R$ is the one
number that reads it out. This is the discrete, integer-checked counterpart to
the thermodynamic-gravity programs that read gravity as an information force on a
screen \cite{jacobson1995, padmanabhan2010, verlinde2011}, to the entanglement
account of emergent geometry \cite{vanraamsdonk2010}, and to the observer-as-
boundary framing where classicality is earned rather than free
\cite{fields2018}. The coarse-causal-power measures of the effective-information
program \cite{hoel2013} give a second yardstick on the intervention side, a
cousin of recoverability read from how much causal power a coarse layer holds
\cite{cluesurf2026repo}.
