\section{The Experiments}

Every result in this paper is checked by a reproducible computation on the substrate, never a measurement against nature.
The full suite is a finite, deterministic, runnable battery \cite{cluesurf2026repo}. This appendix explains the code
scheme, the depth grades, the counts, and indexes the key experiments cited in the paper.

\subsection*{The code scheme}

Each experiment carries a stable code, registered once in the catalog, of the form E-XXX-NNNN. The XXX is a three-letter
arena that groups by topic, GRV for gravity, RLT for relativity, SPN for spin, FLD for fluid, CSM for cosmology, MTH for
method, FND for foundations, and so on. The NNNN is a four-digit number, unique within the arena. The four anchors of this
paper are \texttt{E-GRV-0039}, \texttt{E-GRV-0040}, \texttt{E-GRV-0041}, and \texttt{E-MTH-0001}. Open frontiers are tracked by a separate E-DEC planning
label, which marks an arena's decisive test rather than a catalog entry.

\subsection*{The counts}

The evidence base is 615 experiments across 18 categories. 84 sit at L3, 389 at L2, 128 at L1, and 14 at L0. Of these, 412
back a specific claim in the papers. A result counts only as the gap between the run and its control. Pass conditions are
fixed before running. Where possible they are exact integer or ratio equalities with no tolerance, so a result is an
identity rather than a fit. A run is bit-identical on two machines.

A handful of established results are reproduced across several experiments and cited without a single code: the area law, the
$1/r$ potential, the Born rule, CHSH and the Tsirelson bound, the contextuality values, and fermion universality. Two
attempts in this round did not build cleanly and are reported as such. A metric from the bare tone correlations failed
because the tone field is short-range. A clean geometry-dependent settling time failed because the observable is too noisy.
A negative result is itself a finding.
