\section{Gravity}

Several of the strongest top-down programs reach the same claim. Gravity is
the thermodynamics of the entanglement that holds geometry together. Jacobson
derived the Einstein equation as an equation of state from horizon entropy
\cite{jacobson1995} and later from vacuum entanglement equilibrium
\cite{jacobson2016}. Padmanabhan read gravity off the counting of surface
degrees of freedom \cite{padmanabhan2010}. Verlinde made it an entropic force
\cite{verlinde2011}. Van Raamsdonk made the claim literal, that geometric
connectivity is entanglement structure, so that cutting the entanglement between
two regions tears the space between them apart \cite{vanraamsdonk2010}. Every one
of these needs a finite count of states and a microscopic substrate it does not
supply. The base supplies exactly that, and reaches the same result by a different
route.

\begin{finding}
On a free-fermion realization whose hopping graph is the bond geometry
(\texttt{E-HLG-0021}), dialing one throat bond from open to pinched drives down both the
entanglement entropy across the throat and the geometric connectivity that sews the
two halves, with correlation near one, while the throat
connectivity collapses below a tenth and a fixed far bond keeps over half of its
own. The geometry pinches off exactly where, and only where, the entanglement is
removed. In the mediator witness (\texttt{E-GRV-0041}) two regions sharing no direct bond
become entangled only through a geometric mediator: their mutual information
covaries with the mediator strength at correlation $0.96$ and collapses to order
$10^{-24}$ when the mediator is cut, while a direct bypass bond retains $0.997$
of the entanglement under the same cut. The co-variation is integer- and
correlation-checked with controls that could have failed. The rung as a whole is
Tier P, \textbf{Parallel}: the entanglement-geometry and thermodynamic-gravity
story is reproduced, a full stress tensor sourcing curvature is not.
\end{finding}

The throat result is Van Raamsdonk's picture made spatial and concrete. Split
the system into a left half and a right half. The entanglement across the cut is
the shared structure neither half holds alone, and the geometric connectivity is
how much of the bond web actually bridges the two halves. In the experiment the
bonds are also the channels along which the halves come to share structure, so
closing the one throat bond reduces both readings at once. They fall together
because they are two views of one fact: how strongly the bond ties the halves
together. The far bond is the control. If closing one link quietly broke the
whole graph, the result would be an artifact. It does not, so the severing of
geometry and the loss of entanglement are one event.

The mediator witness is the sharper test, the discrete version of the proposed
tabletop experiment where two masses entangle through their mutual gravity
\cite{marletto2017, bose2017}. The logic is borrowed from constructor theory: a
merely classical go-between cannot entangle two systems that never interact
directly. It can correlate them, the way two letters from one sender are
correlated, but it cannot entangle them into a single joint state. So if the two regions
end up genuinely entangled, and the only path between them was the mediator, the
mediator was not classical. The covariance shows it, the severance confirms it,
and the bypass bond rules out the trivial explanation that cutting any bond
disrupts everything. The mediating geometry must itself carry quantum
structure, and that is read off without ever measuring the mediator.

This is the link the continuum programs assume, now shown on a discrete base. The
same base delivers the finite state count Jacobson and Padmanabhan need, and the
strict determinism the holographic constructions usually reach only through
ensemble averaging. Jacobson, Padmanabhan, Verlinde, and Van Raamsdonk all point
at one destination, and the substrate reaches it from below
\cite{cluesurf2026repo}.

The open items remain. The base reproduces the
entanglement-builds-geometry link and the thermodynamic framing, but it does not
yet derive an Einstein-like field equation from coarse-grained tone-stress rather
than asserting one. Fully reversible curved-bulk gravity is Open. The dark-sector
scoreboard, the radial-acceleration relation that must not depend on mass alone,
is a real-world constraint the substrate has not yet met. Stated plainly,
this rung runs Parallel, and deriving a stress tensor that sources curvature
remains the unsolved problem.
