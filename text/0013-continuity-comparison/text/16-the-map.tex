\section{The Map}

The bridge is real, and it is the strongest correlation in the corpus. Most of the spine is now
measured rather than asserted. Some of it runs parallel to independent programs by a different route. A short list of walls
stays open, and they are shared with everyone working this problem. The map below sorts every result into one of three
tiers. \textbf{Firm} means established on the substrate, with a control that could have failed, graded Derived. \textbf{Parallel}
means the same destination reached by an independent route, not a derivation of the continuum statement, graded Parallel.
\textbf{Open} means a well-posed conjecture with a stated falsifying test, unsolved on both sides, graded Open.

The firm tier rests on exact, checkable results. The conserved current and its exact continuity closure hold as integer-exact identities,
on the flat torus and on the genuinely curved $\{3,4,3,4\}$ mesh alike. One recoverability functional carries horizons,
decoherence, and conservation together. Two regions entangle only through the geometric mediator. The order-1152 coin
gives a four-dimensional lattice gas the isotropy it needs, where cubic coins fail. The 24 directions act as the binary
tetrahedral double cover, so a full turn negates a spinor. One substrate assignment passes every arena, and perturbing one
lever breaks a cluster. These are measurements on an explicit object.

The middle tier runs parallel. Vibe reaches the Born rule and the Tsirelson bound from a deterministic base with no
injected randomness, instantiates spin as concrete 24-cell circulation, builds geometry from entanglement on the
substrate, and supplies the finite state count the thermodynamic gravity arguments need for free. These are the same results
reached by an independent route, not derivations of the continuum statements they echo.

Chronoflux expansion scalar is already done. The lattice fluid bound and geometry-dependent settling are still owed, with the
