The bottom two rungs are Derived on the substrate. Conservation is exact, and its coarse-grained face is the continuity law. These are the firm floor the rest stands on.

The middle rungs are Parallel. Vibe reaches the Born rule, the Tsirelson bound, doubly-special dispersion, area-law gravity, and the $1/r$ potential, but by a discrete route rather than by the continuum's amplitude-phase or thermodynamic argument. We do not claim these as derivations of the continuum statements.

The open rungs are listed below with their falsifiers. Full Lorentz invariance on a fixed lattice is the sharpest. The explicit Einstein-like closure must be derived from coarse-grained tone-stress, not asserted. The Navier-Stokes rung shows both faces, the fine quadratic conserved exactly in \texttt{E-FLD-0009} and the coarse gradient rising only transiently and recurring in \texttt{E-FLD-0010}, so the reversible lattice has no blow-up at either scale and no Millennium-grade regularity proof is claimed. The variation-to-geometry rung is reached only through a gapless free-fermion proxy, because the bare ternary tone field is short-range. The perturbation spectrum of cosmology is open even where the background expansion and the wake-divergence identity of \texttt{E-CSM-0043} are firm.
