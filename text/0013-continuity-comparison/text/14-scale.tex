\section{Scale}

The whole upward program assumes the base coarse-grains into a tower of faithful layers, each with a law of its own, stacked from the cell up to the continuum. Coarse-graining is the single bridge that builds the tower. Group the cells into blocks, read one summary value off each block, and ask how those summaries change from beat to beat. If the blurred picture has a law of its own that does not secretly depend on the fine detail thrown away, the layer is real rather than a convenient label. The question for the whole tower is whether those laws hold at every level without contradicting each other.

The precise test of a single rung is the commuting square. From a micro state there are two routes to the coarse state one beat later. Run the real rule one beat and then coarse-grain, or coarse-grain first and then run the effective rule on the summaries. When the two routes land on the same corner, the coarse layer is closed and faithful. When they disagree, the layer leaks and keeps needing the fine grain to predict itself. Whether the full tower is self-similar, with one effective rule recurring step after step and a single geometric ratio relating each layer to the next, is E-DEC-03, ready to build. The candidate ratio is the per-shell warp factor near 18.28, an exact Perron number forced by the honeycomb, with the shell counts 1, 24, 456, 8376 exact. A flat Euclidean control is the discriminator, since coarse-graining cleans up almost any substrate a little and the result only counts if the flat run fails to clean up while the curved one succeeds. The yardstick for whether each rung is causally real is effective information per layer \cite{hoel2013}, the measure of when a coarse account becomes a sharper cause than the level below it. The tower is one more discrete-to-continuum route of the kind the computational-universe and cellular-automaton programs explore \cite{lloyd2002, kari2005, ord2002}.

\begin{finding}
One assignment of the shared substrate levers passes every arena. Pulling a single lever drops a whole cluster of arenas at once, so the theory is rigid rather than a set of separately tuned fits.
\end{finding}

The discipline that keeps the rungs from contradicting each other is the consistency matrix. Put the arenas down the side and the few substrate parameters across the top, fix one assignment of those parameters, and derive every arena from that single assignment. There is no private column of knobs per arena. The same two levers, the {3,4,3,4} frame with its 24-direction coin and the conserving reversible rule, feed every cell of the table. \texttt{E-MTH-0001} runs four arenas under three assignments with the refutation thresholds fixed in advance.

Each off-assignment breaks a cluster of cells at once rather than just one. Swap in the lossy erasing rule and conservation, reversibility, and continuity fall together, because all three hang on the same conserved property, so the matrix passes only one arena. Swap in the cubic six-direction coin and isotropy fails, since the 24-cell zeroes a fourth-moment anisotropy that the cubic frame leaves stuck, and the four-dimensional continuity closure is undefined on a three-dimensional frame, so that arena falls too. Only the committed assignment closes the matrix. This is Tier F, \textbf{derived}.

The single-lever structure is what makes the theory falsifiable as a whole. Because the levers are shared, no failing arena can be repaired without risking another, and there is no per-result dial to absorb a bad fit. A single real disagreement would not just dent one corner. It would force a shared lever and drag a whole cluster of currently passing arenas down with it. That is the opposite of a collection of separate fits, each with its own knob, where a bad arena is patched in isolation. This first matrix has four firm arenas. The fuller matrix the program aims at adds the open arenas, where the refutation conditions are stated but the cells are not yet green: exact Lorentz invariance on a fixed lattice, the explicit gravity closure, three chiral generations, and selves as causally real. The discipline is the same at every rung. State the thresholds first, run, and report the result, pass or recorded negative.
