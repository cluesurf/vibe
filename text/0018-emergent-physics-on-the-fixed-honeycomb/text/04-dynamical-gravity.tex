\section{Warped Gravity, Horizon Walks, and GR Checks}
\label{sec:gravity}

Section~\ref{sec:relativity} gave the husk relativistic motion and a shared speed $c_u$, but it borrowed the depth warp
that sets $c_u$. This section derives the need for that warp from gravity. Place a mass on one husk dock and ask what
field it makes on the husk around it. In flat 3D space the answer is a
$1/r$ potential. On the honeycomb, the flux from that mass does not stay on the husk. Each floor of the beam behind it
is about eighteen times larger than the one before, so the flux pours down into the bulk and the husk potential dies
off exponentially. The bulk screens gravity. This section asks what weighting of the beam stops the screening, what
orbits then look like, and what light does near a horizon.

The section has three parts. A prescribed pair-density warp $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$ restores an unscreened $1/r$
potential with $G_{\mathrm{eff}} = 0.078593$ and live Newtonian orbits (Tier~B, \coderange{E-GRV-0154}{0158}). An exact
walk on the infinite honeycomb under a prescribed inward drift tips both light rays inward at
$r_{\mathrm{areal}} = 0.6944\, r_s$ (Tier~B, \code{E-GRV-0162}, \code{E-GRV-0163}). Continuum general-relativity (GR)
benchmarks, which take $(G_{\mathrm{eff}}, c_u)$ as inputs and impose the metric by hand, run alongside (Tier~C,
\coderange{E-GRV-0159}{0161}).

\subsection{Bulk screening and the pair-density warp}

With shells $N_D = 1, 18, 330, 6{,}034$ (Table~\ref{tab:shells}), an unweighted cusp Laplacian keeps a bulk spectral
gap of at least $24 - 16.02$ (\code{E-GMT-0044}) and screens husk potentials exponentially (\code{E-GRV-0156}). The
remedy is to make deep links harder to cross. Sweeping depth conductance weights $\ell^{-p}$ across odd Busemann levels
$\ell \in \{1, 3, \ldots, 49\}$ over 1,415 Bloch solves (\code{E-GRV-0154}, \code{E-GRV-0155}, worst relative residual
$3.5 \times 10^{-13}$) determines the exact polynomial resistance scaling $\mathcal{R}(F) \sim F^{q_{\mathcal{R}}}$
separating screened and unscreened beams (Table~\ref{tab:warp-sweep}): the infinite honeycomb is unscreened if and
only if $p \ge 3$, leaving the Randall--Sundrum~II exponent ($p = 1$) \cite{randall1999} screened while $p = 6$ gives
$q_{\mathcal{R}} = +3.02$ and correlation length $\xi = 184$ docks at Busemann level $49$.

The exponent $p = 6$ has a simple reading. Horosphere volume scales as $N_D \sim \ell^3$, so the density of a lone
excitation spread over a floor dilutes as $1/N_D \sim \ell^{-3}$, and the density of Scale-2 pair meetings, which needs
two excitations in one dock, dilutes as its square, $w(D) = 1/N_D^2 \sim \ell^{-6}$.
With 18 downward vertical bonds per dock at depth $D$ weighted by the geometric mean
$\sqrt{w(D)\, w(D+1)} = 1/(N_D N_{D+1})$, the exact vertical link conductance $c_{\mathrm{vert}}(D)$ and cumulative
beam resistance $\mathcal{R}(F)$ implemented in \code{E-GRV-0156} and \code{E-GRV-0157} are
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
w(D) &= \frac{1}{N_D^2},
\qquad
c_{\mathrm{vert}}(D) = 18 N_D \sqrt{w(D)\, w(D+1)} = \frac{18}{N_{D+1}}, \\
\mathcal{R}(F) &= \sum_{D=0}^{F} \frac{1}{c_{\mathrm{vert}}(D)} = \frac{1}{18} \sum_{D=0}^{F} N_{D+1},
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:pair-warp}
\end{equation}
giving $\mathcal{R}(12) = 1.48 \times 10^{15}$ and $\xi = \sqrt{\mathcal{R}(12)} \approx 3.85 \times 10^7$ docks at
graph depth $F = 12$. Defining the unnormalized integer sum $\tilde{\mathcal{R}}(F) = \sum_{D=0}^{F-1} N_D$ certified
by $17\tilde{\mathcal{R}}(F) + 1 \ge 18^F$ in \code{Vibe/Geometry.lean} (Section~\ref{sec:unified}), the two conventions
are linked identically by $18\mathcal{R}(F) = \tilde{\mathcal{R}}(F+2) - 1$. Under Equation~\eqref{eq:pair-warp},
vertical resistance grows exponentially with depth, so flux that enters the beam meets an ever stiffer wall and returns
to the husk, with no reflecting floor and no added depth register. The warp $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$ is a \textbf{Tier~B
imposed measure}. The pair-density reading motivates it, but it is not yet derived from the bare rule.

\subsection{Static \texorpdfstring{$1/r$}{1/r} kernel and two-body orbits}

With the warp in place, the field of a husk mass can be computed exactly. The first attempt, \code{E-GRV-0156},
establishes the exponential resistance $\mathcal{R}(12) = 1.48 \times 10^{15}$ but fails its pre-registered slope gate
on a \mbox{\code{side = 16}} torus ($-1.8177$, with aliased period-2 lateral links). The exact Schur-complement husk
Green function (Dirichlet floor at $D = F + 1$, resolved by Madelung
correction on a $64^3$ cubic husk in \code{E-GRV-0157}, Tier~B) converges floor-independently by floor~12. Under the
canonical unaliased single-face lateral links \mbox{$l_D = 1, 4, 20, 100$} (cumulative $5^D$, Table~\ref{tab:shells}),
the effective husk stiffness and geometric Newton coupling are
\begin{equation}
K_{\mathrm{eff}} = 1.012532,
\qquad
G_{\mathrm{eff}} = \frac{1}{4\pi K_{\mathrm{eff}}} = 0.078593,
\label{eq:geff}
\end{equation}
while the aliased \mbox{\code{period = 2}} torus comparison control ($1, 10, 40, 200$, cumulative $2 \cdot 5^D$) shifts
$K_{\mathrm{eff}}$ by $+1.85\%$ to $1.031237$ and $G_{\mathrm{eff}}$ by $-1.81\%$ to $0.077167$. Across $r = 2\text{--}10$,
the warped husk matches the massless 3D cubic lattice Green function within $0.000044\%$ ($4.41 \times 10^{-7}$),
whereas the raw unwarped bulk control collapses to \mbox{$G(5)/G(1) = 4.35 \times 10^{-6}$}.

To follow two bodies moving in this field, \code{E-GRV-0158} couples two quantum wavepackets
(with continuum non-relativistic $k^2/(2m)$ kinetics) to this kernel via unitary, time-reversible Strang split-step Schr\"odinger--Poisson evolution on an open $32^3$ domain
embedded in a $64^3$ zero-padded Hockney FFT \cite{hockney1970} (Tier~B). The run recovers inverse-square mutual
infall, unequal-mass Newton's third law ($2.17 \times 10^{-9}$ on open Hockney, $2.04 \times 10^{-14}$ on the
separate periodic $T^3$ control), a bound $91.6^\circ$ Keplerian orbit ($\omega_{\mathrm{orb}}$ error $0.073\%$), and
$18.8^\circ$ hyperbolic flyby deflection, while the raw bulk control sends the binary straight apart.

Relativistic corrections are tested at Tier~C. Three continuum GR benchmarks (\coderange{E-GRV-0159}{0161}, not derived
from the bare automaton) take $(G_{\mathrm{eff}}, c_u)$ from Equations~\eqref{eq:geff} and~\eqref{eq:speed-lock} as
inputs and check that standard relativistic gravity is consistent with them. First, \code{E-GRV-0159} (reclassified to Tier~C) prescribes the continuum
isotropic parameterized post-Newtonian (PPN) metric $A(r) = 1 - 2u + 2\beta_{\mathrm{PPN}} u^2$,
$B(r) = 1 + 2\gamma_{\mathrm{PPN}} u$ with $\gamma_{\mathrm{PPN}} = \beta_{\mathrm{PPN}} = 1$ imposed by hand (scaling
the single-site clock phase directly by $\sqrt{A(r)}$ by hand) and verifies numerical recovery of gravitational clock
redshift, Eddington $2\times$ light bending, Shapiro time delay, and 6th-order Yoshida symplectic perihelion precession
\cite{yoshida1990} against temporal-only ($\gamma_{\mathrm{PPN}} = 0$) and Newtonian controls, without deriving
$\gamma_{\mathrm{PPN}} = \beta_{\mathrm{PPN}} = 1$. Second, \code{E-GRV-0160} checks linearized Fierz--Pauli
$\gamma_{\mathrm{PPN}} = 1$ \cite{fierz1939}, a 21-step Lichnerowicz--Einstein diffuse star exterior
($r_s / R_* = 0.054$), and Regge--Einstein deficit convergence \cite{regge1961}. Third, \code{E-GRV-0161} checks a
4,168-orbit 1PN/2.5PN inspiral stopping at $6.98\, r_g$ before the innermost stable circular orbit (ISCO, with no
plunge or merger), warped-beam TT wave propagation (with raw bulk suppression $2.86 \times 10^{-87}$), and an
independent vacuum Schwarzschild $\ell = 2, n = 0$ Regge--Wheeler/Zerilli quasinormal ringdown (QNM) benchmark
evaluated separately from Part~1's pre-ISCO binary and \code{E-GRV-0160}'s diffuse star. Table~\ref{tab:gravity}
collects all verified canonical single-face metrics and controls.

\subsection{Gullstrand--Painlev\'e walk on the infinite honeycomb}

The third part asks what light does close to a mass, where a horizon should form. Two obstacles stand
in the way on finite balls. A static ball has an artificial outer horizon at $t = R$. And any $2 \times 2$ unitary
coin on a static symmetric shift forces $v_r(\mathrm{out}) + v_r(\mathrm{in}) = 0$, so the two light rays can never
both tilt inward (as the cached-ball sweep \code{E-GRV-0162} observes). Experiment \code{E-GRV-0163} (Tier~B / L2)
removes both obstacles. It walks the infinite honeycomb on the fly in exact $O^+(4,1; \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}])$
arithmetic and compiles every coin into exact $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$ words in the $SU(2)$ generators of
Section~\ref{sec:quantum}, with external force and metric angles looked up in \code{Float64}.

First, on-the-fly $O^+(4,1; \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}])$ neighbor generation reproduces static ball geometry identically and
computes exact \code{BigInt} Busemann depths without a pre-allocated boundary, allowing a constant flat-coin walk to
traverse beyond $R = 3$ to radii $r = 4, 5$ with zero frontier hits. Second, every Hartree and local flat-coin operator
compiles into exact \code{BigInt} words over $\{W_1, W_1^\dagger, W_2, W_2^\dagger\}$ (matching Section~\ref{sec:quantum})
with zero determinant or unitarity residual and maximum operator distance $3.96 \times 10^{-4}$, reproducing the
2-beat instantaneous Hartree mean-field two-body attraction of \code{E-GRV-0162} within $0.028\%$ (equal mass) and
$0.029\%$ ($1:3$ mass ratio), explicitly testing instantaneous pairwise static-kernel coupling rather than retarded
bulk gravitational mediation (Table~\ref{tab:inf-engine}).

Third, the horizon. In the river form of Gullstrand--Painlev\'e (GP) coordinates
\cite{unruh1981, hamilton2008river}, space flows inward and light moves at fixed speed relative to the flow. Outside
the horizon the outgoing ray still moves outward. At the horizon it stands still. Inside, the flow is faster than
light, and both rays move inward. In these coordinates the radial coordinate $r_0$ equals the areal radius $r_{\mathrm{areal}}$ (so $r_s / r_0 = 1.44$ gives
$r_{\mathrm{areal}} = 0.6944\, r_s$), space flows inward locally at river velocity $v_{\mathrm{river}} = -c_u \sqrt{r_s / r_0}$,
and light propagates locally at $\pm c_u$ relative to the river. Part~4 of \code{E-GRV-0163} implements a \textbf{local
tangent-space / spatially uniform constant-river-velocity 1D radial-chain benchmark} testing five separate fixed drift
rates $v_{\mathrm{river}} = -n_{\mathrm{drift}} / N_{\mathrm{beats}}$ sampled at the local GP velocities of
$r_s / r_0 \in \{0, 0.25, 0.64, 1.00, 1.44\}$. Over $N_{\mathrm{beats}} = 45$ composite beats, each beat applies
\begin{equation}
U_b = (S_{\mathrm{river}})^{m_b} \circ S_{\mathrm{light}} \circ W_{\mathrm{GP}}(\theta_{c_u}),
\label{eq:gp-walk}
\end{equation}
where $W_{\mathrm{GP}}(\theta_{c_u})$ is the exact \code{BigInt} $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$ local flat warped-beam coin
at canonical single-face $c_u = 0.977925$, $S_{\mathrm{light}}$ is the bijective $\pm 1$ lightcone shift along the
2-slot depth-0 $+x$ geodesic chain (leaving the remaining 22 slots untouched), and $S_{\mathrm{river}}$ is a spatially
uniform global parabolic $O^+(4,1; \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}])$ cusp translation distributing
$n_{\mathrm{drift}} \in \{0, 22, 35, 44, 53\}$ total shifts across $N_{\mathrm{beats}} = 45$ composite beats via
Bresenham scheduling ($m_b \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ shifts on beat $b$).

Three explicit structural boundaries govern Equation~\eqref{eq:gp-walk}:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{1D geodesic support:} Both $W_{\mathrm{GP}}(\theta_{c_u})$ and $S_{\mathrm{light}}, S_{\mathrm{river}}$
keep amplitude strictly on the 2-slot depth-0 $+x$ geodesic chain $\{(x, 0, 0) : x \in \mathbb{Z}\}$.
\item \textbf{No spatial horizon crossing:} Each row of Table~\ref{tab:gp-walk} runs an independent spatially uniform
drift rate $v_{\mathrm{river}} = -n_{\mathrm{drift}} / 45$ rather than a spatially varying $r$-dependent horizon
metric, so no wavepacket crosses dynamically from exterior ($r > r_s$) into interior ($r < r_s$) inside one run (and
$N_{\mathrm{beats}} = 45$ closely approximates $44/45 = 0.977778 \approx c_u = 0.977925$ at $r_s / r_0 = 1.00$).
\item \textbf{Multi-substep composite causality:} Because $c_u = 0.977925$ sits near $1$, attaining
$|v_r(\mathrm{in})| = c_u + |v_{\mathrm{river}}| > 1$ applies $m_b \in \{1, 2\}$ river shifts inside one composite beat
(up to 3 lattice hops per composite beat when $n_{\mathrm{drift}} = 53 > 45$ at $r_s / r_0 = 1.44$). Thus $U_b$ is a
strictly bijective multi-substep coordinate-frame composition rather than a single 1-hop bare QCA beat.
\end{itemize}
