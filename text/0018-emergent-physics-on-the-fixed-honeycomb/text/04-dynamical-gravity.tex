\label{sec:gravity}

growth causes an unweighted bulk Laplacian to leak flux into deep shells, exponentially screening any 3D boundary

To identify the exact threshold separating screened and unscreened infinite-depth columns, \code{E-GRV-0154} and

resistance to floor $F$ scales as $F^q$ with $q = -3.51, -2.04, -0.85, +0.13, +1.09, +3.02$ for $p = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6$
(worst relative Bloch residual $3.5 \times 10^{-13}$ across 1,415 solves). The infinite honeycomb is therefore
unscreened if and only if $p \ge 3$: the Randall--Sundrum II warp ($p = 1$) \cite{randall1999} stays screened

floor 49), and $p = 6$ delivers clean unscreened $1/r$ over the entire read window.

The exponent $p = 6$ requires no tuning. Because 3D horosphere area scales as $N_D \sim \ell^3$, lone Scale-1 vibe
density scales as $1/N_D \sim \ell^{-3}$, so Scale-2 two-vibe pair-interaction density scales as its exact square:
\begin{equation}
w(D) = \frac{1}{N_D^2} \sim \ell^{-6} \qquad (p = 6\ \text{exact}).
\label{eq:pair-warp}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
R(F) = \sum_{D=0}^{F-1} \frac{1}{N_D\, w(D)} = \sum_{D=0}^{F-1} N_D,
\label{eq:col-resistance}
\end{equation}
reaching $R(12) = 1.48 \times 10^{15}$ by floor 12 (screening mass $6.75 \times 10^{-16}$, correlation length
$\xi = 3.85 \times 10^7$ docks, \code{E-GRV-0156}), restoring unscreened 3D $1/r$ gravity with zero reflecting floor
and zero depth register.
