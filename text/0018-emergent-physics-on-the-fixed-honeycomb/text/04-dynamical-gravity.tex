\label{sec:gravity}

Exponential volume growth on a four-dimensional hyperbolic lattice creates an immediate obstruction to long-range
gravity: an unweighted bulk Laplacian drains flux into deep shells and screens any 3D boundary potential exponentially.
How does imposing a pair-density depth warp restore unscreened $1/r$ gravity and post-Newtonian orbits without a
reflecting bulk floor, and how does an exact infinite-honeycomb quantum walk behave when crossing a black-hole horizon
into a genuinely trapped interior?

We audit three sharply separated layers: the Tier~B imposed $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$ warped cusp column, live two-body orbits,
and parameterized post-Newtonian (PPN, weak-field relativistic gravity) observables (\code{E-GRV-0154}--\code{E-GRV-0159}),
the Tier~B / L2 exact \code{BigInt} $R = \infty$ $\{3,4,3,4\}$ walks on prescribed Hartree (mean-field self-consistent
gravitational) and bijective Gullstrand--Painlev\'e backgrounds (\code{E-GRV-0162}, \code{E-GRV-0163}), and the Tier~C
continuum General Relativity consistency benchmarks (\code{E-GRV-0160}, \code{E-GRV-0161}).

Imposing $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$ restores an unscreened $1/r$ potential with canonical single-face $G_{\mathrm{eff}} = 0.078593$
