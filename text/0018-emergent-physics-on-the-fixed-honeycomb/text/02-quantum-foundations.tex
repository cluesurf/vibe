\section{Quantum Foundations and Records}
\label{sec:quantum}

Can a deterministic cellular automaton whose amplitudes belong strictly to a discrete cyclotomic ring reproduce
universal single-qubit interference, satisfy the algebraic symmetry prerequisites compatible with Born probabilities,
and exhibit multipartite pointer decoherence and Bell violation without signaling? This section audits those three
foundational questions across six registered experiments (\coderange{E-QTM-0202}{0207}), separating bare Tier~A
honeycomb theorems from Tier~B / L1 finite-qubit and gate-synthesis benchmarks.

\paragraph{Key findings.}
Composing the bare $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$ coin $C$ with the $\Sigma(648)$ phase gate $P_\omega$ yields two
conjugates $W_1, W_2 \in SU(2)$ whose algebraic 2-adic infinite-order and $\operatorname{Pin}(2)$-exclusion hypotheses
are machine-checked in Lean~4.35.0, implying density in $SU(2)$ (\code{E-QTM-0204}, \code{E-FND-0185}). Explicit
bipartite state-vector arithmetic verifies exact $\mathbb{Z}_6 \times \mathbb{Z}_6$ phase envariance and (with the
$\Sigma(648)$ qutrit Fourier gate $F_3$) exact $S_4$ swap envariance under operationally posited Born weights
(\code{E-QTM-0205}). Standalone L1 multi-qubit circuit benchmarks verify pointer decoherence and non-signaling
record-branch CHSH violation ($2.8258$, \code{E-QTM-0206}), while ballistic open-line propagation on the honeycomb
stalls just below Tsirelson's bound (\code{E-QTM-0202}).

\subsection{Causal window and 2-adic \texorpdfstring{$SU(2)$}{SU(2)} gate density}

Before analyzing interference gates, \code{E-QTM-0203} (Tier~A / L2) performs a 1D geodesic causal-window sanity check
comparing a single lone excitation evolved by the bare coin $C$ along one antipodal line on a periodic flat $D_4$ box
(side length $L = 8$, initial support diameter $m = 1$) and on the true $R = 3$ $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb ball. Inside
the causal horizon $m + 2t \le L$ (through beat $t = 3 \le R$), both substrates agree to exact $0$ with beat-3 quantum
interference difference $L_1 = 0.5625$ against the dephased classical walk, confirming that finite-ball boundaries do
not disturb single-line interference before horizon crossing.

On a single excitation in the bare Tier~A rule $U$, all multi-body layers are silent so bare single-excitation
evolution alone generates only the order-6 cyclic group $\langle C \rangle$. Composing the bare coin $C$ of
Equation~\eqref{eq:coin} with the diagonal phase gate $P_\omega = \operatorname{diag}(1, \omega)$ drawn from the
Tier~B $\Sigma(648)$ qutrit Clifford and link-phase alphabet (and likewise $P_{\zeta_9} = \operatorname{diag}(1, \zeta_9)$
in $\Sigma(648)$) produces one special-unitary two-mode generator inside $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$:
\begin{equation}
W_1 = \omega^2\, C\, P_\omega
  = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} -\omega & 1 - \omega \\ -2 - \omega & 1 + \omega \end{pmatrix} \in SU(2),
\label{eq:su2-gen}
\end{equation}
which has exact $\det W_1 = 1$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(W_1) = 1/2$ (rotation half-angle $\cos\theta = 1/4$). An
elementary parity argument proves that $W_1$ and its conjugate $W_2 = P_\omega W_1 P_\omega^\dagger$ already generate
a dense subgroup of $SU(2)$ without invoking transcendental number theory.

\needspace{7\baselineskip}
\begin{theorem}[2-adic $SU(2)$ density, \code{E-QTM-0204}, \code{E-FND-0185}]
Set $M = 2 W_1 \in \mathrm{Mat}_2(\mathbb{Z}[\omega])$ and $W_2 = P_\omega W_1 P_\omega^\dagger$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ obeys the exact Cayley--Hamilton identity $M^2 = M - 4I$, so the integer trace sequence
$s_n = 2^n \operatorname{Tr}(W_1^n) = \operatorname{Tr}(M^n)$ satisfies
\[
s_0 = 2, \qquad s_1 = 1, \qquad s_{n+2} = s_{n+1} - 4 s_n \qquad (n \ge 0).
\]
\item By induction, $s_n \equiv 1 \pmod 2$ for every $n \ge 1$. Because $W_1^n = \pm I$ implies
$s_n = \pm 2^{n+1} \equiv 0 \pmod 2$, one has $W_1^n \neq \pm I$ for all $n \ge 1$ in both $SU(2)$ and
$PSU(2) \cong SO(3)$.
\item The conjugate $W_2$ does not commute with $W_1$, with exact Frobenius commutator norm squared
$\|[W_1, W_2]\|_F^2 = 27/4$, and satisfies $W_2 W_1 \neq W_1^\dagger W_2$, excluding every abelian $U(1)$ and
normalizer $\operatorname{Pin}(2)$ subgroup.
\end{enumerate}
Because any closed subgroup of $SU(2)$ containing an infinite-order element has identity component either $SU(2)$ or a
maximal torus $U(1)$ whose normalizer is $\operatorname{Pin}(2)$ (acting on $U(1)$ only by $W_1 \mapsto W_1^{\pm 1}$),
the topological closure satisfies $\overline{\langle W_1, W_2 \rangle} = SU(2)$.
\end{theorem}

Every algebraic step of items~(1)--(3) (the Cayley--Hamilton recurrence, 2-adic oddness, infinite order in $SU(2)$ and
$PSU(2)$, non-commutativity, and $\operatorname{Pin}(2)$ exclusion) is machine-checked in Lean~4.35.0
(\code{Vibe/Rule.lean}, Section~\ref{sec:unified}). Topological density in $SU(2)$ then follows from the standard
closed-subgroup classification of $SU(2)$.

Experiment \code{E-QTM-0204} (graded L1) evaluates these finite-ring gates in two concrete benchmarks
(Table~\ref{tab:quantum-a}):
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Synthesized beamsplitter proxy:} Length-$\ell_w = 14$ words over $\{C, C_{1/2}, P_\omega\}$
($\mathbb{Q}(\omega)$) and $\{C, C_{1/2}, P_\omega, P_{\zeta_9}\}$ ($\Sigma(648)$) approach unity under the abstract
two-mode balance formula $\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{BS}} = 1 - |\operatorname{perm}(U)|^2 / (|U_{00}|^4 + |U_{01}|^4)$
(modeled on the optical Hong--Ou--Mandel permanent \cite{hong1987}, noting that bare hard-core ternary slots exclude
double bosonic occupation $|2, 0\rangle$).
\item \textbf{Exact $6 \times 6$ unitary splitter:} On $\mathrm{path}(2) \otimes \mathrm{role}(3)$ in
$\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/6]$, the composite circuit $U_{\mathrm{tot}} = U_3 U_2 U_1$ pairs $U_1 = C \otimes I_3$, the
Tier~B $\Sigma(648)$ qutrit Fourier layer $U_2 = \operatorname{diag}(I_3, Z_3 F_3)$, and the controlled swap
$U_3$ ($|1,0\rangle \leftrightarrow |0,1\rangle$). Its output $U_{\mathrm{tot}}|0,0\rangle$ reduces into
$\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$ with balanced port weights $(1/2, 1/2)$ and error $0$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Operational Born measure and Zurek envariance}

We adopt an explicit, conservative postulate on probability: \textbf{operational Born weights} (squared-amplitude
probabilities mapping complex amplitudes to measurement frequencies) over record branches are posited operationally
rather than claimed as an unconditional derivation from unitary dynamics alone. Unitary evolution fixes amplitudes and
entanglement symmetries, whereas assigning frequencies to branches still relies on symmetry and additivity axioms beyond
bare state-vector evolution.

Within that boundary, \code{E-QTM-0205} (Tier~A / L2 for the bare ball beat, with Tier~B $\Sigma(648)$ fine-graining)
verifies the algebraic symmetry prerequisites of Zurek's entanglement-assisted invariance (envariance) \cite{zurek2005}
via explicit bipartite state vectors on $\mathcal{H}_S \otimes \mathcal{H}_E$. One beat of the bare coin and stream on
the exact $R = 2$ $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb ball prepares a 4-component bipartite state
$|\Psi_2\rangle \in \mathcal{H}_S^{(2)} \otimes \mathcal{H}_E^{(2)}$ with unequal squared Schmidt weights $(3/4, 1/4)$,
zero frontier hits, and zero norm error. Entry-by-entry state-vector multiplication confirms all $36/36$ exact
$\mathbb{Z}_6 \times \mathbb{Z}_6$ unitary phase pairs $U_S(p_0, p_1) \otimes U_E(-p_0, -p_1)$ leave $|\Psi_2\rangle$
invariant.

Because the Eisenstein ring satisfies
\begin{equation}
\frac{1 - \omega}{2(1 + 2\omega)} = -\frac{1 + \omega}{2} \in \mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2],
\label{eq:envariance-split}
\end{equation}
applying the actual $6 \times 6$ $\Sigma(648)$ fine-graining unitary $\operatorname{diag}(I_3, Z_3 F_3)$ on
$\mathrm{path}(2) \otimes \mathrm{role}(3)$ (explicitly noted as a Tier~B role-register extension beyond bare
single-excitation $U$) together with controlled environment-role entanglement maps $|\Psi_2\rangle$ to a 36-component
bipartite state $|\Psi_4\rangle \in \mathcal{H}_S^{(6)} \otimes \mathcal{H}_E^{(6)}$ whose amplitudes reduce exactly
into $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$ with four equal squared moduli $(1/4, 1/4, 1/4, 1/4)$. All $24/24$ explicit $6 \times 6$
$S_4$ swap-and-phase unitaries $U_S(\pi) \otimes U_E(\pi)$ are verified entry-by-entry on $|\Psi_4\rangle$, linking
equal-modulus branch counts $(3, 1)$ directly to $\Pr(\text{cross}) = 3/4$ and $\Pr(\text{keep}) = 1/4$
(Table~\ref{tab:quantum-a}).

\subsection{Pointer decoherence, Bell tests, and line separation}

To test whether synthesized ring-word analyzers reproduce pointer decoherence, Quantum Darwinism redundancy
\cite{zurek2009}, and Clauser--Horne--Shimony--Holt (CHSH) Bell violation \cite{clauser1969, tsirelson1980} without
signaling on finite registers, \code{E-QTM-0206} (Tier~B / L1) runs standalone multi-qubit circuit benchmarks (zero
honeycomb dynamics) using explicit partial traces and a real Hermitian eigensolver under $\ell_w = 14$ $\Sigma(648)$
synthesized analyzer words. Four disjoint environment copies suppress off-diagonal pointer coherence to zero, imprint
one full bit of mutual information per fragment, and violate the classical CHSH bound well beyond $2$ with machine-zero
signaling drift.

By contrast, \code{E-QTM-0202} (Tier~A / L2, registered failure) reveals an explicit dynamical boundary on an open
non-wrapping honeycomb line. Partitioning a two-excitation state by particle order ($k_1 < k_2$, leftmost versus
rightmost particle) and evaluating the nonlocal Gisin--Peres multi-mode Schmidt CHSH upper bound shows that beat~1
reaches the Horodecki ceiling $\sqrt{7} = 2\sqrt{1 + 3/4} \approx 2.6458$, whereas long ballistic separation
($t = 32, 64, 128, 256$) stalls at $2.8273$--$2.8278$, roughly $7 \times 10^{-4}$ below Tsirelson's bound
$2\sqrt{2} \approx 2.8284$ \cite{tsirelson1980}. An auxiliary abstract-singlet direction search in \code{E-QTM-0202}
shows that constructive Eisenstein-triple directions in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/6]$ reach $11/4 = 2.75 > \sqrt{7}$ on an
ideal singlet without closing the open-line dynamical stall.

Separating algebraic gate synthesis from ballistic honeycomb propagation, \code{E-QTM-0207} (Tier~B / L1) runs two
standalone finite-register benchmarks (zero honeycomb dynamics): a reversible $N = 10$ brickwork scrambler approaching
the Page subsystem entropy peak \cite{page1993} while maintaining near-zero global von Neumann entropy
$S_{\mathrm{vN}}$, and exact \code{BigInt} meet-in-the-middle (MITM) $SU(2)$ word synthesis ($\ell_w = 6, \ldots, 22$)
measuring a prepared textbook singlet to $4.87 \times 10^{-10}$ below $2\sqrt{2}$. As a standalone L1 benchmark,
\code{E-QTM-0207} does not close either dynamical open-line ballistic separation (\code{E-QTM-0202}) or bounded-clock
horizon radiation (\code{E-GRV-0134}). Table~\ref{tab:quantum-b} collects all record-branch, open-line, and L1
benchmark metrics.

Pure integer cyclotomic arithmetic in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$ (paired with $\Sigma(648)$ phase and Fourier

$\mathbb{Z}_6$ and $S_4$ envariance unitaries, and synthesizes non-signaling CHSH analyzers on L1 qubit registers,
while keeping the operational Born postulate and open-line ballistic Tsirelson convergence explicitly marked. We turn
next from local quantum interference to three-dimensional rotational isotropy, relativistic boost kinematics, and
species-universal limiting speed across the honeycomb.
