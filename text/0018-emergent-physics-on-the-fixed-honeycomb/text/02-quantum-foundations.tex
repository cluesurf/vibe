\section{Quantum Foundations and Records}
\label{sec:quantum}

A discrete reversible cellular automaton over a finite-generator cyclotomic ring must answer three foundational tests
of quantum mechanics without invoking external axioms or continuous tuning: (1) whether its discrete local gates
generate a dense subgroup of $SU(2)$ capable of arbitrary interference, (2) whether squared-amplitude Born
probabilities are derived rather than postulated, and (3) whether observers internal to the unitary state form
redundant classical-looking pointer records that still violate Bell's inequality without signaling. Four gated
experiments (\code{E-QTM-0203} through \code{E-QTM-0206}) resolve these questions, alongside one honest open-line
limitation (\code{E-QTM-0202}).

\subsection{Honeycomb window and \texorpdfstring{$SU(2)$}{SU(2)} gate density}

First, \code{E-QTM-0203} verifies the exact causal-window equivalence between a side-8 flat periodic $D_4$ lattice and
the true $R = 3$ $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb ball inside the proven horizon $m + 2t \le L$. Through beat 3, box and
honeycomb agree to exact $0$, producing interference distance $L_1 = 0, 0, 0.5625$ against $0$ with the coin off, while
peace-slot leftovers move $0$ across lines and alter occupied amplitudes by $0$.

Second, \code{E-QTM-0204} proves that the working rule's two-mode gate group inside $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$ is
infinite and dense in $SU(2)$. Composing the lone-vibe coin $C$ of Equation~\eqref{eq:coin} with the qutrit phase gate
$P_\omega = \operatorname{diag}(1, \omega)$ gives the special-unitary operator
\begin{equation}
W = \omega^2\, C\, \operatorname{diag}(1, \omega)
  = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} -\omega & 1 - \omega \\ -2 - \omega & 1 + \omega \end{pmatrix} \in SU(2),
\label{eq:su2-gen}
\end{equation}
which has exact $\det W = 1$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(W) = 1/2$ (rotation half-angle $\cos\theta = 1/4$).

\begin{theorem}[Elementary 2-adic infinite order and $SU(2)$ density, \code{E-QTM-0204}, \code{E-FND-0185}]
Set $M = 2W \in \mathrm{Mat}_2(\mathbb{Z}[\omega])$ and $W_2 = P_\omega W P_\omega^\dagger$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ satisfies the exact Cayley--Hamilton identity $M^2 = M - 4I$, so the integer trace sequence
$s_n = 2^n \operatorname{Tr}(W^n) = \operatorname{Tr}(M^n)$ obeys $s_0 = 2$, $s_1 = 1$, and
$s_{n+2} = s_{n+1} - 4s_n$ for all $n \ge 0$.
\item By induction on $n$, $s_n \equiv 1 \pmod 2$ for every $n \ge 1$. Because $W^n = \pm I$ implies
$s_n = \pm 2^{n+1} \equiv 0 \pmod 2$, one has $W^n \neq \pm I$ for all $n \ge 1$ in both $SU(2)$ and $PSU(2) \cong SO(3)$.
\item The conjugate $W_2$ does not commute with $W$, with exact Frobenius commutator norm squared
$\|[W, W_2]\|_F^2 = 27/4$ ($\|[W, W_2]\|_F = 2.5981$), and satisfies $W_2 W \neq W^\dagger W_2$, excluding every
abelian subgroup $U(1)$ and normalizer $\operatorname{Pin}(2)$.
\end{enumerate}
Therefore $\langle W, W_2 \rangle$ is dense in $SU(2)$.
\end{theorem}

This elementary 2-adic parity proof requires neither Kronecker's theorem nor Niven's theorem as an external black box
and is formalized completely in Lean 4 (\code{E-FND-0185}, Section~\ref{sec:unified}). Even though $2$ has
multiplicative order $6$ modulo $9$ and stays inert in $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9]$, short rule words of length $L = 14$
achieve Hong--Ou--Mandel two-particle interference visibility \cite{hong1987} $V = 0.9999924$ in $\mathbb{Q}(\omega)$
and $V = 0.9999996$ under $\Sigma(648)$ lifts, while a 3-step path-and-role splitter hits exact $1/2 = 1/4 + 1/4$
(error $0$) directly inside $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$ (\code{E-QTM-0204}).

\subsection{Born rule via Zurek envariance}

Rather than postulating squared-amplitude probabilities, \code{E-QTM-0205} derives the Born rule from
entanglement-assisted invariance (envariance) \cite{zurek2005} directly on the exact $R = 2$ $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb
ball. A single beat of the working-rule coin and stream evolves a lone vibe into an exact system-environment Schmidt
state with unequal squared weights $(3/4, 1/4)$, zero frontier hits, and zero norm error.

Under Zurek's locality and symmetry premises (E1)--(E4), \code{E-QTM-0205} verifies two exact algebraic steps:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Phase envariance.} All $36/36$ local $\mathbb{Z}_6$ phase rotations on the system are reversed identically
by counter-rotations on the environment, establishing that outcome probabilities cannot depend on relative phases.
\item \textbf{Swap envariance via exact fine-graining.} Because the working ring satisfies the exact identity
\begin{equation}
\frac{1 - \omega}{2(1 + 2\omega)} = -\frac{1 + \omega}{2} \in \mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2],
\label{eq:envariance-split}
\end{equation}
a rule-made qutrit Clifford fine-graining splits the $3/4$ crossing branch into three orthogonal environment branches
of exact equal norm $1/4$ each, producing four equal-amplitude branches overall. Exact $24/24$ $S_4$ swap envariance
across those four branches forces equal probability $1/4$ per branch, hence $P(\text{cross}) = 3/4$ and
$P(\text{keep}) = 1/4$. Iterating dyadic-4 fine-graining through depth $m = 4$ approximates arbitrary probabilities in
$[0, 1]$ within $1.94 \times 10^{-3}$ (\code{E-QTM-0205}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Redundant pointer records and Bell violation}

Experiment \code{E-QTM-0206} tests whether macroscopic observers modeled strictly as redundant record branches inside
the unitary state observe Bell inequality violations without signaling. Starting from a bank-invariant singlet, local
analyzers execute $L = 14$ $\Sigma(648)$ rule-word measurement settings (unitarity error $1.78 \times 10^{-15}$, $100\%$
branch-count conservation error $5.55 \times 10^{-16}$) coupled to disjoint environment probes.

\begin{result}[Pointer decoherence, redundancy, and record-branch CHSH, \code{E-QTM-0206}]
Under unitary environment record growth:
\begin{itemize}
\item Full-copy pointer coherence vanishes identically ($|\rho_{01}(4)| = 0$), while controlled-$C$ partial-copy
probes damp off-diagonal coherence along the exact geometric curve $1/2^k$ ($1, 0.5, 0.25, 0.125, 0.0625$ for
$k = 0..4$).
\item Quantum Darwinism redundancy \cite{zurek2009} reaches $R_{0.01} = 4$ disjoint environment fragments, with each
fragment storing exact mutual information $I(S:F_j) = 1.0000$ bit.
\item Evaluating the Clauser--Horne--Shimony--Holt correlator \cite{clauser1969} strictly over the joint pointer-record
branches yields $\mathrm{CHSH}_{\text{record}} = 2.825774$ (passing the $2.80$ gate), against a failable dephased
classical control of $1.421875 \le 2.0$, with exact no-signaling marginal drift $1.67 \times 10^{-16}$.
\end{itemize}
\end{result}

\subsection{Open-line CHSH saturation without wrap}

Testing raw single-line propagation on an open non-wrapping line (\code{E-QTM-0202}) fails its registered Tsirelson
convergence gate: although every instrument holds ($\max\text{NormErr} = 2.69 \times 10^{-14}$, beat 1 equals
$\sqrt{7}$, beat 32 reads $2.827297$), the open-line CHSH values at beats $32, 64, 128, 256$ read $2.827297, 2.827760,
2.827409, 2.827736$, stalling roughly $7 \times 10^{-4}$ short of $2\sqrt{2}$ because the two particles separate
ballistically. Constructive $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/6]$ unit observables using off-$x$-$z$ Eisenstein triples surpass
$\sqrt{7}$ on a singlet ($2.750000 = 11/4$ at denominator $D = 36$), showing that approaching Tsirelson's bound on an
open domain requires composite rule-word analyzers as in \code{E-QTM-0206} rather than raw single-line ballistic
separation.

\paragraph{Why this matters in practice.}
In practical terms, these results show that quantum behavior does not require bolting a random number generator or an
unexplained wavefunction-collapse axiom onto a discrete lattice. The rule itself generates a dense set of quantum
rotations from exact integer arithmetic, derives Born probabilities from exchange symmetry rather than assuming them,
and lets observers inside the deterministic state agree on redundant classical records while still measuring genuine
Bell correlations with zero faster-than-light signaling.
