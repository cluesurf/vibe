\section{Quantum Foundations and Records}
\label{sec:quantum}

Can a deterministic cellular automaton whose amplitudes belong strictly to a discrete cyclotomic ring reproduce
universal single-qubit interference, satisfy the algebraic symmetry prerequisites compatible with Born probabilities,
and exhibit multipartite pointer decoherence and Bell violation without signaling? This section audits those three
foundational questions across six registered experiments (\code{E-QTM-0202} through \code{E-QTM-0207}), separating bare
Tier~A honeycomb theorems from Tier~B finite-qubit benchmarks.

\paragraph{Key findings.}
Two conjugates of the bare $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$ coin generate a provably dense subgroup of $SU(2)$ via an
elementary 2-adic parity proof (\code{E-QTM-0204}), the bare rule satisfies exact $\mathbb{Z}_6$ phase and $S_4$ swap
envariance prerequisites on $\{3,4,3,4\}$ under operationally posited Born weights (\code{E-QTM-0205}), and explicit
multi-qubit partial traces verify pointer decoherence and non-signaling record-branch CHSH violation ($2.8258$,
\code{E-QTM-0206}), while ballistic open-line propagation stalls just below Tsirelson's bound (\code{E-QTM-0202}).

\subsection{Causal window and 2-adic \texorpdfstring{$SU(2)$}{SU(2)} gate density}

Before analyzing interference gates, \code{E-QTM-0203} (Tier~A) verifies exact causal-window agreement between a
periodic flat $D_4$ box and the true $R = 3$ $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb ball inside the horizon $m + 2t \le L$. Through
beat~3, both substrates agree identically while neutral-slot remainders leave occupied amplitudes unchanged.

Within this discrete rule, composing the single-excitation coin $C$ of Equation~\eqref{eq:coin} with the qutrit phase
gate $P_\omega = \operatorname{diag}(1, \omega)$ produces one special-unitary two-mode generator inside
$\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$:
\begin{equation}
W = \omega^2\, C\, \operatorname{diag}(1, \omega)
  = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} -\omega & 1 - \omega \\ -2 - \omega & 1 + \omega \end{pmatrix} \in SU(2),
\label{eq:su2-gen}
\end{equation}
which has exact $\det W = 1$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(W) = 1/2$ (rotation half-angle $\cos\theta = 1/4$). An elementary
parity argument proves that two conjugates of $W$ already generate a dense subgroup of $SU(2)$ without invoking
transcendental number theory.

\begin{theorem}[2-adic $SU(2)$ density, \code{E-QTM-0204}, \code{E-FND-0185}]
Set $M = 2W \in \mathrm{Mat}_2(\mathbb{Z}[\omega])$ and $W_2 = P_\omega W P_\omega^\dagger$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ obeys the exact Cayley--Hamilton identity $M^2 = M - 4I$, so the integer trace sequence
$s_n = 2^n \operatorname{Tr}(W^n) = \operatorname{Tr}(M^n)$ satisfies $s_0 = 2$, $s_1 = 1$, and
$s_{n+2} = s_{n+1} - 4s_n$ for all $n \ge 0$.
\item By induction, $s_n \equiv 1 \pmod 2$ for every $n \ge 1$. Because $W^n = \pm I$ implies
$s_n = \pm 2^{n+1} \equiv 0 \pmod 2$, one has $W^n \neq \pm I$ for all $n \ge 1$ in both $SU(2)$ and
$PSU(2) \cong SO(3)$.
\item The conjugate $W_2$ does not commute with $W$, with exact Frobenius commutator norm squared
$\|[W, W_2]\|_F^2 = 27/4$, and satisfies $W_2 W \neq W^\dagger W_2$, excluding every abelian $U(1)$ and normalizer
$\operatorname{Pin}(2)$ subgroup.
\end{enumerate}
Therefore $\langle W, W_2 \rangle$ is dense in $SU(2)$.
\end{theorem}

Every step of this 2-adic proof is machine-checked in Lean~4.35.0 (\code{Vibe/Rule.lean}, Section~\ref{sec:unified}).
Short rule words of length $L = 14$ already approach unit Hong--Ou--Mandel (HOM) two-particle quantum interference
visibility \cite{hong1987}, while a 3-step path-and-role splitter achieves an exact balanced $1/2 = 1/4 + 1/4$ split
directly inside $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$ (Table~\ref{tab:quantum-a}).

\subsection{Operational Born measure and Zurek envariance}

We adopt an explicit, conservative postulate on probability: \textbf{operational Born weights} (squared-amplitude
probabilities mapping complex amplitudes to measurement frequencies) over record branches are posited operationally
rather than claimed as an unconditional derivation from unitary dynamics alone. Unitary evolution fixes amplitudes and
entanglement symmetries, whereas assigning frequencies to branches still relies on symmetry and additivity axioms beyond
bare state-vector evolution.

Within that boundary, \code{E-QTM-0205} (Tier~A) verifies the exact algebraic symmetry prerequisites of Zurek's
entanglement-assisted invariance (envariance, a symmetry test linking equal-norm entangled branches to equal weights)
\cite{zurek2005} on the exact $R = 2$ $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb ball. One beat of the coin and stream maps a single
excitation to a system-environment Schmidt state (bipartite entangled decomposition pairing system and environment
branches) with unequal squared weights $(3/4, 1/4)$, zero frontier hits, and zero norm error.

Two exact algebraic symmetries hold directly in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$. Every local $\mathbb{Z}_6$ phase rotation on
the system is reversed identically by a counter-rotation on the environment (phase envariance), and because the ring
satisfies
\begin{equation}
\frac{1 - \omega}{2(1 + 2\omega)} = -\frac{1 + \omega}{2} \in \mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2],
\label{eq:envariance-split}
\end{equation}
a rule-native qutrit Clifford fine-graining splits the $3/4$ crossing branch into three orthogonal equal-norm
environment branches of weight $1/4$ each. Exact $S_4$ swap envariance across the resulting four equal-amplitude
branches links permutation symmetry directly to $P(\text{cross}) = 3/4$ and $P(\text{keep}) = 1/4$, with all numerical
certificates listed in Table~\ref{tab:quantum-a}.

\subsection{Pointer decoherence, Bell tests, and line separation}

To test whether unitary record copying induces pointer decoherence, Quantum Darwinism redundancy (redundant storage of
pointer states across independent environment fragments) \cite{zurek2009}, and Clauser--Horne--Shimony--Holt (CHSH)
Bell violation (a correlation bound separating entanglement from local hidden variables) \cite{clauser1969} without
signaling, \code{E-QTM-0206} (Tier~B / L1) evolves explicit multi-qubit state vectors with full partial traces and a
real Hermitian eigensolver under $L = 14$ $\Sigma(648)$ rule-word analyzers. Four disjoint environment copies suppress
off-diagonal pointer coherence cleanly to zero, imprint one full bit of mutual information per fragment, and violate
the classical CHSH bound well beyond 2 with machine-zero signaling drift.

By contrast, \code{E-QTM-0202} (Tier~A, registered failure) reveals a genuine dynamical boundary on an open
non-wrapping line: although unitarity holds tightly and beat~1 reaches $\sqrt{7}$, ballistic spatial separation stalls
open-line CHSH slightly short of Tsirelson's bound $2\sqrt{2}$ across long runs. Separating algebraic gate synthesis
from ballistic propagation, \code{E-QTM-0207} (Tier~B / L1) benchmarks a reversible brickwork scrambler, the bosonic
Bogoliubov thermal identity, and exact \code{BigInt} meet-in-the-middle (MITM) $SU(2)$ word synthesis on a prepared
singlet. As a standalone L1 benchmark, \code{E-QTM-0207} does not close either dynamical open-line ballistic separation
(\code{E-QTM-0202}) or bounded-clock horizon radiation (\code{E-GRV-0134}). Table~\ref{tab:quantum-b} collects all
record-branch, open-line, and L1 benchmark metrics.
