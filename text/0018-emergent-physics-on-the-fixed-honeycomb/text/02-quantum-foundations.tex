\section{Quantum Foundations, Scattering, and Bare-Rule Universality}
\label{sec:quantum}

A single excitation on $\{3,4,3,4\}$ activates none of the multi-body layers in Equation~\eqref{eq:beat}: each beat
applies only the two-slot coin $C = C_{1/2}^2$ along its line and streams amplitude one hop to each antipodal neighbor.
That single-line sector already supports exact interference, bipartite envariance symmetry, and, when joined with one
phase gate $P_\omega$ from $\Sigma(648)$, a gate algebra dense in $SU(2)$ (\code{E-QTM-0203}--\code{0205},
\code{E-FND-0185}). Finite-register circuits built from the same gates exhibit pointer decoherence and non-signaling
Bell violation (\code{E-QTM-0206}, \code{E-QTM-0207}), while open-line ballistic propagation stalls slightly below
Tsirelson's bound (\code{E-QTM-0202}).

Because an isolated excitation sees only the order-6 cyclic group $\langle C \rangle$, universal control without
external phase gates requires multi-excitation collisions. Section~\ref{sec:bare-smatrix} solves the exact two-body
scattering problem of bare $U$, showing that its 1D restriction lands on two of the three Yang--Baxter integrable
phases on $U(1)$ before 4D $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ breaks integrability, and constructs two zero-external-gate
universality proofs purely inside bare $U$: 3-full-line $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ catalysis (dense $SU(2)$) and
exchange-only $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ decoherence-free-subspace (DFS) collisions (universal $SU(4)$ and bare-analyzer
Bell--CHSH up to $2.8262$).

\subsection{Causal window and \texorpdfstring{$SU(2)$}{SU(2)} gate density}

A first check compares a finite ball with a flat box for this motion. Experiment \code{E-QTM-0203}
(Tier~A / L2) evolves one excitation under the bare coin $C$ along one line, once in a periodic flat $D_4$ box
($L = 8$, initial support $m = 1$) and once on the true $R = 3$ honeycomb ball. Inside the causal window
$m + 2t \le L$ (through $t = 3 \le R$) the two agree exactly, and the beat-3 interference pattern differs from the
dephased classical walk by $L_1 = 0.5625$. Ball boundaries do not disturb single-line interference before the light
cone reaches them.

On its own, the coin generates only the order-6 cyclic group $\langle C \rangle$. Adding one phase gate enlarges it.
Composing the bare coin $C$ of
Equation~\eqref{eq:coin} with the diagonal phase gate $P_\omega = \operatorname{diag}(1, \omega)$ drawn from the
Tier~B $\Sigma(648)$ qutrit Clifford and link-phase alphabet (and likewise $P_{\zeta_9} = \operatorname{diag}(1, \zeta_9)$
in $\Sigma(648)$) produces one special-unitary two-mode generator inside $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$:
\begin{equation}
W_1 = \omega^2\, C\, P_\omega
  = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} -\omega & 1 - \omega \\ -2 - \omega & 1 + \omega \end{pmatrix} \in SU(2),
\label{eq:su2-gen}
\end{equation}
which has exact $\det W_1 = 1$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(W_1) = 1/2$ (rotation half-angle $\cos\theta = 1/4$). A
rotation by this angle never returns exactly to the identity, and together with one conjugate copy it generates a dense
subgroup of $SU(2)$. An elementary parity argument proves this without transcendental number theory.

\needspace{7\baselineskip}
\begin{theorem}[2-adic $SU(2)$ density, \code{E-QTM-0204}, \code{E-FND-0185}]
Set $M = 2 W_1 \in \mathrm{Mat}_2(\mathbb{Z}[\omega])$ and $W_2 = P_\omega W_1 P_\omega^\dagger$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ obeys the exact Cayley--Hamilton identity $M^2 = M - 4I$, so the integer trace sequence
$s_n = 2^n \operatorname{Tr}(W_1^n) = \operatorname{Tr}(M^n)$ satisfies
\[
s_0 = 2, \qquad s_1 = 1, \qquad s_{n+2} = s_{n+1} - 4 s_n \qquad (n \ge 0).
\]
\item By induction, $s_n \equiv 1 \pmod 2$ for every $n \ge 1$. Because $W_1^n = \pm I$ implies
$s_n = \pm 2^{n+1} \equiv 0 \pmod 2$, one has $W_1^n \neq \pm I$ for all $n \ge 1$ in both $SU(2)$ and
$PSU(2) \cong SO(3)$.
\item The conjugate $W_2$ does not commute with $W_1$, with exact Frobenius commutator norm squared
$\|[W_1, W_2]\|_F^2 = 27/4$, and satisfies $W_2 W_1 \neq W_1^\dagger W_2$, excluding every abelian $U(1)$ and
normalizer $\operatorname{Pin}(2)$ subgroup.
\end{enumerate}
Because any closed subgroup of $SU(2)$ containing an infinite-order element has identity component either $SU(2)$ or a
maximal torus $U(1)$ whose normalizer is $\operatorname{Pin}(2)$ (acting on $U(1)$ only by $W_1 \mapsto W_1^{\pm 1}$),
the topological closure satisfies $\overline{\langle W_1, W_2 \rangle} = SU(2)$.
\end{theorem}

Every algebraic step of items~(1)--(3) (the Cayley--Hamilton recurrence, 2-adic oddness, infinite order in $SU(2)$ and
$PSU(2)$, non-commutativity, and $\operatorname{Pin}(2)$ exclusion) is machine-checked in Lean~4.35.0
(\code{Vibe/Rule.lean}, Section~\ref{sec:unified}). Topological density in $SU(2)$ then follows from the standard
closed-subgroup classification of $SU(2)$.

Density is a limit statement. Experiment \code{E-QTM-0204} (graded L1) checks how closely short words in these gates
approach specific targets, in two benchmarks (Table~\ref{tab:quantum-a}):
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Synthesized beamsplitter proxy:} Length-$\ell_w = 14$ words over $\{C, C_{1/2}, P_\omega\}$
($\mathbb{Q}(\omega)$) and $\{C, C_{1/2}, P_\omega, P_{\zeta_9}\}$ ($\Sigma(648)$) approach unity under the abstract
two-mode balance formula $\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{BS}} = 1 - |\operatorname{perm}(U)|^2 / (|U_{00}|^4 + |U_{01}|^4)$
(modeled on the optical Hong--Ou--Mandel permanent \cite{hong1987}, noting that bare single-occupancy ternary slots exclude
double bosonic occupation $|2, 0\rangle$).
\item \textbf{Exact $6 \times 6$ unitary splitter:} On $\mathrm{path}(2) \otimes \mathrm{role}(3)$ in
$\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/6]$, the composite circuit $U_{\mathrm{tot}} = U_3 U_2 U_1$ pairs $U_1 = C \otimes I_3$, the
Tier~B $\Sigma(648)$ qutrit Fourier layer $U_2 = \operatorname{diag}(I_3, Z_3 F_3)$, and the controlled swap
$U_3$ ($|1,0\rangle \leftrightarrow |0,1\rangle$). Its output $U_{\mathrm{tot}}|0,0\rangle$ reduces into
$\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$ with balanced port weights $(1/2, 1/2)$ and error $0$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Born measure and envariance}

When the coin splits an excitation $1/4$ keep and $3/4$ flip, the walk produces two branches of unequal weight.
\textbf{Born weights}, squared amplitudes read as measurement
frequencies, are posited operationally. Unitary evolution fixes amplitudes and the symmetries of entangled states, but
turning those into frequencies still needs symmetry and additivity axioms beyond the state vector. What can be checked
exactly is that the symmetries Zurek's envariance argument relies on are present.

Experiment \code{E-QTM-0205} (Tier~A / L2 for the bare ball beat, with Tier~B $\Sigma(648)$ fine-graining)
verifies the algebraic symmetry prerequisites of Zurek's entanglement-assisted invariance (envariance) \cite{zurek2005}
via explicit bipartite state vectors on $\mathcal{H}_S \otimes \mathcal{H}_E$. One beat of the bare coin and stream on
the exact $R = 2$ $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb ball prepares a 4-component bipartite state
$|\Psi_2\rangle \in \mathcal{H}_S^{(2)} \otimes \mathcal{H}_E^{(2)}$ with unequal squared Schmidt weights $(3/4, 1/4)$,
zero frontier hits, and zero norm error. Entry-by-entry state-vector multiplication confirms all $36/36$ exact
$\mathbb{Z}_6 \times \mathbb{Z}_6$ unitary phase pairs $U_S(p_0, p_1) \otimes U_E(-p_0, -p_1)$ leave $|\Psi_2\rangle$
invariant.

The unequal weights $(3/4, 1/4)$ can be refined into equal ones. Because the Eisenstein ring satisfies
\begin{equation}
\frac{1 - \omega}{2(1 + 2\omega)} = -\frac{1 + \omega}{2} \in \mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2],
\label{eq:envariance-split}
\end{equation}
applying the actual $6 \times 6$ $\Sigma(648)$ fine-graining unitary $\operatorname{diag}(I_3, Z_3 F_3)$ on
$\mathrm{path}(2) \otimes \mathrm{role}(3)$ (explicitly noted as a Tier~B role-register extension beyond bare
single-excitation $U$) together with controlled environment-role entanglement maps $|\Psi_2\rangle$ to a 36-component
bipartite state $|\Psi_4\rangle \in \mathcal{H}_S^{(6)} \otimes \mathcal{H}_E^{(6)}$ whose amplitudes reduce exactly
into $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$ with four equal squared moduli $(1/4, 1/4, 1/4, 1/4)$. All $24/24$ explicit $6 \times 6$
$S_4$ swap-and-phase unitaries $U_S(\pi) \otimes U_E(\pi)$ are verified entry-by-entry on $|\Psi_4\rangle$, linking
equal-modulus branch counts $(3, 1)$ directly to $\Pr(\text{cross}) = 3/4$ and $\Pr(\text{keep}) = 1/4$
(Table~\ref{tab:quantum-a}).

\subsection{Records and Bell correlations}

The finite-register benchmarks \code{E-QTM-0206} and \code{E-QTM-0207} (Tier~B / L1, without honeycomb spatial
dynamics) evaluate pointer decoherence and Bell correlations directly on multi-qubit circuits whose analyzers are
$\ell_w = 14$ words in the $\Sigma(648)$ gate set, computing partial traces exactly. Four disjoint environment copies
drive off-diagonal pointer coherence to zero while each fragment retains one full bit of mutual information about the
system, matching Quantum Darwinism \cite{zurek2009}, and the Clauser--Horne--Shimony--Holt (CHSH) correlator
\cite{clauser1969, tsirelson1980} reaches $2.825774$ with signaling drift at machine zero.

On the honeycomb itself, \code{E-QTM-0202} (Tier~A / L2, registered failure) identifies a dynamical boundary on an
open, non-wrapping line. Partitioning a two-excitation state by particle order ($k_1 < k_2$, leftmost versus
rightmost particle) and evaluating the nonlocal Gisin--Peres multi-mode Schmidt CHSH upper bound shows that beat~1
reaches the Horodecki ceiling $\sqrt{7} = 2\sqrt{1 + 3/4} \approx 2.6458$, whereas long ballistic separation
($t = 32, 64, 128, 256$) stalls at $2.8273$--$2.8278$, roughly $7 \times 10^{-4}$ below Tsirelson's bound
$2\sqrt{2} \approx 2.8284$ \cite{tsirelson1980}. An auxiliary abstract-singlet direction search in \code{E-QTM-0202}
shows that constructive Eisenstein-triple directions in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/6]$ reach $11/4 = 2.75 > \sqrt{7}$ on an
ideal singlet without closing the open-line dynamical stall.

Experiment \code{E-QTM-0207} (Tier~B / L1) adds two standalone register checks: a reversible $N = 10$ brickwork
scrambler approaching the Page subsystem entropy peak \cite{page1993} while maintaining near-zero global von Neumann
entropy $S_{\mathrm{vN}}$, and exact \code{BigInt} meet-in-the-middle (MITM) $SU(2)$ word synthesis
($\ell_w = 6, \ldots, 22$) measuring a prepared textbook singlet to $4.87 \times 10^{-10}$ below $2\sqrt{2}$. As a
standalone L1 check, \code{E-QTM-0207} closes neither the open-line dynamical stall (\code{E-QTM-0202}) nor
bounded-clock horizon radiation (\code{E-GRV-0134}). Table~\ref{tab:quantum-b} collects all record-branch, open-line,
and L1 benchmark metrics.

\subsection{Two-body scattering, Yang--Baxter integrability, and bare-rule universality}
\label{sec:bare-smatrix}

Because $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ is silent for $k < 4$, the entire two-excitation sector of bare $U$ reduces exactly to
$L_2 = C_{1/2} M_{\mathrm{meet}} M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18}) K C_{1/2}$. Eliminating non-propagating components on a
single 1D antipodal line gives an exact $2 \times 2$ transfer matrix and closed-form two-body scattering amplitude
$S_{12}(k_1, b_1; k_2, b_2) = \frac{1}{2}(S_S + 1)I + \frac{1}{2}(S_S - 1)P_{12}$ over the 9-role space
$\mathbb{C}^9 \otimes \mathbb{C}^9$ (Tier~A, exact across all $U(1)$ contact phases $\eta$). Testing the
spectral-parameter Yang--Baxter equation \cite{yang1967, baxter1972} across the entire unit circle reveals only three
isolated integrable phases, $\eta \in \{1, \omega^2, -\omega\}$, and bare $U$'s two 1D collision channels land directly
on two of them:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Like-sign distinct roles ($C_{1/2} M_{\mathrm{meet}} C_{1/2}$, $\eta = \omega^2$):} Satisfies the exact
$SU(9)$ Yangian rational $R$-matrix $S_{12} = \frac{(\lambda_1 - \lambda_2)I - i P_{12}}{\lambda_1 - \lambda_2 + i}$
with closed-form rapidity $\lambda(k, b) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}} \frac{\sin E_b(k)}{\sin k}$ (Yang--Baxter residual
$6.7 \times 10^{-16}$, matching the rational formula to $6.9 \times 10^{-15}$). At total momentum $P = 0$, the bound
pole solves algebraically in the Eisenstein ring as an exact molecule with decay factor $|u| = 1/13$:
\begin{equation}
e^{i E_{\mathrm{bound}}(0)} = \frac{4 + 3\omega}{1 - 3\omega} = \frac{7 + 15\omega}{13} \in \mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/13],
\qquad E_{\mathrm{bound}}(0) = 1.609267\text{ rad},
\label{eq:eisenstein-molecule}
\end{equation}
matching $L = 24$ exact diagonalization to $2.8 \times 10^{-13}$.
\item \textbf{Opposite-sign bounce ($C_{1/2} K C_{1/2}$, $\eta = -\omega$):} Yields exact hard-core exchange
$S_S \equiv -1$ identically ($S_{12} = -P_{12}$, Yang--Baxter residual $1.8 \times 10^{-15}$) with zero bound states at
every momentum $P$.
\item \textbf{4D non-integrability via $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$:} Turning on cross-line scattering shifts the
4D $s$-wave contact phase to $\eta_s = \omega^2 \zeta_{18}$ ($260^\circ$), breaking the spectral-parameter Yang--Baxter
equation at $O(1)$ ($\|\mathrm{YBE}\|_F = 1.8420$), while local $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ breaks the constant braid YBE with
exact Frobenius norm $3/2$ on qutrits. Bare $U$ therefore exhibits a sharp transition from exact 1D Yang--Baxter
integrability to genuine 4D non-integrability.
\end{itemize}

Every bare two-excitation collision gate is also strictly entangling and strictly non-Clifford (up to $96.98\%$ of CNOT
entangling power over two bare $s$-wave beats), and exact \code{BigInt} evolution of an unentangled product state on
$\{3,4,3,4\}$ yields Schmidt weights $(3/4, 1/4)$, concurrence $\sqrt{3}/2$, and entanglement entropy $0.8113$ bits in
one beat (norm error $0$). While an isolated lone excitation is capped at $\langle C \rangle$ (limiting bare-coin CHSH
strictly to $2.000000$), multi-excitation collisions inside bare $U$ establish \textbf{two independent zero-external-gate
universality constructions} (with zero $P_\omega$ or $\Sigma(648)$ additions):
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Single-excitation dense $SU(2)$ via 3-full-line $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ catalysis:} No 2-full-line
non-branching catalyst exists ($0/880$), because all $3{,}696/3{,}696$ 4-subsets of $D_4$ containing an antipodal pair
span at most 3D and satisfy $\chi_{\mathrm{odd}} \equiv 0$. Adding a third full spectator line (7 excitations total at
one dock, keeping $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ silent with zero branching and zero spectator entanglement) induces the exact
\code{BigInt} local gate
\begin{equation}
L_7 = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} \omega & -1 - 2\omega \\ \omega - 1 & \omega \end{pmatrix} \in M_2(\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]),
\qquad \det(L_7 C) = 1,
\qquad \operatorname{Tr}(L_7 C) = -\frac{5}{4}.
\label{eq:catalyst-l7}
\end{equation}
By the 2-adic valuation proof ($M = -4 L_7 C$ satisfies $M^2 = 5M - 16I$, forcing $\operatorname{Tr}(M^n) \equiv 1
\pmod 4$ for all $n \ge 1$), $L_7 C$ has infinite order and does not commute with $C$ ($\|[L_7, C]\|_F = 3\sqrt{6}/4$),
making $\langle C, L_7 \rangle$ dense in $SU(2)$ purely inside bare $U$.
\item \textbf{Universal $SU(4)$ and bare-analyzer Bell--CHSH from bare $M_{\mathrm{meet}}$:} Encoding each logical
qubit in the 2D $S = 1/2$ decoherence-free subspace of three like-sign excitations carrying two roles, pure bare
$M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ collisions on pairs $(1,2)$ and $(2,3)$ preserve the DFS with zero leakage and satisfy
$\operatorname{Tr}(S_{12} S_{23}) = 5/4$ (dense in $SU(2)$ by the same 2-adic argument). Across two 3-excitation DFS
qubits (six excitations in the 9D $S_{\mathrm{tot}} = 1$ irrep), exact commutator closure generates the full
80-dimensional Lie algebra $\mathfrak{su}(9)$, containing universal $SU(4)$ with zero leakage. Using solely forward
bare-$M_{\mathrm{meet}}$ analyzer words of length $\le 8$ at spatially separated docks violates Bell--CHSH to
$2.6412$ ($99.83\%$ of $\sqrt{7}$) on a 1-beat bare pair and $2.8262$ ($99.92\%$ of Tsirelson's $2\sqrt{2}$) on a
bare-DFS singlet, eliminating the need for external $\Sigma(648)$ gates in either state preparation or measurement
(Table~\ref{tab:bare-smatrix}).
\end{enumerate}

Although the operational Born postulate and the open-line Tsirelson stall remain open, the bare multi-excitation beat
is computationally universal and non-integrable in 4D. Because a lone excitation never leaves its initial line,
three-dimensional spatial kinematics requires two excitations interacting through $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$,
which Section~\ref{sec:relativity} analyzes on flat 4D $D_4$ and on the 3D cusp husk.
