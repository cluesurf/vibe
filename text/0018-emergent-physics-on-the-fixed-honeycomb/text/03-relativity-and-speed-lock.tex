\section{Relativistic Kinematics and Speed Lock}
\label{sec:relativity}

Without cross-line coupling, every dock operator acts solely within one of the 12 antipodal $D_4$ lines, trapping
excitations on one-dimensional rails (the bare \emph{line law}) and breaking 3D rotational isotropy completely. How
can the automaton break rail confinement without destroying localized bound structures, recover isotropic relativistic
kinematics in three dimensions, and keep gravity, light, and chiral matter locked to one universal limiting speed
within the binary neutron-star merger observation GW170817 \cite{abbott2017gw170817}, which constrained the speed
difference between gravitational waves and light to $-3 \times 10^{-15} \le \Delta c / c \le +7 \times 10^{-16}$?

\paragraph{Key findings.}
Restricting cross-line scattering to Scale~2 via $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ eliminates single-excitation drain
while breaking 1D rail confinement and recovering $O(k^{2.00})$ 3D isotropy plus relativistic dispersion-relation boost
ratios on the reflecting floor-0 cusp slab at bare speed $c_{\mathrm{eff}} = 0.021267$ (\code{E-RLT-0123},
\code{E-RLT-0124}). At Tier~B, shared-beam minimal coupling locks all massless species algebraically to a single
speed $c_u = 0.977925$, verified to IEEE-754 machine precision ($3.41 \times 10^{-16}$ gravity vs.\ matter,
$2.27 \times 10^{-16}$ vs.\ light, \code{E-RLT-0125}), leaving a $46.0\times$ bare-versus-beam speed gap explicitly
open.

\universecode), fixes the 12-line 4D $s$-wave as an exact eigenstate, opens a finite spectral gap $|1 - \zeta_{18}| =

\subsection{Bare 3D isotropy and relativistic boost kinematics}

the bare control ($u = 1$) stays line-confined with flat Bloch bands, $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ breaks the line

\subsection{Shared-beam speed lock and the bare speed gap}

Relativistic kinematics in one sector is observationally ruled out if gravity, light, and chiral matter propagate at
different limiting speeds. Experiment \code{E-RLT-0125} audits species universality as a strict \textbf{Tier~B
shared-beam kinematic minimal-coupling identity} rather than a bare Tier~A derivation.

On the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ cusp beam equipped with the Tier~B pair-density warp $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$ (Section~\ref{sec:gravity}),
each floor $D = 0, \ldots, F$ carries vertical inertia $M_D = N_D w(D) = 1/N_D$ and lateral stiffness
$K_D = l_D w(D)$. Using the canonical unaliased single-face lateral links \mbox{$l_D = 1, 4, 20, 100$} of
Table~\ref{tab:shells} gives $K_{\mathrm{eff}} = 1.012532$ and $M_{\mathrm{eff}} = 1.058761$. In the decoupled
negative control, unwarped husk light and Weyl fermions travel at $c_{\mathrm{raw}} = 1.0$ while warped gravity travels
at $c_{\mathrm{grav}} = 0.977925$, creating a $2.21\%$ mismatch that exceeds the GW170817 upper bound ($+7 \times
10^{-16}$) by more than 13 orders of magnitude.

When spin-2 transverse-traceless (TT) gravitons ($6(F+1)$ Voigt operator), spin-1 curl-curl Maxwell photons ($3(F+1)$),
and spin-$1/2$ chiral Weyl fermions ($2(F+1)$) are minimally coupled via Kronecker products to the exact same warped
beam operator $H_{\mathrm{warp}}(\mathbf{k})$ with vertical Laplacian $L_{\mathrm{vert}}$ obeying a \textbf{reflecting
Neumann floor} at $D = F$, the unique vertical zero mode $u_0$ satisfies $L_{\mathrm{vert}} u_0 = 0$ identically
(agreeing with the Dirichlet floor used in \code{E-GRV-0157} to $< 10^{-15}$ by $F = 12$ because
$c_{\mathrm{vert}}(12) \sim 10^{-15}$) and cancels every polarization norm and momentum factor algebraically across all
three species:
\begin{equation}
c_{\mathrm{grav}} = c_{\mathrm{light}} = c_{\mathrm{matter}}
= \sqrt{\frac{K_{\mathrm{eff}}}{M_{\mathrm{eff}}}}
= \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{D=0}^{F} l_D w(D)}{\sum_{D=0}^{F} N_D w(D)}}
= 0.977925 < 1.
\label{eq:speed-lock}
\end{equation}

Four quantitative disclosures qualify Equation~\eqref{eq:speed-lock}:
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Algebraic identity at machine precision:} Because all three species share the same Kronecker-product
beam operator by construction, $\Delta c / c = 0$ is an exact algebraic identity verified to IEEE-754 \code{Float64}
precision ($3.41 \times 10^{-16}$ gravity vs.\ matter, $2.27 \times 10^{-16}$ vs.\ light, consistent with GW170817

measurement outperforming an astrophysical bound.
\item \textbf{Nonlocal square-root Weyl operator:} The chiral Weyl operator $D_{\mathrm{Weyl}}(\mathbf{k}) =

\item \textbf{Single-face vs.\ period-2 links and super-Nyquist KK gap:} Canonical single-face links

\item \textbf{Open $46\times$ bare-to-beam speed gap:} The bare Tier~A floor-0 Dirac speed $c_{\mathrm{eff}} =
0.021267$ (\code{E-RLT-0124}) sits $46.0\times$ below canonical Tier~B $c_u = 0.977925$ ($46.4\times$ below the
