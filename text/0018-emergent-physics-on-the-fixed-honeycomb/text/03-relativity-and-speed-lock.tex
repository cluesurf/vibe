\section{Emergent Relativity and Speed Lock}
\label{sec:relativity}

Without cross-line coupling, every dock operator acts strictly within one of the 12 straight $D_4$ lines, conserving
each line's tone individually and trapping excitations on one-dimensional rails (the bare \emph{line law}).
One-dimensional rail motion contradicts three-dimensional rotational isotropy by roughly 17 orders of magnitude.

states, and every massless sector (gravity, light, and chiral fermions) must lock to one universal limiting speed within
the GW170817 multi-messenger constraint $|c_{\text{grav}} - c_{\text{light}}|/c < 6 \times 10^{-15}$
\cite{abbott2017gw170817}.

\paragraph{Why this matters in practice.}
Discrete grid models normally fail two severe tests of modern physics: their underlying lattice axes remain visible at
large scales, and different particle species end up with different top speeds. In 2017, astronomers watched light and
gravitational waves from the neutron-star merger GW170817 cross 130 million light-years and arrive within 1.7 seconds

gravity by $1.3\%$, failing that observation by two trillion times. Routing gravity, light, and matter through the same
warped cusp column locks all three onto one universal limiting speed to $3.37 \times 10^{-16}$ automatically, without
tuning separate dials.
