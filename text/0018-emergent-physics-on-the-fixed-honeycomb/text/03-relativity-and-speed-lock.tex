\section{Relativistic Kinematics, Isotropy, and Speed Bounds}
\label{sec:relativity}

Because a lone excitation never leaves its initial line, the single-excitation sector separates into twelve decoupled
one-dimensional walks per dock with no three-dimensional rotational symmetry (the \emph{line law}). Placing two
identical excitations in opposite slots of one line activates the pair-line mixer $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$,
which transfers amplitude uniformly across all twelve lines in one beat.

On the bare rule (Tier~A), pair scattering produces two distinct continuum regimes: on flat 4D $D_4$, the unique
$s$-wave bound state obeys an exact $SO(4)$-isotropic massive scalar dispersion through both $O(K^2)$ and $O(K^4)$
(first breaking at $O(K^6)$), while on the truncated 3D floor-0 husk, it gives $O(k^{2.00})$ 3D isotropy and
avoided-crossing Dirac dispersion ratios at bare speed $c_{\mathrm{eff}} = 0.021267$ (\code{E-RLT-0123},
\code{E-RLT-0124}). Sweeping $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(u)$ across all 18 cyclotomic roots in $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9]$ and all of
$U(1)$ bounds bare floor-0 speed below $0.129492$, still $7.55\times$ below the Tier~B shared-beam speed
$c_u = 0.977925$ (\code{E-RLT-0125}), where gravity, light, and nonlocal Weyl matter lock to machine precision as an
algebraic identity of the shared warped beam (consistent with GW170817,
$-3 \times 10^{-15} \le \Delta c / c \le +7 \times 10^{-16}$ \cite{abbott2017gw170817}).

\subsection{Pair scattering and the line law}

Mixing every lone excitation across lines fails, as Tier~A negative controls show.
Sweeping a single-bank mixer $\mathrm{F\text{-}G}(\theta) = I_8 + \frac{e^{i\theta}-1}{8} J_8$ drains bound states with
no prethermal plateau (\code{E-SLF-0189}, \code{E-SLF-0198}), while a parity-sensitive neighbor lock protects isolated
line states only because the dock graph is bipartite and immediately leaks once external bath arrivals or pair-store
creation flip local parity (\code{E-SLF-0190}, \code{E-SLF-0192}, \code{E-SLF-0195}).

The rule therefore mixes lines only when a dock holds an identical full-line pair. The $W(F_4)$-isotropic pair-line
mixer $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ of Equation~\eqref{eq:mpair} removes both obstructions (\code{E-SLF-0199},
\code{E-SLF-0200}). Lone excitations stay on their lines, and identical pairs change lines. It leaves $|V_1\rangle$
and all 24 single-excitation slots untouched, scatters an identical full-line pair isotropically across all 12 lines in
one beat (verified at the exact involution $u = -1$ on the $R = 3$ ball in \code{E-SLF-0199} and at $u = \zeta_{18}$ in
\universecode), fixes the 12-line 4D $s$-wave as an exact eigenstate, opens a finite spectral gap $|1 - \zeta_{18}| =
0.3473$, and supports a sparse reversible boundary filter on a 1D $L = 32$ periodic-line bath engine.

\subsection{3D isotropy, 4D \texorpdfstring{$s$}{s}-wave dispersion, and the floor-0 speed ceiling}

The continuum effective Hamiltonian $H_{\mathrm{eff}} = i \ln U$ clarifies the difference between the full 4D $D_4$
bulk and its truncated 3D floor-0 slice (Tier~A). In the palindromic Strang frame $C_{1/2} S(\mathbf{k}) C_{1/2}$ on
flat 4D $D_4$, all $\sigma_y$ Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff splitting errors cancel identically to all orders (versus an
$O(k)$ error of slope $\pi/3$ for non-palindromic $S C$), and inversion parity $E(-\mathbf{k}) = E(\mathbf{k})$ kills
all odd-order dispersion terms. For two identical-tone, identical-role excitations interacting through
$M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$, the infinite 4D $D_4$ two-body problem reduces with zero truncation to a 12-branch
star graph coupled at relative separation $r = 0$. At $\mathbf{K} = \mathbf{0}$, the 12-fold degeneracy splits into 11
non-$s$-wave Eisenstein bound states at $E_p(0) = 1.609267\text{ rad}$ and one unique non-degenerate 4D $s$-wave bound
state at $E_s(0) = 1.770647\text{ rad}$ ($|u_s| = 0.07402$, $\kappa_s = 1.3017\text{ dock}^{-1}$). Because all 12 $D_4$
root lines enter symmetrically, the individual 4D $s$-wave dispersion obeys
\begin{equation}
E_s(\mathbf{K}) = E_s(0) - 0.105504\,\|\mathbf{K}\|^2 + 0.048720\,\|\mathbf{K}\|^4 + c_6(\hat{\mathbf{K}})\,\|\mathbf{K}\|^6 + O(K^8),
\label{eq:4d-swave-bch}
\end{equation}
achieving \textbf{exact $SO(4)$ isotropy through both $O(K^2)$ and $O(K^4)$} (numerical axis spreads $5.1 \times
10^{-10}$ and $3.0 \times 10^{-7}$ across all four 4D symmetry axes) and pushing the first $W(F_4)$ rotational breaking
to $O(K^6)$.

Truncating to the 3D husk changes the rotational order sharply. Closing every bulk-directed line at $D = 0$ defines the
\textbf{reflecting floor-0 slab} (\mbox{\code{period = 4}}, \mbox{\code{floor = 0}}). In the exact twisted-Bloch gauge
induced by the parabolic cusp translations of $O^+(4,1; \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}])$, all 24 single-particle floor-0 bands
are identically flat ($v_g \equiv 0$, topologically edge-caged on 2-dock pairs). Turning on $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(u)$ on

\subsection{Shared-beam speed lock}

Relativistic kinematics in one sector is ruled out observationally if gravity, light, and matter have different
limiting speeds. Experiment \code{E-RLT-0125} couples every species to the same prescribed warped beam (Tier~B) and
compares their speeds. Their agreement is an algebraic identity of the shared coupling. It is not derived from the
bare rule.

On the cusp beam with the Tier~B pair-density warp $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$ (introduced in Section~\ref{sec:gravity}),
each floor $D = 0, \ldots, F$ carries vertical inertia $M_D = N_D w(D) = 1/N_D$ and lateral stiffness
$K_D = l_D w(D)$. Using the canonical unaliased single-face lateral links \mbox{$l_D = 1, 4, 20, 100$} of
Table~\ref{tab:shells} gives $K_{\mathrm{eff}} = 1.012532$ and $M_{\mathrm{eff}} = 1.058761$. In the decoupled
negative control, unwarped husk light and Weyl fermions travel at $c_{\mathrm{raw}} = 1.0$ while warped gravity travels
at $c_{\mathrm{grav}} = 0.977925$, creating a $2.21\%$ mismatch that exceeds the GW170817 upper bound ($+7 \times
10^{-16}$) by more than 13 orders of magnitude.

When spin-2 transverse-traceless (TT) gravitons ($6(F+1)$ Voigt operator), spin-1 curl-curl Maxwell photons ($3(F+1)$),
and spin-$1/2$ chiral Weyl fermions ($2(F+1)$) are minimally coupled via Kronecker products to the exact same warped
beam operator $H_{\mathrm{warp}}(\mathbf{k})$ with vertical Laplacian $L_{\mathrm{vert}}$ obeying a \textbf{reflecting
Neumann floor} at $D = F$, the unique vertical zero mode $u_0$ satisfies $L_{\mathrm{vert}} u_0 = 0$ identically
(agreeing with the Dirichlet floor used in \code{E-GRV-0157} to $< 10^{-15}$ by $F = 12$ because
$c_{\mathrm{vert}}(12) \sim 10^{-15}$) and cancels every polarization norm and momentum factor algebraically across all
three species:
\begin{equation}
c_{\mathrm{grav}} = c_{\mathrm{light}} = c_{\mathrm{matter}}
= \sqrt{\frac{K_{\mathrm{eff}}}{M_{\mathrm{eff}}}}
= \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{D=0}^{F} l_D w(D)}{\sum_{D=0}^{F} N_D w(D)}}
= 0.977925 < 1.
\label{eq:speed-lock}
\end{equation}

Four quantitative disclosures qualify Equation~\eqref{eq:speed-lock}:
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Algebraic identity at machine precision:} Because all three species share the same Kronecker-product
beam operator by construction, $\Delta c / c = 0$ is an exact algebraic identity verified to IEEE-754 \code{Float64}
precision ($3.41 \times 10^{-16}$ gravity vs.\ matter, $2.27 \times 10^{-16}$ vs.\ light, consistent with GW170817
$-3 \times 10^{-15} \le \Delta c / c \le +7 \times 10^{-16}$ \cite{abbott2017gw170817}) rather than a physical
measurement outperforming an astrophysical bound.
\item \textbf{Nonlocal square-root Weyl operator and Cauchy--Schwarz obstruction:} The chiral Weyl operator
$D_{\mathrm{Weyl}}(\mathbf{k}) = \hat{\Sigma}(\mathbf{k}) \otimes H_{\mathrm{warp}}(\mathbf{k})^{1/2}$ is strictly
depth-nonlocal in position space ($|S_{0,D}| > 0$ for every depth $D \ge 2$). Because bosonic operators average the
squared shell speed $v_D^2 = l_D / N_D$ over shell inertia $m_D = N_D w(D)$ ($c_u^2 = \langle v_D^2 \rangle_m$),
whereas any site-local linear multi-floor Dirac operator averages $v_D$ linearly
($c_{\mathrm{Dirac,local}} = \langle v_D \rangle_m$), the strict Cauchy--Schwarz variance identity
$c_u^2 - c_{\mathrm{Dirac,local}}^2 = \operatorname{Var}_m(v_D) > 0$ enforces an irreducible $0.8143\%$ mismatch on
canonical single-face links ($0.2310\%$ on period-2 links), exceeding GW170817 by more than 11 orders of magnitude.
Closing that mismatch with a site-local Dirac operator requires a bulk metric whose lateral shell speed $v_D$ is
uniform across depth.
\item \textbf{Single-face vs.\ period-2 links and super-Nyquist KK gap:} Canonical single-face links
\mbox{$l_D = 1, 4, 20, 100$} give $c_u = 0.977925$, while aliased \mbox{\code{period = 2}} links ($1, 10, 40, 200$)

conditionally in Lean~4 for all $F \ge 2$ in Section~\ref{sec:unified}) and gap the vertical Kaluza--Klein tower at
$m_{\mathrm{KK}} = 3.2725\text{ rad/beat}$ (linearized only, not excluding nonlinear Ho\v{r}ava--Lifshitz scalar modes
\cite{horava2009}). Because $m_{\mathrm{KK}} > \pi$ sits above the 1-beat Nyquist frequency, the symplectic leapfrog
verifier runs at sub-beat resolution $\Delta t = 1/6\text{ beat}$.
\item \textbf{Open $46\times$ bare-to-beam speed gap:} The bare Tier~A floor-0 Dirac speed $c_{\mathrm{eff}} =
0.021267$ (\code{E-RLT-0124}) sits $46.0\times$ below canonical Tier~B $c_u = 0.977925$ ($46.4\times$ below the
\mbox{\code{period = 2}} control). Closing this speed gap and deriving species-universal minimal coupling from the bare
QCA remains open (Section~\ref{sec:open}).
\end{enumerate}
