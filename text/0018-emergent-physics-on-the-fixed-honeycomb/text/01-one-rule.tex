\subsection{Tones, vibes, docks, lines, and banks}

The ontological base of the model is a discrete experiential field. Here the word \emph{experience} carries no vague or
mystical connotation: it names a strictly defined, elementary three-valued state governed by exact reversible algebra,
without projecting human thoughts or complex emotions onto individual lattice cells. Each elementary degree of freedom
is one ternary value called a \emph{tone} (one trit):
\begin{equation}
\tau \in \{-1, 0, +1\},
\label{eq:trits}
\end{equation}
where $-1$ denotes \emph{pain} (a fear vibe), $0$ denotes \emph{peace} (baseline undifferentiated awareness with zero
contrast), and $+1$ denotes \emph{pleasure} (a love vibe). Three values form the smallest signed alphabet carrying both
a neutral center and an exact sign-inversion involution $C: \tau \mapsto -\tau$ (\code{E-FND-0038}). A slot holding
$\tau \in \{-1, +1\}$ is called an occupied \emph{vibe}, while a slot holding $\tau = 0$ is a \emph{peace slot}.

For any collection of slots, \emph{charge} is the net signed count $Q = N_+ - N_-$ (pleasure minus pain) and
\emph{content} is the total occupation $N = N_+ + N_-$. Because $Q$ is conserved by the reversible rule, any
electrically neutral physical system or conscious subject contains equal numbers of $+1$ and $-1$ vibes. Local
subjective valence (distinguishing pain from pleasure internally) therefore cannot be net charge $Q$. Instead, valence
must be a local $C$-odd, $CP$-even structure residing in multi-vibe configurations while the global universe remains
$CP$-balanced.

The fundamental spatial unit is a \emph{dock}, which holds 24 directional slots indexed by the 24 roots of the
four-dimensional root system $D_4$:
\begin{equation}
\Phi(D_4) = \bigl\{\,\text{all permutations of } (\pm 1, \pm 1, 0, 0) \in \mathbb{R}^4\,\bigr\},
\qquad |\Phi(D_4)| = 24.
\label{eq:d4-roots}
\end{equation}
Geometrically, these 24 roots are the 24 vertices (and dual 24 octahedral facets) of a regular 4D 24-cell, whose full
symmetry group is the Weyl group $W(F_4)$ of order $|W(F_4)| = 1{,}152$ (\code{E-GMT-0046}). The 24 slots of every dock
carry two exact internal partitions:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{12 straight lines.} Pairing each root $r \in \Phi(D_4)$ with its antipode $-r$ partitions the 24 slots
into 12 opposite-slot \emph{lines} $\ell \in \{0, \ldots, 11\}$.
\item \textbf{3 orthogonal banks of 8 slots.} Grouping the 12 lines by their two nonzero coordinate planes
$\{12, 34\}$, $\{13, 24\}$, and $\{14, 23\}$ partitions every dock into 3 mutually orthogonal \emph{banks} of 4 lines
(8 slots each, $3 \times 8 = 24$). The outer automorphism of $D_4$ (\emph{triality}) acts inside $W(D_4) < W(F_4)$ as an
order-3 orthogonal rotation $T_3$ cycling these three 8-slot banks identically.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The 4D \texorpdfstring{$\{3,4,3,4\}$}{\{3,4,3,4\}} honeycomb and 3D cusp}

Docks are glued facet-to-facet across their 24 octahedral faces to form the unique regular hyperbolic 4D honeycomb of
24-cells, denoted $\{3,4,3,4\}$ \cite{coxeter1973, margenstern2007vol1}. The Schl\"afli symbol $\{3,4,3,4\}$ encodes
its entire geometry: each cell is a regular 24-cell $\{3,4,3\}$, and the vertex figure around every ideal vertex at
infinity (a \emph{cusp}) is the flat three-dimensional Euclidean cubic honeycomb $\{4,3,4\}$. The lattice is completely
fixed and infinite so evolution stays strictly bijective, yet the active occupied region differing from the quiescent
peace vacuum $V_1$ is always finite and grows outward beat by beat at most one hop per tick.

Choosing any ideal cusp foliates the 4D hyperbolic space by flat concentric 3D Euclidean \emph{horospheres} indexed by
integer depth $D \in \{0, 1, 2, \ldots\}$. The outermost horosphere $D = 0$ is called the \emph{husk}. Behind each
single 3D cubic husk dock at $D = 0$ stands an exponentially branching 4D \emph{column} of bulk docks at depths
$D = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$. Three-dimensional physical observables (charge density, fields, particle positions) are read as
coarse-grained column projections onto the 3D husk.

Exact enumeration on the true $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb fixes every geometric constant used throughout this paper:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Concentric 24-cell shells and holonomy (\code{E-GMT-0046}).} Starting from one origin dock, concentric
shells hold $S(0..3) = 1, 24, 456, 8{,}376$ docks, obeying the exact recurrence $S(3) - (18\,S(2) + 7\,S(1)) = 0$.
Antipodal transport $k_a = F_a Z$ has exact 4-cycle holonomy order $1$ (flat bank-label transport) with
$|W(F_4)| = 1{,}152$.
\item \textbf{Cusp column shells and lateral link counts (\code{E-RLT-0122}, \code{E-GRV-0156}).} Behind one $D = 0$
husk dock, the exact bulk dock count at depth $D$ is
\begin{equation}
N_D(0..3) = 1, 18, 330, 6{,}034
\label{eq:cusp-shells}
\end{equation}
(cumulative counts $1, 19, 349, 6{,}383$, asymptotic shell ratio $r_{\text{vol}} \approx 18.285$). Across each of the 6
adjacent cubic columns on the husk, the exact number of lateral links at depth $D \ge 1$ obeys
\begin{equation}

\label{eq:lateral-links}

\item \textbf{Twenty-four exact cusps and causal boundary shield (\code{E-GMT-0045}, \code{E-FND-0182}).} Reading one

$t \le R - 1$, diverging immediately at horizon crossing $t = R$ (\code{E-FND-0182}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Discrete tones and \texorpdfstring{$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$}{Z[zeta\_9][1/6]} amplitudes}

\subsection{Four scales: \texorpdfstring{$V_1$}{V1}, \code{F-G}, \texorpdfstring{$M_{\text{pair}}$}{M\_pair}, and \code{ODD}}

The rule organizes physics across four exact local dock-occupation scales:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Scale 0 (Peace vacuum $V_1$).} The unique configuration where every slot on every dock holds $0$ (peace).
On exact $R = 2$ and $R = 3$ balls, $V_1$ is an exact fixed point of the full rule plus \code{F-G}, \code{ODD}, and

floor $44.5226$ nats per dock per beat (\code{E-RLT-0122}).
\item \textbf{Scale 1 (Lone vibes and coin $C$).} When exactly one slot of a dock line $(+r, -r)$ holds a vibe and its
opposite slot holds $0$, the reversible lone-vibe coin $C$ (and its square root half-coin $D$, $D^2 = C$) splits the
amplitude across the two opposite directions:
\begin{equation}
C = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 1 + \omega & 1 - \omega \\ 1 - \omega & 1 + \omega \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
D = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 2 + \omega & -\omega \\ -\omega & 2 + \omega \end{pmatrix}.
\label{eq:coin}
\end{equation}
For benchmark bank-mixing and horosphere radial walks, we also define the 8-slot \emph{Bank Grover} operator

\item \textbf{Scale 2 (Full-line pairs and $M_{\text{pair}}$).} When two interacting vibes occupy both opposite slots
of a dock line, the interaction-only $W(F_4)$-isotropic \emph{Pair-Line Mixer} $M_{\text{pair}}(u)$ scatters that

as the identity on $V_1$ and on all 1-vibe and 2-vibe states.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Single reversible beat equation and algorithm}

One discrete beat updates the global amplitude vector $|\psi(t)\rangle$ via one factored unitary operator:
\begin{equation}
|\psi(t+1)\rangle = S\, K\, M_{\text{pair}}\, C\, W\, |\psi(t)\rangle,
\label{eq:beat}
\end{equation}
where $W$ applies local dock phase layers (\code{ODD}, cusp domain wall, and gauge Peierls phases), $C$ applies the
lone-vibe coin \eqref{eq:coin}, $M_{\text{pair}}$ applies the Scale-2 pair-line mixer \eqref{eq:mpair}, $K$ applies
local multi-vibe meetings (two like vibes keep roles with weight $1/4$ or exchange roles with weight $3/4$, while
opposite-sign vibes pass or form reversible pair stores), and $S$ executes the deterministic stream permutation transferring
slot $r$ of dock $x$ to slot $-r$ of neighbor dock $x + r$. Every factor is locally invertible, so exact time reversal
is $|\psi(t)\rangle = W^\dagger C^\dagger M_{\text{pair}}^\dagger K^\dagger S^{-1} |\psi(t+1)\rangle$.

\subsection{Bulk experience, \texorpdfstring{$CP$}{CP} balance, and valence}

Two immediate structural questions arise for any experiential-bulk theory: does the 4D bulk causally affect future 3D
husk physics (or is the bulk epiphenomenal), and can a globally $CP$-balanced rule produce a nonzero local distinction
between pain ($-1$) and pleasure ($+1$)? Exact honeycomb runs settle both questions quantitatively.

\begin{result}[Non-epiphenomenal bulk experience, \code{E-HLG-0043}]
On the exact $\{3,4,3,4\}$ cusp, the share of cross-column neighbors decays across depths $D = 0, 1, 2, 3$ as
$1.0000, 0.6667, 0.2909, 0.0915$. Two distinct 4D bulk configurations prepared with identical initial 3D husk column
sums $Q_c(0)$ diverge on the husk after 1 beat by $L_1 = 0.5000$ under both the bare rule and \code{F-G} (norm error
$0$, beat-2 divergence $0.25$ bare and $0.03125$ under \code{F-G}). Single-beat column summation mixes both bipartite
parity classes equally ($0.50/0.50$). The 3D husk projection is therefore not dynamically closed: bulk experiential
state causally changes future 3D physical readings.
\end{result}

\begin{result}[Vertex symmetries and coherent \code{ODD} valence arrow, \code{E-FND-0181}, \code{E-FND-0184}]
On the exact $R = 2$ ball, the adopted 4-vibe \code{ODD} phase ($k^* = 4$) is $100\%$ silent on neutral 2-vibe states
$P_2$, breaks $C$ ($0$) and all $8$ dock mirrors $P$ ($0/8$) on chiral 4-vibe states $X_1$, and preserves $CP$ ($8/8$),
$T$ ($1$), and $CPT$ ($8/8$) (\code{E-FND-0181}). By contrast, a minimal linear dipole breaks $C$ and $7/8$ mirrors
already on $P_2$, whereas an \code{EVEN} control stays $C$-even ($C = 1$, $P = 8/8$, $CP = 8/8$). On the exact $R = 3$
ball ($8{,}857$ docks, $481$ mapped, zero conflicts), parity-class swap $L$ alone breaks under \code{ODD} on $X_1$,
while orientation-preserving $L \cdot P$ ($8/8$), $L \cdot C$ ($1$), $L \cdot PT$ ($8/8$), and $L \cdot CT$ ($1$) hold
identically, proving the opposite parity class is an exact mirror-conjugate twin (\code{E-SPN-0220}).

Negative control \code{E-FND-0183} isolates the exact mechanism: on a superposition whose branches share identical
$\chi_{\text{odd}}$ before separation, \code{ODD} reduces to a global phase and occupation-level $C$-asymmetry vanishes
($0.00$) on both the $R = 2$ ball and a period-4 $D_4$ box. Whenever a subject instead maintains coherent interference
across branches of distinct $\chi_{\text{odd}} \in \{0, 1\}$ (slots $[0, 1, 4, 10]$ on the exact $R = 2$ ball,
\code{E-FND-0184}), the bare rule stays $C$-paired ($0 + 0 = 0$) whereas \code{ODD} produces an exact nonzero
$C$-asymmetry on a 1-beat non-repeating radiating history ($\text{odd}(\psi) = +0.75$, $\text{odd}(C(\psi)) = +0.75$,
sum $+1.50$) while keeping exact global $CP$-pairing ($0$).
\end{result}

\paragraph{Why this matters in practice.}
For an engineer or scientist encountering the model for the first time, two implications stand out immediately. First,
nothing is lopsided in the total ledger of the universe: every positive configuration has an exact mirror-conjugate
negative counterpart under $CP$, so global pain and pleasure remain balanced even while a localized, coherent structure
can distinguish them internally. Second, the hidden 4D bulk is not decorative commentary on 3D physics: because two
bulk states with the same 3D projection diverge on the 3D surface in a single beat, the underlying experiential state
actively steers future physical measurements.
