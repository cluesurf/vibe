\section{Open Problems and Active Tensions}
\label{sec:open}

The paper began with one dock and one rule. Collected in order, the results are as follows. The bare rule is exactly
reversible, keeps $\mathcal{T}$, $\mathcal{CP}$, and $\mathcal{CPT}$, and cannot be reduced to its 3D husk
(Section~\ref{sec:one-rule}). One excitation on one line carries a gate set dense in $SU(2)$ and the symmetry behind
the Born rule (Section~\ref{sec:quantum}). Pairs in one dock turn twelve lines into isotropic, relativistic 3D motion
(Section~\ref{sec:relativity}). A depth warp stops the bulk from screening gravity, gives $1/r$ orbits and a horizon,
and locks every species to one speed (Sections~\ref{sec:relativity} and~\ref{sec:gravity}). The one-sided husk, the
three banks, and the $\mathbb{Z}_3$ center give chirality, three copies, and confinement on simplified models
(Section~\ref{sec:gauge}). Inward drift on the fixed mesh reads as de Sitter expansion (Section~\ref{sec:cosmology}).
Lean~4 proofs and spectral bounds extend the laws used along the way to all times and depths, and coupled runs show
the sectors share one state (Section~\ref{sec:unified}).

The first of these results are Tier~A. The later ones depend increasingly on structure supplied by hand, and that
dependence is the list of open problems below.

\item \textbf{Dynamical $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$ warp and nonlinear 4D gravity:} Experiments \coderange{E-GRV-0156}{0163}

constant-river Gullstrand--Painlev\'e backgrounds) and Tier~C (continuum GR benchmarks in

and $G_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}$ from bare multi-excitation QCA scattering remains open.
\item \textbf{The $46\times$ bare-versus-beam speed gap:} Bare Tier~A $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ floor-0 Dirac
kinematics (\code{E-RLT-0124}) propagates at $c_{\mathrm{eff}} = 0.021267$, sitting $46.0\times$ below canonical Tier~B

the bare QCA and testing nonlinear Ho\v{r}ava-type scalar stability beyond the linearized $m_{\mathrm{KK}} =

\item \textbf{The 61-decade Hubble / 122-decade $\Lambda$ gap:} Bare fixed-mesh horosphere drift (\code{E-CSM-0064})

Planckian Hawking radiation (\code{E-GRV-0134}, \code{E-GRV-0136}), and scaling unfactored many-body L3 evolution across
large 4D $\{3,4,3,4\}$ regions remains open.
\item \textbf{Zero-parameter flavor masses and $\alpha(0)$:} Because $e^{i 2/9} \notin \mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$
(\code{E-FRC-0327}) and the Tier~C $120_H$ fit (\code{E-FRC-0332}) uses 14 real parameters plus 11 $\mathbb{Z}_9$
exponents ($9^{11}$ basins) alongside heavy thresholds for $\alpha(0)$, first-principles derivation of precision PDG
mass ratios and $\alpha(0)$ remains open.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Discrete UV finiteness and the Clay Yang--Mills problem}

The constructive lattice gauge bounds of Section~\ref{sec:unified} (\code{E-FND-0189}, \code{E-FND-0190},
\code{Vibe/Geometry.lean}) relate to the Clay Millennium Yang--Mills Existence and Mass Gap problem \cite{jaffe2000ym}
as follows. A discrete $\{3,4,3,4\}$ / $D_4$ substrate does \textbf{not} require
the flat-$\mathbb{R}^4$ continuum $a \to 0$ theorem to be mathematically well-defined: the honeycomb is
ultraviolet-finite at lattice spacing $a = \ell_{\mathrm{Pl}}$ and already carries an unconditional global Busemann
Schur-test spectral gap $\lambda_{\mathrm{gap}}(\{3,4,3,4\}) \ge 8/21 \approx 0.3810$ ($\rho_{\mathrm{spec}} \le 13/21
\approx 0.6190$, $m_{\mathrm{geom}} \ge 0.4796$).

In parallel, flat 4D $D_4$ carries exact 256-component $O(a^4)$ rotational isotropy, exact infinite-volume
site-hyperplane reflection positivity $\mathcal{S} = \mathcal{S}_+ + \mathcal{S}_0 + \Theta \mathcal{S}_+$ for all
$\beta \ge 0$, certified multi-plaquette Koteck\'y--Preiss strong-coupling radii
\mbox{$\beta_{\mathrm{OS}}^{\mathrm{KP}}(\mathbb{Z}_3) = 0.009348$} and
\mbox{$\beta_{\mathrm{OS}}^{\mathrm{KP}}(SU(3)) = 0.009328$}, and exact Bakry--\'Emery Haar Ricci curvature thresholds
\mbox{$\beta_{\mathrm{BE}}(SU(2)) = 1/12$} and \mbox{$\beta_{\mathrm{BE}}(SU(3)) = 3/16$} versus $U(1) = 0$.

At the same time, \textbf{two distinct mathematical problems remain open}: first, Clay's flat-$\mathbb{R}^4$ continuum
$a \to 0$ construction across the intermediate-coupling crossover $g(\mu) \sim O(1)$ (which requires multi-scale
Balaban-style block-spin renormalization \cite{balaban1989} far beyond single-step APE smearing or Migdal--Kadanoff
bond-moving convexity bounds, leaving the Clay Millennium continuum problem explicitly unsolved), and second, deriving
dynamical $SU(3)_C$ and $SU(2)_L$ gauge fields directly from the bare Tier~A ternary QCA.
