\begin{abstract}
We specify, formalize, and audit a deterministic, reversible ternary quantum cellular automaton (QCA) on the fixed,
infinite four-dimensional hyperbolic honeycomb of regular 24-cells, $\{3,4,3,4\}$. Each 24-cell (a dock) holds 24
signed ternary tones $\tau \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$ along the $D_4$ roots. One palindromic eight-layer unitary beat,
\[
U = S \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}} \circ C_{1/2} \circ M_{\mathrm{meet}} \circ M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18}) \circ K \circ C_{1/2} \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}},
\]
evolves exact amplitudes in $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ with no floating-point arithmetic and no randomness. States
evolve in the four-dimensional bulk, while three-dimensional observables are read on the flat cubic horosphere
$\{4,3,4\}$ surrounding an ideal cusp. Formal Lean~4 proofs (141 headline theorems across nine modules) and exact
integer audits establish $\{3,4,3,4\}$ uniqueness among regular 4D honeycombs, exact $O^+(4,1; \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}])$
transport and $\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{CP}, \mathcal{CPT}$ symmetry, order-576 $W(F_4)^+$ covariance, $D_4$ fourth-order
isotropy, an unconditional global spectral bound $\rho_{\mathrm{spec}} \le 13/21$, exact 1D Yang--Baxter integrability
broken in 4D by $M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$, two zero-external-gate universality constructions purely inside the
bare beat (reaching bare-analyzer Bell--CHSH $2.6412$ and $2.8262$), and a 4D $s$-wave bound state isotropic through
$O(K^4)$. On prescribed warped or drifting backgrounds, the same geometry supports a shared limiting speed, Newtonian
orbits, strictly 1-hop-causal spatially varying horizon crossing with $U(1,1)$ transfer-matrix Hawking tunneling, a
strictly finite-range $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/2]$ chiral domain wall, cold $\mathbb{Z}_3$ confinement, and horosphere de
Sitter drift, alongside a 24-cell Combes--Thomas lattice Yang--Mills mass gap extending $10.1\times$ ($SU(2)$) and
$22.8\times$ ($SU(3)$) past Koteck\'y--Preiss. Every result is separated into three explicit tiers (bare rule,
prescribed background, or lattice/continuum EFT comparison), and sharp no-go theorems bound the open gaps: intrinsic
bare depth warping, the floor-0 speed ceiling, dynamical gauge fields and nonlinear gravity, zero-parameter flavor and
$\alpha(0)$, the cosmological constant, and the continuum Yang--Mills limit.
\end{abstract}
