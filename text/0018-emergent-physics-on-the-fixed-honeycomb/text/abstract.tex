\begin{abstract}
We specify, machine-check, and audit a deterministic, reversible ternary quantum cellular automaton (QCA) on the fixed,
infinite four-dimensional hyperbolic honeycomb of regular 24-cells, $\{3,4,3,4\}$. Each 24-cell (a dock) holds 24
signed ternary tones $\tau \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$ along the $D_4$ roots. One palindromic eight-layer unitary beat,
\[
U = S \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}} \circ C_{1/2} \circ M_{\mathrm{meet}} \circ M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18}) \circ K \circ C_{1/2} \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}},
\]
evolves exact amplitudes in $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ with no floating-point arithmetic and no randomness. The state
evolves in the four-dimensional bulk, and three-dimensional observables are read on the flat cubic horosphere
$\{4,3,4\}$ around an ideal cusp. Lean~4 proofs and exact integer audits establish local gate unitarity, exact
$\mathcal{T}$, $\mathcal{CP}$, and $\mathcal{CPT}$ symmetry, order-576 $W(F_4)^+$ covariance, the algebraic hypotheses
for $SU(2)$ gate density, envariance prerequisites, $D_4$ fourth-order isotropy, isotropic 3D Dirac kinematics, and an
unconditional spectral bound $\rho_{\mathrm{spec}} \le 13/21$. On prescribed warped or drifting backgrounds, the same
geometry supports a shared limiting speed, Newtonian orbits, Gullstrand--Painlev\'e light-cone tipping, a unitary
chiral domain wall, cold $\mathbb{Z}_3$ confinement, and horosphere de Sitter drift. Each result carries one of three
labels: bare rule, prescribed background, or continuum comparison. The open problems are dynamical gauge fields and
nonlinear gravity from the bare rule, a $46\times$ gap between bare and beam speeds, the cosmological constant, and the
continuum Yang--Mills limit.
\end{abstract}
