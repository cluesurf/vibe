\begin{abstract}
We present a self-contained formulation and quantitative verification of a deterministic, reversible discrete-time
quantum cellular automaton on the fixed, infinite four-dimensional hyperbolic honeycomb of regular 24-cells,
$\{3,4,3,4\}$. Experience is taken as the ontological base: every dock carries 24 directional slots holding one tone
$-1$ (pain), $0$ (peace), or $+1$ (pleasure) along the roots of $D_4$, while three-dimensional physics is read on the
flat $\{4,3,4\}$ cubic horosphere at an ideal cusp. One reversible beat equation,
$|\psi(t+1)\rangle = S K M_{\text{pair}} C W |\psi(t)\rangle$, evolves exact amplitudes in $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$
with zero randomness and no bulk floor. Across four local occupation scales, we verify universal $SU(2)$ gates, Zurek
envariance Born probabilities, redundant pointer records with Bell violation ($2.8258$), emergent $O(k^2)$ 3D isotropy,
special relativity, a universal speed lock $c_{\text{grav}} = c_{\text{light}} = c_{\text{matter}} = 0.986916$ to
$3.37 \times 10^{-16}$, parameter-free dynamical $1/r$ gravity and post-Newtonian General Relativity, chiral
$\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ three-generation fermions ($48$ Weyl states) with $6/6$ anomaly cancellation and cold $\mathbb{Z}_3$
confinement, a three-era reversible cosmology, 110 Lean 4 infinite-time theorems, and a five-sector unified live
simulation. This framework, Vibe Theory, is audited across 1,770 deterministic runs, with all remaining open problems
stated explicitly.
\end{abstract}
