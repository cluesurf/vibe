\begin{abstract}
We present, machine-verify, and audit the mathematical architecture of a deterministic, reversible ternary quantum
cellular automaton (QCA) on the fixed, infinite four-dimensional hyperbolic honeycomb of regular 24-cells,
$\{3,4,3,4\}$, establishing its core algebraic and quantum-mechanical properties and charting preliminary directions
toward relativistic kinematics, warped gravity, chiral gauge models, and cosmology. Evolution runs in the
four-dimensional hyperbolic bulk, where each 24-cell dock holds 24 signed ternary tones $\tau \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$ along
the $D_4$ roots, while three-dimensional observables project onto the flat cubic husk $\{4,3,4\}$ around an ideal cusp.
One palindromic eight-layer unitary beat composed from six distinct local operators,
\[
U = S \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}} \circ C_{1/2} \circ M_{\mathrm{meet}} \circ M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18}) \circ K \circ C_{1/2} \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}},
\]
evolves exact cyclotomic amplitudes in $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ with zero floats and zero randomness
(\universecode). Machine-checked Lean~4 proofs (for the local algebraic building blocks and discrete geometric
invariants) and deterministic exact \code{BigInt} audits establish local gate unitarity, exact discrete $\mathcal{T}$,
$\mathcal{CP}$, and $\mathcal{CPT}$ symmetries, full order-576 $W(F_4)^+$ covariance, the algebraic 2-adic $SU(2)$
gate-density hypotheses, bipartite algebraic envariance prerequisites, isotropic 3D Dirac kinematics, $D_4$ fourth-order
isotropy, and the zero-straddling-triangle geometric premise for 4D site-reflection positivity. Separately, L1
multi-qubit circuit benchmarks verify pointer decoherence and non-signaling Bell violation under synthesized ring-word
analyzers alongside a registered open-line dynamical CHSH stall. As preliminary exploratory directions, warped or
prescribed-background cusp beams and downstream lattice/EFT checks illustrate shared-beam speed locking, Newtonian
and post-Newtonian orbits, bijective constant-river Gullstrand--Painlev\'e tangent-space radial walks, unitary cusp
chirality, cold $\mathbb{Z}_3$ confinement, fixed-mesh horosphere drift, and certified discrete mass-gap bounds. Core
open problems remain for follow-up work, including dynamical non-Abelian gauge fields and nonlinear gravity from the
bare rule, the bare-to-beam speed gap, cosmological-constant stabilization, and the continuum Yang--Mills limit.
\end{abstract}
