\begin{abstract}
We formulate, machine-verify, and audit a deterministic, reversible ternary quantum cellular automaton (QCA) on the
fixed, infinite four-dimensional hyperbolic honeycomb of regular 24-cells, $\{3,4,3,4\}$, testing how far one exact
discrete rule reaches toward quantum mechanics, relativity, gravity, chiral gauge structure, and cosmology. Evolution
runs in the four-dimensional hyperbolic bulk, where each 24-cell dock holds 24 signed ternary tones
$\tau \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$ along the $D_4$ roots, while three-dimensional observables project onto the flat cubic husk
$\{4,3,4\}$ around an ideal cusp. One factored unitary beat,
$U = S \circ K \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}} \circ M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18}) \circ D \circ M_{\mathrm{meet}} \circ D$,
evolves exact cyclotomic amplitudes in $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ with zero floats and zero randomness
(\universecode). Machine-checked Lean~4 proofs and deterministic audits establish exact unitarity, 2-adic $SU(2)$ gate
density, algebraic envariance prerequisites, non-signaling Bell violation, isotropic 3D Dirac kinematics, and $D_4$
fourth-order isotropy and site-reflection positivity. On warped or prescribed-background cusp columns, the framework
further supports universal speed locking, Newtonian and post-Newtonian orbits, bijective Gullstrand--Painlev\'e
trapped-horizon walks, unitary cusp chirality, cold $\mathbb{Z}_3$ confinement, fixed-mesh horosphere expansion, and
certified discrete mass-gap bounds. Core open problems remain, including dynamical non-Abelian gauge fields and
nonlinear gravity from the bare rule, the bare-to-column speed gap, cosmological-constant stabilization, and the
continuum Yang--Mills limit.
\end{abstract}
