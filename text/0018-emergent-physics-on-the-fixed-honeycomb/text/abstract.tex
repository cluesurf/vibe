\begin{abstract}
Vibe Theory models the universe as one fixed, infinite four-dimensional hyperbolic honeycomb, $\{3,4,3,4\}$, whose
every site (dock) carries 24 ternary values updated by one deterministic, reversible rule with exact amplitudes in
$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$. Experience is taken as the base, with trits $-1$ (pain), $0$ (peace), and $+1$ (pleasure),
while three-dimensional physics is read on the flat $\{4,3,4\}$ cubic horosphere at a cusp of the honeycomb. This paper
reports the advances achieved since \emph{The Vibe Theory Model} (Paper 0017), organized across four occupation scales
(0, 1, 2, and 4 vibes per dock) with zero extra dimensions, zero added depth register, and zero reflecting floor.
First, in quantum foundations, an elementary 2-adic parity proof establishes exact infinite order and $SU(2)$ density
of the two-mode gate group (\code{E-QTM-0204}, Hong--Ou--Mandel visibility $0.9999996$), Zurek envariance derives exact
$(3/4, 1/4)$ Born probabilities from one beat on the $R = 2$ honeycomb ball (\code{E-QTM-0205}), and rule-grown
redundant pointer records ($R_{0.01} = 4$) yield record-branch CHSH $2.8258$ against a classical control of $1.4219$
with no-signaling drift $1.67 \times 10^{-16}$ (\code{E-QTM-0206}). Second, an interaction-only $W(F_4)$-isotropic
pair-line mixer $M_{\text{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ breaks the bare line law without disturbing the peace vacuum $V_1$ or lone
vibes (\code{E-SLF-0199}), producing $O(k^{2.00})$ 3D isotropy (\code{E-RLT-0123}), emergent special relativity with
$0.024\%$ time-dilation accuracy (\code{E-RLT-0124}), and a universal speed lock
$c_{\text{grav}} = c_{\text{light}} = c_{\text{matter}} = 0.9869161538$ to $3.37 \times 10^{-16}$ on the shared
warped cusp column (\code{E-RLT-0125}), eighteen times tighter than GW170817 with Kaluza--Klein gap $m_{\text{KK}} =
3.2725$. Third, two-vibe pair density on the cusp shells ($N_D = 1, 18, 330, 6{,}034$) fixes the column warp
$w(D) = 1/N_D^2 = \ell^{-6}$ with zero free parameters (\code{E-GRV-0156}), giving unscreened $1/r$ slope $-1.0029$,
geometric coupling $G_{\text{eff}} = 0.07716700$ (\code{E-GRV-0157}), and live unitary two-body gravitational infall
(slope $-1.9957$, $G_{\text{dyn}} = 0.077513$), bound Keplerian orbits ($\omega$ error $0.071\%$), and $18.5^\circ$
flyby deflection (\code{E-GRV-0158}). Fourth, a $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ unitary cusp domain wall localizes pure
Left-handed Weyl fermions by 29 orders of magnitude (\code{E-SPN-0222}), quantum $\mathbb{Z}_3$-center tunneling in
$\Sigma(648)$ confines linearly at $T = 0$ ($R^2 = 0.999999999999999$) with exact baryon triality (\code{E-FRC-0322}),
and $D_4$ triality yields three chiral generations ($W_{\text{total}} = 3$, zero fourth light generation), exponential
warp hierarchies ($m_3/m_1 > 27{,}000$), and unitary CKM mixing with Jarlskog phase $J_{\text{CP}} = +9.48 \times 10^{-5}$

reproduces a full three-era cosmic history from radiation ($w = +0.326$) through matter ($w = 0.000$) and turnaround
($\Omega_m/\Omega_\Lambda = 2.000$) to de Sitter ($w = -1.000$) (\code{E-CSM-0064}, \code{E-CSM-0065}). Finally, 110
self-contained Lean 4 theorems (\code{VibeTheorems.lean}, zero \code{sorry}, zero \code{axiom}, \code{E-FND-0185})
prove six infinite-time laws for all $t \in \mathbb{N}$ and all floors $F \in \mathbb{N}$, and a live coupled 4D
unitary simulation ($\dim = 259{,}200$, \code{E-FND-0186}) runs gravity, chirality, $\mathbb{Z}_3$ gauge flux,
$M_{\text{pair}} + \text{ODD}$, and quantum interference together inside one reversible state vector. Every numerical
claim cites its exact deterministic experiment in the 1,768-run suite, and every open limitation is stated explicitly.
