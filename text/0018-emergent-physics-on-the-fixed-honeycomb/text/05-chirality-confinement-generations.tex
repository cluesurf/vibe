\section{Chirality, Confinement, and Gauge EFT Checks}
\label{sec:gauge}

Unlike a periodic cubic lattice, the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ cusp boundary is one-sided: every dock on the $D = 0$ husk connects
to 18 bulk neighbors below it and to none above. That geometry, paired with the three orthogonal 16-cell banks of each
dock and the $\mathbb{Z}_3$ center $\omega = \zeta_9^3$ of the amplitude ring, supplies discrete structures matching
three standard requirements of lattice particle physics: chiral boundary modes under the Nielsen--Ninomiya constraint
\cite{nielsen1981}, zero-temperature color triality and linear confinement, and three-fold flavor multiplicity.

The first three subsections evaluate these mechanisms on simplified Tier~B lattice models (\code{E-SPN-0221},
\code{E-SPN-0222}, \code{E-FRC-0322}, \code{E-FRC-0323}), upgraded with a strictly finite-range pure-\code{BigInt}
$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/2]$ 3+1D chiral domain wall (replacing \code{E-SPN-0222}'s transcendental Bessel tails) and a
sharp audit of symmetric mass generation (SMG) via bare $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ versus the 14-term $D_4$ Cayley 4-form.
The final subsection audits Tier~C continuum $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ EFT compatibility (\coderange{E-FRC-0324}{0332}),
paired with a zero-continuous-parameter look-elsewhere audit of Koide/flavor ans\"atze and an exact-$\mathcal{CP}$
Nelson--Barr alternative to the DFSZ axion tensions. Dynamical continuous gauge bosons from the bare rule remain open.

\subsection{Domain-wall chirality and symmetric mass generation}

Following Kaplan and Shamir \cite{kaplan1992, shamir1993}, a one-sided boundary can localize a massless chiral mode on
the surface while gapping bulk excitations. Experiment \code{E-SPN-0221} (Tier~B, L1) first verifies a Hermitian domain
wall on a flat 1D depth-24 chain ($m_0 = 0.5$), trapping a Left-handed mode at $D \le 2$ (weight $63/64 = 0.9844$
versus Right $2.09 \times 10^{-13}$), with 1D Weyl dispersion residual $5.61 \times 10^{-15}$ and the $W = 2$ doubler
gapped at $0.5200$.

The Hermitian wall is then replaced by a unitary one. To place a discrete-time split-step quantum walk
\cite{asboth2013} in the ring of the automaton ($C$, $C_{1/2}$ with $C_{1/2}^2 = C$, and $\zeta_{18} = -\zeta_9^5$), \code{E-SPN-0222} (Tier~B, L2) builds
a flat 1D depth-24 momentum-parameterized walk (a Tier~B domain-wall analog, not the bare $\{3,4,3,4\}$ QCA) with
Wilson-projector angles $\alpha_1(\mathbf{k}) = \pi/3 - (2\pi/9) P(\mathbf{k})$ and
$\alpha_2(\mathbf{k}) = (2\pi/3) P(\mathbf{k})$, where $P(\mathbf{k}) = \prod_{j=1}^3 (1 + \cos k_j)/2$:
\begin{equation}
U(\mathbf{k}) = \bigl(U_1(\mathbf{k})\, U_2(\mathbf{k})\, U_1(\mathbf{k}) \otimes I_2\bigr)\, S_{\mathrm{husk}}(\mathbf{k}).
\label{eq:unitary-wall}
\end{equation}
Here $U_1(\mathbf{k})$ applies the intra-layer rotation $\widetilde{C}_1(\mathbf{k}) = R_x(2\alpha_1(\mathbf{k}))$ on
$(|D, L\rangle, |D, R\rangle)$ for each $D \in \{0, \dots, 23\}$, $U_2(\mathbf{k})$ applies the inter-layer rotation
$\widetilde{C}_2(\mathbf{k}) = R_x(2\alpha_2(\mathbf{k}))$ on $(|D, R\rangle, |D+1, L\rangle)$ for $D \in \{0, \dots, 22\}$ (leaving
$|0, L\rangle$ and $|23, R\rangle$ unpaired), and $S_{\mathrm{husk}}(\mathbf{k}) = \operatorname{diag}(V_W(\mathbf{k}),
V_W(\mathbf{k})^\dagger)$ is a hand-inserted 3D Strang-split Pauli shift
$V_W(\mathbf{k}) = e^{-i k_x \sigma_x/2} e^{-i k_y \sigma_y/2} e^{-i k_z \sigma_z} e^{-i k_y \sigma_y/2} e^{-i k_x \sigma_x/2}$
acting on the two-component Weyl spinor. At the 8 high-symmetry TRIM points $\mathbf{k} \in \{0, \pi\}^3$, $V_W(\mathbf{k})
= \pm I_2$ and the depth coins reduce strictly into $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{k} = \mathbf{0}:&\quad \widetilde{C}_1 = R_x(2\pi/9), \quad \widetilde{C}_2 = R_x(4\pi/3) = \omega^2 C^\dagger,\\
\mathbf{k} \in \{0,\pi\}^3 \setminus \{\mathbf{0}\}:&\quad \widetilde{C}_1 = R_x(2\pi/3) = (-\omega) C, \quad \widetilde{C}_2 = I.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:wall-coins}
\end{equation}
At generic $\mathbf{k}$ away from the 8 TRIM points, however, nesting $\cos(\alpha_1(\mathbf{k}))$ produces
infinite-range Bessel hopping tails $J_n(\pi/9) \notin \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_9)$ ($|c_1| = 0.1105$ down to $|c_6| = 2.99
\times 10^{-8}$). Replacing the momentum-space angle with strictly 1-hop real-space shift conjugations of the exact
equatorial $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/2]$ compound coin $K_{\mathrm{small}} = R_{\pi/9}(2\pi/9) R_x(-4\pi/9)
R_{\pi/9}(2\pi/9)$ (with elementary inter-layer bond $V_2 = R_{5\pi/9}(2\pi/9)$) removes all transcendental tails
identically across the entire 3D Brillouin zone: the depth operator $V_1(\mathbf{k})$ has exact \code{BigInt}
Laurent-polynomial unitarity and chiral symmetry ($0$ residual over 27 real-space offsets, $\max L_\infty = 1$,
denominators strictly $2^p$), zero spurious $\nu_\pi$ violations and zero disconnected $\nu_0$ pockets across a
$24^3 = 13{,}824$-point 3D BZ scan, an exact \code{BigInt} Left zero mode at $\Gamma$
($\mathrm{Left}(D \le 2) = 1.000000$, $\mathrm{Right}(D \le 2) = 0$), all 7 doublers strictly gapped (min 0-gap
$0.5496$, min $\pi$-gap $0.1166$), and the accidental husk zero at $(2\pi/3, \pi/2, 0)$ lifted to $1.5014\text{ rad}$.

Restricting the 3D Weyl husk operator strictly inside $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_9)$ (without adjoining $i = \sqrt{-1}$) uncovers
an exact number-theoretic \textbf{$\mathfrak{p}_3$-adic chirality-velocity obstruction}. In the maximal real subfield
$K^+ = \mathbb{Q}(2\cos(2\pi/9))$, the prime $(3) = \mathfrak{p}_3^3$ is totally ramified with odd valuation
$v_{\mathfrak{p}_3}(3) = 3$. Because traceless Hermitian $2 \times 2$ matrices over $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_9) =
K^+(\sqrt{-3})$ carry the determinant quadratic form $\langle 1, 1, 3 \rangle$ (anisotropic over $\mathbb{F}_3$,
whereas $\langle c, c, c \rangle$ is isotropic over $\mathbb{F}_3$), no finite-range 2-component QCA strictly over
$\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_9)$ can achieve cubic-isotropic velocities $v_x = v_y = v_z$. The minimal pure-$\mathbb{Z}[\omega][1/2]$
triad $(A_x, A_y, A_z) = (\sigma_x, C^\dagger \sigma_z C - C\sigma_z C^\dagger = \sqrt{3}\,\sigma_y, \sigma_z)$ uses
12 real-space offsets with zero $i$, yields exact metric-isotropic velocities $(1, \sqrt{3}, 1)$ ($1.22\%$ cone spread
at $|\mathbf{p}| = 0.05$ under $y_{\mathrm{phys}} = y/\sqrt{3}$), and requires adjoining $i$ to $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_{36}][1/2]$
for cubic $(1, 1, 1)$ isotropy.

On any finite $N = 24$ slab, Nielsen--Ninomiya \cite{nielsen1981} places an exact Right-handed mirror on the far wall
$D = 23$ (weight $0.999914$ on $D \ge 21$ in \code{E-SPN-0222}, $1.000000$ in the finite-range wall, net chirality $0$,
Figure~\ref{fig:domain-wall}). Removing the mirror on a finite slab without breaking chiral symmetry requires symmetric
mass generation (SMG \cite{wang2022smg}). An exact audit of the bare 4-body operator $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ yields two
sharp no-go theorems: (i) $\chi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ vanishes identically on every 8-slot inscribed 16-cell ($0/1{,}120$
4-tuples), and (ii) diagonal $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ and number-conserving single-dock $U_{\mathrm{dock}}$ cannot lift
single-particle states into a unique gapped symmetric vacuum. Achieving SMG requires extending the bare diagonal phase
by the minimal 14-term pure 4-fermion $D_4$ octonionic Cayley 4-form (6 diagonal plus 8 off-diagonal 4-fermion terms,
zero bilinears). Exact diagonalization on 8 Majorana ($\dim = 16$) and 16 Majorana ($\dim = 256$) mirror banks
confirms a unique $\mathrm{Spin}(7)$-invariant ground state (degeneracy $1$, residual $4.7 \times 10^{-16}$),
single-particle mirror gap $\Delta E_{1p} = 14 g$, exact zero condensate ($0.00$ across all 28 and 120 bilinears), and
an exact Floquet SMG gate $U_{\mathrm{SMG}} = P_0 + \zeta_9 P_1 + \zeta_9^2 P_2 \in \mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/2]$.
Lifting this 0+1D mirror-gapping mechanism to a full interacting 3+1D chiral boundary lattice remains open.

\subsection{Three-bank multiplicity}

Each dock has three banks (Section~\ref{sec:one-rule}), and one wall per bank gives three copies of the chiral mode.
Experiment \code{E-FRC-0323} (Tier~B) forms the block-diagonal product $I_3 \otimes U(\mathbf{k})$ across the three
mutually orthogonal 16-cell banks, cycled by the inner coordinate 3-cycle $T_3 \in S_4 < W(D_4)$ via
$S_4 / V_4 \cong S_3$ (distinct from outer $D_4$ triality $\mathrm{Out}(D_4) \cong W(F_4)/W(D_4)$) and commuting with
the $\mathbb{Z}_3$ center $\omega = \zeta_9^3$ of $\Sigma(648)$. Total winding is $\nu_{\mathrm{total}} = 3$ with no
fourth mode. The count of three comes from the three banks put in as input. Nothing in the dynamics selects against a
fourth generation. Part~2 is a qualitative toy hierarchy using hand-assigned
shell anchors $[3, 2, 0]$, UV coin steps $m_{\mathrm{UV}} = 2$ (for $Q_L, d_R$) and $1$ (for $u_R$), off-diagonal coupling
$1/\sqrt{2}$, $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$, and discrete $\zeta_9$ phase matrices. It yields a unitary Cabibbo--Kobayashi--Maskawa
(CKM) matrix and nonzero Jarlskog $\mathcal{CP}$ invariant \cite{jarlskog1985}, while missing Particle Data Group (PDG)
$m_b/m_d \approx 895$ by $31\times$ ($27{,}598$) and $J_{\mathrm{CP}} = 3.08 \times 10^{-5}$ by $3.1\times$
($+9.48 \times 10^{-5}$). Its $1.000$ degenerate control sets off-diagonal mixing $K_{\mathrm{mix}} = I_3$.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{$\mathbb{Z}_3$}{Z3} ladder confinement}

The ring's cube root of unity $\omega$ is the center of $\Sigma(648)$, and it supplies a $\mathbb{Z}_3$ gauge group,
the smallest with color triality. Classical link updates freeze at $T = 0$, because zero kinetic energy blocks
tunneling out of $U_p = 1$, so the test must be quantum. Experiment \code{E-FRC-0322} (Tier~B) evaluates the exact zero-temperature quantum Kogut--Susskind
Hamiltonian \cite{kogut1975} and discrete-time Floquet unitary ($\Delta t = 4\pi/9$ at $g^2 = 1$) for the $\mathbb{Z}_3$
center $\{1, \omega, \omega^2\}$ of $\Sigma(648)$ ($EU = \omega UE$) on a quasi-(1+1)D open 6-plaquette ladder (14
vertices, 19 links, gauge-invariant $\dim = 729$):
\begin{equation}
H = H_E + H_B = g^2 \sum_{\ell} \bigl(1 - \operatorname{Re} E_\ell\bigr) - \frac{1}{g^2} \sum_p \operatorname{Re} U_p,
\qquad
U_{\Delta t} = e^{-i\Delta t H_E} e^{-i\Delta t H_B}.
\label{eq:ks-z3}
\end{equation}
Because a 1-plaquette-wide ladder confines linearly for all $g^2 > 0$ by 1D flux conservation (unlike 3+1D $\mathbb{Z}_3$
gauge theory, which has a finite-coupling deconfinement transition), \code{E-FRC-0322} is a quasi-(1+1)D
exact-diagonalization benchmark. Across quark separations $r_{q\bar{q}} = 1, \dots, 5$, it verifies linear confinement,
exact $\mathbb{Z}_3$ baryon triality (three like charges bind while two like charges have empty physical Hilbert space
and triality-neutral $q \equiv 0 \bmod 3$ pairs have zero string tension), and exact $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ Floquet
tunneling (survival $1/729 = 0.00137$), with exact Gauss-law invariance holding by construction of the gauge-invariant
loop basis. Table~\ref{tab:gauge-b} collects all Tier~B chirality, generation, and confinement metrics.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{Spin}(10)$}{Spin(10)} EFT, cyclotomic flavor bounds, and Nelson--Barr \texorpdfstring{$\mathcal{CP}$}{CP}}

Nine \textbf{Tier~C} continuum EFT studies (\coderange{E-FRC-0324}{0332}) test whether standard $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$
\cite{georgi1975, fritzsch1975} (often written $SO(10)$ in particle literature) and Pati--Salam \cite{pati1974} grand
unified theory (GUT), electroweak (EW), Yukawa, and quantum chromodynamics (QCD) Strong-$\mathcal{CP}$ EFTs are
compatible with invariants read off from $\{3,4,3,4\}$ (the three 8-slot banks, the $F_4$ root ratio $\sqrt{2}$, shell
ratio $b = 6{,}034/330$, and $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_9)$ phases). None of these continuum EFTs are derived from the bare
automaton.

\paragraph{Anomaly structure, Koide bounds, and zero-parameter flavor audit.}
In exact \code{BigInt}, \code{E-FRC-0324} verifies textbook $\mathrm{Cl}(10)$ representation theory replicated across 3
copies ($3 \times 16 = 48$ Weyl states, not derived from 24-cell operators), checking $6/6$ perturbative gauge and
Witten anomaly cancellations, $\sin^2\theta_W|_{\mathrm{GUT}} = 3/8$, and an $S_3$ toy free energy selecting staggered
shells $(0, 2, 3)$ for tuned parameters $g_{\mathrm{Pauli}} = 2.5$ and $g_{\mathrm{cusp}} = 40$. Under the quadratic
mass ansatz $M = M_0^2$ with $3 \times 3$ Hermitian circulant
$M_0(r, \delta) = v_0\bigl(I_3 + \frac{r}{2}(e^{i\delta} T_3 + e^{-i\delta} T_3^\dagger)\bigr)$ and any positive
diagonal warp $D_w = \operatorname{diag}(w_0, w_1, w_2)$, exact algebra (\code{E-FRC-0325}, \code{E-FRC-0327}) proves
Koide's relation \cite{koide1983}, the warped-shell lower bound, and the cyclotomic phase obstruction:
\begin{equation}
Q_{\mathrm{Koide}}(M_0^2) = \frac{2 + r^2}{6} = \frac{2}{3} \iff r^2 = 2,
\qquad
Q_{\mathrm{Koide}}\bigl((D_w M_0 D_w)^2\bigr) \ge \frac{2}{3},
\qquad
e^{i 2/9} \notin \mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6].
\label{eq:koide-identities}
\end{equation}
A dedicated zero-continuous-parameter cyclotomic audit sharpens this obstruction: neither $m = 9$ nor $m = 18$ has any
non-degenerate root inside the Brannen positivity window $[0, \pi/12]$ (minimum phase error $57.1\%$), even

$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ fails exact Lebesgue look-elsewhere control ($p = 0.0385\text{--}0.7697$). Likewise,
zero-float $\{3,4,3,4\}$ shell textures capture order-of-magnitude quark mass decades ($m_c/m_u = 474.2$ vs $487.4$,
$m_b/m_s = 57.6$ vs $53.7$) and TBM-perturbed PMNS angles ($3.2\%\text{--}10.4\%$) only when hyperbolic shell factors
$N_D$ are included (pure $1/6$-power textures fail catastrophically), while missing precision PDG flavor data (matrix
CKM $J$ is $11.3\times$ small, PMNS $\delta_{\mathrm{CP}} = 93.7^\circ$ sits in the wrong quadrant). Moreover,
\code{E-FRC-0327}'s standalone texture uses $M_{\mathrm{PS}} = 1.44 \times 10^{16}\,\mathrm{GeV}$, in five-decade
tension with \code{E-FRC-0328} (Tension~5 in Section~\ref{sec:open}).

\paragraph{Strong-$\mathcal{CP}$: DFSZ axion tensions versus exact-$\mathcal{CP}$ Nelson--Barr.}
