\section{Lean~4 Proofs, Gauge Bounds, and Coupled Runs}
\label{sec:unified}

Because direct numerical runs probe only finite balls, finite beams, and bounded beat counts, three structural
properties require all-time or infinite-volume verification: which algebraic and geometric laws hold for every
$t \in \mathbb{N}$ and every depth $D \in \mathbb{N}$, how far rigorous non-perturbative mass-gap bounds extend on the
honeycomb and flat 4D $D_4$, and whether all sectors coexist reversibly inside one quantum state. Experiments
\coderange{E-FND-0185}{0190}, paired with the expanded Lean~4.35.0 suite and the 24-cell Combes--Thomas lattice bound,
address these in turn.

The zero-dependency Lean~4.35.0 suite now spans nine proof modules and 141 headline theorems (160 public theorems
total), adding formalizations of Coxeter's 4D honeycomb classification and $\{3,4,3,4\}$ uniqueness, exact
$O^+(4,1; \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}])$ transport with $S R S = R$ and palindromic $\mathcal{T}/\mathcal{CP}/\mathcal{CPT}$,
and the global Busemann Schur bound $\rho_{\mathrm{spec}}(\{3,4,3,4\}) \le 13/21$ (\code{E-FND-0185},
\code{E-FND-0190}). On flat 4D $D_4$ triangular plaquettes, combining Koteck\'y--Preiss cluster bounds
(\code{E-FND-0189}) with a 24-cell vertex-corner Combes--Thomas Witten-Laplacian bound proves an explicit
infinite-volume Osterwalder--Schrader mass gap across $0 < \beta < N^2/(16\sqrt{7})$, extending $10.12\times$
($SU(2)$) and $22.79\times$ ($SU(3)$) past Koteck\'y--Preiss and $2\times$ weaker in bare continuum coupling $g_0^2$
than $\mathbb{Z}^4$, while coupled runs keep every sector intact in one reversible state (\code{E-FND-0186},
\code{E-FND-0187}). The flat-$\mathbb{R}^4$ continuum Yang--Mills limit \cite{jaffe2000ym} remains open.

\subsection{Constructive lattice gauge and mass-gap bounds}

Three \textbf{Tier~C} studies (\coderange{E-FND-0188}{0190}) together with an explicit 24-cell Combes--Thomas
Witten-Laplacian theorem establish constructive non-perturbative lattice gauge and spectral bounds on $\{3,4,3,4\}$
and flat 4D $D_4$ \cite{wilson1974, osterwalder1978, seiler1982, kotecky1986, callan1990, tomboulis1983, bakry1985,
helffer1994, combes1973, bodineau1999}. Section~1 of \code{E-FND-0190} (now formalized in \code{Vibe/SchurGap.lean})
proves an exact spectral-geometry bound on the infinite $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb itself, whereas the remaining bounds
apply to 4D lattice Yang--Mills theory on flat $D_4$.

In \code{E-FND-0188}, exact real-symmetric $\mathbb{Z}_3$ center-gauge transfer matrices on $D_4$ keep a strictly
positive mass gap across $\beta \in [0.1, 8.0]$ ($\min m_{\mathrm{gap}} = 1.313$ for $\dim = 3, 9, 27$), 2-loop pure
Yang--Mills asymptotic scaling holds to $2.0 \times 10^{-15}$, and free $D_4$ dispersion suppresses rotational
anisotropy to $O(k^4)$ versus $\mathbb{Z}^4$ $O(k^2)$. Part~2 runs eight frontier engines live, auditing 3 structural
zero-fit checks, 10 fitted or continuum-GR consistency checks, and the 5 cross-sector EFT tensions of
Section~\ref{sec:open}.

In \code{E-FND-0189}, exact \code{BigInt} enumeration of flat 4D $D_4$ confirms 8 equilateral triangles per link
($d_p = 21$, $\Delta_p = 22$, minimal 3-simplex polymer $n_{\min} = 4$) and zero straddling triangles across $x_0 = 0$
over all 2,592 slab translates ($1{,}196 \leftrightarrow 1{,}196$ paired half-space triangles plus $200$ in-plane
triangles $\mathcal{S}_0$). That partition gives exact infinite-volume Osterwalder--Seiler site-hyperplane reflection
positivity $\mathcal{S} = \mathcal{S}_+ + \mathcal{S}_0 + \Theta \mathcal{S}_+$ for all $\beta \ge 0$. Applying the
multi-plaquette Koteck\'y--Preiss (KP) criterion \cite{kotecky1986} to the full 27-irrep $SU(3)$ character activity
$\mathcal{M}(\beta) = e^\beta / c_0(\beta) - 1$ certifies infinite-volume convergence and a strong-coupling gap
$m_{\mathrm{KP}}(\beta) \in [0.154, 2.237]$ ($\beta \in [0.001, 0.008]$):
\begin{gather}

\qquad

alongside exact 2D $a \to 0$ Migdal--Witten $O(a^2)$ convergence and gauge-covariant polar-projected $SU(3)$ APE link

\item \textbf{Bakry--\'Emery Ricci curvature and 24-cell Combes--Thomas OS mass gap:} In the bi-invariant metric

\cite{helffer1994, bodineau1999} with Combes--Thomas link tilting \cite{combes1973} exploits two geometric features
specific to 4D $D_4$ triangles: every pair of edges sharing a $D_4$ triangle has midpoint separation

\begin{equation}
\Delta_{\mathrm{OS}}(\beta) \ge 2\operatorname{arccosh}\bigl(c^*(\beta)\bigr)
\ge 2\operatorname{arccosh}\!\left(\frac{\sqrt{\frac{N^4}{4\beta^2} - 7} - 13}{8}\right) > 0,
\label{eq:combes-thomas-gap}
\end{equation}
where $c^*(\beta) > 1$ solves $16 c^3 + 52 c^2 + 40 c + 4 = (N^4 / 4\beta^2) c$. This certifies
$\beta < 0.094491$ for $SU(2)$ (\textbf{$10.12\times$} past $\beta_{\mathrm{OS}}^{\mathrm{KP}} = 0.009337$) and
$\beta < 0.212605$ for $SU(3)$ (\textbf{$22.79\times$} past $\beta_{\mathrm{OS}}^{\mathrm{KP}} = 0.009328$), with
independent TypeScript and Python verification (12-digit agreement, 4th-order finite-difference Hessian checks
$\le 5 \times 10^{-10}$, full $L = 4$ $D_4$ torus check). Because exact continuum matching gives
$\beta_{\mathbb{Z}^4} = 2\beta_{D_4}$ ($\beta_{D_4} = N/g_0^2$), $D_4$ reaches $2\times$ weaker bare continuum
coupling $g_0^2$ than $\mathbb{Z}^4$ ($g_0^2 > 16\sqrt{7}/N$ vs $(24 + 8\sqrt{57})/N$), while still breaking down at
plaquette angle $\theta = \pi$ past $\beta_{\mathrm{BE}}^{\mathrm{24c}}$ (leaving the $a \to 0$ continuum crossover
open).
\end{enumerate}
