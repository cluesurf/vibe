\paragraph{Why this matters in practice.}
Two classic objections face any computational theory of physics: finite simulations cannot prove what happens after
billions of steps, and separate sector models might destroy each other when combined. Formalizing 110 zero-sorry
theorems in Lean 4 addresses the first concern by locking down stationarity, unitarity, gate density, gravity
unscreening, and speed universality for all beats $t \in \mathbb{N}$ and all depths $F \in \mathbb{N}$. Running
gravity, chirality, gauge flux, pair scattering, and quantum interference together inside one live state vector
addresses the second by showing that all five sectors coexist cleanly, with each physical signature vanishing only when
its own mechanism is switched off.
