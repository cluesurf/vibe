\section{Master Index of Cited Experiments}
\label{sec:index}

Every experiment code cited in this paper is indexed below as registered in \code{test/registry.csv}
\cite{cluesurf2026repo} (total suite size: 1,790 deterministic runs, zero pseudo-randomness). Failed and partial gates
are preserved and labeled explicitly. Each entry records its canonical tier (A: bare microscopic QCA, exact
$\{3,4,3,4\}$ geometry, and exact algebraic invariants, B: warped-beam, cusp-projection, or prescribed-background
lattice walks plus L1 quantum and gauge-lattice benchmarks, C: constructive flat 4D $D_4$/$\{3,4,3,4\}$ lattice
gauge-theory bounds and downstream continuum EFT compatibility checks) and substrate depth grade (L1 or L2, with no L3
claim made). Three heavy sweep scripts (\code{E-SLF-0189}, \code{E-SLF-0190}, \code{E-SLF-0195}) default to fast local
smoke mode unless invoked with their full-run environment flag, and the verdicts below record their registered full runs.
