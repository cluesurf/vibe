\section*{Introduction}

We analyze a deterministic, reversible quantum cellular automaton (QCA) defined on the fixed, infinite tiling of
four-dimensional hyperbolic space by regular 24-cells, the honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$
\cite{coxeter1956, coxeter1973, margenstern2007vol1}. Each 24-cell carries 24 signed ternary states
$\tau \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$ evolved by a single local unitary beat over the cyclotomic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$,
with three-dimensional observables read on the flat cubic horosphere $\{4,3,4\}$ surrounding an ideal cusp. The paper
serves as a self-contained mathematical specification, a Lean~4.35.0 proof audit, and a deterministic computational
baseline \cite{cluesurf2026repo}. Every geometric object, ring, operator, symmetry, and observable is defined on first
use so the entire construction can be checked and re-implemented directly from the text.

Throughout, we maintain a strict boundary between theorems of the bare automaton, behaviors obtained on prescribed
warped or drifting backgrounds, and compatibility checks against standard lattice gauge theory and continuum effective
field theories (EFTs). Alongside each positive construction, we record the exact no-go theorems and negative controls
that delimit where the bare rule stops.

\subsection*{Construction overview}

The construction has four stages. The first is a single regular 24-cell, which we call a \emph{dock}. It has 24 octahedral facets, one doorway for each of the 24 roots of the $D_4$ root system
\cite{conway1999}. Each doorway is a \emph{slot}, and each slot carries one signed ternary value
$\tau \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$: an excitation of either sign, or nothing. Opposite doorways pair into 12 straight
\emph{lines}, and the lines group into 3 \emph{banks} of 4 mutually perpendicular lines each.

Second, docks are glued face to face to fill the space. The result is the honeycomb $\{3,4,3,4\}$, a curved
four-dimensional space in which the number of docks within distance $r$ grows roughly eighteenfold with each step.
Third, one ideal point at infinity, a \emph{cusp}, is fixed. Relative to it, the honeycomb splits into a stack of flat
three-dimensional floors, each an
ordinary cubic grid $\{4,3,4\}$. The outermost floor is the \emph{husk}, where three-dimensional observables are read.
Behind each husk dock lies an exponentially widening \emph{beam} of docks at depths $D = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$, the
four-dimensional \emph{bulk}.

Fourth, time advances in discrete \emph{beats}. Each beat applies one palindromic eight-layer unitary operator built from six
local operators,
\begin{equation}
U = S \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}} \circ C_{1/2} \circ M_{\mathrm{meet}} \circ M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18}) \circ K \circ C_{1/2} \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}},
\label{eq:intro-beat}
\end{equation}
read right to left. The local layers act inside each dock according to how many of its slots are occupied, and the
final layer $S$ streams every excitation through its doorway into the neighboring dock. All matrix entries lie in the
cyclotomic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$. The rule has no continuous angle, no floating-point operation, no added
depth register, and no reflecting wall in the bulk. A self-contained reference script (\universecode) in the companion
repository \cite{cluesurf2026repo} implements the infinite honeycomb in exact integer arithmetic over
$O^+(4,1; \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}])$ together with all eight layers of Equation~\eqref{eq:intro-beat}.

\subsection*{Related work}

The construction draws on four lines of research in mathematical physics and computer science. In quantum
information theory, Meyer \cite{meyer1996} formulated Dirac quantum lattice gases and proved the scalar 1D QCA no-go
theorem, Schumacher and Werner \cite{schumacher2004} axiomatized reversible quantum cellular automata and strict causal
propagation, Arrighi \cite{arrighi2019} and Farrelly \cite{farrelly2020} surveyed QCA dynamics, and
Bia\l ynicki-Birula \cite{bialynicki1994}, Strauch \cite{strauch2006}, and Asb\'oth and Obuse \cite{asboth2013} showed
how unitary discrete-time lattice walks reproduce Weyl, Dirac, Maxwell, and topological boundary-mode kinematics.
't~Hooft's cellular automaton interpretation \cite{thooft2016} restricts microscopic evolution to deterministic
permutations of a classical basis. The automaton here instead evolves complex superpositions with exact cyclotomic
amplitudes, and it does so on a curved 4D honeycomb in place of a flat lattice.

On the geometric side, Coxeter \cite{coxeter1956, coxeter1973} classified regular hyperbolic honeycombs including
$\{3,4,3,4\}$ in $\mathbb{H}^4$, Conway and Sloane \cite{conway1999} systematized the $D_4$ root lattice and 24-cell,
and Margenstern \cite{margenstern2007vol1} analyzed computation and cellular automata on curved tessellations. Modern
discrete holography uses negatively curved tilings to model bulk-to-boundary maps, notably in the holographic
error-correcting codes of Pastawski, Yoshida, Harlow, and Preskill \cite{pastawski2015happy}, while Randall and Sundrum
\cite{randall1999} showed how warped higher-dimensional geometry localizes gravity onto a lower-dimensional slice. Here
the flat $\{4,3,4\}$ ideal cusp horosphere of $\{3,4,3,4\}$ furnishes a canonical 3D Euclidean slice backed by an
exponentially branching 4D bulk beam.

In lattice field theory and statistical mechanics, the 24-vector 4D face-centered hypercubic ($D_4$) lattice (similar in
4D to the dual body-centered hypercubic lattice $D_4^*$ up to a $45^\circ$ rotation and $\sqrt{2}$ scaling) is known
to eliminate leading $O(a^2)$ rotational anisotropy, both in the lattice-gas hydrodynamics of Frisch et al.
\cite{frisch1987fchc} and in Celmaster's lattice gauge formulation \cite{celmaster1982}. Our non-perturbative mass-gap
audits draw directly on Wilson's lattice gauge action \cite{wilson1974}, the Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian
\cite{kogut1975}, Osterwalder--Seiler reflection positivity \cite{osterwalder1978, seiler1982}, Koteck\'y--Preiss
cluster expansions \cite{kotecky1986}, Callan--Wilczek negative-curvature infrared gapping \cite{callan1990}, Tomboulis
character-expansion decimation \cite{tomboulis1983}, and Bakry--\'Emery Ricci curvature bounds \cite{bakry1985}, while
maintaining a sharp boundary between discrete lattice theorems and the continuum Clay Millennium Yang--Mills problem
\cite{jaffe2000ym}.

Finally, machine-checked proof engineering in Lean~4 \cite{demoura2021lean4} makes it practical to verify infinite-time
and infinite-depth algebraic laws directly inside a small trusted kernel rather than relying solely on finite-grid
simulation.

\subsection*{Verification tiers}

Because the later sections on gravity, matter, and cosmology introduce external structure beyond the microscopic beat,
every claim and deterministic run is assigned one of three tiers (Table~\ref{tab:tiers}, Figure~\ref{fig:tiers-map}).
\textbf{Tier~A} covers exact properties of the bare automaton and the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb with nothing added.
\textbf{Tier~B} evaluates the same geometry on a prescribed background, such as an imposed depth warp, a fixed radial
flow, or a simplified domain-wall or ladder lattice. \textbf{Tier~C} tests standard lattice gauge theory and continuum
effective field theories (EFTs) for compatibility with geometric and algebraic invariants read off from $\{3,4,3,4\}$.
Tier~C results are \textbf{not derived from the bare automaton}. Section~\ref{sec:open} catalogs the six open gaps
separating these tiers together with the no-go theorems that sharpen them.

Every quantitative claim cites either a registered identifier in \code{test/registry.csv} \cite{cluesurf2026repo}
(1,790 deterministic runs) or a standalone deterministic verification script in the companion repository. No script uses
pseudo-randomness, and each pairs pre-registered pass/fail gates with negative controls (line-confined, unwarped bulk,
dephased classical, frozen $g^2 = 0$, and sector-off ablations). The bare reference kernel (\universecode), exact
Coxeter frame builders, Lean-mirrored certificates (\code{E-FND-0185}), exact small-horizon QCA runs
(\code{E-QTM-0203}, \code{E-QTM-0205}, \code{E-FND-0181}, \code{E-FND-0183}, \code{E-FND-0184}), the infinite-honeycomb
radial engine (\code{E-GRV-0163}), and the exact cyclotomic domain-wall, 1-hop horizon, and Nelson--Barr verifiers run
in arbitrary-precision \code{BigInt} arithmetic, while larger long-time, spectral, multi-qubit, and continuum checks run
in IEEE-754 \code{Float64} with explicit unitarity and residual gates.

A second grade records substrate depth. \textbf{L1} denotes pure algebra or a finite-register benchmark, \textbf{L2}
denotes an exact sector or prescribed-background evolution on true $\{3,4,3,4\}$ balls, infinite Coxeter traversals, or
cusp beams, and \textbf{L3} denotes unfactored many-body dynamics across large 4D regions. Every run reported here is
L1 or L2, and no L3 claim is made. The numerical audits are paired with a zero-dependency Lean~4.35.0 formal proof suite
of 141 headline theorems (160 public theorems including 19 exported helpers, plus 170 private helpers) across nine proof
modules and the root aggregator \code{Vibe.lean}, with 0~\code{sorry}, 0~\code{axiom}, and 0~\code{native\_decide}.
The six baseline modules (94 headline theorems) pass full Lean~4.35.0 kernel compilation and \code{\#print axioms}
verification, while the three newer modules (\code{SchurGap.lean}, \code{Uniqueness4D.lean}, and
\code{CoxeterTransport.lean}, 47 headline theorems) pass the deterministic structural audit (\code{check:lean
--no-build}) and independent exact Python arithmetic cross-checks pending host \code{lake build} execution. Failed
gates remain in the catalog and are reported beside passing ones.

\subsection*{Outline}

The paper builds from the local 24-cell junction to the full hyperbolic honeycomb, and then follows the dynamics across
single-particle, two-particle, gravitational, chiral, cosmological, and infinite-time regimes.

\textbf{Section~\ref{sec:one-rule}} defines the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb, its uniqueness among regular 4D hyperbolic
tilings, its flat $\{4,3,4\}$ cusp foliation (shells $N_D = 1, 18, 330, 6{,}034$, single-face lateral links
$l_D = 1, 4, 20, 100$), the amplitude ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$, and the palindromic eight-layer beat $U$
(Tier~A). It establishes two structural facts used throughout: the 3D husk is dynamically non-closed within one beat
because of the 4D bulk behind it, and $U$ conserves $\mathcal{T}$, $\mathcal{CP}$, $\mathcal{CPT}$, and order-576
$W(F_4)^+$ covariance while generating local $\mathcal{C}$-asymmetry through 4-body interference.

\textbf{Section~\ref{sec:quantum}} analyzes the single-line coin and two-body collision algebra (Tier~A and~B). Beyond
the 2-adic $SU(2)$ density hypotheses and bipartite envariance prerequisites, it classifies the exact 1D two-body
$S$-matrix (hitting two of the three Yang--Baxter integrable phases on $U(1)$ before 4D $M_{\mathrm{pair}}$ breaks
integrability) and constructs two zero-external-gate universality mechanisms purely inside bare $U$, yielding
bare-analyzer Bell--CHSH violations up to $2.6412$ and $2.8262$.

\textbf{Section~\ref{sec:relativity}} turns to multi-line kinematics and relativistic dispersion (Tier~A and~B). Pair
scattering breaks the single-excitation line law, producing an exact $SO(4)$-isotropic 4D $s$-wave bound state through
$O(K^4)$ on flat $D_4$ and $O(k^2)$-isotropic floor-0 Dirac kinematics at $c_{\mathrm{eff}} = 0.021267$. A sweep over
all 18 cyclotomic roots in $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9]$ establishes a hard floor-0 speed ceiling of $0.129492$ ($7.55\times$
below the Tier~B shared-beam speed $c_u = 0.977925$), while Cauchy--Schwarz fixes an irreducible $0.814\%$ speed
mismatch for site-local linear Dirac operators on a non-uniform beam.

\textbf{Section~\ref{sec:gravity}} examines bulk gravitational screening, the prescribed depth warp $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$,
and horizon walks (Tier~B and~C). Exact $O^+(4,1)$ vertex-transitivity proves that bare $U$ alone forces constant depth
weighting, establishing that $w(D) = 1/N_D^2$ requires both a symmetry-breaking cusp-anchored flux background and
2-body pair coupling. On the warped beam, the static potential recovers $1/r$ gravity ($G_{\mathrm{eff}} = 0.078593$)
and Newtonian orbits. For horizons, a 2-band Gross--Nesme--Vogts--Werner (GNVW) no-go lemma leads to a minimal
4-component strictly 1-hop-causal spatially varying horizon-crossing QCA (verified in pure \code{BigInt}
$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/2]$) exhibiting exponential peeling and $U(1,1)$ transfer-matrix Hawking tunneling, alongside
continuum general-relativity checks.

\textbf{Section~\ref{sec:gauge}} treats lattice chirality, confinement, flavor textures, and Strong-$\mathcal{CP}$
structure (Tier~B and~C). Replacing transcendental momentum-space coins yields a strictly finite-range pure-\code{BigInt}
$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/2]$ 3+1D chiral domain wall accompanied by a $\mathfrak{p}_3$-adic obstruction forbidding exact
cubic $(1,1,1)$ velocities inside $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_9)$, while mirror gapping requires extending bare diagonal
$\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ by the 14-term $D_4$ Cayley 4-form. A cold $\mathbb{Z}_3$ ladder demonstrates linear
confinement, zero-parameter look-elsewhere audits bound cyclotomic Koide and flavor ans\"atze, and a pure-$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9]$
Nelson--Barr mass matrix offers a Strong-$\mathcal{CP}$ alternative to the DFSZ axion tensions.

\textbf{Section~\ref{sec:cosmology}} evaluates inward horosphere drift on the fixed mesh, where expanding floor volume
reads on the husk as de Sitter expansion with zero site creation (Tier~B), leaving a 61-decade bare Hubble gap
(122 decades in $\Lambda$) alongside continuum cosmology benchmarks (Tier~C).

\textbf{Section~\ref{sec:unified}} collects all-time and infinite-volume results: the 9-module, 141-headline-theorem
Lean~4.35.0 suite, the unconditional $\{3,4,3,4\}$ Busemann Schur bound $\rho_{\mathrm{spec}} \le 13/21$, a 24-cell

\textbf{Section~\ref{sec:open}} summarizes the six open frontiers, the five active cross-sector EFT tensions, and the
