\section*{Scope and Methodology}
\label{sec:scope}

This paper documents the post-0017 state of the Vibe Theory research program \cite{pollard2026model, cluesurf2026repo}.
In \emph{The Vibe Theory Model} (Paper 0017), every working experiment ran on a flat periodic $D_4$ stand-in box whose
behavior matched the $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb only for one dock over one beat, matter remained trapped on
one-dimensional lines under the working rule, gravity required an added depth register, the Born rule and pointer
records remained posited rather than derived, no chiral weak sector reached the cusp, no cosmic expansion carrier
existed on the fixed mesh, and finite honeycomb balls hit a 4-beat boundary horizon.

The work reported here replaces those stand-ins and open barriers with exact cusp geometry, parameter-free operators in
the algebraic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$, infinite-time formal proofs, and a single coupled live simulation across
five physical sectors.

\subsection*{Verification discipline}

Every quantitative claim in this paper is tied to a deterministic experiment identifier in the repository catalog
\code{test/registry.csv}, which now records 1,768 experiments (up from 1,474 in Paper 0017). The methodology enforces
four strict rules across every run.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Zero pseudo-randomness.} No experiment calls \code{Math.random} or any stochastic sampler. Initial states,
sector probes, and control sweeps are constructed by exact algebraic enumeration or deterministic sequences.
\item \textbf{Exact cyclotomic arithmetic.} Core local gates and symbolic proof certificates execute over exact integer
rings ($\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$, and $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9]$ with denominators dividing powers of $6$) using
arbitrary-precision integers, eliminating rounding ambiguity from algebraic identities.
\item \textbf{Pre-registered gates and mandatory negative controls.} Every experiment specifies numerical pass criteria
before execution and runs explicit negative controls (bare line-law controls, unwarped flat-bulk controls, dephased
classical controls, frozen $g^2 = 0$ gauge controls, and sector-by-sector ablations).
\item \textbf{Honest accounting of failures and open limits.} Negative results remain in the catalog and are cited
directly alongside positive theorems. In particular, open-line CHSH saturation without periodic wrap fails its
convergence gate (\code{E-QTM-0202}), equal-$\chi_{\text{odd}}$ superpositions produce zero occupation-level
$C$-asymmetry (\code{E-FND-0183}), unconditional lone-vibe frame mixers drain bound line selves on the exact honeycomb
(\code{E-SLF-0189}, \code{E-SLF-0198}), the unweighted cusp Laplacian remains gapped (\code{E-GMT-0044}), and precision
PDG mass ratios and nonlinear Einstein backreaction remain open (\code{E-FRC-0323}, Section~\ref{sec:open}).
\end{enumerate}
