\section*{Introduction and Methodology}
\label{sec:scope}

How far can a single deterministic, reversible discrete-time quantum cellular automaton (QCA) on a fixed
four-dimensional hyperbolic tessellation reach toward reproducing the mathematical structures of quantum mechanics,
relativistic kinematics, gravitational dynamics, chiral gauge theory, and cosmology, and where do the exact boundaries
lie between microscopic derivations and downstream effective models?

This paper provides a self-contained mathematical definition, Lean~4.35.0 formal proof audit, and quantitative
experimental report of a concrete ternary QCA on the infinite 4D $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb of regular 24-cells
\cite{cluesurf2026repo}. Every geometric object, cyclotomic ring, local operator, symmetry symbol, and observable is
defined from first principles on first use.

\subsection*{Overview of the automaton}

The state space resides on a fixed, infinite four-dimensional hyperbolic tessellation of regular 24-cells, denoted
$\{3,4,3,4\}$ in Schl\"afli notation \cite{coxeter1973, margenstern2007vol1}. Every 24-cell (a \emph{dock}) holds 24
directional \emph{slots} aligned with the 24 roots of the $D_4$ root system, partitioned canonically into 12 antipodal
\emph{lines} and 3 mutually orthogonal 8-slot \emph{banks}. Each slot carries one signed ternary state value
$\tau \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$ on the mesh, representing occupied signed excitations ($\pm 1$) and the neutral state ($0$).

The microscopic state evolves reversibly inside the full four-dimensional hyperbolic \emph{bulk} of $\{3,4,3,4\}$, while
three-dimensional spatial physics is read as a coarse-grained projection onto the flat Euclidean cubic \emph{husk}
$\{4,3,4\}$ surrounding an ideal vertex (cusp) on the boundary at infinity. Evolution proceeds in discrete ticks
(\emph{beats}) under one factored reversible unitary operator,
\begin{equation}
U = S \circ K \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}} \circ M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18}) \circ D \circ M_{\mathrm{meet}} \circ D,
\label{eq:intro-beat}
\end{equation}
whose matrix entries lie strictly in the cyclotomic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$. The rule has zero continuous angle
parameters, zero floating-point operations, zero added depth register, and zero reflecting bulk boundary. A
self-contained, zero-import, pure-\code{BigInt} reference script (\universecode) in the companion repository
\cite{cluesurf2026repo} implements the infinite $\{3,4,3,4\}$ Coxeter transport and all seven microscopic layers of
Equation~\eqref{eq:intro-beat}.

\subsection*{Related work}

This construction connects four established lines of research in mathematical physics and computer science. In quantum
information theory, Schumacher and Werner \cite{schumacher2004} axiomatized reversible quantum cellular automata and
strict causal propagation, later surveyed by Arrighi \cite{arrighi2019} and Farrelly \cite{farrelly2020}, while
Bialynicki-Birula \cite{bialynicki1994}, Strauch \cite{strauch2006}, and Asb\'oth and Obuse \cite{asboth2013} showed
how unitary discrete-time lattice walks reproduce Weyl, Dirac, Maxwell, and topological boundary-mode kinematics. Our
automaton extends that lineage by formulating a multi-scale interacting ternary rule on a curved 4D hyperbolic 24-cell
honeycomb with exact amplitudes in $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ rather than floating-point flat-lattice coins.

On the geometric side, Coxeter \cite{coxeter1973} classified regular hyperbolic honeycombs and Margenstern
\cite{margenstern2007vol1} analyzed computation and cellular automata on curved tessellations. Modern discrete
holography uses negatively curved tilings to model bulk-to-boundary maps, notably in the holographic error-correcting
codes of Pastawski, Yoshida, Harlow, and Preskill \cite{pastawski2015happy}, while Randall and Sundrum
\cite{randall1999} showed how warped higher-dimensional geometry localizes gravity onto a lower-dimensional slice. Here
the flat $\{4,3,4\}$ ideal cusp horosphere of $\{3,4,3,4\}$ furnishes a canonical 3D Euclidean slice backed by an
exponentially branching 4D bulk column.

In lattice field theory and statistical mechanics, the 24-vector 4D face-centered hypercubic ($D_4$) lattice is known
to eliminate leading $O(a^2)$ rotational anisotropy, both in the lattice-gas hydrodynamics of Frisch et al.
\cite{frisch1987fchc} and in Celmaster's lattice gauge formulation \cite{celmaster1982}. Our non-perturbative mass-gap
audits draw directly on Wilson's lattice gauge action \cite{wilson1974}, the Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian
\cite{kogut1975}, Osterwalder--Seiler reflection positivity \cite{osterwalder1978}, Koteck\'y--Preiss cluster
expansions \cite{kotecky1986}, Callan--Wilczek negative-curvature infrared gapping \cite{callan1990}, Tomboulis
character-expansion decimation \cite{tomboulis1983}, and Bakry--\'Emery Ricci curvature bounds \cite{bakry1985}, while
maintaining a sharp boundary between discrete lattice theorems and the continuum Clay Millennium Yang--Mills problem
\cite{jaffe2000ym}.

Finally, recent machine-checked proof engineering in Lean~4 \cite{demoura2021lean4, trinh2024alphageometry} makes it
practical to verify infinite-time and infinite-depth algebraic laws directly inside a small trusted kernel rather than
relying solely on finite-grid simulation.
