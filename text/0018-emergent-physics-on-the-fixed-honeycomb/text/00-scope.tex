\section*{Introduction and Methodology}

Can a single deterministic, reversible discrete-time quantum cellular automaton (QCA) on a fixed four-dimensional
hyperbolic tessellation provide a clean, exact mathematical architecture for modeling quantum mechanics and relativistic
kinematics without floating-point arithmetic, and what preliminary directions and open boundaries emerge when that
geometry is interfaced with gravity, chiral gauge theory, and cosmology?

This paper serves as a self-contained mathematical specification, Lean~4.35.0 formal proof audit, and deterministic
experimental baseline for a concrete ternary QCA on the infinite 4D $\{3,4,3,4\}$ honeycomb of regular 24-cells
\cite{cluesurf2026repo}. Our goal is to lock down the exact algebraic and geometric foundations without gaps, report
initial exploratory studies cleanly separated by verification tier, and isolate the open problems for follow-up papers.
Every geometric object, cyclotomic ring, local operator, symmetry symbol, and observable is defined from first
principles on first use.

\subsection*{Overview of the automaton}

The state space resides on a fixed, infinite four-dimensional hyperbolic tessellation of regular 24-cells, denoted
$\{3,4,3,4\}$ in Schl\"afli notation \cite{coxeter1956, coxeter1973, margenstern2007vol1}. Every 24-cell (a \emph{dock})
holds 24 directional \emph{slots} aligned with the 24 roots of the $D_4$ root system \cite{conway1999}, partitioned
canonically into 12 antipodal \emph{lines} and 3 disjoint 8-slot \emph{banks} (three inscribed 16-cells). Each slot
carries one signed ternary state value $\tau \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$ on the mesh, representing occupied signed excitations
($\pm 1$) and the neutral state ($0$).

The microscopic state evolves reversibly inside the full four-dimensional hyperbolic \emph{bulk} of $\{3,4,3,4\}$, while
three-dimensional spatial physics is read as a coarse-grained projection onto the flat Euclidean cubic \emph{husk}
$\{4,3,4\}$ surrounding an ideal vertex (cusp) on the boundary at infinity. Evolution proceeds in discrete ticks
(\emph{beats}) under one palindromic eight-layer reversible unitary operator composed from six distinct local operators,
\begin{equation}
U = S \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}} \circ C_{1/2} \circ M_{\mathrm{meet}} \circ M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18}) \circ K \circ C_{1/2} \circ \Phi_{\mathrm{odd}},
\label{eq:intro-beat}
\end{equation}
whose matrix entries lie strictly in the cyclotomic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$. The rule has zero continuous angle
parameters, zero floating-point operations, zero added depth register, and zero reflecting bulk boundary. A
self-contained, zero-import, pure-\code{BigInt} reference script (\universecode) in the companion repository
\cite{cluesurf2026repo} implements the infinite $\{3,4,3,4\}$ Coxeter transport in $O^+(4,1; \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}])$ and
all eight palindromic layers of Equation~\eqref{eq:intro-beat}.

\subsection*{Related work}

This construction connects four established lines of research in mathematical physics and computer science. In quantum
information theory, Meyer \cite{meyer1996} formulated Dirac quantum lattice gases and proved the scalar 1D QCA no-go
theorem, Schumacher and Werner \cite{schumacher2004} axiomatized reversible quantum cellular automata and strict causal
propagation, Arrighi \cite{arrighi2019} and Farrelly \cite{farrelly2020} surveyed QCA dynamics, and
Bia\l ynicki-Birula \cite{bialynicki1994}, Strauch \cite{strauch2006}, and Asb\'oth and Obuse \cite{asboth2013} showed
how unitary discrete-time lattice walks reproduce Weyl, Dirac, Maxwell, and topological boundary-mode kinematics.
Whereas 't~Hooft's cellular automaton interpretation \cite{thooft2016} restricts microscopic evolution to deterministic
permutations of a classical ontological basis, the automaton here evolves complex cyclotomic superpositions in
$\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$ on a curved 4D hyperbolic 24-cell honeycomb rather than floating-point flat-lattice coins.

On the geometric side, Coxeter \cite{coxeter1956, coxeter1973} classified regular hyperbolic honeycombs including
$\{3,4,3,4\}$ in $\mathbb{H}^4$, Conway and Sloane \cite{conway1999} systematized the $D_4$ root lattice and 24-cell,
and Margenstern \cite{margenstern2007vol1} analyzed computation and cellular automata on curved tessellations. Modern
discrete holography uses negatively curved tilings to model bulk-to-boundary maps, notably in the holographic
error-correcting codes of Pastawski, Yoshida, Harlow, and Preskill \cite{pastawski2015happy}, while Randall and Sundrum
\cite{randall1999} showed how warped higher-dimensional geometry localizes gravity onto a lower-dimensional slice. Here
the flat $\{4,3,4\}$ ideal cusp horosphere of $\{3,4,3,4\}$ furnishes a canonical 3D Euclidean slice backed by an
exponentially branching 4D bulk beam.

In lattice field theory and statistical mechanics, the 24-vector 4D face-centered hypercubic ($D_4$) lattice (similar in
4D to the dual body-centered hypercubic lattice $D_4^*$ up to a $45^\circ$ rotation and $\sqrt{2}$ scaling) is known
to eliminate leading $O(a^2)$ rotational anisotropy, both in the lattice-gas hydrodynamics of Frisch et al.
\cite{frisch1987fchc} and in Celmaster's lattice gauge formulation \cite{celmaster1982}. Our non-perturbative mass-gap
audits draw directly on Wilson's lattice gauge action \cite{wilson1974}, the Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian
\cite{kogut1975}, Osterwalder--Seiler reflection positivity \cite{osterwalder1978, seiler1982}, Koteck\'y--Preiss
cluster expansions \cite{kotecky1986}, Callan--Wilczek negative-curvature infrared gapping \cite{callan1990}, Tomboulis
character-expansion decimation \cite{tomboulis1983}, and Bakry--\'Emery Ricci curvature bounds \cite{bakry1985}, while
maintaining a sharp boundary between discrete lattice theorems and the continuum Clay Millennium Yang--Mills problem
\cite{jaffe2000ym}.

Finally, machine-checked proof engineering in Lean~4 \cite{demoura2021lean4} makes it practical to verify infinite-time
and infinite-depth algebraic laws directly inside a small trusted kernel rather than relying solely on finite-grid
simulation.

\subsection*{Three-tier taxonomy and verification discipline}

A disciplined mathematical-physics audit must never blur microscopic QCA theorems with prescribed-background quantum
walks or continuum effective field theory (EFT) calculations. Every claim and registered run in this paper is assigned
to one of three strict tiers summarized in Table~\ref{tab:tiers} and mapped against the six open boundaries in
Figure~\ref{fig:tiers-map}. Tier~C studies test structural compatibility with $\{3,4,3,4\}$ invariants and surface
cross-sector tensions, and are \textbf{explicitly not derived from the bare microscopic automaton}.

Every quantitative claim cites a deterministic experiment identifier in \code{test/registry.csv}
\cite{cluesurf2026repo} (1,790 registered runs). All scripts run deterministically with zero pseudo-randomness and pair
pre-registered pass/fail gates with mandatory negative controls (line-confined, unwarped bulk, dephased classical,
frozen $g^2 = 0$, and sector-off ablations). The bare reference kernel (\universecode), exact Coxeter frame builders,
Lean-mirrored certificates (\code{E-FND-0185}), exact small-horizon QCA runs (\code{E-QTM-0203}, \code{E-QTM-0205},
\code{E-FND-0181}, \code{E-FND-0183}, \code{E-FND-0184}), and the infinite-honeycomb radial engine (\code{E-GRV-0163})
execute in arbitrary-precision \code{BigInt} cyclotomic arithmetic, while larger long-time, spectral, multi-qubit, and
continuum benchmark runs (\code{E-QTM-0202}, \code{E-QTM-0206}, \code{E-QTM-0207}, \code{E-HLG-0043},
\code{E-FND-0182}, \code{E-RLT-0125}, and downstream EFT runs) execute in IEEE-754 \code{Float64} with explicit
unitarity and norm-drift gates.

Each run is also graded by substrate depth: \textbf{L1} denotes pure algebra or finite-qubit benchmarks, \textbf{L2}
denotes exact sector or prescribed-background runs on true $\{3,4,3,4\}$ balls, infinite Coxeter traversals, or cusp
beams, and \textbf{L3} denotes unfactored many-body dynamics across large 4D honeycomb regions. Every computational
run here sits at L1 or L2 (no L3 claim is made), paired with 94 substantive Lean~4.35.0 kernel-verified theorems (113
public theorems including 19 exported helper lemmas) across six proof modules plus the root aggregator
\code{Vibe.lean} (0~\code{sorry}, 0~\code{axiom}, 0~\code{native\_decide}). Reproducing the full Lean audit locally
requires building the \code{.olean} artifacts with \code{pnpm run check:lean} under Lean~4.35.0. Failed gates remain
in the catalog and are reported directly alongside positive findings.

\subsection*{Roadmap and logical arc of the paper}

The eight sections form one connected progression from bare algebraic definition to machine-checked theorems,
constructive mass-gap bounds, and explicit open boundaries:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Section~\ref{sec:one-rule} (Automaton, geometry, and rule, Tier~A):} Defines the signed ternary mesh,
24-cell $D_4$ geometry, exact $\{3,4,3,4\}$ shells ($N_D = 1, 18, 330, 6{,}034$, single-face $l_D = 1, 4, 20, 100$),
polynomial ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$, the palindromic eight-layer beat operator $U$, 3D beam non-closure
($L_1 = 0.5000$, \code{E-HLG-0043}), exact global $\mathcal{CP}$, $\mathcal{T}$, and $\mathcal{CPT}$ symmetry with
coherent $\Phi_{\mathrm{odd}}$ $\mathcal{C}$-asymmetry (\universecode, \code{E-FND-0181}, \code{E-FND-0184}), and the
self-contained \universecode\ engine.
\item \textbf{Section~\ref{sec:quantum} (Quantum foundations, Tier~A/B):} Proves the algebraic 2-adic $SU(2)$
gate-density hypotheses and evaluates abstract two-mode beamsplitter balance plus an exact $6 \times 6$ path-and-role
splitter (\code{E-QTM-0204}), audits bipartite algebraic Zurek envariance prerequisites under an operational Born
measure (\code{E-QTM-0205}), tests L1 multi-qubit pointer decoherence and record-branch Bell correlations ($2.8258$,
\code{E-QTM-0206}, \code{E-QTM-0207}), and reports the open-line ballistic Bell-correlation stall (\code{E-QTM-0202}).
\item \textbf{Section~\ref{sec:relativity} (Kinematics and speed lock, Tier~A/B):} Shows how Scale-2
$M_{\mathrm{pair}}(\zeta_{18})$ breaks 1D rail confinement (\code{E-SLF-0199}) to yield $O(k^{2.00})$ 3D isotropy and

(\code{E-RLT-0123}, \code{E-RLT-0124}), and audits the Tier~B shared-beam speed lock $c_u = 0.977925$ (period-2
