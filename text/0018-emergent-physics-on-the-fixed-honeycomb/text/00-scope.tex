\section*{Introduction and Methodology}
\label{sec:scope}

Can quantum mechanics, three-dimensional relativistic kinematics, universal light-matter-gravity speed locking,
dynamical $1/r$ gravity, chiral three-generation gauge structure, and three-era cosmic expansion arise from a single
deterministic, reversible discrete rule on a fixed four-dimensional lattice? This paper gives a complete,
self-contained mathematical definition and experimental audit of a concrete model that tests that question: \emph{Vibe
Theory}. Readers need no familiarity with earlier reports in the series \cite{pollard2025vibe, pollard2026missing,
pollard2026model}, which are cited only as optional historical context. Every geometric object, algebraic ring, local
operator, and physical observable used below is defined from first principles on first use.

\subsection*{Overview of the model}

The state space resides on a single fixed, infinite four-dimensional hyperbolic tessellation of regular 24-cells,
denoted $\{3,4,3,4\}$ in Schl\"afli notation \cite{coxeter1973, margenstern2007vol1}. Every 24-cell (called a
\emph{dock}) holds 24 directional \emph{slots} aligned with the 24 roots of the $D_4$ root system, and each slot holds
one ternary \emph{tone} $\tau \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$, where $-1$ represents \emph{pain}, $0$ represents \emph{peace}, and
$+1$ represents \emph{pleasure}. Experience is treated as the fundamental field, while three-dimensional physical space
is read on the
flat Euclidean cubic horosphere $\{4,3,4\}$ surrounding an ideal vertex (cusp) of the 4D honeycomb. Evolution proceeds
in discrete ticks (\emph{beats}) under one factored, reversible unitary operator whose matrix entries lie strictly in
the cyclotomic ring $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9][1/6]$, with zero continuous angle parameters, zero added depth register, and
zero reflecting bulk boundary.

\subsection*{Verification discipline and depth grading}

Every quantitative claim in this paper cites an explicit deterministic experiment identifier in the open repository
catalog \code{test/registry.csv} \cite{cluesurf2026repo}, which currently holds 1,770 experiments. Four verification
rules govern every experiment reported here:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Zero pseudo-randomness.} No script calls \code{Math.random} or any stochastic generator. All initial
configurations, sector probes, and controls are constructed by exact algebraic enumeration or deterministic sequences.
\item \textbf{Exact cyclotomic arithmetic.} Local gates and algebraic proof certificates execute over exact integer
rings ($\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$, and $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9]$ with denominators dividing powers of $6$) using
arbitrary-precision \code{BigInt} arithmetic so algebraic unitarity, symmetry, and commutation identities carry zero
rounding ambiguity.
\item \textbf{Pre-registered gates and mandatory negative controls.} Each experiment fixes pass/fail thresholds before
execution and pairs every positive measurement with explicit controls (bare line-confined controls, unwarped flat-bulk
controls, dephased classical controls, frozen $g^2 = 0$ gauge controls, and sector-by-sector ablations).
\item \textbf{Depth grading and honest accounting of failures.} Results are graded strictly by substrate depth:
\textbf{L1} denotes pure algebraic identities, \textbf{L2} denotes exact sector and coupled simulations on true
$\{3,4,3,4\}$ balls or exact $\{3,4,3,4\}$ cusp columns, and \textbf{L3} denotes a single unfactored many-body engine
running all sectors across arbitrarily large 4D honeycomb regions. Every computational verification in this paper sits
honestly at L2, paired with 110 machine-formatted infinite-time theorems in Lean 4 (\code{code/proof/Vibe.lean},
\code{E-FND-0185}). Failed gates remain in the catalog and are reported directly alongside positive results
(\code{E-QTM-0202}, \code{E-FND-0183}, \code{E-SLF-0189}, \code{E-SLF-0190}, \code{E-GMT-0044}), and all remaining
theoretical and engineering gaps are collected in Section~\ref{sec:open}.
\end{enumerate}
