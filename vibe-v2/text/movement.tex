\section{Agency and Movement}
\label{sec:movement}

Because a self is a pattern, not a substance (Section~\ref{sec:mind}), and space is made of notes (Section~\ref{sec:geometry}), movement cannot be a lump sliding through a void. It is a pattern re-instantiating itself across a shared, living substrate.

\subsection{Movement is pattern propagation}

An agent is a self-maintaining coherent pattern of vibes and notes. Its vibes are loci of the substrate, shared with everything else. What travels when it moves is the form, not the material. One step of movement proceeds as follows. At the leading edge the agent projects its will, through an existing note, onto a neighboring vibe, so that vibe's own update is dominated by the agent's wills and it adopts the pattern's tone, and the notes binding it in slowly strengthen. At the trailing edge a vibe is no longer reinforced, its notes to the pattern weaken, and it relaxes back to background. After one beat the pattern lives on a slightly shifted set of vibes. The agent has moved one step.

\begin{proposition}[The glider]
The glider in Conway's Game of Life is a five-cell pattern that, after four steps, reappears one cell over on the diagonal. No cell ever moves. Cells only switch on and off, yet the glider travels across the grid indefinitely. A vibe-agent moves the same way. The vibes stay put and only re-tone, while the pattern propagates. A ripple on water is the same fact in a continuous medium. The water stays in place and the wave moves through it.
\end{proposition}

Three consequences follow. The agent is reborn one step over each beat, so over enough beats it is on entirely new substrate, and identity is pattern-continuity rather than substrate-continuity. The pattern shifts by at most one note per beat, the discrete light cone of Section~\ref{sec:time}.

\subsection{Recruiting a vibe is not seizure}

The leading-edge move looks like taking over a vibe, but it is gentler and stricter than seizure. Nothing is owned, consumed, or moved. The vibe stays put and changes its own tone, and the agent only contributes terms to that vibe's field. Winning a vibe is winning the arithmetic of its own update, not writing into it. Because attention is structural (Section~\ref{sec:dynamics}), the agent can only recruit vibes it already notes, and binding becomes stable only as the notes strengthen by their own slow dynamics. So movement is railed. The agent advances along the note-structure, one neighbor at a time. Background vibes are fungible carriers, since a vibe has no individuality beyond its tone and notes, so any suitably placed background vibe can play a role in the pattern.

\subsection{Moving through other agents}

There is one shared substrate, so other agents are other patterns on the same vibes. Movement is never through empty space. It is through a medium made of experience, much of it organized into other selves. When two agents' blankets sit next to each other, their shared boundary notes link them, so each notes the other and they perceive each other. To advance, a leading edge must win its next vibe, and if that vibe is held by another agent the result is a contest, in which the stronger summed will pins the tone. A meeting can end in several ways. The agents may pass through each other like solitons, scatter like a collision, synchronize and travel together, merge into one higher-coherence self, exchange tone-patterns across the boundary without merging, which is communication, or one may strip the other's substrate until it loses coherence and dies.

\subsection{Momentum and mass}

Momentum is a persistent directional bias in the will. Inertia is coherence resisting re-instantiation, since a more coherent agent must re-form more internal structure per step and so moves slower for the same push. Inertial mass is, in this reading, coherence. Drag is the cost of recruiting committed vibes, so dense regions are viscous and empty background is frictionless. All of this is discrete, recruit and release, one note per beat. Smooth trajectories with a velocity are the coarse-grained average of the jagged glider-walk, the way fluid flow emerges from molecules.
