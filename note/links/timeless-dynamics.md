# Vibe to Timeless Dynamics

Timeless Dynamics (James Lombardo) builds physics from one primitive, distinguishability
measured by the Fisher-Rao metric, and makes time emergent. This sheet lists what vibe
and Timeless Dynamics each derive on their own, where they line up, and where they part.
Our most complete bridge, 10 experiments.

## Shared invariants (the survivors)

The structures both theories **derive, not assume**, from different primitives.

- **Time = accumulated irreversible distinction.** Vibe: the wake, record count 1 to
  8857, arc length monotone (E-FND-0051). TD: accumulated Fisher-Rao arc length. Neither
  assumes time.
- **Records are the arrow.** Vibe: reversible knit recovers the start exactly, Hamming 0
  (E-FND-0049). TD: the recordability condition.
- **Fisher-Rao is forced.** Vibe: 24-cell relabeling singles it out, deviation 6.7e-16
  vs ad-hoc 0.99 (E-FND-0057). TD: Chentsov's theorem. Same metric, two starts.
- **Born rule from a conserved norm.** Vibe: norm concentration and envariance. TD:
  Gleason on the complexified Fisher-Rao.
- **A Lyapunov ceiling on record persistence.** Vibe: contrast 0.041 above, 0.241 below
  the exponent (E-QTM-0092). TD: the Hyperion chaos threshold. The freshest survivor,
  push here.

Five shared survivors, more than any other theory.

## Divergences

- **Continuous vs discrete.** Every map is an embedding or coarse-graining, never a
  bijection.
- **The sign.** Vibe's activity map drops the mirror. TD does not use the sign vibe
  treats as fundamental.
- **Holography.** Vibe reads its cusp as a screen. TD explicitly rejects holography. Same
  coordinate structure, opposite reading. A fork worth putting to him.
- **Gravity.** TD claims Newtonian gravity from the information potential. Vibe finds that
  potential screened in the bulk, so it can only live on the flat cusp.
