# Vibe to Chronoflux

Chronoflux (Roy Herbert, 38 papers) builds physics from one conserved current of time,
its only law being zero divergence. It is the continuous version of what vibe does
discretely, so this sheet derives Chronoflux from vibe rather than adopting it.

## Shared invariants (the survivors)

- **A single conserved local quantity is the whole ontology.** Vibe: charge conserved to
  the integer, 1112 pairs made and 1112 annihilated, net zero (E-FND-0008). Chronoflux:
  div J = 0. Same status, two grains. The anchor.
- **Time = a positive divergence of the conserved flow.** Vibe: the wake registers as a
  strictly positive edge divergence that equals the count of newly born cells exactly and
  is zero in the settled bulk (E-CSM-0043). Chronoflux: the expansion scalar is the
  global thinning of the river. The cleanest one-to-one in the set.
- **Records as recoverability.** Vibe: reversibility preserves records (E-FND-0049).
  Chronoflux: the recoverability functional, accessible over total, unifying entropy,
  decoherence, and horizons.
- **Gravity is a coarse-grained stress response.** Vibe: area-law gravity from the
  conserved substrate (E-GRV-0047). Chronoflux: curvature forced to match the river's
  stress.

## Divergences

- **Continuous vs discrete.** Chronoflux's fitted continuum constants (alpha_0, the
  scalar mass, the relaxation time) do not import.
- **A dissipative rate.** Chronoflux carries a Lindblad collapse rate as a coefficient.
  Vibe is fully reversible, so collapse emerges from the dynamics with no added rate.
- **Astrophysics and the wider claims.** Ringdown echoes, the Millennium Problems, and
  inertia engineering sit beyond what vibe models, so they are not imported.
- **The central unbuilt calculation.** The explicit coarse-graining from lattice
  conservation to div J = 0 for a named field. A well-posed conjecture, not yet a result.
