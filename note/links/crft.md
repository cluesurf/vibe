# Vibe to CRFT

CRFT (Brent Borgers, 69 papers) proposes that reality is information projected from a
higher-dimensional source down to the 3D world we see, at a fixed cost. Several of its
ideas line up with structures vibe has measured. This sheet maps those, and notes which
parts sit outside what vibe's method uses.

## Shared invariants (the survivors)

Structures both frameworks arrive at, each from its own starting point.

- **The area law: the boundary dominates the bulk.** Vibe: about 94 percent of cells sit
  in the outermost shell and the bulk radius grows as log of the cell count, against a
  flat volume-law control (E-HLG-0001, E-GRV-0003). CRFT: reality as a projection from
  higher dimensions. The projection idea is, in vibe, a measured property of the
  geometry.
- **A self is what persists under noise (selection as law).** Vibe: deterministic
  variation plus persistence-selection raises fitness while the no-selection control stays
  flat (E-SLF-0002). CRFT: the Law of Residue, apparent laws as what survives.
- **A conserved quantity creates the stable self.** Vibe: the bare rule churns and makes
  no stable self, a conserved quantity lets one settle into an attractor (E-SLF-0008).
  CRFT: hard constraints increase stability, from the AI-alignment side.

## What vibe does not import

Where the two methods differ.

- **The Borgers Impedance** (alpha times root two, the fixed cost). Vibe derives its
  scales from the {3,4,3,4} growth rate and adds no tuned constants, so a single fixed
  impedance does not fit vibe's method.
- **The named-constant set** (137, 17, the golden ratio, root two) and the E = I w^2
  framing. These are numeric correspondences, and vibe's rule against fitted constants
  keeps them out.
- **Qualia as an octonionic projection.** Vibe shares the octonion algebra (D4 to E8),
  but treats experience as an emergent layer to earn by measurement, so it does not adopt
  the qualia mechanism.
- **The dated transformation events.** These are forecasts outside what vibe models.
