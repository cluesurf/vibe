# Vibe to Orchestrated Objective Reduction

Orch-OR (Hameroff and Penrose) says consciousness comes from gravity collapsing a quantum
state inside neurons. Vibe reaches the same endpoint, a definite record that survives a
warm noisy environment, without any quantum coherence. So the two agree on the result and
disagree on the cause, which this sheet lays out. 6 experiments.

## Shared invariants (the survivors)

- **A definite record persists under noise.** Vibe: the definite outcome survives across
  a size sweep while coherence is destroyed (E-QTM-0049) and survives thermal noise
  (E-QTM-0050). Orch-OR: reduction yields one definite outcome. Vibe reaches it with no
  coherence bill.
- **The record holds its spatial form while a coherent field disperses.** Vibe:
  E-SLF-0162. Orch-OR: the reduced state is the stable classical trace.
- **Integration collapses under anesthetic.** Vibe: E-SLF-0161. Orch-OR: anesthetics
  break the process that binds experience.
- **A collapse law set by energy and time.** Vibe: E-QTM-0051. Orch-OR: the objective
  reduction time near h-bar over the gravitational self-energy.

## Divergences

- **The mechanism, the big one.** Orch-OR needs sustained quantum coherence collapsed by
  gravity. Vibe needs none: the record is classical and survives decoherence and thermal
  noise by not depending on coherence at all. Same destination, opposite bet on how you
  get there.
- **Gravitational self-collapse.** Vibe has no objective-reduction gravity term. Its
  definiteness comes from an arrow amplifying and a self holding, not from spacetime
  self-energy.
- **Microtubules.** No substrate-specific biology is imported. The claim is about records
  and selves in general, not one organelle.
