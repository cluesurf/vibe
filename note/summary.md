## 1. What exists

1. **Only experience exists**

   - Reality is made of experiencing entities.
   - These entities are called **vibes**.
   - There is no non-experiential substrate underneath.

2. **The totality of vibes is the mind**

   - “Mind” and “vibe” are synonyms at different scales.
   - The universe itself is a mind.

## 2. Fundamental elements

3. **Vibe**

   - A vibe is a fundamental unit of experience.
   - It has no internal structure.
   - It exists only by relating.

4. **Tone**

   - Each vibe has a tone.
   - Tone is the quality of experience.
   - Tones vary, but remain within a bounded set.

5. **Link**

   - A link is an experiential connection between vibes.
   - To be linked is to experience another vibe.

6. **Fill**

   - Fill is how a vibe experiences others.
   - It is receptive influence (pull).

7. **Will**

   - Will is how a vibe influences others.
   - It is projected influence (push).

## 3. Time and change

8. **Beat**

   - Reality advances in discrete steps called beats.
   - A beat is the cause of the next state.

9. **Flow**

   - Flow is the continuous sequence of beats.
   - Flow is time as experienced.

## 4. Rules

10. **Rules are structural necessities**

    - Rules are not chosen.
    - Rules are not external.
    - Rules are the minimal constraints required for experience to
      persist.

11. **Rules govern transformation**

    - They define how tones update.
    - They prevent collapse, explosion, or stasis.
    - They apply at all scales.

12. **Rules never change**

    - Structure changes.
    - Perspective changes.
    - Rules do not.

## 5. Interaction dynamics

13. **Every vibe updates by negotiation**

    - A vibe's next tone depends on:

      - its previous tone (inertia)
      - incoming fill (relation)
      - outgoing will (expression)

14. **No randomness**

    - All updates are deterministic.
    - Determinism is contextual, not isolated.

## 6. Emergence of structure

15. **Coherence**

    - When vibes strongly influence each other, they form clusters.
    - Coherent clusters behave as higher-level vibes.

16. **Nested minds**

    - Vibes compose into larger vibes.
    - Minds exist within minds.
    - Nesting is scale-based, not infinite abstraction.

## 7. Choice and agency

17. **Base vibes do not choose**

    - They respond according to rules.

18. **Choice emerges at scale**

    - In coherent clusters, influences compete.
    - Resolution takes time.

19. **Choice is delayed determinism**

    - Choice is not randomness.
    - Choice is not rule-breaking.
    - Choice is the felt resolution of competing influences.

20. **Free will is experiential, not fundamental**

    - It is real from the inside.
    - It is structured from the outside.

## 8. Space and separation

21. **Distance is weak connection**

    - Space emerges from low fill strength.

22. **Closeness is strong relation**

    - Nearness is experiential, not geometric.

## 9. Meaning and value

23. **Balance is fundamental**

    - Vibes tend toward stability.
    - Extremes generate tension.

24. **Suffering and harmony are structural**

    - Hell and heaven are patterns of tone.
    - They are not places, but configurations.

## 10. Summary axioms (song-ready)

You can treat these as the “chorus truths”:

- Experience is all there is
- Vibes are the units
- Links are the feeling
- Beats are the time
- Rules are the grammar
- Will is the push
- Fill is the pull
- Choice is coherence resolving
