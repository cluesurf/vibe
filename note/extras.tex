
\subsection{Other Open Questions}

Several other fundamental questions await resolution. What happens to isolated vibes with zero links? Do they vanish per the relationality axiom, or persist in some dormant state? The binding mechanism presents another puzzle: how exactly do millions of individual vibes create unified conscious experience? We also lack understanding of qualia mapping: how specific experiential qualities correspond to particular vibe configurations. Finally, the first beat paradox remains: if causation requires time, but time emerges from beats, what drives the initial beat?

These questions represent fertile ground for future theoretical development and may require new mathematical tools or conceptual frameworks to resolve.
