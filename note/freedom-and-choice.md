# Freedom, Choice, and the Urge: The Model

A standing record of how the model treats freedom and choice, kept so the insight is not
lost. The mechanism is implemented and tested in P43, and rests on the universality of
P44 and the selves-as-attractors of P34.

## The puzzle

A vibe just follows the rule. Its next tone is a fixed function of its neighbours. So
where is freedom? Is everything determined? Are we preprogrammed, perfectly, one hundred
percent?

## The resolution in one line

The base is deterministic, with no randomness. But determined does not mean preprogrammed,
and it does not mean random. There is a real third option: **self-determined, jointly
authored, and computationally irreducible.** That is where freedom lives.

## The model

- A **self** is a stable attractor, a self-maintaining pattern of vibes (P34). Its stored
  patterns are the options it can settle into.
- An **urge** is a bias field reaching the self from a subtler (slower, deeper) layer,
  through the notes. It is not a command, it is a pull.
- A **choice** is the self settling under the urge. The local field is the self's own
  structure plus the urge bias, and the self settles to one of its attractors. The self
  can resonate with the urge (amplify it, take it up) or damp it, and which one happens is
  set by the self's own pattern. That resonance is the felt "accepting" of an urge.

## What is established (P43)

Five things, all measured:

1. **Determinism, not random.** The same self and urge always give the same choice.
2. **Self-determination.** The same urge gives different selves different choices, so the
   self's own structure is a real cause, not a passive channel.
3. **The urge matters too.** The same self under different urges can choose differently,
   so neither the self alone nor the urge alone fixes the outcome. No part suffices.
4. **Agency scales with structure.** A stronger, deeper self imposes its own pattern over
   the urge. A richer self has more say.
5. **Irreducibility.** The choice takes several beats to settle and is not the one-step
   response to the urge. It must be lived out, not read off.

## Why we are not preprogrammed

Two facts break the idea of a perfect prewritten program, even though the base is
deterministic:

- **No complete initial condition.** The universe grows from a first element and keeps
  growing. There is no finite moment holding all the data of all time, so there is no
  complete program written in advance to run. You cannot be preprogrammed by an initial
  state that never existed.
- **Computational irreducibility.** The substrate is universal (P44), so its far future
  cannot be computed by any shortcut faster than letting it happen. The outcome does not
  exist anywhere in advance to be read off. It comes into being only by being enacted.

So the future is determined by the whole, yet not prewritten, not random, and not
forecastable even in principle. Freedom is the self being the place, and the means, by
which the whole resolves into one outcome, drawn from a depth that never bottoms out, and
made only by being lived.

## A walkthrough: how a choice feels from inside

This is the hard part to grasp quickly, so here is a concrete mental walkthrough you can
run in your head. Picture a small decision: you are reading, and the question arises,
keep reading or get up and walk.

1. **You are a landscape.** Picture yourself not as a chooser standing outside, but as a
   landscape of valleys, your leanings, habits, mood, history, all the couplings that make
   you you. Right now you are resting in the "reading" valley. Each valley is one of your
   stable patterns, an option you can settle into.
2. **An urge is a tilt.** A pull arrives from a deeper, subtler layer, a restlessness, a
   nudge toward "walk." It does not command you. It tilts your landscape a little toward
   the "walk" valley.
3. **The choice is the settling.** Under the tilt, the ball rolls. It may stay in
   "reading" if that valley is deep for you, or roll over into "walk" if the tilt is
   enough. Where it ends up is the choice. The rolling takes a moment, the back-and-forth
   you feel as weighing.
4. **It is determined.** Given your exact landscape and the exact tilt, where the ball
   settles is fixed. Rewind to the identical state and it settles the same way every time.
   There is no dice.
5. **Yet it is not preprogrammed.** No one wrote down where the ball would end up in
   advance. There was no complete script, because the whole that fixes it was never
   finished and never handed over. And you could not have known the result without
   actually letting it roll. The only way to find the answer was to live the settling.
6. **And it is yours.** It was your landscape that decided. Another person, a different
   landscape, given the very same tilt, would settle into a different valley. The choice
   is yours precisely because your structure is what tipped it.
7. **The felt part is the settling itself.** From the inside it feels like considering,
   leaning, and then deciding. That feeling is not an illusion laid over a hidden script.
   The deciding IS the rolling. The determinism does not remove the choosing, because the
   choosing is the deterministic settling, done by you, drawn by a deeper pull, and
   knowable only by being lived.

So the recipe to hold in your head: **you are a landscape, the urge is a tilt, the choice
is the rolling.** Fixed given the landscape and the tilt, authored by your landscape,
sourced from a depth you did not write, and real only once it has actually rolled. That
is how a choice can be completely determined and still be a genuine, free, lived choice.

## The honest boundary

This models the STRUCTURE of choice: self-determination, joint authorship, agency-scaling,
irreducibility, and the urge mechanism. Whether that structure is identical to the felt
act of choosing, the lived sense of freedom from the inside, is the hard boundary that
sits under all of the framework. We model and measure the structural correlates. The felt
choice itself is the wild face, and naming its mechanism is not the same as living it.
