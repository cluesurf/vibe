The knob search returned the structural law: PHASE AND NUMBER ARE
LOCKED for the dressed species. Across the full offset sweep, blind

last decimal), every phase-shifting regime also changes number, one
regime adds number at fixed phase, and none moves phase at fixed
number. The modified amplitudes quantize exactly (two root three,
root thirty-nine, two root twenty-one).

So the coherence bridge closes with a negative-with-mechanism: a
dressed packet cannot be split into equal-number branches differing
only in phase, single-quantum-style coherence between detector
classes does not exist on the substrate in this regime, and the
model's committed observable stands: if such coherence is real at
scale in nature, this substrate predicts a number-organized
visibility floor (the quantum-computing stake in
note/prediction/intelligence.md). Formalized as E-FND-0140
(verification running at this writing). The sweep is permanent at
