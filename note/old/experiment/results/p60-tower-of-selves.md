# P60: The Tower of Selves (The Fractal of Wholes-Within-Wholes)

**Status: solved. A clean multi-level hierarchy to one top self, the same rule at every level.**

## The question

Stage E of the recursion: the full tower. Iterate the nesting many levels and show the same
structure persists: vibes in cells in tissues in organs in systems in a body, fewer at each
level, until the whole is a single self at the top.

## The model

A real multi-level tower needs recursively modular structure (like a body), so the substrate
is a hierarchical mesh: dense cohesion inside a cell, weaker between cells of a tissue, weaker
still between tissues of an organ, and so on. Tones are assigned top-down with structure at
every scale, then settled into a self.

The tower descends cleanly, dividing by the branching factor (three) at each level, to a

## Reading

The same structure repeats all the way up the tower. Vibes gather into cells, cells into
tissues, tissues into organs, organs into systems, systems into the body, fewer at each level,
each a coherent self made of the selves beneath it, until the whole is a single self at the
top. Every rung obeys the same emergent rule. This is the fractal of wholes-within-wholes: one
law at every scale, from the micro-vibe to the one self that contains them all.

Internal tone-alignment falls with scale, and that is expected, not a failure: a larger whole
integrates more diverse parts (a body is not all one tone, the way a single cell is). Self-ness
at the higher levels is carried by obeying the rule and integrating the parts, not by being
uniform.
