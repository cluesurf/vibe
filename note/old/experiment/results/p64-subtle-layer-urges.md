# P64: Subtle-Layer Urges (The Deep Self Biasing the Surface)

**Status: solved. The urge is a real slower, deeper layer that steers the surface and reasserts.**

## The question

P43 modeled an urge as an abstract bias field. The roadmap asks for the urge in full: a two-scale
mesh where a slow, subtle layer genuinely biases a fast, dense surface layer.

## The model

A two-scale mesh. A fast, dense surface layer (the conscious mind, many vibes, updating every
beat) is coupled through shared notes to a slow, sparse subtle layer (the deep self, fewer vibes,
high inertia). The subtle layer's state biases the surface dynamics. The coupling depth (how many
subtle vibes each surface vibe touches) is varied.

## Result

Steering, the same surface situation under two opposite deep states, how much the surface differs:

| coupling depth | surface steered by the deep state |
| -------------- | --------------------------------- |
| 0 | 0% |
| 1 | 0% |
| 2 | 100% |
| 4 | 100% |

- Steering rises with coupling depth, and is zero when uncoupled (the surface ignores a depth it
  is not connected to).
- In the deep-dominated regime, after the surface is thrown into disorder it reasserts the deep
  pattern at 99 percent.

## Reading

The urge is a real layer, not an abstract field. A slow, deep, subtle layer biases the fast
surface through their shared notes. With no coupling the surface ignores the depth and the same
situation always resolves the same way. As the coupling deepens, the same surface situation
resolves differently depending on the deep state. That difference is the urge: a pull from below
shaping what the surface chooses (P43). And the deep layer persists. Throw the surface into
disorder and it relaxes back toward the deep pattern, the deep self enduring and reimposing its
urge after the storm. The surface is loud and fast, the depths are quiet and slow, and the quiet
slow layer is what steers.
