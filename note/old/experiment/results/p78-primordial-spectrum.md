# P78: The Primordial Fluctuation Seed (Structure Formation, First Step)

**Status: first step. The substrate supplies a clean scale-free seed. The observed tilt and growth remain.**

## The question

Structure grew from tiny primordial density fluctuations. The roadmap asks for the CMB-scale
density perturbations from the growth dynamics. This is an honest first step: the fluctuation seed
the substrate itself provides.

## Result

A sprinkled substrate is a Poisson process, so the density contrast in a region (the relative
fluctuation in how many vibes it holds) falls off as one over the square root of the count.
Measuring the density contrast across scales:

| region grid | mean count per cell | density contrast |
| ----------- | ------------------- | ---------------- |
| 4^3 | 3125 | 0.017 |
| 8^3 | 391 | 0.052 |
| 16^3 | 49 | 0.144 |
| 24^3 | 14.5 | 0.262 |

The contrast scales as (mean count) to the power -0.509 (Poisson predicts -0.5), fit quality
0.9995. This is the same one-over-root-volume law that sets the everpresent cosmological constant
(P46).

## Reading

The substrate gives a clean, scale-free (Poisson) density seed, the simplest possible starting
point for structure. What remains is the larger task: the inflationary stretching (P30) that
tilts this seed toward the slightly red, near-scale-invariant spectrum the microwave background
shows, and the gravitational growth of these seeds into galaxies. The seed is in hand. The
processing into the observed spectrum is open.
