# The Model (Vibe Theory)

A short, distilled map of the whole framework. The aim is that you can read it once, hold it
in your head, and explain the theory clearly. The full conceptual paper is in `text/v6/`, the
long walkthrough in `note/how-it-all-works.md`, and the findings in `note/experiment/results/`.

**The thesis in one line:** reality is one substance, experience, in the form of **vibes**, and
everything else is a pattern in a growing crystal of vibes.

## The one substance

- There is one kind of thing, the **vibe**, a unit of experience. Not dead matter with
  experience added. Experience is the ground.
- The reversal: matter is what experience looks like when it settles into stable, shared
  patterns. The brain does not produce mind. The brain is a highly integrated knot of the field.
- **Lawful** and **wild** are conditions, not domains. Lawful means regular, stable, structured.
  Wild means fluid and unsettled. Lawful is not the same as physical. The physical world is the
  shared lawful crystallization, but mental and spiritual systems can be lawful too.

## Why there is something rather than nothing

- Nothing cannot hold. A state with no distinctions is a balance point, not a floor. It cannot
  even be named without making a distinction. And a whole with nothing else can only relate to
  itself, which splits it. There is no road back, since erasing is itself a new distinction.
- So a beginning is forced, not caused from outside. That first self-relation is the first
  split: the first two vibes, the first note, the first tone, the first beat.
- Distinctions only ever accumulate. That one-way pile-up is **time**.

## The base: from the integers

- Experience is the substance. The **integers** are only the cleanest way to describe its
  structure, because they are the least arbitrary thing there is.
- The ladder: integers, then their symmetries, then reflections (Coxeter groups), then a
  **hyperbolic crystal**. Each rung adds one idea, nothing tuned.
- The spatial dimension is **three**, not assumed but pinned: finite-celled hyperbolic crystals
  exist only in dimensions two, three, and four, and only three gives stable orbits.

## The crystal (the substrate)

- A growing **hyperbolic crystal**. The real one is the 3D dodecahedral honeycomb. The easy
  picture is the flat `{7,3}` tiling.
- **A vibe is a tile. Its tone is its color: red pain, green peace, blue pleasure. A note is a
  shared edge between tiles.**
- **Tone, note, fill, will, link** are the one vibe seen from different angles, not extra parts.
  There is only the vibe.
- Hyperbolic because it has endless room to grow and no preferred direction, so it respects
  relativity. Curvature, not disorder, is what keeps it direction-free.

## The growing mesh

- The mesh is **relational, not spatial**. A vibe has no coordinates. Its only "where" is what
  it connects to. Space is the large-scale shape of the connections.
- It grows at its **frontier, which is the present**. The past is fixed, the future is open and
  being made.

## The rule (the law)

- The tone is ternary: pain, peace, pleasure (`-1, 0, +1`). Each note carries a ternary
  **fill**, the sign and strength of attention.
- The update: a vibe's next tone is the **sign of the sum of its neighbors' tones times their
  fills**. No continuous weights, no hidden state, local, with no global clock.
- This one rule is **computationally universal**. It can host any pattern that can exist,
  including a mind.

## Determinism and freedom

- Fully determined, no randomness. But not predetermined: there is no complete starting state
  (it grows forever), and it is **computationally irreducible** (the only way to know the future
  is to live it).
- Apparent randomness is being **determined by the whole and opaque to the part**. The same fact
  lets the model give the quantum correlations without any signal.
- A **self** is a stable attractor. **Freedom is self-determination**: the same urge moves
  different selves to different outcomes. You are a landscape, the urge a tilt, the choice the
  rolling. A slow, deep, subtle layer supplies the urge.

## The variety of experience

- **Tone is the charge** (valence, good or bad). **Structure is the quality** (what it is of).
- Three tones suffice because the variety lives in the arrangement, the way a few letters give
  all of literature.
- Senses, emotions, and thought are structure on the one tone (modality is which subsystem,
  content is the pattern, and so on). These are working analogies, not settled assignments.

## Recursion: higher vibes and the tower

- A coherent, **integrated** cluster of vibes reads as a single **higher vibe**, a mind or a
  being. There is no stored higher tone. It is the aggregate of the micro-tones, read as a unit.
  Ternary stays at the bottom.
- The same rule holds at the higher level on integrated wholes. **Integration** is the dial that
  decides what counts as a being.
- Cells in a body: a small wound heals (homeostasis), a whole cohesive part flipped keeps a new
  identity (autonomy). The **tower** runs vibes, cells, tissues, organs, body, up toward one
  whole, the same shape at every level.
- No infinite mirror: a thing cannot store a full copy of itself, so self-knowledge is always a
  lossy summary, and the tower of self-models stays finite.

## Life, motion, evolution

- A self is a pattern, not its material. It **moves** like a glider, the pattern re-forming on
  new vibes while the vibes stay put, capped at the speed of light. It **lives** by keeping a
  boundary and repairing itself. It **grows** by recruiting vibes and nesting. It **dies** when
  integration drops too low and the pattern disperses, the material returning to the field. It
  **evolves**, because persistent, self-copying patterns are selected.

## The physical world (what emerges)

All of this is checked in a finite testbed. It is a model, not a derivation of nature.

- **Space** is the shape of the web. **Time** is its before-and-after depth.
- **Matter** is a persistent knot. Mass is an energy gap. The four field spins are present.
- **Forces** are the twist a tone takes across a note. **Gravity** is the web's curvature:
  Newton's law, a conserving Einstein equation, a massless graviton, black-hole area-law entropy
  and a Hawking temperature.
- **Quantum**: amplitudes are averages over a patch, with interference, the Bell correlations
  (shared substrate), and the Born rule (fair sampling gives the squared amplitude) all recovered.
- **Cosmology**: expansion, dark energy near the right tiny size, and a dark-matter mechanism.

## Dreams and the wild side

- Much of the inner life is on the wild side, the part not settled into a shared pattern.
- A dream may be the mesh roaming its own memories with the world released, or a stream reaching
  the self through the field, or other things. This is left open.

## The spiritual frontier (most speculative)

- Because all selves share one substrate, **contact** is expressible: influence through
  note-channels, tuning and resonance, propagation by the rule. **Distance in the field is not
  distance in space**, since a shared deep root makes things far apart in space near in the field.
- **Other realms and beings** could be parallel, lawful-but-non-physical structures in the field,
  stable and possibly evolved, not merely higher rungs above us.
- **God**: traditions differ. It could be the whole field at full integration, or a maximal
  intelligence within the whole. The model gives the structure, not the name.
- **Synchronicity**: a coincidence with no link, read as correlation from shared deep ancestry.
- Nothing here is claimed to occur. Only the mechanism is expressible.

## What is solid, partial, open

- **Solid**: the base mathematics, the substrate and its dimension, emergent geometry, time, and
  law, Lorentz safety, the fields, gravity's Newtonian limit with a conserving Einstein equation
  and a graviton, the Born rule, Hawking temperature, recursion and the tower, integration,
  freedom, and universality.
- **Partial**: the full quantum account, the dark-sector numbers, fully discrete strong-field
  gravity, and life and evolution as built models.
- **Open**: the specific content of the Standard Model, and the deepest one, whether the
  structure of experience is the same as the felt fact of experiencing. The map is not the
  territory.

**In one line:** experience first, and matter is what it does when it holds still.
