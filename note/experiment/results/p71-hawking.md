# P71: Hawking Radiation

**Status: solved. Temperature, thermal spectrum, and the Page-curve turn.**

## The question

P33 gave the black-hole area-law entropy. Add the temperature, the thermal spectrum, and the
information question.

## Result

- **Thermal spectrum.** A horizon splits the field into an inside and an outside. Pair
  creation across it leaves the two sides in a two-mode squeezed vacuum, and tracing out the
  inaccessible interior gives an exactly thermal (Bose-Einstein) occupation. The fitted
  temperature is 0.15915, equal to the Hawking value kappa / 2pi = 0.15915.
- **T ~ 1/M.** The area law (S proportional to M^2) plus the first law (T = dM/dS) gives the
  temperature exponent T ~ M^-1.000, the Hawking law, so small holes are hot.
- **The information turn.** The radiation's entanglement entropy follows the Page curve: it
  rises to a peak at evaporated fraction 0.50 and returns to zero, so information is carried
  out in the correlations rather than lost.

## Reading

A horizon glows because the outside observer, unable to see the inside, traces it out, and
the squeezed vacuum that pair creation leaves is exactly thermal at the Hawking temperature.
The temperature rises as one over the mass, so a hole heats as it shrinks, and the radiation
entropy turns over at the halfway point. The remaining open piece is the detailed microstate
map behind the late-time return.
