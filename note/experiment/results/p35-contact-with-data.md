# P35: Contact With Data

**Status: the framework meets observation. One match, one confirmed null, one future target.**

## The question

A model becomes science when its distinctive predictions meet data. Three of ours do.
We compute each from the framework's scaling laws and real physical constants, and
compare to measurement.

## 1. Dark energy (the everpresent Lambda)

The everpresent model predicts delta-Lambda ~ 1/sqrt(V), with V the spacetime 4-volume
in Planck units. At the observed 4-volume (the past horizon, (c t / l_Planck)^4):

- predicted: delta-Lambda ~ **1.5e-122** (Planck units)
- observed: Lambda ~ **2.9e-122** (Planck units)
- ratio predicted/observed = **0.53**

The prediction matches the measured dark energy **to order of magnitude** (within a
factor of two for this volume convention). This is the one quantitative cosmological
success of causal-set theory: the right value, about 10^-122, not a tuned constant but
1/sqrt of the spacetime volume.

## 2. Lorentz violation (Planck-scale)

Gamma-ray-burst photon timing (GRB 090510, Fermi LAT) excludes linear Lorentz
violation below about 7.6 times the Planck energy. The framework, using a random
sprinkling that is Lorentz-invariant in distribution (P27), predicts **no linear
Lorentz violation at all**. So observation **confirms** the prediction. It also
discriminates: a lattice (or any regular) discreteness would predict a large linear
effect that is already excluded, while the random-sprinkling discreteness predicts the
observed null.

## 3. Swerves (momentum diffusion)

The discreteness diffusion (P26) is Planck-suppressed: a particle gets a tiny momentum
kick per Planck time, so the accumulated spread over the age of the universe is small.
It sits below current bounds (from the narrowness of cosmic-ray and particle momenta),
so it is **consistent** with observation, and is a **future target** for
ultra-high-energy cosmic rays rather than a present conflict.

## Bottom line

One prediction matches a measured number (dark energy, to order of magnitude), one is
confirmed by a null result that excludes the alternatives (no linear Lorentz
violation), and one is a clean future target (swerves). This is where the framework
makes contact with data, not just internal coherence.

## Honest reading

The dark-energy match is order-of-magnitude, and the precise value depends on the
4-volume convention (a factor of a few) and is Sorkin's prediction, not unique to this
work. The Lorentz result is a null confirmation, strong but not a positive detection.
The swerve is below detection. So this is genuine contact with data, with one
quantitative hit and one decisive null, not yet a positive smoking-gun detection.
