# P49: The Crystal Is Hidden and Hierarchical

**Status: demonstrated. A regular crystal hides from inside and is tree-like.**

## The questions

Two implications of the tessellation base, made testable:
1. Can reality be a crystal you cannot catch from inside?
2. Is the base natively hierarchical and tree-like?

## Result

**Hidden from inside** (Lorentz anisotropy, the measurement that catches a lattice):

| substrate | anisotropy |
| --------- | ---------- |
| hyperbolic crystal {7,3} | 0.040 |
| random foam (sprinkle) | 0.078 |
| flat lattice | 1.000 |

The crystal reads the same as the random foam (both near zero) and nothing like the flat
lattice (1.000). So the crystal hides behind the very test that exposes a lattice. From
inside, you cannot tell the ordered crystal from a random foam.

**Hierarchical and tree-like** (Gromov delta-hyperbolicity, 0 means a tree):

| substrate | delta |
| --------- | ----- |
| hyperbolic crystal {7,3} | 1.5 (small, tree-like) |
| flat lattice | 19.0 (large, grid-like) |

The crystal has small bounded delta (tree-like, natively hierarchical), while the flat
lattice has large delta (a grid, not hierarchical).

## Reading

Order at the foundation is undetectable from within (a hyperbolic crystal reads like a
random foam), and it brings the branching, nested hierarchy the framework wanted for its
layered structure, where a flat lattice does not. Reality can be an exact, ordered,
hierarchical crystal and never be caught from inside.
