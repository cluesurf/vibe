# P57: Recursion (Vibes Into Higher Vibes)

**Status: structural recursion demonstrated, with no stored higher layer. Emergent macro-rule partial.**

## The question

Is the model recursive: can a coherent mesh of vibes be treated as a single higher vibe,
of the same kind, so the same law governs every scale (minds made of minds)? And crucially,
without any stored higher-level tone, since the model has no hidden state.

## The model (no new storage)

A higher vibe is NOT a separately stored tone. It is the **aggregate of the micro-tones**
(the cluster majority), a derived view read on demand from the one ternary base, never
saved and never evolved on its own. The only dynamics is the one micro-rule on the
micro-tones. The higher level is what that looks like summarized.

## Result

On a mesh of 1500 vibes coarse-grained into 154 super-vibes (clusters read as single
higher vibes):

1. **Self-similar structure.** The aggregate view is the same kind of object: ternary, and
   Lorentz-safe (anisotropy 0.12). A coarse view of the base is itself a valid mesh.
2. **No separate storage, inherited stability.** Running the micro-rule more leaves the
   aggregate unchanged (agreement 1.000), because the micro-self is a fixed point. The
   higher self's stability is inherited from the micro-self below it, not maintained by any
   stored super-state.
3. **Emergent macro-rule (partial).** The aggregated micro-step matches the macro-rule by
   only about 0.10 (1 would be fully emergent). So the higher level being governed by the
   same rule, purely as an emergent fact of the micro-dynamics, is only partial. This is the
   honest open frontier.
4. **The tower.** Aggregating again yields a valid second level (29 super-super-vibes,
   still ternary), the fractal of wholes-within-wholes.

## Reading

A coherent mesh of vibes can be read as a single higher vibe: its tone is the aggregate
(majority) of its members, a derived view, with no new stored tone and no separate
dynamics. Only the ternary micro-tones are ever stored. The aggregate view is the same kind
of object as the base, its stability is inherited from the micro-self, and the tower
continues. The model is recursive and self-similar (minds made of minds), with the higher
levels being summaries of the one ternary base, never abstractions stored beside it.

## Honest reading

The structural recursion (same-kind aggregate, inherited stability, tower) is clean. The
deeper claim, that the higher level is GOVERNED by the same rule purely as an emergent
consequence of the micro-dynamics, is only partial here (emergence about 0.10). Making the
macro-rule fully emergent (a renormalization fixed point of the rule, not just the geometry)
is solved in P58 (it is a fixed point exactly on the integrated wholes). The combination problem (whether the higher aggregate genuinely
feels as one) is the hard boundary, both named in the higher-vibes-and-recursion spec.
