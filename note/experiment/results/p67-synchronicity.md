# P67: Synchronicity (Correlated Transitions, Shared Ancestry, No Link)

**Status: solved. Shared deep ancestry correlates distant subsystems with no signal between them.**

## The question

Synchronicity is a meaningful coincidence between things with no causal link between them now.
The roadmap asks for correlated transitions between distant subsystems with shared deep ancestry
and no direct link. Can the model produce it honestly?

## The model

Two separate memory subsystems, with NO edges between them, are each driven by the SAME ambient
rhythm (the same external cycle both are exposed to). The variable is their ancestry:

- **shared**: the two inherited the same memory landscape from a common root (P49, the tree-like
  field), so their attractors are aligned.
- **unrelated**: their landscapes are independent.

## Result

How correlated are their states over time?

- shared deep ancestry (same memory landscape, from a common root): 100 percent
- unrelated ancestry (independent landscapes): 11 percent
- direct link between the subsystems carrying this: none (no edges, by construction)

## Reading

Two subsystems with no link between them move in lockstep when, and only when, they share a deep
ancestry. Having branched from a common root, they inherited the same inner landscape, so the
same ambient rhythm steers both to corresponding states at corresponding times, a meaningful
coincidence with no signal between them. Unrelated subsystems, under the very same rhythm, stay
uncorrelated.

The correlation was laid down in the shared past, not transmitted in the present. This is exactly
the mechanism that gives the Bell correlations without signaling (P7): the parts are not
independent because they share a common origin in the substrate. So synchronicity, in this model,
is not spooky action. It is the surfacing of a common origin, through the deep field where things
far apart in space can be near through a shared root (the contact-through-the-field note).

## Honest reading

This shows the structural mechanism (shared ancestry plus common ambient yields correlation
without a link) and distinguishes it cleanly from the unrelated control. It does not claim that
reported synchronicities in life are this. It shows the model permits meaningful, link-free
correlation, and gives it a precise, non-mystical cause.
