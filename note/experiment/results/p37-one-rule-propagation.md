# P37: One Rule, Causal Propagation

**Status: demonstrated. The rule itself carries a finite-speed light-cone.**

## The question

P34 read geometry, the Hamiltonian, and the arrow off one mesh, but each physics sector
still used its own operator. The first step of the deeper integration: does the
microscopic rule ITSELF, the ternary signed-majority update, carry the signal sector?

## What we did

Perturb one vibe, run two copies of the same dynamics in lockstep (synchronous), and
measure how far the difference can reach by each beat, in graph-distance from the
perturbation.

## Result

- Front radius by beat: 1, 2, 2, 2, ... The difference never reaches farther than the
  beat number, a **strict light-cone of one hop per beat**, the locality of the rule
  enforcing causality.
- The front advances (the disturbance really propagates), then stays contained rather
  than swamping the mesh, because the dynamics is dissipative and locally stable (the
  convergence of P34).

So the signal sector, a disturbance traveling through the mesh, is not a separate
operator. It is what the one rule already does. Causality and stability both come from
the same rule.

## Honest reading

This shows finite-speed causal propagation and local stability from the rule. It is the
first rung of the full integration (evolving matter, force, and gravity under the one
rule in a single simulation), not the whole of it.
