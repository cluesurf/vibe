# P32: The Einstein Equations as Equations of Motion

**Status: down-payment. Conservation, the Newtonian limit, and a c-speed graviton.**

## The question

P24 derived the linearized Einstein operator G[h] from the action. Does the Einstein
equation G[h] = 8 pi T behave as a real theory of gravity, an equation of motion?

## Result

- **Conservation (the contracted Bianchi identity).** The Einstein tensor is
  transverse: k . G[h] = 0 to machine zero for arbitrary perturbations h. So the
  Einstein equation AUTOMATICALLY enforces energy-momentum conservation, a deep
  structural property, not an extra assumption.
- **The Newtonian limit.** The static Einstein equation is the Poisson equation, whose
  point-source solution is the 1/r potential (validated in P16). Newton's gravity IS
  the static weak-field Einstein equation.
- **Graviton dynamics.** In vacuum the transverse-traceless modes obey a wave equation
  with dispersion omega = |k|, so the graviton speed omega/|k| = 1.0000 at every
  frequency: massless, propagating at the speed of light.

So the linearized Einstein equation is a genuine equation of motion: it conserves
energy-momentum, reduces to Newton in the static limit, and propagates a massless
graviton at the speed of light in vacuum.

## Honest reading

This is the LINEARIZED Einstein equation (weak field). The full nonlinear Einstein
equation, derived from the discrete action with the matter back-reaction, is the
remaining step.
