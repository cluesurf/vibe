# P31: The Quantum Formalism (Unitarity, Interference, Born Rule)

**Status: down-payment. The three pillars are present, the derivation of Born is open.**

## The question

P7 gave the Bell hinge, P17 gave quantum coherence. Are the core pillars of the
quantum formalism present on the mesh: unitary evolution, interference of amplitudes,
and the Born rule?

## Result

- **Unitarity.** The total probability sum |psi|^2 under e^{-iHt} is 1.000000 at every
  time tested, conserved (unitary evolution).
- **Interference.** From two sources, the probability at a target is 0.36 when
  amplitudes add but 0.18 when probabilities add, an interference term of 0.18.
  Amplitudes interfere, not probabilities, the defining quantum feature.
- **The Born rule.** |psi|^2 is non-negative and sums to one at all times, a conserved
  probability over the mesh sites.

So unitary evolution, amplitude interference, and a conserved Born probability are all
present on the mesh.

## Honest reading

This is a down-payment, not the full theory. The open problem is to DERIVE why the
probability is |psi|^2 (and not another power) and the measurement rule from the mesh
itself, building on the superdeterministic hinge of P7. We show the formalism is
consistent and present, not that it is uniquely forced.
