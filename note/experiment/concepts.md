# Concepts: the cross-index

The arenas are folders, but the ideas cut across them. This file walks the big
concepts and points into the arenas (and a few landmark experiments) where each
is tested. Use it when you know the idea but not the arena. The master map is
[readme.md](readme.md).

## The base

- **The forced substrate.** Why {3,4,3,4}, the 24-cell, and D4, and not some
  other lattice. Reached from many independent routes, and rival lattices shown
  to fail. See [foundations](foundations/readme.md) (the substrate forced),
  [geometry](geometry/readme.md) (the 24-cell and F4 spine), and
  [substrate-survey](substrate-survey/readme.md) (the comparative scoreboards).
- **The ternary tone.** The atom of distinction, the smallest alphabet with a
  vacuum and a mirror, and why its three is not the triality three. See
  [foundations](foundations/readme.md) (the ternary tone).
- **The one reversible conserving rule (the knit).** One local rule that
  conserves charge and momentum, runs backward exactly, and is forced unique by
  the substrate symmetry. See [foundations](foundations/readme.md) (the one
  reversible rule), [computation](computation/readme.md) (the rule as reversible
  computation), and [fluids](fluids/readme.md) (the reversible bulk is inviscid).

## Time, records, and emergence

- **Reversibility, records, and the arrow.** The rule erases nothing, so the
  arrow of time is emergent, not fundamental. See
  [foundations](foundations/readme.md) (exact reversibility, records, and the
  arrow), [cosmology](cosmology/readme.md) (the arrow at cosmic scale), and
  [quantum](quantum/readme.md) (reversibility and ontology).
- **Emergence and coarse-graining.** How smooth continuum physics comes out of
  the discrete base through a coarse-graining tower and a renormalization flow.
  See [renormalization](renormalization/readme.md) (the tower and the RG flow),
  [foundations](foundations/readme.md) (from determinism to quantum), and the
  emergence sub-themes throughout [selves](selves/readme.md).

## The Standard Model

- **The gauge group and charges.** U(1), SU(2), SU(3), charge quantization, and
  the electroweak structure emerging from the substrate. See
  [gauge](gauge/readme.md) (emergent U(1), non-abelian gauge, the algebra spine,
  anomaly cancellation, coupling constants).
- **Mass, Koide, and generations.** The fermion mass hierarchy, the Koide
  relation, and the still-open three-generations count. See
  [gauge](gauge/readme.md) (mass hierarchy, Koide, three generations) and
  [spin](spin/readme.md) (triality and the generations problem).
- **Spin and fermions.** Spinors, the spin-1/2 double cover, the Dirac equation,
  triality, anyons, and emergent matter from exclusion. See
  [spin](spin/readme.md) start to finish, with the geometric origin in
  [geometry](geometry/readme.md) (spinors from tilings).

## The quantum

- **The quantum from determinism.** Superposition, interference, the Born rule,
  and the emergent complex unit from a discrete deterministic base. See
  [quantum](quantum/readme.md) (the substrate-to-quantum bridge, Born-rule
  statistics).
- **Entanglement and the shared past.** Bell and CHSH, nonlocality, and the
  superdeterminism / shared-past mechanism (the largest cluster in the arena).
  See [quantum](quantum/readme.md) (entanglement and the shared-past mechanism).
- **Measurement and settling.** Decoherence, measurement as settling, and the
  open question of the single definite outcome. See
  [quantum](quantum/readme.md) (measurement, settling, and decoherence).

## Spacetime and gravity

- **Dimension and flat space.** Why space is three-dimensional and flat, and how
  the thin boundary sits on the hyperbolic bulk. See
  [geometry](geometry/readme.md) (dimensional selection, the horosphere as flat
  3D space) and [cosmology](cosmology/readme.md) (dimension, flat space,
  horizons).
- **Lorentz and the light cone.** The hard problem: recovering relativity from a
  fixed lattice. See [relativity](relativity/readme.md) (the emergent light
  cone, the preferred-frame problem, dispersion and low-energy recovery).
- **Gravity as emergent.** Entropic and thermodynamic gravity: curvature from
  the area law, Einstein as an equation of state, the graviton, horizons, and
  the dark sector. See [gravity](gravity/readme.md).
- **Holography.** Bulk-boundary duality, Ryu-Takayanagi, celestial holography,
  and holographic error-correcting codes on the discrete substrate. See
  [holography](holography/readme.md).
