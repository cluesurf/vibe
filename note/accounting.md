# Creating a Theory of the Universe

- Account for all known observations in the universe.
- Give reproducible results.
- Predict something that can be experimentally confirmed.
- Be understandable by human beings.

## Fundamental Observations in the Universe

### Physical Micro Observations

1. **Wave-Particle Duality**: Particles such as electrons and photons exhibit both wave-like and particle-like behavior depending on observation.
1. **Quantum Superposition**: A quantum system seems to exist in multiple states simultaneously until measured.
1. **Quantum Entanglement**: Particles can be instantaneously correlated over vast distances, defying classical locality.
1. **Quantum Uncertainty**: The position and momentum of a particle seems to not both be precisely determined at the same time.
1. **Quantum Tunneling**: Particles can pass through potential barriers they classically shouldn't be able to overcome.
1. **Vacuum Fluctuations**: Empty space contains fluctuations of energy that give rise to virtual particles.
1. **Quantum Decoherence**: The transition from quantum superpositions to classical probabilities through interaction with the environment.
1. **Pauli Exclusion Principle**: No two fermions (like electrons) can occupy the same quantum state simultaneously.
1. **Quantum Field Theory**: All fundamental particles arise as excitations of underlying quantum fields.
1. **Nonlocality**: Correlations between entangled particles cannot be explained by local hidden variables.
1. **Spin Quantization**: Particles have intrinsic spin that follows discrete quantization rules.
1. **Measurement Problem**: The act of measurement seems to influence the outcome of quantum states.

### Physical Macro Observations

1. **Relativity of Time & Space**: Time and space seem to be interwoven into spacetime, and time dilates at high speeds.
1. **Equivalence of Mass and Energy**: Mass and energy are interchangeable.
1. **Gravity as Spacetime Curvature**: Gravity is the warping of spacetime by mass and energy.
1. **Black Holes & Event Horizons**: Singularities where gravitational pull prevents even light from escaping.
1. **Expansion of the Universe**: The universe is expanding, with galaxies receding from each other.
1. **Cosmic Microwave Background Radiation**: Relic radiation from a potential Big Bang.
1. **Entropy**: The total entropy (disorder) of a closed system always increases.

### Physical Other Observations

1. **The Arrow of Time**: Time appears to move forward due to entropy increase.
1. **Horizon & Information Paradox**: Information loss in black holes challenges unitarity in quantum mechanics.
1. **Gravitational Waves**: Ripples in spacetime generated by accelerating massive objects.
1. **Fine-Tuning of Physical Constants**: The universe's physical constants appear precisely set to allow life to exist.
1. **Baryogenesis & Nucleosynthesis**: The processes that led to the formation of matter in the early universe.
1. **Quantum Gravity & Planck Scale**: The need for a theory to unify quantum mechanics with gravity.
