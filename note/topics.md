## 🔹 Panpsychism & Combination Problem

1. **David Chalmers – "The Combination Problem for Panpsychism"** (2016)
   → Definitive taxonomy of the challenges facing panpsychism; introduces subject, quality, and structure combination problems.

2. **Philip Goff – "Consciousness and Fundamental Reality"** (2017)
   → Synthesizes Russellian monism and panpsychism; proposes consciousness as a ubiquitous feature of the physical world.

3. **Gregg Rosenberg – *A Place for Consciousness* (2004)**
   → Proposes a causal model of consciousness and the "carrier hypothesis," linking causation with phenomenal experience.

4. **William Seager – "Consciousness, Information, and Panpsychism"** (1995)
   → Early formulator of the combination problem; ties consciousness to information structure.

5. **Galen Strawson – "Realistic Monism: Why Physicalism Entails Panpsychism"** (2006)
   → Argues that physicalism logically requires consciousness to be fundamental.

6. **Hedda Hassel Mørch – "Panpsychism and Causation"** (2014)
   → Argues for a version of emergent panpsychism with macro-to-micro causal priority.

---

## 🔹 Quantum Mechanics & Consciousness

7. **Roger Penrose & Stuart Hameroff – "Orchestrated Objective Reduction" (Orch-OR)**
   → Hypothesis that consciousness arises from quantum-level processes in microtubules; controversial but widely discussed.

8. **Michael Lockwood – *Mind, Brain, and the Quantum* (1989)**
   → Connects quantum theory with mind-body dualism; explores nonlocality and experiential unity.

---

## 🔹 Information-Theoretic & Structural Foundations

9. **Giulio Tononi – "Consciousness as Integrated Information: A Provisional Manifesto"** (2008)
   → Introduces Integrated Information Theory (IIT) and the Φ measure of consciousness.

10. **John Wheeler – "It from Bit" (1989)**
    → Proposes that information is the fundamental building block of the universe.

11. **Stephen Wolfram – *A New Kind of Science* (2002)**
    → Suggests that simple computational rules can explain the complexity of physics and perhaps even consciousness.

---

## 🔹 Idealism & Metaphysical Alternatives

12. **Bertrand Russell – *The Analysis of Matter* (1927)**
    → Early foundation of Russellian monism, proposing that the intrinsic nature of matter may be mental.

13. **William James – "Does Consciousness Exist?" (1904)**
    → Radical empiricist challenge to dualism; basis for modern neutral monism.

14. **Alfred North Whitehead – *Process and Reality* (1929)**
    → Proposes a process-based metaphysics where experience is primary and everything is in flux.

15. **Ernst Mach – *The Analysis of Sensations* (1886)**
    → Influenced Russell and James; an early neutral monist approach where sensations form the basis of reality.

---

## 🔹 Systems & Simulation

16. **Donald Hoffman – "Conscious Realism" (2014+)**
    → Proposes that reality consists of interacting conscious agents and that perception is a user interface.

17. **Douglas Hofstadter – *I Am a Strange Loop* (2007)**
    → Examines recursion and self-awareness as central to consciousness.
