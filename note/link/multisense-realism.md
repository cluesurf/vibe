# Vibe to Multisense Realism

Multisense Realism (Craig Weinberg) is a sense-first monism: sense, the raw fact of felt
appearance, is the absolute foundation, and matter and mind are both downstream, different
"densities" of the same aesthetic phenomena seen at different perceptual frame-rates
(eigenmorphism). It shares vibe's founding axiom, that experience is the floor and matter is a
derived appearance, more directly than almost any other model surveyed. It also diverges from
vibe on one decisive point, and it is a sharp one: Weinberg holds that experience is sense and
not information, that computation can never be conscious, and that a discrete deterministic
substrate is materialism sneaking back in. So this is the most divergent of the link sheets:
the axiom is shared, the engine is refused. It is included because the shared axiom is real,
and the disagreement is worth stating cleanly rather than avoiding. 3 experiments.

## Shared invariants (the survivors)

Structures both reach from different starting points, kept honest about how few they are.

- **Experience is the base, matter is a derived appearance.** Vibe: the one substance is the
  vibe, experience itself, and physics is grown underneath it. MSR: sense is fundamental and
  matter is its most persistent, widely-shared appearance. Same direction of explanation, and
  both say the hard problem dissolves because experience was never produced from non-experience.
- **A self is a partial, scale-relative window on the whole.** Vibe: a self is an emergent
  mid-layer pattern, a lossy filter that maintains its own centre ([`E-SLF-0007`](../../test/experiment/selves/autonomous-self.ts), [`E-SLF-0006`](../../test/experiment/selves/attention-workspace.ts)).
  MSR: a mind is a filtered selection from the Aesthetic Holos, a window at one frame-rate.
  Both make the individual a partial view of one totality, not a separate substance.
- **Matter is the coarse, widely-agreed layer.** Vibe: the classical, shared world is what
  survives coarse-graining as an invariant level ([`E-SLF-0019`](../../test/experiment/selves/causal-emergence.ts)). MSR: matter is the most
  persistent, most publicly-shared appearance of sense. A parallel picture of the physical as
  the durable coarse face, though MSR reads it as appearance rather than a derived structure.

## Divergences

- **Discrete and computable vs continuous and non-mechanical, the decisive fork.** Vibe's base
  is a finite mesh, ternary tone, discrete beats, one reversible rule, so it is a computation
  and physics is meant to be derived on it. MSR holds that experience is sense and not
  information, that computation is a "mindless mind", and that a discrete deterministic base is
  materialism in experiential clothing. Vibe would hear "discrete" as generative grammar, MSR
  hears it as dead mechanism. This single split is the whole story, and it is genuine.
- **No formal model on the MSR side.** MSR is fully interpretive: no mathematics, no generative
  mechanism, no falsifiable prediction, and its quasi-physical claims (a power law around the
  speed of light) are correspondences, not derivations. So the correspondence table above is
  vibe measuring structures that MSR describes in prose, not shared results.
- **The fourfold correspondences.** MSR's Dialectic Tetragrammaton maps senses to forces, seasons
  and tarot suits. These are evocative correspondences with no mechanism to import, so they stay
  out, as fitted correspondences do from every sheet.

The honest reading: this is a shared starting axiom with one deep disagreement, not an
experiment-backed structural match like Chronoflux or Timeless Dynamics. The one bridge worth
offering Weinberg is in his own terms, whether "discrete" must mean "dead", or whether an
experiential base can have a discrete generative grammar without becoming the materialism he
rightly rejects.
