# Vibe to Orchestrated Objective Reduction

Orch-OR (Hameroff and Penrose) says consciousness comes from gravity collapsing a quantum
state (a superposition, many possibilities held at once) inside neurons. Vibe reaches the
same endpoint, a definite record (one settled outcome), and it survives a warm noisy
environment without needing any quantum coherence (a fragile superposition kept alive). So
the two agree on the result and disagree on the cause, which this sheet lays out. 6
experiments.

## Shared invariants (the survivors)

Structures both reach, from different starting points. (An invariant here is what stays
fixed: a definite outcome that holds, a pattern that persists.)

- **A definite record persists under noise.** Vibe: the settled outcome survives across a
  size sweep while its coherence (the superposition) is destroyed (E-QTM-0049), and
  survives thermal noise (E-QTM-0050). Orch-OR: reduction (the collapse to one outcome)
  yields a definite result. Vibe reaches it without paying the coherence cost.
- **The record holds its spatial shape while a coherent wave spreads out.** Vibe:
  E-SLF-0162. Orch-OR: the collapsed state is the stable classical trace left behind.
- **Integration collapses under anesthetic.** Integration is how tightly the parts bind
  into one whole. Vibe: E-SLF-0161. Orch-OR: anesthetics break the process that binds
  experience.
- **A collapse law set by energy and time.** Vibe: E-QTM-0051. Orch-OR: the collapse time
  is Planck's constant divided by the gravitational self-energy of the superposed mass.

## Divergences

- **The mechanism, the big one.** Orch-OR needs a fragile quantum superposition held alive
  and then collapsed by gravity. Vibe needs none: its record is classical and survives
  decoherence and heat by never depending on a superposition in the first place. Same
  result, opposite account of how you get there.
- **Gravity-driven collapse.** Vibe has no gravity term forcing the collapse. Its
  definiteness comes from vibe's arrow (its value-and-time direction) amplifying a tiny
  difference, and a self (a stable self-maintaining pattern) holding the result, not from
  the energy of curved spacetime.
- **Microtubules.** Vibe imports no brain-specific biology. Orch-OR bets on microtubules
  (protein structures inside neurons), where vibe's claim is about records and selves in
  general.
