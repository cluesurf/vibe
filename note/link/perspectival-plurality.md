# Vibe to Perspectival Plurality

Perspectival Plurality (Joseph McCard) makes experience fundamental and defines it as
"immanently registering activity", activity whose own unfolding modifies the conditions of
its continuation, taken before any split into inner and outer. From that one term it builds a
ladder of organisation (coherence, minimal self, centering, focused self, identity) and, in
its cosmology, argues that reality is a fundamental plurality of perspectives with no single
mind behind them (contra a cosmos-first or mind-first whole). It is the closest of the
consciousness models to vibe's own structural language, because its self is a persistent
pattern of constraints rather than a substance, and its proposed route to a physical order,
"jointly constituted invariants", is vibe's own move. This sheet derives the shared structure
from vibe. 6 experiments.

## Shared invariants (the survivors)

Structures both reach from different starting points. (An invariant here is what stays fixed:
a self that persists, a here that localises, structure that survives every viewpoint.)

- **Identity is recursive persistence of a pattern, not a substance.** Vibe: a self is a
  pattern that keeps re-recovering itself under the arrow ([`E-SLF-0002`](../../test/experiment/selves/active-persistence.ts)), and its record
  holds its shape ([`E-SLF-0162`](../../test/experiment/selves/classical-record-persistence.ts)). McCard: identity is "the recursive persistence of
  constraint-patterns through transformation", the Ship of Theseus reframed as preserved
  organisation. The same definition of a self, one measured and one argued.
- **A minimal self is a localised here that recurs.** Vibe: a self individuates a stable
  centre out of the field ([`E-SLF-0027`](../../test/experiment/selves/coarse-individuality-transition.ts), [`E-SLF-0007`](../../test/experiment/selves/autonomous-self.ts)). McCard: the minimal self is the
  structural invariant produced when coherent registration recurs, "a here from which
  experience is given", like a centre of gravity, not a thing.
- **Choice is determined yet irreducible.** Vibe: an outcome is fixed by the dynamics yet
  cannot be reduced to a smaller description of it ([`E-SLF-0020`](../../test/experiment/selves/choice-determined-yet-irreducible.ts)). McCard: freedom is the
  wanting formed now in the "spacious present", one act felt from within and redescribed from
  without as a causal chain, giving up "could have done otherwise with a fixed past". Both
  place authorship inside the dynamics rather than outside it.
- **Physical order as what no perspective can vary alone.** Vibe: coarse structure emerges as
  what survives coarse-graining, a causal level that is real because it is invariant
  ([`E-SLF-0019`](../../test/experiment/selves/causal-emergence.ts)). McCard: his construction debt is answered by "jointly constituted
  invariants", objective structure as the fixed point of what no single perspective can move.
  The same proposal, which vibe already has partial results for.

## Divergences

- **First-personality posited vs derived.** McCard makes first-personality primitive, an
  intrinsic-nature posit in the Russellian family, and is careful it is a posit rather than a
  dissolution of the hard problem. Vibe requires the structures of a self to be derived on a
  discrete substrate, not taken as primitive. Where he stops at "it is primitive", vibe asks
  for the construction.
- **Continuous vs discrete.** His registering activity is described as a continuous field with
  a formally apophatic ceiling. Vibe's base is a discrete ternary mesh, with continuity only
  emergent under coarse-graining, so vibe reads his field as a coarse-grained face rather than
  the ground.
- **No mechanism for the ladder.** His coherence-to-identity ladder is argued, not run. Vibe
  gives each rung an experiment that could have failed. The construction debt he names openly,
  jointly constituted invariants, is exactly the corner vibe tries to build rather than assert.
