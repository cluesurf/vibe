# Vibe to Cosmopsychism

Cosmopsychism (Philip Goff) starts from one premise, that consciousness is the one thing we
know directly, so it should be the base ("physics tells us what matter does, consciousness
is what matter is"). Its hardest problem is combination (how do micro-experiences fuse into
one mind), and Goff's answer is to reverse the direction: begin with the universe as one
conscious subject and get individual minds by decomposition, not by building up. Vibe reaches
the same one-field-many-selves picture by cutting selves out of a single conserved field, and
it turns the combination problem into a measurable transition rather than a standing puzzle.
This sheet derives the shared structure from vibe. 6 experiments.

## Shared invariants (the survivors)

Structures both reach from different starting points. (An invariant here is what stays
fixed: the base substance, the direction selves are made, a definite whole that binds.)

- **Experience is the one base, matter is downstream.** Vibe: the one substance is the
  vibe, and physics is grown underneath it rather than producing it. Goff: the intrinsic
  nature of physical structure just is experience (Russellian monism), so there is one
  stuff, and physics only ever describes what it does. The same base bet, argued by a
  mainstream philosopher where vibe assumes it.
- **Many selves from one field, by decomposition.** Vibe: a self is a local gathering of
  the one field, never an assembly of separate minds, and the combination-to-a-subject step
  is a measured transition ([`E-SLF-0163`](../../test/experiment/selves/combination-transition.ts), [`E-SLF-0027`](../../test/experiment/selves/coarse-individuality-transition.ts)). Goff: the cosmos is the one
  conscious subject and individuals are segments carved from it. Same direction, one-from-many
  refused in favour of many-from-one.
- **A composite whole is a real level, not a sum of parts.** Vibe: a composite self is its
  own level with its own closure ([`E-SLF-0033`](../../test/experiment/selves/composite-self-level.ts)). Goff: the unified subject is fundamental
  and the parts are derivative, which is the same priority of whole over part.
- **Integration is what makes the one subject.** Vibe: binding parts into one whole is a
  real, breakable achievement ([`E-SLF-0013`](../../test/experiment/selves/binding-improves-model.ts)). Goff: the combination problem is exactly the
  question of how parts bind into one experience, which he leaves open where vibe measures it.

## Divergences

- **No physics, no substrate, the big one.** Goff supplies no lattice, no rule, no beat, and
  derives no physical law. His base is characterised philosophically (intrinsic nature,
  subject, quality) and is structurally empty. Vibe's entire lower half has no counterpart in
  him and cannot be lifted from him.
- **Combination left open vs dissolved.** Goff grants a new primitive may be needed to make
  mental combination work. Vibe makes combination the wrong operation from the start, since
  selves are cut from one field, and hands over a mechanism (Markov-blanket gatherings), so
  the problem becomes a transition to measure rather than a debt to pay.
- **Cosmic purpose.** Goff's later work writes teleology into the cosmos (fine-tuning to a
  "Godish" ground, drifting toward classical theism). Vibe's base is reversible, conserving,
  and blank, with any arrow or value emergent bookkeeping high in the self layer, never
  written into the law. This fork is total and should be stated plainly, not smoothed over.
