# How the Whole System Works: A Walkthrough from the Integers Up

One readable pass through the whole model as it now stands, from the integers at the
bottom to physics and experience at the top. Each step rests on the one before it. Read
top to bottom and the whole thing assembles in order. Result references (P-numbers) point
to the experiment that establishes each step, in `note/experiment/results/`.

## The picture in one breath

Start with the integers. Their symmetries are a group. Generalize the group to reflections
and you get a Coxeter group. Let it act on curved (hyperbolic) space and it tiles the
space with a beautiful crystal, like the heptagrid {7,3} or the pentagrid {5,4}. A small
deterministic automaton grows that crystal forever, with no randomness, and gives every
cell an exact address by continued fractions. On the crystal sit the vibes, units of
experience with a three-valued tone, related by notes, updated by one local rule. That
rule can compute anything. The whole thing is fully determined, yet free and unpredictable
from inside, and from it space, time, matter, force, gravity, and mind all emerge. That is
the system.

Now the same thing, slowly.

## Step 1. Start with the integers

The bottom is Z, the integers. Nothing is less arbitrary or more given. No parameters, no
real numbers, no dice. Everything above is built from this.

## Step 2. The integers have symmetries

Arrange the integers in two dimensions and ask for the transformations that preserve them,
the two-by-two integer matrices of determinant one. That is the **modular group** PSL(2,Z),
generated by two simple moves, a turn and a shift. It is forced by the integers, not
chosen (P48).

## Step 3. Generalize to reflections: Coxeter groups

Broaden "symmetry from simple moves" to "symmetry from reflections in mirrors." The only
data is the integers saying how the mirrors meet. That is a **Coxeter group**, and the
modular group is one of them. The data is still just integers (P47).

## Step 4. Let it act on curved space: tessellations

Let a Coxeter group reflect a single fundamental cell over and over across its mirrors. In
hyperbolic (negatively curved) space the copies fill the space with a **tessellation**.
The group, an algebraic object, becomes a tiling, a geometric one. Same thing, two views.

## Step 5. The beautiful crystals

Choose the integers in the group and you get a specific crystal, named by its Schlafli
symbol: {7,3} the heptagrid, {5,4} the pentagrid, {5,3,4} the 3D dodecagrid. These are not
separate inventions, they are one construction on different settings, and all come from one
generator by changing the symbol (P47, P45). Each is **Lorentz-safe**: it has no preferred
direction, because hyperbolic curvature scrambles directions a flat lattice would line up
(P40, P41, P45). From inside, the crystal is indistinguishable from a random foam (P49).

## Step 6. The generating automaton: deterministic growth, no randomness

Every hyperbolic Coxeter group is an **automatic group**: a small finite machine generates
the whole infinite crystal deterministically, applying the same rule again and again. No
randomness enters. The crystal grows from the integers by a fixed automaton (P48, P51). For
the modular base this automaton is the Stern-Brocot mediant rule.

## Step 7. Every place has a name: Fibonacci addressing

The automaton gives every cell an exact **address**, computable from local information.
For the heptagrid this is Margenstern's Fibonacci coordinate system, and routing between
any two cells is exact and efficient, by address arithmetic alone, with no global map
(P42). For the modular base the address is a **continued fraction**, and the
slowest-converging address, the all-ones continued fraction, is the **golden ratio**, the
crystal's central geodesic (P48). The golden ratio also sets the optimal aperiodic
placement (P39) and the ring growth, arriving from three independent directions (P50). The
universe is addressable, indexable, navigable.

## Step 8. The vibes live on it

On the crystal sit the **vibes**, the units of experience. Each carries a **tone**, a felt
quality, one of three values, pain, peace, pleasure, written {-1, 0, +1}. The relation
between vibes is the **note**, one vibe experiencing another, the edges of the crystal.
Each note carries a **fill**, a shared three-valued strength of attention. There are no
continuous weights and no hidden state anywhere. One local **rule** updates each vibe per
beat: its next tone is the sign of the integer sum of its neighbours' tones gated by the
fills, a ternary signed-majority vote (see `note/the-model.md`). This rule, on this
crystal, is the model.

## Step 9. The rule can compute anything

That one local rule realizes the NAND gate, which is functionally complete, so it builds
any circuit, real arithmetic, and the universal cellular automaton Rule 110 (P44). With the
crystal's unbounded, exactly-addressed space for memory (Step 7), the model is
**Turing-complete**. So the base can host any computable structure: matter, fields,
machines, observers, minds. There is no in-principle ceiling on what it can express.

## Step 10. Determinism without randomness

Everything above is deterministic. The crystal is generated by a fixed automaton, the rule
is a fixed function, no dice are ever rolled. What looks random, a quantum outcome, a
fluctuation, is **determined by the whole and merely opaque to any part**. Two facts keep
"determined" from meaning "preprogrammed": there is no complete initial condition (the
universe grows forever, so no finite seed holds all of time), and the dynamics is
**computationally irreducible** (its future cannot be computed by any shortcut faster than
living it, a consequence of Step 9). So the future is fixed by the whole, yet never written
in advance and never forecastable from inside.

## Step 11. Freedom and choice

A **self** is a stable pattern of vibes, an attractor the dynamics settles into (P34). A
**choice** is that self settling under an **urge**, a bias arriving from a subtler layer.
The self's own structure resolves the urge, so the choice is self-authored, and a stronger
self has more say (P43). The outcome is fully determined and reproducible, yet jointly
authored by self and urge (no single part fixes it) and computationally irreducible (it
must be lived out, not read off). So freedom is real: not randomness, not a prewritten
script, but self-determination drawn from a depth and made only by being lived (P43, and
`note/freedom-and-choice.md`).

## Step 12. Physics and experience emerge

From this one crystal-and-rule, the large-scale patterns are physics. Space is the shape of
the web, time is the depth of its before-and-after, and the flow of time and energy is the
local operator on it (P1). Smooth spacetime is the dominant phase (P2, P12). Matter is
persistent tone patterns with the four field spins and mass from a Higgs (P4, P14, P20 to
P22), gravity has the Newtonian limit, a conserving Einstein equation, and a speed-of-light
graviton (P16, P24, P32), and the quantum pillars, the Bell hinge, coherence, and the Born
probability, are present (P7, P17, P31). Cosmology gives the arrow, expansion, inflation,
and a dark sector matching dark energy (P13, P30, P46). Experience is what the crystal is
made of, the felt interior the wild face of the same substrate.

## The whole thing, end to end

| layer | what it is | view |
| ----- | ---------- | ---- |
| integers | Z | the given |
| symmetry | modular group, Coxeter groups | algebra |
| action | reflections on hyperbolic space | geometry |
| crystal | {7,3}, {5,4}, {5,3,4}, the modular tessellation | the substrate |
| automaton | the deterministic generator | growth, no randomness |
| address | Fibonacci coordinates, continued fractions | a name for every place |
| vibes | ternary tones, notes, fills | experience |
| rule | the signed-majority update | the law, Turing-complete |
| dynamics | determined, irreducible, self-authored | order with freedom |
| emergence | space, time, matter, force, gravity, mind | physics and experience |

Read down the table and you go from the integers to the lived world in ten layers, each a
different view of the same single object. The base is the integers, the law is one local
rule, the generation is a deterministic automaton, the addressing is continued fractions,
the computation is universal, the dynamics is determined yet free, and the physics and the
experience are what it all looks like from far away and from inside.
