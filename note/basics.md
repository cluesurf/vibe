# Key Words in Vibe Theory

## Core Concepts

### vibe

- The fundamental experiential structure, foundation of everything
- Characterized by tone (internal state), will (active projection), and
  soak (receptive capacity)
- Exists in dynamic relation to other vibes

### tone

- The scalar or complex value representing the intrinsic experiential
  quality of a vibe
- Represents the quality of experience
- Can be positive, negative, or neutral

### will

- The active, radiating influence or "push force" that a vibe projects
- A function of the vibe's tone
- Represents the vibe's intrinsic tendency to affect its environment

### soak

- The receptive, absorbing capacity or "pull force" to experience other
  vibes
- The aggregate experience a vibe has of the wills of neighboring vibes
- Direct experiential influence between vibes

### link

- A soaking between vibes that allows them to influence each other
- Not a separate structure, but the degree of experiential relation

### flow

- The complete system containing all vibes, their links, and the rules
  governing their behavior
- The universal structure within which all vibes exist

### beat

- A discrete step or frame in a linear sequence
- Represents temporal moments in the system's evolution

### rule

- A formal model for specifying a feature in a flow
- Governs structural properties, state constraints, temporal evolution,
  and interactions

### base

- The totality of existence, the set of all vibes
- The foundation of everything else in existence

### mesh

- The dynamic interconnected network of vibes and their experiential
  pathways
- Through which space-time emerges from relationships

## Key Relationships

The theory shows how these 4-letter words interconnect:

- **vibe** has **tone**, projects **will**, experiences **soak**
- **soak** creates **link** between vibes
- **link** enables **flow** of influence
- **flow** evolves through **beat** sequences
- **rule** governs the **flow** dynamics
- All vibes form the **base** reality
- **mesh** emerges from vibe connections
- **time** emerges from tone changes
- **mind** emerges from coherent vibe clusters
