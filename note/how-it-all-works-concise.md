# How the Whole System Works: The Short Version

One quick pass through the whole model, from the integers at the bottom to physics and
experience at the top. Each step rests on the one before it. For the full, gap-filling
treatment see `note/how-it-all-works.md`. Result references (P-numbers) are in
`note/experiment/results/`.

## The picture in one breath

Start with the integers. Their symmetries are a group. Generalize the group to reflections
and you get a Coxeter group. Let it act on curved (hyperbolic) space and it tiles the space
with a beautiful crystal, like the heptagrid {7,3} or the pentagrid {5,4}. A small
deterministic automaton grows that crystal forever, with no randomness, and gives every cell
an exact address by continued fractions. On the crystal sit the vibes, units of experience
with a three-valued tone, related by notes, updated by one local rule. That rule can compute
anything. The whole thing is fully determined, yet free and unpredictable from inside, and
from it space, time, matter, force, gravity, and mind all emerge. That is the system.

## The ladder

1. **Integers.** Start with Z, the least arbitrary thing there is, no free parameters.
2. **Symmetries: the modular group.** The symmetries of the integers form the modular group
   PSL(2,Z), generated by two simple moves. Forced, not chosen (P48).
3. **Reflections: Coxeter groups.** Generalize symmetry to reflections in mirrors (a
   kaleidoscope). The data is still integers, the angles between the mirrors. The modular
   group is one of these (P47).
4. **Action on curved space: a tessellation.** Reflect one tile across its mirrors over and
   over in hyperbolic (saddle-shaped, ever-opening) space, and the copies fill the space with
   a tiling.
5. **The crystals.** Choose the integers and you get a named crystal: {7,3} the heptagrid,
   {5,4} the pentagrid, {5,3,4} the 3D dodecagrid. All from one generator (P47, P45). Each is
   **Lorentz-safe**: no preferred direction, because hyperbolic curvature scrambles the
   directions a flat lattice would line up (P40, P41, P45). From inside it is indistinguishable
   from a random foam (P49), and it is tree-like and hierarchical (P49).
6. **The automaton.** A small finite machine grows the infinite crystal deterministically, no
   randomness. For the modular base it is the Stern-Brocot mediant rule (P48, P51).
7. **Addressing.** Every cell has an exact, locally computable name: Margenstern's Fibonacci
   coordinates, with exact routing between any two cells (P42). For the modular base the
   address is a continued fraction, and its central geodesic is the **golden ratio**, the same
   constant the sunflower placement and the ring growth use, arriving from three independent
   directions (P39, P48, P50).
8. **The vibes.** On the crystal sit the vibes, units of experience. Each has a **tone**
   (felt quality, one of -1, 0, +1, pain, peace, pleasure). The relation is the **note**, one
   vibe experiencing another. Each note carries a ternary **fill** (attention). No continuous
   weights, no hidden state (see `note/the-model.md`).
9. **The rule.** One local law, run in beats: a vibe's next tone is the sign of the sum of its
   neighbors' tones gated by the fills, a ternary signed-majority vote. Asynchronous, no master
   clock.
10. **Universality.** That one rule realizes NAND, builds an adder, and expresses the universal
    Rule 110, so with the addressable crystal it is **Turing-complete** and can host any
    computable structure (P44).
11. **Determinism without randomness.** Everything is determined, no dice. But not
    preprogrammed: there is no complete starting state (the universe grows forever) and the
    dynamics is computationally irreducible (you must run it to know it). What looks random is
    determined by the whole and opaque to the part.
12. **Freedom and choice.** A self is a stable attractor (P34). A choice is the self settling
    under an urge from a subtler layer. It is determined and reproducible, yet self-authored
    (the same urge gives different selves different outcomes), agency grows with the self's
    depth, and it is irreducible. Determined, not random, not predetermined by any part (P43).
    Picture it as: you are a landscape, the urge is a tilt, the choice is the rolling.
13. **What emerges.** Space is the shape of the web (P5, P52), time its before-and-after depth
    and the emergent local Hamiltonian (P1), the arrow its one-way growth (P13). Matter is
    persistent tone patterns with the four field spins and mass from a Higgs (P4, P14, P20 to
    P22), forces are the twist across a note (P8), gravity gives Newton, a conserving Einstein
    equation, a speed-of-light graviton, singularity resolution, and the black-hole area law
    (P16, P24, P32, P28, P33). The quantum pillars (interference, the Bell hinge, the Born
    probability) are present (P7, P17, P31). Cosmology gives the arrow, expansion, inflation,
    dark energy at the right magnitude, and a dark-matter mechanism (P13, P30, P46, P18).
    Experience is what the crystal is made of, the felt interior the wild face of the same
    substrate.

## The whole thing, end to end

| layer | what it is | view |
| ----- | ---------- | ---- |
| integers | Z | the given |
| symmetry | modular group, Coxeter groups | algebra |
| action | reflections on hyperbolic space | geometry |
| crystal | {7,3}, {5,4}, {5,3,4}, the modular tessellation | the substrate |
| automaton | the deterministic generator | growth, no randomness |
| address | Fibonacci coordinates, continued fractions | a name for every place |
| vibes | ternary tones, notes, fills | experience |
| rule | the signed-majority update | the law, Turing-complete |
| dynamics | determined, irreducible, self-authored | order with freedom |
| emergence | space, time, matter, force, gravity, mind | physics and experience |

Read down the table and you go from the integers to the lived world in ten layers, each a
different view of the same single object. The base is the integers, the law is one local rule,
the generation is a deterministic automaton, the addressing is continued fractions, the
computation is universal, the dynamics is determined yet free, and the physics and the
experience are what it all looks like from far away and from inside.
