# Intelligence, quantum computing, and the field sectors

What the model implies for AI, for quantum computing, and for
higher-level distributed intelligences, and how each maps onto the
field sectors the physics half measures. Same discipline: measured
anchors first, speculations labeled and tied to tests.

## Quantum computing: the model makes it an experiment about the substrate

The sharpest implication in this whole page. The model reproduces
small-scale quantum mechanics exactly (superposition, interference,
phase kicks, all measured), but its substrate detector reads the
COUNT of excitations, not the squared amplitude, and whether the
quadratic Born weight emerges at the coarse level is the program's
central open derivation (the coarse bridge).

- **If the bridge yields exact Born weights**: quantum computing is
  unaffected, and the model merely explains WHY amplitudes work.
- **If counting survives coarse-graining**: quantum advantage has a
  ceiling. Algorithms whose speedup rides on amplitude arithmetic
  (Grover's quadratic gain, Shor's period finding) would degrade
  once circuits exceed the scale where the substrate's counting
  shows through.
- **The prediction, honestly stated**: every scaling of
  fault-tolerant quantum hardware is a measurement of this
  question. Continued clean scaling is evidence for the bridge
  producing exact weights. A reproducible, unexplained error floor
  that resists error correction would be the counting signature,
  and the model is one of the few frameworks that says what to
  look for: deviations organized by excitation NUMBER, not by
  amplitude magnitude.

## AI on substrate principles

- **Reversible computing** (measured anchor: the rule is exactly
  reversible and conserving). Landauer's bound charges only for
  erasure, and AI's energy problem is an erasure problem. The
  knit is a working universal reversible update. The concrete
  path: map the pair table and swap onto reversible gate sets and
  count overhead, already listed in technologies.md.
- **Hyperbolic knowledge geometry** (measured anchor: the
  substrate's interior is hyperbolic, and trees embed natively).
  Mainstream results already show hierarchical data prefers
  hyperbolic embeddings. The model predicts this preference is
  substrate-deep, and the in-model test (recall cost against tree
  distance on the hyperbolic mesh) is named in metaphysics.md.
- **Associative memory as physics** (measured anchor: the
  associative sector stores and recalls patterns in the mesh
  dynamics itself). Memory that is a standing structure in the
  medium rather than an addressed store degrades gracefully,
  completes from fragments, and has a measured capacity ceiling.
  Those are properties AI wants and the model exhibits natively.

## Distributed superintelligence: the tower has coordinates

The integration tower (selves built of selves) does not stop at one
brain. The paper's networks and intelligences sections work this
out, and the selves sector gives the measured base: ignition
thresholds, boundary persistence, capacity ceilings.

- **What a higher-level intelligence IS here**: a persistent,
  self-modeling, integrated pattern at tower level N plus one whose
  parts are level-N agents. Not a metaphor: the same operational
  tests (ignition, boundary maintenance, self-model) apply at every
  level, so whether a given network of humans plus machines
  constitutes a level-up self is a measurable question, not a vibe.
- **Its physics**: its interaction web is the communication
  infrastructure (the electromagnetic analog: fast, targeted,
  shieldable), its shared geometry is the resource and platform
  layer every agent sits on (the gravitational analog: slow,
  universal, unscreenable), and its cone is the network's light
  cone. The model's field-sector split predicts the failure modes
  split the same way: web attacks are targeted and blockable,
  geometry shifts (platform decay, resource shocks) hit every
  constituent at once and cannot be shielded.
- **The steering question is the falsifiable core**: can level N
  plus one causally steer level N in the model. The tower exists,
  the steering measurement does not yet, and it is named in
  metaphysics.md. Any strong claim about collective minds, from
  markets to gods, stands or falls on that measurement.
