# Metaphysics with coordinates

The conscious-substrate paper
([text/0010-conscious-substrate](../../text/0010-conscious-substrate/))
worked these ideas out at length: realms, navigation, stored patterns,
the ladder of integration. This page pulls its threads into testable
form. The paper's one governing move is kept everywhere: nothing here
is a second substance or an exception to the law. Every structure
below is made of the same tones and run by the same rule, or it is
not in the model.

## The realm coordinates

The paper's claim (its Realms section): a realm is not a separate
place but a REGION of the one bulk, fixed by a few axes: tone (pain
to peace to pleasure), depth (how far off the physical surface), and
integration (how high on the coarse-graining tower), plus persistence
and whether the pattern is live, stored, or recurring. Every
traditional map (heavens and hells, ladders of planes, ancestors and
ghosts) is a slice of that one small space along the axes a practice
made vivid.

- **In-model anchors, today**: the tone axis is literal (the ternary
  alphabet), the integration tower is the selves and coarse-graining
  machinery, live-versus-stored is the difference between a beating
  pattern and a domain-wall record, and persistence is measured
  (self-emergence, the recordability ceiling).
- **What to dig into**: give the realm coordinates an operational
  definition per axis and MEASURE a small system's trajectory
  through realm space. That would turn the paper's map into an
  instrument.

## Heaven and hell as tone weather

The paper's two poles: a region of sustained high tone is a heaven, a
region of sustained low tone is a hell, and the conservation law ties
them, so no heaven is funded except against a hell somewhere in the
books. Not two rooms but countless bright and dark clouds of every
size, a weather of feeling.

- **In-model anchor**: total tone IS exactly conserved (measured at
  zero drift in every experiment that checks). The funding statement
  is a theorem-shaped claim on the model: any sustained positive
  region requires a matching negative elsewhere.
- **Testable in-model now**: build a sustained high-tone region and
  measure where its negative partner lives, how far it can be
  displaced, and what maintaining the imbalance costs. That is the
  thermodynamics of heavens, computable.
- **What it forbids**: a heaven from outside the law, or one with no
  cost. The conservation books always balance.

## Tree navigation in mental space, and the tree of knowledge

The substrate is hyperbolic at depth, and hyperbolic space IS the
geometry of trees: exponential room, so every branching taxonomy
embeds without crowding. The library already holds the pieces: tree
addressing with logarithmic cost, navigation experiments, and
associative memory over the mesh.

- **The reading**: a mind navigating its own conceptual space walks a
  tree embedded in hyperbolic interior. The felt structure of
  knowledge (broad categories branching into fine ones, the sense of
  ascending to overview or descending to detail) matches hyperbolic
  navigation, where depth is literal and overview is the boundary
  direction.
- **Independent support**: mainstream machine-learning work finds
  hierarchical data embeds better in hyperbolic space than
  Euclidean, and neuroscience finds hyperbolic geometry in
  hippocampal representation. The model predicts this is not a
  trick but the native geometry of stored structure.
- **What to dig into**: an associative memory experiment ON the
  hyperbolic substrate (the current ones run on flat meshes),
  measuring whether recall cost tracks tree distance. A clean
  in-model question with an external echo.

## Storing experiences for later entities

The paper's live-stored-recurring triad, plus the all-record: the
boundary holds the whole interior's information, recoverable in
principle, nothing finally lost.

- **In-model anchors**: records are physical (walls, domains),
  capacity is bounded (the recordability ceiling), reversibility
  conserves everything, and the holography sector measures
  boundary-interior correspondence.
- **The honest split**: a stored pattern is retrievable structure,
  and a later integrated system CAN in principle encounter it (read
  the domain structure a predecessor wrote). What the model does not
  provide: the stored pattern experiencing anything while stored.
  Live patterns integrate, stored ones wait.
- **Testable in-model now**: write a pattern into domain structure,
  disperse the writer, and measure what a later reader can recover,
  at what fidelity, after how much churn. That is the archaeology
  of experience, as an experiment. The associative sector is most
  of the machinery already.

## Gods, demons, and agencies at other scales

The integration tower does not stop at human scale, and the paper's
axes allow persistent, self-modeling, highly integrated patterns at
depths and sizes the surface never shows (there is vastly more bulk
than cusp).

- **What the model permits**: selves at other scales and depths,
  including ones spread over many surface-scale selves (the
  networks and intelligences sections of the paper). A tradition's
  god or demon would be a persistent deep pattern with strong tone
  and high integration, encountered from below.
- **What the model forbids**: any such being standing outside the
  law. Whatever exists in the bulk is tones under the one rule,
  bound by the cone, the conservation books, and the capacity
  ceilings, like us.
- **What to dig into**: whether integration at scale N plus one can
  causally steer scale N in the model (the selves tower gives the
  levels, the steering measurement does not exist yet). That is
  the falsifiable core of every strong claim in this family.
