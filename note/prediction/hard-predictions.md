# Hard predictions

Each entry is a call that could kill the model. That is the point. A
theory that cannot lose is not saying anything.

## Things that can never be observed

- **No free magnetic monopole, ever.** The magnetic field here is an
  exact lattice identity (div B = 0 to one part in a trillion,
  measured). A single confirmed monopole detection falsifies the
  model outright.
- **The strong-CP electric dipole moment is exactly zero.** The
  vacuum-angle contribution to the neutron's electric dipole moment
  has no home in the model: there is no theta parameter to tune. Any
  confirmed nonzero theta-EDM kills it.
- **CPT is never violated.** The committed rule's CPT is exact by
  measurement (zero mismatches under pure charge conjugation with
  time reversal). Any confirmed CPT violation, at any precision,
  kills it. Nature currently bounds CPT violation ferociously, so
  the model stands with the strictest experimental tradition here.
- **No signal outruns the cone.** One tone moves one D4 step per
  beat. Locality is exact, not approximate. Any confirmed
  faster-than-light signaling falsifies the model (and much else).

## Numbers staked

- **The Cabibbo angle within four percent**, from honeycomb growth
  geometry with zero adjustable numbers. Already measured in the
  suite. A better derivation should close the four percent, and a
  future re-measurement that WIDENS the gap is evidence against.
- **The breather carries exactly one half lattice energy unit.** The
  identification of the model's Planck constant from the unit-kick
  law (hbar = 3 over 2 pi) predicts this as its consistency
  companion. An independent energy measure on the model that
  disagrees breaks the identification (E-FLD-0019).
- **The viscosity floor.** The model's gas sits at about eight times
  the KSS bound in its own kick-law units. If a coarse-grained or
  denser regime of the same rule ever lands BELOW the bound, the
  hbar identification or the bound conjecture is wrong, and either
  outcome is publishable.
- **Wall quanta come in whole sheets.** Domain-wall content is
  quantized in units of six times side cubed (static sector) and
  whole side-cubed sheets (turning sector), never fractions. Any
  regime producing fractional wall content breaks the design law.

## Structural predictions awaiting a lab mapping

- **Mass is oscillation.** Every massive species at rest ticks at an
  exact internal frequency (the breather's period six, twice the
  vacuum clock). The lab counterpart is the de Broglie internal
  clock, for which real experiments exist (electron channeling
  resonance). The model predicts such internal-clock signatures are
  universal for massive particles, not peculiar to electrons.
- **Phase kicks are integer.** A particle crossing a wall picks up
  exactly one clock unit of phase or exactly none, never a fraction.
  Coarse-grained, this becomes the quantization of geometric phase.
  A system exhibiting stable FRACTIONAL phase kicks at the
  substrate-analog level would falsify the mechanism.
- **Protected species exist.** Under the committed turning schedule a
  small number of modes interact with almost nothing while still
  propagating (their motion never meets the interaction window). The
  natural mapping is the neutrino sector. The model predicts
  protection is kinematic (a scheduling fact), not a new charge, so
  it can be broken by the right medium, which is testable in-model
  now and in principle distinguishes it from charge-based sterility.
