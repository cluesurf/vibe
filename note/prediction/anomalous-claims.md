# Anomalous claims, assessed inside the model

The claims people actually ask about: telepathy, psychic
teleportation, intuitive resonance, and their kin. The house rules
apply here with extra force. For each claim: what it would require
physically, what the model permits or forbids, the clean test, and
the verdict. The model is deterministic, local, and reversible, so
it forbids more than it permits, and a forbidding is a prediction.

## Psychic teleportation (matter or information without traversal)

- **Would require**: state appearing at a distant location without a
  path through the intervening cells, or a signal outrunning the
  cone.
- **The model says**: FORBIDDEN. One tone moves one step per beat,
  locality is exact, and the six couples plus the turning schedule
  give no nonlocal channel. The one geometry-side channel (the
  active map, growth) also propagates locally. There is no
  wormhole shortcut in the committed rule: earlier wormhole
  experiments lived on non-committed geometries and did not carry.
- **Clean test**: any pre-registered demonstration of
  faster-than-light signaling. None exists after a century of
  looking, which is the model's prediction continuing to hold.
- **Verdict**: the model bets against it, flatly. This is a hard
  prediction, not an open question.

## Telepathy (mind-to-mind transfer without known channel)

- **Would require**: either an unknown physical channel between
  nervous systems, or correlation mistaken for communication.
- **The model says**: no new channel. Every interaction runs through
  the connected interaction web (the electromagnetic-like sector) or
  through shared geometry. But the model gives the second reading
  real teeth: two regions born of the same growth domain share a
  clock phase FOREVER, because a reversible universe cannot forget.
  Correlation from shared history, with no communication, is a
  measured, exact effect here. Experiences of "knowing together"
  would be common-cause correlation, the same structure Bell tests
  distinguish from signaling.
- **Clean test**: paired-subject experiments where the claimed
  transfer is separated from every common cause (shielding, timing
  isolation, pre-registered analysis). Decades of such tests have
  found nothing above chance once common causes are controlled,
  which matches the model's account: remove the shared cause and
  the correlation goes.
- **Verdict**: transfer, no. Shared-history correlation, yes, and
  the model says exactly where it comes from and when it must
  vanish. What to dig into: the measured-shared-past machinery, and
  whether its correlation structure matches the phenomenology
  people report.

## Electromagnetic intuitive resonance (weak-field coupling between organisms)

- **Would require**: nervous systems coupling through ordinary
  electromagnetic fields at very low amplitude.
- **The model says**: PERMITTED in kind, questionable in magnitude.
  This one is not exotic at all: it is ordinary physics, and the
  model contains the ordinary carrier sector. Real laboratories
  measure brain fields at femtotesla scales and cardiac fields
  further out. Whether another nervous system can DETECT such
  fields above its own noise floor is a signal-to-noise question,
  not a mechanism question.
- **Clean test**: entrainment experiments with shielding as the
  control: if a claimed resonance survives a mu-metal room, it is
  not electromagnetic, and the model then predicts it is common
  cause or artifact. Cryptochrome-based magnetoreception in birds
  shows biology CAN detect fields near noise floors, so the
  magnitude question deserves real measurement rather than
  dismissal.
- **Verdict**: worth testing properly, with the shield as the
  knife. The model adds one specific expectation: any real
  coupling must ride the ordinary carrier sector, so it shields,
  attenuates, and delays like electromagnetism, with no residue.

## Precognition and premonition

- **Would require**: information flowing from future to past.
- **The model says**: FORBIDDEN as signaling. The rule is
  deterministic and time-symmetric in the CPT sense, but the
  thermodynamic arrow (growth) orders records strictly: a record
  exists only of what is in its past cone. What the model DOES
  permit: a deterministic universe makes the future in-principle
  computable from the present, so successful anticipation is
  modeling skill, and the phenomenology of intuition (fast,
  unconscious prediction) needs no new physics.
- **Clean test**: pre-registered prediction of genuinely causally
  disconnected events. The model bets no such protocol ever
  succeeds above chance.
- **Verdict**: against, as physics. For, as unconscious inference,
  which is a neuroscience question.

## Life after death, the soul, and what reversibility actually buys

- **The model says, and this is exact**: nothing is ever erased.
  The rule is reversible, the echo returns bit for bit, so the full
  microstate of every life remains encoded in the universe's state
  forever, dispersed but not destroyed. That is a theorem of the
  model, not a comfort. What the model does NOT say: that the
  dispersed record constitutes experience, or can be gathered
  again. Integration (the selves sector) is what experiences, and
  integration is exactly what dispersal removes.
- **Clean test**: none. This is interpretation, labeled as such.
- **Verdict**: the model offers conservation of record, not
  survival of experience, and honesty requires saying both halves.

## The general rule for any claim not listed

State it as a channel. Ask which sector carries it (the interaction
web, or geometry), what its cone is, and what shields it. If the
claim needs no channel, it is correlation, and then the shared-past
machinery says what correlations are possible. If it needs a channel
faster than the cone, the model bets against it with its life.
