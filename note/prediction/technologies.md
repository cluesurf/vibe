# Technologies

What could be engineered from the model's mechanisms. Ordered from
buildable-today to contingent-on-the-big-claim. Each entry names the
mechanism, the artifact, and the first concrete step.

## Buildable today (no belief required)

- **The deterministic quantum laboratory.** Every interference,
  collapse, Bell, and decoherence result in the suite reruns bit for
  bit on a laptop. No physics lab can offer that. The artifact: a
  teaching and research instrument where a student SETS the hidden
  state and watches the Born-rule statistics emerge, and a researcher
  tests foundations proposals (objective collapse, superdeterminism,
  retrocausality) against a system whose ground truth is inspectable.
  First step: package the suite's quantum sector as a standalone
  notebook-friendly library.
- **Reversible-computing patterns.** The rule is exactly reversible
  and conserving, and the suite holds working associative memory,
  routing, and computation experiments on it. Reversible logic is the
  known route past the Landauer energy floor. The artifact: circuit
  patterns (the knit as a universal reversible gate family) for
  adiabatic and superconducting reversible hardware. First step: map
  the pair table and swap clause onto a standard reversible gate set
  and count the overhead against Toffoli-based designs.
- **The control-gated method itself.** Every claim here is gated by a
  negative control that could say no, with corrections published in
  place, and the whole record is machine-checkable. The artifact: the
  methodology, exported. Any AI-assisted research group can adopt the
  window rule, the group-sweep rule, and the sampling-phase rule
  today. First step: the method note already exists, publish it
  standalone.

## Near-term, mechanism-driven

- **Perfect-fluid design.** The gas's viscosity-to-entropy ratio
  lands in the graphene band with nothing tuned (E-FLD-0019),
  because momentum diffuses through counter-moving pair rotation.
  The artifact: lattice-gas-inspired designs for electron-fluid
  devices (viscous Dirac-fluid channels, vorticity-based signal
  processing) prototyped in the model before fabrication in
  graphene. First step: reproduce a published graphene Poiseuille
  profile in-model, then vary geometry in-model to predict an
  unpublished one, then ask an experimental group to check it.
- **Clock-domain engineering.** Growth writes phase domains, walls
  between domains are quantized surface objects with an exact period,
  and detectors are just walls two beats out of step. The artifact:
  in any medium with a condensate phase (superconductors, cold
  atoms, time crystals), the model gives a design language for
  domain-wall memories and phase-class detectors. The time-crystal
  mapping is the sharpest: the model's vacuum IS a time crystal, and
  its wall physics predicts which defect patterns are stable. First
  step: map the commensurability law onto driven time-crystal
  experiments where drive period plays the role of birth offset.
- **Protected channels.** A protected species propagates while
  interacting with almost nothing, as a scheduling fact rather than a
  charge. The artifact: decoherence-avoiding channels in engineered
  lattices (photonic or cold-atom), where a mode is protected because
  its group velocity never meets the interaction window, a design
  principle checkable in existing photonic-lattice hardware. First
  step: translate the protection condition (motion commensurate with
  the schedule, never meeting the swap window) into a photonic
  Floquet-lattice condition and simulate it with standard tools.

## Contingent on the big claim

- **Substrate-limit computing.** If space itself is the crystal, the
  ultimate computer is patterned directly in clock domains and
  walls: memory as domain topology, logic as wall collisions, all of
  it reversible. Not buildable, but the model makes the resource
  theory concrete (what a sheet of wall costs, what a kick carries),
  so the theory of such machines can be written now.
- **Gravity-side instruments.** In the model, clock-rate gradients
  ARE the gravitational potential. If the mapping holds, extreme
  clock metrology (optical lattice clocks already resolve
  centimeter-height redshifts) becomes the direct probe of the
  substrate sector, and the model's quantized-wall cosmology (the
  dark-wall candidate) predicts step-like structure in clock-rate
  maps at cosmological scales. First step: state the wall signature
  in observable units once the coarse bridge fixes the lattice scale.
