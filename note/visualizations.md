## Visualization

The base visualization of a vibe is a single constantly radiating
vibration, like a ripple of sound in 3D space, a pulse.

[sound illustration]

But this doesn't capture the full essense of how to imagine it. It is
more complex and rich than that. Especially since the system is
ultimately a network of interacting and somehow nested or subsectioned
vibes.

As such, here are 12 other _aspects_ of the intuition of vibes in a vibe
mesh. These illustrations should not be taken as _exactly_ how the vibe
mesh looks or is structured, they are just aspects, or simplified
visualizations of something more complex. It is important to just use
these as a springboard for your imagination and sense, not taking these
at face value. These are just rough approximations of something very
rich and not fully understood yet.

[8 illustrations, 4 per row]

1. water (8 ripples)
2. cubes (nested, subdivided by 8, nested 3 deep, can discuss)
3. smoke (turbulent fluid dynamics illustration)
4. network (3d graph, cells with links connecting)
5. leaf (voronoi diagram, cells of a leaf)
6. weave (complex branching and joining)
7. web (spider web)
8. tree (just complex branching hierarchical tree, in 3D)

One way to look at it, which serves as a simple exercise for imagining
the system, is to imagine an octree, but not as literal nested boxes,
but as interacting but relational vibrations. The higher level
"containers" have their own experience which is not necessarily the sum
of the parts, and yet the nested boxes have their own self experience as
well. So there is integration and isolation at the same time.

It's hard to imagine it simply as a network of nodes, like a
mathematical graph, because how does a hierarchical graph look in that
case? Graphs are typically imagined as flat balls with links. But if you
attach labels to each graph node, and they are hierarchically organized,
this can also be a way of considering the system somehow.

Other ways to visualize it are as nested fractals, tilings, or
kaleidoscopes.

## Higher-Order Structure

Emergence of higher-level structure can occur through many different
means, many of which haven't currently been elaborated on or even
considered or thought about. These do not depend on central control, but
instead on local rules, pattern reinforcement, and energy exchange
across the system.

- resonance: When vibrations align in phase, they amplify and stabilize
  each other.
- synchrony: Local oscillations fall into rhythm (e.g. fireflies
  blinking, neurons firing).
- interference: When waves or vibes overlap in a reinforcing way.
- attractors: Systems naturally settle into stable patterns or rhythms
  (like strange attractors in chaos theory).
- etc..

These, combined with volition, intention, and motivation, can result in
any possible structure being ultimately evolved.

But the system is limited by rules of the framework, locality of
exchanges, and choices made by each experiential node.

## Memory

How can a human remember past experiences?

One way to structure this is to have nested nodes, each with their own
state (a simple boolean value for example), and each node can contain
arbitrary levels of nesting. Even if each node only experienced positive
or negative at its level, it each nested node could contain a bit of
information. A "recollection", like when a human mentally remembers
something, would be navigating through nested vibes again, walking
through an experiential pattern essentially.

This implies that some deeply nested nodes of some sort would have the
purpose of "storing" past experiences. That is, they go into dormant
mode when not being activated, but when activated, trigger a chain
reaction of walking through associations and experiences again. Figuring
out the exact structure of how such a memory of experiences could form
and be structured is something to investigate.
