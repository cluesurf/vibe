Thomas Nagel – “What Is It Like to Be a Bat?” (1974)
Introduced the irreducibility of subjective experience to physical explanation.

Frank Jackson – “Epiphenomenal Qualia” (1982)
The Mary thought experiment—used by Chalmers and others to argue against physicalism.

David Chalmers – “Facing Up to the Problem of Consciousness” (1995)
Defines the hard problem and positions consciousness as non-reducible.

William Seager – “Consciousness, Information, and Panpsychism” (1995)
Coined the term combination problem; early proponent of modern panpsychism.

Philip Goff – “Why Panpsychism Doesn’t Help with the Hard Problem of Consciousness” (2009)
Discusses the limits of panpsychism and sharpens the formulation of the combination problem.

Hedda Hassel Mørch – “Panpsychism and Mental Causation: A Solution to the Combination Problem” (2014)
Explores how to ground mental causation in panpsychism.

- Composition/Nihilism
- Emergence vs. Reduction
- Quiddities and Russellian Monism

Bertrand Russell – “The Analysis of Matter” (1927)
Early advocate for Russellian Monism, which underpins many modern panpsychist views.

Galen Strawson – “Realistic Monism: Why Physicalism Entails Panpsychism” (2006)
Argues that any consistent physicalist must accept panpsychism.

Leibniz – Monadology (1714)
Pre-modern view of reality as composed of simple experiencing substances (monads), relevant to modern cosmopsychism.

Integrated Information Theory (IIT) – Giulio Tononi
Quantifies consciousness via integrated information (Φ); relevant to panpsychism debates.

Global Workspace Theory (GWT) – Bernard Baars, Stanislas Dehaene
Competing theory of consciousness as widespread broadcast in the brain.

Tononi – “Consciousness as Integrated Information: A Provisional Manifesto” (2008)
Proposes Φ as a measure of consciousness.

Koch & Tononi – “Can a Photodiode Be Conscious?” (2014)
Discusses IIT's radical implications, overlapping with panpsychist ideas.

- Quantum entanglement & holism
- Observer effect
- Cosmopsychism

Roger Penrose & Stuart Hameroff – “Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch-OR)”
Quantum theory of consciousness suggesting proto-consciousness in spacetime geometry.

Sean Carroll – “Why Consciousness Is Not a Quantum Phenomenon” (2016)
A critical response — useful for grounding the debate.

Gregg Rosenberg – “A Place for Consciousness” (2004)
Introduces causal significance of experience in physics.

Hedda Hassel Mørch – “The Integrated Information Theory of Consciousness as a Scientific Theory of Panpsychism” (2020)
Argues that IIT naturally leads to panpsychism.

Philip Goff – “The Universe as Consciousness” (2017)
Argues for a cosmopsychist interpretation of panpsychism.

Luke Roelofs – “Combining Minds: How to Think About Composite Subjectivity” (2019)
In-depth analysis of the combination problem and composite minds.

Itay Shani – “Cosmopsychism: A Holistic Approach to the Metaphysics of Experience” (2015)
Advocates for universe-scale consciousness as fundamental.
